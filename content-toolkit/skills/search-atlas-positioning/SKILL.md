# Search Atlas positioning

## The one principle
Sell the outcome, not the machine. Lead with the result the customer wants, never the mechanism that delivers it. The hole, not the drill. "Command your engine" sells the drill; "wake up to growth you didn't chase" sells the hole.

## The line
The growth engine that runs your marketing while you sleep. ("runs," not "does.")

## What we are
An agentic AI marketing platform that runs SEO, AEO, Google Ads, Meta Ads, content, and site health on its own, fixes what breaks through self-healing loops, builds the assets, and reports back inside Slack, Teams, and ClickUp through the Search Atlas Coworker.

## Who it's for
Agencies, in-house teams, and brands that want to grow across every channel without adding headcount or stitching together a dozen tools.

## The wedge (point of difference)
Everyone else stops short of doing the work. Semrush hands you SEO data and leaves execution to you. Jasper and Scalenut generate content but won't run ads or fix your site. Profound, Peec, and Scrunch show you where you're slipping in AI answers but don't act on it. Even Ploy, the closest, runs your site and stops there. Search Atlas runs every channel and repairs its own work. That last part is what none of them claim.

## The five pillars (every piece of copy advances at least one)
1. It runs while you sleep. Works in the customer's absence, overnight, unasked.
2. It's self-healing. Notices what slipped (a ranking, a broken page, a leaking ad set, a lost AI citation) and fixes it on its own. Keep "self-healing loops" alive.
3. It lives in your workspace. The Search Atlas Coworker posts in Slack, Teams, and ClickUp, and it speaks first. Always show it surfacing something unprompted.
4. Every channel, and it builds the assets. Writes the content, builds the whole site, designs the ads, ships the PR.
5. Priced like a coworker, not a tool stack. Anchor against a hire or agency retainer, never a software bundle.

## The command-and-control ban (this is the one that gets violated)
Cut command, operate, manage, take control, 1 click unless the word sits right next to the autonomy promise. They're verbs of labor that contradict "you don't do the work." Reframe: the customer commands the outcome; the product runs the engine. Even hands-on moments are framed as switching the Atlas Agent on for a channel, not learning to drive a console.

## Never call it a tool
Search Atlas and its features are never "tools." Use platform, coworker, growth engine, channel, or skill. "Tools" is only for the fragmented stack we replace ("you bought 15 tools").

## Naming
- Brand: Search Atlas (two words). Never SearchAtlas, Atlas, or S/A.
- In-app agentic interface: Atlas Agent — the chatbot/agent inside the Search Atlas platform. Use for anything done in the app.
- Messaging-app presence: Search Atlas Coworker (one word coworker, no hyphen) — Search Atlas inside Slack, Teams, ClickUp; the one that posts and speaks first. Don't swap the two: in-app = Atlas Agent, messaging apps = Coworker.
- Sub-products (OTTO, GBP Galactic, Smart Ads, etc.): features or skills of Search Atlas, not co-equal brands in top-level copy.

## Audience note for mixed rooms (summits, challenges, webinars)
Never frame the product as a cheaper replacement for agencies. Dual-anchor: brands hear "a team for less than a hire"; agencies hear "run more clients without added headcount, under your own brand (white-label)."

## Headline territories
- While you sleep: "The growth engine that runs your marketing while you sleep."
- The coworker: "Hire the marketing coworker that never sleeps."
- Self-healing: "It fixes what breaks before you notice."
- Does the work: "Your marketing runs itself now."
