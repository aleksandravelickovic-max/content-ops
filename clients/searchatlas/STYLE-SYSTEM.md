## 1. Brand & Audience

- **Company:** SearchAtlas — B2B SEO and AI visibility platform. Founded by Manick Bhan.
- **Primary audience:** SEO professionals, digital marketers, agency owners, and in-house marketing teams evaluating or using SEO tooling.
- **Secondary audience:** Business owners exploring SEO software for the first time.
- **Brand pillars:** Technical authority, product-led specificity, data-backed claims.

## 2. Voice & Tone

- Voice: knowledgeable peer — direct, technical where warranted, never condescending.
- Register: B2B SaaS editorial. Closer to Ahrefs blog or Search Engine Journal than to generic marketing copy.
- Tone adjusts by content type: product pages are spec-driven and concise; blog posts allow more explanatory depth; case studies lead with quantified outcomes.

## 3. Terminology

### 3.1 Required terms

| Term | Use |
|---|---|
| Domain Power | SearchAtlas proprietary metric — never "domain authority" (that's Moz) |
| OTTO SEO | Always capitalized. The AI SEO autopilot product. |
| Content Genius | The AI content creation tool — not "content writer" or "AI writer" |
| Scholar | The 12-dimension content grading system. Always uppercase. |
| GBP Galactic | Google Business Profile management tool. Never "GMB" (deprecated Google term). |
| Site Explorer | Not "site analyzer" or "domain explorer" |

### 3.2 Strict avoidance list

| Avoid | Use instead |
|---|---|
| Domain authority / DA | Domain Power (SearchAtlas metric) |
| GMB / Google My Business | Google Business Profile / GBP |
| AI-powered (as standalone claim) | Name the specific AI capability |
| All-in-one (without specifics) | List the actual tool categories included |
| Best SEO tool | Describe what it does; let the reader conclude |

## 4. Accuracy & Technical Standards

- All product capability claims must be verifiable against `raw/knowledge/products/`.
- All statistics must be verifiable against `raw/knowledge/facts/` or `raw/knowledge/proof/`.
- Competitor comparisons must use data from `raw/knowledge/competitors/` — never invent feature gaps.
- Pricing claims must match `raw/knowledge/facts/search-atlas-pricing-range.md` and `search-atlas-plan-details.md`.
- Testimonial quotes must come verbatim from `raw/knowledge/testimonials/` — never paraphrase or fabricate.

## 5. SEO Standards

- Follow universal style-system meta standards (title < 60 chars, description 140–160 chars).
- Primary keyword in first sentence of body content.
- Use SearchAtlas-specific entity names (Domain Power, OTTO SEO, Scholar) as exact-match terms where natural.
