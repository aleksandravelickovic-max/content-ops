# Article Brief: AI CMO vs Marketing Agency

## Primary Keyword
AI CMO vs marketing agency

## Secondary Keywords
autonomous platform vs agency retainer; AI marketing vs hiring agency; when to use AI vs agency; agency replacement AI

## Search Intent
Decision comparison — an in-house buyer currently paying an agency retainer wants to know if an autonomous AI platform can replace that spend.

## Audience
In-house marketing leads and founders currently paying agency retainers of $3,000–$10,000/month who are evaluating whether to replace that spend with an AI platform.

## Product Angle
Search Atlas explicitly competes with agency retainers at the product level. Cost model: SA $99–$399/month vs. retainer $3K–$10K/month (directional). Transition framework for moving from agency to platform included.

## Competitors (SERP)
Generic marketing agency retainer models; generalist digital agencies. SERP is thin and addresses "marketing automation vs agency" generically, not the specific comparison of autonomous AI platform vs. retainer agency.

## SERP Interpretation
The reader is a founder or in-house marketing lead who is spending $3K–$10K/month on an agency and is wondering whether an autonomous platform covers the same ground for less. They want an honest answer: what does the agency do that the platform cannot, and what does the platform do that the agency charges for?

The best format: structured comparison by function, cost model, and a transition framework for teams making the switch.

## Article Angle
What an autonomous AI platform replaces in an agency retainer vs. what it does not. Cost model is directional. Transition framework for teams moving from agency to platform. Be honest: agencies still win for brand strategy, PR, influencer, and creative.
