# Article Brief: AI Marketing Automation Mistakes

## Primary Keyword
AI marketing automation mistakes

## Secondary Keywords
AI CMO limitations; risks of autonomous marketing; why AI marketing fails; over-relying on AI marketing

## Search Intent
Problem/failure — practitioners who have had bad experiences with AI marketing tools or want to avoid known failure modes.

## Audience
Marketing managers and founders who have deployed AI tools and seen mixed results, or who are evaluating autonomous marketing and want to know the risk surface.

## Product Angle
Search Atlas has direct observational context from OTTO SEO and Atlas Brain deployments. Failure modes include: wrong Knowledge Graph configuration, skipping Advanced Mode review, deploying before baseline data exists, siloed paid and SEO data layers, ignored LLM visibility gaps, and misreading AI reporting as human-ready.
