## SERP Interpretation
The reader is a founder or marketing lead with a leadership gap. They want a specific answer — which model do I need — not a both-sides summary. The SERP is dominated by fractional CMO providers who are structurally biased toward recommending hybrid. No result uses measurable criteria for when each model is correct. The opportunity is the objective comparison.

The best format: decision comparison ending in a stage-based matrix with a clear recommendation per row.

## Article Angle
The decision variable is where the gap is — not cost. A strategic gap (no defined ICP, no channel strategy) calls for a fractional CMO. An execution gap (strategy exists, no capacity to run it) calls for an AI CMO platform. Most early-stage companies have both; sequence matters.
