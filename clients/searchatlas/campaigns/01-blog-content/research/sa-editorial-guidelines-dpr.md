## Content Format

- **Length:** 1,200–1,800 words
- **Structure:** Introduction + 3–5 sections + conclusion
- **Tone:** Educational and direct — written for experienced practitioners, not beginners
- **Style:** Data-backed, specific, no filler — closer to Lenny's Newsletter or the Ahrefs blog than to marketing copy

---

## Quality Commitments

- Original analysis or proprietary data where relevant — not a rehash of what's already published
- Access to SearchAtlas platform studies (Domain Power research, OTTO SEO performance data, LLM Visibility benchmarks) for data-backed claims
- Every claim supported by a named source, internal study, or concrete example
- No AI-generated filler — all drafts are human-written and human-edited before submission
- Angles pitched specifically to your audience, not recycled across multiple publications

---

## Source Standards

- All statistics sourced and cited inline
- Sources are named — no "studies show" or "experts say"
- Data is current — we do not cite research older than two years unless it is the primary study on a topic
- Proprietary SearchAtlas research cited with methodology notes available on request

---

## Visuals

- Original charts, graphs, or platform screenshots provided with every piece
- Assets can be rebranded for your publication on request
- All visuals are created from real platform data or primary research — no stock imagery or generic diagrams

---

## Editorial Flexibility

- Open to revisions per your editorial feedback — we treat your guidelines as the authority
- No demands on link placement, anchor text, or mention frequency
- If a link to searchatlas.com is included, it is editorially justified — we will not push back if you remove or adjust it
