What Is Atlas Coworker?

Atlas Coworker is Search Atlas's workspace integration layer — it brings Atlas Agent's full marketing execution capabilities into Slack, Microsoft Teams, and ClickUp.

It runs the same AI agent that operates inside the Search Atlas dashboard. That agent audits your site, proposes fixes, writes and grades content, builds and manages Google Ads campaigns, monitors your GBP listings, and tracks your brand presence in AI-generated search results. Atlas Coworker makes all of that available from a chat thread — no dashboard switch required.

Unlike general-purpose AI coworkers such as Viktor, Lindy, or Manus, Atlas Coworker is purpose-built for marketing. It connects natively to OTTO SEO, Content Genius, GBP Galactic, Smart Ads, and LLM Visibility — the same tool stack used by 50,000+ websites. When it runs a campaign audit, it already has your keyword data, your Domain Power, your GSC performance, and your competitor rankings. No integrations to build.

Changes go through human approval before anything publishes.

OTTO SEO — Autonomous Site Optimization

OTTO SEO connects to your site via a JavaScript pixel, audits against GSC data, and deploys on-page fixes directly: title tags, meta descriptions, internal links, schema markup, canonical tags, and Open Graph data. Changes deploy without a developer. The team approves, rejects, or rolls back each change individually from the Slack thread or the dashboard. OTTO SEO completes work that previously required months of manual SEO in compressed time.

Rank Tracker — Live Position Monitoring

Track rankings for up to 6,000 keywords on the Pro plan — locally by city and zip code, or nationally. Content Genius — SEO-Grounded Content Production

Content Genius generates drafts from keyword research and live SERP data — not a blank AI prompt. Request a brief or a full draft from the Slack thread; the output is SEO-grounded and ready for editorial review, not for an SEO specialist to rebuild from scratch.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

General

What is Atlas Coworker?
Atlas Coworker is Search Atlas's AI marketing execution layer for Slack, Microsoft Teams, and ClickUp. It runs the same Atlas Agent that operates inside the Search Atlas dashboard — giving marketing teams access to SEO, content, paid media, and LLM visibility capabilities from their existing messaging tools.
What does Atlas Coworker actually do?
It executes marketing tasks from a chat thread: running site audits, tracking keyword rankings, generating SEO-grounded content briefs, building Google Ads campaigns, updating Google Business Profile listings, and monitoring brand presence in AI-generated search results. It proposes changes and waits for human approval before anything deploys.
How is Atlas Coworker different from Viktor or Lindy?
Viktor and Lindy are general-purpose AI coworkers with broad workflow automation capabilities but no native marketing intelligence. Atlas Coworker is purpose-built for marketing: it connects natively to OTTO SEO, Rank Tracker, Content Genius, GBP Galactic, Smart Ads, and LLM Visibility. When it runs a campaign audit, it already has your keyword data, GSC performance, and competitor rankings — no integrations to build, no context to re-explain.
Who is Atlas Coworker built for?
Marketing teams that run SEO, content, paid media, and local search from a single platform and want those operations accessible from Slack or Teams. It works for in-house teams, agencies managing multiple clients, and lean teams where one person handles multiple marketing channels.
Is Atlas Coworker a replacement for the Search Atlas dashboard?
No. Atlas Coworker is an access layer — it gives your team a way to run operations from Slack or Teams without switching to the dashboard for every task. The full Search Atlas dashboard remains available for deeper work, configuration, and reporting.

Capabilities & Integration

Which messaging platforms does Atlas Coworker support?
Slack launched at the Search Atlas Summit in June 2026. Microsoft Teams and ClickUp are scheduled for July 2026. Telegram, WhatsApp, and Zoom are on the roadmap.
What external tools can Atlas Coworker connect to?
Atlas Coworker connects to 3,000+ tools via native connectors: HubSpot, Salesforce, Meta Ads, TikTok Ads, LinkedIn Ads, Twitter/X Ads, Google Calendar, Google Drive, GitHub, GitLab, Vercel, Intercom, and more. Workspace admins control which connectors are shared across the team and which require individual credentials.
Can I run it on mobile?
Yes. Because Atlas Coworker works through Slack and Teams, it operates on any device those apps run on — including iOS and Android. No desktop required.
How does the approval system work?
Every action Atlas Coworker proposes — an OTTO SEO fix, an ad campaign change, a GBP update — goes through a human approval step before it deploys. You can approve, reject, or roll back individual changes from the thread or from the Search Atlas dashboard. Nothing goes live without explicit confirmation.

Pricing & Plans

Is Atlas Coworker included in my Search Atlas plan?
Yes. Atlas Coworker is included in all Search Atlas plans — Starter ($99/month), Growth ($199/month), and Pro ($399/month). It is not a separate purchase. AI execution actions consume Universal Credits from your plan's shared credit bank.
What are Universal Credits?
Universal Credits replace per-feature quotas. Credits are shared across all tools and actions on your plan — OTTO SEO fixes, Content Genius drafts, Smart Ads operations, and Atlas Coworker executions all draw from the same pool. Credits can be topped up. Search Atlas optimizes costs over time, so the price per action trends down as infrastructure improves.
Is there a free trial?
Yes. All plans include a 7-day free trial with full platform access, including Atlas Coworker. No credit card required to start.
