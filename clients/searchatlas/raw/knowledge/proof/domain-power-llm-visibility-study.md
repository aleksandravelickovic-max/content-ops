## Study
"Relationship Between Domain Power, Domain Rating, Domain Authority and LLM Visibility Score in Citations", executed by Manick Bhan and the Search Atlas research team.

## Samples
- 21,767 domains analyzed for DP, DR, DA correlation with LLM visibility
- 368,972 domains assessed with visibility scores between 50–100% (competition-tier subset)
- Data collection window: August 25 – October 24, 2025
- Published 2025-11-10 by Manick Bhan (Founder, CEO/CTO). Public report: https://searchatlas.com/research/relationship-between-domain-power-domain-rating-domain-authority-and-llm-visibility-score-in-citations/

## Key findings
- Weak/slightly negative correlations between DP, DR, DA and LLM visibility
- Declining win rates across all authority ranges
- Competition-tier effect: visibility falls as co-mentioned domains rise — responses citing 1–2 domains hold consistently higher visibility than those competing against 6+
- Platform examples: Google ≈100% visibility when sole cited domain; YouTube 90–100% across low-to-mid competition (1–5 domains)
- LLMs are relevance-driven, not authority-driven
- Legacy authority metrics do not predict LLM citation behavior

## Product impact (INTERNAL ONLY — not in public report)
Updated Domain Power scoring model to capture LLM-driven relevance patterns. Recalibrated DP weighting so LLM visibility signals, co-mention frequency, and response-level prominence operate as primary predictors rather than legacy authority correlations. NOTE: this recalibration does not appear in the public report — do not attribute it to the study in published content.
