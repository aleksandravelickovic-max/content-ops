Coworker / AI-CMO positioning threaded through the **current dark homepage design**. Hero ships with 4 variants to test; V1 ("while you sleep") is the approved direction. Live preview: `html/preview.html`.

## Positioning through-line
Search Atlas is the marketing coworker (your AI CMO) that does the work and tells you in Slack. That story now runs through every section, not just the hero: the problem → the "while you slept" timeline → "one coworker, every asset" → the never-stopping loop → "Meet the Search Atlas Coworker" → priced like a hire.

## Two flags before publish (not blocking)
1. **Autonomy answer** — copy now says "Yes, that's the point. It deploys fixes automatically… approval mode optional." Confirm with product that **auto-deploy is the real default** (an earlier product FAQ said approval-gated). If approval is the default, run hero **V3** instead.
2. **Headline stats** — 100k installs, $1B managed, 5,000 agencies, 90% in 30 days, 350+ integrations are the concept's placeholders. Need data + legal sign-off before publish. Testimonials are the **real verbatim** quotes, not placeholders.
