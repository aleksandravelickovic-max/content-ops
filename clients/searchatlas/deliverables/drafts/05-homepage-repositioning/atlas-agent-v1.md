## 7. Frequently Asked Questions
*Keep the heading and all questions.* Rewrites below — cut "tool" for Search Atlas, "one system," "execution system."

**What is an AI SEO agent?**
> An AI SEO agent is software that plans and runs SEO work for you instead of stopping at a report. Atlas Agent goes further than search alone: it turns plain-language requests into real actions across SEO, content, authority, paid ads, local, and AI search visibility, all from one place.

**What is Atlas Agent?**
> Atlas Agent is the conversational AI SEO agent inside Search Atlas. It runs your marketing work from a single conversation, so you don't switch between products to get something done.

**How is Atlas Agent different from other AI tools?**
> Most AI tools stop at insights or hand you a draft to finish. Atlas Agent does the work directly and applies the changes to your site, content, authority, and visibility.

**Who is Atlas Agent for?**
> Marketers, agencies, and teams running SEO, content, and growth across several channels who want the work handled without doing it all themselves.

**Do I need technical skills to use it?**
> No. Atlas Agent works in plain language. You describe the outcome you want, and it runs the workflows and ships the changes. No code, no complex setup.

**What is Vibe SEO?**
> Vibe SEO means running SEO through plain-language requests to an AI instead of doing each step by hand. Atlas Agent is built for it: you describe the goal, and it turns the request into finished work.
