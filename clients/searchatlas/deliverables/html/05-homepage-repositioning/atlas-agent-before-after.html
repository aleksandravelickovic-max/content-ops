What changed and why

  • Killed the command-and-control spine. "One command activates your entire marketing execution," "control everything through conversation," and "every command moves instantly to action" all contradict the promise that you don't do the work.
  • Stopped calling it a tool. The live page calls Atlas Agent an "AI SEO tool" and an "execution system." The rewrite uses agent, platform, and the work it does.
  • Dropped the antithesis pattern ("Most AI tools stop at insights… Atlas Agent is an assistant that…") and stated the wedge straight.
  • Led with the outcome: say what you want, the work gets done. Kept Atlas Agent (in-app) distinct from the Coworker (Slack/Teams/ClickUp).
  • Built out Playbooks as its own section with real playbook names, instead of one passing mention.

Grouped by channel; you can also build your own.
