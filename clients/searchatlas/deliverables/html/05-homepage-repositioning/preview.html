Does Search Atlas make changes on its own?

Yes, that's the point. Search Atlas deploys fixes and optimizations automatically across your channels. If you'd rather sign off first, switch on approval mode and nothing ships until you say so.

How fast will I see results?

Search Atlas starts working the moment it's installed. Most sites see movement within a few days, and many within the first few weeks.

Does Search Atlas really live in Slack, Teams, and ClickUp?

Yes. The Search Atlas Coworker posts updates and answers your questions right inside the tools your team already uses, so there's no extra dashboard to keep checking.

Do I need developer or technical skills?

No. Search Atlas handles the implementation and the technical work itself, so you don't need a developer to act on anything.

Yes. Deliver the dashboards, reports, and results under your own brand.

Yes, a 7-day free trial with the full platform, so you can watch it work before you commit.
