What it does

H2: From a request to finished work

Atlas Agent turns a request into finished work. It reads your site and your data, sizes up your competitors, then runs the actual steps: audits, on-page and technical fixes, content, local listings, paid campaigns, and AI-visibility checks. Campaigns launch, optimizations ship, and content publishes without you moving between products.

It carries context from one request to the next, so a goal you set on Monday still shapes what it does on Thursday. That continuity is what keeps growth moving across search engines and AI answers.

How you put it to work

H2: Set the goal once. Pick how hands-on you want to be.

You work with Atlas Agent the way you’d brief a teammate: tell it what you want in plain language. It plans the steps and runs them across every connected channel. Two modes cover how much oversight you want.

  • Fast mode: minimal setup, quick outcomes. Good for routine work you want off your plate.
  • Advanced mode: the agent pauses for your sign-off on high-impact changes before anything goes live, so nothing ships unseen.

Playbooks

H2: Pick a playbook, and it runs the whole sequence

Atlas Agent comes with a library of playbooks: ready-made workflows for the jobs you run over and over. You launch one, and the agent follows the proven sequence end to end, then tells you what it did. Some run on a schedule, so the work repeats without you starting it again.

A few from the library:

  • Fix Critical On-Page SEO Issues: rewrites titles, meta descriptions, and headings on your highest-value pages
  • Generate a Complete Backlink Strategy: reads your site and your competitors, then builds a backlink plan with link types, pacing, and budget
  • Launch Core Google Ads Campaign: builds conversion-ready campaigns for a business, with every product and service structured in
  • Expand AI Search Topics & Questions: finds the questions people ask ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity so you can track where your brand shows up
  • Generate Local Landing Pages: plans and builds location pages for the cities you have demand in, and skips the ones you already cover

Playbooks are grouped by channel, from SEO and authority to content, local, paid ads, AI visibility, and full site builds, so the right one is easy to find. You can also build your own.

The difference

H2: Most AI SEO agents stop at advice. This one ships the work.

Most AI SEO platforms are recommendation engines. They generate advice or draft text and leave the execution to you, which means the to-do list still lands on a person. Atlas Agent performs the tasks directly from the conversation and applies the changes to your site, content, authority, and visibility.

What it runs

H2: Every channel, from one conversation

One agent covers the work that usually takes a stack of separate logins:

  • SEO: technical and on-page fixes plus site health, through OTTO
  • Content: drafts and publishes content through Content Genius

It runs inside Search Atlas today. When you want it where your team already works, the Search Atlas Coworker brings the same agent into Slack, Teams, and ClickUp.
