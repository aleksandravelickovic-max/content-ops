## Critical findings (must fix before publish)

### C1 — /storage-unit-near-me link missing on all 5 pages
**Scope requirement:** Every page must include a contextual link to `/storage-unit-near-me`. Not documented in optimization notes on any page. Either absent or undocumented. Confirm and add if missing — best placed in the "Find [X] Near You" CTA section of each page.
**Affects:** All 5 pages

### C2 — "Our" ownership language in pricing sections and FAQs
The platform is a marketplace aggregator. Phrases implying ownership are a style system violation.
- Car: "facilities listed through **our search tool**"
- Boat: "facilities **you'll find through our search tool**"
- RV: "facilities in **our search network**"
- Wine (FAQ): "Use **our search tool** to find climate controlled storage facilities near you"
- Motorcycle (FAQ): "Use **our search tool** to find storage facilities near you"

Fix consistently: → "facilities listed on FindSelfStorage.com" / "the FindSelfStorage.com search" / "facilities in the network"
**Affects:** All 5 pages

### C3 — RV page is missing the climate control section
Every other vehicle page has an explicit H2 covering when climate control matters. The RV page has no equivalent section. This is a scope gap and creates inconsistency across the set. Climate control availability for RVs is a real search query — it belongs on this page.
**Fix:** Add "Do You Need Climate Controlled RV Storage?" section mirroring the car/boat/motorcycle approach.
**Affects:** RV only

### C4 — Formatting errors
Three formatting errors in the submitted drafts:
- **Wine page** (Who Needs Wine Storage): "Wine purchased to age for years before opening**Restaurant** and hospitality businesses" — missing line break between two bullet items. Will publish broken.
- **Motorcycle page** (keywords block): "Indoor motorcycle storage**Motorcycle** winter storage" — missing line break.
- **RV page** (meta keywords): "How much does rv storage cos" — truncated, missing final "t".
**Affects:** Wine, Motorcycle, RV

### C5 — "Whilst" in motorcycle intro
"A dedicated motorcycle storage unit gives your bike a secure, protected space at a price that fits most budgets **whilst** keeping it ready to ride when the season starts again."
British English on a US platform. Replace with "while."
**Affects:** Motorcycle only

## Major findings (fix before publish)

### M1 — Car page H1 uses "Vehicle" instead of "Car"
H1: "Car Storage Units: Find Indoor, Covered & Outdoor **Vehicle** Storage Near You"
The primary keyword is "car storage" not "vehicle storage." This dilutes exact-match signal in the H1. All other pages keep the primary noun consistent through the H1 ("Boat Storage Units: Find Indoor, Covered & Outdoor **Boat** Storage Near You").
**Fix:** "Car Storage Units: Find Indoor, Covered & Outdoor Car Storage Near You"
**Affects:** Car only

### M2 — Car page H2 uses ownership framing
H2: "Types of Car Storage Units **We Connect You With**"
"We connect you with" implies agency/operation, not a neutral marketplace. This is the only heading across all 5 pages with this framing.
**Fix:** "Types of Car Storage Units" or "Car Storage Unit Types Explained"
**Affects:** Car only

### M3 — Car and Boat intros follow identical syntactic template
The two intros share the same sentence scaffold:
> "Whether you [need/are doing X, Y, or Z], [noun] storage units offer a safer and more [adjective] solution/alternative than leaving your [item] exposed in a [location]."

Any reader who visits both pages will notice this. It signals copy-paste production, not original writing.
**Fix:** Rewrite the Boat intro with a distinct opening structure. Boat storage has specific triggers (dock access, HOA restrictions, winter haul-out timing) that can lead the page uniquely.
**Affects:** Boat (primary fix), Car (secondary)

### M4 — Specialty Vaults (wine page) may be unverifiable
The wine page introduces "Specialty Wine Vaults" as a storage type available through the platform. This is not confirmed in the FindSelfStorage.com style system or raw research. If the platform does not list specialty wine vaults, this describes a service it cannot deliver.
**Fix:** Confirm with CSM whether specialty wine vault facilities are actually in the network. If not confirmed, remove or reframe as a general market context note ("Some markets have dedicated wine vault facilities, though availability varies. Use the search to filter for climate-controlled options near you.")
**Affects:** Wine only

### M5 — Motorcycle meta title is weak
"Motorcycle Storage Units Near You | **All Storage Types**"
"All Storage Types" is generic and adds no keyword value. Car uses "All **Car** Storage Types"; Boat uses "All **Boat** Storage Types." Motorcycle's secondary segment breaks the pattern and loses keyword specificity.
**Fix:** "Motorcycle Storage Units Near You | All Motorcycle Storage Types" or "Indoor & Outdoor Motorcycle Storage Near You"
**Affects:** Motorcycle only

### M6 — SERP competitor analysis not documented
The task scope requires identifying 3–5 direct competitors for structure ideas. No deliverable includes this documentation on any page. This may mean it was done but not logged, or not done. Either way, the ClickUp deliverable is incomplete.
**Fix:** Add a brief competitor notes section to each page's optimization notes: which 3–5 pages were reviewed, what structural insights they produced.
**Affects:** All 5 pages (deliverable gap)

### M7 — Internal links not listed in deliverables
The scope requires listing all internal links added per page. The optimization notes confirm CTAs and cross-links were updated but do not enumerate the actual links added. Incomplete deliverable for the ClickUp task.
**Fix:** Add "Internal Links Added" section to each page's optimization notes with the full list.

## Minor findings

### N1 — CTA closing blocks are copy-paste across all 5 pages
The "Find [X] Storage Units Near You" CTA section is virtually identical across all pages:
> "Ready to [verb] your [vehicle/collection]? Search self storage facilities near you to compare [types] options by size, price, and location. The search is completely free with no credit card required and no obligation. Compare prices, check availability, and reserve your unit online in minutes."

This is fine for conversion copy (consistency has value), but the "Compare prices, check availability, and reserve your unit online in minutes" sentence appears verbatim on every page. Consider varying the final sentence by page to reduce the copy-paste fingerprint at scale.

### N2 — Prep checklist section openers are formulaic on vehicle pages
Each vehicle page opens its prep checklist with the same construction:
- Car: "Take these steps before parking your vehicle to prevent..."
- Boat: "Properly preparing your boat before storing it protects..."
- RV: "Proper preparation protects your RV from..."
- Motorcycle: "Proper preparation protects the engine, paint, tires..."

These are textbook AI-produced checklist intros. Each should open with something specific to the vehicle type rather than a generic "preparation protects X" frame.

### N3 — "Solution" in boat intro
"boat storage units offer a safer and more practical **solution** than leaving your watercraft exposed"
"Solution" is a generic SaaS filler word. The contrast already lands without it.
**Fix:** "boat storage units are a safer, more practical option than leaving your watercraft exposed"

### N4 — Propane step in RV prep checklist is underweight
The RV prep checklist includes "Check and note the propane" as a single step with minimal guidance. Every other step has 2–3 sentences. Propane is a safety item — an incomplete instruction here is a liability gap.
**Fix:** Expand to cover: turn off the valve at the tank, why propane must be off for enclosed storage, and whether to remove tanks entirely for long-term storage.

### N5 — "Near you" duplication in wine CTA
"Search facilities near you to find climate controlled storage options **near you**."
Simple copy error. Fix to: "Find climate controlled storage options near you" or "Search nearby facilities to compare climate-controlled options."
