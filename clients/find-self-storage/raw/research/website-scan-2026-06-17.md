## Gaps and quality flags

- Facility count ("over 10,000") — unverified, no citation. Competitive context: US Storage Units 10,000+; US Self Storage 20,000+. FSS is at the lower end.
- "lowest prices" claim appears once on-site but is not backed by a price-match policy. Handle with care.
- Student discount ("potential 15% savings with student ID") — hedged, no named facilities, low confidence. Do not repeat without confirmation.
- No affiliate/referral disclosure despite operating as a referral marketplace. Relevance for editorial: do not make claims about relationships with facilities.
- Blog URL inconsistencies — may indicate content management issues; avoid referencing specific blog URLs in content without checking.
- About Us is thin — no history, no team, no mission statement beyond core service pitch.
