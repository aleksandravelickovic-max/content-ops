# Find Self Storage Service Taxonomy

Source: STYLE-SYSTEM.md, refreshed website intelligence, and local client research.

## Storage Types

- Standard Self Storage: General storage units for household, personal, or business belongings.
- Climate-Controlled Storage: Storage units with temperature or humidity control where facility features support it.
- Drive-Up Storage: Ground-level units with direct vehicle access for loading and unloading.
- 24-Hour Access Storage: Storage facilities or units that allow access outside standard office hours, when available.
- Business Storage: Storage for business inventory, equipment, documents, or supplies.
- Student Storage: Short-term or seasonal storage for students between housing terms.
- Vehicle Storage: Storage category for cars, RVs, boats, motorcycles, trailers, and similar vehicles.
- Car Storage: Vehicle storage for cars, with indoor, covered, or outdoor options depending on facility.
- RV Storage: Storage for recreational vehicles, with size and coverage options depending on facility.
- Boat Storage: Storage for boats and marine trailers, with facility-specific access and space limits.
- Motorcycle Storage: Storage options for motorcycles and small recreational vehicles.
- Trailer Storage: Storage for utility trailers, travel trailers, and other towable vehicles.
- Wine Storage: Specialized storage for wine where climate and facility features support it.
