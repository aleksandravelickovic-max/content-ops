## Company Overview

Pekas Smith is an Arizona law firm dedicated exclusively to Social Security Disability cases, including Social Security Disability Insurance (SSDI) and Supplemental Security Income (SSI).

The firm represents individuals who can no longer work because of medical conditions and need help navigating the federal disability benefits system. Representation may begin with the initial application and continue through reconsideration, hearings, and appeals.

Social Security Disability claims involve strict eligibility rules and complex procedures. Many applicants receive denials before securing benefits. Pekas Smith helps clients prepare applications, organize supporting medical evidence, and present their cases before the Social Security Administration.

## Primary Audience

Arizona residents who are unable to maintain employment because of a disabling medical condition.

Common situations:
- Individuals applying for SSDI or SSI benefits
- Applicants whose disability claims have already been denied
- Claimants preparing for a disability hearing
- Individuals seeking legal help during the appeals process

## Core Problems the Firm Solves

- Determining whether a medical condition meets Social Security's disability criteria
- Completing the detailed documentation required for an application
- Responding to claim denials
- Preparing for hearings during the appeals process
- Presenting medical evidence effectively

Content frequently references federal disability programs and procedures. Common terms:

- Social Security Administration (SSA)

The client is very particular about keyword usage and terminology accuracy. Wording should follow their preferred phrasing closely.
