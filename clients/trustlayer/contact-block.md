## CTA contexts

### Blog post CTA (all blog content)

The blog CTA directs the reader toward their next step with TrustLayer — a demo request, meeting, or TrustScore.

**Approved blog CTA structures:**

> Ready to stop chasing COIs manually? Request a demo at [trustlayer.io](https://trustlayer.io/) and see how TrustLayer automates collection, verification, and monitoring across your vendor network.

> Get your free TrustScore at [trustlayer.io](https://trustlayer.io/) to see how your third-party risk management compares.

> Schedule a meeting at [trustlayer.io/contact](https://trustlayer.io/contact) to see the platform in action.

Rules:
- Must direct the reader to trustlayer.io or trustlayer.io/contact
- Must use one of the confirmed CTA actions (meet, get started, get TrustScore)
- Tone: direct and practical — not a hard sell
- Do not add a phone number until one is confirmed by the CSM
- Do not use generic "learn more" or "contact us" without a specific next step

### Solution / feature page CTA

For solution pages and feature pages, the CTA should match the page's primary audience and stage in the funnel.

**Approved phrasing:**
> See TrustLayer in action — [request a demo](https://trustlayer.io/contact).

> Get your free TrustScore and find out where your vendor compliance stands.
