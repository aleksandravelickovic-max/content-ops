## Company Overview

TrustLayer is an AI-powered third-party risk management platform. It automates the collection, verification, and management of compliance documents — primarily certificates of insurance (COIs), along with W-9s, business licenses, and contracts. The platform replaces manual email, spreadsheet, and phone workflows with an automated pipeline: vendors receive requests, submit documents directly (no sign-in required), and TrustLayer validates, stores, and monitors compliance status in real time.

TrustLayer does not issue insurance. It does not underwrite coverage. It verifies and tracks compliance documents and flags gaps — it does not guarantee that any vendor is compliant.
