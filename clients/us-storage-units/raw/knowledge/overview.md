## Platform Description

US Storage Units (USStorageUnits.com) is a self-storage comparison and reservation marketplace. It is not a storage facility operator. The platform connects users with storage providers across the United States, allowing them to search, compare, and reserve units online at no cost.

Network size (as of client brief):
- 10,000+ storage facilities in the network
- 2,000,000+ storage units available for browsing and reservation

Users find storage by entering a city name or ZIP code. The platform displays available units across nearby facilities in real time, with pricing, unit size, amenities, and availability shown side by side. Once a user selects a unit, they complete a free reservation — no credit card required, no obligation, cancelable or changeable at any time.

## Audience

Primary use cases that drive traffic and reservations:

| Audience segment | Primary need |
|---|---|
| People moving or downsizing | Short-term overflow storage during a move |
| Renters relocating | Temporary storage between leases |
| Homeowners | Seasonal item storage; decluttering |
| Small businesses | Inventory, equipment, or document storage |
| Vehicle owners | Car, RV, or boat storage |

These users are typically in decision mode — they have an immediate storage need and are comparing options on price and location. Content should serve the comparison-and-reservation intent, not build long-term brand affinity.

## Brand Voice

Utilitarian and direct. The platform's primary job is to reduce friction between a user with a storage need and a facility that can serve it. Copy should be:

- Action-oriented — move users toward search, compare, reserve
- Low-friction — emphasize ease and no-obligation
- Practical — unit sizes, features, and pricing matter more than emotional language
- Transactional — this is a marketplace, not a lifestyle brand

Avoid aspirational, lifestyle-driven, or editorial tones. This is not a blog client.

## Storage Types and Specifications

All size and specification data below is confirmed from the client brief.

### Standard Self-Storage

Indoor self-storage units at a partner facility.

| Unit size | Typical use |
|---|---|
| 3×4 ft (locker) | Small locker; ceiling height approx. 3–4 ft |
| 5×5 | Small closet; boxes, small furniture |
| 5×10 | One room of furniture and boxes |
| 10×10 | One- to two-bedroom apartment contents |
| 10×15 | Two- to three-bedroom home contents |
| 10×20 | Three- to four-bedroom home; vehicle |
| 10×30 | Large home; multiple vehicles |

Standard ceiling height: 8–9 ft (lockers are 3–4 ft).

### Climate-Controlled Storage

Indoor units with temperature and/or humidity regulation. Often located in multi-floor facilities with elevator access. Best for electronics, documents, wood furniture, artwork, and clothing.

### Drive-Up Storage

Ground-level units with direct vehicle access — load and unload without carrying items through a building. Similar to a garage-style unit.

### 24-Hour Access Storage

Facilities that allow tenants to access their unit at any hour, including overnight and weekends.

### Car/Vehicle Storage

- 10×10: small cars
- 10×20: midsize cars, SUVs, trucks
- 10×30: large trucks, oversized vehicles

Available in three configurations: enclosed (most protection), covered (overhead shelter, open sides), and uncovered (outdoor parking).

### RV Storage

Outdoor or covered spaces designed for recreational vehicles. Size and configuration vary by facility. City-level URL: `/{state}/rv-storage-{city}`.

### Boat Storage

Outdoor, covered, or indoor spaces for boats and watercraft. Configuration and access vary by facility.

### Portable Storage

Storage units delivered to the customer's location, loaded at the customer's pace, then picked up and stored or transported. Also known as portable storage containers or moving pods.

### Business Storage

For small businesses, e-commerce sellers, and contractors. Includes warehouse-style spaces with loading docks at some facilities. Used for inventory, equipment, product pallets, and document archiving.

## Differentiators from US Self Storage (Sister Site)

US Self Storage (usselfstoragelocator.com or similar) is a sister platform in the same network. Key distinctions:

| Dimension | US Storage Units | US Self Storage |
|---|---|---|
| Network size | 10,000+ facilities | 20,000+ facilities |
| Primary CTA emphasis | Find → compare → reserve | Browse and locate |
| Core brand claim | Guaranteed lowest prices | Broader directory |
| Blog/editorial | None — marketplace only | May have editorial content |
| Booking flow | Free reservation, no credit card | Varies |

Do not conflate the two. When writing for USStorageUnits.com, cite 10,000+ facilities — not 20,000+.

---

## What the Platform Does NOT Do

- Does not own or operate any storage facility
- Does not set pricing at individual facilities
- Does not guarantee specific unit availability at a specific facility
- Does not have a blog or editorial section
- Does not publish phone numbers for individual facilities (users are directed to the site's booking flow)
