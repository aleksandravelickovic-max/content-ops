# US Storage Units Service Taxonomy

Source: STYLE-SYSTEM.md, refreshed website intelligence, and local client research.

## Storage Types

- Standard Self-Storage: Storage units from small lockers through larger units, with size details verified per page.
- Climate-Controlled Storage: Indoor temperature or humidity-regulated storage for sensitive belongings.
- Drive-Up Storage: Ground-level units with direct vehicle access and garage-style doors.
- 24-Hour Access: Storage facilities or units with any-hour access where available.
- Business Storage: Storage for inventory, equipment, records, and business supplies.
- Car / Vehicle Storage: Storage for cars and broader vehicle needs, with covered, enclosed, or outdoor options depending on facility.
- RV Storage: Storage for recreational vehicles, with configuration verified by facility.
- Boat Storage: Storage for boats, trailers, and marine equipment.
- Portable Storage: Portable units delivered, loaded on site, and picked up for storage or moves.
