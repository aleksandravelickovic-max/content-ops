## How the CTA flow works

Users do not call a number or fill out a contact form. The conversion path is:

1. User searches by city name or ZIP code on the platform
2. Results show available units at nearby facilities with real-time pricing
3. User selects a unit and completes a reservation
4. No credit card is required; reservation is free, with no obligation

All page CTAs should point toward this flow.

## Approved CTA language

Use these phrasings to close pages. Adapt wording to context — do not treat them as rigid templates.

**Standard reserve CTA:**
> Search for storage units near you and reserve for free — no credit card required, no obligation, cancel or change your reservation at any time.

**With price claim:**
> Lock in your cheapest rate and reserve your storage unit online. It only takes a few minutes — and it's free.

**For location pages:**
> Find available storage units in [City, State] and reserve for free. Compare prices across nearby facilities and lock in your rate today — no credit card, no commitment.

**For vehicle/specialty storage pages:**
> Search available [RV/boat/car] storage near you, compare facilities and pricing, and reserve online at no cost. Guaranteed lowest prices on the web.

## Rules

- Never substitute a vague "contact us" or generic "learn more" for a reserve-focused CTA.
- Do not invent a phone number or contact form URL until confirmed.
- CTA tone should be direct and low-friction — not pushy, not soft-sell.
- Always include at least one of these confirmed claims in the CTA: "free to reserve," "no credit card required," "guaranteed lowest prices on the web," or "cancel or change at any time."
