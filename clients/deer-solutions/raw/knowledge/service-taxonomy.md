# Deer Solution Service Taxonomy

Source: Deer Solution website reviewed on 2026-07-14.

## Deer Control Services

- Deer Control Repellent Service: Professional service that applies deer repellent to help protect plants and shrubs from deer damage.
- Free At-Home Consultation and Estimate: Initial appointment where the property is reviewed and the homeowner receives a deer control estimate.
- Customized Deer Control Protection Plan: Property-specific plan created with input from the homeowner after the consultation.
- Certified Deer Damage Expert Visit: Service consultation performed by a trained deer control professional who evaluates the landscape and deer damage concern.
- Professional Repellent Application: Ongoing application service using Deer Solution's proprietary deer repellent.

## Repellent Positioning

- Proprietary Deer Repellent: Deer repellent described by the company as all-natural and designed to deter deer by taste and smell.
- Plant and Shrub Protection: Core customer outcome category focused on protecting flowers, shrubs, and landscaping from deer browsing.
- Family, Pet, and Environment-Friendly Framing: Website positioning for the repellent. Keep this wording tied to the source; do not add unsupported safety claims.
- Clear-Drying, Low-Odor Application: Website positioning that the repellent dries clear and does not have foul odors.

## Business / Franchise Context

- Independently Owned Local Branches: Deer Solution locations are independently owned and operated.
- Franchise Opportunity: Separate business ownership path linked from the main site. Do not mix this with homeowner service pages unless the brief is franchise-focused.
