# Na Pali Coast Boat Tour: Why Makana Charters Stands Apart

The Na Pali Coast runs along Kauai's northwest shore — sheer green cliffs, hidden sea caves, and waterfalls that drop directly into the Pacific. A Na Pali Coast boat tour is the only way to see most of it. Makana Charters has been running these tours from the same waters for over 200 years, continuing the legacy of a Native Hawaiian family whose connection to this coastline is documented back to the 1800s. This page covers the route, our fleet, what separates us from other operators, and how to book.

## What the Na Pali Coast Actually Looks Like From the Water

What does a Na Pali Coast boat tour route cover?

**A standard tour departs Kikiaola Harbor and moves along the coast through Polihale, Kalalau Valley, the Honopu Archway, sea caves, and back — covering the full northwestern face of the cliffs over 4.5 to 5 hours.**

The coast reveals itself gradually from the water. Polihale, where the sand meets the cliffs at the island's western edge, marks the start of the Na Pali section. From there, the ridgeline rises into the fluted green walls of the cliffs, deepening as the boat moves northeast. Kalalau Valley opens on the port side — carved by a stream that has been running for millions of years, visible now only by boat or a multi-day hike.

What is the Honopu Archway?

**The Honopu Archway is a natural rock arch at the base of the Na Pali cliffs, known as a filming location for King Kong, Six Days Seven Nights, a James Bond film, and Pirates of the Caribbean.**

The arch connects two beaches that have no road access and no hiking trail in. The only way to reach Honopu on your own is to swim ashore. On the tour, you see it from the water — close enough to understand why film crews kept returning.

What sea caves does the Na Pali Coast boat tour enter?

**We enter Pirates Cave, also known by its Hawaiian name Waiahuakua, and Rainbow Falls — as long as ocean conditions allow.**

Sea caves on the Na Pali Coast form where the ocean has carved into volcanic rock over centuries. Pirates Cave is large enough for the catamaran to enter when conditions are right. Our captains read the swell before approaching; if the ocean says no, we move on. Rainbow Falls takes its name from the light that refracts through the mist inside the cave on the right day.

What wildlife is commonly seen on the Na Pali Coast?

**Spinner dolphins and Pacific Bottlenose dolphins are commonly spotted along the route, and it is rare that we do not see them.**

Hawaiian green sea turtles, known as honu, surface regularly near the snorkel stop. Diverse reef fish and coral appear underwater during the roughly one-hour snorkel, at locations that vary by ocean conditions. Nualolo Kai and Kekaha are among the most common snorkel spots. Our guides know the tides and habitats, which is why they tend to find the animals before guests notice anything at all. Sightings are never guaranteed; the ocean decides.

## What Makes Makana Different

### 200-Year Native Hawaiian Family History

What is the history behind Makana Charters?

**Makana Charters continues the legacy of Liko Kauai Cruises, a Native Hawaiian family enterprise with documented roots on the Na Pali Coast going back over 200 years.**

"Makana" is the Hawaiian word for gift. The name reflects what this family has been passing down through generations: knowledge of these waters, their tides, their caves, their history. Our office at Hale Pili sits on Waimea River, the same land the family has operated from for generations. When you book with Makana, you are going out with a family for whom this coast is home — the same waters, the same caves, the same horizon their family has known for over 200 years.

### Closest Departure Point to the Coast

Why does the departure harbor matter for a Na Pali Coast tour?

**We depart from Kikiaola Harbor in Kekaha — the closest launch point to the Na Pali Coast — which means up to an hour more time on the cliffs compared to boats leaving from Port Allen.**

Most operators on Kauai depart from Port Allen, on the south shore. Getting from Port Allen to the Na Pali Coast takes time, and that time cuts directly into how long you spend at the cliffs, the caves, and the snorkel spot. Kikiaola Harbor sits at the island's western edge, minutes from where the Na Pali cliffs begin. Less transit, more coast.

### Smaller Groups

How many guests are on a Makana Charters tour?

**Our tours carry between 12 and 32 guests depending on the boat — smaller than most operators on the island.**

Fewer guests means more room on deck, fewer people competing for the same view, and more time for the crew to spend with you rather than managing a crowd. On private charters, the group size is entirely your own. On shared tours, the cap holds.

### Boats Built for Sea Cave Access

Why can Makana Charters enter sea caves when other boats cannot?

**Our catamarans are built to enter the Na Pali Coast sea caves safely — a capability most larger tour boats do not have.**

When conditions are right, our catamaran moves into the cave mouth and the daylight drops away. The walls close in, the sound of the water amplifies off the rock, and light comes back from the far side. Larger high-capacity boats stay at the entrance — their size prevents them from following us in. Access still depends on what the ocean is doing that day. Our captains make the call on the water.
