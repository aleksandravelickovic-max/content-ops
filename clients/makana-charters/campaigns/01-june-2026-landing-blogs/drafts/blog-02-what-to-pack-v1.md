# What to Pack for a Na Pali Coast Snorkel Tour

Yes, snorkel gear is included on your Na Pali Coast snorkel tour. Makana Charters provides snorkel gear, flotation devices, a deli-style lunch, soft drinks, and local juices for every guest. Your packing list is shorter than most people expect. What you bring yourself comes down to sun protection, a towel, and a few personal items for comfort on the water.

---

## What Makana Provides

Your Na Pali Coast snorkel tour includes everything you need for the water and the meal. We outfit every guest with snorkel gear and flotation devices before the snorkel stop, so there is no need to rent equipment separately on the island.

Lunch is served onboard: a deli-style spread with turkey, ham, roast beef, and a vegetarian option, plus fruits and snacks. Soft drinks and local juices are included throughout the tour.

Every boat has an onboard restroom. Our larger catamarans also have freshwater showers, so you can rinse off after snorkeling before the return to Kikiaola Harbor.

The full tour runs 4.5 to 5 hours, and from the time you board to the time you return, your basics are covered.

## What to Wear on a Na Pali Coast Tour

Wear your swimsuit or beachwear under your clothes. You will be in the water for about one hour at the snorkel stop, so plan on getting wet. A hat matters more than most guests realize. Sun exposure on the water is direct and lasts the full tour.

Pack a light layer for the boat ride. The catamaran moves fast, and the wind off the ocean is cooler than the air on shore, especially in the morning. A lightweight shirt or thin cover-up is enough.

Wear shoes or sandals you are comfortable walking in at the harbor, even if you plan to go barefoot on the boat. Kikiaola Small Boat Harbor has uneven surfaces near the dock.

Reef-safe sunscreen is the right call near coral. The snorkel stop puts you directly above reef habitat, and standard sunscreen washes off into the water. Switch to a reef-safe formula before your tour.

## What to Bring

Pack a towel. We have freshwater showers on our larger boats, but we do not supply towels. A quick-dry travel towel packs flat and takes up almost no space.

Plan to reapply sunscreen during the tour. What you put on at the harbor will wear off well before you return. Reef-safe formulas only, near the coral.

Sunglasses and a hat earn their place on the water. UV reflects off the ocean surface and hits harder than it feels on land.

For photos, a waterproof phone case is worth it. Phones are the most common item lost overboard, and a case with a lanyard or float attachment removes that risk. A waterproof camera works too.

If you are prone to seasickness, talk to your doctor before the tour and take any medication before you board. Over-the-counter options stop working once symptoms start.

You are welcome to bring your own snorkel mask if you have a preferred fit. We provide gear for every guest, but if you use prescription lenses or own a mask that fits better, bring it.

Light personal snacks or an extra drink are fine if you want something beyond the lunch spread. Space is shared among all guests, so keep any bag small.

---

## What to Leave Behind

Leave valuables at your hotel. Phones and cameras can be secured in a waterproof case, but jewelry, watches, and anything you cannot afford to lose or get wet should stay ashore. The boat is active on the water and spray comes aboard regularly.

Skip the large bags. Storage space on the catamaran is shared, and a small dry bag or a backpack holds everything on this list.

---

## A Note on Seasickness

Most guests on a Na Pali Coast snorkel tour do not have issues. Our catamarans are built specifically for these waters, and the crew knows how to read conditions. That said, the route includes open ocean, and sea state varies by day and season.

If you have a history of motion sickness on boats, talk to a doctor before your tour. Over-the-counter options work best when taken before boarding, not after symptoms start. If your doctor prescribes a patch, it needs time to take effect, so plan ahead.
