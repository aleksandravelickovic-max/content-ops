# What to Pack for a Na Pali Coast Snorkel Tour

Yes, snorkel gear is included on your Na Pali Coast tour. Makana Charters provides snorkel gear, flotation devices, a deli-style lunch, soft drinks, and local juices for every guest. Your packing list is lighter than you might expect. What you bring yourself comes down to sun protection, a towel, and a few personal items for comfort on the water.

---

## What Makana Provides

Your Na Pali coast snorkel tour includes everything you need for the water and the meal. We outfit every guest with snorkel gear and flotation devices before the snorkel stop, so there is no need to rent equipment separately on the island.

Lunch is served onboard. We put out a deli-style spread with turkey, ham, roast beef, and a vegetarian option, along with fruits and snacks. Soft drinks and local juices are included throughout the tour.

Every boat has an onboard restroom. Our larger catamarans also have freshwater showers, so you can rinse off after snorkeling before the return to Kikiaola Harbor.

The full tour runs 4.5 to 5 hours. From the time you board to the time you return, your basics are covered.

## What to Wear on a Na Pali Coast Tour

Wear your swimsuit or beachwear under your clothes. You will be in the water for about one hour at the snorkel stop, so plan on getting wet. A hat matters more than most guests realize — sun exposure on the water is direct and sustained across the full tour.

Bring a light layer for the boat ride. The catamaran moves fast, and the wind off the ocean is cooler than the air on shore, especially in the morning. A lightweight shirt or a thin cover-up works.

Wear shoes or sandals you are comfortable walking in at the harbor, even if you plan to go barefoot on the boat. Kikiaola Small Boat Harbor has uneven surfaces near the dock.

For sunscreen: reef-safe formulas are the considerate choice near coral. The snorkel stop puts you directly above reef habitat, and non-reef-safe sunscreen washes off into the water. We recommend switching to a reef-safe formula before your tour.

## What to Bring

Bring a towel. We provide freshwater showers on our larger boats, but we do not supply towels. A quick-dry travel towel packs flat and works well.

Bring extra sunscreen. What you apply at the harbor will wear off during the tour. Reef-safe sunscreen is preferred near the coral.

A hat and sunglasses protect you on the water. UV exposure reflects off the ocean surface and hits harder than it feels on land.

If you want photos, bring a waterproof phone case or a waterproof camera. Phones are the most common item lost overboard. A case with a lanyard or float attachment gives you peace of mind.

If you are prone to seasickness, consult your doctor before the tour and take any medication as directed before you board. Do not wait until you are already on the water.

You are welcome to bring your own snorkel mask if you have a preferred fit. We provide gear for every guest, but if you use prescription lenses or have a mask that fits better, bring it along.

Light personal snacks or an extra drink are fine if you want something not on the lunch spread. Space on the boat is shared among all guests, so keep any bag small.

---

## What to Leave Behind

Leave valuables at your hotel. Phones and cameras can be secured in a waterproof case, but jewelry, watches, and anything you cannot afford to lose or get wet should stay ashore. The boat is active on the water and spray comes aboard regularly.

Do not bring bulky luggage or large bags. Storage space on the catamaran is shared. A small dry bag or a backpack is enough for everything on this list.

---

## A Note on Seasickness

Most guests on a Na Pali coast snorkel tour do not have issues. Our catamarans are built specifically for these waters. The route does include open ocean, and conditions vary by day and season.

If you have a history of motion sickness on boats, consult a doctor before your tour. Over-the-counter options work best when taken before boarding, not after symptoms start. If your doctor prescribes a patch, those take time to reach full effect, so plan ahead.
