# How Far in Advance Should You Book a Na Pali Coast Tour in Summer?

Book your Na Pali Coast boat tour before you arrive on Kauai. Summer (June through August) is the busiest stretch of the year on the water, and tours sell out. The earlier you lock in a date, the more options you have for timing, boat size, and group type.

---

## Why Summer Is Peak Season for Na Pali Coast Tours

The swell that rolls in during winter settles significantly from June through August, which means fewer cancelled tours and more consistent access to the coast. Calmer water opens up the sea caves. Pirates Cave (Waiahuakua), Rainbow Falls, and the Honopu Archway become accessible as long as ocean conditions allow. Snorkeling at locations like Nualolo Kai and Tunnels runs more reliably when the sea is settled. Spinner dolphins, Pacific Bottlenose dolphins, and honu (Hawaiian green sea turtle) are commonly spotted along the route.

The demand side follows. Families travel between school years, and West Coast visitors make the flight. More people on the island, more stable days on the water, and a finite number of boats means spots fill early. We run a fleet that carries between 12 and 32 guests per tour, smaller than many operators on the island. Fewer spots per departure is the tradeoff for a smaller group experience.

## What Happens If Your Tour Is Cancelled

If your tour is cancelled due to weather or ocean conditions, you can reschedule (subject to availability), receive a credit, or get a full refund. You are not charged for a tour that doesn't run. That matters especially if you're booking close to your departure date, since Kauai weather can shift quickly even in summer.

We monitor conditions closely before each departure. When we call a cancellation, it's because the coast isn't safe or accessible. The same conditions that close the tour would prevent sea cave entry and make snorkeling difficult anyway. For questions about the cancellation policy on your specific booking, call (808) 338-9980.
