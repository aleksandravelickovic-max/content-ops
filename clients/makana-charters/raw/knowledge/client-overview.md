## Brand Name Meaning

"Makana" means Gift in Hawaiian. The name refers to the gift of place and history passed down through the family for over 200 years — the Na Pali Coast, the waters, the stories.

---

## Family & Heritage

- Family's documented journey began along the Waimea River at their current office location ("Hale Pili")
- Family is documented as the first to show the Na Pali Coast to visitors — over 200 years ago
- The Na Pali Coast connection spans many generations; crew members go to the coast on their own time
- "5th great grandparents" cited as the beginning of the family's legacy here
- Royal Hawaiian lineage referenced (governess-level ancestry)

## Tours Offered

### Standard Na Pali Coast Tours
- Duration: 4.5–5 hours
- Departure: Kikiaola Small Boat Harbor, Kekaha
- Includes: snorkel gear + flotation devices, deli-style lunch (turkey, ham, roast beef, vegetarian), soft drinks, local juices, fruits + snacks
- Snorkel time: ~1 hour
- Alcohol: not served; guests may bring their own (only after snorkeling)

### Private Na Pali Coast Charters
- Same experience, exclusive boat
- Makana boat (up to 12): purchase one standard ticket
- Na Pali Kai III: call for groups over 12
- Customizable itinerary

### Sunset Cruises
- Afternoon/evening tours; golden-hour coastal views
- Separate offering from morning tours

## Route & Highlights

Departs Kikiaola Harbor → Polihale shoreline → Kalalau Valley waterfalls → Na Pali Coast sea caves → snorkel stop

**Named sea caves:**
- Pirates Cave (Waiahuakua)
- Rainbow Falls
- Honopu Archway — filming location for King Kong, Six Days Seven Nights, James Bond, Pirates of the Caribbean

**Wildlife commonly seen:**
- Spinner dolphins
- Pacific Bottlenose dolphins
- Hawaiian green sea turtles (honu)
- Dozens of fish species, coral reefs

**Snorkel locations:** Makole, Ke'e Beach (Tunnels), Nualolo Kai, Kekaha, Kaumakani

## Competitive Advantages

1. Native Hawaiian family ownership — documented 200-year history on Na Pali Coast
2. Closest departure point (Kikiaola Harbor) — up to 1 hour less transit time vs. Port Allen competitors
3. Smaller group sizes — 12–32 passengers vs. competitors' larger vessels
4. Sea cave access — boats built to enter caves safely
5. New fast power catamarans
6. All crew are long-time Kauai locals or lifelong islanders
7. Cultural narration: Hawaiian history, marine biology, local knowledge built over a lifetime
8. Private charter availability
