# Makana Charters Service Taxonomy

Source: STYLE-SYSTEM.md, refreshed website intelligence, and local client research.

## Tours and Charters

- Na Pali Coast Boat Tours: Standard Kauai boat tours along the Na Pali Coast with snorkeling, lunch, cultural narration, and sea cave viewing when conditions allow.
- Private Charters: Private boat experiences for groups that want a customized pace, boat, and itinerary within safety and ocean-condition limits.
- Na Pali Snorkeling Tours: Tours with snorkel gear, flotation devices, and snorkel stops selected by ocean conditions.
- Na Pali Sea Cave Tours: Boat tours that may visit sea caves such as Pirates Cave, Rainbow Falls, and Honopu Archway when conditions allow.
- Wildlife Viewing: Tour experience category for dolphins, Hawaiian green sea turtles, reef fish, and other wildlife commonly seen on route.
