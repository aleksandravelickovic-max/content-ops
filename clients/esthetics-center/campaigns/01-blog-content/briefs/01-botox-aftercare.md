## SERP Analysis

**Search intent:** Informational — post-treatment or pre-treatment patients want to know exactly what behaviors to avoid and *why* the rules exist, not just a list of don'ts.
**Dominant format:** Numbered listicle (7–10 mistakes) with explanatory paragraphs per item, often capped with FAQ. This post should follow that format but go deeper on the science behind each rule.

### What competitors cover
- No touching / rubbing treated areas (24 hrs)
- Lying down restriction (4 hrs)
- Avoiding exercise (24 hrs)
- Alcohol and blood-thinning supplements
- Heat and sun exposure (saunas, hot yoga)
- Makeup timing
- Skincare ingredient adjustments (retinoids, AHAs)
- Results timeline (3–5 days onset, 10–14 days full effect)
- Bruising management

### Content gaps we exploit (our differentiators)
- **Complication response protocol** — competitors skip it entirely; what to watch for hour by hour and who to call
- **Skincare reintroduction order** — competitors say "avoid retinoids" but never say when and how to bring them back
- **The facial exercise paradox** — gentle facial movement is encouraged while body exercise is restricted; almost no competitor explains this nuance
- **OTC medication specifics** — name the culprits (ibuprofen, aspirin, vitamin E, omega-3s) with concrete pre/post windows
- **First-timer vs. repeat patient** differences in what rules matter most
