**Search intent:** Informational with transactional secondary — readers are researching the procedure before consulting; they want to know how it works, what's realistic, which products are used, and whether they qualify.
**Dominant format:** Long-form educational guide with FAQ section. Hybrid listicle-plus-guide also performs well. Clinical depth tends to rank for this keyword.

### What competitors cover
- What jawline filler is and how it works
- Anatomy of the mandible and how filler interacts with bone structure
- Filler product comparison (HA fillers vs. collagen stimulators like Radiesse)

- Reversibility with hyaluronidase

### Content gaps we exploit (our differentiators)
- **Candidacy and disqualifiers** — who should NOT get jawline filler (prior implants, significant bone loss, severe laxity, contraindications) is almost absent from competitor content
- **Face shape guidance beyond gender** — competitors address "men vs. women" but not oval vs. round vs. square vs. heart-shaped face approach; we can own this
- **Surgical vs. filler realistic comparison** — what filler can and cannot do vs. a jawline implant or genioplasty; competitors avoid this comparison
- **First 72 hours post-treatment** — swelling pattern, firmness, positional changes for this specific area; not covered by competitors
- **Combination sequencing** — why a provider might add masseter Botox in the same visit; what each modality contributes and why
- **Vascular occlusion awareness** — serious adverse events near the mandible are almost entirely absent from consumer-facing content; covering this transparently builds trust

## Outline

### Opening hook direction
Address the reader who sees a less defined jaw in photos, feels the lower face has softened, or notices early jowling. Make them feel seen without being clinical or harsh. Transition: there's a non-surgical approach that can restore structure.

### H2 Structure

**H2 1: What Jawline Contouring with Filler Actually Does**
- Micro-queries: "what does jawline filler do," "can filler really define your jawline"
- Clarify the goal: adding structure and projection along the mandibular border, softening the pre-jowl sulcus, improving the chin-to-jaw ratio. Not weight loss, not facial slimming — structural definition.

**H2 2: Who Is a Good Candidate — and Who Isn't**
*(→ Dr. Rice trust signal #1 placement here)*
- Micro-queries: "am i a good candidate for jawline filler," "who should not get jawline filler"
- Strong candidates: mild to moderate laxity, defined bone structure, good skin quality, realistic goals. Disqualifiers: active skin infections, pregnancy, history of severe allergic reactions, significant pre-existing laxity (surgical territory), prior jawline implants.
- Dr. Rice credential: double board-certified facial plastic surgeon — candidate evaluation is a surgical-level clinical assessment.

**H2 3: Your Face Shape Matters — How We Customize the Approach**
- Micro-queries: "jawline filler for round face," "best jawline filler approach for my face shape"
- Brief breakdown by shape: round (add lateral projection), oval (minor definition along border), square (soften angles while defining border), heart/diamond (add lower-third fullness). Competitors miss this entirely.

**H2 4: Jawline Filler for Women vs. Men — Different Goals, Different Results**
- Micro-queries: "jawline filler for women natural look," "how much filler for male jaw contouring"
- Women: softer, elongated definition with smooth border. Men: sharper mandibular angle projection, broader ramus definition. Product choice and placement differs.

**H2 5: Which Filler Products We Use and Why**
*(→ Dr. Rice trust signal #2 placement here)*
- Micro-queries: "what filler is best for jawline," "is Volux or Radiesse better for jaw"
- High-G-prime HA fillers (Juvederm Volux, Voluma XC, Restylane Lyft): structural support, reversible. Collagen stimulators (Radiesse): longer-lasting biostimulatory effect, not reversible. Product choice depends on anatomy, goals, and patient history. 25+ years, 12,000+ patients framing.

**H2 6: Combining Jawline Filler with Other Treatments**
- Micro-queries: "jawline filler and masseter botox same visit," "can you combine jawline filler with PDO threads"
- Masseter Botox in the same session: addresses muscle-related fullness while filler adds bony definition — they work on different structures. Timing of RF or thread combinations: usually staged, not same session. Brief sequencing logic.

**H2 7: Your Results — Timeline, Longevity, and What the First Week Looks Like**
- Micro-queries: "how long does jawline filler last," "jawline filler swelling how long"
- Day 1–3: swelling, firmness, possible asymmetry (normal). Days 4–7: settling begins. Week 2: first accurate look at results. Longevity: 12–18 months for HA fillers; up to 24 months for Radiesse. Touch-up strategy.

**H2 8: Risks, What We Watch For, and How to Report Concerns**
*(→ Dr. Rice trust signal #3 / CTA placement)*
- Micro-queries: "is jawline filler safe," "jawline filler complications what to watch"
- Normal: swelling, firmness, minor asymmetry for up to 2 weeks. Watch for: blanching (vascular compression), persistent hard lumps, asymmetry worsening past 2 weeks. Hyaluronidase available for HA reversal.
