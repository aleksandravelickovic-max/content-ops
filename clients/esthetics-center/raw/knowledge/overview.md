## Company Overview

Esthetics Center is a medical aesthetics practice offering plastic surgery, medical spa treatments, and advanced skincare services. The clinics focus on helping patients improve their appearance through both surgical and non-surgical procedures.

Services include facial plastic surgery, injectable treatments, skin rejuvenation procedures, and professional skincare treatments. Treatments are performed by licensed medical professionals under the supervision of a double board-certified facial plastic surgeon.

Locations: Sacramento, Rocklin, and El Dorado Hills (Northern California).

## Primary Audience

Most patients come from the Sacramento region and surrounding Northern California communities.

Typical patients:
- Adults considering cosmetic surgery
- Patients seeking non-surgical facial treatments
- Individuals interested in skin rejuvenation and anti-aging procedures
- Clients looking for medically supervised aesthetic care

Many patients are focused on improving confidence while maintaining natural-looking results.

## Core Problems the Clinic Solves

- Safe cosmetic procedures performed by qualified medical professionals
- Access to both surgical and non-surgical treatment options
- Advanced technology and modern aesthetic techniques
- Guidance from experienced practitioners when choosing treatments
- Natural-looking improvements rather than dramatic changes
