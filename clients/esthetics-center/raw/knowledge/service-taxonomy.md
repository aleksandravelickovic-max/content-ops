# Esthetics Center Service Taxonomy

Source: STYLE-SYSTEM.md, refreshed website intelligence, and local client research.

## Services

- Facial Plastic Surgery: Surgical facial aesthetics services performed by qualified clinical providers.
- Injectable Treatments: Botox, filler, and related injectable treatments for wrinkle softening, facial balancing, and volume restoration.
- Medical Spa Services: Non-surgical aesthetic services delivered in a medical spa setting.
- Professional Skincare Treatments: Clinical skincare treatments for texture, tone, maintenance, and skin quality.
- Skin Rejuvenation Procedures: Procedures focused on improving skin texture, tone, collagen response, and visible aging concerns.
- Laser and Energy-Based Treatments: Device-based treatments for resurfacing, pigmentation, redness, hair reduction, or skin quality, where confirmed per page.
