# Contact block — Esthetics Center

The closing CTA must appear in every blog post (STYLE-SYSTEM.md §5.4). The compliance gate checks for both the phone number and contact URL.

## Canonical values

- Phone: **(916) 941-9400**
- Contact form: **https://www.estheticscenter.com/contact**

## Where it must appear

1. The closing CTA of every blog post.
2. Any page section where a consultation is being offered.

Both the phone and contact URL must appear together — not split across sections.

## Approved CTA phrasing (blog posts)

Soft CTA — helpful, not pushy:
> Ready to learn more? Schedule a free consultation with our team. Call us at (916) 941-9400 or visit estheticscenter.com/contact to book a personalized appointment.

With Dr. Rice trust signal (for CTA placement):
> Dr. Reginald Rice and our team are here to help. With over 25 years of experience and more than 12,000 patients treated, we're ready to build a plan around your goals. Call (916) 941-9400 or contact us at estheticscenter.com/contact.

## Rules

- Never substitute a generic "contact us" without the actual phone and URL.
- Tone: helpful and guidance-focused — not a hard sell.
- The CTA is one of the three required Dr. Rice trust signal placements in blog posts.
