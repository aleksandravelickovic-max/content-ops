### 3.3 Banned phrases (throat-clearing, hedges, closers)

Delete entirely:
- "It's important to note that..." / "It's worth mentioning that..."
- "In today's [X] landscape..."
- "Let's dive/delve into..."
- "In this article, we will..."
- "To be fair..." / "To be honest..."
- "At the end of the day..."
- "When it comes to..."
- "In terms of..."
- "In conclusion..." / "To sum up..." / "To wrap up..."
- "Feel free to reach out" / "Don't hesitate to..."
- "Moving forward" / "Going forward"
- "Due to the fact that" → because
- "In order to" → to
- "Has the ability to" → can
- "With that in mind..." / "Building on this foundation..." / "That being said..."
- "Imagine a world where..." / "Picture this:" / "Have you ever wondered..."
- "Here are X things you need to know about Y"

### 3.4 Banned structures

**The "Not just X — it's Y" pattern:** Never frame something as more than it is by negating a lesser version.
- Bad: "This isn't just a medspa — it's a complete aesthetic experience."
- Good: Say what it actually is.

**Rule of three:** AI defaults to grouping in threes. Vary it — use one, two, or four. Match the number to what you actually have to say.

**Synonym stacking:** Do not list near-synonyms as padding.
- Bad: "a comprehensive, sophisticated, and robust team"
- Good: "a skilled team" (or describe what makes them skilled)

### 3.5 Tone and rhythm tells

- **Uniform sentence length:** Mix short and long. Let some run. Cut others hard.
- **Participial phrases:** "Leveraging advanced techniques, the team..." is an AI fingerprint. Restructure: "The team used better techniques" or just state the result.
- **"Ensuring" clauses:** "...ensuring accuracy and safety." These trailing clauses are usually filler. If the claim is important, give it its own sentence with evidence.

## 7. SEO Standards

**Before writing any piece:** Run a real SERP analysis. Do not simulate it. Steps:
1. Access the internet and search the primary keyword (language: English, location: United States)
2. Analyze top 5 ranking pages for: search intent, LSI terms, content format patterns, subtopics, micro-queries, content gaps
3. Identify which gaps deserve full sections vs. paragraphs
4. Present findings before proceeding

**Meta deliverables (provide before writing the introduction):**
- 3 meta title options: 55–60 characters, include primary keyword, stimulate curiosity
- 3 meta description options: 130–150 characters, include primary keyword, CTR-driven, state a benefit, highlight a unique angle
- 3 H1 title options: SEO-oriented, CTR-driven

**Outline requirements:**
- Build only after SERP analysis
- Cover all critical competitor topics; expand beyond their depth
- Mark where content matches competitor coverage vs. where it expands
- Include 2–3 micro-queries under each H2 (internal structure only — not visible in output)
- Each paragraph answers one micro-query
- 1 table per article where appropriate; 2–3 lists per article
