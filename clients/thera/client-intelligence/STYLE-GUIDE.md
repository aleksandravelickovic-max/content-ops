## 1. Brand & Audience

Thera Physical and Occupational Therapy is a specialized rehabilitation clinic in Midtown Manhattan. The practice focuses on post-surgical recovery after breast surgery, cancer treatment, and procedures affecting lymphatic function and mobility.

Services: one-on-one, in-person therapy sessions. Physical and occupational therapists work directly with each patient. Treatment plans are tailored to restore mobility, reduce swelling, and improve quality of life during recovery.

Patients travel from across the Tri-State Area.

**Audience:** Breast cancer survivors and individuals recovering from breast surgery. This includes people who have undergone mastectomy, lumpectomy, or breast reconstruction; those managing post-surgical complications (swelling, pain, restricted movement); people searching for specialized rehabilitation after cancer treatment; and patients who found insufficient support through general physical therapy.

**Content must address someone actively navigating recovery** — not someone researching treatment academically. These are patients dealing with real physical challenges and looking for guidance on their options.

## 2. Voice & Tone

**Register:** Empathetic, expert, and patient-centered. Supportive and informative without being clinical, cold, or distant.

The audience is facing significant health challenges. Content must honor that reality. It should not minimize what patients are going through, oversell outcomes, or sound like a general healthcare marketing piece.

| Goal | Not this | Use this |
|---|---|---|
| Introduce a service | "We offer cutting-edge lymphedema therapy" | "Thera specializes in lymphedema therapy for people recovering from breast surgery or cancer treatment" |
| Acknowledge difficulty | "Recovery is a journey" | Describe the specific challenges (restricted movement, persistent swelling) that bring patients here |
| Establish expertise | "Our experienced team" | "Our therapists hold advanced certifications in lymphedema therapy and post-surgical recovery" |
| Frame outcomes | "We'll help you feel like yourself again" | "Treatment programs are designed to restore mobility, reduce swelling, and improve daily movement" |

## 3. Terminology

### 3.1 Required terms

- **Physical and occupational therapists** — the correct descriptor for the Thera team; do not use "specialist" alone without specifying the credential
- **Lymphedema therapy** — the specific therapy for lymphatic complications
- **Post-surgical recovery** — preferred phrase for the recovery context
- **One-on-one therapy** / **individualized therapy** — key differentiator; maintain in all content
- **Breast rehabilitation** — Thera's specialty focus area
- **Certified lymphedema therapist** (if confirmed by CSM) — use credential accurately

### 3.2 Strict avoidance list

| Term | Reason |
|---|---|
| "cure" / "cured" | Cannot claim curative outcomes |
| "guaranteed results" | Cannot guarantee rehabilitation outcomes |
| "eliminate" (for a condition) | Therapy reduces or improves — not eliminates |
| Generic "physical therapy" (as a synonym for Thera) | Thera does specialized, not general, PT |
| "cutting-edge" / "innovative" / "revolutionary" | Vague promotional language |
| Clinical coldness (jargon-heavy descriptions without patient-centered framing) | Contradicts the empathetic register |
| Minimizing patient experience ("It's a simple recovery") | Disrespects the audience's actual situation |
| "journey" | Overused, vague — describe the actual process |

## 4. Accuracy & Technical Standards

- Thera is located in Midtown Manhattan — do not claim other locations without confirmation.
- The practice provides physical and occupational therapy. Do not claim other modalities (e.g., massage therapy, chiropractic) without confirmation.
- Lymphedema therapy requires specific certification (CLT — Certified Lymphedema Therapist). Do not claim certifications without CSM confirmation of which practitioners hold them.
- The audience includes patients who have undergone mastectomy, lumpectomy, and breast reconstruction. These are distinct procedures with distinct recovery profiles — do not conflate them.
- Do not invent specific treatment protocols, session structures, or recovery timelines.
- "Tri-State Area" is the geographic reference for where patients travel from. Do not expand beyond this without confirmation.

---

## 5. Specificity Rules

- Name specific conditions and their effects rather than using vague terms. "Limited shoulder mobility after mastectomy" is more useful than "post-surgical limitations."
- When describing what Thera treats, be specific: lymphedema, scar tissue restrictions, soft tissue restrictions, limited range of motion, post-surgical swelling. Use the accurate clinical terms where they serve clarity.
- Attribute outcomes to individualized care — not general statements about the practice.
- When writing about what makes Thera different from general physical therapy: describe the specific gap that general PT doesn't fill (lymphatic complications, post-mastectomy mobility, specialized cert levels).
