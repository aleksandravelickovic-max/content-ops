## Company Overview

Thera Physical and Occupational Therapy is a specialized rehabilitation clinic located in Midtown Manhattan. The practice focuses on helping patients recover from breast surgery, cancer treatment, and other procedures that affect lymphatic function and mobility.

The clinic provides one-on-one, in-person therapy sessions designed to support recovery through individualized care. Physical and occupational therapists work directly with each patient to address specific functional challenges and discomfort related to surgery or medical treatment.

Patients travel from across the Tri-State Area to receive care at Thera, where treatment plans are tailored to restore mobility, reduce swelling, and improve quality of life during recovery.

## Primary Audience

Most patients are breast cancer survivors or individuals recovering from breast surgery.

Common situations:
- Patients who have undergone mastectomy, lumpectomy, or breast reconstruction
- Individuals managing post-surgical complications such as swelling, pain, or restricted movement
- People searching for specialized rehabilitation after cancer treatment
- Patients who have been unable to find answers through general physical therapy providers

Content should address the concerns of someone actively navigating recovery and looking for guidance on what options exist.

## Core Problems Thera Solves

Many patients experience long-term physical challenges after surgery or cancer treatment that are not always addressed during standard medical care.

Common issues:
- Limited shoulder or arm mobility after breast surgery
- Persistent swelling related to lymphedema
- Scar tissue and soft tissue restrictions
- Lymphatic complications following cosmetic or medical procedures
- Pain or discomfort affecting daily movement

Thera provides rehabilitation programs designed to restore movement, reduce discomfort, and support long-term recovery.

## Brand Positioning

Thera presents itself as a clinic focused on specialized rehabilitation that many traditional therapy providers do not offer.

Key differentiators:
- Dedicated focus on breast rehabilitation and lymphatic care
- Individualized therapy sessions rather than generalized treatment programs
- Advanced certifications in lymphedema therapy and post-surgical recovery
- A practice centered on specialized care for underserved rehabilitation needs

## Brand Voice

Empathy, expertise, and patient-centered care. Supportive and informative without sounding overly clinical or distant.

## Messaging Themes

**Specialized care:** Many rehabilitation needs following surgery or cancer treatment require therapists with specific training and experience.
**Individualized treatment:** Each patient receives focused one-on-one care designed around their recovery goals.
**Comprehensive rehabilitation:** Treatment programs may address mobility, swelling, lymphatic health, and scar tissue management.
**Support throughout recovery:** Patients often receive care before surgery, during treatment, and throughout the recovery process.
