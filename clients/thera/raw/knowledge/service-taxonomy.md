# Thera Service Taxonomy

Source: STYLE-SYSTEM.md, refreshed website intelligence, and local client research.

## Conditions and Services

- Lymphedema: Therapy support for lymphedema management and related swelling concerns.
- Post-Mastectomy Recovery: Therapy support after mastectomy, lumpectomy, or reconstruction.
- Post-Surgical Swelling: Treatment support for swelling and restricted movement after surgery.
- Scar Tissue Restrictions: Therapy for scar tissue and soft tissue restrictions affecting movement or comfort.
- Limited Shoulder and Arm Mobility: Support for shoulder and arm mobility limitations after breast surgery.
- Lymphatic Complications: Treatment support for lymphatic complications after cosmetic or medical procedures.
- Pain Affecting Daily Movement: Therapy support for pain that limits daily movement.
