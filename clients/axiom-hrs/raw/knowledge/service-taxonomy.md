# Axiom HRS Service Taxonomy

Source: STYLE-SYSTEM.md, refreshed website intelligence, and local client research.

## Services

- Payroll Processing: Payroll administration support for wage calculation, deductions, tax handling, and recurring payroll workflows.
- Benefits Enrollment and Administration: Benefits setup, enrollment, employee changes, and plan administration support.
- HR Outsourcing and Hands-On Support: External HR support for employers that need operational help without building a full internal HR team.
- HR Technology: UKG-powered HR technology for employee data, workflows, reporting, and workforce administration.
- Time and Labor Management: Time tracking, attendance, scheduling, and labor management support.
- Compliance Support: Support for tax, labor law, policy, and employment compliance workflows.
- Employee Lifecycle Management: HR support from onboarding through changes, reviews, documentation, and offboarding.
- Learning Management: Training and learning administration for employee development and compliance needs.
