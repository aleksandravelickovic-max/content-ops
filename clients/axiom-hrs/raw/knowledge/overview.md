# Axiom HRS — Client Overview

Source: client brief provided May 2026

---

## Company Overview

Axiom HRS provides human capital management (HCM) solutions that combine payroll, HR technology, and hands-on support. The company helps organizations manage payroll, workforce data, compliance requirements, and employee programs through a single integrated platform.

Axiom partners with UKG's HCM software while delivering personalized HR outsourcing services and operational support. This combination allows businesses to automate administrative tasks while still receiving guidance from experienced HR professionals.

The platform supports the full employee lifecycle, from recruitment and onboarding through long-term workforce management.

**Important positioning note:** Axiom HRS is a single-database, rip-and-replace solution — not an integration layer or connector. Never position it as something that works with or connects to existing HR/payroll systems.

## Primary Audience

Business owners, HR leaders, and operations managers responsible for workforce management.

Typical clients:
- Small and mid-sized businesses managing growing teams
- HR departments needing payroll and compliance support
- Business owners seeking outsourced HR services
- Organizations looking to modernize workforce management systems

Many of these companies want to simplify HR operations while maintaining compliance and improving employee experience.

## Core Problems the Company Solves

- Processing payroll accurately while meeting tax requirements
- Tracking employee hours and attendance across teams
- Managing benefits enrollment and administration
- Maintaining compliance with changing labor laws and tax regulations
- Collecting workforce data needed for reporting and decision-making

## Brand Positioning

- An all-in-one payroll and HR solution
- A provider combining technology with hands-on HR expertise
- A partner helping companies manage the entire employee lifecycle
- A workforce management solution built around people as well as processes

## Brand Voice

Expertise while remaining approachable. Knowledgeable, helpful, and people-focused.

## Messaging Themes

**People-centered HR:** Workforce systems should support both employees and employers.
**Simplified workforce management:** Technology and support help reduce administrative workload.
**Compliance support:** Businesses benefit from guidance when navigating tax, payroll, and labor regulations.
**Employee lifecycle management:** Solutions address the full employment journey — from hiring through long-term development.

## Client Feedback (Historical)

- Replace "Integrated" (e.g., "Integrated HR and Payroll platform") with "Unified" or "Single-Database" to better reflect their positioning
- Avoid messaging that suggests Axiom connects with existing HR/payroll systems — position it as a full replacement (rip-and-replace), not an integration with current tools
- Make sure the overall messaging reinforces that Axiom is a single-database solution, not a system built on integrations
