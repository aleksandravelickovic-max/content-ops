## 1. Brand & Audience

Axiom HRS provides human capital management (HCM) solutions combining payroll processing, HR technology, and hands-on operational support through a single platform. Axiom partners with UKG's HCM software.

**Positioning:** Axiom HRS is a single-database, rip-and-replace solution. It replaces existing payroll and HR systems — it does not connect to, integrate with, or sit alongside them. This is a non-negotiable framing requirement.

**Audience:** Business owners, HR leaders, and operations managers at small to mid-sized businesses. Typical pain points: manual payroll processes, multiple disconnected HR systems, compliance risk, administrative overhead.

---

## 2. Voice & Tone

**Register:** Knowledgeable, helpful, and people-focused. Approachable without being casual.

Content should feel like guidance from an experienced HR professional, not a software vendor. Avoid corporate buzzwords and SaaS-speak. Axiom is about people and operations, not just technology.

| Goal | Not this | Use this |
|---|---|---|
| Describe the platform | "Integrated HR and Payroll platform" | "Unified HR and Payroll platform" or "single-database HR solution" |
| Describe what it replaces | "Works with your existing systems" | "Replaces your current payroll and HR systems with one platform" |
| Frame the UKG relationship | "Connects your HR tools" | "Runs on UKG's HCM technology with Axiom's hands-on support" |
| Communicate simplification | "Streamlines workflows" | "Processes payroll, tracks time, and manages benefits in one place" |

## 3. Terminology

### 3.1 Required terms

- **Unified** — use instead of "integrated" when describing the platform
- **Single-database** — use to reinforce platform architecture when relevant
- **Human capital management (HCM)** — define at first use; use HCM after
- **UKG** — reference as the HCM software partner (not "a third-party tool")
- **Rip-and-replace** — internal framing; useful context for content decisions but not a phrase for customer-facing copy
- **Employee lifecycle** — the scope of what Axiom manages (hiring → long-term workforce management)
- **HR outsourcing** — when describing the service model

## 4. Accuracy & Technical Standards

- Do not claim specific compliance certifications without source confirmation.
- UKG is the software; Axiom HRS provides the support, expertise, and outsourcing layer. Keep the distinction clear.
- Do not invent specific feature names, pricing tiers, or integration specifics.
- "Hands-on support" is a core differentiator — it is what separates Axiom from a pure SaaS offering. Maintain this distinction.
- Employee lifecycle scope: recruitment, onboarding, payroll, benefits, time tracking, compliance, long-term workforce management. Do not claim capabilities outside this scope without confirmation.

---

## 5. Specificity Rules

- Describe what the platform actually does, not how it makes people feel. "Processes multi-state payroll and calculates tax withholdings" is better than "simplifies complex payroll."
- When writing about compliance: name the type of compliance (payroll tax, labor law, benefits administration) rather than generic "compliance."
- When contrasting Axiom with typical HR challenges: describe the actual pain (managing payroll in multiple systems, running manual reconciliations) rather than vague "inefficiencies."
