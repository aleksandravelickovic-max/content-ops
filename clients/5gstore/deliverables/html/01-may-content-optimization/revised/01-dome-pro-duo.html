Peplink Dome Pro Duo | Outdoor Dual 5G Router, IP67 (APAC / EMEA)

The Peplink Dome Pro Duo is an outdoor dual 5G router built for mobile and fixed deployments that require high throughput, true hardware redundancy, and full-weather durability across APAC and EMEA networks. Two independent 5G modems with redundant SIM slots, an IP67-rated enclosure, Wi-Fi 6, integrated GPS, and an operating range of -40°F to 149°F (-40°C to 65°C) make it a capable fit for railway rolling stock, maritime vessels, fleet vehicles, and permanent outdoor enterprise installations.

With 5G downlink speeds up to 3.4 Gbps, SpeedFusion bonding across 2 PepVPN peers, and Wi-Fi 6 supporting both access point and Wi-Fi WAN operation, this IP67 5G cellular router consolidates connectivity, redundancy, and cloud management into a single dome-form-factor unit powered by a single PoE cable.

What the Peplink Dome Pro Duo Delivers

Dual Independent 5G Modems with Redundant SIM Support

Two fully independent 5G modems run simultaneously, each with its own redundant SIM slot configuration. Running two separate modems rather than a single modem with dual SIM means both carrier connections stay active at the same time, enabling true load balancing and active failover without any switching delay between links.

Each modem supports Nano-SIM (4FF), Peplink eSIM with 2 profiles per modem, and RemoteSIM via SIM Injector or FusionSIM. This SIM flexibility supports centralized SIM management for large deployments without requiring physical access to each unit.

5G downlink peaks at 3.4 Gbps with 900 Mbps uplink. CAT-20 LTE fallback provides 1.6 Gbps downlink and 200 Mbps uplink when 5G coverage is unavailable.

IP67 Enclosure with Railway and Industrial Certifications

The rugged outdoor IP67 enclosure is fully dust-sealed and water-resistant. The operating temperature range of -40°F to 149°F (-40°C to 65°C) covers arctic winters and high-heat industrial environments equally. Humidity tolerance runs 15% to 95% non-condensing.

Certifications include CE, RoHS, EN 50124-1 (Railway Application, Insulation Coordination), EN 50155 (Railway Application, Rolling Stock, Electronic Equipment), and EN 61000 (Electromagnetic Compatibility). The EN 50155 certification makes the Dome Pro Duo one of the few commercial 5G routers specifically validated for deployment on railway rolling stock.

SpeedFusion Bonding and VPN

The Dome Pro Duo supports SpeedFusion bonding across 2 PepVPN peers out of the box. SpeedFusion combines both 5G modem connections into a single aggregated tunnel, increasing total throughput and maintaining session continuity even when one modem loses signal. Hot Failover keeps active sessions alive through a link failure without re-establishing them from scratch.

Pairing the Dome Pro Duo with a Peplink Balance SDX Pro at a hub or datacenter location allows the SpeedFusion tunnel to terminate on enterprise-grade hardware at the core of the network. A Virtual WAN Activation License can extend peer capacity on compatible Peplink devices.

Wi-Fi 6 with AP and Wi-Fi WAN Support

Wi-Fi 6 radios operate simultaneously on 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz with 2x2 MIMO, supporting up to 150 recommended concurrent users. The Wi-Fi interface operates in AP mode for client connectivity or Wi-Fi WAN mode to pull in a nearby Wi-Fi network as an additional WAN source, giving the Dome Pro Duo a third connectivity path alongside its two cellular modems.

8x8 internal cellular antennas and 2x2 internal Wi-Fi antennas are fully integrated into the enclosure, eliminating external cable runs that are common failure points in outdoor deployments.

Integrated GPS

A dedicated internal GPS antenna provides real-time location tracking for fleet management, asset tracking, and geofencing applications. GPS data is accessible through InControl 2, Peplink's cloud management platform, alongside device status, cellular signal metrics, and remote configuration tools.

PoE-Powered with PoE Output

The Dome Pro Duo accepts power from an 802.3at PoE+ source (without PoE output) or an 802.3bt PoE++ source (with PoE output enabled). A single Ethernet cable carries both power and data to the unit, keeping installation cabling minimal. The 802.3at PoE+ output port allows the Dome Pro Duo to power a downstream device such as an IP camera or access point directly, without a separate PoE injector at the installation point.

Maximum power consumption is 24W without PoE output active.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Peplink Dome Pro Duo certified for US carriers?

No. The Dome Pro Duo (DOM-PRO-DUO-5GN-PRM) is designed for APAC and EMEA carrier networks and is not certified for use with US carriers, including AT&T, T-Mobile, or Verizon. Buyers in North America looking for a US-certified dual-modem outdoor Peplink router should consider the Pepwave MAX HD2 Dome IP67 instead.

What makes the Dome Pro Duo different from a single-modem outdoor router?

The Dome Pro Duo runs two fully independent 5G modems simultaneously. Each modem maintains its own carrier connection at the same time, allowing true load balancing across both links and active failover with no switching delay if one modem loses signal. A single-modem router with dual SIM can only connect to one carrier at a time, switching to the backup SIM only after the primary connection fails.

What SIM options does the Dome Pro Duo support?

Each modem supports three SIM input methods: a physical Nano-SIM (4FF) slot, Peplink eSIM with 2 profiles per modem, and RemoteSIM via a SIM Injector or FusionSIM. RemoteSIM allows centralized SIM management from a remote location, which is particularly useful for large fleet or railway deployments where physical SIM access is impractical.

What is the EN 50155 certification, and why does it matter?

EN 50155 is a European standard for electronic equipment used on railway rolling stock. It specifies requirements for power supply variations, temperature cycling, humidity, vibration, and shock that equipment must withstand in a rail environment. The Dome Pro Duo's EN 50155 certification means it has been tested and validated for permanent installation on trains and railcars, not just general outdoor use.

How does SpeedFusion work on the Dome Pro Duo?

SpeedFusion bonds both modem connections into a single aggregated tunnel between the Dome Pro Duo and a SpeedFusion-capable hub device such as the Peplink Balance SDX Pro. Traffic is distributed across both modems at the packet level, increasing total throughput and maintaining active sessions through individual link failures. The Dome Pro Duo supports 2 PepVPN peers by default.

Can the Dome Pro Duo power other devices via PoE?

Yes. When powered by an 802.3bt PoE++ source, the Dome Pro Duo's 802.3at PoE+ output port can power a downstream device such as an IP camera or Peplink access point directly. This eliminates the need for a separate PoE injector at the installation point and reduces cabling complexity in outdoor deployments.
