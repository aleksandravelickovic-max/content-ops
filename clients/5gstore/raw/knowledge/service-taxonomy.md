# 5Gstore Service Taxonomy

Source: STYLE-SYSTEM.md, refreshed website intelligence, and local client research.

## Product Categories

- Enterprise Routers: Peplink and related business routers for cellular, WAN, failover, and multi-location networking.
- Mobile Hotspots: Portable 4G and 5G hotspot devices for mobile broadband use cases.
- Signal Boosters: Cellular signal booster hardware. Writers must describe mechanism and conditions, not guaranteed signal outcomes.
- Antennas: External cellular and networking antennas for routers, modems, hotspots, and booster setups.
- Hardware Accessories: Cables, splitters, mounts, adapters, and device-specific networking accessories.
- Software Licenses / Cloud Services: Peplink SpeedFusion Connect and related licenses or cloud services, with firmware and license limits verified before writing.
