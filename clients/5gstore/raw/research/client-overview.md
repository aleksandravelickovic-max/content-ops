## Company Overview

5Gstore is an online retailer specializing in 4G and 5G networking equipment designed to improve cellular connectivity. The company sells routers, antennas, signal boosters, and related accessories used to strengthen mobile broadband performance.

The business evolved from MDG Computer Services, Inc., a company founded in the late 1980s that developed early software and eCommerce platforms. In the mid-2000s, the founder shifted focus toward mobile broadband technology, which led to the creation of 5Gstore.

Today, the company supplies networking equipment to both consumers and enterprise customers seeking stronger cellular internet performance. Products are supported by knowledgeable technical staff who assist customers with equipment selection, setup, and troubleshooting.

The company has received thousands of positive customer reviews and holds an A+ rating with the Better Business Bureau.

## Primary Audience

- Remote workers and home users with weak cellular signals
- RV owners and mobile internet users
- Small businesses needing reliable cellular backup internet
- Enterprise IT teams deploying mobile connectivity solutions
- System integrators and network professionals

These buyers often look for technical guidance when choosing equipment.

## Core Problems the Company Solves

Many locations experience unreliable or slow cellular connectivity. This can affect both residential internet use and business operations. Common issues include:

- Weak cellular signal strength indoors
- Unreliable mobile internet connections
- Lack of wired internet options in rural areas
- Difficulty choosing compatible networking hardware
- Managing cellular connectivity across multiple devices or locations

5Gstore provides hardware and technical expertise designed to improve signal strength and maintain reliable cellular connectivity.
