Peplink GPIO Cable | 10ft Direct Wire DC Power (ACW-634)

The Peplink GPIO cable (ACW-634) is the manufacturer's direct wire DC power cable for select Peplink BR1, BR2, and Transit routers. At 10 feet, it connects a compatible Peplink router directly to a vehicle's 12V DC power source using bare lead termination, with no adapter block required.

This Pepwave direct wire cable is the correct cable for enabling ignition sensing on supported devices. The four-wire design includes a dedicated orange ignition wire that connects to the vehicle's ignition feed, allowing the router to power on and off automatically with the engine. What the Peplink ACW-634 GPIO Cable Does

Direct Wire DC Power for Vehicle Installations

The ACW-634 connects compatible Peplink routers to a 12V vehicle power system using a permanent direct wire installation. The bare lead ends attach to the vehicle's positive and negative power feed, bypassing the need for a cigarette lighter adapter or external power brick. This makes it the right cable for permanent vehicle installations in fleet vehicles, transit systems, RVs, and mobile command units where a clean, low-profile power connection is required.

The cable delivers 12V at 2.0A output to the router, which matches the power requirements of the compatible BR1, BR2, and Transit models.

Ignition Sensing via the Orange Wire

Ignition sensing detects the ignition signal status of a vehicle and allows the cellular router to start up or shut down when the engine of that vehicle is turned on or off. The ACW-634 enables this feature through its orange wire, which connects to the vehicle's positive ignition feed.

A configurable time delay setting allows the router to remain powered on for a specified period after the engine is turned off. This is useful for deployments where the router needs to complete data syncs, upload logs, or maintain a connection briefly after the vehicle stops before powering down.

Ignition sensing is optional. If the feature is not required, the orange wire can be left unconnected, and the router will draw power continuously from the red and black wires.

GPIO Functionality

GPIO (General Purpose Input/Output) refers to the configurable signal pins accessible through this cable on supported devices. GPIO pins can be configured in Peplink firmware to trigger actions based on external input signals, or to output signals to external devices. Common GPIO use cases include door open/close detection, alarm triggers, and external device control in fleet and transit deployments.

The ACW-634 is the required cable for accessing GPIO functionality on compatible models. GPIO behavior is configured in the router's local web interface or through InControl 2 after the cable is installed.

Installation Overview

The ACW-634 connects the 4-pin mini Molex end to the DC power port on the router. The bare lead ends connect directly to the vehicle's power system:

  • Red wire to permanent positive 12V feed
  • Black wire to ground
  • Orange wire to ignition positive feed (optional, for ignition sensing)
  • Brown wire left unconnected

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Peplink GPIO cable used for?

The Peplink ACW-634 is a 10ft direct wire DC power cable that connects compatible BR1, BR2, and Transit routers to a 12V vehicle power source. It also enables ignition sensing and GPIO functionality on supported devices. It is the correct cable for permanent vehicle installations where a bare lead direct wire connection is preferred over an adapter block.

What does ignition sensing do on Peplink routers?

Ignition sensing allows the router to power on automatically when the vehicle engine starts and shut down when the engine turns off. A configurable delay setting keeps the router active for a set period after the engine stops, which allows the device to complete uploads or syncs before powering down. The orange wire in the ACW-634 connects to the vehicle's ignition positive feed to enable this feature.

Is ignition sensing required when using the ACW-634?

No. Ignition sensing is optional. If it is not needed, the orange wire can be left unconnected. The router will draw continuous power from the red and black wires as long as the vehicle's power system is active.

What do the four wires on the ACW-634 do?

The red wire connects to the permanent 12V positive feed. The black wire connects to ground. The orange wire connects to the ignition positive feed for ignition sensing and is optional. The brown wire is reserved for future firmware features and should be left unconnected.

Does this cable work alongside a Peplink Virtual WAN license?

These are separate products serving different purposes. The ACW-634 is a power and GPIO cable for vehicle installations. A Peplink Virtual WAN license activates additional WAN ports on compatible Peplink routers by converting LAN ports to WAN interfaces. If your deployment requires both a direct wire vehicle power connection and additional WAN ports, both products can be used on the same device independently.

What hardware revision do I need for compatibility?

Hardware revision matters for some models. The BR1 Classic requires HW3. The BR1 Mini requires HW3 (PrimeCare Model). The BR1 Mini 5G is compatible with HW1 and HW2.
