What the Inseego FG2000e 5G Router Delivers

Gigabit-Class 5G with LTE Fallback

The FG2000e is built on the Qualcomm Snapdragon SDX55 platform, which supports sub-6 GHz 5G NR and LTE fallback in a single modem. Multi-carrier firmware allows the device to operate on most major global carriers without carrier-specific hardware, making it practical for deployments across multiple regions or carriers without managing separate SKUs.

WAN throughput reaches 1 Gbps, giving wired clients connected to the LAN ports access to the full cellular connection speed. LTE fallback activates automatically when 5G coverage is unavailable, keeping the connection active across varying network conditions without manual intervention.

Wi-Fi 6 Dual-Band for Up to 128 Devices

Wi-Fi 6 radios operate simultaneously on 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz with a 4x4 MIMO antenna configuration. The FG2000e supports up to 128 concurrent Wi-Fi clients, which covers medium-density office environments, retail locations, small warehouses, and branch deployments with a large number of connected devices.

Wi-Fi 6 delivers improved spectral efficiency over Wi-Fi 5, particularly in environments where multiple devices are active simultaneously. OFDMA and MU-MIMO in Wi-Fi 6 allow the router to serve multiple clients in a single transmission window rather than sequentially, reducing latency across congested networks. WPA3 security is supported alongside WPA2 for wireless client protection.

Dual SIM with Auto-Switching

The FG2000e accepts two nano-SIM (4FF) cards with automatic carrier switching. If the primary SIM loses service or falls below a signal threshold, the device switches to the secondary SIM without requiring manual configuration. This gives deployments a carrier redundancy path without a separate failover device.

Multi-carrier firmware extends this flexibility to most major global carriers on a single hardware unit. Ethernet WAN auto-switching is also supported, allowing the FG2000e to use a wired internet connection as primary and fall back to cellular automatically, or vice versa, depending on network configuration.

Enterprise Security with Inseego Secure

The FG2000e includes OpenVPN and IPsec VPN support for encrypted site-to-site and remote access connections. Access control features restrict which devices and users can connect to the network. Inseego Secure adds an enterprise-grade security layer with end-to-end device protection, threat identification, real-time monitoring, and alerts covering malware, ransomware, and spyware threats.

This combination of VPN, access control, and active threat monitoring covers the security requirements of regulated industries and enterprise IT teams that cannot rely on perimeter security alone.

Inseego Connect Advanced Management

Remote management runs through Inseego Connect Advanced, which provides fleet-level device management, diagnostics, configuration, and firmware updates from a single dashboard. IT teams managing multiple FG2000e deployments across sites can push configuration changes, monitor device health, and run diagnostics without dispatching technicians to each location.

Cloud management is included with the device. Tiered upgrade options are available for larger fleet deployments that require additional management capacity or advanced analytics.

Wired Connectivity and IP Passthrough

Three Gigabit Ethernet LAN ports and one Gigabit Ethernet WAN port are available on the rear panel. Any of the ports can be configured as LAN or WAN, giving the FG2000e flexibility in how it fits into an existing network topology. IP Passthrough allows the device to hand its WAN IP address directly to a downstream router or firewall, which is the standard configuration for deployments where a separate security appliance sits between the FG2000e and the LAN.

Two TS9 female cellular antenna connectors support external antenna attachment for installations where the internal antennas are insufficient due to building construction, signal obstructions, or distance from a tower.
