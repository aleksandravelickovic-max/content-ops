What the Peplink Virtual WAN License Does

Converts a LAN Port to a WAN Port

The vWAN license activates Peplink's Virtual WAN feature on a compatible device, which reassigns a physical LAN port to operate as a WAN interface. The newly activated WAN port behaves identically to a factory-configured WAN port. It supports health checks, WAN smoothing, load balancing rules, and SpeedFusion bonding alongside any existing WAN connections on the device.

This is a software license applied to the router, not a hardware modification. The physical port is already present on the device. The license activates its use as a WAN interface in firmware.

Adds WAN Capacity Without Upgrading Hardware

Buying a higher-tier router to gain additional WAN ports carries a significant cost increase. The vWAN license adds one WAN port at a fraction of that cost when the existing hardware supports the feature. Networks that need three or four active WAN connections on a Balance One or Balance 20X can add the required ports through licensing rather than replacing the router.

One license adds one port. Deployments needing multiple additional WAN connections require one LIC-VWAN per port.

Supports SpeedFusion Bonding and Load Balancing

The activated vWAN port participates fully in all Peplink WAN management features. It appears in the WAN configuration alongside physical WAN ports and can be included in SpeedFusion bonding tunnels, outbound policy rules, and failover priority settings. For deployments using SpeedFusion to bond multiple cellular connections, a vWAN license enables an additional cellular modem or secondary ISP connection to join the bonded tunnel without adding a second router.

Compatible with Peplink Virtual WAN Technology

Peplink's Virtual WAN technology is designed to give network administrators flexible control over port assignments across the router's physical hardware. The vWAN license is the activation key that enables this flexibility on supported models. It is firmware-based and tied to the router's serial number at activation.
