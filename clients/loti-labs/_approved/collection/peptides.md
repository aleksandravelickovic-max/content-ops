### What are research peptides used for in laboratory settings? 

Research peptides are used in controlled laboratory environments to study biological mechanisms, including hormone signaling, immune response, and tissue biology. Researchers use them to investigate molecular interactions and cellular processes under repeatable, documented conditions. 

### How should lyophilized peptides be stored? 

Lyophilized peptides should be stored at room temperature for up to four weeks or refrigerated for up to one to two years before reconstitution. Once reconstituted, peptides should be used within two to four weeks. Keep all peptides away from direct sunlight and moisture. Always check the individual product page for compound-specific storage guidance to protect research reliability.

### What is the difference between lyophilized peptides and research liquids? 

Lyophilized peptides are freeze-dried compounds that require reconstitution before use in a research setting. Research liquids are pre-formulated liquid-form compounds ready for direct laboratory application. Lyophilized peptides offer a longer shelf life of up to two years when refrigerated, while research liquids typically remain stable for 12 to 18 months at room temperature.
