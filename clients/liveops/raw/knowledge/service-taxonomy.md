# Liveops Service Taxonomy

Source: STYLE-SYSTEM.md, refreshed website intelligence, and local client research.

## Customer Support Services

- Customer Service Outsourcing: Flexible customer support services delivered through the Liveops network and platform.
- Customer Service: Customer support across voice, chat, email, text, and other supported channels.
- Sales and Lead Generation: Support for sales conversations, lead follow-up, and revenue-related customer interactions.
- Technical Support: Customer technical support services for brands that need scalable help capacity.
- Back Office Support: Operational support work that helps customer programs handle volume and administrative tasks.
- LiveNexus by Liveops: AI service offering for assessing, integrating, and calibrating AI in customer experience operations.

## Delivery Models and Locations

- Onshore Call Center Services in the US: US-based customer support capacity using the Liveops remote network model.
- Nearshore Call Center Services: Nearshore support model for closer time-zone alignment and scalable customer service coverage.
- Offshore Call Center Services: Offshore customer support model for global coverage and capacity flexibility.
- Call Center Outsourcing in India: India-based outsourced customer support option for scalable support needs.
- Call Center Outsourcing in South Africa: South Africa-based support option for voice-first and high-empathy interactions.
- Call Center Outsourcing in the UK: UK-based customer support option for brands needing United Kingdom service coverage.
- Nearshore Call Center Solution in Mexico: Mexico nearshore support model for bilingual coverage and time-zone alignment.
- Call Center Outsourcing in the Philippines: Philippines support option for voice, quality, and scale.
