# Liveops — External Messaging Guidelines

Source: External Messaging Guidelines (1).pptx.pdf
Ingested: 2026-06-03

---

## Brand Identity

**Brand name:** Liveops — NEVER "LiveOps" (capital O is retired; several rebrands ago)
**Tagline:** "It's not outsourcing, it's outsmarting."

---

## What We Are / What We Are Not

**Liveops IS:**
- A cloud-based workforce solutions company
- A marketplace connecting enterprises with a network of independent remote agents
- A flexible, scalable customer service workforce model
- A partner for omnichannel customer support (voice, chat, email, text)

**Liveops is NOT:**
- A call center
- A CCaaS platform
- A staffing agency
- A traditional outsourcing company

---

## Approved Brand Positioning Language

- "It's not outsourcing, it's outsmarting."
- "Modern customer service model"
- "Empathetic support"
- Agents "within our network" or "our network of agents"
- "Precision scheduling" (replaces "right-time scheduling" — retired term)
- "Remote" (not "virtual")
- "Certification / certified" (not "training / trained")
- "Flexible / scalable / agile" — use in this priority order

---

## Unique Value Propositions (UVPs)

1. **Rapid scalability** — Scale capacity up or down in hours, not weeks
2. **Marketplace for top talent** — Network of independently certified agents compete for programs
3. **Cost efficiency without compromise** — Variable cost model; no idle capacity
4. **First-mover advantage** — 25+ years of operational experience
5. **Versatile geographic coverage** — Distributed agent network across the US
6. **Highly adaptable** — Omnichannel, platform agnostic, enterprise-grade security
7. **AI-empowered** — AI tools that augment agent performance

**Company profile:** Fortune 500 and mid-to-large enterprises; $100M–$1B+ revenue range

---

## Core Problems Liveops Solves

- Scaling during demand spikes (seasonal peaks, product launches, open enrollment)
- Maintaining service quality through rapid scale-up
- High fixed costs of in-house call centers
- Limited staffing flexibility
- Meeting rising customer expectations without proportional headcount growth

---

## Banned External Terms

These terms must never appear in external-facing content:

- LiveOps (capital O)
- Call center (as self-description)
- CCaaS
- Staffing agency
- Outsourcing (without the "outsmarting" reframe)
- VirtualFlex (retired product/term)
- Right-time scheduling (replaced by: precision scheduling)
- Gig (as a descriptor for Liveops agent work)
- Our agents / Liveops agents (use: agents within our network)
- Training / trained (use: certification / certified)
- Hire / hiring (for agents)
- Employee / associate / partner (for agents)
- Team / family / workforce (when implying employment relationship)
