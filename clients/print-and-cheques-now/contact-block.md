## Approved CTA structure

The blog post CTA should give the reader a clear next step. It must not be generic.

**Standard CTA:**
> Ready to stop overpaying for cheques? Order at chequesnow.ca or call 1-866-760-2661. Every order is backed by the Bank Acceptance Guarantee — accepted by your bank or reprinted at no charge.

**Minimal variant (for tight layouts):**
> Order cheques at chequesnow.ca or call 1-866-760-2661.

**Variant emphasizing volume savings:**
> Get up to 60% more cheques than your bank charges for the same order. Order at chequesnow.ca or call 1-866-760-2661.

Rules:
- Must include chequesnow.ca or 1-866-760-2661 (or both)
- Mention the Bank Acceptance Guarantee or volume savings where natural — do not force both in every CTA
- Tone: practical and direct — not a sales pitch
- Do not use "Feel free to" / "Don't hesitate to" or any banned closer from STYLE-SYSTEM §3.3

---

## Rules

- Never substitute a generic "contact us" for the actual CTA elements.
- Never use "Don't hesitate to reach out" or "Feel free to contact us" — banned phrases (STYLE-SYSTEM §3.3).
- Always use "up to" qualifier if quoting volume figures in the CTA.
- "Bank Acceptance Guarantee" is a proper name — capitalize it.
