## Explore Luxury New Developments in Porto
### Northern Portugal

A city shaped by its river, its Atlantic edge, and a long-established cultural identity.

Porto is one of northern Portugal's most established cities for luxury new developments, attracting international buyers and families relocating from the UK and across Europe. Central areas such as Ribeira, Sé, and Miragaia reflect the historic core, with narrow streets, older buildings, and neighbourhood patterns that have continued largely unchanged.

Across the river, Vila Nova de Gaia has seen significant waterfront regeneration alongside the port wine lodges and industries that define the area. This part of the city has become an important focus for new luxury homes, with a growing proportion of opportunities available only through off-plan and off-market routes.

To the west, Foz do Douro and Boavista offer broader streets, green spaces, and direct access to the Atlantic coastline. New supply in these established districts is limited, meaning access to contemporary homes is often secured early or privately.

For buyers relocating to Porto from the UK and beyond, access to luxury new developments is increasingly defined by timing. The most desirable off-plan and off-market opportunities across Porto's riverfront and coastal districts rarely reach open property portals.

**Can international buyers purchase property in Porto without residency?**
Yes. There are no restrictions on foreign ownership in Portugal. International buyers can acquire freehold apartments, villas, development plots, and entire buildings. Ownership rights are the same for Portuguese and non-Portuguese nationals. **How does the Golden Visa work for buyers relocating to Porto?**
Portugal's Golden Visa is a residency-by-investment programme open to non-EU nationals, requiring a minimum €500,000 investment in a CMVM-regulated alternative investment fund. Direct property purchase is not a qualifying route following regulatory changes in 2023. The programme requires a minimum stay of 7 days per year in Portugal, grants a renewable residency card every two years with access to 29 Schengen region countries, and offers potential eligibility for Portuguese citizenship after a 10-year qualifying period. A spouse, dependent children, and parents over 65 can be included in the application.
