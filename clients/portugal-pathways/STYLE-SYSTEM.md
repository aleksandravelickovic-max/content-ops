## 1. Brand & Audience

Portugal Pathways is an advisory firm helping high-net-worth individuals navigate relocation, residency, and investment in Portugal. The firm coordinates access to legal, tax, financial, and real estate professionals — it does not provide regulated advice directly.

Website: https://www.portugalpathways.io/

**All content must be written in British English.** This is non-negotiable. (Recognised not recognized. Organised not organized. Emphasise not emphasize. Licence/practise as nouns vs. verbs. Metre not meter. Etc.)

**Audience:** High-net-worth individuals and internationally mobile professionals. Typical profiles:
- Investors researching residency-by-investment (primarily Golden Visa via CMVM-regulated funds)
- Entrepreneurs considering the D2 Entrepreneur Visa
- Professionals and retirees evaluating the D7 (passive income) or D8 (digital nomad) pathways
- International buyers seeking off-market and off-plan luxury property in Portugal

These are sophisticated readers making significant financial and life decisions. Content should inform, not persuade. It should treat them as capable of assessing information without being sold to.

## 2. Voice & Tone

| Attribute | Rule |
|---|---|
| Professional | Credible tone appropriate for legal and financial topics — not warm, not cold, not promotional |
| Client-centred | Focus on the client's goals and decision-making process |
| Balanced | Present information without promotional claims; acknowledge complexity and uncertainty |
| Measured | Avoid certainty around outcomes, approvals, or financial returns |

**What the voice sounds like in practice (from approved location pages):**

- "Access to luxury new developments in Lisbon is increasingly defined by timing." — factual, direct
- "New supply in central Lisbon is structurally limited, with construction in historic districts tightly restricted by planning rules." — explains *why* supply is constrained; no spin
- "Returns are market-driven and not guaranteed." — states the limitation plainly
- "Portugal Pathways provides a comparison framework and professional introductions rather than regulated advice." — precise about what the firm does and does not do

- "Portugal is the most attractive destination for HNWI investors" — superlative, promotional
- "The Golden Visa is your gateway to EU residency" — implies a guarantee
- "Maximise your tax efficiency before NHR ends" — implies regulated tax advice
- "World-class record of delivering success" — vague, promotional (this appears on the website but should not appear in content)

## 3. Terminology

### 3.1 Required terms and spellings (British English)

**Residency programmes:**
- **Golden Visa** — residency-by-investment programme; minimum €500,000 in a CMVM-regulated alternative investment fund. Property is not a qualifying route since 2023.
- **D7 Visa** — passive income / retirement visa
- **D2 Visa** (Entrepreneur Visa) — for non-EU nationals starting or investing in a business in Portugal
- **D8 Visa** (Digital Nomad Visa) — for remote workers
- **IFICI** (NHR 2.0) — Incentivised Tax Regime for qualified professions; the successor to NHR
- **NHR** (Non-Habitual Residency) — closed to new entrants 31 December 2023; existing holders still in their 10-year period

**Portuguese institutions and regulators:**
- **AIMA** — the Portuguese immigration authority (do not say "SEF" — AIMA replaced SEF)
- **CMVM** — Portuguese financial regulator (fund governance)
- **SNS** (Serviço Nacional de Saúde) — the public health system

**Property terms:**
- **CPCV** (Contrato-Promessa de Compra e Venda) — promissory contract; define at first use
- **Escritura** — completion deed
- **NIF** — Portuguese tax identification number
- **IMT** — Property Transfer Tax
- **AIMI** — additional annual property tax on high-value assets
- **off-plan** — hyphenated
- **off-market** — hyphenated
- **Golden Triangle** — the Algarve's prime resort area (Quinta do Lago, Vale do Lobo, Vilamoura)

**Preferred phrasing:**
- "luxury new developments" — the primary property descriptor for location pages
- "residency-by-investment programme" — for the Golden Visa
- "planning controls" and "heritage protections" — for explaining supply constraints
- "structurally limited" — for scarcity framing
- "coordinating with licensed professionals" or "working with licensed legal/tax professionals" — for how Portugal Pathways delivers advice-adjacent services

## 4. Accuracy & Technical Standards

### 4.1 Regulatory accuracy

- **Golden Visa:** Qualifying investment is minimum €500,000 in a CMVM-regulated alternative investment fund. Direct property purchase is not a qualifying route since 2023. Do not imply property qualifies.
- **Residency approval:** Always discretionary, subject to AIMA review, policy changes, and applicant suitability. Never imply automatic approval.
- **NHR:** Closed to new entrants 31 December 2023. Do not present NHR as available to new applicants. Reference IFICI (NHR 2.0) for the current incentivised tax regime.
- **AIMA vs SEF:** AIMA is the current immigration authority. SEF was dissolved. Use AIMA.
- **D2 timeline:** Company formation 1–2 days (simple cases); D2 visa processing typically 2–4 months.

### 4.2 Portugal Pathways scope

Portugal Pathways does not provide:
- Regulated investment advice
- Regulated tax advice
- Regulated legal advice

Content must make this clear when topics approach these areas. Use phrasings like:
- "Portugal Pathways provides introductions to licensed professionals rather than regulated advice."
- "Suitability, risk assessment, and specific product recommendations require authorised professionals."
- "Cross-border tax and investment considerations typically require specialist guidance."

### 4.3 Property and financial claims

- Capital appreciation: always note returns are market-driven and not guaranteed.
- Cost of living comparisons: only cite verified figures, attributed to Portugal Pathways research or a named source (e.g., PMI Report).
- Off-market access claims: "More than 35% of luxury off-market and off-plan opportunities are not available on property portals" and "access to over 250 developers" are verified claims from the client. Use them accurately; do not inflate.
- Payment structures: "typically" or "common frameworks include" — not presented as universal.

## 5. Specificity Rules

**Location content must include:**
- Named districts and neighbourhoods (not just "central Lisbon")
- Specific distances and travel times (format: "approximately X minutes' drive")
- Named schools, hospitals, and cultural venues where relevant
- Factual descriptions of what defines the area (planning framework, heritage protections, geography)

**Do not describe locations with generic lifestyle superlatives.** Instead, describe what is factually true about the place and let the reader draw the conclusion.

- Bad: "Lisbon is one of Europe's most vibrant cities with an unrivalled lifestyle offer."
- Good: "Lisbon's food scene ranges from neighbourhood tascas to Time Out Market Lisboa, where some of Portugal's leading chefs present regional produce in a single riverside location."

**Supply constraint framing (from approved examples):**
When explaining why prime property is scarce:
- "New supply in [area] is structurally limited, with construction tightly restricted by planning rules."
- "Heritage protections, geography, and planning controls limit where new homes can be delivered."
- "New homes enter the market through redevelopment or replacement within established plots — not through new construction phases."

5. **H2: "Why Are International Buyers/Investors Choosing [Location]?"**
   - 1–2 paragraphs framing the investment/relocation case
   - Bullet list: "What this means for international buyers" — 3 points
   - Optional stats block where verified data is available
6. **H2 sections:** 3–5 contextual sections answering specific questions (cultural, amenities, lifestyle, cost of living, international community)
7. **FAQ section** (optional): Q&A format, question-based headings
