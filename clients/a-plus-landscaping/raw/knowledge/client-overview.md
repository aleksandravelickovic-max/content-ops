## Company

A Plus Landscaping is a family-owned outdoor living design-build company founded by Marcus Waldner in 2006 and based in Reinholds, Pennsylvania. The company designs and builds custom outdoor environments for homeowners across Central Pennsylvania.

CSM: Arianna Trabanino
Website: https://apluslandscaping.com/
Phone: (717) 335-1690

## What the Company Does

Design-build outdoor living — not lawn care or maintenance. Projects move through a custom design phase and are then constructed by certified installers. Larger projects may include 3D renderings before construction begins. Every project is backed by a limited lifetime warranty. Financing is available through HFS Financial.

**CRITICAL: Do not mention the warranty on any page except the patio page.**

## Primary Audience

Homeowners across Central Pennsylvania considering a significant backyard project:
- Homeowners (not renters)
- Already considering improving their outdoor space
- Comparing multiple contractors before choosing
- Value craftsmanship and reliability over lowest price
- Typically long-term investments that change how homeowners use their property

## Core Problems the Company Solves

- Backyards that lack usable seating or gathering areas
- Outdoor spaces not designed for entertaining or relaxing
- Uneven or poorly graded landscapes
- Aging patios, decks, or retaining walls
- Uncertainty about how to design a cohesive outdoor space
