# A Plus Landscaping Service Taxonomy

Source: Client website navigation and homepage context supplied on 2026-07-14.

## Positioning

- Custom Outdoor Living Design & Build: Design and construction for custom outdoor living spaces in South Central PA, with a focus on hardscaping, landscaping, craftsmanship, work ethic, and integrity.
- Hardscape Financing: Financing option through HFS Financial for landscaping and outdoor living projects, including longer term payment options for larger outdoor projects.

## Outdoor Living Design

- Outdoor Living Design: Planning and design for custom outdoor spaces, from the initial property walk through the construction plan.

## Hardscaping

- Hardscaping: Structural outdoor construction for patios, driveways, retaining walls, steps, pillars, permeable pavers, stone decks, and concrete overlays.
- Patios: Custom patio design and installation for usable outdoor living areas.
- Paver Driveways: Paver driveway installation for residential entries and vehicle surfaces.
- Retaining Walls: Wall systems that support grade changes, manage soil, and make patios or other outdoor features possible.
- Steps & Pillars: Stone, block, or paver features that create transitions, entries, and built accents within a hardscape.
- Permeable Pavers: Paver systems designed to let water drain through the surface instead of running across it.
- Stone Decks: Stone or paver deck surfaces for outdoor living areas that need a masonry finish.
- Concrete Overlays: Overlay systems that resurface or upgrade existing concrete areas.

## Decks & Structures

- Decks & Structures: Built outdoor structures that define shade, gathering space, transitions, and covered areas.
- Decks: Custom deck construction for raised or ground level outdoor living.
- Pergolas: Open shade structures used to define seating, dining, or transition areas.
- Pavilions: Roofed outdoor structures for covered seating, dining, or entertainment areas.
- Gazebos: Freestanding covered structures for a defined seating or focal area.
- Porches: Covered entry or living spaces attached to the home.
- Louvered Pergolas: Adjustable pergola systems that control shade and weather exposure.

## Pools & Waterscapes

- Pools & Waterscapes: Water centered outdoor features that can be part of a larger backyard design.
- Custom Pools: Pool design and construction shaped around the property and the larger outdoor living plan.
- Streams: Constructed water features that add movement through the landscape.
- Ponds: Built pond features used as landscape focal points.
- Water Feature: Decorative or functional water elements for outdoor living spaces.
- Water Falls: Waterfall features that add movement, sound, and visual interest to a landscape.
- Spas: Spa features integrated into a pool area or outdoor living design.

## Landscaping & Lighting

- Landscaping & Lighting: Planting, tree, lighting, and maintenance work that completes the hardscape and outdoor living plan.
- Landscaping: Landscape installation that supports the overall outdoor design.
- Planting: Plant selection and installation for beds, borders, and finished outdoor spaces.
- Trees: Tree selection and installation as part of landscape design.
- Landscape Lighting: Outdoor lighting for plantings, paths, gathering spaces, and the overall property.
- Hardscape Lighting: Lighting built into or around patios, walls, steps, pillars, and other masonry features.
- Landscape Maintenance Packages: Ongoing maintenance packages for completed landscapes.

## Outdoor Features

- Outdoor Features: Add ons that make patios and backyard spaces more usable for cooking, gathering, and relaxing.
- Outdoor Kitchens: Built outdoor cooking areas designed around the patio or larger outdoor living plan.
- Firepits/Fireplaces: Fire features for gathering areas, including built in fireplaces and firepits.
- Grills & Appliances: Outdoor grills and appliances used in kitchen or entertainment builds.
- Pizza Ovens: Outdoor pizza oven features for cooking and entertaining.
- Patio Furniture: Furniture selections or products that complete outdoor seating and dining areas.
