## Two CTA contexts — do not mix them

### Consumer CTA (all consumer blog posts, city/state pages, storage info pages)

The consumer CTA directs the reader to search for, compare, and reserve a storage unit on USSelfStorage.com.

**Approved consumer CTA structure:**
> Ready to find storage near you? Search by city or ZIP code on USSelfStorage.com to compare pricing, amenities, and reviews across 20,000+ facilities — then reserve online for free.

**Minimal variant (for tight page layouts):**
> Compare storage options near you and reserve for free at USSelfStorage.com.

Rules:
- Must direct the reader to the platform URL
- Must mention the free online reservation
- Must give the reader a clear next step (search, compare, reserve)
- Tone: practical and helpful — not a hard sell
- Do not add a phone number until one is confirmed by the CSM

### B2B CTA (Storage Promoter Blog posts only)

The B2B CTA directs facility operators to list their facility on USSelfStorage.com or to a relevant operator resource. It does not direct operators to search for a storage unit.

**Approved B2B CTA structure (placeholder — confirm specific operator URL with CSM):**
> List your facility on USSelfStorage.com and reach customers actively comparing storage options in your area. [Operator listing URL — confirm with CSM before use.]

Rules:
- Must address facility operators, not consumers
- Must not use consumer-facing language ("find a storage unit," "reserve online")
- Specific B2B landing URL and contact details must be confirmed with the CSM before a piece goes live

## Rules

- Never substitute a generic "contact us" for the actual CTA elements.
- Never use "Don't hesitate to reach out" or "Feel free to contact us" — these are banned phrases (STYLE-SYSTEM §3.3).
- Consumer and B2B CTAs must not be mixed. If a post targets consumers, use the consumer CTA. If it targets facility operators, use the B2B CTA.
