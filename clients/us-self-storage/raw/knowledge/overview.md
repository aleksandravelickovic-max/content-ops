## Company Overview

US Self Storage (USSelfStorage.com) is a self-storage comparison marketplace and editorial resource. It connects consumers with 20,000+ storage facilities and 2,000,000+ storage units across the United States. Users search by city or ZIP code, compare pricing, unit sizes, amenities, photos, and customer reviews, then reserve online at no cost.

USSelfStorage.com is not a storage operator. It does not own, manage, or operate any storage facilities. It is an aggregator and lead-generation platform. Every reference to facilities, units, pricing, or availability refers to third-party providers in the network — not to the platform itself.

In addition to its marketplace function, USSelfStorage.com operates a substantial editorial section covering storage tips, packing guides, moving advice, organization content, and unit size guidance. A separate blog section — the Storage Promoter Blog — targets facility operators with B2B content.

## Audiences

USSelfStorage.com serves two distinct audiences. Writers must identify which audience a piece targets before drafting.

### Consumer audience (primary)
- People planning a move or in the middle of relocating
- Homeowners storing seasonal or overflow items
- Renters between apartments needing short-term storage
- People downsizing who need long-term storage
- Small business owners storing inventory, equipment, or documents
- Vehicle owners looking for car, RV, or boat storage
- Anyone seeking packing, moving, or organization guidance

### B2B audience (Storage Promoter Blog only)
- Self-storage facility owners and operators
- Property managers overseeing storage facilities
- Investors evaluating the self-storage market
- Storage industry professionals seeking operational guidance

Content for the B2B audience is published under the Storage Promoter Blog URL structure and has a fundamentally different tone, framing, and purpose from consumer content. Do not mix framing between the two audiences in a single piece.

## Storage Types Offered in the Network

These are the storage types available through the platform (confirmed). Writers may describe these in content.

- **Standard self-storage** — enclosed, indoor units at standard ambient temperature
- **Climate-controlled storage** — units with temperature and/or humidity regulation, suitable for sensitive items
- **Drive-up access units** — ground-floor units accessible directly from a vehicle, no interior hallways
- **Car storage** — dedicated spaces for personal vehicles
- **Vehicle storage** — broader category including cars, trucks, and other vehicles
- **RV storage** — spaces sized and configured for recreational vehicles
- **Boat storage** — spaces for boats and watercraft
- **Month-to-month rentals** — flexible lease terms with no long-term commitment required

## Editorial Content Areas

### Consumer editorial (blog + storage info hub)

Published under `/self-storage-blog`, `/storage-tips`, `/storage-info`, and `/self-storage/blog/{article-slug}`.

Topics covered:
- Storage tips and packing guides (how to pack efficiently, what to pack first, protective materials)
- Moving advice (budgeting for a move, move timelines, checklists, hiring vs. DIY moving)
- Organization and unit layout guides (how to organize a storage unit, maximize vertical space)
- Storage unit size guides (how to choose the right size unit)

- Self-storage news and industry updates
- Seasonal storage content (holiday decoration storage, sports equipment, etc.)
- Climate-controlled storage explainers (what needs climate control, why)

### B2B editorial (Storage Promoter Blog)

Published under `/storage-promoter-blog/{article-slug}`. This is an entirely separate content vertical.

B2B topics include:
- Marketing and SEO guidance for storage facility operators
- Pricing strategy and revenue management for storage businesses
- Technology tools for storage facility management
- Industry trends and market data
- Customer acquisition and retention for storage operators

B2B content treats USSelfStorage.com as a distribution and listing partner, not as a service consumed by the reader. Tone is professional and operator-focused. Never mix B2B framing into consumer-facing content.

## What the Platform Does NOT Do

Writers must keep these boundaries clear in all content:

- USSelfStorage.com does not own, operate, or manage any storage facility
- The platform does not set or guarantee pricing — pricing is set by individual facilities
- The platform does not guarantee availability — availability is shown in real time from facility data
- The platform does not handle move-in logistics or physical storage — it connects users with facilities
- Do not write "our storage units," "our facilities," "our climate-controlled units," or any possessive language that implies platform ownership of storage infrastructure
