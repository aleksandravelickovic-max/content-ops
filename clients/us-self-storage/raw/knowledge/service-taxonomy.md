# US Self Storage Service Taxonomy

Source: STYLE-SYSTEM.md, refreshed website intelligence, and local client research.

## Storage Types

- Standard Self-Storage: General storage units for personal or business storage needs.
- Climate-Controlled Storage: Storage units with temperature or humidity control where available.
- Drive-Up Access Units: Ground-level storage units with direct vehicle access.
- Month-to-Month Rentals: Flexible rental terms for storage users who do not need long contracts.
- Vehicle Storage: Broader category covering cars, RVs, boats, and similar vehicles.
- Car Storage: Storage options for cars depending on facility availability.
- RV Storage: Storage options for recreational vehicles.
- Boat Storage: Storage options for boats and related trailers.
