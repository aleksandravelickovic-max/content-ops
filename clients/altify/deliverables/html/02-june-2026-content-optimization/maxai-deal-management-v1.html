Deal management breaks down when reps have to guess

Deal management fails for one main reason: reps don't know which deal to work on next. Most CRMs store deals well and rank them poorly, which pushes Revenue Teams back onto spreadsheets, gut feel, and whichever opportunity is making the most noise that week.

Reps prioritize by instinct, not by signal

Without a system that ranks deals using real signals, like how engaged the buying group is, how well it's mapped, and where the deal sits against its stage, reps default to prioritizing by size or manager pressure. That's not the same as prioritizing by what's actually at risk or actually ready to close.

Deal risk surfaces too late

By the time a deal stalls visibly in a pipeline report, the real problem usually started weeks earlier: a missing stakeholder, a buying group that went quiet, a competitor gaining ground unnoticed.

Account and stakeholder data goes stale fast

Relationship maps and account plans typically get built once, during a QBR or a deal kickoff, and nobody touches them again. Buying committees change. Org charts don't update themselves.

MaxAI addresses each of these directly, from inside Salesforce.

How MaxAI turns Salesforce into a deal management system

MaxAI is part of Altify's Account Planning AI Cloud, and it runs inside Salesforce: no separate login, no second system to keep in sync. It reads the account and opportunity data reps are already entering and turns that data into the prioritization, coaching, and relationship intelligence a deal management system is supposed to provide.

Prioritize deals and accounts automatically

MaxAI reads the signals already sitting in Salesforce, deal age relative to stage, stakeholder coverage, and engagement patterns, and surfaces which deals and accounts need attention now. It answers "which deals should I work today" inside the opportunity record itself, without a separate dashboard for the rep to interpret.

Catch deal risk while there's still time to act

Once a deal is flagged, MaxAI turns the underlying signals into a plain-language summary of what's at risk and what to do about it. Coaching prompts point the rep toward the next high-value action, flagging a missing stakeholder or a deal review that's overdue, before the deal shows up as "stalled" in a pipeline report.

Keep relationship maps current without manual entry

That matters for deal management specifically because a buying group with a missing stakeholder is one of the clearest early risk signals a deal review can catch, and the signal only works if the map stays current.

Simplify competitive research inside the deal record

MaxAI automates the step of identifying which competitors are actually relevant to a given opportunity, so reps spend their qualification time on strategy rather than searching for the competition. That research feeds the same prioritization and risk signals above: a deal with an unaddressed competitive threat ranks differently than one without.
