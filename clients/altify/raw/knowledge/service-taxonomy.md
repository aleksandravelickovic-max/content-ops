# Altify Service Taxonomy

Source: Altify website navigation, screenshots supplied 2026-07-14, STYLE-SYSTEM.md, and local live-baseline research.

## Enterprise Solutions

- Altify Insights: Salesforce-native insight mapping for buyer priorities, stakeholder motivation, and relationship context inside complex B2B deals.
- Altify Sales Process: Sales process management that aligns seller activity to the customer buying cycle inside Salesforce.
- Altify Opportunities: Opportunity management for complex deals, deal risk, stakeholder coverage, and disciplined execution.
- Altify Accounts: Account planning for strategic accounts, relationship visibility, whitespace analysis, account objectives, and expansion planning inside Salesforce.
- Revenue Enablement Services: Consulting and enablement support for methodology adoption, sales process development, coaching, KPI measurement, analytics, and Salesforce execution.

## AI And Platform

- MaxAI: Salesforce-native AI for account planning and deal management. It surfaces buying insights, maps relationships, flags deal risk, and guides sellers through next actions.
- Account Planning AI Cloud: Altify's platform category for account planning, opportunity execution, relationship intelligence, methodology, AI workflows, and enablement services.

## Feature Modules

- Relationship Map: Buying group and stakeholder mapping that shows roles, influence, sentiment, champions, blockers, and relationship gaps.
- Insight Map: Buyer insight mapping that connects customer goals, pressures, initiatives, and obstacles to the seller's strategy.
- TeamView: Collaborative account review and peer coaching view for account plans and opportunity strategy.
- Test and Improve: Review and coaching workflow that tracks how account or deal strategy changes after feedback.
- Opportunity Map: Whitespace and opportunity visualization for current, potential, and closed-won revenue across an account.

## Solutions By Role

- CRO & Sales Leadership: Revenue execution and forecast visibility for leaders responsible for predictable growth, risk visibility, and board-level accountability.
- CTO / IT: Salesforce-native revenue execution for technical leaders who care about architecture, governance, data security, and avoiding disconnected systems.
- Sales Operations: CRM alignment, process adherence, pipeline hygiene, data quality, and operational visibility for RevOps and Sales Ops teams.
- Sales Enablement: Methodology adoption, coaching at scale, measurable behavior change, and consistent seller execution.
- Sales Representative / Account Executive: Account strategy, buying group visibility, deal progression, and relationship mapping for sellers working complex B2B opportunities.

## Content And Tools

- Sales Velocity Calculator: Interactive calculator for B2B revenue teams to estimate sales velocity and find execution breakdowns.
- Strategic Revenue Execution: Core positioning category for turning account and opportunity strategy into daily Salesforce execution.
