Company Overview

Altify provides AI-enhanced account planning and sales execution solutions built natively within Salesforce. The platform helps enterprise revenue teams plan strategic accounts, map stakeholder relationships, qualify opportunities, and manage complex sales cycles.

Altify combines software, sales methodology, and consulting support to improve deal qualification, shorten sales cycles, and increase win rates in enterprise B2B sales environments.

Core capabilities include:

* Account planning  
* Relationship mapping  
* Opportunity qualification  
* Sales process alignment with customer buying cycles  
* Revenue growth planning

Altify operates primarily within Salesforce environments and positions itself as a Salesforce-native solution for enterprise revenue teams.

## Primary Audience

Enterprise revenue leaders and strategic sellers.

Primary roles:

* Chief Revenue Officers (CROs)  
* Sales VPs  
* Enterprise Account Executives  
* Strategic Account Managers  
* Revenue Operations leaders

Typical company profile:

* Enterprise B2B organizations  
* Complex, multi-stakeholder sales cycles  
* Long deal timelines  
* High deal values

## Core Problems Altify Solves

Writers should frame content around enterprise sales execution challenges.

Common problems include:

* Lack of visibility into decision makers and buying influence  
* Poor understanding of customer priorities and business goals  
* Weak deal qualification  
* Low win rates and slow sales cycles  
* Ineffective or inconsistent account planning  
* Difficulty identifying pipeline expansion opportunities

Altify positions its platform as a way to create structured, repeatable revenue execution processes.

## Brand Positioning

Altify positions itself as:

* AI-enhanced  
* Salesforce-native  
* Built for enterprise revenue teams  
* Focused on strategic account selling  
* Focused on customer-centric revenue execution

The company emphasizes structured sales methodology and relationship-driven selling rather than transactional sales activity.

## Brand Voice

Content should reflect enterprise-level sales thinking, not basic sales tips. Tone should be:

| Attribute | Writing Rule |
| :---- | :---- |
| Confident | Speak with authority about enterprise sales strategy |
| Educational | Explain frameworks, sales methodology, and revenue strategy |
| Results-focused | Emphasize measurable outcomes such as win rate, deal size, and revenue growth |
| Strategic | Focus on planning, execution, and long-term customer relationships |

## Terminology Guidelines

**Preferred terminology:**

* Salesforce-native  
* Relationship Mapping  
* Revenue Team  
* Strategic Account Planning  
* Revenue Execution  
* Customer-centric selling

Appreciate detailed, long-form content. The writer should try to get the main focus link higher up in the content.

Add more internal links for Altify content (2-3 internal links for every 500 words). We should make opportunities to add them and make sure they make sense.

**Avoid:**

* The word “tool” | Use instead: solution, platform, engine, system

## Messaging Themes

* **Customer-centric selling:** Enterprise sales success depends on understanding customer priorities and aligning sales strategy with customer goals.

* **Structured sales execution:** Revenue teams perform better when they follow consistent planning frameworks and repeatable processes.

* **Relationship intelligence:** Understanding stakeholder relationships improves deal qualification and decision-maker engagement.

* **Revenue growth through strategic planning:** Account planning identifies pipeline expansion and retention opportunities.

## Client Sensitivity

Altify is a high-priority client. Writers should:

* Maintain strict adherence to terminology  
* Avoid casual or generic language  
* Maintain a professional, enterprise tone  
* Follow brand documentation closely
