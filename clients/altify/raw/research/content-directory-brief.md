# Altify Content Directory Brief

## Company Overview

Altify is the Account Planning AI Cloud, built for enterprise revenue teams that need stronger account planning, opportunity execution, sales methodology adoption, and predictable revenue growth.

Altify helps sales organizations put the customer at the center of every interaction by combining:

- AI-enhanced software
- Proven sales methodology
- Salesforce-native execution
- Revenue enablement services
- Account planning and deal management frameworks

Altify is not positioned as "just software." It is a combination of technology, strategy, methodology, coaching, and enablement support designed to help revenue teams execute consistently and grow strategic accounts.

## Core Positioning

Altify helps enterprise sales teams:

- Build stronger customer relationships
- Identify key decision-makers and influence networks
- Improve account planning and opportunity management
- Increase forecast accuracy
- Standardize sales methodology execution
- Improve data quality inside Salesforce
- Reduce manual sales admin work
- Coach sellers more consistently
- Uncover whitespace, cross-sell, and upsell opportunities
- Drive predictable revenue growth

Primary positioning phrase:

> Altify is the Account Planning AI Cloud for enterprise revenue teams.

## Brand Promise

Altify helps revenue teams move from fragmented, manual, inconsistent selling to structured, customer-centered execution inside Salesforce.

> Plan smarter. Execute consistently. Grow predictably.

## Core Themes for Content Production

### 1. Salesforce-native execution

Altify should be positioned as living inside Salesforce, not as another disconnected system.

Preferred language:
- Built inside Salesforce
- Works where sellers already work
- Turns Salesforce into a system of action
- No new data silos
- No disconnected workflows
- Native execution layer

Avoid framing Salesforce as insufficient. Instead, say Altify helps teams operationalize strategy inside Salesforce.

### 2. Methodology becomes execution

Sales methodology should not live in decks, documents, or training sessions only.

Preferred language:
- Embed methodology into daily workflows
- Turn training into execution
- Reinforce best practices in the flow of work
- Standardize seller behavior
- Guide sellers through proven steps
- Make coaching consistent and measurable

### 3. Relationship intelligence

Preferred language:
- Map buying groups
- Identify decision-makers, influencers, blockers, and champions
- Understand political dynamics
- Build multithreaded relationships
- Expose relationship gaps
- Navigate complex stakeholder networks

### 4. Predictable revenue

For executive audiences, position Altify around consistency, risk visibility, and forecast confidence.

Preferred language:
- Predictable revenue growth
- Forecasts leaders can defend
- Earlier risk visibility
- Cleaner pipeline
- Stronger commit accuracy
- More reliable deal health
- Consistent execution across teams

### 5. Reducing manual work

For sellers and operators, emphasize reduced admin, less manual research, fewer spreadsheets, more selling time.

Preferred language:
- Reduce admin drag
- Eliminate manual account planning
- Stop rebuilding org charts
- Replace static plans
- Automate research
- Spend more time selling
- Keep insights current inside Salesforce

## Tone and Voice

Altify content should sound: enterprise-ready, clear, direct, confident, strategic, practical, outcome-driven.

Avoid:
- Overly fluffy language
- Generic AI buzzwords
- Long abstract claims without business impact
- Excessive hype
- Repeating "AI-powered" too many times
- Copying guideline sentences verbatim
- Overusing em dashes

Preferred style:
- Short, strong headings
- Clear H2 and H3 hierarchy
- Problem → solution → outcome flow
- Persona-specific pain points
- Concrete business outcomes

## Reusable Landing Page Structure

### 1. Hero Section
- H1 with primary keyword
- Short subheadline
- Primary CTA

### 2. Core Pain Section
- H2, intro paragraph, H3 pain points with short explanations

### 3. Altify Solution Section
- H2, H3 solution blocks, short explanation under each, optional micro-CTA line

### 4. Trust / Social Proof Section
- H2, bullet-point outcomes, stats, or proof points

### 5. FAQ Section
- Persona-specific questions, slightly expanded answers, no repetition of previous sections
