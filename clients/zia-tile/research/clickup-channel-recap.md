## 0. Executive summary (read this first)

Zia Tile is a $50K MRR brand-sensitive Shopify client now finishing month two. The first two months have produced extensive output — a complete technical SEO audit, 31 March product pages drafted, 60+ April pieces queued, OTTO metadata deployments live (alt text + meta keywords), the December 2025 blog URL migration recovery planned, and a manual-mode link-building program with ~95 placements/month — but no customer-facing content has been published to Jamie's satisfaction yet. The first content delivery (Mar 27) came back with hard feedback. The second round (Apr 24) surfaced factual errors. A third, fully rebuilt round of three reference pieces (Blog #4 Cotto, Cotto product page, Cotto collection page) went to Jamie on **May 12**. Everything downstream depends on her response.

Internally, the editorial system has been rebuilt — Content Production Toolkit, Writing Guide v2 (May 4), 35-point QA Checklist, dual editor review by Aleksandra + Emanuel before Alex sees anything — and the technical workstreams have continued in parallel under Andrés Mora (technical lead since May 7), Steffen Ploeger (audit/SEO), Finn Samsing (OTTO), and Dayanna Pinzon (link building).

The risk is not the work being done. The risk is that after nine weeks, Jamie still has nothing on her live site she is proud of, and Alex's Apr 29 note — *"running out of runway"* — accurately characterizes where client confidence sits. Month three begins now. The May 12 reference docs are the moment the engagement either gets a second wind or enters a recovery conversation.
