# Collection page template — Zia Tile

Skeleton for a material/category collection page. Load the matching `materials/{material}.md` before writing. The golden reference is `campaigns/02-march-april-updates/drafts/collection-cotto.md`.

Collection pages use the same nine-section spine as product pages (STYLE-SYSTEM.md §8.5) but open at the category level, not a single SKU. Key collection-specific rules live in §7.

---

## 1. Opening copy (§7.1)
- Establish what the material is, where it comes from, and what makes it distinctive, in Zia's voice.
- "Our Take on [Material]" framing works as the opener.
- Do NOT read like a product spec.
- Remove filler closers: "well-suited for any climate, with sealing before and after grouting" (Jamie deleted this).
- No "charming." No standalone "wholly unique."

## 2. About / How It's Made
Craft heritage + production narrative for the material. Material-appropriate (see material config). No kiln-type or light-exposure claims without confirmation (§4.10).

## 3. Inherent Variation
Variation language per material config (`variation_language`). Trio chips/pits/crazing for zellige only.

## 4. Preparation and Curation
Mix boxes, dry layout, disperse variation. Overage 15-20% + 25% for uniform look.

## 5. Installation and Finishing
PPE/Prop 65 + drywall flush/mitered edges/Schluter strips + sealing per `sealing_profile` + contact block (§4.5).

## 6. Tile Usage
Residential + Commercial as `###` H3. Freeze/thaw + pool/spa per material config. Inline sealing + slip-resistance spec in wet-area bullets (§4.8).

## 7. FAQ (§7.3)
- Sealing FAQ must separate Cotto and Cotto Allende — never a mixed bullet list.
- Do not call Cotto Allende "a sub-category in this collection." Write it as its own line.
- Match the language and structure of Zia's actual installation guides; do not paraphrase from memory.
- Cross-reference every climate/application claim against the PDP usage charts.
- Contact CTA in the overage FAQ and installation FAQ (email + phone).
- Where relevant, include the Cotto vs Cotto Allende framing (pools/spa + freeze/thaw).

## 8. Order & Shipping
Verbatim §11.2 copy (same as product template).

## 9. Closing
Proper concluding paragraph. Never a CTA-style or generic takeaway closer (§2.5). Contact line present (§4.6).

---

## Repetition guard (§7.2)
Run a word-frequency check before handoff. Recurring flags:
- "Charming unglazed pieces add an earthy aesthetic" appeared twice — cut the second.
- "Traditional" twice in the same passage — switch second use to "classic range."
- Repeated adjectives/descriptors across the page.

## Capitalization (§7.4)
Match ziatile.com exactly: Cotto, Cotto Allende, Adobe, Fired Earth, Red Clay, Blanco, Madera, Oscura capitalized. "terra cotta" lowercase as general material.

## Pre-submission
Run §12 Pre-Submission Checklist (Collection Pages block). Pipeline gates via material-guard, terminology-lint, contact-line-check, person-consistency, voice-judge.
