# Zia Tile Service Taxonomy

Source: STYLE-SYSTEM.md, refreshed website intelligence, and local client research.

## Collections

- Zellige: Handmade Moroccan glazed tile with surface variation, chips, pits, crazing, sheen, and tonal range as expected characteristics.
- Unglazed Natural Zellige: Unglazed Moroccan zellige colorway for earthy, matte installations.
- Cement Tile: Pigmented cement tile made with mineral pigments and hydraulic press methods. Matte, unfired, and pattern-driven.
- Cotto: Unglazed San Miguel de Allende clay tile with earthy variation and exterior suitability, including freeze/thaw regions.
- Cotto Allende: Hand-glazed tile made with the same clay body as Cotto, with pool and spa suitability where confirmed.
- Ceramics: Japanese molded and glazed ceramic tile, including matte, satin, glossy, flat, and textured options.
- Field Trip Japan: Extruded and die-cut Japanese ceramic sub-line.
- Glass Mosaics: Northern Spain recycled-glass mosaics hand-placed on 12x12 grids.
- Terrazzo: Venetian-inspired tile made with steel molds, crushed marble or stone, hydraulic press, grinding, and polishing.
- Marble: Solid marble tile from Italian and Spanish stones, with honed or polished finishes.
- Roman Mosaics: Ancient-Rome-inspired mosaic patterns made from premium stone.
- Cantera Stone: Mexican volcanic stone with earthy colorways, aggregate, and mineral inclusions.
- Limestone: Solid-block limestone from Moroccan and French sources with honed or bush-hammered finishes.
- Terra Forms: Dimensional sculptural tile with relief and texture beyond flat installations.

- Sample Ordering: Sample program with pricing and trade sample rules verified before writing.
