# Zia Tile — Colorway Temperature Reference

Pulled from live ziatile.com collection pages on 2026-06-02. Use to describe the range of a collection without listing every colorway name. Verify against the live site before using in copy — catalog evolves.

**Jamie's rule:** Describe the range, not the list. E.g., "spanning warm terra cottas and muted sage greens" not "Adobe, Fired Earth, Red Clay, Peyote..."

---

## Cotto (Unglazed)

**Warm:** Adobe (sun-bleached tan), Fired Earth (warm terra cotta), Red Clay (reddish-brown), Madera (deep cocoa-brown)
**Cool:** Oscura (matte black)
**Neutral:** Blanco (whitewashed — bridges warm clay body with a cooler brightened surface)

Range description: primarily warm earth tones; Oscura and Blanco are the outliers.

---

## Cotto Allende (Glazed)

**Warm:** Arroyo (light tan), Baja (light beige), Posada (light brown), Lupe (reddish-brown), Dorado (tan to deep ochre), Creosote (earthy tan-brown), Cacao (dark brown)
**Cool:** Albar (off-white), Puebla (white speckled), Sayulita (pale green-beige), Valverde (green glossy), Primavera (green glossy), Peyote (sage green to greenish-blue), Azul (deep blue), Piedra (navy blue), Pedregal (light blue-gray), Roca (light gray), Condesa (earthy brown-gray)

Range description: the warm half reads as earthy and sun-baked; the cool half introduces jewel tones and cool-ground neutrals that Cotto unglazed doesn't carry.

## Zellige

**Warm:** Glazed Earth (beige-tan), Unglazed Natural (tan-beige), Casablanca (cream), Pure White (warm-neutral), Amber (orange-brown), Burnt Sugar (dark brown), Brownstone (medium-dark brown), Sumac (dark reddish-brown), Desert Bloom (pink-peach), Nana's Lipstick (bright red-pink), Pietro Pink (soft pink), Terra Rosa (dark red), Rouge (deep red), Cayenne (red-orange)
**Cool:** Prairie Green (muted green), Racing Green (forest green), Savanna (matte green), Tidepool (teal-green), Jade (bright green), Plum (purple), Purple Iris (purple), Souk 2 (light blue accents), Skylight (blue)
**Transitional:** Za'atar (greenish-yellow)

Range description: the broadest palette in the Zia line — spans saturated earthy reds and pinks through deep jewel blues and greens.

## Ceramics (includes Field Trip: Japan)

**Warm:** Oxblood (glossy red-brown), Wild Fig (reddish-brown), Pecan (brown), Sepia (reddish-brown), Pink Dahlia (matte pink), Field Thistle (pink matte), Citron (yellow-gold), Yuzu (golden yellow), Tangelo (orange), Pale Ochre (ochre yellow), Toast (golden-brown), Sakura (light pink-beige), Peach Blossom (peach), Linen/Wool/Biscuit/Barley/Birch (cream-beige-tan), Feather (off-white beige), Buckeye (warm brown), Oxblood (reddish-brown)
**Cool:** Ponderosa (olive-green), Kelp Forest (olive green glossy), Eucalyptus (pale green), Spanish Moss (greenish-grey), Cypress (light green), Frogtown (olive/dark green), Morning Light (light blue)
**Neutral:** Alpha White, Alabaster White (cool-neutral whites), Earl Grey (neutral greige)

Range description: dominated by warm neutrals and earthy tones; greens and the single blue are the cool outliers.

## Terrazzo

**Warm:** Neutra (white-gray-beige-brown flecks), Bungalow (brown-white), Tamarisk (warm earth), Las Palmas (light pink), Jacinto (brown-yellow-beige-white), Sunmor (brown-cream), Indio (pink), Date Palm (reddish-brown with orange-tan chips), Red Rocks (geometric pink), Guava (reddish-brown multicolor), Highball Jaipur Pink (pink), Collins Rust (rust), Petrichor (dark gray with brown-orange chips), Nocino (dark brown with red-tan chips)
**Cool:** Mirage (cool gray), Sputnik (light neutral), Gemini (gray), Draper Dune (neutral gray), Smoketree (gray), Oscar (blue speckled), Idyllwild (teal), Parker (cool gray), Deepwell (blue), Moon Rock (gray), Thunderbird (dark gray multicolor), Andromeda (gray), Calypso (gray with warm accents), Alexander (cool neutral gray), Gaia (greenish-gray), Flagstaff (dark green), Savoye (cool neutral)
**Transitional:** Artemis (green — reads warm or neutral depending on context)

Range description: the warm half clusters around earthy pinks, rusts, and browns; the cool half is anchored in grays and greens with a few blues.

## Cement Tile

**Warm:** Cadmium (orange), Pompeii (terra cotta), Rust (burnt orange), Coral (reddish-brown), Atomic (red), Terra Cotta (warm terra cotta), Protea (dusty pink), Canyon (terra cotta-rust), Mustard (golden yellow), Jaipur Pink (light beige), Bone (cream), Bisbee Pink (pale pink), Cotton (off-white), Saddle (light tan-brown), Clay (light tan), Dune (cream), Ginger (golden orange)
**Cool:** Hyannis (slate blue), Midnight (deep blue), Elemental Blue (blue), Zeppelin (greenish-gray-blue), Emerald (green), Everglade (deep green), Salvia (muted green), Sage (soft green), Noble Green (forest green), Nile (pale green), Saguaro (green), Monstera (green), Hailstone (light blue-gray), Slate (gray-blue)
**Neutral:** White (pure white)

Note: patterned sets (Kepler, Madrid, Arco, Zig Zag) combine warm and cool solids.

## Marble (Solids)

**Warm:** Giallo Reale (honey-gold with tan and pink swirls), Rosso Alicante (reddish-brown)
**Cool:** Carrara (white with gray veining), Nero Marquina (black with white veining), Verde Alpi (dark green with lighter green-white veins), Grigio Carnico (dark gray with white veining), Bardiglio Imperiale (gray)

Range description: a tight, clearly polarized palette — two warm Italian stones, five cool ones.

---

## Roman Mosaics

Inherits the marble solids palette in small-format mosaic cuts and patterned combinations.

**Warm:** Giallo Reale 5/8 (golden-brown), Rosso Alicante 5/8 (reddish-brown)
**Cool:** Carrara 5/8 (white-gray), Verde Alpi 5/8 (dark green), Bardiglio Imperiale 5/8 (gray), Grigio Carnico 5/8 (dark gray), Nero Marquina 5/8 (black)

Patterned combinations (Check, Mini Check, Ventaglio): follow the temperature of their component stones.

## Glass Mosaics

**Warm:** Tulip (light pink), Roma (red), Kumquat (orange), Canary (yellow), Rye (yellowish-brown), Kiwi (yellow-green), Marmot (dark brown)
**Cool:** Clover (green), Jasper (dark green), Piscine (light blue), Damascus (dark blue), Vespertine (dark teal), Marine Layer (light gray-blue), Jet Black (black)
**Neutral/Transitional:** Milk (off-white cream), Paperwhite (bright white), Lichen (olive green), Tranquil Blue (pale blue), Umber (dark brown)

## Limestone

**Warm:** Buff (light neutral-warm), French Limestone (beige-light brown), French Cobblestone (neutral beige)
**Cool:** Monument (gray), Basilica (gray), Basilica Bush Hammered (textured gray)

Range description: the simplest palette in the line — three warm beige-tones vs. two cool grays.

---

## Cantera Stone

**Warm:** Mesa (beige-tan volcanic aggregate), Sierra (beige-light brown speckled), Yuma (pinkish with rough texture), Sedona (reddish-brown with gray-tan chips)
**Cool:** Caldera (off-white with gray-brown speckles), Acacia (light gray), Badlands (gray base with mixed chips), Volcan (dark brown with black-gray chips)

Note: Volcan reads dark rather than traditionally warm despite its brown-black tones — classify as cool-dark.
