### Universal install rules across materials
- Blend tiles from all boxes before starting
- 15-20% overage minimum (Marble, Cotto)
- Work in 2'x2' sections maximum
- 48 hours thin-set cure before sealing/grouting
- 1/8" tile spacers (cement uses 1/16")
- pH-neutral cleaners only — acidic cleaners damage every material
- Mapei Ultracolor Plus FA (glazed) or Flexcolor CQ (unglazed/stone) grout
- 511 Porous Plus pre-grout sealer for natural stone, terrazzo, cement, unglazed zellige
- Fila Matte Finish Protective Wax for interior top coat (NOT outdoor — UV discolors it)
- Re-seal every 1-2 years for porous materials in wet/high-traffic areas
