# **How to use this document**

This guide is the single source of truth for Zia Tile's 16 material categories. Use it before drafting any product page, collection page, or editorial article that references material capabilities, production methods, or installation requirements.

### **What's in each entry**

* Production & origin — material classification, country of origin, finish, grout joint guidance

* Performance specs — third-party tested Mohs hardness, DCOF, breaking strength, and absorption (sourced from current Zia spec sheets)

* Formats — the dimensions and tile counts available in the line

* Tile Usage — the brand-approved use cases for that material, sourced directly from the live Zia Tile site

* Care & Installation — sealing, grout, and cleaning protocols specific to the material

* Writer Notes — material-specific guidance, distinctions from neighboring categories, and points where brand storytelling extends past the spec sheet

### **Reading the Tile Usage chart**

Every Tile Usage chart uses the same eleven rows. The brand-approved framing column matches the customer-facing language on the live Zia Tile Tile Usage modules. Residential and Commercial charts on-site are typically identical for each material; where they differ, the entry calls it out explicitly.

### **Sourcing**

Every entry has been reconciled against three sources: the Zia spec sheet (where one exists), the corresponding Zia installation guide, and the live Tile Usage chart on the Zia Tile site. Where the sources disagreed, the live Tile Usage chart took precedence for use case approvals, and the spec sheet took precedence for performance numbers and origin facts.

### **Writer Notes**

| WRITER NOTES This is the glazed field tile line — 4x4, 2x6, and hex formats only. Mosaic formats (2x2 square and trapezoid on mesh) are a separate entry. The single difference between Zellige (Standard) and Unglazed Natural Zellige on the Tile Usage chart is Pools \+ Spas: ✓ for glazed, ✗ for unglazed. Glazed zellige is approved for pools, spas, and full-submersion installations when properly sealed. Slip resistance: glazed zellige DCOF is 0.16, well below the 0.42 wet-floor safety threshold. For wet-floor or commercial-floor applications, always include the install guide language: "slip resistance can be increased by increasing grout joint size to a minimum of 1/8" and/or applying an anti-slip product after installation is complete." Required terminology: chips, pits, and crazing (always as a trio); inherent/innate variation; dynamic glazes; each tile (not every); Our Casablanca / Zia's Casablanca (always anchored). Avoid: defects, slightly different, withstands (for freeze/thaw), shower surround, trim pieces, grout spacing. |

They cannot be soaked, must be pre-grout sealed with 511 Porous Plus across the whole layout, and CANNOT be used in pools, fountains, or any fully-submerged installation. If a SKU contains any unglazed tiles, treat it as Unglazed Natural Zellige Mosaic. Slip resistance: same DCOF as standard glazed zellige (0.16). For wet floor applications, include the slip resistance language with a 1/8" minimum grout joint and/or anti-slip product. |

### **Care & Installation**

| Care Item | Details |
| :---- | :---- |
| **Sealing — Pre-Grout** | REQUIRED. Apply 511 Porous Plus Penetrating Sealer in thin layers until fully saturated, before grouting. Allow to cure 48 hours. |
| **Sealing — Top Coat** | After grouting, apply Fila Matte Finish Protective Wax for interior installations. For outdoor installations, re-seal with 511 Porous Plus instead — Fila Matte Wax discolors under UV exposure. |
| **Grout** | Mapei Flexcolor CQ (NOT Ultracolor Plus FA — that is for glazed only) |
| **Pre-installation** | DO NOT soak unglazed natural zellige tiles. Soaking causes efflorescence that is difficult to remove. Clean dust with a damp cloth instead. |
| **Cleaning** | pH-neutral cleaner only. Spills must be cleaned promptly — stains on unsealed unglazed zellige may be permanent. |
| **Re-sealing** | Re-seal at least every 1–2 years, more often in wet areas |

### **Writer Notes**

| WRITER NOTES Pools \+ Spas is ✗ for Unglazed Natural Zellige. This is the single Tile Usage difference between glazed and unglazed lines. The unglazed clay body is too porous to handle continuous full submersion, even with sealing. Slip resistance: DCOF 0.84, which is exceptional — well above the 0.42 wet-floor safety threshold. Unglazed natural zellige is a strong wet-floor performer (the highest DCOF among the zellige lines) and does NOT need anti-slip treatment to clear safety thresholds in shower or wet floor applications. Mohs hardness is 3 — significantly softer than glazed zellige (Mohs 8). The unglazed surface scratches more easily, which is part of how it develops its celebrated patina. Writers should frame patina as a defining characteristic, not a flaw. Critical sealing protocol: pre-grout 511 Porous Plus \+ Fila Matte Wax topcoat. Glazed zellige sealing language does NOT apply here. Always reference the Zellige Installation Guide explicitly when writing sealing copy for unglazed. Patina, natural aging, and visible wear should be expected and embraced. Surface stains on unsealed tiles can be permanent — always lead with sealing requirements before any aesthetic claim. |
