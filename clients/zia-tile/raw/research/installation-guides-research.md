# Zia Tile Installation Guides — Consolidated Research (April 2026)

Source: 13 first-party installation guides on ziatile.com. Captured to support product page audits and brief future editorial work.

## A. Per-guide summaries

### 1. Cantera — `/pages/cantera-installation-guide`
- **Sections:** Reviewing Your Order · Before Beginning · Installation Required Material · Laying Cantera · Floor / Wall / Commercial / Wet Areas · Pre-Grout Sealing · Grouting · Top Coat Sealer · Interior / Outdoor · Trim · Maintenance · Cleaning · Re-Sealing · Installation Safety
- **Freeze/thaw:** Not addressed
- **Sealing:** 511 Porous Plus pre-grout (saturate); Fila Matte Finish Protective Wax interior; 511 Porous Plus only outdoor (UV concern)
- **Slip / grout:** DCOF 0.53–0.63. 1/8" spacers. "Slip resistance can be increased by increasing grout joint size and/or applying an anti-slip product after installation."
- **Edges:** Mitered 45°, Schluter strips, drywall flush
- **PPE / Prop 65:** Dust mask + eye protection. WARNING text + www.P65Warnings.ca.gov
- **Contact:** info@ziatile.com

### 2. Cement — `/pages/cement-installation-guide`
- **Sections:** Reviewing · Before Beginning · Installation · Laying Tiles · Pre-Grout Sealing · Grouting · Top Coat · Trim · Maintenance · Cleaning · Re-Sealing · Safety
- **Freeze/thaw:** Not addressed
- **Sealing:** 511 Porous Plus pre-grout (24–48 hr cure); Fila Matte Wax interior. "Cement tile should be re-sealed at least every 1-2 years."
- **Slip / grout:** DCOF >1. 1/16" spacers
- **Edges:** Miter 45°, drywall flush, Schluter strips
- **PPE / Prop 65:** Present
- **Contact:** info@ziatile.com

### 3. Ceramics — `/pages/ceramics-installation-guide`
- **Sections:** Reviewing · Installation · Required Material · Tile Finishes · Trim · Maintenance · Cleaning · Safety
- **Freeze/thaw:** Not addressed
- **Sealing:** Matte ceramics only — 511 Porous Plus as grout release. 24–48 hr cure. Avoid over-sealing.
- **Slip / grout:** DCOF Matte 0.74 / Satin 0.45 / Glossy 0.30. Recommended 3/16", minimum 1/8".
- **Edges:** Miter or Schluter (drywall flush not mentioned)
- **PPE / Prop 65:** Present
- **Contact:** info@ziatile.com

### 4. Cotto — `/pages/cotto-installation-guide`
- **Sections:** Full template (Reviewing · Before Beginning · Installation · Required Material · Laying · Floor / Wall / Wet · Pre-Grout · Grouting · Top Coat · Interior / Outdoor · Trim · Maintenance · Cleaning · Re-Sealing · Safety)
- **Freeze/thaw:** Not explicit
- **Sealing:** "After laying the tile and prior to grouting, apply the 511 Porous Plus sealer." Interior Fila Wax. Outdoor: "DO NOT use Fila Matte Finish Protective Wax in outdoor applications as UV rays can cause discoloration." Re-seal "as often as needed."
- **Slip / grout:** DCOF 0.98. 1/8" recommended
- **Edges:** Miter 45°, Schluter, drywall flush "to eliminate the need for trim"
- **Contact:** info@ziatile.com

### 5. Cotto Allende — `/pages/cotto-allende-installation-guide`
- **Freeze/thaw:** Not addressed
- **Sealing:** "Cotto Allende tiles must be sealed on-site." 511 Porous Plus pre-grout, 1–2 day cure. "The sealer will also act as a grout release. Do not proceed to grouting unless your sealer is fully cured!" Wet areas re-seal yearly.
- **Slip / grout:** DCOF 0.48. 1/8" recommended
- **Edges:** Drywall flush / floor-to-ceiling preferred; miter or Schluter alternative
- **PPE / Prop 65:** Present. "If extensive cutting is required, Zia Tile recommends this work be done outside or in a well-ventilated area."
- **Contact:** info@ziatile.com

### 6. Japanese Brick Ceramics (Field Trip: Japan) — `/pages/japanese-brick-ceramics-installation-guide`
- **Freeze/thaw:** Not addressed
- **Sealing:** 48 hr cure before sealing/grouting; products not specified
- **Slip / grout:** DCOF Flat 0.40 / Etched 0.45 / Furrowed 0.37. 3/16" recommended, 1/8" minimum
- **Edges:** Miter or Schluter
- **Contact:** info@ziatile.com

### 7. Glass Mosaics — `/pages/glass-mosaics-installation-guide`
- **Sections:** Most stripped — Reviewing · Before Beginning · Installation · Laying Tiles · Grouting · Maintenance · Cleaning · Safety · Variation Scale
- **Freeze/thaw:** Not addressed
- **Sealing:** Not specified for glass
- **Slip / grout:** DCOF 0.42. No specific joint width.
- **Edges:** Not discussed
- **Note:** Most thinly documented guide; no edge guidance

### 8. Limestone — `/pages/limestone-installation-guide`
- **Sealing:** Two-stage. Interior Fila Wax; outdoor 511 Porous Plus only. UV exclusion of wax outdoor.
- **Slip / grout:** Honed 0.50, bush hammer 0.56. 1/8" spacers
- **Edges:** Drywall flush, miter 45°, Schluter strips

### 9. Marble — `/pages/marble-installation-guide`
- **Sealing:** 511 Porous Plus pre-grout; Fila Wax interior; 511 Porous Plus outdoor. 1–2 day cure interior; 24–48 hr outdoor
- **Slip / grout:** DCOF 0.28–0.62 by stone. 1/8" spacers
- **Edges:** Drywall flush / floor-to-ceiling preferred; miter 45°; Schluter for commercial

### 10. Roman Mosaics — `/pages/roman-mosaics-installation-guide`
- **Sealing:** 511 Porous Plus pre-grout; Fila Wax interior; 511 Porous Plus outdoor (no wax due to UV)
- **Slip / grout:** DCOF 0.28–0.62. No explicit joint width
- **Edges:** Miter, Schluter, drywall flush

### 11. Terrazzo — `/pages/terrazzo-installation-guide`
- **Sealing:** "Terrazzo tiles must be sealed on-site." 511 Porous Plus pre-grout; Fila Wax interior; 511 Porous Plus only outdoor. Re-seal "at least every 1-2 years."
- **Slip / grout:** DCOF 0.51. 1/8" recommended
- **Edges:** Floor-to-ceiling / drywall flush, miter, Schluter

### 12. Zellige — `/pages/zellige-installation-guide`
- **Sections:** Most condensed — Reviewing · Before Beginning · Installation · Trim · Ongoing Maintenance
- **Freeze/thaw:** **Not addressed** — gap relative to style guide §4.1
- **Sealing:** Glazed — 511 Porous Plus after grout for wet areas (showers, pools, spas). Unglazed Natural — 511 Porous Plus before grouting, then Fila Matte Wax. Re-seal "at least every 1-2 years."
- **Slip / grout:** Glazed DCOF 0.16. Walls edge-to-edge or 1/16". Floors: "minimum of 1/8" grout spacing is recommended."
- **Edges:** Floor-to-ceiling / drywall flush, miter 45°, Schluter strips
- **PPE / Prop 65:** Present, well-ventilated cutting

### 13. Zellige Mosaics — `/pages/zellige-mosaics-installation-guide`
- **Freeze/thaw:** Not addressed
- **Sealing:** "Installers should fully seal glazed tiles and grout lines that are exposed to continuous moisture with 511 Porous Plus Penetrating Sealer after grout is complete." Unglazed Natural — 511 Porous Plus before grouting, Fila Wax after. Re-seal "at least every 1-2 years."
- **Slip / grout:** Glazed DCOF 0.16. Mesh sheets use less than 1/8". Tiles can be removed from mesh for larger joints
- **Edges:** Miter 45° ("Your experienced installer should be familiar with cutting the edges at a 45-degree angle"), Schluter, drywall flush

## B. Cross-guide consistency

- **Two structural tiers:** Full template (Cantera, Cotto, Cotto Allende, Marble, Roman Mosaics, Zellige Mosaics, Limestone, Terrazzo, Cement) vs. Stripped (Ceramics, Japanese Brick, Glass Mosaics, Zellige main). The flagship Zellige main guide is the most condensed of all — surprising.
- **Freeze/thaw silence on every guide.** Cantera, Cotto, Cotto Allende, Limestone, Marble all have outdoor sections without climate scoping. Style guide v2.0 §4.1 demands explicit zellige-NOT-suitable language; the Zellige guide does not say this.
- **Sealing pattern is uniform** for stone / cement / cotto / terrazzo: 511 Porous Plus pre-grout → Fila Matte Wax interior → 511 Porous Plus only outdoor (UV exclusion).
- **Re-seal cadence:** "at least every 1-2 years" appears in Cement, Terrazzo, Zellige, Zellige Mosaics. Cotto Allende wet areas tighten to "at least every year." Cotto/stone use "as often as needed."
- **Grout joints by material:** 1/16" Cement · 1/8" Cantera/Cotto/Cotto Allende/Limestone/Marble/Terrazzo/Zellige floors · 3/16" rec / 1/8" min Ceramics + Japanese Brick · edge-to-edge or 1/16" Zellige walls · less than 1/8" Zellige Mosaics · not specified Glass + Roman Mosaics
- **Slip resistance phrasing is uniform** across all guides: *"Slip resistance can be increased by increasing grout joint size and/or applying an anti-slip product after installation is complete."*
- **PPE / Prop 65 universal.** Standard wording: *"Installers should always wear a protective face covering (e.g., dust mask) and eye protection when unpacking, cutting and installing Zia Tile products."* Plus *"WARNING: Cancer and Reproductive Harm -- www.P65Warnings.ca.gov"*
- **Contact universal:** info@ziatile.com
- **Custom-order lead times absent from all 13 guides.** Style guide's 10–12 (cement) / 11–15 (cotto) / 13–17 (zellige) week figures must come from product overview, not installation guides.
- **Edge finishing:** Drywall flush + miter + Schluter trio appears in nearly every full-template guide; absent from Glass Mosaics; partial (no drywall flush) in Ceramics + Japanese Brick.

## C. Conflicts with v2.0 style guide

1. **Freeze/thaw silence on Zellige guides** — style guide §4.1 mandates explicit "not suitable for freeze/thaw climates." Product copy must add affirmatively, not borrow silence.
2. **"Trim" as section header** — every full-template guide uses TRIM. Acceptable as a heading, but writers must not let "trim pieces" leak into product page body copy (banned per §3.2).
3. **Glass Mosaics guide lacks edge finishing** — §4.4 requires drywall flush + miter + Schluter in every product page Installation section. Source from another guide.
4. **Zellige main guide is condensed** — PPE/Prop 65 is cleaner in the Mosaics guide. Source from there.
5. **No guide carries the v2.0 "dry spaces" sealing wording** — that exact phrase (§4.2) is product-page-only mandate; not in source guides.
6. **"Shower surround" never appears** — guides correctly use "showers, pools, spas" or "wet areas."
7. **No custom-order lead times** in any guide.

## D. Quote-worthy authoritative wording (borrow verbatim)

- **Slip resistance (universal):** *"Slip resistance can be increased by increasing grout joint size and/or applying an anti-slip product after installation is complete."*
- **Sealing pre-grout:** *"Apply 511 Porous Plus sealer in thin layers until the stones become fully saturated."* (Limestone, Marble, Roman)
- **On-site sealing:** *"Cotto Allende tiles must be sealed on-site."* / *"Terrazzo tiles must be sealed on-site."*
- **UV wax exclusion:** *"DO NOT use Fila Matte Finish Protective Wax in outdoor applications as UV rays can cause discoloration."* (Cotto, Limestone, Marble)
- **Zellige glazed wet areas:** *"Installers should fully seal glazed tiles and grout lines that are exposed to continuous moisture with 511 Porous Plus Penetrating Sealer after grout is complete."* (Zellige Mosaics)
- **Re-seal cadence:** *"at least every 1-2 years, or as often as needed depending on traffic"* (Terrazzo, Zellige, Zellige Mosaics) · *"Cotto Allende tile in wet areas should be re-sealed at least every year"*
- **Grout joints:** *"minimum of 1/8" grout spacing is recommended"* (Zellige floors) · *"3/16" recommended and the minimum is 1/8""* (Japanese Brick, Ceramics)
- **Edges:** *"drywall flush with the finished surface of your tile to eliminate the need for trim"* (Cotto) · *"Your experienced installer should be familiar with cutting the edges at a 45-degree angle"* (Zellige Mosaics)
- **PPE canonical:** *"Installers should always wear a protective face covering (e.g., dust mask) and eye protection when unpacking, cutting and installing Zia Tile products."*
- **Cutting context:** *"If extensive cutting is required, Zia Tile recommends this work be done outside or in a well-ventilated area."* (Cotto Allende, Glass Mosaics, Zellige)
- **Prop 65 warning:** *"WARNING: Cancer and Reproductive Harm -- www.P65Warnings.ca.gov"*
