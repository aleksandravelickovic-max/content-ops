# Cotto — material rules

Cotto is Zia's terra cotta line. "Cotto" is always capitalized (proprietary collection name). "terra cotta" lowercase is the general material category. Cotto is unglazed.

## Freeze/thaw — SUITABLE (any climate)

Suitable for exterior use in any climate, including freeze/thaw regions. State this affirmatively; it is a selling point. (§4.1)

## Pools & spas — NOT SUITABLE

NOT suitable for pools or spas (full submersion). Suitable for outdoor floors, patios, and courtyards in any climate. (§4.2)

> Approved FAQ wording (§8.3): "Cotto tiles are suited for exterior floors in any climate, but not for applications where they are fully submerged in water, like pools or spas."

## Sealing — profile: cotto-unglazed (§4.3)

- Seal before grouting with 511 Porous Plus.
- After grouting: Fila Matte Wax for interior; second coat of 511 Porous Plus for exterior.
- Sealing applies in all installations, including dry spaces.
- NEVER name sealer products in customer-facing copy. Direct readers to the Installation Guide. Product names above are internal reference only.
- Approved dry-space wording: "Always seal Cotto according to our installation instructions, whether in dry spaces or in areas exposed to moisture, such as pools, spas, and showers."
- Cotto and Cotto Allende sealing instructions must NEVER be combined into a single mixed instruction set.

## Slip resistance (§4.4)

- Cotto DCOF: 0.98 (well above the 0.42 threshold for level interior floors).
- Wet commercial floors: grout joints at least 1/8", plus anti-slip treatment after installation.
- Slip language belongs under the shower/wet-area section, not under general interior floor bullets.

## Variation language — tone, shape, edge profile, thickness (§4.11)

- Use: tone, shape, edge profile, thickness.
- FORBIDDEN: chips, pits, crazing. Cotto is unglazed; it has no crazing. The chips/pits/crazing trio is zellige-only.
- Overage rationale wording (§8.4): "to account for curation and cuts" — not "natural variation inherent to handmade tile."
- Prep instruction: "open and mix" — not "blend."

## Unverified claims to avoid (§4.10)

- Do not specify kiln type (wood-fired, gas-fired) without written confirmation from Zia.
- Do not claim UV/light exposure deepens tone. Patina references use and time only.
