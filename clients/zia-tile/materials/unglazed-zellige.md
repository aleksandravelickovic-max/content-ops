# Unglazed Natural Zellige — material rules

Unglazed zellige is a SEPARATE category from glazed zellige (§4.9). Different rules for pool/spa, different sealing copy, different variation language. Per the Materials Reference Guide v3, it has its own row in the Tile Usage matrix.

## Hard rule: do NOT inherit the glazed template

- Do NOT apply the glazed zellige usage template or sealing copy to unglazed pages.
- The chips/pits/crazing trio is documented for GLAZED zellige only (§4.11). Do NOT assume it transfers to unglazed.
- Pool/spa rule differs from glazed zellige.

## Freeze/thaw — NOT SUITABLE

Per matrix: Unglazed Zellige Ext F/T = ✗. Same as glazed zellige for freeze/thaw.

## Pools & spas — NOT SUITABLE

Per matrix: Unglazed Zellige Pool/Spa = ✗. THIS IS THE KEY DIVERGENCE from glazed zellige. Do not transfer the glazed pool approval to unglazed pages.

## Sealing — profile: unglazed-zellige

Sealing required. Direct readers to the Installation Guide; never name sealer products in customer-facing copy.

## Variation language

Use: surface variation, finish-specific characteristics, color, shape, thickness. The chips/pits/crazing trio is GLAZED-zellige only. Unglazed zellige is unfired clay; do not borrow that language.
