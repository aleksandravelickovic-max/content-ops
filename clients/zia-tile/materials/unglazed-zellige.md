# Unglazed Natural Zellige — material rules

Unglazed zellige is a SEPARATE category from glazed zellige (§4.9). Different language for sealing, surface variation, and finish-specific characteristics.

## Hard rule: do NOT inherit the glazed template

- Do NOT apply the glazed zellige usage template or sealing copy to unglazed pages.
- Request category-specific guidance from Alex first.
- The chips/pits/crazing trio is documented for glazed/fired surfaces; do not assume it transfers to unglazed without confirmation.

## Freeze/thaw, pools/spas, sealing — VERIFY

STYLE-SYSTEM does not publish standalone unglazed-zellige rules. material-guard must flag any draft that asserts freeze/thaw, pool/spa, or a specific sealing protocol on an unglazed-zellige page until Alex confirms the category-specific guidance.
