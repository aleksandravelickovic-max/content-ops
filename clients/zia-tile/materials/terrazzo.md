# Terrazzo — material rules

Steel molds, crushed marble and stone aggregate, hydraulic press, ground and polished. 12-inch square format. Cement matrix + visible aggregate is what defines terrazzo.

## Freeze/thaw — NOT SUITABLE

Not suitable for outdoor floors that may receive snow or ice. Suitable for interior installations and for exterior floors/walls in non-freeze/thaw climates only. (Per audited v3 draft.)

## Pools & spas — SUITABLE WITH FULL SEALING

Pools and spas compatible with full sealing per the installation guide. (Per audited v3 draft.)

## Sealing — profile: terrazzo-two-step

Two-step protocol: pre-grout sealing and a top coat, per the Terrazzo Installation Guide. Re-seal every 1 to 2 years depending on traffic. Direct readers to the Installation Guide; never name sealer products.

## Slip resistance

DCOF 0.51. For wet floors and commercial wet rooms: increase grout joint to a minimum 1/8" and apply an anti-slip product after installation.

## Variation language

- Use: aggregate distribution (shifts tile to tile), tonal variation, minor crazing, edge irregularity.
- Hand-finished; subtle variation in color and aggregate distribution is expected.
