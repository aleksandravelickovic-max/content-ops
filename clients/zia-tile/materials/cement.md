# Cement Tile — material rules

Mineral pigments + cement, hydraulic press, NEVER fired. Matte surface. 68 colors. Vietnam, 19th-century European method.

## Freeze/thaw — NOT SUITABLE (non-freeze/thaw only)

Suitable for exterior use in non-freeze/thaw climates only. (§11, §12)

## Pools & spas — VERIFY

Not stated in STYLE-SYSTEM. Confirm with Alex before asserting.

## Sealing — profile: cement-standard

Cement is porous and unfired; sealing required. Direct readers to the Installation Guide; never name sealer products in customer-facing copy.

## Variation language

- Cement is matte and never fired. Do NOT use glaze language.
- "Crazing" is borderline glaze terminology — do NOT use on cement pages unless Alex confirms the install guide documents crazing for cement. (Open question from REWRITE-PLAN.)
- Use: tone, pattern, surface character, minor variation.

## Material-defining note

"Never fired" is the key distinction from terra cotta and ceramics. Pigment is mineral, pressed hydraulically, cured rather than kiln-fired.
