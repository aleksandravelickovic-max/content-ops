# Zellige (glazed) — material rules

Handcrafted Moroccan clay tile, 800+ years of tradition. Glazed. This file covers GLAZED zellige only. Unglazed zellige is a separate category — see `unglazed-zellige.md`.

## Freeze/thaw — NOT SUITABLE

NOT suitable for outdoor installation in freeze/thaw climates. State this plainly and affirmatively (§4.1). For zellige, affirmative-prose disclosure in the body is non-negotiable, not just the usage chart.

## Pools & spas — VERIFY

STYLE-SYSTEM does not state a pool/spa rule for zellige explicitly. Confirm with Alex before asserting pool/spa suitability. Do not auto-approve.

## Sealing — profile: zellige-glazed

- Sealing required per Installation Guide; specifically pools, spas, showers, and dry spaces.
- NEVER name sealer products in customer-facing copy.
- Approved dry-space wording: "Always seal [tile name] according to our installation instructions, whether in dry spaces or in areas exposed to moisture, such as pools, spas, and showers."

## Variation language — chips, pits, and crazing TRIO (§3.1, §4.11)

- "Chips, pits, and crazing" must always appear as a trio for zellige surface characteristics. These are features, not defects.
- Also approved: dynamic glazes (the way color and sheen shift with light — zellige-specific term, do not borrow for other materials), innate/inherent variation, surface texture, sheen.
- "Perfectly imperfect" — use in context only, never as a standalone claim.
- FORBIDDEN: "defects," "imperfections," "grout spacing" (use "grout joints"), unqualified "unique."

## Installation section required elements (§4.5)

PPE + Prop 65 (protective face/eye coverings when working with zellige, thinset, sealants, grout; link ziatile.com/proposition-65-warnings); drywall flush + mitered edges + Schluter strips; sealing for all installations; contact info (info@ziatile.com + 310-844-1170).
