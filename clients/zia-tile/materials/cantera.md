# Cantera Stone — material rules

Volcanic stone from Mexico. 7 earthy colorways. Visible aggregate and mineral inclusions. Suitable indoor and outdoor.

## Freeze/thaw — NOT SUITABLE

Per the Materials Reference Guide v3 Quick Reference (live-site precedence): Cantera is not suitable for exterior freeze/thaw. Ext Non-F/T is ✓. STYLE-SYSTEM §11's "indoor + outdoor" wording was too broad — the reference doc reconciled against the live Tile Usage chart sharpens it to non-F/T exteriors only.

## Pools & spas — NOT SUITABLE

Per the Materials Reference Guide v3 Quick Reference: Cantera is not suitable for pools/spas.

## Sealing — profile: natural-stone

Volcanic stone is porous; sealing required. Direct readers to the Installation Guide; never name sealer products.

## Variation language

- Use: visible aggregate, mineral inclusions, tone, natural stone variation.
- Natural volcanic stone — do NOT apply zellige (chips/pits/crazing) or terra cotta variation language.

## Colorway note
