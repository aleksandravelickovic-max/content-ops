# Glass Mosaics — material rules

98% recycled glass, produced with renewable energy in Northern Spain. Hand-placed on 12x12 grids. 7 patterns.

## Freeze/thaw — SUITABLE

Suitable for exterior freeze/thaw per the audited v3 draft (usage chart marks Exterior Floors Freeze/Thaw as ✓).

## Pools & spas — SUITABLE

Approved for pools and spas (saltwater or chlorine), per audited v3 draft.

## Sealing — profile: glass-none (key distinction)

Glass tile is naturally stain-resistant. Only OPTIONAL grout sealing — the "Sealing Required" usage row is marked ✗ for glass. Do NOT apply the mandatory two-step or natural-stone sealing copy to glass pages.

## Slip resistance

For shower floors and wet floors: use a minimum 1/8" grout joint and apply an anti-slip product after installation.

## Variation language

- Use: color, sheen, hand-placement variation across the grid.
- Glass is not a fired clay or natural stone. Do NOT use the chips/pits/crazing trio or terra cotta variation language.
