## About

No two zellige tiles are the same. Pieces vary in size, surface, and glaze across each box, with hairline crazing and chamfered edges that come from how the material is hand-formed rather than flaws to correct.

Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with zellige, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with [California Prop 65](https://www.ziatile.com/proposition-65-warnings). Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered cuts or Schluter strips. Traditional trim and edge pieces do not exist for zellige.

Seal zellige according to our installation instructions, whether the application is dry or exposed to moisture (pools, spas, showers). Reach out to our team at [info@ziatile.com](mailto:info@ziatile.com) with any material or installation questions.

## How It's Made

Zellige is hand-formed in Morocco, where the technique has been refined for more than 800 years and passed across generations of artisans.

The process begins with raw clay, which is mined from local hillsides, soaked, filtered for impurities, and hand-pressed into molds. The tiles are laid to dry in the Moroccan sun, flattened and shaped by hand, then dried further before glazing.

Each tile is hand-dipped in glaze and arranged inside an earthen kiln, where uneven heat creates the tonal variation that defines zellige. No two pieces leave the kiln the same, and the handmade nature and variation are hallmarks of this material. To prepare for installation, open and mix all boxes before setting them to achieve an ideal layout. The tiles will carry the marks of the day they were fired.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What color is Glazed Earth zellige?

Glazed Earth zellige is a warm beige-neutral with a glaze that ranges across the earth tone spectrum. Individual tiles carry positions from lighter buff to deeper clay and warm brown, with the full range most visible once the tiles are laid together. The colorway reads warmer under natural light and may appear slightly more muted under cooler artificial sources, though it remains within the warm neutral range throughout.

### What layout patterns work with the Glazed Earth 2x6 Bejmat format?

Running bond with offset horizontal joints is the most common approach for the 2x6 Bejmat. Herringbone draws more attention to the format's elongated shape. Vertical stack creates a quieter surface where each tile reads as a narrow vertical element. With Glazed Earth's wide tonal range, horizontal layouts tend to blend the earth tone variation gradually across the surface, while herringbone and vertical layouts can produce more visible contrast between individual tiles.

### What materials pair well with Glazed Earth zellige?

Natural stone, wood, and linen-textured surfaces sit naturally alongside Glazed Earth zellige. The warm beige-neutral glaze works with warm-toned natural materials without competing with their texture. Unlacquered brass and warm matte black complement the earth tones in surrounding fixtures. White and cream cabinetry also pairs well, allowing the Glazed Earth surface to read as a grounded, warm field against a cleaner background.

### Can Glazed Earth 2x6 zellige be used in a shower?

Yes. Glazed Earth 2x6 can be used on shower walls, shower floors, and steam showers. Sealing is required for all shower applications. For shower floor installations, slip resistance can be increased by sizing grout joints to a minimum of ⅛" and applying an anti-slip treatment after installation is complete. Refer to the [Zellige Installation Guide](https://ziatile.com/pages/zellige-installation-guide) for full instructions.

### How does Glazed Earth 2x6 compare to Amber 4x4 zellige?

Both Glazed Earth and Amber are warm-neutral zellige colorways, but they sit in different parts of the warm spectrum. Glazed Earth moves through buff, clay, and warm brown — a wider tonal range with more earth undertones. Amber carries honey tones that read as a warmer, more golden neutral. The choice depends on whether a multi-toned earth surface or a more uniform honey-warm tone fits the installation, and whether the elongated 2x6 Bejmat format or the square 4x4 format is the right call for the space.

### Does Glazed Earth 2x6 zellige work in a pool or spa?

Yes. Glazed Earth 2x6 is suitable for pool and spa applications in both saltwater and chlorine environments. Sealing is required; apply sealer to all tiles and grout lines exposed to continuous moisture after grouting is complete. Consult the [Zellige Installation Guide](https://ziatile.com/pages/zellige-installation-guide) for specific sealing instructions and reach out to our team at [info@ziatile.com](mailto:info@ziatile.com) with any project-specific questions.
