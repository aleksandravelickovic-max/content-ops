## About

No two zellige tiles are the same. The Gambit 7 mosaic combines three colorways — Absinthe, Tidepool, and Portuguese Blue — each with a glaze that shifts subtly with changing light. Pieces vary in size, surface, and glaze across each box, with hairline crazing and chamfered edges that come from how the material is hand-formed rather than flaws to correct.

Before setting any sheet, separate and mix sheets from multiple boxes to distribute the three colorways evenly across the layout and avoid visible clustering of any single glaze. Lay out the composition on a flat surface before setting to confirm how the blue-green palette distributes. Individual tiles can be removed from the mesh backing if adjustments are needed at edges or perimeters.

Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with zellige, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with [California Prop 65](https://www.ziatile.com/proposition-65-warnings). Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered cuts or Schluter strips. Traditional trim and edge pieces do not exist for zellige. Seal Gambit 7 according to our installation instructions, whether in dry areas or areas exposed to moisture. ## How It's Made

Gambit 7 is produced in Fez, Morocco, through the same fully manual process that defines all traditional zellige. Raw clay is pulled from local hillsides, soaked in water, filtered for impurities, and worked by hand until it reaches the right consistency for pressing into molds. Each element of the mosaic is then shaped again by hand to refine its edges and surface.

After drying in the Moroccan sun, each element is hand-dipped in one of three glazes: Absinthe, Tidepool, or Portuguese Blue. Because the glaze is applied by hand, coverage varies within each colorway, and that variation produces the tonal range visible across the finished mosaic. The elements are then hand-assembled onto mesh backing, forming the 8x8 sheets that arrive on site ready for installation.

The final stage is the kiln. Earthen kilns do not distribute heat evenly, and the results of that unevenness are not incidental. The chips, pits, crazing, and shifting tones that emerge from the firing process are what make each Gambit 7 mosaic its own.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Gambit 7 zellige good for floors?

Yes. Gambit 7 is suitable for floors, including high-traffic areas such as kitchens, primary bathrooms, and entryways. The mosaic format contributes to slip resistance through higher grout joint density, which makes it particularly appropriate for shower floors and wet-area installations. For wet floor applications, size grout joints to a minimum of ⅛" and apply an anti-slip treatment after installation is complete.

### What grout color works best with Gambit 7 zellige?

A light neutral grout — soft white, light grey, or cream — allows the Absinthe, Tidepool, and Portuguese Blue palette to remain the focal point while minimizing visual separation between elements. A darker grey grout can be used where the intention is to emphasize the hand-formed edges and surface irregularity of each piece, though the three-color composition generally reads more clearly against a neutral grout. Use Mapei Ultracolor Plus FA, which works well with glazed zellige and cleans off without staining if wiped promptly after application.

### Do zellige tiles require grout?

Yes. Zellige installations require grout between tiles. Traditional installations can appear closely set, but grout is necessary to protect tile edges and support long-term durability. For Gambit 7, grout also plays a design role by shaping how the three colorways transition across the field — particularly where saturated Portuguese Blue meets the cooler Tidepool range.

### Can Gambit 7 zellige be used on exterior walls?

Yes. Gambit 7 can be used on exterior walls in non-freeze/thaw climates. The mosaic format performs reliably on vertical surfaces where the glazed finish and variation are protected from direct structural impact. It is not suitable for outdoor installations in freeze/thaw climates. Sealing is required for all exterior applications.

### Why do designers choose zellige tile?

Designers choose zellige for its handcrafted surface, inherent material variation, and light-responsive glaze. Each tile is individually formed, dipped, and fired, producing the chips, pits, and crazing that define its surface character. The result is an installation where the surface shifts with light and viewing angle rather than reading as static or uniform — a quality that is particularly apparent in multi-colorway mosaics like Gambit 7, where three distinct glazes interact across the full installation.

### Can I mix Gambit 7 with other zellige colors?

Yes. Gambit 7 can be mixed with other colorways in Zia's zellige collection. Each colorway is produced using the same traditional methods, so tiles and mosaic sheets share consistent surface character across the range. Gambit 7 works particularly well alongside neutral whites and softer tones, where the three-color mosaic reads as a focal point against a quieter surrounding palette.
