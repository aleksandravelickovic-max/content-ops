## About

No two zellige tiles are the same. Pieces vary in size, surface, and glaze across each box, with hairline crazing and chamfered edges that come from how the material is hand-formed rather than flaws to correct.

Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with zellige, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with [California Prop 65](https://www.ziatile.com/proposition-65-warnings). Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered cuts or Schluter strips. Traditional trim and edge pieces do not exist for zellige.

Seal zellige according to our installation instructions, whether the application is dry or exposed to moisture (pools, spas, showers). Reach out to our team at [info@ziatile.com](mailto:info@ziatile.com) with any material or installation questions.

## How It's Made

Zellige is hand-formed in Morocco, where the technique has been refined for more than 800 years and passed across generations of artisans.

The process begins with raw clay, which is mined from local hillsides, soaked, filtered for impurities, and hand-pressed into molds. The tiles are laid to dry in the Moroccan sun, flattened and shaped by hand, then dried further before glazing.

Each tile is hand-dipped in glaze and arranged inside an earthen kiln, where uneven heat creates the tonal variation that defines zellige. No two pieces leave the kiln the same, and the handmade nature and variation are hallmarks of this material. To prepare for installation, open and mix all boxes before setting them to achieve an ideal layout. The tiles will carry the marks of the day they were fired.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What color is Casablanca White zellige?

Casablanca White zellige is a light neutral with a glaze that shifts between near-white, pale grey, and warmer off-white tones depending on light and viewing angle. The colorway reads brightest under direct natural light and settles toward the pale grey and off-white end of the range under cooler or lower light. The hand-dipping process and uneven kiln heat mean individual tiles carry different positions in that tonal range, producing a surface that reads as unified without being uniform.

### What grout joint size works best for the 2x2 format?

For wall installations, the traditional method uses no standard grout joint — tiles are laid edge to edge or with a 1/16" spacing if a specific grout line is preferred. For floor installations, a minimum of ⅛" grout joint is recommended. In wet or commercial floor applications, grout joints can be widened beyond ⅛" to increase slip resistance. The 2x2 format produces more grout coverage per square foot than larger formats, which contributes to traction underfoot.

### Can the Casablanca White 2x2 be mixed with other Casablanca formats?

Yes. Casablanca White 2x2 can be mixed with other Casablanca White formats — including the 4x4 and 2x6 — or with other colorways in Zia's zellige collection. Each format is produced using the same traditional methods and shares consistent surface character across the range. Mixing the 2x2 with a larger format is a common approach for combining field tile with detail work in kitchens and bathrooms.

### How does the 2x2 format work on a floor versus a wall?

On a wall, the 2x2 format produces a surface where the variation in each tile is experienced up close. On a floor, the smaller format means more grout joints across the surface, which increases traction but also requires careful curation to distribute tonal variation evenly. For floor installations, a minimum of ⅛" grout joint is recommended, and slip resistance can be further increased by widening joints and applying an anti-slip treatment after installation.

### Can Casablanca White 2x2 zellige be used in a pool or spa?

Yes. Casablanca White 2x2 is suitable for pool and spa applications in both saltwater and chlorine environments. Sealing is required; apply sealer to all tiles and grout lines exposed to continuous moisture after grouting is complete. Consult the [Zellige Installation Guide](https://ziatile.com/pages/zellige-installation-guide) for specific sealing instructions and reach out to our team at [info@ziatile.com](mailto:info@ziatile.com) with any project-specific questions.

### What grout should I use with Casablanca White zellige?

Mapei Ultracolor Plus FA works well with glazed zellige. A grout color matched closely to the near-white of the tile produces the most cohesive surface, allowing the tonal variation across tiles to read without interruption from the joints. Contrasting grout colors can stain the glazed surface and may be difficult to remove if excess grout is not wiped away promptly after application.
