## About

No two zellige tiles are the same. Pieces vary in size, surface, and glaze across each box, with hairline crazing and chamfered edges that come from how the material is hand-formed rather than flaws to correct.

Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with zellige, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with [California Prop 65](https://www.ziatile.com/proposition-65-warnings). Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered cuts or Schluter strips. Traditional trim and edge pieces do not exist for zellige.

Seal zellige according to our installation instructions, whether the application is dry or exposed to moisture (pools, spas, showers). Reach out to our team at [info@ziatile.com](mailto:info@ziatile.com) with any material or installation questions.

## How It's Made

Zellige is hand-formed in Morocco, where the technique has been refined for more than 800 years and passed across generations of artisans.

The process begins with raw clay, which is mined from local hillsides, soaked, filtered for impurities, and hand-pressed into molds. The tiles are laid to dry in the Moroccan sun, flattened and shaped by hand, then dried further before glazing.

Each tile is hand-dipped in glaze and arranged inside an earthen kiln, where uneven heat creates the tonal variation that defines zellige. No two pieces leave the kiln the same, and the handmade nature and variation are hallmarks of this material. To prepare for installation, open and mix all boxes before setting them to achieve an ideal layout. The tiles will carry the marks of the day they were fired.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What color is Pure White zellige?

Pure White zellige is a clean white with a glaze that carries subtle tonal variation depending on light and viewing angle. Individual tiles range from bright white to faint warm or cool undertones, with the differences between tiles most visible once they are laid together. Under natural light, the surface reads as white with movement; under cooler artificial light, the glaze may carry slightly more coolness. The variation is a product of the hand-dipping process and the uneven heat of the earthen kiln.

### Is Pure White 2x2 zellige good for floors?

Yes. Pure White 2x2 can be used on interior floors in both low- and high-traffic areas. The compact format contributes to slip resistance through higher grout joint density, which makes it appropriate for shower floors and wet-area floor installations. For wet floor applications, increase grout joints to a minimum of ⅛" and apply an anti-slip treatment after installation to improve traction.

### Can I install Pure White 2x2 zellige in a kitchen?

Yes. Pure White 2x2 is commonly installed as a kitchen backsplash, where the reflective glaze responds well to both natural and artificial light. The small 2x2 format and close-set installation create the dense, mosaic-like field associated with traditional Moroccan zellige. As with all zellige, proper sealing and routine cleaning with a pH-neutral cleaner are required in areas exposed to cooking oils, steam, and heat.

### What tiles pair well with Pure White 2x2 zellige?

Pure White 2x2 pairs well with materials that introduce contrast or tonal depth. Natural stone such as travertine, limestone, and marble provides visual grounding, while warm woods balance the brightness of the glaze. Darker colorways from Zia's zellige collection can be used to define borders or patterned layouts, particularly in kitchens and bathrooms where a white field frames a deeper accent color.

### Can Pure White 2x2 be mixed with other zellige colors in the collection?

Yes. Pure White 2x2 can be mixed with other colorways in Zia's zellige collection. Each colorway is produced using the same traditional methods, so tiles and mosaic sheets share consistent surface character across the range. Pure White works particularly well alongside deeper solid colorways, where the white mosaic frames or counterpoints a saturated field, and pairs naturally with warm neutrals where a more cohesive, tonal composition is the design direction.

### Does Pure White 2x2 zellige work in a pool or spa?

Yes. Pure White 2x2 is suitable for pool and spa applications in both saltwater and chlorine environments. Sealing is required; apply sealer to all tiles and grout lines exposed to continuous moisture after grouting is complete. Consult the [Zellige Installation Guide](https://ziatile.com/pages/zellige-installation-guide) for specific sealing instructions and reach out to our team at [info@ziatile.com](mailto:info@ziatile.com) with any project-specific questions.
