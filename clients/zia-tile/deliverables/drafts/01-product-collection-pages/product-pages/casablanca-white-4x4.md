## About

No two zellige tiles are the same. Pieces vary in size, surface, and glaze across each box, with hairline crazing and chamfered edges that come from how the material is hand-formed rather than flaws to correct.

Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with zellige, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with [California Prop 65](https://www.ziatile.com/proposition-65-warnings). Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered cuts or Schluter strips. Traditional trim and edge pieces do not exist for zellige.

Seal zellige according to our installation instructions, whether the application is dry or exposed to moisture (pools, spas, showers). Reach out to our team at [info@ziatile.com](mailto:info@ziatile.com) with any material or installation questions.

## How It's Made

Zellige is hand-formed in Morocco, where the technique has been refined for more than 800 years and passed across generations of artisans.

The process begins with raw clay, which is mined from local hillsides, soaked, filtered for impurities, and hand-pressed into molds. The tiles are laid to dry in the Moroccan sun, flattened and shaped by hand, then dried further before glazing.

Each tile is hand-dipped in glaze and arranged inside an earthen kiln, where uneven heat creates the tonal variation that defines zellige. No two pieces leave the kiln the same, and the handmade nature and variation are hallmarks of this material. To prepare for installation, open and mix all boxes before setting them to achieve an ideal layout. The tiles will carry the marks of the day they were fired.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What color is Casablanca White zellige?

Casablanca White zellige is a light neutral with a glaze that shifts subtly depending on light and viewing angle. The colorway reads as white across the field, but the hand-dipping process and uneven kiln heat mean individual tiles carry tonal variation — some brighter, some with a softer or warmer cast. This variation is what gives the tile its character across a completed installation.

### Does Casablanca White zellige show surface imperfections?

Yes. Each tile carries chips, pits, and hairline crazing as inherent characteristics of the handmade process, not as quality defects. These surface features are expected and consistent with traditional zellige production, where clay is hand-shaped and fired in earthen kilns with uneven heat distribution. Mixing tiles from all boxes before setting helps distribute these characteristics evenly across the installation.

### Can Casablanca White 4x4 be used on a shower floor?

Yes. Casablanca White 4x4 is suitable for shower walls, shower floors, and steam showers. Sealing is required for all shower applications. For shower floor installations, slip resistance can be increased by sizing grout joints to a minimum of ⅛" and applying an anti-slip treatment after installation is complete. Refer to the [Zellige Installation Guide](https://ziatile.com/pages/zellige-installation-guide) for sealing instructions.

### What grout color pairs well with Casablanca White zellige?

A grout color matched closely to the tile — a warm white or off-white — allows the variation within each tile to read across the surface without interruption. A contrasting darker grout will define each tile more clearly and draw attention to the grid. Contrasting grout can also be more difficult to clean if excess grout is not removed promptly after installation.

### Can Casablanca White zellige be mixed with other colors in the collection?

Yes. Casablanca White 4x4 can be combined with other zellige colorways. Each tile in Zia's zellige collection is produced using the same traditional methods, so pieces share consistent thickness, format, and surface character across the range. Casablanca White pairs well alongside warmer neutrals and more saturated colorways, where it functions as a lighter field against a more color-forward accent.

### Does Casablanca White zellige vary between production runs?

Yes. The colorway can shift between batches. Each run of zellige is produced by hand, and the clay, glaze, and kiln conditions change from one production run to the next. For projects requiring tiles from multiple orders, ordering the full quantity at once gives the most consistent result across the installation.
