## About

No two zellige tiles are the same. The Rubric 4 mosaic uses Tidepool and Pure White — Tidepool a blue-green with a glaze that shifts through kelpy greens and seafoam blues, Pure White a light neutral. Pieces vary in size, surface, and glaze across each box, with hairline crazing and chamfered edges that come from how the material is hand-formed rather than flaws to correct.

Before setting any sheet, separate and mix sheets from multiple boxes to distribute tonal variation evenly across the full layout. Lay out the composition on a flat surface before setting to confirm how the mosaic pattern reads and how the two glazes distribute. Individual tiles can be removed from the mesh backing if adjustments are needed at edges or perimeters.

Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with zellige, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with [California Prop 65](https://www.ziatile.com/proposition-65-warnings). Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered cuts or Schluter strips. Traditional trim and edge pieces do not exist for zellige. Seal Rubric 4 according to our installation instructions, whether in dry areas or areas exposed to moisture. Reach out to [info@ziatile.com](mailto:info@ziatile.com) with any material or installation questions.

## How It's Made

Rubric 4 begins in the same place as all traditional zellige. The clay is pulled from the hillsides surrounding Fez, Morocco, soaked in water, and filtered for impurities before being worked by hand until it reaches the right consistency for pressing into molds. For Rubric 4, the molds shape the small geometric elements that make up the mosaic composition.

After drying in the Moroccan sun, each element is dipped individually in Tidepool or Pure White glaze. Because the glaze is applied by hand, coverage varies from piece to piece, and that variation is what produces the tonal range visible across the finished mosaic. Each element is then hand-assembled onto a mesh backing, forming the sheets that arrive on site ready for installation.

The final stage is the kiln. Earthen kilns do not distribute heat evenly, and the results of that unevenness are not incidental. The chips, pits, crazing, and shifting tones that emerge from the firing process are what make each Rubric 4 mosaic its own.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What colors are in the Rubric 4 zellige mosaic?

Rubric 4 uses Tidepool and Pure White — two glazes that anchor opposite ends of the mosaic's color contrast. Tidepool is a blue-green with a glaze that shifts through kelpy greens, seafoam blues, and cooler mid-tones tile to tile. Pure White is a light neutral with a glaze that moves between near-white and pale off-white. The contrast between the two glazes defines the geometric pattern across the mosaic field, and the variation within each glaze means the composition shifts and reads differently at different viewing angles and under different light conditions.

### Can Rubric 4 zellige mosaic be used in a shower?

Yes. Rubric 4 can be used on shower walls, shower floors, and steam showers. Sealing is required for all shower applications. For shower floors, slip resistance can be increased by sizing grout joints to a minimum of ⅛" and applying an anti-slip treatment after installation. The mosaic format contributes to surface traction through higher grout coverage per square foot compared to large-format tiles, which makes it well suited to shower floor applications. Refer to the [Zellige Installation Guide](https://ziatile.com/pages/zellige-installation-guide) for full instructions.

### Can Rubric 4 be used in a pool?

Yes. Rubric 4 is suitable for pool and spa applications in both saltwater and chlorine environments. Sealing is required; apply sealer to all tiles and grout lines exposed to continuous moisture after grouting is complete. The Tidepool blue-green glaze reads particularly well at pool waterlines, where the color interacts with reflected water. Consult the [Zellige Installation Guide](https://ziatile.com/pages/zellige-installation-guide) for specific sealing instructions.

### What grout color works best with Rubric 4 zellige mosaic?

Grout color affects how the contrast between Tidepool and Pure White reads across the mosaic. A white grout matched to the Pure White tiles produces a surface where the blue-green Tidepool pattern is the clear visual focus. Light grey or neutral grout softens the contrast and makes the geometric pattern read as more integrated. Darker grout, such as a warm grey or charcoal, introduces a third element that frames each tile more distinctly and deepens the overall composition. Avoid blue-tinted or cool grouts that compete with the Tidepool glaze rather than complementing it.

### How do I install Rubric 4 zellige mosaic sheets?

Rubric 4 arrives on site as mesh-mounted sheets. Before setting any sheet, separate and mix from multiple boxes to distribute tonal variation evenly across the full layout. Lay out the full composition on a flat surface and confirm how the Tidepool and Pure White distribution reads before setting any tile. Individual tiles can be removed from the mesh backing if adjustments are needed at edges or perimeters. Use wedge spacers to maintain consistent spacing between sheets and plan for the installation to take two to three times longer than standard ceramic.

### Can Rubric 4 be mixed with solid zellige tiles?

Yes. Rubric 4 mosaic can be used alongside solid zellige tiles in Tidepool or Pure White to create a layered installation. Using the Rubric 4 mosaic as a feature area against a field of solid Tidepool or Casablanca White square or Bejmat tiles is a common approach that brings the geometric pattern into dialogue with a simpler surrounding field. Each component is produced using the same traditional methods and shares consistent surface character. Plan the full layout during the dry stage before setting begins.
