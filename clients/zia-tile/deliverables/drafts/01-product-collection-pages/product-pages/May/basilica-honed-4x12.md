## About

Zia's limestone is sourced from two regions: Honed Limestone quarried near Fez in three colorways (Buff, Monument, and Basilica) across 6x6, 4x12, 12x12, and 12x24 formats; and French Limestone quarried in Bordeaux in 12-inch and 20-inch varying-length tiles plus French Cobblestone. Basilica is also offered in a single bush-hammered 4x12 variant. Variation in color, tone, and natural inclusions across pieces is inherent to the material; lay out the stone on a flat surface and confirm the color balance reads the way you want before setting any piece.

Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with limestone, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with [California Prop 65](https://www.ziatile.com/proposition-65-warnings). Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered edges or Schluter strips. The full installation procedure is in the Limestone Installation Guide. ## How It's Made

Limestone is quarried from solid blocks near Fez, Morocco, and around Bordeaux, France, in workshops where the material has been cut and honed for centuries. Each block is sized down to the format dimensions and finished by stone masons at the source.

The honed finish is achieved by gently smoothing the surface to a clean, soft matte. The bush-hammered finish is achieved by striking the face with a textured hammer head, which uniformly pits the surface for a more rustic, aged character. Both finishes preserve the natural mineral signature of the stone.

Limestone is a living material. Subtle tonal variation across pieces is inherent to natural stone, and no two tiles are identical. The soft patina that develops over years of use is a defining feature of the material and the reason limestone has carried so many of the floors that survive from antiquity into the present.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the Basilica colorway?

Basilica is the cool grey colorway in Zia's honed limestone line, occupying the cooler and lighter end of the range alongside the warm-toned Buff and darker Monument. The grey tone comes from the mineral composition of the stone at the quarry near Fez and carries natural variation in depth and warmth tile to tile. The overall reading across the installation is a restrained, cool grey with the subtle tonal movement inherent to natural sedimentary stone.

### Can Basilica 4x12 limestone be used in a shower?

Yes. Basilica 4x12 can be used on shower floors and shower walls. Seal the stone before grouting with 511 Porous Plus, and follow with Fila Matte Protective Wax as a final top coat for interior wet applications. Size grout joints to a minimum of 1/8" to support slip resistance on the floor. An anti-slip treatment can be applied after installation to further increase traction in high-exposure areas.

### How do I seal Basilica honed limestone?

To seal Basilica honed limestone, apply 511 Porous Plus in thin layers until the stone is fully saturated, wiping off any excess before it dries. Allow the sealer to cure fully before grouting. For interior installations, follow with Fila Matte Protective Wax as a final top coat. For exterior applications, apply a second coat of 511 Porous Plus in place of wax — UV exposure causes wax to discolor over time outdoors. Periodic resealing is required to maintain stain and moisture resistance.

### What pairs well with Basilica honed limestone?

Basilica's cool grey pairs naturally with warm wood tones and natural timber, which offset the stone's cool register with organic warmth. Unlacquered brass and warm bronze fixtures sit well alongside the grey. Plaster walls in warm white or pale sand keep the surrounding surfaces light without competing with the stone. For a cooler, more minimal palette, matte black fixtures and grey-white walls align with Basilica's temperature and reinforce a quieter, more restrained scheme.

### Can Basilica 4x12 limestone be used outdoors?

Yes, in non-freeze/thaw climates. Basilica 4x12 is suitable for exterior floors and walls in climates that do not experience freezing temperatures. It is not rated for freeze/thaw exposure. For exterior applications, seal with 511 Porous Plus before and after grouting and use a second coat of 511 Porous Plus as the final top coat — do not use Fila Matte Wax on exterior installations.

### Can Basilica be mixed with other Zia limestone colorways?

Yes. Basilica can be mixed with Buff and Monument in the Zia honed limestone line. All three colorways are produced from the same quarry near Fez and share the same clay body, format dimensions, and surface finish, so they can be combined across a layout. When mixing colorways, plan the arrangement during the dry stage and blend tiles from all boxes to distribute tonal variation evenly across the field.
