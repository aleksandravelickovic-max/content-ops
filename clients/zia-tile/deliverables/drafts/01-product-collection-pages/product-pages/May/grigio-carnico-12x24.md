## About

Marble has been quarried continuously since Roman antiquity, and Zia's marble is cut from blocks pulled from quarries across Italy and Spain. Each block is finished near Verona, where stone masons hone the surface to the soft matte finish that defines the line.

The line includes six core colorways: Carrara, Nero Marquina, Grigio Carnico, Rosso Alicante, Giallo Reale, and Verde Alpi. Together, they cover the full range of designed marble installations, from the quiet veining of Carrara to the deep black field of Nero Marquina and the warm ochres of Giallo Reale, and they pair cleanly with the patterned compositions in our Marble Patterns line.

Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with marble, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with [California Prop 65](https://www.ziatile.com/proposition-65-warnings). Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered edges or Schluter strips.

## How It's Made

Marble begins as an ancient metamorphic rock, compressed under heat and pressure over millions of years. Zia's marble range is quarried across Italy and Spain, and Italy has been the principal source of architectural marble in the West since Roman antiquity.

Each block is cut into the format dimensions and honed by stone masons in workshops near Verona, producing the soft matte surface that defines the line. Veining and mineral variation are inherent to the stone, and no two pieces are identical.

Marble is a living material. It develops natural patina with use, taking on the soft luster that has carried Roman floors and Renaissance halls into the present, and that becomes part of the story of any room it is set into.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What does Grigio Carnico marble look like?

Grigio Carnico is an Italian marble with a cool grey base and complex dark veining that runs through the stone in linear, expressive patterns. The grey field carries subtle tonal variation across pieces — from lighter silver-grey to deeper charcoal tones — while the darker veining introduces strong linear movement across the surface. In a large-format 12x24 installation, the veining reads at room scale as a continuous pattern across the field, with each piece carrying a distinct section of the stone's natural movement.

### Is Grigio Carnico suitable for a shower floor?

Yes. Grigio Carnico 12x24 can be used on shower floors and shower walls. Seal the stone before grouting with 511 Porous Plus, and follow with Fila Matte Protective Wax as a final top coat for interior installations. Size grout joints to a minimum of 1/8" to support slip resistance on the floor. Do not use a rubber mallet during installation — compact the marble pieces with the palm of your hand only, as impact can create micro-fractures that grow over time.

### How do I seal Grigio Carnico marble?

To seal Grigio Carnico marble, ensure the stone is clean and dry before applying 511 Porous Plus in thin layers until the stone is fully saturated. Wipe off any excess before it dries — over-application can result in an uneven film. Allow the sealer to cure fully before grouting. For interior installations, follow with Fila Matte Protective Wax as a final top coat. For exterior applications, apply a second coat of 511 Porous Plus rather than wax — UV exposure causes wax to discolor over time outdoors. Reseal as needed based on traffic and moisture exposure.

### Can Grigio Carnico 12x24 be used outdoors?

Yes, in non-freeze/thaw climates. Grigio Carnico 12x24 is suitable for exterior floors and walls in climates that do not experience freezing temperatures. It is not rated for freeze/thaw cycling. For exterior applications, seal with 511 Porous Plus before and after grouting and use a second coat of 511 Porous Plus as the final top coat.

### How do I clean Grigio Carnico marble?

To clean Grigio Carnico marble, use a pH-neutral cleaner with warm water for routine maintenance. Vacuum or sweep regularly to remove grit and abrasive debris. Blot spills immediately rather than wiping, particularly for oil and grease. For heavier staining, use Miracle Sealants Heavy Duty Cleaner (Acid Substitute). Never use vinegar, lemon juice, or bleach — acidic cleaners etch and damage the marble surface over time.

### Can Grigio Carnico 12x24 be mixed with other Zia marble colorways or formats?

Yes. Grigio Carnico can be mixed with other colorways in the Zia marble line — including Carrara for a grey-on-grey composition, or with Nero Marquina for a higher-contrast pairing. It also pairs with the patterned Marble Patterns compositions that incorporate Grigio Carnico as one of the colorways. When mixing formats or colorways, confirm the full layout during the dry stage before setting any stone.
