Each terrazzo tile is a cement-based composite with crushed marble and stone aggregates ground and polished to a matte surface. Aggregate distribution varies tile to tile. Blend tiles from all boxes before installation and arrange a layout to disperse aggregate evenly.

Perhaps the most versatile tile Zia produces, with endless colorways, patterns, and shapes, Zia strives to offer the world's best curated collection of encaustic terrazzo tiles. While generally consistent in appearance, each handmade tile is inherently unique. Durable and versatile, matte terrazzo tile is an easy choice for many installations.

Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with terrazzo, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with [California Prop 65](https://www.ziatile.com/proposition-65-warnings). Proper installation and protection of terrazzo tile is key, so please ensure you work with an installer who has experience specifically installing terrazzo tile, and who has carefully reviewed [Zia's installation guide](https://ziatile.com/pages/terrazzo-installation-guide).

## How It's Made

Terrazzo is produced using techniques refined by Venetian stone masons since the 15th century, when leftover marble offcuts were combined with cement to floor courtyards and palazzo halls. The same craft is preserved in the workshops where Zia's terrazzo is produced today.

Crushed marble and stone aggregates are arranged in steel molds and covered with liquid cement, then hydraulically pressed into a powdered concrete base. Once cured, the surface is ground and polished to the matte finish that defines the line.

Each tile is finished by hand, which produces the subtle tonal variation, occasional crazing, and slight edge irregularity that separate hand-pressed terrazzo from machine-set composite tile. The cement matrix and the visible aggregate are what define terrazzo as a material; the press and the finish are what carry that definition into the floor.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What does Moon Rock terrazzo look like?

Moon Rock is a warm grey cement terrazzo with stone aggregate flecks of varying size and tone distributed across the grey field. The aggregate creates a speckled surface that reads as mineral-rich and natural, evoking the texture of granite or natural stone without the directional movement of veined marble. The warm grey cement matrix keeps the overall tone grounded and approachable, while the aggregate flecks introduce visual complexity that varies from tile to tile across the installation.

### Does Moon Rock 12x12 terrazzo require sealing?

Yes. Cement terrazzo tile requires sealing before and after grouting. Apply 511 Porous Plus in thin layers until the tiles are fully saturated before grouting, wiping off any excess before it dries. For interior installations, finish with Fila Matte Finish Protective Wax as a top coat. For outdoor installations in non-freeze/thaw climates, apply a second coat of 511 Porous Plus in place of wax — UV exposure causes wax to discolor over time outdoors.

### Can Moon Rock 12x12 be used in a shower?

Yes. Moon Rock 12x12 can be used on shower floors and shower walls. Terrazzo has a DCOF of 0.51, which meets the wet area slip resistance threshold for floor applications. Seal the tile before and after grouting as required, and size grout joints to a minimum of ⅛". An anti-slip product can be applied after installation to further increase traction in wet floor areas.

### How do I clean Moon Rock terrazzo tile?

To clean Moon Rock terrazzo, vacuum or sweep regularly to remove abrasive debris that can scratch the surface. For routine cleaning, use a pH-neutral cleaner such as Miracle Sealants pH Neutral Tile and Stone Cleaner. Avoid acidic or abrasive cleaners — vinegar, lemon juice, and bleach will damage the cement matrix over time. For heavier staining, use Miracle Sealants Heavy Duty Cleaner (Acid Substitute). Blot spills promptly rather than wiping to prevent spreading.

### What goes well with Moon Rock terrazzo?

The warm grey palette of Moon Rock pairs with a wide range of materials. White oak and light natural wood create a warm contrast with the grey ground. Warm plaster walls, linen, and other natural textiles complement the earthy, mineral character of the aggregate surface. Matte white or cream cabinetry and millwork allow the terrazzo floor to define the ground plane. In commercial settings, Moon Rock's warm grey reads as refined without the coldness of cooler grey materials — well suited to boutique retail, hotel lobbies, and residential entryways.

### Can Moon Rock 12x12 be mixed with other terrazzo colorways or formats?

Yes. Moon Rock 12x12 can be used alongside other terrazzo colorways or formats in the Zia collection. Tiles from different colorways should be assessed during a dry layout to confirm that the aggregate and base tone combination works before setting begins. Blend tiles from all boxes — including any mixed colorways or formats — to distribute aggregate variation evenly across the full installation and confirm the visual balance reads as intended.
