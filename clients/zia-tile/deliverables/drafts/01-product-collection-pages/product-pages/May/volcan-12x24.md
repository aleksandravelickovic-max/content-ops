## About

Cantera is the volcanic stone that built colonial Mexico, used continuously for centuries in courtyards, fountains, and the carved facades of cathedrals and haciendas. Zia's Cantera line carries that material into a residential and commercial collection sized for both intimate rooms and broad public installations.

The line is offered in eight earthy colorways, each carrying its own mineral signature: Mesa, Caldera, Acacia, Sierra, Yuma, Sedona, Badlands, and Volcan. Formats range from 6x6 and 4x12 mosaics to 12x12, 12x24, and 24x24 floor tiles, so a single Cantera scheme can run from a textured shower wall in a primary bathroom to a hotel courtyard or restaurant patio in the same colorway.

Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with cantera, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with [California Prop 65](https://www.ziatile.com/proposition-65-warnings). Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered edges or Schluter strips.

## How It's Made

Cantera begins as a porous volcanic rock, formed over millions of years from cooled lava and ash. Solid blocks are pulled from quarries in Mexico, then sized down to the format dimensions of the line: 6x6 and 4x12 mosaics, 12x12 and 12x24 planks, and 24x24 large-format floor tiles.

Each piece is honed and finished by hand at the source. The finishing approach intentionally preserves the visible aggregate, mineral inclusions, and oxidation markings of the volcanic chemistry, which is what gives each colorway its individual mineral signature rather than a surface treatment that would mask it.

Once finished, the tiles are graded and packed for shipping. After installation, the stone continues to develop. Cantera takes on a soft patina with use, similar to marble or limestone, and the sealing protocol on the install guide protects the surface while still allowing the material to age into the room around it.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What color is Volcan 12x24 cantera?

Volcan cantera is a warm brown volcanic stone with tonal variation built into the material itself. The color shifts slightly from tile to tile due to natural variation in the volcanic rock, and each piece carries its own distribution of aggregate, mineral inclusions, and oxidation markings within that warm brown range. At 12x24 inches, the larger surface area of each tile makes the individual mineral variation more prominent — each plank presents a distinct section of the stone's character across a broader field.

### How does the 12x24 plank format affect the installation?

The 12x24 plank format introduces a strong directional quality to the floor. Running bond with offset horizontal joints creates a layout with clear orientation and draws the eye across the surface. Staggered layouts with 50% offset are common for plank-format natural stone. The larger format also means fewer grout joints compared to 12x12, which lets the surface read with more continuity. Tonal balance across the full layout should be confirmed during the dry stage, mixing from all boxes to avoid adjacent tiles reading as too similar in tone.

### Does Volcan 12x24 require sealing?

Yes. Cantera tile requires sealing before and after grouting. Apply 511 Porous Plus in thin layers until the stone is fully saturated before grouting, wiping off any excess before it dries. For interior installations, finish with Fila Matte Finish Protective Wax as a top coat. For outdoor installations, apply a second coat of 511 Porous Plus in place of wax — UV exposure causes wax to discolor over time.

### Can Volcan 12x24 be used on an exterior patio?

Yes, in non-freeze/thaw climates only. Volcan 12x24 is suitable for exterior floor applications in climates that do not experience freeze/thaw conditions. It is not suitable for any outdoor floor installation in climates that get snow or ice. For exterior applications, seal before and after grouting and apply a second coat of 511 Porous Plus as a top coat in place of wax.

### How do I clean Volcan 12x24 cantera?

To clean Volcan 12x24, vacuum or sweep regularly to remove abrasive debris. For routine cleaning, use a pH-neutral cleaner such as Miracle Sealants pH Neutral Tile and Stone Cleaner. For heavier staining, Miracle Sealants Heavy Duty Cleaner (Acid Substitute) is the recommended product. Avoid acidic cleaners — including vinegar, lemon juice, and bleach — and blot spills promptly rather than wiping.

### Can Volcan 12x24 be mixed with other Volcan formats or cantera colorways?

Yes. Volcan 12x24 can be mixed with other Volcan formats or with other Cantera colorways in the Zia collection. All Cantera formats share consistent material character cut from the same volcanic rock. When mixing formats, assess the visual balance during a dry layout before setting begins, and blend tiles from all boxes to distribute tonal and aggregate variation evenly. Mixing 12x24 planks with 12x12 squares in the same colorway is a common approach for defining zones within an installation.
