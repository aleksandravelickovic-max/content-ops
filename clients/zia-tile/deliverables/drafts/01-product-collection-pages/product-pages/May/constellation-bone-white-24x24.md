## About

Each terrazzo tile is a cement-based composite with crushed marble and stone aggregates ground and polished to a matte surface. Aggregate distribution varies tile to tile. Blend tiles from all boxes before installation and arrange a layout to disperse aggregate evenly.

Perhaps the most versatile tile Zia produces, with endless colorways, patterns, and shapes, Zia strives to offer the world's best curated collection of encaustic terrazzo tiles. While generally consistent in appearance, each handmade tile is inherently unique. Durable and versatile, matte terrazzo tile is an easy choice for many installations.

Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with terrazzo, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with [California Prop 65](https://www.ziatile.com/proposition-65-warnings). Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered edges or Schluter strips.

Proper installation and protection of terrazzo tile is key, so please ensure you work with an installer who has experience specifically installing terrazzo tile, and who has carefully reviewed [Zia's installation guide](https://ziatile.com/pages/terrazzo-installation-guide).

## How It's Made

Terrazzo is produced using techniques refined by Venetian stone masons since the 15th century, when leftover marble offcuts were combined with cement to floor courtyards and palazzo halls. The same craft is preserved in the workshops where Zia's terrazzo is produced today.

Crushed marble and stone aggregates are arranged in steel molds and covered with liquid cement, then hydraulically pressed into a powdered concrete base. Once cured, the surface is ground and polished to the matte finish that defines the line.

Each tile is finished by hand, which produces the subtle tonal variation, occasional crazing, and slight edge irregularity that separate hand-pressed terrazzo from machine-set composite tile. The cement matrix and the visible aggregate are what define terrazzo as a material; the press and the finish are what carry that definition into the floor.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What does Constellation Bone White terrazzo look like?

Constellation Bone White is a warm bone white cement terrazzo with a scattered aggregate composition that distributes stone flecks across the pale field. The bone white cement matrix provides a soft, warm base — less stark than pure white, with a natural warmth that reads as cream-adjacent. The aggregate distribution across the 24x24 format means each tile presents a slightly different arrangement of stone flecks, producing a floor with the characteristic tonal movement of handmade terrazzo.

### Does Constellation Bone White require sealing?

Yes. Cement terrazzo tile requires sealing before and after grouting. Apply 511 Porous Plus in thin layers until the tiles are fully saturated before grouting, wiping off any excess before it dries. For interior installations, finish with Fila Matte Finish Protective Wax as a top coat. For outdoor installations in non-freeze/thaw climates, apply a second coat of 511 Porous Plus in place of wax — UV exposure causes wax to discolor over time outdoors.

### Can Constellation Bone White 24x24 be used in a shower?

Yes. Constellation Bone White 24x24 can be used on shower floors and shower walls. Terrazzo has a DCOF of 0.51, which meets the wet area slip resistance threshold for floor applications. Seal the tile before and after grouting and size grout joints to a minimum of ⅛". An anti-slip product can be applied after installation to further increase traction in high-exposure wet floor areas.

### What pairs well with Constellation Bone White terrazzo?

The warm bone white palette of Constellation makes it a neutral foundation that pairs with a wide range of materials. Natural wood and warm timber tones create an organic, light-filled combination. Aged brass and matte black fixtures both complement the warm white field. Soft plaster walls and linen-textured surfaces sit naturally alongside the handmade terrazzo character. In kitchens and bathrooms, the bone white base opens up the space while the scattered aggregate adds enough visual interest to prevent the floor from reading as blank.

### How do I clean Constellation Bone White terrazzo?

To clean Constellation Bone White terrazzo, vacuum or sweep regularly to remove abrasive debris. For routine cleaning, use a pH-neutral cleaner such as Miracle Sealants pH Neutral Tile and Stone Cleaner. Avoid acidic or abrasive cleaners — vinegar, lemon juice, and bleach will damage the cement matrix. On a white or near-white surface, prompt blotting of spills is especially important to prevent staining from absorbing into the cement before it is cleaned.

### What grout color works best with Constellation Bone White 24x24?

A grout matched closely to the bone white matrix — a warm white or very light cream — creates the most seamless result and allows the aggregate distribution to read as the defining surface character. A slightly warmer greige grout creates soft definition between tiles without pulling strongly against the pale base. Avoid contrasting or cool grouts on this colorway, as the high visibility of grout lines in a light field at 24x24 scale makes a mismatched grout more apparent than it would be in a darker or smaller-format tile.
