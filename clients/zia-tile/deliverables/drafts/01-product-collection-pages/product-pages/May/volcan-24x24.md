## About

Cantera is the volcanic stone that built colonial Mexico, used continuously for centuries in courtyards, fountains, and the carved facades of cathedrals and haciendas. Zia's Cantera line carries that material into a residential and commercial collection sized for both intimate rooms and broad public installations.

The line is offered in eight earthy colorways, each carrying its own mineral signature: Mesa, Caldera, Acacia, Sierra, Yuma, Sedona, Badlands, and Volcan. Formats range from 6x6 and 4x12 mosaics to 12x12, 12x24, and 24x24 floor tiles, so a single Cantera scheme can run from a textured shower wall in a primary bathroom to a hotel courtyard or restaurant patio in the same colorway.

Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with cantera, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with [California Prop 65](https://www.ziatile.com/proposition-65-warnings). Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered edges or Schluter strips.

## How It's Made

Cantera begins as a porous volcanic rock, formed over millions of years from cooled lava and ash. Solid blocks are pulled from quarries in Mexico, then sized down to the format dimensions of the line: 6x6 and 4x12 mosaics, 12x12 and 12x24 planks, and 24x24 large-format floor tiles.

Each piece is honed and finished by hand at the source. The finishing approach intentionally preserves the visible aggregate, mineral inclusions, and oxidation markings of the volcanic chemistry, which is what gives each colorway its individual mineral signature rather than a surface treatment that would mask it.

Once finished, the tiles are graded and packed for shipping. After installation, the stone continues to develop. Cantera takes on a soft patina with use, similar to marble or limestone, and the sealing protocol on the install guide protects the surface while still allowing the material to age into the room around it.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What color is Volcan 24x24 cantera?

Volcan cantera is a warm brown volcanic stone with tonal variation inherent to the material. The color shifts tile to tile as each slab carries its own distribution of aggregate, mineral inclusions, and oxidation markings within the warm brown range. At 24x24 inches, the individual character of each stone slab is more visible than in smaller formats — each tile presents a wide cross-section of the volcanic rock's mineral composition, and the variation across the full floor reads as natural, large-format stone rather than uniform tile.

### What spaces work best with the 24x24 format?

The 24x24 format works best in spaces with enough floor area and ceiling height to accommodate the scale of the tile without the installation feeling oversized. Large open-plan living areas, grand entryways, expansive bathrooms, and exterior courtyards are the most natural fits. In commercial settings, hotel lobbies, restaurant floors, and outdoor hospitality spaces benefit from the continuity and visual weight of the large format. Smaller rooms generally read better with 12x12 or 12x24 formats.

### Does Volcan 24x24 require sealing?

Yes. Cantera tile requires sealing before and after grouting. Apply 511 Porous Plus in thin layers until the stone is fully saturated before grouting, wiping off any excess before it dries. For interior installations, finish with Fila Matte Finish Protective Wax as a top coat. For outdoor installations, apply a second coat of 511 Porous Plus in place of wax — UV exposure causes wax to discolor over time.

### Can Volcan 24x24 be used in a courtyard?

Yes, in non-freeze/thaw climates only. Volcan 24x24 is suitable for exterior courtyard and patio floor applications in climates without freeze/thaw conditions. It is not suitable for outdoor installation in climates that experience snow or ice. For exterior applications, seal before and after grouting and apply a second coat of 511 Porous Plus as a protective top coat in place of wax.

### How do I install 24x24 cantera tiles correctly?

Large-format cantera tiles require a fully prepared, flat substrate — any variation in the substrate surface becomes more visible at this scale. Use a large-format tile setting mortar and back-butter each tile in addition to covering the substrate. Blend tiles from all boxes during the dry layout stage to distribute tonal and aggregate variation before any tile is set. The larger format reduces the number of cuts required but increases the difficulty of each cut, so allow additional time and plan perimeter cuts carefully during the layout stage.

### Can Volcan 24x24 be used alongside smaller Volcan formats?

Yes. Volcan 24x24 can be mixed with smaller Volcan formats — 12x12 or 12x24 — in the same installation. Using the 24x24 in a main floor field alongside 12x12 or 12x24 in a border or transition zone is a common approach for large commercial or residential projects. All Cantera formats share the same volcanic rock source and material character. Blend tiles from all boxes across all formats and confirm the full layout during the dry stage before setting.
