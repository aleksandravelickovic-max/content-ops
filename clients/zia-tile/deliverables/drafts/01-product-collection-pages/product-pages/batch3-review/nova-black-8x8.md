## About

Each cement tile carries the hand of the maker in the pigment lay, the press, and the cure: slight irregularities at the edge and small color drifts that read as a single soft matte field once the floor is set. Color shifts subtly tile to tile within every colorway, with the deeper pigments carrying broader internal contrast than the lighter cream and bone tones.

Expect tonal variation across the box, surface character that softens with patina, and small pits or air pockets in the surface, all natural to a hand-poured pigmented cement tile.

Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with cement tile, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with [California Prop 65](https://www.ziatile.com/proposition-65-warnings). Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered edges or Schluter strips.

## How It's Made

Cement tile is hand-poured in Vietnam using techniques refined by French and Catalan craftspeople in the 1850s, when pigmented cement was developed as a more affordable alternative to stone mosaic. The Vietnamese workshops preserve the original method exactly: one tile at a time, by hand.

Each tile begins with mineral pigments laid by hand into a steel pattern frame, one color at a time, on top of a powdered concrete base inside a steel mold. The mold is closed, and the tile is hydraulically pressed at 3,000 lbs psi, soaked in water, and cured on drying racks for seven days. The tile is never fired, which is what distinguishes cement tile from ceramic tile.

The 3 mm pigmented surface layer is what gives cement tile its longevity and its characteristic patina. There is real color depth in the surface, not just a topcoat, which means the tile can wear and re-seal over decades without losing its color.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does the Nova Black pattern read in a room?

The Nova Black pattern places a white eight-pointed star on a black ground — a high-contrast geometry that reads as a graphic field across the full installation rather than as an isolated motif. The 8x8 format gives the star enough scale to read at full visual strength rather than compressing the geometry into fine detail.

### Can Nova Black be used as a field tile, or is it better suited as a feature?

Nova Black reads as a complete field tile — the bold star repeat covers the floor or wall as a single unified composition rather than as a decorative accent within a neutral field. The high contrast and 8x8 scale make it strong enough to carry an entire room. That said, Nova Black can also be combined with Zia's solid black or white cement tiles to frame a defined field or create a border transition. Contact [info@ziatile.com](mailto:info@ziatile.com) to discuss pattern combinations before ordering.

### How does the 8x8 scale affect the star pattern's readability in a smaller room?

In a compact entry or bathroom, the 8x8 star reads at full graphic scale — each tile presents a complete star centered on the black ground, and the repeat resolves quickly across the space. In smaller rooms, this means the pattern registers with more visual weight per square foot than a smaller-format star pattern would. A dry layout confirms how the star geometry centers in the room and where the perimeter cuts fall. Contact [info@ziatile.com](mailto:info@ziatile.com) or 310-844-1170 before starting the layout.

### How should the Nova Black pattern be centered in a room?

Centering a repeat pattern like Nova Black starts with a dry layout before any tile is set. Snap chalk lines at the center of the room and work outward in all four directions, adjusting until the cut pieces at opposite walls are equal in width. For the star pattern, an asymmetric border cut on one wall but not the other creates a visual imbalance that is difficult to correct after installation. Zia recommends contacting [info@ziatile.com](mailto:info@ziatile.com) or 310-844-1170 before starting.

### Can Nova Black be used in a shower?

No. Nova Black is not approved for shower walls or shower floors. Contact [info@ziatile.com](mailto:info@ziatile.com) or 310-844-1170 for guidance on suitable alternatives for wet enclosure applications.

### Can Nova Black be installed outdoors?

Nova Black is suited for exterior walls in non-freeze/thaw climates. It is not approved for exterior floor applications. The tile is not suitable for installation in climates where the surface may freeze. Contact [info@ziatile.com](mailto:info@ziatile.com) for questions about outdoor wall applications.

### What grout joint width does Zia recommend for Nova Black?

Use 1/16" tile spacers for the recommended grout joint. A tight joint reads as a more continuous field and lets the black-and-white star pattern carry across the surface without interruption.

### Is Nova Black compatible with underfloor radiant heating?

Yes. Nova Black cement tile is compatible with all heated floor systems. The thermal mass of the cement body holds and releases heat gradually, which suits radiant systems well. Follow the thinset manufacturer's specifications for heated-floor installations and confirm that the subfloor is fully cured before setting tile.

### How does Nova Black cement tile develop patina with wear?

The matte pigmented surface softens and deepens with time and traffic. The black ground holds its depth well, and the contrast with the white star remains legible as the surface develops patina. In high-traffic areas, the patina develops faster and concentrates in the most-traveled zones first. Periodic resealing maintains the surface and allows the patina to develop evenly.
