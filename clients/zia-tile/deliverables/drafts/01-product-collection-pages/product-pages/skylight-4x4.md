## About

No two zellige tiles are the same. Pieces vary in size, surface, and glaze across each box, with hairline crazing and chamfered edges that come from how the material is hand-formed rather than flaws to correct.

Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with zellige, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with [California Prop 65](https://www.ziatile.com/proposition-65-warnings). Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered cuts or Schluter strips. Traditional trim and edge pieces do not exist for zellige.

Seal zellige according to our installation instructions, whether the application is dry or exposed to moisture (pools, spas, showers). Reach out to our team at [info@ziatile.com](mailto:info@ziatile.com) with any material or installation questions.

## How It's Made

Zellige is hand-formed in Morocco, where the technique has been refined for more than 800 years and passed across generations of artisans.

The process begins with raw clay, which is mined from local hillsides, soaked, filtered for impurities, and hand-pressed into molds. The tiles are laid to dry in the Moroccan sun, flattened and shaped by hand, then dried further before glazing.

Each tile is hand-dipped in glaze and arranged inside an earthen kiln, where uneven heat creates the tonal variation that defines zellige. No two pieces leave the kiln the same, and the handmade nature and variation are hallmarks of this material. To prepare for installation, open and mix all boxes before setting them to achieve an ideal layout. The tiles will carry the marks of the day they were fired.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What color is Skylight zellige?

Skylight zellige is a sky-blue tile with a glaze that shifts between pale blue, muted grey, and cooler mid-tones depending on light and viewing angle. The colorway reads brighter in natural daylight and softens slightly under artificial light. The hand-dipping process and uneven kiln heat mean individual tiles carry different tonal depths, producing a surface that reads differently across the full installation.

### What colors complement Skylight zellige?

Skylight zellige pairs well with both warm and cool palettes due to its soft blue-grey tone. Neutral materials such as cream, sand, and warm white create a restrained backdrop. Unlacquered brass and matte black fixtures tend to balance the cool hues of the glaze. In Zia's zellige collection, Skylight pairs particularly well with warm neutrals and deeper cool tones, where the contrast creates a layered, considered composition.

### Does Skylight zellige work in a small kitchen?

Yes. Skylight zellige works well in small kitchens. The glazed surface reflects light, which helps a compact space feel more open, and the soft sky-blue tones read well against both natural and artificial light. Used as a kitchen backsplash, the variation in tone and surface adds depth without overwhelming the room.

### How does Skylight zellige pair with wooden flooring?

Skylight zellige pairs well with wooden flooring. The contrast between the cool glazed surface of the tile and the warm texture of natural wood creates a grounded composition. This pairing is common in interiors that rely on natural material contrasts, where a cool surface against warm wood keeps the space from reading as uniformly one temperature.

### Can Skylight zellige be used in a pool or spa?

Yes. Skylight 4x4 is suitable for pool and spa applications in both saltwater and chlorine environments. Sealing is required; apply sealer to all tiles and grout lines exposed to continuous moisture after grouting is complete. Consult the [Zellige Installation Guide](https://ziatile.com/pages/zellige-installation-guide) for specific sealing instructions.

### Can Skylight zellige be mixed with other formats in the collection?

Yes. Skylight 4x4 can be mixed with other formats in Zia's zellige collection. Each format is produced using the same traditional methods, so tiles share consistent surface character across the range. When mixing formats, plan the layout in advance and blend tiles from multiple boxes during the dry layout stage to achieve even distribution of tone and surface variation across the full installation.
