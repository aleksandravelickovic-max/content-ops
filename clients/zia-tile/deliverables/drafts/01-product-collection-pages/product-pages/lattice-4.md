## About

No two zellige tiles are the same. The Lattice 4 mosaic combines two colorways — Pure White and Casablanca — each with a glaze that shifts subtly with changing light. Pieces vary in size, surface, and glaze across each box, with hairline crazing and chamfered edges that come from how the material is hand-formed rather than flaws to correct.

Before setting any sheet, separate and mix sheets from multiple boxes to distribute variation evenly. Lay out the full composition on a flat surface before setting to confirm how the lattice pattern reads across the field and how the two colorways are balanced. Individual tiles can be removed from the mesh backing if adjustments are needed at edges or perimeters.

Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with zellige, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with [California Prop 65](https://www.ziatile.com/proposition-65-warnings). Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered cuts or Schluter strips. Traditional trim and edge pieces do not exist for zellige. Seal the Lattice 4 mosaic according to our installation instructions, whether in dry areas or areas exposed to moisture. ## How It's Made

The Lattice 4 begins with two separate production runs, one for Pure White and one for Casablanca, each following the same process that has defined traditional zellige for more than 800 years. Raw clay sourced from Moroccan hillsides is soaked, filtered, and worked by hand until it reaches the consistency needed for pressing into molds.

Each colorway dries in the Moroccan sun before being dipped individually in glaze. Pure White and Casablanca sit close to each other in the neutral range, but the hand-dipping process means each tile develops its own tonal character. The two glazes never land in exactly the same place twice, which is what produces the subtle variation visible within the lattice pattern across a full installation.

After firing in earthen kilns — where uneven heat produces the chips, pits, crazing, and tonal shifts inherent to handmade zellige — the individual tiles from both colorways are hand-placed onto mesh sheets in the lattice configuration. That final step, arranging two distinct glazes into a single geometric pattern, is what makes each Lattice 4 sheet a small expression of the same craft tradition that produced the palace mosaics zellige was originally made for.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What colorways make up the Lattice 4 mosaic?

The Lattice 4 mosaic combines two colorways from Zia's zellige collection — Pure White and Casablanca — arranged in a lattice pattern on 8x8 mesh sheets. Both colorways are light neutrals produced using the same traditional Moroccan methods, and each carries its own subtle tonal range that shifts with changing light. The two glazes sit close enough in tone to read as a cohesive surface while remaining distinct enough to define the lattice pattern.

### What is the lattice pattern?

The Lattice 4 is a geometric lattice composition formed by the arrangement of Pure White and Casablanca zellige tiles on an 8x8 mesh sheet. The interlocking geometry references the traditional Moroccan mosaic tradition of installing zellige in decorative geometric and floral shapes, and the neutral two-color palette produces a subtle pattern that reads differently at varying distances — the lattice geometry is clearest up close, while the surface reads as a cohesive light neutral field from farther away.

### How different are Pure White and Casablanca zellige in the Lattice 4?

Pure White and Casablanca are close in tone, which is what makes the lattice pattern work as a subtle geometric rather than a high-contrast composition. Pure White carries a glaze that ranges from bright white to faint warm or cool undertones; Casablanca shifts between near-white, pale grey, and warmer off-white tones. Because both glazes are applied by hand and fired in uneven kilns, the degree of contrast between the two colorways also shifts from tile to tile and sheet to sheet.

### What is a zellige mosaic?

A zellige mosaic is a pre-arranged composition of individual handcrafted zellige tiles mounted on a mesh backing sheet. Each tile is produced using the same traditional Moroccan process as standard zellige. The mosaic format allows a fixed pattern — such as the lattice composition in the Lattice 4 — to be installed consistently across a surface without requiring the installer to arrange individual tiles on site.

### How does a mosaic installation differ from a single-tile installation?

With single zellige tiles, each piece is placed individually and the installer controls spacing and layout tile by tile. With a mesh-mounted mosaic, the pattern and spacing are already established on the sheet, and the installer sets the sheet as a unit. Sheets from multiple boxes should be separated and mixed before installation to distribute variation evenly, and individual tiles can be removed from the mesh backing if adjustments are needed at edges or perimeters.

### Does the Lattice 4 mosaic work alongside other zellige colorways?

Yes. The Lattice 4 Pure White + Casablanca mosaic works well alongside other colorways in Zia's zellige collection. Both colorways sit in the neutral range, so the mosaic pairs naturally with solid colorways used as field tile or border detail. It works particularly well alongside deeper colorways where the light neutral lattice provides a counterpoint to a more saturated field.
