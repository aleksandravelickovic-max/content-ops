## How It's Made

Cotto Allende is handmade in San Miguel de Allende, Mexico, using the same clay base as standard Cotto with a glaze applied before firing. The result is a sealed surface that opens up applications such as pools, spas, and steam showers, which aren't appropriate for unglazed terra cotta.

Natural clay is pressed into molds and sun-dried, then glazed in either gloss or satin finish before high-firing. The glaze develops fine hairline cracks during cooling, known as crazing, which is a normal characteristic of glazed clay tile and not a defect.

Color saturation is more controlled than unglazed Cotto, while the underlying clay body still produces handmade variation across the line. The finish determines how the tile reads in light, with each formulation producing a distinct surface character.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is Cotto Allende?

Cotto Allende is a glazed terra cotta tile made from natural clay in San Miguel de Allende, Mexico. The clay is hand-pressed into molds, sun-dried, and high-fired with a glaze applied before firing. This process produces a smoother, more sealed surface than unglazed cotto, with inherent variation in tone and the chips, pits, and crazing that develop as the glaze settles across the clay body during firing.

### Is Condesa 4x4 suitable for pools and spas?

Yes, Condesa 4x4 Cotto Allende is suitable for both saltwater and chlorine pool and spa installations. Sealing with 511 Porous Plus is required before and after grouting to protect both the tile surface and grout under continuous water exposure. Do not use Fila Matte Wax on Cotto Allende — 511 Porous Plus is the only recommended sealer for this line.

### Is Condesa 4x4 suitable for flooring?

Yes, Condesa 4x4 is suitable for flooring in both residential and commercial settings. A minimum 1/8" grout joint is required to support alignment and installation accuracy across the field. Cotto Allende has a DCOF of 0.48. In wet floor applications, slip resistance can be increased by maintaining this grout joint size and/or applying an anti-slip treatment after installation is complete.

### How do I clean Cotto Allende tile?

To clean Cotto Allende, use a pH-neutral cleaner with warm water and a soft cloth. Avoid acidic or abrasive products, which can affect the glazed surface and make crazing more visible over time. Sweep or vacuum regularly to remove abrasive debris. Resealing with 511 Porous Plus is required in wet and high-traffic areas to maintain surface performance.

### How long do terra cotta tiles last?

Terra cotta tile lasts for decades when installed and maintained correctly. Cotto Allende develops a natural patina over time while maintaining its structural integrity under regular use. Long-term performance depends on proper substrate preparation, consistent maintenance, and adherence to sealing requirements at each stage of installation.

### Can Condesa 4x4 be mixed with other Cotto Allende colorways?

Yes. Condesa 4x4 can be combined with other colorways in the Cotto Allende collection. Each colorway is produced using the same clay body and firing process, so tiles share consistent thickness, format, and surface character across the range. The deep brown of Condesa pairs well against lighter colorways such as Puebla or Cacao to create contrast across the field.
