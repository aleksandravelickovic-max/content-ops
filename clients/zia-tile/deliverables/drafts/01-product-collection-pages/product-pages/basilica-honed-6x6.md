## How It's Made

Limestone is quarried from solid blocks near Fez, Morocco, and around Bordeaux, France, in workshops where the material has been cut and honed for centuries. Each block is sized down to the format dimensions and finished by stone masons at the source.

The honed finish is achieved by gently smoothing the surface to a clean, soft matte. The bush-hammered finish is achieved by striking the face with a textured hammer head, which uniformly pits the surface for a more rustic, aged character. Both finishes preserve the natural mineral signature of the stone.

Limestone is a living material. Subtle tonal variation across pieces is inherent to natural stone, and no two tiles are identical. The soft patina that develops over years of use is a defining feature of the material and the reason limestone has carried so many of the floors that survive from antiquity into the present.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What color is Basilica limestone?

Basilica is a cool grey limestone — a softer, cooler neutral than Buff (warm cream-to-tan) or Monument (soft neutral grey). The honed finish produces a smooth, matte surface that softens reflectivity while keeping the stone's mineral markings visible. Natural tonal variation is present across each tile, including subtle fossil impressions and mineral inclusions inherent to sedimentary stone.

### How does Basilica differ from Monument limestone?

Basilica and Monument are both cool-toned honed limestones quarried near Fez, Morocco, finished to the same smooth matte surface and following the same installation protocol. Monument reads as a soft neutral grey with subdued mineral tones. Basilica carries a cooler, slightly deeper grey that can read as more architectural in direct comparison. Both are offered in the 6x6 square format, and the distinction is primarily one of tonal temperature and depth.

### What design styles suit Basilica limestone?

Basilica works across modern, transitional, and classical interiors because the honed finish holds quiet while still contributing material depth. In contemporary spaces, the cool grey supports minimal layouts with clean architectural lines. In traditional and rustic environments, the stone introduces softness and earthy texture without visual heaviness. The consistency of the honed finish is what makes it versatile across design directions.

### Can Basilica 6x6 be used outdoors?

Yes. Basilica 6x6 can be used on exterior floors and walls in non-freeze/thaw climates. It is not suitable for outdoor installations where snow or ice are present. For exterior applications, seal with a second coat of 511 Porous Plus rather than Fila Matte Wax — UV exposure causes wax to discolor outdoors.

### Can Basilica 6x6 be used on a shower floor?

Yes. Basilica 6x6 can be used on shower floors and shower walls. Honed limestone has a DCOF of 0.50, which meets the wet area slip resistance threshold. Seal the tile before and after grouting as required, and size grout joints to a minimum of ⅛" to support traction underfoot. An anti-slip product can be applied after installation to further increase slip resistance in high-exposure areas.

### How do I care for Basilica limestone?

To care for Basilica limestone, seal the stone before and after grouting to reduce absorption and protect against staining. Ongoing maintenance requires pH-neutral stone cleaners and regular dry removal of dust and grit to prevent surface abrasion. Avoid acidic and abrasive products — including vinegar, bleach, and citrus-based cleaners — which can etch the stone permanently. Periodic resealing is required based on traffic and exposure.
