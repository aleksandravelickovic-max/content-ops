# Brief: Bathroom Tile Ideas

**Client:** Zia Tile
**Type:** Blog
**Material:** Multi-material (zellige, marble, limestone, Japanese ceramics)
**Slug:** bathroom-tile-ideas

---

## Target keyword

Primary: bathroom tile ideas
Secondary: marble bathroom tile, zellige bathroom, ceramic bathroom tile, limestone bathroom tile

## Search intent

Informational / inspiration. Reader is planning a bathroom renovation or new build and wants guidance on material options, design considerations, and practical performance specs.

## Zia angle

Showcase four core Zia materials suited to bathroom settings. Each material gets its own section approached from a different angle: origin and craft, performance specs, application guidance. Classic arc (zellige, marble, limestone) to contemporary (Japanese ceramics).

## Title

H1: Bathroom Tile Ideas
No subtitle. No colon pattern. SEO keyword in H1.

## Person

Third person throughout. "Zia's zellige," "the collection," "the Zia team."

## Structure

1. Intro (~80 words): what the bathroom asks of tile (moisture, wear, light, longevity)
2. H2: zellige in the bathroom (Moroccan craft, shower walls, backsplash, pool/spa approved)
3. H2: Marble floors and walls (quarried stone, honed for wet floors, named stones)
4. H2: Limestone's natural character (Fez + Bordeaux, two finishes, DCOF specs)
5. H2: Japanese ceramics for the contemporary bathroom (Nagoya, finish-specific DCOF, interior only)
6. H2: A note on finish and selection (DCOF benchmark, project contact)
7. Closing paragraph: material qualities over surface appearance; contact line

## Key facts to include

- zellige: chips, pits, and crazing (trio); dynamic glazes; pools/spas suitable; NOT freeze/thaw; sealing required
- Marble: Carrara, Nero Marquina, Grigio Carnico, Rosso Alicante from Italy/Spain; honed for wet floors; NOT pools/spas; outdoor OK in non-F/T climates; sealing required
- Limestone: Fez/Morocco + Bordeaux/France; honed DCOF 0.50, bush-hammered 0.56; two-step sealing; NOT pools/spas; NOT freeze/thaw outdoors
- Ceramics: Nagoya, 1,000+ years; matte DCOF 0.74 (floors + walls), satin 0.45 (floors + walls), glossy 0.30 (walls only); NOT pools/spas; NOT exterior

## Hard rules

- No banned terms: products, sources, shower surround, vary slightly, normal for, most often specified, the correct choice, luxury, sophisticated, truly, simply, laid, built to, recessive
- No em dashes
- No second person
- No colon-subtitle title
- zellige always lowercase; never at sentence start
- No invention: only confirmed colorways and specs
- Contact line (email + phone) in closing section
