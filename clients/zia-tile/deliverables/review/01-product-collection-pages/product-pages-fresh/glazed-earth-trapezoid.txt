About
No two zellige tiles are the same. Pieces vary in size, surface, and glaze across each box, with hairline crazing and chamfered edges that come from how the material is hand-formed rather than flaws to correct.


Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with zellige, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with California Prop 65. Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered cuts or Schluter strips. Traditional trim and edge pieces do not exist for zellige.


Seal zellige according to our installation instructions, whether the application is dry or exposed to moisture (pools, spas, showers). Reach out to our team at info@ziatile.com with any material or installation questions.

How It's Made
Zellige is hand-formed in Morocco, where the technique has been refined for more than 800 years and passed across generations of artisans.


The process begins with raw clay, which is mined from local hillsides, soaked, filtered for impurities, and hand-pressed into molds. The tiles are laid to dry in the Moroccan sun, flattened and shaped by hand, then dried further before glazing.


Each tile is hand-dipped in glaze and arranged inside an earthen kiln, where uneven heat creates the tonal variation that defines zellige. No two pieces leave the kiln the same, and the handmade nature and variation are hallmarks of this material. To prepare for installation, open and mix all boxes before setting them to achieve an ideal layout. The tiles will carry the marks of the day they were fired.

Frequently Asked Questions
What color is Glazed Earth trapezoid zellige?
Glazed Earth is a warm beige-neutral zellige glaze that ranges across the earth tone spectrum, with each tile carrying its own position through the hand-dipping and kiln-firing process. Those differences distribute across the surface as the installation grows. Blending tiles from all boxes before the dry layout is essential for even distribution of that variation.
How does the trapezoid format affect the Glazed Earth pattern?
The trapezoid interlocks with adjacent tiles to form a repeating scale-like geometric structure across the installation. In horizontal orientation, the angled sides stack to produce a layered horizontal field; in vertical orientation, the composition takes on more directional movement. The full pattern only resolves across multiple tiles, so confirming the intended orientation and layout during the dry stage before setting begins is required.
What makes the trapezoid format different from square and rectangular zellige?
The trapezoid is the only format in Zia's zellige line that builds a geometric pattern through the shape of the tile itself, rather than through grout joint arrangement. Where a 4x4 square or 2x6 Bejmat produces its visual rhythm through layout pattern, the trapezoid's angled sides interlock to create a scale-like geometric field that is inherent to the format. The chips, pits, and crazing characteristic of glazed zellige are present across the trapezoid surface in the same way as other formats, and the same variation and blending principles apply.
Can Glazed Earth trapezoid be used in a pool?
Yes. Glazed Earth trapezoid is suitable for pool and spa applications in both saltwater and chlorine environments. Sealing is required for all zellige installations; for pools and spas, apply 511 Porous Plus Penetrating Sealer after grouting is complete and allow it to cure fully before the installation is exposed to water. Because the trapezoid requires consistent piece orientation to resolve the pattern correctly, confirm the full pool layout during the dry stage before setting begins. 
Can Glazed Earth Trapezoid zellige be used in a shower?
Yes. Glazed Earth trapezoid can be used on shower walls and shower floors. Sealing is required for all shower applications. Glazed zellige has a DCOF of 0.16, below the 0.42 wet-floor threshold for level interior floors. Size grout joints to a minimum of 1/8 inch and apply an anti-slip treatment after installation to increase slip resistance on shower floors. Refer to the Zellige Installation Guide for full instructions.
Can Glazed Earth trapezoid be mixed with other Glazed Earth formats?
Yes. Glazed Earth trapezoid can be mixed with other Glazed Earth formats, including the 2x6 Bejmat and 4x4 square, or with other colorways in Zia's zellige collection. All formats share the same traditional production methods and consistent surface character within the colorway. Plan any format-mixed layout during the dry stage to confirm how the trapezoid pattern transitions into adjacent field formats before setting begins.
