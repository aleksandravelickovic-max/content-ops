Zia's Gambit 1 is a handcrafted Moroccan zellige mosaic built from small geometric elements in Casablanca and Pure White glazes, mesh-mounted on 8x8 sheets for installation. Artisans historically installed zellige in mosaics featuring decorative geometric and floral shapes within elaborately designed palaces.


Our Gambit 1 zellige mosaic is hand-formed, dipped in glaze, and fired in earthen kilns in Morocco, where the method has been refined over more than 800 years. Each tile carries the chips, pits, hairline crazing, and tonal shifts that come from working at this scale by hand. We recommend ordering 15–25% overage so the installation has room for hand-selection, color blending, and the tile breakage inherent to the material. Before laying any piece, unpack every box and mix the tiles on a flat surface to plan the layout and confirm the color balance.


Zellige tile installation is labor-intensive, often likened more to masonry than traditional tiling due to the irregular nature of the handmade material. The traditional method sets pieces edge-to-edge with minimal grout, but irregular shapes mean wedge spacers help keep layouts consistent and protect chamfered edges as the tiles are set. Plan for installations 2–3 times longer than standard ceramic. Every piece needs to be sorted, soaked, arranged, and hand-adjusted for size.

About
No two zellige tiles are the same. Pieces vary in size, surface, and glaze across each box, with hairline crazing and chamfered edges that come from how the material is hand-formed rather than flaws to correct.


Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with zellige, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with California Prop 65. Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered cuts or Schluter strips. Traditional trim and edge pieces do not exist for zellige.


Seal zellige according to our installation instructions, whether the application is dry or exposed to moisture (pools, spas, showers). Reach out to our team at info@ziatile.com with any material or installation questions.

How It's Made
Zellige is hand-formed in Morocco, where the technique has been refined for more than 800 years and passed across generations of artisans.
The process begins with raw clay, which is mined from local hillsides, soaked, filtered for impurities, and hand-pressed into molds. The tiles are laid to dry in the Moroccan sun, flattened and shaped by hand, then dried further before glazing.
Each tile is hand-dipped in glaze and arranged inside an earthen kiln, where uneven heat creates the tonal variation that defines zellige. No two pieces leave the kiln the same and the handmade nature and variation are hallmarks of this most special material. 
To prepare for installation, open and mix all boxes before setting them to achieve an ideal layout. The tiles will carry the marks of the day they were fired.

Frequently Asked Questions
Can I use Gambit 1 zellige mosaic in a shower?
Yes. Gambit 1 can be used on shower walls, shower floors, and steam showers. Sealing is required for all shower applications. For shower floors, grout joints should measure at least 1/8" and an anti-slip treatment should be applied after installation. The mesh-mounted format contributes to surface traction through higher grout coverage per square foot, making it particularly suited to shower floor applications.
Is Gambit 1 zellige mosaic good for floors?
Yes. Gambit 1 works on interior floors, particularly in low-traffic residential areas such as guest bathrooms and bedrooms. The mosaic format supports slip resistance through increased grout density, and performance improves further when grout joints measure 1/8" and an anti-slip treatment is applied after installation. High-traffic floor installations require careful planning to accommodate the inherent variation in thickness and edge profile.
Can Gambit 1 zellige be used on exterior walls?
Yes. Gambit 1 can be used on exterior walls in non-freeze/thaw climates. The mosaic performs well on vertical surfaces where the glazed finish and variation are protected from direct structural impact. It is not suitable for exterior floor installations in any climate. Sealing is required for all exterior applications.
Can I mix Gambit 1 with other zellige colors?
Yes. Gambit 1 can be mixed with other colorways in Zia's zellige collection. Each colorway is produced using the same traditional methods, so tiles and mosaic sheets share consistent surface character across the range. The white Gambit 1 glaze works well as a lighter, patterned element alongside deeper solid colorways, creating visual layering without overwhelming the composition.
What sealing product should I use on Gambit 1 zellige?
Gambit 1 requires sealing in all installations, including dry interior spaces. Apply 511 Porous Plus Penetrating Sealer after grouting. For interior installations, follow with Fila Matte Finish Protective Wax as a topcoat. For exterior installations, apply a second coat of 511 Porous Plus instead of the wax, as Fila Matte Wax discolors under UV exposure. Consult the Zellige Installation Guide for specific application instructions and product details, or contact info@ziatile.com or 310-844-1170 with questions.
How much Gambit 1 do I need?
Start with the square footage of your installation and add 15 to 20%. Mosaic sheets require more overage than single-tile zellige because individual tiles will be removed and reset at edges, corners, and transitions. Open all boxes and mix sheets together before the layout begins. Arrange them by thickness so the surface reads as level once set.
