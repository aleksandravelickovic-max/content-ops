Order Details + Installation
Marble is a stone with significant variations in color, tone, veining, and surface texture, along with naturally occurring imperfections. Images shown are representative of a sample of this material and may not display all potential variation. A minimum of 15–20% overage is recommended to account for curation and cuts, though additional overage allows for an even more controlled appearance.
Careful installation is essential with this soft, porous stone. Before setting any marble, unpack and mix pieces from all boxes to balance color and veining across the layout. Use a thinset suitable for natural stone and ensure full back-buttering for complete coverage, then seal the stone twice – before and after grouting. Clean only with pH-neutral products, resealing periodically as needed.

About
Marble has been quarried continuously since Roman antiquity, and Zia's marble is cut from blocks pulled from quarries across Italy and Spain. Each block is finished near Verona, where stone masons hone the surface to the soft matte finish that defines the line.


The line includes six core colorways: Carrara, Nero Marquina, Grigio Carnico, Rosso Alicante, Giallo Reale, and Verde Alpi. Together, they cover the full range of designed marble installations, from the quiet veining of Carrara to the deep black field of Nero Marquina and the warm ochres of Giallo Reale, and they pair cleanly with the patterned compositions in our Marble Patterns line.


Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with marble, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with California Prop 65. Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered edges or Schluter strips.

How It's Made
Marble begins as an ancient metamorphic rock, compressed under heat and pressure over millions of years. Zia's marble range is quarried across Italy and Spain, and Italy has been the principal source of architectural marble in the West since Roman antiquity.


Each block is cut into the format dimensions and honed by stone masons in workshops near Verona, producing the soft matte surface that defines the line. Veining and mineral variation are inherent to the stone, and no two pieces are identical.


Marble is a living material. It develops a natural patina with use, taking on the soft luster that has carried Roman floors and Renaissance halls into the present, and that becomes part of the story of any room it is set into.

Frequently Asked Questions
What does Grigio Carnico marble look like?
Grigio Carnico is an Italian marble with tonal variation inherent to each piece through the natural mineral composition of the stone. No two pieces carry identical coloring or veining, and those differences distribute across the installation as pieces from all boxes are blended before the dry layout. The honed finish diffuses light evenly across the surface without reflectivity.
Is Grigio Carnico suitable for a shower floor?
Yes, on both shower walls and shower floors. Grigio Carnico has a DCOF of 0.62, well above the 0.42 wet-floor threshold. Apply 511 Porous Plus before grouting and allow the sealer to cure fully before grouting begins. After the grout has cured, apply Fila Matte Finish Protective Wax as the final top coat for interior wet areas. Size grout joints to a minimum of 1/8 inch for shower floor applications. Press pieces into place with the palm only during installation, as rubber mallets can create micro-fractures in the stone that develop over time.
How do I seal Grigio Carnico marble?
Apply 511 Porous Plus in thin layers until the stone is fully saturated before grouting, wiping off any excess before it dries. The sealer must cure fully before grouting begins. For interior installations, apply Fila Matte Finish Protective Wax as the final top coat after the grout has cured. For exterior installations, apply a second coat of 511 Porous Plus in place of wax, as UV exposure causes wax to discolor over time. Reseal as needed based on traffic and moisture exposure. See the Marble Installation Guide for the full sealing sequence.
Can Grigio Carnico 12x24 be used outdoors?
Yes, in non-freeze/thaw climates only. Grigio Carnico 12x24 is suitable for exterior floors and walls in climates that do not experience freeze/thaw conditions. For all exterior applications, seal with 511 Porous Plus before grouting and apply a second coat of 511 Porous Plus as the final top coat after the grout has cured. Do not use Fila Matte Finish Protective Wax outdoors.
How do I clean Grigio Carnico marble?
Grigio Carnico requires regular sweeping or vacuuming to remove grit and abrasive debris. For routine maintenance, use a pH-neutral cleaner with warm water. Blot spills immediately rather than wiping. For heavier staining, use Miracle Sealants Heavy Duty Cleaner (Acid Substitute). Avoid vinegar, lemon juice, and bleach, as acidic cleaners etch and damage the marble surface over time.
Can Grigio Carnico 12x24 be mixed with other Zia marble colorways or formats?
Yes. Grigio Carnico 12x24 can be combined with other colorways and formats across Zia's marble line. All marble formats are cut to consistent dimensions within each format size group, so colorways and formats can be combined across a single installation with planning during the dry layout stage. Grigio Carnico also appears in the patterned compositions in Zia's Marble Patterns line, allowing pattern-set combinations in the same installation. Blend pieces from all boxes within each colorway before setting to ensure even tonal distribution. For questions, contact us at info@ziatile.com or 310-844-1170.
