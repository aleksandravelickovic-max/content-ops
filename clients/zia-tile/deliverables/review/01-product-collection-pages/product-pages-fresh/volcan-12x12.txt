About
Cantera is the volcanic stone that built colonial Mexico, used continuously for centuries in courtyards, fountains, and the carved facades of cathedrals and haciendas. Zia's Cantera line carries that material into a residential and commercial collection sized for both intimate rooms and broad public installations.


The line is offered in eight earthy colorways, each carrying its own mineral signature: Mesa, Caldera, Acacia, Sierra, Yuma, Sedona, Badlands, and Volcan. Formats range from 6x6 and 4x12 mosaics to 12x12, 12x24, and 24x24 floor tiles, so a single Cantera scheme can run from a textured shower wall in a primary bathroom to a hotel courtyard or restaurant patio in the same colorway.


Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with Cantera, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with California Prop 65. Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered edges or Schluter strips.

How It's Made
Cantera begins as a porous volcanic rock, formed over millions of years from cooled lava and ash. Solid blocks are pulled from quarries in Mexico, then sized down to the format dimensions of the line: 6x6 and 4x12 mosaics, 12x12 and 12x24 planks, and 24x24 large-format floor tiles.


Each piece is honed and finished by hand at the source. The finishing approach intentionally preserves the visible aggregate, mineral inclusions, and oxidation markings of the volcanic chemistry, which is what gives each colorway its individual mineral signature rather than a surface treatment that would mask it.


Once finished, the tiles are graded and packed for shipping. After installation, the stone continues to develop. Cantera takes on a soft patina with use, similar to marble or limestone, and the sealing protocol on the install guide protects the surface while still allowing the material to age into the room around it.

Frequently Asked Questions
What color is Volcan cantera?
Volcan Cantera is a warm brown volcanic stone with tonal variation built into the material itself. The color is set by the natural mineral composition of the volcanic rock, which produces differences in tone, aggregate distribution, and oxidation character from tile to tile. Each piece carries its own arrangement of mineral inclusions, oxidation markings, and aggregate within the warm brown range, which means no two tiles present the same surface.
Does Volcan 12x12 require sealing?
Yes. Cantera tile requires sealing before and after grouting. Apply 511 Porous Plus in thin layers until the stone is fully saturated before grouting, wiping off any excess before it dries. The sealer must fully cure before grouting begins. For interior installations, apply Fila Matte Finish Protective Wax as a top coat after the grout has cured. For outdoor installations, apply a second coat of 511 Porous Plus in place of wax, as UV exposure causes wax to discolor over time. Cantera should be resealed periodically based on traffic and wear. See the Cantera Installation Guide for the full sealing sequence.
Can Volcan 12x12 be used outdoors?
Yes, in non-freeze/thaw climates only. Volcan 12x12 is suitable for exterior floor and wall applications in climates that do not experience freeze/thaw conditions. The porous volcanic stone body absorbs water during freeze/thaw cycling, which causes the material to crack and spall. Volcan 12x12 is not suitable for any outdoor installation where the surface may get snow or ice. For all exterior applications, seal with 511 Porous Plus before grouting and apply a second coat of 511 Porous Plus as the top coat after the grout has cured. Do not use Fila Matte Wax outdoors. See the Cantera Installation Guide for the full outdoor sealing sequence.


How does cantera differ from other natural stones? 
Cantera is a volcanic stone, which distinguishes it from sedimentary stones like limestone and metamorphic stones like marble. The volcanic formation process produces a surface defined by visible aggregate, mineral inclusions, and oxidation markings that shift from tile to tile. Cantera has a DCOF of 0.53 to 0.63 across the colorway range, is relatively lightweight for a natural stone, and develops a natural patina with use. Because the surface is unglazed and porous, sealing before and after grouting is required.
How do I clean Volcan 12x12 cantera? 
Volcan 12x12 requires regular sweeping or vacuuming to remove abrasive debris that can scratch the surface. For routine cleaning, use a pH-neutral cleaner such as Miracle Sealants pH Neutral Tile and Stone Cleaner. For heavier staining, Miracle Sealants Heavy Duty Cleaner (Acid Substitute) is the recommended product. Avoid acidic cleaners, including vinegar, lemon juice, and bleach, as these damage the stone surface over time. Blot spills promptly rather than wiping.
Can Volcan 12x12 be mixed with other Volcan formats? 
Yes. Volcan 12x12 can be mixed with other Volcan formats, including the 4x12, 12x24, and 24x24. All formats are cut from the same volcanic rock source and share consistent material character across the colorway. Blend tiles from all boxes, regardless of format, during the dry layout stage before setting begins.
