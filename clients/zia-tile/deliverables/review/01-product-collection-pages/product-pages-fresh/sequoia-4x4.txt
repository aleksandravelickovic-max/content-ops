About
Zia's ceramics are the design counterpoint to our handmade zellige. They were developed with a Japanese potters' village outside Nagoya, where high-firing extends back more than 1,000 years, to produce a tile precise enough for tight grout joints and clean layouts. 
The collection spans matte, satin, and glossy finishes plus the textured Field Trip: Japan series, in three core formats: 2x8 and 2x4 rectangles and 4x4 squares. Color and tone vary slightly across high-fired ceramics — less than handmade zellige, but enough that mixing boxes before the set still matters. 
Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with ceramic, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with California Prop 65. Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered edges or Schluter strips.
Sealing requirements vary by finish: matte ceramics must be sealed before grouting as a grout release; satin and glossy finishes do not require pre-grout sealing. For any material or installation questions, reach out to our team at info@ziatile.com.

How It's Made 
Ceramic tile is designed in Los Angeles and made in Japan, where the line is produced using high-temperature firing and precision glaze application. The result is a clean, precisely sized tile with consistent color across boxes, a different product category from handmade clay tile.
Clay is molded into shape, dried, glazed, and fired at high temperature. The matte, satin, and gloss finishes are achieved through different glaze formulations, each producing distinct surface character and slip-resistance performance.
Each format is produced to consistent dimensions across the line, which allows for predictable installation and clean grout lines. Subtle variation in color and surface is normal and reflects the hand-finishing step in production.

Frequently Asked Questions 
What is the Sequoia colorway?
Sequoia is a tranquil, muted green with earthy warmth and no strong blue or yellow cast. In satin finish, the surface carries a subtle sheen that enriches the color without the reflectivity of high-gloss. The 4x4 square format creates an even grid suited to floors, bathroom walls, backsplashes, and shower enclosures.
What is the difference between satin and matte finish?
Satin finish bridges matte and glossy. Matte reads as completely flat and non-reflective. Satin introduces a modest sheen that deepens color perception while stopping short of high-gloss intensity. For slip resistance, satin ceramic measures DCOF 0.45, which outperforms glossy (0.30) but trails matte (0.74). This positions satin as suitable for wet floors when grout joints are sized appropriately.
Does Sequoia 4x4 require sealing?
No. Satin ceramic does not need pre-grout sealing. The satin glaze surface is dense enough to resist grout haze bonding, unlike matte finishes. In wet applications such as shower floors and walls, seal the grout lines after installation to guard against moisture penetration and staining.
Can Sequoia 4x4 be used on a shower floor?
Yes. Sequoia 4x4 works on shower floors and walls. The DCOF of 0.45 meets wet area safety thresholds. Set grout joints to a minimum of 1/8" for underfoot traction. Apply an anti-slip treatment after installation if your specific application requires additional slip resistance.
Can Sequoia 4x4 be installed on an exterior floor?
No. Sequoia 4x4 is not rated for exterior floors in any climate. It can be used on exterior walls in non-freeze/thaw climates only. Contact info@ziatile.com or 310-844-1170 before ordering if you are planning an outdoor installation.
How do I clean satin ceramic tile?
Use warm water with pH-neutral cleaner for routine upkeep. Vacuum or sweep regularly to prevent grit from abrading the surface. Blot spills immediately rather than wiping. Steer clear of acidic cleaners, including vinegar, lemon juice, and bleach, which compromise the glaze. For stubborn marks, Miracle Sealants Heavy Duty Cleaner (Acid Substitute) works well.
