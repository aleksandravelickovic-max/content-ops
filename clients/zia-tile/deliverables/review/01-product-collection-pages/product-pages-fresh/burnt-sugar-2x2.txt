We recommend ordering 15–25% overage so the installation has room for hand-selection, color blending, and the tile breakage inherent to the material. Before laying any piece, unpack every box and mix the tiles on a flat surface to plan the layout and confirm the color balance.


Zellige tile installation is labor-intensive, often likened more to masonry than traditional tiling due to the irregular nature of the handmade material. The traditional method sets pieces edge-to-edge with minimal grout, but irregular shapes mean wedge spacers help keep layouts consistent and protect chamfered edges as the tiles are set. Plan for installations 2–3 times longer than standard ceramic. Every piece needs to be sorted, soaked, arranged, and hand-adjusted for size.

About
No two zellige tiles are the same. Pieces vary in size, surface, and glaze across each box, with hairline crazing and chamfered edges that come from how the material is hand-formed rather than flaws to correct.


Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with zellige, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with California Prop 65. Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered cuts or Schluter strips. Traditional trim and edge pieces do not exist for zellige.


Seal zellige according to our installation instructions, whether the application is dry or exposed to moisture (pools, spas, showers). Reach out to our team at info@ziatile.com with any material or installation questions.

How It's Made
Zellige is hand-formed in Morocco, where the technique has been refined for more than 800 years and passed across generations of artisans.


The process begins with raw clay, which is mined from local hillsides, soaked, filtered for impurities, and hand-pressed into molds. The tiles are laid to dry in the Moroccan sun, flattened and shaped by hand, then dried further before glazing.


Each tile is hand-dipped in glaze and arranged inside an earthen kiln, where uneven heat creates the tonal variation that defines zellige. No two pieces leave the kiln the same, and the handmade nature and variation are hallmarks of this material. To prepare for installation, open and mix all boxes before setting them to achieve an ideal layout. The tiles will carry the marks of the day they were fired.

Frequently Asked Questions
Can I use Burnt Sugar 2x2 as a kitchen backsplash?
Yes. Burnt Sugar 2x2 is commonly installed as a kitchen backsplash, where the dark brown glaze provides contrast against lighter cabinetry and countertops. The small format creates the dense, mosaic-like field associated with traditional Moroccan zellige. Proper sealing is required, and routine cleaning with a pH-neutral cleaner is recommended in areas exposed to cooking oils, steam, and heat.
Is Burnt Sugar 2x2 suitable for bathroom floors?
Yes. Burnt Sugar 2x2 can be used on bathroom floors in both low- and high-traffic areas. The compact format contributes to slip resistance through higher grout joint density, making it appropriate for shower floors and wet-area installations. For wet floor applications, grout joints should measure at least 1/8" and an anti-slip treatment should be applied after installation.
Does Burnt Sugar zellige require sealing?
Yes. Sealing is required in all zellige installations, including dry interior walls. Apply sealer before grouting according to the Zellige Installation Guide. For pool and spa installations, apply sealer to all tiles and grout lines after grouting and allow full cure before filling. Reach out to info@ziatile.com or 310-844-1170 with any sealing or installation questions.
What grout color works best with Burnt Sugar zellige?
A grout color matched closely to the mid-range brown of the glaze produces a cohesive surface, allowing the tonal variation across tiles to read as a continuous field. Contrasting lighter grout defines each tile individually and draws attention to the mosaic format. Use Mapei Ultracolor Plus FA, which works well with glazed zellige and cleans off without staining if wiped promptly after application.
Does Burnt Sugar 2x2 work in a pool or spa?
Yes. Burnt Sugar 2x2 is suitable for pool and spa applications in both saltwater and chlorine environments. Sealing is required after grouting for all tiles and grout lines exposed to continuous moisture. Refer to the Zellige Installation Guide for specific sealing instructions, or reach out to info@ziatile.com or 310-844-1170 with project-specific questions.
What are chips, pits, and crazing on Burnt Sugar zellige?
Chips, pits, and crazing are the signature of handmade zellige. Chips and pits occur naturally during sun-drying and hand-shaping. Crazing appears as fine hairline patterns across the glaze, a direct result of how the earthen kiln fires each tile differently.
