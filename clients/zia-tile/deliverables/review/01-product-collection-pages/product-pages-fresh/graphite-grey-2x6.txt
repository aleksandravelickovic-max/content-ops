About
No two zellige tiles are the same. Pieces vary in size, surface, and glaze across each box, with hairline crazing and chamfered edges that come from how the material is hand-formed rather than flaws to correct.


Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with zellige, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with California Prop 65. Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered cuts or Schluter strips. Traditional trim and edge pieces do not exist for zellige.


Seal zellige according to our installation instructions, whether the application is dry or exposed to moisture (pools, spas, showers). Contact Zia's team at info@ziatile.com or 310-844-1170 with any material or installation questions.

How It's Made
Zellige is hand-formed in Morocco, where the technique has been refined for more than 800 years and passed across generations of artisans.


The process begins with raw clay, which is mined from local hillsides, soaked, filtered for impurities, and hand-pressed into molds. The tiles are laid to dry in the Moroccan sun, flattened and shaped by hand, then dried further before glazing.


Each tile is hand-dipped in glaze and arranged inside an earthen kiln, where uneven heat creates the tonal variation that defines zellige. No two pieces leave the kiln the same, and the handmade nature and variation are hallmarks of this material. To prepare for installation, open and mix all boxes before setting them to achieve an ideal layout. The tiles will carry the marks of the day they were fired.

Frequently Asked Questions
What color is Graphite Grey 2x6 zellige?
Graphite Grey 2x6 zellige carries a complex glaze that moves between blue, pink, and grey tones depending on the light and the tile. Individual pieces hold different positions within that range, and those differences distribute across the installation as the field grows. The result is a surface that reads as grey from a distance and reveals considerably more tonal complexity at close range. Blending tiles from all boxes before the dry layout is essential for even tonal distribution across the finished surface.
What is the Bejmat format?
Bejmat is the traditional Moroccan name for this elongated rectangular zellige format, sized here at 2x6 inches. The elongated rectangle offers more directional flexibility than a square format. The same tile can produce very different installations depending on whether it is laid horizontally in running bond, vertically, or in a pattern like herringbone. Layout direction should be established during the dry stage before setting begins, as the full visual effect of the pattern only resolves across multiple tiles.
What layout patterns work best with Graphite Grey 2x6 zellige?
Running bond in horizontal orientation aligns joints in a consistent rhythm and creates a structured directional field across the surface. Herringbone introduces more visual movement and brings out tonal variation in the glaze by presenting adjacent tiles at opposing angles, making individual glaze differences more visible across the field. Vertical stack produces a more formal, column-like reading. In all patterns, plan the layout during the dry stage with tiles spread out to confirm the intended direction and tonal distribution before setting begins. See the Zellige Installation Guide for layout and installation guidance. 
Can Graphite Grey 2x6 zellige be used in a shower?
Yes. Graphite Grey 2x6 can be used on shower walls, shower floors, and steam showers. Sealing is required for all shower applications. Glazed zellige has a DCOF of 0.16, below the 0.42 wet-floor threshold for level interior floors. Size grout joints to a minimum of 1/8 inch and apply an anti-slip treatment after installation to increase slip resistance on shower floors. See the Zellige Installation Guide for the full wet-area sealing sequence.
What makes Graphite Grey zellige different from a standard grey tile?
Graphite Grey is a glazed zellige tile, which means the grey is not a single flat color applied to a uniform surface. The glaze moves between blue, pink, and grey tones across individual tiles, and the hand-dipping and earthen kiln process produces chips, pits, and crazing as inherent surface characteristics. Each tile presents a different position within the colorway's range, and the full depth of the glaze only becomes apparent once tiles from multiple boxes are mixed and spread across the dry layout.
Can Graphite Grey 2x6 be mixed with other zellige formats in the same installation?
Yes. Graphite Grey 2x6 Bejmat can be mixed with other formats in Zia's zellige collection, including the 4x4 square in the same or a complementary colorway. All zellige formats are produced using the same traditional methods and share consistent surface character across the line. When mixing formats, plan the full layout during the dry stage before setting any tile, and blend tiles from all boxes within each format to confirm even tonal distribution. For questions about format combinations, contact us at info@ziatile.com or 310-844-1170.
