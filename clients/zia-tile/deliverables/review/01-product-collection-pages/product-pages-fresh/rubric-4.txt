About
No two zellige mosaic sheets read alike. Tiles vary in size, surface, and glaze across each box, with hairline crazing and chamfered edges that come from how the material is hand-formed rather than flaws to correct. Open every box in your shipment and arrange a trial layout on a flat surface before setting tile, so the color balance and variation settle into the layout you want.


Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with zellige, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with California Prop 65. Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered cuts or Schluter strips. Traditional trim and edge pieces do not exist for zellige.


Seal the zellige mosaic according to our installation instructions, whether the application is dry or exposed to moisture (pools, spas, showers). Reach out to our team at info@ziatile.com with any material or installation questions.

How It's Made
Zellige mosaic is handmade in Morocco using the same traditional techniques as field tile, refined by artisans whose families have practiced the craft for generations. The mosaic format is hand-placed on mesh backing for installation as designed sheets.


The process begins with raw clay mined from local Moroccan hillsides. Artisans soak the clay in water and filter it for impurities, then hand-press the resulting paste into molds before laying the tiles to dry in the Moroccan sun. Each tile is flattened and shaped by hand before glazing.


Artisans hand-dip each tile in glaze and arrange them inside an earthen kiln, where uneven heat distribution creates the dynamic glazes and tonal variation that define traditional zellige. Once fired, tiles are hand-placed on mesh sheets. Every sheet is an expression of the artisan's work that day, beautifully imperfect and unique.

Frequently Asked Questions
What colors are in the Rubric 4 zellige mosaic?
Rubric 4 uses Tidepool and Casablanca, two glazes that anchor opposite ends of the mosaic's color contrast. Tidepool is a blue-green glaze that shifts through kelpy greens and seafoam blues tile to tile. Casablanca is a light neutral with a glaze that moves between near-white and pale off-white. The contrast between the two glazes defines the geometric pattern across the mosaic field, and the variation within each glaze means the composition reads differently at different viewing angles and under different light conditions.
Can Rubric 4 zellige mosaic be used in a shower?
Yes. Rubric 4 can be used on shower walls, shower floors, and steam showers. Sealing is required for all shower applications. Glazed zellige has a DCOF of 0.16, below the 0.42 wet-floor slip-resistance threshold for level interior floors. For shower floor installations, size grout joints to a minimum of 1/8 inch and apply an anti-slip treatment after installation is complete. Refer to the Zellige Installation Guide for full sealing and installation instructions. 
Can Rubric 4 be used in a pool?
Yes. Rubric 4 is suitable for pool and spa applications in both saltwater and chlorine environments. Sealing is required for all zellige installations; for pools and spas, apply 511 Porous Plus Penetrating Sealer after grouting is complete and allow it to cure fully before the installation is exposed to water. Consult the Zellige Installation Guide for application steps and re-sealing intervals.
What grout color works best with Rubric 4 zellige mosaic?
Grout color is a compositional decision here. White matched to Casablanca puts the Tidepool pattern in sharp focus. Neutral or light grey softens the contrast and lets the geometry recede. Warm grey or charcoal frames each tile individually and adds depth. Confirm the selection during the dry layout stage before setting begins.
How do I install Rubric 4 zellige mosaic sheets?
Rubric 4 arrives as mesh-mounted 8x8 inch sheets. Soak sheets in room-temperature water for one to two minutes before setting. Individual tiles can be lifted from the mesh backing to adjust placement at edges and perimeters. Separate and mix sheets from multiple boxes before laying to distribute variation evenly across the field. For the full step-by-step installation process, refer to the Zellige Installation Guide.


Can Rubric 4 be mixed with solid zellige tiles?
Yes. Rubric 4 works alongside solid Tidepool or Casablanca in square or Bejmat format. How the two are balanced is a compositional choice: Rubric 4 as a feature area against a solid field reads differently than an even distribution across the surface. Plan the full layout during the dry stage before setting begins.
