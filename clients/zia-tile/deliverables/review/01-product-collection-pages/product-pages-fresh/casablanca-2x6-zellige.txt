About
No two zellige tiles are the same. Pieces vary in size, surface, and glaze across each box, with hairline crazing and chamfered edges that come from how the material is hand-formed rather than flaws to correct.


Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with zellige, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with California Prop 65. Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered cuts or Schluter strips. Traditional trim and edge pieces do not exist for zellige.


Seal zellige according to our installation instructions, whether the application is dry or exposed to moisture (pools, spas, showers). Reach out to our team at info@ziatile.com with any material or installation questions.

How It's Made
Zellige is hand-formed in Morocco, where the technique has been refined for more than 800 years and passed across generations of artisans.


The process begins with raw clay, which is mined from local hillsides, soaked, filtered for impurities, and hand-pressed into molds. The tiles are laid to dry in the Moroccan sun, flattened and shaped by hand, then dried further before glazing.


Each tile is hand-dipped in glaze and arranged inside an earthen kiln, where uneven heat creates the tonal variation that defines zellige. No two pieces leave the kiln the same, and the handmade nature and variation are hallmarks of this material. To prepare for installation, open and mix all boxes before setting them to achieve an ideal layout. The tiles will carry the marks of the day they were fired.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the Bejmat format?
Bejmat is the traditional Moroccan name for this rectangular zellige format, sized here at 2x6 inches. The elongated rectangle offers more directional flexibility, and the same tile can produce very different installations depending on whether it is laid horizontally, vertically, or in a pattern like herringbone. 
What layout patterns work with the 2x6 Bejmat format?
Running bond with offset horizontal joints is one of the most common approaches. Herringbone adds visual movement and draws more attention to the format's elongated shape. Vertical stack creates a quieter, more ordered surface where the tile reads as a narrow vertical element. Plan the arrangement during the dry layout stage before setting any tile.
Can Casablanca White 2x6 zellige be used in a shower?
Yes. Casablanca White 2x6 can be used on shower walls, shower floors, and steam showers. Sealing is required for all shower applications. For shower floor installations, slip resistance can be increased by sizing grout joints to a minimum of ⅛" and applying an anti-slip treatment after installation is complete. Refer to the Zellige Installation Guide for full instructions.
Can Casablanca White 2x6 be mixed with other formats in the collection?
Yes. Casablanca White 2x6 can be mixed with other formats or colorways in Zia's zellige collection. Each format is produced using the same traditional methods, so tiles share a consistent surface character across the range. When mixing formats, plan the layout in advance and blend tiles from multiple boxes during the dry layout stage to achieve even distribution of tone and surface variation across the full installation.
Does Casablanca White 2x6 zellige work in a pool or spa?
Yes. Casablanca White 2x6 is suitable for pool and spa applications in both saltwater and chlorine environments. Sealing is required; apply sealer to all tiles and grout lines exposed to continuous moisture after grouting is complete. Consult the Zellige Installation Guide for specific sealing instructions and reach out to our team at info@ziatile.com or 310-844-1170 with any project-specific questions.
Do the Casablanca 2x6 rectangles require a specific grout width?
No, hand-shaped edges vary from tile to tile, which allows for a traditional installation with a close grout joint. For wet floor applications, increase grout joint size to a minimum of ⅛" and consider applying an anti-slip treatment after installation is complete.
