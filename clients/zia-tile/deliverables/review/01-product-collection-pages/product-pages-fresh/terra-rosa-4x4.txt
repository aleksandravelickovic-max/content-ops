We recommend ordering 15–25% overage so the installation has room for hand-selection, color blending, and the tile breakage inherent to the material. Before laying any piece, unpack every box and mix the tiles on a flat surface to plan the layout and confirm the color balance.


Zellige tile installation is labor-intensive, often likened more to masonry than traditional tiling due to the irregular nature of the handmade material. The traditional method sets pieces edge-to-edge with minimal grout, but irregular shapes mean wedge spacers help keep layouts consistent and protect chamfered edges as the tiles are set. Plan for installations 2–3 times longer than standard ceramic. Every piece needs to be sorted, soaked, arranged, and hand-adjusted for size.

About
No two zellige tiles are the same. Pieces vary in size, surface, and glaze across each box, with hairline crazing and chamfered edges that come from how the material is hand-formed rather than flaws to correct.


Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with zellige, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with California Prop 65. Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered cuts or Schluter strips. Traditional trim and edge pieces do not exist for zellige.


Seal zellige according to our installation instructions, whether the application is dry or exposed to moisture (pools, spas, showers). Contact Zia's team at info@ziatile.com or 310-844-1170 with any material or installation questions.

How It's Made
Zellige is hand-formed in Morocco, where the technique has been refined for more than 800 years and passed across generations of artisans.


The process begins with raw clay, which is mined from local hillsides, soaked, filtered for impurities, and hand-pressed into molds. The tiles are laid to dry in the Moroccan sun, flattened and shaped by hand, then dried further before glazing.


Each tile is hand-dipped in glaze and arranged inside an earthen kiln, where uneven heat creates the tonal variation that defines zellige. No two pieces leave the kiln the same, and the handmade nature and variation are hallmarks of this material. To prepare for installation, open and mix all boxes before setting them to achieve an ideal layout. The tiles will carry the marks of the day they were fired.

Frequently Asked Questions
What color is Terra Rosa zellige?
Terra Rosa zellige features dimensional reds across a dynamic finish that ranges from warm to cool depending on the light and the tile. Each piece holds its own position in that range, and the variation distributes across the installation to build a surface with tonal depth and warmth.
Can Terra Rosa 4x4 zellige be used in a shower?
Yes. Terra Rosa 4x4 can be used on shower walls, shower floors, and steam showers. Glazed zellige has a DCOF rating of 0.16, below the 0.42 wet-floor slip-resistance threshold for level interior floors.  For shower floor installations, size grout joints to a minimum of 1/8" and apply an anti-slip treatment after installation is complete. Sealing is required for all shower applications. Refer to the Zellige Installation Guide for full instructions.
Can Terra Rosa 4x4 be used in a pool?
Sealing is required for all Terra Rosa zellige installations, including dry interior walls. Apply 511 Porous Plus Penetrating Sealer after grouting is complete. For pools, spas, and showers, re-seal every one to two years, or more frequently depending on traffic and wear. Use a pH-neutral cleaner for routine maintenance, as bleach, vinegar, and acid-based products damage both the glaze and the sealer. Full application instructions are in the Zellige Installation Guide. For questions specific to your installation, contact us at info@ziatile.com or 310-844-1170.
Can Terra Rosa be mixed with other zellige colorways? 
Yes. Terra Rosa can be mixed with other colorways in Zia's zellige collection. Pairing a warm neutral field tile alongside Terra Rosa as a feature or accent area is a common approach, where the neutral field lets the earthy pink read as a deliberate compositional element. Plan any mixed-colorway layout during the dry stage before setting begins.
What sealing protocol does Terra Rosa zellige require? 
Zellige requires sealing for all applications. For pools, spas, showers, and any surface exposed to continuous moisture, apply 511 Porous Plus penetrating sealer to all tiles and grout lines after grouting is complete. Full sealing instructions are in the Zellige Installation Guide.
Is Terra Rosa 4x4 rated for outdoor installations? 
Terra Rosa is suited to exterior wall installations and exterior floor installations in non-freeze/thaw climates only. Zellige is not rated for exterior floors in freeze/thaw regions. For outdoor use in mild climates, apply a penetrating sealer in accordance with the installation guide and re-seal as needed depending on exposure. Full installation guidance is in the Zellige Installation Guide.
