We recommend ordering 15–25% overage so the installation has room for hand-selection, color blending, and the tile breakage inherent to the material. Before laying any piece, unpack every box and mix the tiles on a flat surface to plan the layout and confirm the color balance.


Zellige tile installation is labor-intensive, often likened more to masonry than traditional tiling due to the irregular nature of the handmade material. The traditional method sets pieces edge-to-edge with minimal grout, but irregular shapes mean wedge spacers help keep layouts consistent and protect chamfered edges as the tiles are set. Plan for installations 2–3 times longer than standard ceramic. Every piece needs to be sorted, soaked, arranged, and hand-adjusted for size.

About
No two zellige tiles are the same. Pieces vary in size, surface, and glaze across each box, with hairline crazing and chamfered edges that come from how the material is hand-formed rather than flaws to correct.


Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with zellige, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with California Prop 65. Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered cuts or Schluter strips. Traditional trim and edge pieces do not exist for zellige.


Seal zellige according to our installation instructions, whether the application is dry or exposed to moisture (pools, spas, showers). Reach out to our team at info@ziatile.com with any material or installation questions.

How It's Made
Zellige is hand-formed in Morocco, where the technique has been refined for more than 800 years and passed across generations of artisans.


The process begins with raw clay, which is mined from local hillsides, soaked, filtered for impurities, and hand-pressed into molds. The tiles are laid to dry in the Moroccan sun, flattened and shaped by hand, then dried further before glazing.


Each tile is hand-dipped in glaze and arranged inside an earthen kiln, where uneven heat creates the tonal variation that defines zellige. No two pieces leave the kiln the same, and the handmade nature and variation are hallmarks of this material. To prepare for installation, open and mix all boxes before setting them to achieve an ideal layout. The tiles will carry the marks of the day they were fired.

Frequently Asked Questions
What color is Amber zellige?
Amber zellige is a warm honey-toned neutral with a glaze that shifts in tone depending on light and viewing angle. The hand-dipping process and uneven kiln heat mean individual tiles carry different amounts of depth, so no two tiles carry exactly the same tone.
Can Amber zellige be used in a pool or spa?
Yes. Amber 4x4 is suitable for pool and spa applications in both saltwater and chlorine environments. Sealing is required after grouting for all tiles and grout lines exposed to continuous moisture. Refer to the Zellige Installation Guide for specific sealing instructions, or reach out to info@ziatile.com or 310-844-1170 with questions.
Can Amber zellige be mixed with other colors in the collection?
Yes. Amber 4x4 can be combined with other colorways in Zia's zellige collection. Each colorway is produced using the same traditional methods, so tiles share consistent thickness, format, and surface character across the range. Amber works well alongside warm whites, creams, and earthy tones in the collection, where the warm neutral reads as a grounded field against more saturated colorways.
Can Amber zellige be used in a shower?
Yes. Amber 4x4 is suitable for shower walls, shower floors, and steam showers. Sealing is required for all shower applications. For shower floors, grout joints should measure at least 1/8" and an anti-slip treatment should be applied after installation. Soak glazed zellige tiles in room-temperature water for one to two minutes before setting to ensure proper adhesion.
Does the Amber colorway vary between orders?
Yes. Each batch of zellige is produced by hand, and clay, glaze, and kiln conditions shift from one run to the next. Tiles from separate orders may differ in tone and surface character. Ordering your full quantity at once ensures the most consistent appearance across your project.
What overage should I order for Amber 4x4?
For a standard layout, order 15 to 20% overage to account for curation and cuts. For projects requiring a more uniform appearance, plan for 25% overage to allow greater flexibility during the dry layout phase.
