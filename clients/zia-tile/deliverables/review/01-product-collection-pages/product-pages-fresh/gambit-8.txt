About
No two zellige tiles are the same. Pieces vary in size, surface, and glaze across each box, with hairline crazing and chamfered edges that come from how the material is hand-formed rather than flaws to correct.


Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with zellige, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with California Prop 65. Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered cuts or Schluter strips. Traditional trim and edge pieces do not exist for zellige.


Seal zellige according to our installation instructions, whether the application is dry or exposed to moisture (pools, spas, showers). Reach out to our team at info@ziatile.com with any material or installation questions.

How It's Made
Zellige is hand-formed in Morocco, where the technique has been refined for more than 800 years and passed across generations of artisans.
The process begins with raw clay, which is mined from local hillsides, soaked, filtered for impurities, and hand-pressed into molds. The tiles are laid to dry in the Moroccan sun, flattened and shaped by hand, then dried further before glazing.
Each tile is hand-dipped in glaze and arranged inside an earthen kiln, where uneven heat creates the tonal variation that defines zellige. No two pieces leave the kiln the same and the handmade nature and variation are hallmarks of this most special material. 
To prepare for installation, open and mix all boxes before setting them to achieve an ideal layout. The tiles will carry the marks of the day they were fired.

Frequently Asked Questions
What colors are in the Gambit 8 zellige mosaic?
Gambit 8 uses three glazes: Plum, Purple Iris, and Portuguese Blue. All three sit in the deep purple and blue-purple range, and each glaze carries tonal variation tile to tile as a result of the hand-dipping process and uneven kiln heat. Blending sheets from all boxes before the dry layout is essential for even distribution of that tonal range across the finished installation.
What is the difference between Gambit 8 mosaic sheets and standard zellige field tile?
Gambit 8 arrives pre-assembled on mesh-backed 8x8 sheets rather than as individual field tiles. The mesh backing sets pieces edge-to-edge with less grout spacing than a standard 1/8 inch field-tile joint, which is a defining visual characteristic of the mosaic format. Individual tiles can be lifted from the mesh backing for edge cuts and perimeter adjustments. Sheets from multiple boxes should be separated and blended before being distributed to distribute tonal variation evenly. Installation time runs two to three times longer than standard ceramic, consistent with all zellige.
Can Gambit 8 be used in a shower?
Yes. Gambit 8 can be used on shower walls and shower floors. Sealing is required for all shower applications. Glazed zellige has a DCOF of 0.16, below the 0.42 wet-floor threshold for level interior floors. Size grout joints to a minimum of 1/8 inch and apply an anti-slip treatment after installation to increase slip resistance on shower floors. See the Zellige Installation Guide for the full wet-area sealing sequence.
Can Gambit 8 be used in a pool?
Yes. Gambit 8 is suitable for pool and spa applications in both saltwater and chlorine environments. Sealing is required for all zellige installations; for pools and spas, apply 511 Porous Plus Penetrating Sealer after grouting is complete and allow it to cure fully before the installation is exposed to water. Because the three-glaze composition requires consistent sheet orientation to resolve the color distribution correctly, confirm the full pool layout during the dry stage before setting begins. 
How do I install Gambit 8 zellige mosaic sheets?
Gambit 8 arrives on mesh-backed 8x8 sheets. Before setting any sheet, separate and mix from multiple boxes to distribute the three glazes evenly across the full layout. Lay the full composition on a flat surface to confirm color distribution and pattern before applying any adhesive. Soak sheets in room-temperature water for one to two minutes before setting. Individual tiles can be removed from the mesh backing for edge and perimeter cuts. See the Zellige Installation Guide for the full mosaic installation sequence.
Can Gambit 8 be mixed with solid zellige colorways?
Yes. Gambit 8 can be used alongside solid zellige tiles in Plum, Purple Iris, or Portuguese Blue. Using Gambit 8 as a feature area against a solid field of one of its component glazes is a common approach that connects the mosaic composition to the surrounding installation without introducing a second palette. Plan the full layout during the dry stage before setting begins.
