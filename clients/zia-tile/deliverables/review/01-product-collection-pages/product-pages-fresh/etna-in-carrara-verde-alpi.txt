Order Details + Installation
Marble is a stone with significant variations in color, tone, veining, and surface texture, along with naturally occurring imperfections. Images shown are representative of a sample of this material and may not display all potential variation. A minimum of 15–20% overage is recommended to account for curation and cuts, though additional overage allows for an even more controlled appearance.
Careful installation is essential with this soft, porous stone. Before setting any marble, unpack and mix pieces from all boxes to balance color and veining across the layout. Use a thinset suitable for natural stone and ensure full back-buttering for complete coverage, then seal the stone twice – before and after grouting. Clean only with pH-neutral products, resealing periodically as needed.

About
The Marble Patterns line is Zia's translation of Roman pavement and Renaissance inlay into nine geometric compositions designed to be installed as continuous mosaics. The patterns are Piazza, Etna, Verona, Lorenzo, Lucca, Piazzetta, Lombardy, Diamante, and Isola, ranging from multi-format compositions like Etna and Verona to single-format arrangements like Piazza and Diamante.
Each pattern can be ordered in any combination of the six core marble colorways: Carrara, Nero Marquina, Grigio Carnico, Rosso Alicante, Giallo Reale, and Verde Alpi. The line is built to pair cleanly with Marble Solids, so patterned floors can run into solid borders or solid bathrooms can carry a patterned shower or fireplace within the same residential or commercial scheme.
Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with marble, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with California Prop 65. Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered edges or Schluter strips.

How It's Made
Each pattern starts from the same Italian and Spanish marble blocks used for Marble Solids. Stone masons in workshops near Verona cut the blocks down to the individual piece sizes the pattern requires, then hone every piece by hand to the same soft matte finish that defines the rest of the marble line.
Once honed, the pieces are hand-assembled into the pattern's specific geometry: multi-format compositions have each format laid in sequence according to the design, while single-format patterns are arranged into the repeating geometry that gives them their name. Patterns built for mosaic installation are mounted onto mesh sheets so the layout holds through transit and through the thinset cure.
Pattern compositions ship as designed arrangements, ready to lay as a continuous surface. The combination of natural veining across each colorway and the human hand at every step of cutting, honing, and assembling is what gives each pattern its character at the joint level and as a single graphic across the room.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the Etna marble pattern?
Etna is a multi-format geometric marble composition from Zia's Marble line. The geometry resolves only when pieces of each dimension are laid in the correct order at every joint. A full dry layout before setting is essential to confirm how the composition reads across the space and to identify where cuts are needed at the perimeter.
What is the color combination in Etna in Carrara + Verde Alpi?
Carrara and Verde Alpi are two contrasting Italian marble colorways, each carrying natural veining variation built into the stone at the quarry. In the Etna composition, the contrast between the two colorways defines the geometric pattern. Each marble's veining and tonal range shift tile to tile as a result of natural variation, and those differences distribute across the installation as the field grows. Blending pieces from all boxes during the dry layout stage distributes each colorway's tonal variation evenly across the finished surface.
How do I install Etna in Carrara + Verde Alpi?
The Etna pattern requires each piece to be laid in the correct orientation and sequence for the geometry to resolve correctly. Unpack all pieces and confirm the pattern's arrangement during the dry stage before setting any stone. Press pieces with the palm only during installation. Rubber mallets can create micro-fractures in the stone that grow over time. Seal before and after grouting, and grout in small sections, wiping any excess immediately to prevent staining the stone surface.
How do I seal Etna in Carrara + Verde Alpi marble?
Apply 511 Porous Plus in thin layers until all stone pieces are fully saturated, wiping off any excess before it dries. The sealer must cure fully before grouting begins. For interior installations, apply Fila Matte Finish Protective Wax as the final top coat after the grout has cured. For exterior installations in non-freeze/thaw climates, apply a second coat of 511 Porous Plus in place of wax, as UV exposure causes wax to discolor over time. See the Marble Installation Guide for the full sealing sequence.


Can Etna in Carrara + Verde Alpi be used in a shower?
Yes, on both shower walls and shower floors. Seal the stone before and after grouting. This composition includes Carrara, which has a DCOF of 0.28, below the 0.42 wet-floor threshold. For shower floor installations, size grout joints to a minimum of 1/8 inch and apply an anti-slip treatment after installation is complete.
Can Etna be ordered in other colorway combinations?
Yes. Etna can be ordered in any combination of the six core marble colorways in Zia's Marble Patterns line: Carrara, Nero Marquina, Grigio Carnico, Rosso Alicante, Giallo Reale, and Verde Alpi. Each colorway pairing produces a different visual character in the Etna geometry. Contact us at info@ziatile.com or 310-844-1170 to discuss colorway combinations before ordering.
