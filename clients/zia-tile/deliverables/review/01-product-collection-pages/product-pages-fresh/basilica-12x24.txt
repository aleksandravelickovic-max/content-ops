About
Zia's limestone is sourced from two regions: Honed Limestone quarried near Fez in three colorways (Buff, Monument, and Basilica) across 6x6, 4x12, 12x12, and 12x24 formats; and French Limestone quarried in Bordeaux in 12-inch and 20-inch varying-length tiles plus French Cobblestone. Basilica is also offered in a single bush-hammered 4x12 variant. Variation in color, tone, and natural inclusions across pieces is inherent to the material; lay out the stone on a flat surface and confirm the color balance reads the way you want before setting any piece.
Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with limestone, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with California Prop 65. Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered edges or Schluter strips. The full installation procedure is in the Limestone Installation Guide. For any material or installation questions, reach out to our team at info@ziatile.com.

How It's Made
Limestone is quarried from solid blocks near Fez, Morocco, and around Bordeaux, France, in workshops where the material has been cut and honed for centuries. Each block is sized down to the format dimensions and finished by stone masons at the source.


The honed finish is achieved by gently smoothing the surface to a clean, soft matte. The bush-hammered finish is achieved by striking the face with a textured hammer head, which uniformly pits the surface for a more rustic, aged character. Both finishes preserve the natural mineral signature of the stone.


Limestone is a living material. Subtle tonal variation across pieces is inherent to natural stone, and no two tiles are identical. The soft patina that develops over years of use is a defining feature of the material and the reason limestone has carried so many of the floors that survive from antiquity into the present.

Frequently Asked Questions
What does the Basilica colorway look like, and where is it quarried?
Basilica is a grey honed limestone in a neutral color palette, quarried near Fez, Morocco. The colorway carries the tonal variation, fossil impressions, and natural fissures characteristic of the Fez quarry, and no two pieces carry exactly the same arrangement of natural inclusions. 
What variation should I expect across Basilica 12x24 pieces?
Basilica carries the variation inherent to natural limestone: tonal differences across pieces, fossil impressions, subtle fissures, and oxidation marks. Before installation, lay out all pieces on a flat surface and blend across boxes to confirm the color balance reads evenly throughout the installation. There is a face and an underside to each limestone piece; they are not reversible, so ensure each stone is installed face side up.
Can Basilica 12x24 limestone be used outdoors in a freeze/thaw climate?
No. Basilica 12x24 limestone is not rated for exterior floor installations in freeze/thaw climates. Limestone is susceptible to freeze/thaw cycling, which can cause the stone to crack or spall over time under repeated expansion and contraction. 


For outdoor floor applications, Basilica is suited to non-freeze/thaw climates only: patios, courtyards, and pool surrounds in mild-weather regions. For exterior wall use, apply a penetrating sealer before and after grouting.
Is Basilica 12x24 suitable for shower floors and wet areas?
Yes. Basilica 12x24 is suited to shower wall and floor installations. Honed limestone has a DCOF rating of 0.50, above the 0.42 wet-floor slip-resistance threshold for level interior floors. For commercial wet-floor applications, increase grout joints to a minimum of 1/8" and apply an anti-slip treatment after installation. Sealing is required for all wet applications; the full sealing protocol is in the Limestone Installation Guide.
Does Basilica 12x24 require sealing?
Yes. All limestone requires a two-stage sealing sequence. Before grouting, apply 511 Porous Plus penetrating sealer in thin layers until the stone is fully saturated, then allow it to cure. After the grout cures, apply Fila Matte Finish Protective Wax for interior installations or a second coat of 511 Porous Plus for exterior applications. Acidic cleaners must be avoided at every stage as they will damage the stone. Full instructions are in the Limestone Installation Guide.
Can Basilica 12x24 be used in a pool or spa?
No. Basilica 12x24 limestone is not rated for pool or spa applications. Limestone is a natural, porous stone that is not designed for continuous full submersion or exposure to chlorinated or saltwater environments. For pool and spa applications across the Zia collection, zellige and Cotto Allende are the appropriate material categories.
How does the Basilica 12x24 relate to the rest of Zia's limestone collection?
Basilica is one of three Honed Limestone colorways quarried near Fez, alongside Buff and Monument, and is the only colorway in the line also available in a bush-hammered finish (in the 4x12 format). 


The 12x24 rectangle is the largest format in the Honed Limestone range and suits large-format floor fields and floor-to-ceiling wall applications where fewer grout joints allow the stone surface to read continuously. All three Fez colorways share the same honed finish character and require the same two-stage sealing protocol.
