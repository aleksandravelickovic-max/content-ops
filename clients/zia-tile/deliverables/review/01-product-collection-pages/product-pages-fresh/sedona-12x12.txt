About
Cantera is the volcanic stone that built colonial Mexico, used continuously for centuries in courtyards, fountains, and the carved facades of cathedrals and haciendas. Zia's Cantera line carries that material into a residential and commercial collection sized for both intimate rooms and broad public installations.


The line is offered in eight earthy colorways, each carrying its own mineral signature: Mesa, Caldera, Acacia, Sierra, Yuma, Sedona, Badlands, and Volcan. Formats range from 6x6 and 4x12 mosaics to 12x12, 12x24, and 24x24 floor tiles, so a single Cantera scheme can run from a textured shower wall in a primary bathroom to a hotel courtyard or restaurant patio in the same colorway.


Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with Cantera, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with California Prop 65. Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered edges or Schluter strips.

How It's Made
Cantera begins as a porous volcanic rock, formed over millions of years from cooled lava and ash. Solid blocks are pulled from quarries in Mexico, then sized down to the format dimensions of the line: 6x6 and 4x12 mosaics, 12x12 and 12x24 planks, and 24x24 large-format floor tiles.


Each piece is honed and finished by hand at the source. The finishing approach intentionally preserves the visible aggregate, mineral inclusions, and oxidation markings of the volcanic chemistry, which is what gives each colorway its individual mineral signature rather than a surface treatment that would mask it.


Once finished, the tiles are graded and packed for shipping. After installation, the stone continues to develop. Cantera takes on a soft patina with use, similar to marble or limestone, and the sealing protocol on the install guide protects the surface while still allowing the material to age into the room around it.

Frequently Asked Questions
What color is Sedona Cantera?
Sedona Cantera is a neutral pink volcanic stone with tonal variation built into the material itself. The color is set by the natural mineral composition of the rock, and oxidation patterns, mineral inclusions, and aggregate distribution shift from tile to tile within the pink range. Blending from all boxes before the dry layout distributes that variation across the field before any piece is set.
Can Sedona 12x12 be used in a shower?
Yes. Sedona 12x12 Cantera can be used on shower floors and shower walls. Cantera has a DCOF of 0.53 to 0.63 across the colorway range, providing strong slip resistance in wet conditions. Seal with 511 Porous Plus before grouting and apply Fila Matte Finish Protective Wax as the final top coat after the grout has cured for interior wet areas. Size grout joints to a minimum of 1/8 inch for shower floor applications. 
Does Sedona 12x12 require sealing?
Yes. Cantera tile requires sealing before and after grouting. Apply 511 Porous Plus in thin layers until the stone is fully saturated before grouting, wiping off any excess before it dries. The sealer must cure fully before grouting begins. For interior installations, apply Fila Matte Finish Protective Wax as the final top coat after the grout has cured. For outdoor installations, apply a second coat of 511 Porous Plus in place of wax, as UV exposure causes wax to discolor over time. Reseal periodically based on traffic and wear. See the Cantera Installation Guide for the complete sealing sequence.
Can Sedona 12x12 be used outdoors?
Yes, in non-freeze/thaw climates only. Sedona 12x12 is suitable for exterior floors and walls in climates that do not experience freeze/thaw conditions. The porous volcanic stone is not suitable for freeze/thaw cycling. For all exterior applications, seal with 511 Porous Plus before grouting and apply a second coat of 511 Porous Plus as the final top coat after the grout has cured. Do not use Fila Matte Finish Protective Wax for outdoor installations. See the Cantera Installation Guide for the outdoor sealing sequence.
How do I clean Sedona cantera?
Sedona Cantera requires regular sweeping or vacuuming to remove abrasive grit and debris from the surface. For routine cleaning, use a pH-neutral cleaner with warm water. Miracle Sealants pH Neutral Tile and Stone Cleaner is well suited for Cantera. Blot spills promptly rather than wiping. The porous volcanic stone absorbs liquid readily, and prompt blotting reduces stain penetration before it reaches the stone body. Avoid acidic cleaners including vinegar, lemon juice, and bleach, as these damage the porous stone surface over time. For heavier staining, use Miracle Sealants Heavy Duty Cleaner (Acid Substitute). 
Can Sedona 12x12 be mixed with other cantera colorways or formats?
Yes. Sedona 12x12 can be mixed with other colorways and formats in Zia's Cantera line. Sedona sits at the warmer end of the eight-colorway spectrum. Mixing it with cooler or more neutral Cantera tones introduces temperature contrast within the same material, which can be effective in large-format floor installations. Assess the visual balance during a dry layout before setting any piece, and blend tiles from all boxes to distribute tonal and mineral variation evenly across the installation.
