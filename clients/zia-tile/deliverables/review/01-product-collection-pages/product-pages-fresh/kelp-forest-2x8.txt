About
Zia's ceramics are the design counterpoint to our handmade zellige. 
The collection spans matte, satin, and glossy finishes plus the textured Field Trip: Japan series, in three core formats: 2x8 and 2x4 rectangles and 4x4 squares. Color and tone vary across high-fired ceramics - less than handmade zellige, but enough that mixing boxes before the set still matters. 
Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with ceramic, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with California Prop 65. Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered edges or Schluter strips.
Sealing requirements vary by finish: matte ceramics must be sealed before grouting as a grout release; satin and glossy finishes do not require pre-grout sealing. For any material or installation questions, reach out to our team at info@ziatile.com.

How It's Made 
Ceramic tile is designed in Los Angeles and made in Japan, where the line is produced using high-temperature firing and precision glaze application. The result is a clean, precisely sized tile with consistent color across boxes, a different product category from handmade clay tile.
Clay is molded into shape, dried, glazed, and fired at high temperature. The matte, satin, and gloss finishes are achieved through different glaze formulations, each producing distinct surface character and slip-resistance performance.
Each format is produced to consistent dimensions across the line, which allows for predictable installation and clean grout lines. Subtle variation in color and surface is normal and reflects the hand-finishing step in production.

Frequently Asked Questions 
What is the Kelp Forest colorway?
Kelp Forest is a deep, saturated green. The glossy surface reflects light across the installation, giving the color more visual depth than the same tone would carry in a matte or satin finish. The 2x8 subway format suits wall applications, including backsplashes, bath walls, and feature surfaces.
Can glossy ceramic be used on a floor?
Glossy ceramic has a DCOF of 0.30, which is below the recommended threshold for wet floor areas. It is best suited to walls, backsplashes, and dry floor applications where slip resistance is not a primary concern. For shower floors or any wet floor installation, matte ceramic (DCOF 0.74) or satin ceramic (DCOF 0.45) is the appropriate choice. Kelp Forest 2x8 can be used on shower walls.
Does Kelp Forest 2x8 require sealing?
No. Glossy ceramic does not require pre-grout sealing. The dense glaze surface resists grout haze without a sealer. For wet installations such as shower walls, sealing the grout lines after installation is recommended to protect the grout from moisture and staining.
Can Kelp Forest 2x8 be installed outdoors?
Kelp Forest 2x8 can be used on exterior walls in non-freeze/thaw climates only. It is not suitable for exterior floor installations in any climate. Contact info@ziatile.com or 310-844-1170 to discuss your specific location and climate before ordering.
How do I clean glossy ceramic tile?
To clean glossy ceramic tile, wipe with warm water and a pH-neutral cleaner. Glossy surfaces show water spots and fingerprints more readily than matte or satin finishes, so regular wiping keeps the surface looking clean. Avoid abrasive scrubbers and acidic cleaners, as these can damage the glaze. For heavier buildup, Miracle Sealants Heavy Duty Cleaner (Acid Substitute) is the recommended product.
Can I mix Kelp Forest 2x8 with matte or satin ceramics?
Yes. Kelp Forest 2x8 can be mixed with other Zia ceramics, for example, pairing the glossy green with a matte neutral for contrast across a backsplash or feature wall. Because finishes carry different light-reflective properties, the visual effect of mixing them is worth testing in a dry layout before setting. Sealing requirements differ by finish: matte tiles require pre-grout sealing; satin and glossy do not.
