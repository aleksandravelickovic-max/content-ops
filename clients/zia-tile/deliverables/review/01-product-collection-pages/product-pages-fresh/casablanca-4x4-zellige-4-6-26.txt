We recommend ordering 15–25% overage so the installation has room for hand-selection, color blending, and the tile breakage inherent to the material. Before laying any piece, unpack every box and mix the tiles on a flat surface to plan the layout and confirm the color balance.


Zellige tile installation is labor-intensive, often likened more to masonry than traditional tiling due to the irregular nature of the handmade material. About
No two zellige tiles are the same. Pieces vary in size, surface, and glaze across each box, with hairline crazing and chamfered edges that come from how the material is hand-formed rather than flaws to correct.


Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with zellige, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with California Prop 65. Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered cuts or Schluter strips. Traditional trim and edge pieces do not exist for zellige.


Seal zellige according to our installation instructions, whether the application is dry or exposed to moisture (pools, spas, showers). Contact Zia's team at info@ziatile.com or 310-844-1170 with any material or installation questions.

How It's Made
Zellige is hand-formed in Morocco, where the technique has been refined for more than 800 years and passed across generations of artisans.


The process begins with raw clay, which is mined from local hillsides, soaked, filtered for impurities, and hand-pressed into molds. The tiles are laid to dry in the Moroccan sun, flattened and shaped by hand, then dried further before glazing.


Each tile is hand-dipped in glaze and arranged inside an earthen kiln, where uneven heat creates the tonal variation that defines zellige. No two pieces leave the kiln the same, and the handmade nature and variation are hallmarks of this material. To prepare for installation, open and mix all boxes before setting them to achieve an ideal layout. The tiles will carry the marks of the day they were fired.

Frequently Asked Questions
Does each Casablanca 4x4 tile arrive with a consistent thickness?
Artisans shape each zellige tile by hand, which results in variation in thickness, shape, and color across the collection. Expect and embrace these unique characteristics, as they allow your installer to maintain the organic surface that defines the traditional zellige aesthetic.
Can I install the Casablanca 4x4 on a shower floor?
Yes, our 4x4-inch zellige tiles function well on shower floors, shower walls, and within steam showers. Slip resistance can be improved by increasing grout joint size to a minimum of 1/8 inch and/or applying an anti-slip treatment after installation is complete. Sealing is required for all installations.
Why do some Casablanca 4x4 tiles feature small surface cracks?
Fine lines on zellige’s glazed surface, known as crazing, occur naturally during the kiln firing process. These characteristic marks, along with occasional pits and edge chips, represent the artisan's craft and do not compromise the structural integrity of the tile.
How do I clean my zellige tile once the installation is complete?
Always clean zellige tiles with pH-neutral products to maintain the tiles’ glaze. Regularly vacuum or sweep the surface to remove abrasive debris that might scratch the tile over time.
What are the characteristic features of each Casablanca 4x4 tile?
Our Casablanca zellige 4x4 tile features variation in color, shape, and thickness. Zia’s 4x4 Casablanca exhibits characteristics such as chips, pits, and crazing. These elements are traditional characteristics of handmade Moroccan tile that create a perfectly imperfect aesthetic.
Can I mix Casablanca 4x4 with other colors in your collection?
Yes, our Casablanca zellige 4x4s complement any of the 35+ unique colors in our handcrafted zellige collection. Artisans use the same traditional Moroccan methods for every shade, allowing you to mix these 4x4 squares with other tones to create a custom installation that showcases the full spectrum of our available glazes.
