Order Details + Installation
We recommend a 15-20% overage to best curate an Unglazed Natural zellige installation, allowing for sufficient tiles within the desired range. Before installation, mix tiles from all boxes and arrange your layout dry to disperse variation evenly. Do not pre-soak unglazed zellige, as soaking causes efflorescence that is difficult to remove.
Unglazed Natural zellige requires a different sealing protocol than glazed zellige. Apply 511 Porous Plus Penetrating Sealer before grouting, then finish with Fila Matte Wax for interior installations or 511 Porous Plus for exterior. Plan for installation to take 2 to 3 times longer than standard ceramic tile, since each piece is sorted, arranged, and adjusted by hand for size variation.

About 
Unglazed Natural zellige is the raw form of traditional Moroccan zellige. The glaze that defines our other zellige colorways is omitted, leaving the matte clay face exposed and letting the natural tone of the local clay carry the tile. Mix all boxes in your shipment before beginning your installation, and lay out an ideal arrangement on a flat surface before setting any tile to confirm the color balance reads the way you want.
Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with zellige, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with California Prop 65. Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered edges or Schluter strips. Traditional trim and edge pieces do not exist for this material.
This colorway requires a sealing sequence distinct from glazed zellige: seal before grouting and apply a top coat after, both per the Zellige Installation Guide. Always seal unglazed zellige according to our installation instructions, whether in dry areas or areas exposed to moisture such as pools, spas, and showers. Stains on the raw clay surface can become permanent if grouting or use begins before sealing is complete. For any material or installation questions, reach our team at info@ziatile.com.

How It's Made
Unglazed Natural zellige is hand-formed in Morocco by the same artisans who shape our glazed zellige, using techniques refined and passed down through generations. The glaze step is omitted, which preserves the raw clay face that gives this tile its matte color and natural surface tone.
The craft begins with clay pulled from local Moroccan hillsides. Artisans soak the raw clay in water and filter it for impurities, then hand-press the paste into wooden molds before laying the tiles to dry under the Moroccan sun. Each tile is hand-flattened and shaped, then dried again before firing.
Tiles fire in earthen kilns without glaze, and the uneven heat of the kiln produces the matte clay surface and tile-to-tile color shifts that read as the signature of unglazed zellige. As the surface meets daily use, it deepens into a patina the way unsealed limestone or terra cotta does, recording wear instead of resisting it.

Frequently Asked Questions 
How does Unglazed Natural differ from glazed zellige?
Unglazed Natural differs from glazed zellige in surface, sealing protocol, grout selection, and approved applications. The absence of a glaze layer produces a matte, porous surface rather than the reflective hand-dipped finish of glazed colorways. Unglazed Natural must be sealed with 511 Porous Plus before grouting to protect the exposed clay body from grout staining. It uses Mapei Flexcolor CQ grout rather than Ultracolor Plus FA, and it is not approved for pool or spa applications.
What color is Unglazed Natural zellige?
Unglazed Natural zellige presents a range of warm, earthy tones drawn directly from the clay body. The surface reads as matte and light-absorbing, with tone variation across each tile and across the full installation. Individual tiles carry different positions across the warm clay spectrum, and the variation in color, shape, thickness, chips, pits, and crazing is inherent to how the material is made.
Can Unglazed Natural 2x6 be used in a shower?
Yes. Unglazed Natural 2x6 can be used on shower walls and shower floors. Sealing is required both before and after grouting to protect the exposed clay body from moisture and staining. Apply 511 Porous Plus before grouting, allow it to fully cure, then grout with Mapei Flexcolor CQ, and apply Fila Matte Wax as a final coat for interior applications. For shower floor installations, size grout joints to a minimum of ⅛" and apply an anti-slip treatment after installation. Refer to the Zellige Installation Guide for full instructions.
Can Unglazed Natural 2x6 be used in a pool or spa?
No. Unglazed Natural zellige is not suitable for pool or spa applications. The unglazed clay body is not approved for continuous submersion in saltwater or chlorine environments. The material is suitable for showers, shower floors, and other wet areas where it is exposed to moisture but not submerged.
What grout color works best with Unglazed Natural zellige?
A grout color that closely matches the tile's warm clay tone produces the most cohesive surface, allowing the variation in chips, pits, and crazing to read as a continuous field rather than drawing attention to the grout lines. Use Mapei Flexcolor CQ grout, not Ultracolor Plus FA — Flexcolor CQ is required for Unglazed Natural to prevent staining of the exposed clay body during grouting.
Does the Unglazed Natural colorway vary between orders?
Yes. Each batch reflects differences in clay composition, drying conditions, and kiln firing, which influence the final tone. Variation in color, shape, thickness, chips, pits, and crazing should be expected across shipments. Ordering sufficient material at one time gives the most consistent result within a single installation.
