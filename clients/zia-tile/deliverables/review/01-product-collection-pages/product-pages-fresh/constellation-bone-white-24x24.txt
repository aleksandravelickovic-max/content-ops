Order Details + Installation
We recommend a 15–20% overage on all terrazzo orders to account for any cuts needed. Before installation, blend tiles from all boxes and arrange a layout to evenly disperse aggregate distribution. Use 1/8" tile spacers for the recommended grout joint. 


Installers will need to slightly adjust grout joint spacing (shim) or use wedge spacers to maintain straight grout lines. Where necessary, tile edges may be trimmed with a wet saw to assist with alignment.

About
Each terrazzo tile is a cement-based composite with crushed marble and stone aggregates ground and polished to a matte surface. Aggregate distribution varies tile to tile. Blend tiles from all boxes before installation and arrange a layout to disperse aggregate evenly.


Perhaps the most versatile tile Zia produces, with endless colorways, patterns, and shapes, Zia strives to offer the world's best curated collection of encaustic terrazzo tiles. While generally consistent in appearance, each handmade tile varies in aggregate distribution and surface character. Durable and versatile, matte terrazzo tile is an easy choice for many installations.


Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with terrazzo, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with California Prop 65. Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered edges or Schluter strips.


Proper installation and protection of terrazzo tile is key, so please ensure you work with an installer who has experience specifically installing terrazzo tile, and who has carefully reviewed Zia's installation guide.

How It's Made
Terrazzo is produced using techniques refined by Venetian stone masons since the 15th century, when leftover marble offcuts were combined with cement to floor courtyards and palazzo halls. The same craft is preserved in the workshops where Zia's terrazzo is produced today.


Crushed marble and stone aggregates are arranged in steel molds and covered with liquid cement, then hydraulically pressed into a powdered concrete base. Once cured, the surface is ground and polished to the matte finish that defines the line.


Each tile is finished by hand, which produces the subtle tonal variation, occasional crazing, and slight edge irregularity that separate hand-pressed terrazzo from machine-set composite tile. The cement matrix and the visible aggregate are what define terrazzo as a material; the press and the finish are what carry that definition into the floor.

Frequently Asked Questions
What does Constellation Bone White terrazzo look like?
Constellation Bone White is a terrazzo with a Bone White base and a scattered aggregate composition of crushed marble and stone. The aggregate distribution shifts from tile to tile, which means each piece presents a different arrangement of stone across the surface. Blending tiles from all boxes before installation distributes that variation evenly and prevents adjacent tiles from reading as too similar or too different in aggregate density.
Does Constellation Bone White require sealing?
Yes. Apply 511 Porous Plus in thin layers until the tiles are fully saturated before grouting, wiping off any excess before it dries. The sealer must cure fully before grouting begins. For interior installations, apply Fila Matte Finish Protective Wax as the top coat after the grout has cured. For outdoor installations, apply a second coat of 511 Porous Plus in place of wax, as UV exposure causes wax to discolor over time. See the Terrazzo Installation Guide for the full sealing protocol.
Can Constellation Bone White 24x24 be used in a shower?
Yes, on both shower walls and floors. Terrazzo has a DCOF of 0.51, above the 0.42 wet-floor threshold. Seal before and after grouting and size grout joints to a minimum of 1/8 inch for shower floor applications.
How do I clean Constellation Bone White terrazzo?
Constellation Bone White requires regular sweeping or vacuuming to remove abrasive debris that can scratch the surface. For routine cleaning, use a pH-neutral cleaner such as Miracle Sealants pH Neutral Tile and Stone Cleaner. Avoid acidic cleaners, including vinegar, lemon juice, and bleach, as these damage the cement matrix. On a light-colored surface, blot spills promptly rather than wiping to prevent absorption into the cement before removal.
Can Constellation Bone White be mixed with other terrazzo colorways?
Yes. Constellation Bone White can be used alongside other terrazzo colorways in the Zia collection. All terrazzo formats share consistent thickness and production method, so colorways combine across a single installation without sizing conflicts. Plan the color arrangement during the dry stage and blend tiles from all boxes within each colorway before setting begins. Assess the aggregate and base tone combination against adjacent tiles before any piece is set.
