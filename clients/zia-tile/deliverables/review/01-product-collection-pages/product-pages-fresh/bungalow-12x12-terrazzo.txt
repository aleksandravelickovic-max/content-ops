Order Details + Installation
We recommend a 15–20% overage on all terrazzo orders to account for any cuts needed. Before installation, blend tiles from all boxes and arrange a layout to evenly disperse aggregate distribution. Use 1/8" tile spacers for the recommended grout joint. 


Installers will need to slightly adjust grout joint spacing (shim) or use wedge spacers to maintain straight grout lines. Where necessary, tile edges may be trimmed with a wet saw to assist with alignment.

About
Each terrazzo tile is a cement-based composite with crushed marble and stone aggregates ground and polished to a matte surface. Aggregate distribution varies tile to tile. Blend tiles from all boxes before installation and arrange a layout to disperse aggregate evenly.


Perhaps the most versatile tile Zia produces, with endless colorways, patterns, and shapes, Zia strives to offer the world's best curated collection of encaustic terrazzo tiles. While generally consistent in appearance, each handmade tile varies in aggregate distribution and surface character. Durable and versatile, matte terrazzo tile is an easy choice for many installations.


Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with terrazzo, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with California Prop 65. Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered edges or Schluter strips.


Proper installation and protection of terrazzo tile is key, so please ensure you work with an installer who has experience specifically installing terrazzo tile, and who has carefully reviewed Zia's installation guide.

How It's Made
Terrazzo is produced using techniques refined by Venetian stone masons since the 15th century, when leftover marble offcuts were combined with cement to floor courtyards and palazzo halls. The same craft is preserved in the workshops where Zia's terrazzo is produced today.


Crushed marble and stone aggregates are arranged in steel molds and covered with liquid cement, then hydraulically pressed into a powdered concrete base. Once cured, the surface is ground and polished to the matte finish that defines the line.


Each tile is finished by hand, which produces the subtle tonal variation, occasional crazing, and slight edge irregularity that separate hand-pressed terrazzo from machine-set composite tile. The cement matrix and the visible aggregate are what define terrazzo as a material; the press and the finish are what carry that definition into the floor.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is terrazzo tile?
Terrazzo tile is a composite material made by embedding chips of marble, quartz, granite, and other stone aggregates into a cement binder. The mixture is hydraulically pressed into molds, cured, and ground to expose the embedded aggregates and produce a smooth, matte surface. The visible pattern comes from the full-body composition of the tile, not a surface treatment applied afterward.
What are the natural characteristics of Bungalow 12x12?
Bungalow 12x12 characteristics flow directly from its production process. Aggregate size, distribution, and color vary from tile to tile, creating a surface that reads as consistent in overall tone while retaining natural variation across the installation. Minor edge irregularities, subtle tonal shifts, and occasional fine surface crazing are inherent to hand-pressed terrazzo and should be expected across the order.
Does terrazzo tile require sealing?
Yes. Cement terrazzo tile requires sealing before and after grouting. Apply 511 Porous Plus in thin layers until the tiles are fully saturated before grouting, wiping off excess before it dries. For interior installations, finish with Fila Matte Finish Protective Wax as a top coat. For outdoor installations, apply a second coat of 511 Porous Plus instead of wax, since UV exposure causes wax to discolor over time.
Can Bungalow 12x12 be used on a shower floor?
Yes. Bungalow 12x12 can be used on shower floors and shower walls. Terrazzo has a DCOF of 0.51, which meets the wet area slip resistance threshold for floor applications. Seal the tile before and after grouting as required, and size grout joints to a minimum of 1/8". An anti-slip product can be applied after installation to further increase traction in high-exposure wet floor areas.
How do I clean terrazzo tile?
Vacuum or sweep regularly to remove grit that can scratch the surface. For routine cleaning, use pH-neutral cleaner such as Miracle Sealants pH Neutral Tile and Stone Cleaner. For heavier staining, use Miracle Sealants Heavy Duty Cleaner (Acid Substitute). Avoid acidic or abrasive cleaners, including vinegar, lemon juice, and bleach, because these damage the cement matrix over time.
How does the 12x12 format work in a terrazzo installation?
The 12x12 format produces a continuous surface with fewer grout joints than smaller formats, allowing the ivory base and earth-toned aggregate of Bungalow to read across a wider field. Tonal balance across the full layout requires careful curation before setting begins, as aggregate distribution becomes more visible at this scale. Set grout joints to 1/8" and plan for minor shimming and wedge spacer adjustments to maintain straight grout lines.
