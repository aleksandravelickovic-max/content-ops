About
Zia's ceramics are the design counterpoint to our handmade zellige. They were developed with a Japanese potters' village outside Nagoya, where high-firing extends back more than 1,000 years, to produce a tile precise enough for tight grout joints and clean layouts. 
The collection spans matte, satin, and glossy finishes plus the textured Field Trip: Japan series, in three core formats: 2x8 and 2x4 rectangles and 4x4 squares. Color and tone vary slightly across high-fired ceramics — less than handmade zellige, but enough that mixing boxes before the set still matters. 
Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with ceramic, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with California Prop 65. Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered edges or Schluter strips.
Sealing requirements vary by finish: matte ceramics must be sealed before grouting as a grout release; satin and glossy finishes do not require pre-grout sealing. For any material or installation questions, reach out to our team at info@ziatile.com.

How It's Made 
Ceramic tile is designed in Los Angeles and made in Japan, where the line is produced using high-temperature firing and precision glaze application. The result is a clean, precisely sized tile with consistent color across boxes, a different product category from handmade clay tile.
Clay is molded into shape, dried, glazed, and fired at high temperature. The matte, satin, and gloss finishes are achieved through different glaze formulations, each producing distinct surface character and slip-resistance performance.
Each format is produced to consistent dimensions across the line, which allows for predictable installation and clean grout lines. Subtle variation in color and surface is normal and reflects the hand-finishing step in production.

Frequently Asked Questions 
What color is Iconic Blue 4x4?
Iconic Blue is a deep, grounded blue ceramic in matte finish. The matte glaze diffuses light evenly across the surface, producing a consistent non-reflective result that holds the color at all viewing angles. Individual tiles carry slight variation from the high-fire process, and those differences distribute evenly when tiles from all boxes are blended before the dry layout.
Does Iconic Blue 4x4 require sealing before grouting?
Yes. Iconic Blue is a matte finish ceramic and must be sealed with 511 Porous Plus before grouting. The sealer acts as a grout release, preventing grout haze from bonding permanently to the matte surface. Apply in a thin, even layer and allow it to cure fully before grouting begins. Satin and glossy ceramics do not require this step. See the Ceramic Installation Guide for the complete sequence.
Can Iconic Blue 4x4 be used on a shower floor?
Yes, on both shower walls and floors. Matte ceramic has a DCOF of 0.74, well above the 0.42 wet-floor threshold. Seal before grouting as required for all matte ceramics and size grout joints to a minimum of 1/8 inch.
Can Iconic Blue 4x4 be used in a pool?
Yes. Iconic Blue 4x4 is suitable for pool waterlines, spa interiors, and fountain installations in both saltwater and chlorine environments. Apply 511 Porous Plus as a pre-grout sealer before grouting, as required for all matte ceramics.
Can Iconic Blue 4x4 be installed on an exterior floor?
No. Iconic Blue 4x4 is not suitable for exterior floor installations in either freeze/thaw or non-freeze/thaw climates. It can be used on exterior walls in non-freeze/thaw climates. For material and application questions, contact us at info@ziatile.com or 310-844-1170.
Can Iconic Blue 4x4 be mixed with other Zia ceramics?
Yes. Iconic Blue 4x4 combines with other colorways and finishes across Zia's ceramics collection. Within each format size group, all standard ceramics share consistent dimensions. When mixing finishes, apply the pre-grout sealer to matte tiles only and allow it to cure fully before grouting the full field. Lay out the complete installation dry before applying any adhesive to confirm color balance and finish transitions.
