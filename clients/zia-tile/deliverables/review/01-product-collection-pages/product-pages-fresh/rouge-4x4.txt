We recommend ordering 15–25% overage so the installation has room for hand-selection, color blending, and the tile breakage inherent to the material. Before laying any piece, unpack every box and mix the tiles on a flat surface to plan the layout and confirm the color balance.


Zellige tile installation is labor-intensive, often likened more to masonry than traditional tiling due to the irregular nature of the handmade material. Plan for installations 2–3 times longer than standard ceramic. Every piece needs to be sorted, soaked, arranged, and hand-adjusted for size.

About
No two zellige tiles are the same. Pieces vary in size, surface, and glaze across each box, with hairline crazing and chamfered edges that come from how the material is hand-formed rather than flaws to correct.


Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with zellige, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with California Prop 65. Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered cuts or Schluter strips. Traditional trim and edge pieces do not exist for zellige.


Seal zellige according to our installation instructions, whether the application is dry or exposed to moisture (pools, spas, showers). Reach out to our team at info@ziatile.com with any material or installation questions.

How It's Made
Zellige is hand-formed in Morocco, where the technique has been refined for more than 800 years and passed across generations of artisans.


The process begins with raw clay, which is mined from local hillsides, soaked, filtered for impurities, and hand-pressed into molds. The tiles are laid to dry in the Moroccan sun, flattened and shaped by hand, then dried further before glazing.


Each tile is hand-dipped in glaze and arranged inside an earthen kiln, where uneven heat creates the tonal variation that defines zellige. No two pieces leave the kiln the same, and the handmade nature and variation are hallmarks of this material. To prepare for installation, open and mix all boxes before setting them to achieve an ideal layout. The tiles will carry the marks of the day they were fired.

Frequently Asked Questions
What color is Rouge zellige?
Rouge zellige is a deep red with a glaze that shifts in tone depending on light and viewing angle. The depth of the red varies across tiles; some carry a richer, more saturated tone, others sit slightly lighter, because the hand-dipping process and the uneven heat of the earthen kiln produce different results each time. 
Can Rouge zellige be used in a shower?
Yes. Rouge 4x4 is suitable for shower walls, shower floors, and steam showers. Sealing is required for all shower applications. For shower floor installations, slip resistance can be increased by sizing grout joints to a minimum of ⅛" and applying an anti-slip treatment after installation is complete. Refer to the Zellige Installation Guide for full instructions.
Does the Rouge colorway vary between production runs?
Yes. Each batch of zellige is produced by hand, and clay, glaze, and kiln conditions shift from one run to the next. Tiles from separate orders may differ in tone and surface character. Ordering your full quantity at once ensures the most consistent appearance across your project.
Can Rouge zellige be used in a pool or spa?
Yes. Rouge 4x4 is suitable for pool and spa applications in both saltwater and chlorine environments. Sealing is required; apply sealer to all tiles and grout lines exposed to continuous moisture after grouting is complete. Consult the Zellige Installation Guide for specific sealing instructions and reach out to our team at info@ziatile.com or 310-844-1170 with project-specific questions.
Can I use Rouge as an accent rather than a full field?
Yes. Rouge works well as an accent, border, or feature wall. The saturated depth of the red draws the eye, making it ideal for defining a specific zone or framing a neutral field tile. Use Rouge to anchor a kitchen island, define a bathroom niche, or create a fireplace surround. Plan the accent layout during dry layout to ensure the color reads as intentional.
How should I prepare Rouge tiles before installation?
Before you lay out the installation, open all boxes and mix the tiles together. This distributes the tonal variation across the full field rather than concentrating darker or lighter tiles in one area. Arrange them by thickness so you have a level plane to work with. Set aside any pieces you'll need for cuts or detail work.
