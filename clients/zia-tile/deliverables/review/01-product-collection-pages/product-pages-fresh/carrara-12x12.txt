Order Details + Installation
Marble is a stone with significant variations in color, tone, veining, and surface texture, along with naturally occurring imperfections. Images shown are representative of a sample of this material and may not display all potential variation. A minimum of 15–20% overage is recommended to account for curation and cuts, though additional overage allows for an even more controlled appearance.
Careful installation is essential with this soft, porous stone. Before setting any marble, unpack and mix pieces from all boxes to balance color and veining across the layout. Use a thinset suitable for natural stone and ensure full back-buttering for complete coverage, then seal the stone twice – before and after grouting. Clean only with pH-neutral products, resealing periodically as needed.

About
Marble has been quarried continuously since Roman antiquity, and Zia's marble is cut from blocks pulled from quarries across Italy and Spain. Each block is finished near Verona, where stone masons hone the surface to the soft matte finish that defines the line.


The line includes six core colorways: Carrara, Nero Marquina, Grigio Carnico, Rosso Alicante, Giallo Reale, and Verde Alpi. Together, they cover the full range of designed marble installations, from the quiet veining of Carrara to the deep black field of Nero Marquina and the warm ochres of Giallo Reale, and they pair cleanly with the patterned compositions in our Marble Patterns line.


Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with marble, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with California Prop 65. Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered edges or Schluter strips.

How It's Made
Marble begins as an ancient metamorphic rock, compressed under heat and pressure over millions of years. Zia's marble range is quarried across Italy and Spain, and Italy has been the principal source of architectural marble in the West since Roman antiquity.


Each block is cut into the format dimensions and honed by stone masons in workshops near Verona, producing the soft matte surface that defines the line. Veining and mineral variation are inherent to the stone, and no two pieces are identical.


Marble is a living material. It develops a natural patina with use, taking on the soft luster that has carried Roman floors and Renaissance halls into the present, and that becomes part of the story of any room it is set into.

Frequently Asked Questions
What does the Carrara colorway look like, and where is it quarried?
Carrara is a honed marble quarried across Italy and Spain, cut near Verona. The Carrara colorway carries the veining and tonal variation inherent to the stone, with each piece showing the mineral character of the quarry source. Before installation, lay out all pieces on a flat surface to confirm the color balance and veining distribution reads evenly throughout the installation.
What variation should I expect across Carrara 12x12 pieces?
Carrara carries the variation inherent to natural marble: tonal differences across pieces, veining patterns, natural fissures, and minor surface inclusions are all characteristics of the stone. There is a face and an underside to each marble piece; they are not reversible, so ensure each stone is installed face side up. Blend pieces from all boxes before setting to distribute veining and tonal variation evenly across the field.
Can Carrara 12x12 marble be used outdoors in a freeze/thaw climate?
No. Carrara 12x12 marble is not rated for exterior floor installations in freeze/thaw climates. Marble is susceptible to freeze/thaw cycling, which can cause the stone to crack or spall under repeated expansion and contraction. For outdoor floor applications, Carrara is suited to non-freeze/thaw climates only: patios, courtyards, and pool surrounds in mild-weather regions. For exterior wall use, apply a penetrating sealer before and after grouting.
Is Carrara 12x12 suitable for shower floors and wet areas?
Yes. Carrara 12x12 is suited to shower wall and floor installations. Honed marble carries a DCOF that varies by specific stone type; the Marble Installation Guide and your licensed installer can provide current slip-resistance guidance for your application. 


For shower floor and wet-floor installations, widen grout joints to a minimum of 1/8" and apply an anti-slip treatment after installation as a precaution. Sealing is required for all wet applications.
Does Carrara 12x12 require sealing?
Yes. All marble requires a two-stage sealing sequence. Before grouting, apply 511 Porous Plus penetrating sealer in thin layers until the stone is fully saturated, then allow it to cure. After the grout cures, apply Fila Matte Finish Protective Wax for interior installations or a second coat of 511 Porous Plus for exterior applications. Acidic cleaners must be avoided at every stage as they will damage the stone. Full instructions are in the Marble Installation Guide.
Can Carrara 12x12 be used in a pool or spa?
No. Carrara 12x12 marble is not rated for pool or spa applications. Marble is a natural, porous stone not designed for continuous submersion or exposure to chlorinated or saltwater environments. For pool and spa applications across the Zia collection, zellige and Cotto Allende are the appropriate material categories.
How does the Carrara 12x12 relate to the rest of Zia's marble collection?
Carrara is one of six core colorways in the Zia marble line, alongside Nero Marquina, Grigio Carnico, Rosso Alicante, Giallo Reale, and Verdi Alpi. The Carrara colorway anchors the collection and serves as the base for patterned marble compositions in the Marble Patterns line. 


The 12x12 square format is suited to large-format floor fields and full-height wall applications where the stone surface reads with minimal grout interruption. All six colorways share the same honed finish and require the same two-stage sealing protocol.
