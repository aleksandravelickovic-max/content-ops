## How It's Made -- \[ACCORDION\]

Each Aegean 4x4 starts as raw clay sourced from the hillsides outside Fez. Artisans soak it, filter it, and work it by hand until it holds the right consistency for pressing into molds. From that point forward, every step is done individually, tile by tile.

  

After drying in the sun, each piece is hand-shaped before being dipped in glaze. The depth of the Aegean blue forms at this stage, and because each dip is done by hand, no two tiles carry exactly the same tone coming out of it.

  

Firing takes place in earthen kilns where the heat is uneven by design. The chips, pits, crazing, and tonal variation that result are not flaws to be corrected. They are the material expressing the conditions of its production, which is what makes each Aegean 4x4 entirely its own.

## Frequently Asked Questions -- \[ACCORDION\]  

### What color is Aegean zellige?

Aegean zellige is a deep blue zellige with a glaze that shifts in tone depending on the light and angle. The name references the Aegean Sea, and the colorway carries that association in the way the glaze moves between deeper and lighter blue tones across a full installation. No two tiles carry exactly the same tone due to the hand-dipping process and the uneven heat of the kiln.

### Does Aegean zellige work in a bathroom?

Yes, Aegean zellige works particularly well in a bathroom. The deep blue glaze interacts with the light conditions typical of bathroom spaces, reading differently under natural daylight than under warmer artificial light in the evening. On shower walls and bathroom walls, the surface variation and tonal shifts are experienced at close range, which is where the handcrafted quality of the glaze is most visible.

### Can Aegean zellige be used in a pool or spa?

Yes, our Aegean zellige 4x4 functions reliably in both saltwater and chlorine environments. The deep blue glaze is particularly well-suited to pool and spa applications where the color reinforces the water context. Sealing is required. Apply 511 Porous Plus to all tiles and grout lines exposed to continuous moisture after grouting is complete. Consult the Zellige Installation Guide for full sealing instructions.

### Can I mix Aegean zellige with other colors in the collection?

Yes, our Aegean zellige 4x4 can be mixed with other colors in Zia's zellige collection. Each colorway is produced using the same traditional methods, so tiles share consistent thickness, format, and surface character across the range. Aegean pairs particularly well with lighter neutrals in the collection, where the contrast in tone creates a layered, considered composition without competing colorways.

### Does the Aegean colorway vary between orders?

Yes, the Aegean colorway can vary between production runs. Each batch of zellige is produced by hand, and the clay, glaze, and kiln conditions shift from one run to the next. Tiles from separate orders may differ in tone and surface character. If the project requires tiles from multiple orders, ordering the full quantity at once gives the most consistent result across the installation.

### How does Aegean zellige compare to Tidepool zellige?

Aegean and Tidepool are both blue-toned zellige colorways, but they sit in different parts of the color spectrum. Aegean reads as a deep, singular blue, with tonal shifts that stay within the blue range. Tidepool moves across a broader range, straddling blue-green tones. Aegean tends to read as more grounded and directional in an installation. The choice between the two depends on whether a focused, deep blue or a more dynamic, color-shifting surface is the design intent.
