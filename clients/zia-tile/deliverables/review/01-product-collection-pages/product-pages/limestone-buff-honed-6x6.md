## How It's Made — \[ACCORDION\]

Zia's Buff Honed Limestone 6x6 is quarried outside of Fez, Morocco, where local stone masons have worked this material for generations. Each block is formed over millennia from compressed sedimentary deposits, carrying color, tone, and surface variation that no cutting or finishing process removes.

  

Once quarried, blocks are cut into individual 6x6 tiles and brought to the finishing stage. Each surface is mechanically ground with progressively finer abrasives until it reaches a smooth, flat, matte result. The honing process does not seal the stone. It reveals it, leaving natural markings and tonal shifts intact

  

Over time, foot traffic and exposure will develop a soft patina on the surface. This is expected behavior for limestone and is considered part of its character in a long-term installation.

## Frequently Asked Questions — \[ACCORDION\]  

### What is honed limestone?

Honed limestone is produced by grinding the stone's surface with fine abrasives until it reaches a smooth, flat, matte result. The process removes surface irregularities without introducing a reflective sheen, leaving the stone's natural color and markings visible. The matte surface also provides better traction than polished stone, making it practical for both floor and wall applications.

### Is honed limestone good for a bathroom floor?

Yes, honed limestone is suitable for a bathroom floor. Its matte, non-glossy surface provides better traction in wet areas, reducing the risk of slips compared to polished stone. The limestone honed finish hides scratches, wear, and water spots more effectively.

### Can honed limestone be used outdoors?

Yes, honed limestone may be used on exterior floors and walls in non-freeze/thaw climates. It is not suitable for outdoor installations where snow or ice are present. For outdoor applications, seal with 511 Porous Plus only. Do not use Fila Matte Finish Protective Wax outdoors, as UV exposure causes discoloration over time.

### How do I maintain honed limestone over time?

To maintain honed limestone over time, re-seal as often as needed based on traffic and moisture exposure. Clean with pH-neutral products only and address spills promptly. Over time, the stone will develop a natural patina, which is an expected characteristic of limestone.

### What grout should I use with honed limestone?

Mapei Flexcolor CQ works well with honed limestone. A lighter neutral or a color matched closely to the stone tends to produce the most cohesive result. Contrasting grout colors can stain the surface and may be difficult to remove from natural stone if excess grout is not wiped away promptly.

### What causes the variation in honed limestone tiles?

The variation in honed limestone tiles comes from the stone's natural formation process. Limestone is a sedimentary rock built up over millennia from compressed mineral deposits. The honing process reveals these characteristics rather than concealing them, which is why variation across an order is expected and considered part of the material's defining quality.
