## How It's Made

Zia's Carbon Black 4x4 Zellige tile is produced through an artisanal tilemaking process that preserves the structural unpredictability of authentic zellige manufacturing.

Clay is sourced from Moroccan hillsides and processed through repeated soaking and filtration to remove impurities and stabilize texture. Artisans then hand-press the clay into molds, forming the base structure of each tile before allowing it to dry slowly under controlled sun exposure. This stage regulates moisture loss and introduces early surface tension variations.

Once partially cured, each tile is hand-shaped into its final square profile. No mechanical calibration is used, which results in slight dimensional shifts that remain visible in the finished installation.

Glazing is applied by hand dipping each tile into pigment baths. This process creates uneven glaze accumulation that becomes more pronounced during firing. Tiles are then placed into an earthen kiln where heat flow is intentionally inconsistent. This uneven firing process produces tonal layering, surface pooling, and variation in sheen across the batch.

The final result is a surface system defined by controlled irregularity, where every tile reflects the perfectly imperfect beauty that results from artisanal handling and kiln behavior.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the characteristics of Carbon Black Zellige tiles?

Carbon Black 4x4 Zellige tiles are defined by a deep, highly pigmented black surface with subtle tonal shifts created through hand-applied glaze and kiln variation. Each tile features an uneven profile, including irregular edges and surface movement, along with chips, pits, and crazing that are inherent to Zia Tile Zellige production methods. 

### What color looks good with the Carbon Black Zellige tile?

Carbon Black Zellige tile pairs most effectively with light neutral tones such as white, ivory, or warm beige, which highlight its reflective surface and create contrast within a Zellige tile pattern. It also works with natural wood, stone, and metallic finishes like brass to emphasize depth and material layering. In more expressive compositions, saturated tones such as deep green or muted terra cotta can enhance the richness of the black Zellige tile without overwhelming the surface.

### Is the Carbon Black 4x4 Zellige tile good for floors?

Yes, Carbon Black 4x4 zellige can be used on floors. When used on floors, professional installation with proper grout joints and careful layout planning is required to manage variation and ensure surface balance. For wet floor applications, sizing grout joints to a minimum of ⅛" and/or applying an anti-slip treatment after installation supports long-term performance and safety.

### What tile to pair with the Carbon Black Zellige?

Carbon Black zellige pairs well with lighter tiles in Zia's zellige collection, particularly soft white, cream, or sand-toned colorways that create contrast against the deep surface. It also combines well with neutral stone or handmade ceramic tiles to build layered mixed-material installations. For more structured designs, pairing with lighter-toned natural wood helps establish rhythm and separation between surfaces.

### Are Zellige tiles difficult to clean?

No, zellige tiles are not difficult to clean when maintained correctly, though they require care consistent with handmade glazed surfaces. Clean with pH-neutral products to preserve glaze integrity and surface variation. Avoid harsh acids, bleach, and abrasive cleaners, which can affect the finish over time. In wet areas, sealing supports long-term performance and protects the natural surface characteristics of the tile.
