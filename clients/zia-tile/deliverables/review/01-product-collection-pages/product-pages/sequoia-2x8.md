## How It's Made

Zia's Sequoia 2x8 is produced outside Nagoya, Japan, in a region where ceramic production has been practiced for over 1,000 years. The process begins with carefully selected raw materials that are refined and formed through an extrusion process to achieve the consistent 2x8 format.

Once formed, tiles are dried under controlled conditions to stabilize the clay body and prevent warping. The tiles then undergo high-temperature kiln firing, which defines their density, strength, and low porosity. This firing stage is critical for performance across walls, backsplashes, and wet environments.

The satin glaze is applied before firing and reacts to kiln heat to produce a smooth, low-sheen surface with stable evergreen tonal behavior. Variations in temperature and tile placement within the kiln influence how the glaze settles, producing controlled tonal shifts across the surface. These differences become visible across larger wall and floor installations, where surface depth is defined by the glaze response rather than a uniform color application.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is the Sequoia ceramic tile good for shower walls?

Yes, the Sequoia ceramic tile is good for shower walls. Zia's Sequoia 2x8 functions as a reliable ceramic shower tile due to its high-fired construction and satin finish. It performs well in wet environments when properly installed, and sealing is required to support long-term performance on shower walls.

### Does the Sequoia 2x8 require sealing?

Yes, the Sequoia 2x8 requires sealing. As part of proper installation, apply a penetrating sealer before grouting to act as a grout release and reduce the risk of staining or haze. Resealing over time supports the longevity of the surface and grout joints.

### Is the Sequoia ceramic tile suitable for a kitchen backsplash?

Yes, the Sequoia ceramic tile is suitable for a kitchen backsplash. The 2x8 format creates a directional layout, while the satin surface supports easy maintenance. As a ceramic tile for kitchen backsplash systems, it provides a durable, cleanable surface that holds up to daily use.

### How to clean a ceramic tile?

To clean a ceramic tile, remove loose debris with sweeping or vacuuming, then clean with warm water and a pH-neutral solution. Avoid acidic or abrasive cleaners, as they can affect the finish and grout joints. Rinse thoroughly and dry the surface to prevent residue buildup.

### Is ceramic tile high maintenance?

No, ceramic tile is not high maintenance. It is a durable and water-resistant material that requires only routine cleaning with a pH-neutral solution. Periodic grout maintenance and sealing keep the installation performing consistently across both ceramic tile walls and floors.

### How long will ceramic tiles last?

Ceramic tiles will last for decades when installed and maintained correctly. High-fired ceramic surfaces retain their structure and finish over time, making them a long-term solution for ceramic tile flooring and wall applications in residential and commercial settings.
