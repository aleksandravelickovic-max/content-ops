## How It's Made

Our 8x9 Hex + Red Clay is produced in San Miguel de Allende, Mexico, where locally sourced clay is processed into a fired material defined by density, porosity, and mineral-driven variation. The clay is extracted from regional deposits, then soaked and filtered to remove inconsistencies while preserving its natural composition. Each tile is hand-pressed into a hexagonal mold rather than mechanically extruded, which creates controlled irregularity in edge definition and thickness and introduces the subtle dimensional shifts that become visible once the field is installed.

  

After forming, each tile is air-dried for one to two weeks. This drying phase reduces internal moisture gradually and stabilizes the clay body without flattening the natural movement in the material. The tiles are then fired in traditional kilns at high temperatures, where heat distribution shifts naturally across the chamber. This variation produces the tonal range that defines the Red Clay colorway.

  

The tiles are kept unglazed to preserve the raw clay surface, which is what allows the material to develop a natural patina over time. Because the palette comes from the clay itself rather than an applied glaze, the color is embedded in the full body of the tile, which is part of why unglazed cotto deepens into a richer patina rather than wearing thin over time.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is cotto tile?

Cotto is a handmade terra cotta tile made from high-fired, unglazed clay that preserves the natural behavior of fired earth. Each tile is formed from mineral-rich clay and kiln-fired without a surface coating, which produces the visible tonal movement, matte texture, and inherent variation in tone, shape, edge profile, and thickness that define the material. These characteristics reflect traditional terra cotta methods rather than indicating defects.

### What are the characteristics of the 8x9 Hex + Red Clay?

The 8x9 Hex + Red Clay is a hexagon terra cotta tile with deep red, burnt orange, and clay-based tonal variation produced through kiln exposure and mineral composition. Each tile measures 8" x 9" in a hexagonal geometry with a 5/8" thickness, designed for durable floor and wall installations. The unglazed surface produces natural variation in tone, shape, edge profile, and thickness across each installation — characteristics inherent to handmade cotto.

### Is the 8x9 Hex + Red Clay good for flooring?

Yes, the 8x9 Hex + Red Clay is well suited to flooring in both residential and commercial environments. The dense, high-fired clay body supports high-traffic use while maintaining surface variation over time. The unglazed surface provides natural traction, which makes the format appropriate for kitchens, entryways, and interior circulation spaces. When properly sealed and maintained, the tile develops a stable patina that reinforces its long-term character rather than showing surface wear.

### Can a hexagon terra cotta tile make a room look bigger?

Yes, a hexagon terra cotta tile layout can visually expand a space by breaking up the rigid grid patterns typical of square formats. The interlocking geometry creates directional movement across the surface, which allows the eye to read the floor as a continuous field rather than segmented units. Larger hex formats also reduce grout line density, which strengthens the perception of uninterrupted flooring. The effect is most pronounced when grout joints are closely matched to the clay tone, minimizing visual breaks across the installation.

### How do I care for terra cotta floor tiles?

Caring for terra cotta floor tiles requires pH-neutral cleaning products and consistent maintenance that protects the porous clay body. Routine cleaning should include dry sweeping or vacuuming, followed by damp mopping with a non-acidic solution. Avoid abrasive or acidic cleaners, which can affect sealed surfaces and interrupt patina development. Periodic resealing is required to maintain resistance against moisture and staining, particularly in high-use installations such as kitchens and entryways. Over time, the surface evolves naturally into a patina that is characteristic of fired clay rather than a sign of wear.

Each colorway is produced using the same clay body and firing process, so tiles share consistent thickness and surface character across the range. The deep red of Red Clay pairs naturally with the classic Adobe and Fired Earth tones, or contrasts effectively against the unexpected tones of Blanco, Madera, and Oscura.
