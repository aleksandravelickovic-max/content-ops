## How It's Made

Our Gambit 1 begins in the same place as all traditional zellige. The clay is pulled from the hillsides surrounding Fez, Morocco, soaked in water, and filtered for impurities before being worked by hand until it reaches the right consistency for pressing into molds. For Gambit 1, the molds shape the small geometric elements that make up the mosaic composition.

  

After drying in the Moroccan sun, each element is dipped individually in our Casablanca and Pure White glaze. Because the glaze is applied by hand, coverage varies from piece to piece, and that variation is what produces the tonal range visible across the finished mosaic. Each element is then hand-assembled onto a mesh backing, forming the sheets that arrive on site ready for installation.

  

The final stage is the kiln. Earthen kilns do not distribute heat evenly, and the results of that unevenness are not incidental. The chips, pits, crazing, and shifting tones that emerge from the firing process are what make each Gambit 1 mosaic its own.

## Frequently Asked Questions 

### What floors pair well with Gambit 1 zellige?

Gambit 1 zellige pairs well with flooring that balances the white glazed surface and geometric mosaic pattern. Warm wood flooring creates an organic contrast that softens the rhythm of the mosaic, while natural stone flooring reinforces the handcrafted character of the installation. 

### Can I use Gambit 1 zellige in a shower?

Yes, you can use Gambit 1 zellige in a shower, including shower walls, shower floors, and steam showers. Sealing is required for all shower applications. For shower floors, slip resistance can be increased by sizing grout joints to a minimum of ⅛" and/or applying an anti-slip treatment after installation. The mosaic format also performs particularly well on shower floors, where the smaller elements and higher grout coverage contribute to surface traction.

### What grout color works best with Gambit 1 zellige?

Grout color is a design decision that affects how the mosaic reads across the installation. A white grout matched closely to the Casablanca glaze produces a seamless surface that emphasizes the continuous field. Light grey or neutral grout softens the variation and highlights the hand-formed edges of each element. Darker grout introduces contrast and makes the geometric pattern more pronounced across the full installation.

### Is Gambit 1 zellige good for floors?

Yes, Gambit 1 zellige can be used on interior floors, particularly in low-traffic residential areas such as guest bathrooms and bedrooms. The mosaic format contributes to slip resistance in everyday conditions, and performance improves further when grout joint size is increased to a minimum of ⅛" and/or when an anti-slip treatment is applied after installation. High-traffic floor installations require careful planning to account for the inherent variation in thickness and edge profile.

### Can Gambit 1 zellige be used on exterior walls?

Yes, Gambit 1 zellige can be used on exterior walls in non-freeze/thaw climates. The mosaic performs well on vertical surfaces where the glazed finish and variation are protected from direct structural impact. It is not suitable for exterior floor installations in any climate that experiences freeze/thaw conditions. For outdoor installations, proper sealing is required to maintain surface integrity over time.

### Can I mix Gambit 1 with other colors in the collection?

Yes, you can mix Gambit 1 with other colors in Zia's zellige collection. Each colorway is produced using the same traditional methods, so tiles and mosaic sheets share consistent surface character across the range. The white Casablanca glaze of Gambit 1 works particularly well alongside deeper solid colorways, where the geometric mosaic reads as a lighter, patterned counterpoint to a larger field.
