  - Before installation, open and mix the contents of each box to distribute tonal and surface variation evenly across the layout.

### Installation and Finishing

  - All installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with cotto, thinset, sealants, and grout in accordance with California Prop 65.
  - For finishing, bring the drywall flush with the finished tile surface, miter exposed edges at 45 degrees, or complete the edge with Schluter strips.
  - Seal Stars & Cross + Red Clay with 511 Porous Plus before and after grouting. For interior applications, finish with Fila Matte Wax as a final coat. For exterior applications, apply a second coat of 511 Porous Plus in place of wax.
  - Plan the layout carefully. The Stars & Cross geometry depends on precise orientation during the dry layout stage to resolve across the full field. Grout joints should be sized to a minimum of 1/8".
  - Always seal Stars & Cross + Red Clay according to the Zia Tile Installation Guide, whether in dry spaces or in areas exposed to moisture, such as showers. Note that Stars & Cross + Red Clay is not suitable for pools or spas.

## How It's Made

Our Stars & Cross + Red Clay begins with natural clay drawn from the hills around San Miguel de Allende, a region that has produced terra cotta for centuries. The clay is refined with water until it reaches a workable density, then hand-pressed into the Stars & Cross molds that define the interlocking geometry of the format. This shaping stage is where the pattern is established, and the curves and angles of the specialty shape require careful handling throughout the process.

  

After forming, each tile is left to dry for one to two weeks under controlled conditions. This gradual drying releases moisture slowly and stabilizes the structure before firing. The tiles are then fired at high temperatures in traditional kilns, where heat distribution shifts naturally across the chamber. 

  

The tiles are kept unglazed to preserve the raw clay surface, which is what allows the material to develop a natural patina over time. The patterned cotto tile that results carries both the architectural language of the interlocking format and the earthy tonal depth of the fired clay itself.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What color is Stars & Cross + Red Clay terra cotta tile? 

The Stars & Cross + Red Clay terra cotta tile features a warm red clay base that shifts across earthy red, brick, and orange-brown tones. Each handmade terra cotta tile carries natural firing variation, so no two pieces read exactly the same once installed. The overall effect across terra cotta tile flooring is a continuous field of warm, sun-baked clay color with subtle tonal movement.

### What paint color goes with Stars & Cross + Red Clay terra cotta tile?

Stars & Cross + Red Clay pairs best with paint colors that either soften or contrast its warm clay base. White, cream, and off-white expand light and keep the tile visually grounded. Soft beige and muted grey maintain a neutral field that lets the pattern lead. Navy, charcoal, and deep green create a higher contrast that sharpens the geometry of the interlocking shape and draws more attention to the pattern itself.

### Can I use Stars & Cross + Red Clay terra cotta tile in the kitchen? 

Yes, Stars & Cross + Red Clay can be used in a kitchen for both floors and walls. The tile performs well in backsplash and flooring applications when properly sealed to protect against moisture and staining. For kitchen floors, sealing before and after grouting is required to support long-term durability and surface performance in high-use areas, and a minimum 1/8" grout joint is recommended.

### What are the characteristics of terra cotta tiles? 

Zia terra cotta is formed from natural clay that is shaped, dried, and kiln-fired, producing visible variation in tone, shape, edge profile, and thickness. Each handmade tile carries inherent differences in color and surface density, and these differences become more pronounced across a full installation. This variation defines the material's surface behavior rather than indicating defects.

### How to seal terra cotta tile?

To seal Stars & Cross + Red Clay, apply 511 Porous Plus before grouting to saturate the surface and reduce absorption. After grouting, apply a second coat of 511 Porous Plus to protect both the tile and grout. For interior applications, finish with Fila Matte Wax as a final protective coat. For exterior applications, apply a second coat of 511 Porous Plus in place of wax. Periodic resealing is required to maintain resistance to moisture and staining while preserving the natural surface patina.
