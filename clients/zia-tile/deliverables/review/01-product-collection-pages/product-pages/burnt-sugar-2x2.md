## How It's Made

Our Burnt Sugar 2x2 is produced in Fez, Morocco, through the same traditional process that defines all Zia zellige. Raw clay is pulled from local hillsides, soaked in water, filtered for impurities, and worked by hand until it reaches the right consistency for pressing into 2x2 square molds. Each tile is then shaped and refined by hand before drying in the Moroccan sun.



After drying, each tile is dipped individually in glaze. The Burnt Sugar glaze is where the dark brown Moroccan tile takes form, with undertones emerging through hand-dipped application. Because each dip is done by hand, the coverage varies from tile to tile, and that variation produces the layered tonal movement visible across the full installation.



The final stage is the kiln. Earthen kilns do not distribute heat evenly, and the results of that unevenness are not incidental. The chips, pits, crazing, and shifting tones that emerge from the firing process are what make each Burnt Sugar 2x2 its own.

### Can Burnt Sugar 2x2 zellige be used in a kitchen?

Yes, Burnt Sugar 2x2 zellige is suitable for kitchen applications, particularly as a brown backsplash tile. The glazed surface handles heat and moisture on vertical surfaces, and the dark brown tonal range introduces depth across the wall plane. The irregular edges support tight, close-set installation layouts where the zellige pattern develops through natural alignment rather than uniform spacing.

### Is Burnt Sugar zellige good for a bathroom floor?

Yes, Burnt Sugar zellige is suitable for bathroom floors in low- to moderate-traffic residential areas. The compact 2x2 format and handmade edges require careful leveling during installation. For wet floor applications, size grout joints to a minimum of 1/8" and/or apply an anti-slip treatment after installation. Zellige is more commonly specified for walls and shower installations, though floor use is well supported with proper preparation.

### Do zellige tiles need sealing?

Yes, zellige tiles require sealing in all wet and submerged applications. The glazed surface itself is water-resistant, but sealing protects the grout joints and installation system, and supports long-term performance in showers, pools, and spas. Sealing is strongly recommended in any area exposed to frequent moisture, and is required for pool, spa, and shower installations.

### Can I mix Burnt Sugar with other zellige colors in the collection?

Yes, you can mix Burnt Sugar 2x2 with other colors in Zia's zellige collection. Each colorway is produced using the same traditional methods, so tiles share consistent surface character across the range. Burnt Sugar pairs particularly well with lighter neutrals, warm creams, and softer earth tones in the collection, where the dark caramel palette reads as a focal accent against a quieter surrounding field.

### What grout color works best with Burnt Sugar zellige?

The best grout color for Burnt Sugar zellige depends on how the installation should read. A grout matched closely to the dark brown glaze produces a seamless, monolithic surface where the individual tiles recede and the tonal movement carries the visual field. A lighter cream or warm neutral grout defines each tile more clearly and emphasizes the 2x2 geometry. Mid-tone grey and soft brown grouts sit between the two, softening the grid while preserving tonal depth.
