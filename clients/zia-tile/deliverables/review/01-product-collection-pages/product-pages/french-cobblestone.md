  - All installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with limestone, thinset, sealants, and grout in accordance with California Prop 65.

## How It's Made

Zia's French Cobblestone Limestone Tile is quarried near Bordeaux, France, a region where limestone has shaped construction methods for centuries. The material draws from the architectural traditions of the Dordogne region, where limestone flooring tile and wall applications define historic structures through durability and material consistency. 

  

Each block is extracted from sedimentary deposits formed through compressed marine matter over time. Local stone masons cut the blocks into cobblestone pieces, following natural fracture lines within the limestone. This process creates variable lengths and irregular edges that define the cobblestone format.

  

The surface is finished in either honed or bush-hammered form. Honed limestone tile is mechanically ground to produce a flat, matte surface suited to interior floors and walls. Bush-hammered limestone introduces a textured finish that increases surface grip, making it suitable for outdoor limestone tile applications where additional traction is required.

No artificial finishes or coatings are applied. The color, fossil markings, and tonal variation visible in each French limestone tile are inherent to the stone itself and remain consistent across horizontal and vertical installations.

## Frequently Asked Questions 

### Is French Cobblestone limestone a good floor tile? 

Yes, French Cobblestone limestone is a good floor tile for both residential and commercial applications. This limestone flooring tile is dense enough to handle high-traffic areas such as kitchens, entryways, and primary bathrooms when properly installed and sealed. The cobblestone format introduces natural variation across the surface, while the stone itself provides long-term performance and develops a patina over time that reflects normal use rather than wear.

### What color is the French Cobblestone limestone tile?

French Cobblestone limestone tile presents a neutral, earth-based palette that typically includes soft ivory, warm beige, light taupe, and muted grey tones. These colors appear with natural shifts across each limestone tile due to mineral composition and fossil presence. The result is a consistent tonal range across the installation without uniform repetition, making it suitable for both limestone floor tile and wall applications.

### Which tile to pair with French Cobblestone limestone?

French Cobblestone limestone pairs with materials that either match its neutral tone or contrast its surface texture. This French limestone tile works well alongside wood-look tiles, cement tiles, or smoother stone surfaces to create balance across a space. In limestone tile backsplash or mixed-material installations, grout selection plays a dynamic role, with color-matched grout producing a continuous field and contrasting grout emphasizing the cobblestone pattern.

### How to clean French limestone?

To clean French limestone, use a pH-neutral stone cleaner with water and a soft cloth or mop. Avoid acidic or abrasive cleaning products, including vinegar and bleach, as they can damage the surface. Regular sweeping removes abrasive debris, while prompt cleaning of spills helps prevent staining. Proper sealing supports easier maintenance over time.

### Is French limestone durable?

Yes, French limestone is highly durable when used in appropriate applications and maintained correctly. While strong under foot traffic, limestone remains a porous natural stone and requires sealing to protect against moisture and staining. With proper care, limestone maintains its structural integrity and develops a natural surface patina over time.
