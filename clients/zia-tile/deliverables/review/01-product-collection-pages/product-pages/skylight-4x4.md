## How It's Made

The clay that becomes each Skylight 4x4 is pulled from the hillsides surrounding Fez, Morocco. Artisans soak it in water, filter it for impurities, and work it by hand until it reaches the right consistency for pressing into molds. The shaping is done entirely by hand, which is why no two tiles sit at exactly the same depth or carry the same edge.

After drying in the Moroccan sun, each tile is dipped individually in glaze. The Skylight glaze is where the sky-blue tones take form. Because each dip is done by hand, the coverage varies from tile to tile, and that variation is what produces the tonal range visible across a full installation.

The final stage is the kiln. Earthen kilns do not distribute heat evenly, and the results of that unevenness are not incidental. The chips, pits, crazing, and shifting tones that emerge from the firing process are what make each Skylight 4x4 its own.
