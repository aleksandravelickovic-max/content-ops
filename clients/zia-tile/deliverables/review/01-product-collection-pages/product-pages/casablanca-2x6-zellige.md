## How It's Made — \[ACCORDION\]

Our Casablanca 2x6 carries the legacy of **Moroccan craftsmanship** in a rectangular format, produced using the same traditional methods artisans have refined over centuries.

  

The process begins with **raw clay mined** from local hillsides. Artisans soak and filter the clay to remove impurities, then press the malleable material into slabs and set them to dry in the Moroccan sun. Once the clay reaches the correct consistency, each 2x6 is hand-shaped into its final rectangular profile.

  

Artisans then hand-dip each piece in glaze before placing it in an earthen kiln. Heat distributes unevenly throughout the kiln, creating variation and dynamic glazes across the surface. Each tile reflects the conditions under which it was fired and the work of the artisan.

## Frequently Asked Questions — \[ACCORDION\]  

### Which orientation is best for the Casablanca 2x6 rectangle?

The Casablanca 2x6 rectangle can be installed in either a horizontal running bond or a vertical stack. A horizontal layout creates a **classic subway appearance**, while a vertical stack emphasizes height. Each tile's variation will remain visible in either orientation.

### Can a herringbone pattern be created with Casablanca 2x6 zellige tiles?

Yes, a **herringbone pattern** works with Casablanca 2x6 zellige. The rectangular Bejmat format supports geometric layouts such as herringbone and **basketweave**. A dry layout before installation helps confirm alignment.

### Do the Casablanca 2x6 rectangles require a specific grout width?

Hand-shaped edges vary from tile to tile, which allows for a traditional installation with a close grout joint. For wet floor applications, increase **grout joint** size to a minimum of ⅛" and consider applying an anti-slip treatment after installation is complete.

### How does the 2x6 format compare to the 4x4 in smaller spaces?

The rectangular profile makes the 2x6 particularly effective in tight or narrow installations. A vertical stack on a compact bathroom wall creates the impression of height, while a horizontal running bond on a short backsplash extends the visual width of the space.

### Can Casablanca 2x6 be mixed with other colors in the collection?

Yes, Zia's Casablanca 2x6 Bejmat can be mixed with other colors in the Zellige collection. Each tile shares the same production method, allowing for cohesive multi-tone installations. Laying out the tile before installation helps control color distribution.

### How do the hand-shaped edges of the 2x6 affect the final layout?

The hand-shaped edges of the Casablanca 2x6 create variation across the final layout. This prevents a uniform machine-cut line and reinforces the character of handmade Moroccan zellige.
