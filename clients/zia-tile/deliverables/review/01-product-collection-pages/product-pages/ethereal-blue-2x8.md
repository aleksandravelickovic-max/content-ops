## How It's Made

Zia's Ethereal Blue 2x8 is produced in and around Nagoya, Japan, a region with more than a millennium of ceramic craft history. Clay is refined and formed into the 2x8 subway format using precision molds that control shape consistency while still allowing material responsiveness during firing. Each tile is then dried under regulated conditions to stabilize moisture content before glazing begins.

  

The glossy surface is created through a hand-applied glaze that interacts with the tile differently depending on thickness and placement. High-temperature firing locks in both the structure and the glaze, producing density, water resistance, and a reflective finish that responds directly to surrounding light conditions.

  

During kiln firing, controlled heat variation produces the final surface behavior of Ethereal Blue. This stage defines the depth and movement of the glaze, where color settles into layered blue tones rather than a flat pigment application. Each tile exits the kiln with calibrated but visible variation that accentuates the character of the installation, particularly across large-scale wall and floor fields.

## Frequently Asked Questions 

### What color is Ethereal Blue ceramic?

Ethereal Blue ceramic is a deep, saturated blue with glaze shifts that move between tones depending on light and viewing angle. The glossy finish amplifies the depth of the color, which is why the tile reads differently on a backsplash versus a shower wall versus a pool surround. Each tile carries a subtle tonal variation that becomes most visible once installed as a full field.

  

### Is Ethereal Blue ceramic tile suitable for the kitchen?

Ethereal Blue is well-suited to kitchen installations, particularly as a backsplash where the glossy surface reflects light and defines the vertical plane behind cooking areas. The elongated 2x8 subway format supports horizontal running bond, vertical stack, and herringbone arrangements, each of which changes how the color reads across the space. For kitchen floor applications, slip resistance should be confirmed with the installer based on project conditions.

### Is Ethereal Blue ceramic tile suitable for bathroom floors?

Ethereal Blue can be installed in a bathroom, including on shower walls, shower floors, and as a vanity backsplash. The glossy glaze stands up well to moisture exposure when properly installed and sealed. For shower floor applications, slip resistance can be increased by sizing grout joints to a minimum of ⅛" and/or applying an anti-slip treatment after installation to improve traction in wet conditions.

### How long will ceramic tiles last?

Properly installed ceramic tile can last several decades, often exceeding 20–50 years depending on installation quality, traffic conditions, and maintenance. In stable interior environments such as walls and low-impact floors, ceramic tile performance can extend significantly longer. Longevity is influenced by substrate preparation, grout maintenance, and the correct use of sealing systems in wet areas.

### Can Ethereal Blue be used in a pool or spa?

Yes, Ethereal Blue 2x8 subway tile is suitable for pools and spas, including saltwater and chlorine environments. The deep blue palette reads particularly well underwater, where the reflective surface interacts with the movement of the water. Sealing is required before grouting, and periodic resealing is recommended to maintain long-term performance across submerged and waterline installations.

### How to clean a ceramic tile floor?

To clean ceramic floor tile, use a pH-neutral cleaner and warm water with a soft mop or cloth. Routine sweeping or vacuuming prevents surface abrasion from grit. Avoid acidic cleaners, bleach, and abrasive pads, which can degrade the glaze over time. For grout lines, periodic gentle scrubbing helps maintain appearance without affecting the glazed tile surface.
