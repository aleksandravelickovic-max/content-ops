# Cement

## Pour. Press. Patina.

Zia's encaustic cement tiles are handmade using techniques established by French and Catalan craftspeople in the 1850s. Each tile is individually hand-poured into a steel mold with natural mineral pigments applied one layer at a time, then hydraulically pressed to form a dense cement body with a pigmented surface layer that becomes part of the tile itself. Unlike ceramic tile, cement tile is never fired — it air-dries. This is a defining characteristic of the material and the reason it develops a patina over time.

Tonal shifts and surface movement are inherent to the production process and should be expected across an order. Available in squares, hexagons, and mosaic layouts across a wide range of solid colors and geometric patterns, Zia's cement tile collection suits floors and walls in residential and commercial spaces alike. Cement tile is not suitable for exterior installation in freeze/thaw climates.

### How are Zia cement tiles made?

Each tile is made by hand using a steel mold, natural pigments, and a hydraulic press. Unlike ceramic tile, cement tile is formed rather than fired — this is the defining characteristic of hydraulic tile production. Patterns are created by applying individual pigments into a handmade metal frame, one layer at a time. The tile is then hydraulically pressed at approximately 3,000 PSI, soaked in water to initiate curing, and left to dry on racks for seven days. This process requires experienced artisans for the precision of pigment placement and pressing.

### Does cement tile need special care?

Yes, cement tile is porous and must be sealed before and after grouting. Sealing protects the surface and maintains long-term performance, especially in kitchens and other high-use environments.

Maintenance requirements:

  - Reseal every one to two years, or more frequently in wet or high-traffic areas
  - Apply a matte wax sealer indoors for added surface protection
  - Clean only with pH-neutral products

These steps preserve the surface as the tile ages and develops a patina, without altering its natural matte character.

### How to clean cement tile

Dry clean regularly with a soft broom, dust mop, or vacuum to prevent buildup. For wet cleaning, use a pH-neutral tile cleaner only. Avoid acidic or abrasive products, as these can stain or dull the surface. For deeper cleaning, a heavy-duty pH-neutral solution intended for natural stone such as Miracle Heavy Duty Cleaner (Acid Substitute) can be used safely on sealed surfaces.

### How do I install cement tile?

Cement tile requires a professional installer experienced with porous materials.

Before installation:

  - Blend tiles from all boxes to balance natural variation
  - Confirm the layout and dry-fit the design where applicable

During installation:

  - Use a high-quality medium-bed thinset
  - Lightly wet the back of each tile before setting
  - Press tiles into place by hand — do not use mechanical impact
  - Keep surfaces level throughout installation

Sealing and grouting:

  - Seal the tiles before grouting using a penetrating sealer
  - Grout in small 2x2 sections using Mapei Flexcolor CQ and wipe clean as work progresses
  - For dark or contrasting grout, use a grout bag to avoid staining
  - For interior installations, apply a matte protective wax as a final protective coat
  - For exterior installations, apply an additional coat of penetrating sealer instead of wax

For full sequencing and technical requirements, refer to the [Cement Tile Installation Guide](https://ziatile.com/pages/cement-installation-guide). For project-specific installation questions, the Zia team is available at info@ziatile.com or 310-844-1170.

### How does cement tile hold up over time?

Cement tile's density and thickness make it suitable for residential and commercial traffic when properly installed and maintained. Over time, cement tile develops a patina that reflects use, a defining characteristic of the material. Regular cleaning with pH-neutral products preserves the surface without altering its matte character.

### What is encaustic cement tile?

Encaustic cement tile is a handmade hydraulic tile produced by applying natural mineral pigments into a steel mold, one layer at a time, pressing the tile at high pressure, and air-curing it without firing in a kiln. The pigmented surface layer becomes part of the tile body itself during pressing — this is what gives encaustic tile its color depth and matte finish.

### How does cement tile compare to ceramic?

Cement tile and ceramic tile suit different design intentions. Cement tile is structurally denser, formed from cement, marble powder, and natural mineral pigments, then air-cured rather than fired. Each tile is hand-poured and pigment-placed before pressing, producing subtle tonal shifts and surface movement that are inherent to the process. The surface develops a patina over time.

Ceramic tile is kiln-fired and engineered for uniformity, suiting applications where a precise, repeatable surface is the goal. The choice depends on the desired finish, the degree of surface variation, and the material character the installation calls for.
