# Glass Mosaics

## Solid and Patterned Glass Mosaic Tile in Vibrant Colorways

Zia's glass mosaics collection is the brand's most sustainability-forward line, produced in Northern Spain in a facility powered primarily by renewable wind and solar energy. Each tile is made from 98% recycled glass — melted, individually formed, and fired in electric kilns — then hand-placed on 12x12 mesh-backed grids in solid and patterned configurations.

Zia's design team created seven intentional patterns, drawing from artworks, fashion textiles, and locations including Los Angeles's own Union Station. Glass is naturally stain-resistant and does not require sealing, making it one of the lower-maintenance materials in the collection. The resulting sheets are suited for kitchen backsplashes, shower walls and floors, pools, spas, and exterior surfaces.

### How are Zia glass mosaics made?

Zia's glass mosaic tiles begin with discarded glass, melted and individually formed in a mold, then fired in electric kilns powered primarily by renewable energy. Each tile is cooled, removed from its mold, and hand-placed on 12x12 grids in solid sheets and patterned compositions.

### Do glass mosaics need special care?

Glass mosaics do not require sealing. The non-porous surface is naturally resistant to staining and requires minimal long-term maintenance. Installation quality is essential for long-term performance. Zia recommends working with installers experienced in glass mosaic systems to ensure proper substrate preparation, adhesive application, and sheet alignment.

### How to clean glass mosaic tiles

Sweep or vacuum regularly to remove dust and prevent abrasion. For routine cleaning, wipe floors or walls with warm water and a soft cloth, and address spills promptly.

For deeper cleaning, a pH-neutral cleaner such as Miracle Sealants pH Neutral Tile and Stone Cleaner is recommended. For more intensive residue or buildup, Miracle Sealants Heavy Duty Cleaner (Acid Substitute) can be used safely on the surface.

### How do I install glass mosaic sheets?

Glass mosaic sheets require precision handling and experience with small-format mesh-mounted tile systems. Zia recommends working with professional installers to ensure proper alignment, adhesion, and finish consistency.

Before installation:

  - Blend sheets from all boxes to distribute tonal and pattern variation evenly
  - Ensure the substrate is clean, flat, and properly prepared

During installation:

  - Remove the paper backing from each sheet carefully
  - Apply epoxy thinset evenly in one direction to prevent air pockets
  - Press sheets firmly into place using hand pressure for full contact

After placement:

  - Allow the thinset to fully cure before grouting
  - Grout in small sections using epoxy grout and wipe clean immediately to avoid grout haze

Glass mosaics do not require sealing at any stage. For full technical specifications, refer to the [Glass Mosaics Installation Guide](https://ziatile.com/pages/glass-mosaics-installation-guide). For project-specific installation questions, the Zia team is available at info@ziatile.com or 310-844-1170.

### How are glass mosaic tiles used?

Glass mosaic tiles are used across residential and commercial applications where moisture resistance, cleanability, and visual clarity are priorities. Each sheet arrives pre-arranged on 12x12 mesh-mounted grids, allowing for precise alignment across walls and floors in solid and patterned configurations. Common applications include kitchen backsplashes, shower walls and floors, pools, spas, exterior facades, and feature walls. The small format allows the material to adapt to curved surfaces, niches, and transitions without compromising alignment.

### Can glass mosaic tile be used on a shower floor?

Yes, glass mosaic tile can be used on a shower floor. The small format of mosaic sheets increases the number of grout joints across the surface, which improves traction compared to larger-format tiles. Slip resistance can be further increased by applying an anti-slip treatment after installation. Zia recommends working with a professional installer experienced in glass mosaic systems for shower floor applications.

### How does glass mosaic tile hold up over time?

The non-porous glass body does not absorb moisture, resists staining, and maintains its surface appearance without sealing. Longevity depends on installation quality, substrate stability, and proper grout selection. Regular cleaning with a pH-neutral cleaner is sufficient for ongoing maintenance.

### How much glass mosaic tile is needed for a project?

Start with the total square footage: measure each surface in feet, multiply length by width, and add all sections together. Add a minimum of 10 to 15% overage to account for cuts and any sheets set aside during installation. For project-specific quantity guidance, contact the Zia team at info@ziatile.com or 310-844-1170.

### Can glass mosaic tiles be mixed with other tile materials?

Yes, glass mosaic tiles can be mixed with other tile materials in the same installation. Combining glass mosaic sheets with a larger-format field tile is a common approach in both residential and commercial projects, particularly where the mosaic defines a specific zone such as a shower floor, niche, or backsplash accent. When mixing materials, confirm that grout joint widths are consistent across the transition and that both materials are laid out dry before setting to confirm the junction reads as intentional. Be mindful of varying thicknesses across collections.
