# Cantera

## Stone from Fire

Cantera is a stone that has been central to the art and architecture of Mesoamerica for millennia. From ancient Aztec structures to cathedrals in San Miguel de Allende, this volcanic stone appears in buildings where material, structure, and permanence intersect. Zia's collection preserves this heritage through cantera stone tile cut from volcanic rock quarried in Mexico and hand-finished to reveal its naturally textured surface.

Cantera is formed from volcanic ash and mineral deposits compressed over time into a porous, lightweight stone with visible aggregate and inherent variation in tone and texture. Zia's collection reveals this structure through earthy hues ranging from soft neutral tones to deeper mineral-rich colors, reflecting the geological composition of the quarry.

### Does cantera tile need special care?

Cantera is a porous stone and must be sealed before and after grouting to protect its surface against moisture and staining. For interior installations, apply a penetrating sealer before grouting and a matte protective wax as a final coat. For exterior installations, apply an additional coat of penetrating sealer instead of wax, as UV exposure causes wax to discolor over time. Reseal regularly based on traffic levels and moisture exposure. Use only pH-neutral cleaners to avoid altering or damaging the stone. Cantera is not suitable for pools or spas due to its porosity.

### How to clean cantera tile

Remove grit and debris regularly with sweeping, dusting, or vacuuming to prevent surface scratching. For routine cleaning, use a pH-neutral cleaner with a soft mop or cloth. Avoid acidic or abrasive cleaners, which can etch or alter the natural finish. Clean spills promptly to reduce the risk of absorption and staining.

### How do I install cantera tile?

Cantera tile requires a professional installer experienced in natural stone, with careful attention to layout, curing, and sealing.

Before installation:

  - Blend tiles from all boxes to evenly distribute natural variation

During installation:

  - Use a medium-bed thinset and lightly dampen the back of each tile before setting
  - Set tiles and compact with a rubber mallet to achieve a level surface
  - Allow the thinset to fully cure before sealing and grouting

Sealing and grouting:

  - Apply a penetrating sealer before grouting once the surface is fully cured
  - Grout in small 2x2 sections using Mapei Flexcolor CQ and wipe clean as work progresses
  - For interior installations, apply a matte protective wax as a final coat
  - For exterior installations, apply an additional coat of penetrating sealer instead of wax

### What is the meaning of "cantera"?

Cantera comes from Spanish and translates to "quarry" or "stone pit." In architecture, cantera stone refers to volcanic stone extracted from natural quarries, traditionally used in Mexican and Central American building. The term identifies stone that is cut directly from the earth rather than manufactured or cast.

### What is cantera stone used for?

Cantera stone tile suits a wide range of residential and commercial applications. Interior uses include floors in entryways, living areas, and kitchens, as well as fireplace surrounds. For exterior applications, cantera works on facades, patio surfaces, and exterior wall cladding. Cantera is compatible with underfloor radiant heating systems. It is not suitable for pools or spas due to its porosity.

### What kind of stone is cantera?

Cantera is a type of volcanic tuff, formed from compressed volcanic ash, dust, and mineral deposits. This geological formation produces a stone that is lightweight, porous, and naturally varied in tone and texture. The composition includes visible mineral inclusions and small air pockets, which contribute to its characteristic surface movement and earthy color range.
