## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where do Zia's ceramic tiles come from?

Zia's ceramic tiles are designed in Los Angeles and crafted in a potter's village outside Nagoya, Japan, where the tradition of structured ceramic production spans thousands of years — with earliest functional ceramics tracing back to the Jōmon period between 10,500 and 300 BC. The artisans behind the line blend traditional methods with contemporary production requirements to achieve the collection's precise, modern-yet-enduring forms.

### What colors, finishes, and formats does Zia's ceramics collection offer?

Zia's ceramic tile collection is available in matte, satin, and gloss finishes, along with the textured options in the Field Trip: Japan line, which includes flat, etched, and furrowed surfaces. Colors range from light neutrals to bold solids — matte finishes include Agave, Citron, Iconic Blue, Ironwood, Linen, Portland Grey, and more; satin finishes include Alpha White, Cosmic Black, Eucalyptus, Field Thistle, and others. Colorways may be mixed in the same installation, though differing finishes should be considered. ### How are Zia's ceramic tiles made?

Zia's ceramic tiles are crafted near Nagoya, Japan, where the tradition of ceramic production spans thousands of years. The core ceramics begin with clay, molded and dried ahead of glazing and high-temperature firing. The Field Trip: Japan line follows a more precise method — Japanese clay is passed through a die-cut mold, cut into individual tiles, hand-glazed, and fired once. Sizing and coloring remain consistent throughout the collection.

### Does ceramic tile need special care?

Zia's ceramic tiles require care to maintain their surface and finish over time. Matte ceramic tiles must be sealed before grouting using a penetrating sealer as a grout release to avoid grout haze and staining. Use only pH-neutral cleaners and avoid acidic or abrasive products. Matte ceramics should be resealed every one to two years, or more frequently depending on traffic, wear, and moisture exposure.

### How to clean ceramic tile

Regular sweeping or vacuuming prevents surface abrasion. For routine cleaning, floors and walls can be wiped or mopped with warm water and a pH-neutral cleaner such as Miracle Sealants pH Neutral Tile and Stone Cleaner. For heavier stains, use Miracle Sealants Heavy Duty Cleaner (Acid Substitute). Avoid bleach, vinegar, or acidic products, which dull the surface over time.

### How do I install ceramic tile?

Ceramic tile is best handled by a professional installer with experience in ceramic systems.

Installation process:

  - Blend tiles from all boxes to evenly distribute natural variation
  - Use a high-quality medium-bed thinset and wet the back of each tile before setting
  - Press tiles into place with the palm of the hand — do not use mallets
  - Allow the thinset to fully cure
  - If installing matte ceramic tile, seal the tiles before grouting using a penetrating sealer
  - Grout in small sections using a high-quality sanded grout and wipe clean as work progresses

For detailed step-by-step instructions, refer to the [Ceramics Installation Guide](https://ziatile.com/pages/ceramics-installation-guide). For project-specific installation questions, the Zia team is available at info@ziatile.com or 310-844-1170.

### Is ceramic tile suitable for floors?

Ceramic tile is a reliable option for interior floors where durability, moisture resistance, and ease of maintenance are required. High-fired ceramic tile resists staining, surface wear, and water exposure when installed over a properly prepared substrate with consistent grout joints. Matte finishes offer more grip in wet conditions; satin and glossy finishes are easier to clean and maintain. Ceramic tile supports radiant heating systems.

### How is ceramic tile used?

Ceramic tile suits residential and commercial applications as a wall and floor surface. In kitchens, backsplash installations protect vertical surfaces from moisture and residue while maintaining a continuous, easy-to-clean finish. Bathroom walls and floors provide a stable, water-resistant surface when paired with proper waterproofing and installation. Ceramics are approved for shower walls and floors; sealing grout joints after grouting is required in these environments.

### How does ceramic tile hold up over time?

Ceramic tile performs well long-term when properly installed and maintained. High-temperature firing creates a dense tile body that holds its structure, while the matte or glazed surface protects against staining and wear. Longevity depends on installation quality, substrate stability, and routine maintenance with pH-neutral products. Movement or improper substrate preparation can lead to cracking or surface failure over time.

### How much ceramic tile is needed for a project?

Start with the total square footage of the surface. Measure the length and width of each surface in feet, multiply them together, and add all sections for walls or multiple floors. Plan for a minimum of 10 to 15% overage to account for cuts, variation, and any tiles set aside during installation.
