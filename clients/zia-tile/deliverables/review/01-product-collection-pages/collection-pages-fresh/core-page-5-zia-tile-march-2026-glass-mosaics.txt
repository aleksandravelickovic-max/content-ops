Glass Mosaics
Old Glass. New Tricks.
The glass mosaics collection marks our first foray into glass tile, developed as our most sustainable line to date. Our explorations landed us in Northern Spain, where the process is as important as the result.
Crafted in a facility that relies primarily on renewable wind and solar energy and fired in electric kilns, our glass mosaics marry craft and consciousness. The mosaic tiles are made from 98% recycled glass, and the resulting 12x12 grids of mosaic tiles feature vibrant colors across solid and repeatable patterns. Our design team created seven intentional patterns, drawing inspiration from artworks, fashion textiles, and locations like Los Angeles's own Union Station.

Frequently Asked Questions 
Where do Zia’s glass mosaic tiles come from?
While inspired by global design, our glass mosaics are produced in Northern Spain, in a facility that values sustainability, supporting our desire to use primarily renewable energy and recycled materials. Each glass mosaic tile is made from 98% recycled glass, built on reuse rather than raw extraction. This production environment allows consistency in color calibration and sheet formation while maintaining strict environmental standards. The artisans who craft this line pride themselves both on best-in-class glassworks and a commitment to recycling everything possible, from water to cardboard waste.
How are Zia glass mosaics made?
Zia's glass mosaic tiles begin with discarded glass, allowing for tiles composed of 98% recycled materials. The glass is melted and individually formed in a mold, then fired in electric kilns powered primarily by renewable energy. As each tile is cooled and removed from its mold, they are hand-placed on grids, in patterns, and on solid sheets. Our seven intentional patterns draw inspiration from artworks, fashion textiles, and locations like Los Angeles's Union Station. Whether solid, patterned, or a mix of the two, the collection suits highly designed installations.
Do glass mosaics need special care?
While all handmade tiles need reasonable care, glass mosaics are one of the easier materials to install and maintain. Installation quality is essential for long-term performance. We recommend working with detail-oriented installers experienced in glass tile for the best installation results.
How do you clean glass mosaic tiles?
Cleaning glass mosaic tiles correctly starts with regular sweeping or vacuuming to remove dust and prevent abrasion. For routine cleaning, wipe floors or walls with warm water or a soft cloth and clean spills promptly to maintain surface clarity.
For deeper cleaning, a pH-neutral cleaner such as Miracle Sealants pH Neutral Tile and Stone Cleaner is recommended. For more intensive residue or potential stains, Miracle Sealants Heavy Duty Cleaner (Acid Substitute) can be used safely on the surface. The non-porous surface is compatible with a wide range of cleaning solutions and performs well in both residential and high-traffic commercial environments."
How do I install glass mosaic sheets?
Installing glass mosaic sheets requires precision handling and experience with small-format tiles. We recommend working with professional installers.
Before installation:
* Begin by blending tiles from every box to evenly distribute natural variation
* Ensure substrate is clean, flat, and properly prepared
During installation:
* Carefully remove the paper backing from each sheet
* Evenly apply epoxy thinset 
* Press sheets firmly into place using hand pressure
After placement:
* Allow the thinset to fully cure before grouting
* Use epoxy grout in small sections and wipe excess grout immediately to avoid grout haze
Glass mosaics do not require sealing at any stage of installation or after completion. For full technical specifications and detailed installation steps, refer to the Glass Mosaics Installation Guide.
How are glass mosaic tiles used?
Glass mosaic tiles are used across residential and commercial applications where moisture resistance, cleanability, and impressive design are the focus. The mosaics arrive pre-set on 12x12 mesh-backed sheets, allowing for precise spacing in both solids and patterns. Common applications include kitchen backsplashes, shower walls and floors, pools and spas, exterior facades, and feature walls. The small format allows the material to adapt to curved surfaces, niches, and transitions without compromising alignment.
Can glass mosaic tile be used on a shower floor?
Yes, glass mosaic tile can be used on a shower floor. The small format of mosaic sheets increases the frequency of grout lines across the surface, which improves traction compared to larger-format tiles. Slip resistance can be further increased by applying an anti-slip treatment after installation is complete. Zia recommends working with a professional installer experienced in glass tiles in wet areas.
Can glass mosaic tiles be mixed with other tile materials?
Yes, glass mosaic tiles can be mixed with other tile materials in the same installation. Combining glass mosaic sheets with a larger-format field tile is a common approach in both residential and commercial projects, particularly where the mosaic defines a specific zone such as a shower floor, niche, or backsplash accent. When mixing materials, confirm that grout joint widths are consistent across the transition and that both materials are laid out dry. Be mindful of the varying thicknesses across various collections.
What patterns does Zia's glass mosaic collection offer?

The patterned designs are built from combinations of solid glass mosaic colors arranged in repeating geometric compositions. Curated color combinations include Roma and Tulip, Jet Black and Milk, Lichen and Milk, and Lemon Drop and Kiwi. Each glass mosaic pattern arrives on 12x12 mesh-backed sheets.
