### Verified guidance to use (repo installation guides)

**Zellige:**
- Grout is required in all installations; never dry-stack — grout protects tile edges over time.
- Grout fills chips, pits, and crazing (the trio) and blends them into the surface.
- Walls: edge-to-edge or 1/16" grout joints, wedge spacers to align uneven edges.
- Floors: minimum 1/8" grout joints, staggered layout increases slip resistance.
- Commercial/wet floors: at least 1/8" grout joints plus anti-slip treatment after installation (STYLE-SYSTEM §4.4 phrasing).
- Glazed zellige: soak 1–2 minutes before setting; NO pre-grout sealing; post-grout sealing in wet areas (showers, pools, spas).
- Unglazed natural zellige: do NOT soak; MUST seal before grouting; sealer fully cured before grout.
- Work in sections no larger than 2'x2'; remove excess grout immediately; grout cures ~48 hours; thin set cures 48 hours before grouting.
- Grout color guidance (zellige guide): match grout color closely to the tile color to complement the variation.
- Specific grout products named in the guide (Mapei Ultracolor Plus FA / Flexcolor CQ) are **internal reference only** — never name brands in customer-facing copy (§4.3). Direct readers to the Installation Guide.

**Cement:**
- Never fired; matte; porous — MUST be sealed before grouting, every installation.
- 1/16" spacers (narrow grout joints so the pattern reads continuously).
- Do not grout the entire floor at once; wipe excess immediately — dried grout can stain permanently.
- Contrasting grout color: apply with a grout bag, not a float, to reduce staining and haze.
- Grout color guidance (cement guide): a lighter neutral grout reads traditional; matching grout closely to the tile reads more modern.
- pH neutral cleaners only; acidic cleaners damage cement tile.
- Top-coat sealing after grouting (interior wax profile / exterior penetrating profile — describe without brand names).

**Both:** PPE (dust mask, eye protection) when working with grout, thin set, sealants — Prop 65 link required: https://ziatile.com/proposition-65-warnings. Overage: 15-20% standard, 25% for a more uniform look. Contact line info@ziatile.com + 310-844-1170 in closing and installation FAQ.
