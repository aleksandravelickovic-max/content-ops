## Naming decision — "brushed" vs "bush-hammered" (critical)

Verified against the live collection page (fetched 2026-07-02) and `materials/limestone.md`:

- **Live catalog finish names:** Honed, Bush Hammered (plus Antiqued Polished and Heathered observed on the live page; not in the material config, so not asserted in the article).
- **Material config + STYLE-SYSTEM §11:** honed or bush-hammered.
- **Zia does not sell a finish called "brushed."** In the industry, brushed (wire-brushed) and bush-hammered (struck with a multi-pointed hammer) are different texturing techniques that answer the same design need: a matte, textured surface.
- **Treatment:** "brushed limestone" is used as the search term readers type, then explicitly mapped to Zia's actual textured finish, bush-hammered. The article never asserts Zia sells a brushed finish. Keyword positions (H1 variant, meta, comparison heading) use "brushed" only in the vs-search framing; catalog claims use "bush-hammered."

## Verified product facts (sources: materials/limestone.md, STYLE-SYSTEM §11, live collection page)

- Quarried solid blocks near Fez, Morocco and Bordeaux, France.
- Finishes asserted: honed, bush-hammered.
- Colorway names observed on live page: French Limestone, Buff, Monument, Basilica, Belgian Bluestone, French Cobblestone. Article names Buff, Monument, and Basilica as tones and French Limestone, Belgian Bluestone, and French Cobblestone as lines, without asserting relationships beyond what the listing shows.
- Formats observed on live page: 4x12, 6x6, 12x12, 12x24, 24x24, plus 12" and 20" varying-length planks.
- Develops a natural patina over time (wear and time only, never light/UV).
- Freeze/thaw: NOT suitable. Exterior non-freeze/thaw: suitable.
- Pools/spas: NOT suitable.
- Sealing: porous; sealed before and after grouting (two-step pre-grout + top-coat per Limestone Installation Guide). Never name sealer products; direct readers to the Installation Guide.
- Slip resistance for wet floors: minimum 1/8" grout joints plus anti-slip treatment after installation.
- Care: pH-neutral cleaners only; acidic or abrasive cleaners etch the surface.
- Variation language: tone, natural patina, honed or bush-hammered finish, natural stone variation. No zellige or terra cotta variation language (no chips/pits/crazing, no dynamic glazes).
- Overage: 15-20% standard, 25% for a more uniform look.

## SERP research (2026-07-02)

Top organic coverage for "limestone tile" and "honed vs brushed limestone": Tile & Mosaic Depot (pros/cons/best uses guide), limestone.com learning center, Stone Center (15 finish types), Quorn Stone (finish guide + cleaning guide), Texas Travertine (architect's guide, cleaning guide), Citadel Stone (tumbled vs honed vs brushed for pool decks), GMS Werks (honed vs brushed vs polished), Natural Stone Sales (honed vs tumbled).

**What ranks:** definitional guides that compare finishes on texture, slip resistance, maintenance, and room fit, plus standalone care/sealing how-tos. Nearly all are generic supplier content with no origin story and no specific formats or colorways.

**Coverage gaps Zia can own:** named quarry origins (Fez, Bordeaux), specific formats and colorways in application context, honest suitability limits (freeze/thaw, pools), and editorial register. Competitors also conflate brushed with bush-hammered or skip the distinction entirely.

**Consensus points to reflect:** honed = ground smooth, matte, favored for interiors; brushed/textured = more grip, hides wear, favored where floors see water or traffic; limestone is porous and needs sealing; pH-neutral cleaning only; acids (vinegar, citrus) etch.

**Caution:** several results push brushed limestone for pool decks and exteriors. Zia's limestone is not suitable for pools/spas or freeze/thaw exteriors. Do not borrow those claims.
