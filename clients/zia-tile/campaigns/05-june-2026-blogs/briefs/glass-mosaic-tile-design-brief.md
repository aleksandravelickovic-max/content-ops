## Material rules (materials/glass-mosaics.md — non-negotiable)

- Freeze/thaw: SUITABLE for exterior freeze/thaw.
- Pools & spas: SUITABLE (saltwater or chlorine).
- Sealing: glass is naturally stain-resistant; only OPTIONAL grout sealing. Do NOT apply mandatory sealing copy.
- Slip: shower floors / wet floors need minimum 1/8" grout joints plus an anti-slip product after installation. Slip language only in wet-area sections.
- Variation language: color, sheen, hand-placement variation across the grid. NO chips/pits/crazing, no zellige "dynamic glazes."
