**Coverage patterns across the SERP:**
- Definition: cement + sand + mineral pigments, hydraulically pressed, air-cured, never fired, matte finish.
- Durability framing: heavy foot traffic, decades of service with proper substrate prep, sealing, and cleaning.
- Named commercial venues: restaurants, cafes, hotels, resorts, lobbies, offices, salons, retail.
- Sealing cadence: pre-seal before grout, seal after install, reseal every 1-2 years depending on traffic; penetrating sealers preferred over topical (topical can wear and get slippery).
- Cleaning: pH-neutral cleaner, soft mop, no acidic or harsh chemicals, quick spill response, grit control.

**Coverage gaps competitors leave open (our angle):**
- Almost no one addresses slip resistance with a concrete spec; most punt to "consult a professional." We give the grout joint minimum (1/8") plus anti-slip treatment for wet commercial floors.
- Aging is framed defensively (stain risk) rather than as lived-in character and patina, which is the honest story for encaustic cement in hospitality.
- Floors vs walls split for commercial settings is rarely covered.
- Overage planning for large commercial orders is absent everywhere.
