**What top pages cover:**
- Surface taxonomy: surround, face, mantel, hearth, firebox, full feature wall, chimney breast (Clé's structure).
- Heat basics: working fireboxes are lined with firebrick/refractory material; decorative, gas, and electric fireplaces allow more freedom on the surfaces around the opening.
- Material menus: ceramic/porcelain, natural stone, cement, zellige, brick. Competitors (Clé, Zelligery, Riad) assert zellige heat-resistance directly.
- Maintenance: glazed tile cleans easily; cement and unglazed tile require sealing; patina develops over time.
- Trends noted in SERP summaries: matte finishes, earthy tones (terracotta, slate, soft grays), handmade texture, blue-and-white Delft tradition, geometric/hexagon feature installs.

**What top pages miss (our gap to fill):**
- Specific formats and colorways tied to each surface (Clé gives no sizing guidance).
- The vertical-surface vs. firebox distinction handled honestly instead of blanket "heat-resistant" claims.
- Outdoor fireplace climate rules (freeze/thaw) — absent from every reviewed page.
- Grout and layout as design decisions.

**PAA-pattern questions observed (Houzz forum + SERP):** zellige on a hearth; tile size for surrounds; tile over existing brick; sealing; zellige vs. cement.
