## Verified product facts (write from these only)

**Roman Mosaics** (materials/roman-mosaics.md, confidence: high):
- Same premium stone as Solid Marble; ancient-Rome-inspired mosaic patterns; Italian stone heritage.
- Pool/spa: SUITABLE (selling point vs Marble). Exterior: NOT suitable, freeze/thaw or otherwise — interior + pool/spa only.
- Sealing: natural-stone profile, required; direct to Installation Guide; never name sealer products.
- Variation language: veining, tone, natural stone variation, mosaic pattern. No chips/pits/crazing.

**Glass Mosaics** (materials/glass-mosaics.md, confidence: high):
- Northern Spain, 98% recycled glass, renewable energy, hand-placed on 12x12 grids, seven patterns (do not name patterns — not verified).
- Pool/spa: suitable. Freeze/thaw exterior: suitable. Sealing: optional grout sealing only — do not apply stone or zellige sealing copy.
- Variation: color, sheen, hand-placement variation across the grid.

**Zellige mesh-backed mosaics** (materials/zellige.md, STYLE-SYSTEM §11):
- Glazed Moroccan clay, 800+ years; mesh-backed mosaics exist in the range.
- Pool/spa: suitable. Freeze/thaw exterior: NOT suitable (state affirmatively in prose).
- Chips, pits, and crazing as a trio; dynamic glazes (zellige only). Colorways gated `verify` — name none.

**Slip resistance (§4.4):** wet commercial floors need grout joints of at least 1/8 inch plus anti-slip treatment after installation.
**Overage (§9):** 15-20% standard plus 25% for a more uniform look. Never 10-15%.

## SERP research

Reviewed top organic results for "mosaic tile": medtile.com, mir-mosaic.com, tilebar.com (Tileist), tilechoices.com, whytile.com, emilamerica.com; plus fireclaytile.com and edwardmartin.com for the shower-floor angle and msisurfaces.com / thetileshop.com for install/grout coverage.

**Consensus definition across the SERP:** small pieces (tesserae), typically 2 inches or smaller, arranged in patterns and mounted on mesh-backed sheets. Materials covered: stone, glass, ceramic/porcelain, metal.

**Coverage gaps (our angle):**
1. Competitors define the category but rarely ground it in a real production method — none describe stone chiseled to 5/8-inch squares or hand-placed glass grids. Specificity is the differentiator.
2. Nobody separates suitability by material (stone vs glass vs glazed clay) for pools, exteriors, and sealing. The SERP hand-waves "check with your installer."
3. "Roman mosaic" results are either museum reproductions or generic floral sheets — an authentic ancient-Rome-pattern angle in real marble is open.
4. SERP framing is defensive ("withstands heat and splashes") — Zia's positive-framing rule reads better and differentiates.

**People Also Ask / FAQ candidates (from SERP):**
1. What is mosaic tile? (definitional, all results)
2. What size are mosaic tiles? (2 inches or smaller consensus)
3. Are mosaic tiles good for shower floors? (grout joints = grip; slope adaptation)
4. Can mosaic tile be used in a pool?
5. How is mosaic tile installed? (mesh sheets, thinset, 24-hr cure)
6. Does marble mosaic tile need sealing? (stone needs sealing; glass does not)
7. What grout for mosaic tile? (unsanded for tight joints — keep at approach level, not product level)
8. How much mosaic tile should be ordered? (overage — Zia's 15-20% + 25% rule)
9. What is Roman mosaic tile? (secondary keyword)
10. Why is mosaic tile more expensive than field tile? (craftsmanship, sheeting — optional)
