How It's Made

Each Casablanca 2x2 begins where all traditional zellige begins. Artisans source raw clay from local Moroccan hillsides, soak it in water, filter it for impurities, and work it until it reaches a consistency suitable for hand-pressing into molds.

Each tile is shaped individually by hand into the 2x2 format, which is why variation in thickness, edge, and surface texture is inherent to this scale. Once formed, the tiles dry in the Moroccan sun before each piece is dipped individually in glaze.

The light neutral Casablanca glaze settles differently on each tile. Firing takes place in earthen kilns, where heat moves unevenly through the chamber. That unevenness produces the chips, pits, crazing, and tonal shifts that make each Casablanca 2x2 entirely its own.

About

Inherent Variation

  • Our Casablanca 2x2 is a light neutral zellige with a glaze that shifts subtly with changing light.
  • Each handcrafted zellige tile remains wholly unique, and this innate variation defines the line's charm.
  • You should expect natural variation in color, shape, and thickness, alongside characteristic chips, pits, and crazing.

Preparation and Curation

  • Mix each box in your shipment before beginning your project to ensure a balanced distribution of color and tone.
  • Arrange an ideal layout before installation to ensure the variation remains balanced to your liking.
  • Any chips, pits, and color variation should be evenly dispersed.
  • We suggest a 15–20% overage on all tile orders to provide enough material to curate your unique mix. For a more uniform look, we recommend 25% overage.

Installation and Finishing

  • Installers must always wear protective face and eye coverings when working with zellige, thinset, sealants, and grout in accordance with California Prop 65.
  • While traditional trim and edge pieces do not exist for zellige, we recommend bringing the drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile or finishing your project with mitered edges or Schluter strips.
  • Always seal Casablanca zellige 2x2 in areas exposed to moisture, such as pools, spas, and showers.
  • You may reach out to our team directly at info@ziatile.com with any specific installation questions.

⚠ WARNING: Cancer and Reproductive Harm. Learn more about Proposition 65 Warnings.

Zia’s zellige incorporates centuries of craftsmanship in every piece, a celebration of this handcrafted Moroccan art form. Historically installed in decorative geometric and floral shapes in the most elaborately designed palaces, our curated selection of handmade tile includes a rainbow of colors and a variety of shapes and sizes for homes and public spaces around the globe. With sun-baked clay to hand-applied glazes, no two zellige tiles are exactly alike.

Expect significant variations in precise size, color, and tone, as well as pits and chips inherent in all handmade Moroccan zellige. These characteristics represent the natural imperfections of this zellige tile, creating the iconic “perfectly imperfect” look. Order Details + Installation

We recommend a 15-20% overage to best curate a zellige installation, allowing for sufficient tiles within the desired range. Before installation, unpack and mix all boxes to best plan the layout.

Zellige tile installation is labor-intensive, often likened more to masonry than traditional tiling due to the irregular nature of the handmade material. Traditionally, zellige is installed edge-to-edge with minimal to no grout spacing; however, we recommend wedge spacers to adjust the spacing between irregular tiles to maintain a consistent layout and protect the tiles’ edges. Plan for an installation 2-3 times longer than standard ceramic tile, accounting for extra time to sort, soak, arrange, and manually adjust each piece for size variations.
