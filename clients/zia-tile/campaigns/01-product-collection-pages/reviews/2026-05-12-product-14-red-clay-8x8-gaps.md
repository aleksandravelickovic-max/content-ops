# Product Page 14 — Red Clay 8x8 Square — gaps vs Jamie's feedback

A PDP for the Red Clay 8x8 Cotto square. Jamie's 4/30 content connect and the 5/8 afternoon PDP structural call laid out the exact PDP architecture this page is supposed to honor: SKU-specific "romance copy" at the top (color + size + format-specific selling angle), then category-standardized accordion sections (Order & Shipping, About, Tile Usage, How It's Made, Order & Shipping, Installation Guide). This draft has the right scaffolding but most of the load-bearing copy is missing or off-register.

## Gaps

### 1. Six of seven accordion sections are still "[Template Pending]"
**Jamie's ask:** "Order details, installation… would be consistent for the whole category. … This whole accordion is consistent by category. … the highlighted intro here is the only section in a category that we really want to hyper customize. … moving into order, details, and installation. This would be consistent for the whole category."
**Source:** 2026-04-30 Zia Tile / LinkGraph content connect
**Where this piece falls short:** L13–32 list seven accordion bars — Order Details + Installation, About, Tile Usage, How It's Made, Order & Shipping, Installation Guide — and every single one is marked "[Template Pending]." The 4/30 deal was that category-level accordion templates would be written once per material (e.g., one for Cotto) and copy-pasted across every SKU in that category. For a Red Clay 8x8 PDP drafted April 2026, those templates should already be slotted in.

### 2. SKU-specific romance copy is too thin and doesn't lean into color, size, or format the way Jamie set up
**Jamie's ask:** "This top romance section is where we would ideally love to get a little more granular with color, with size. And that's where we can really lean into the specific SKUs. … the top intro is a little bit more context, history, sales copy."
**Source:** 2026-05-08 afternoon PDP and collection page structural updates connect; 2026-04-30 content connect
**Where this piece falls short:** L8–10 are the only romance copy and total ~75 words. L8: "Our Red Clay 8x8 is a Cotto tile in traditional, deep warm red. Each tile is high-fired, carrying the natural tonal range of the clay across the surface." Generic. Nothing about Red Clay being "the deepest red, closest to the raw material itself" (Jamie's own framing from 4/30). Nothing about 8x8 as a standard square that anchors residential floors. Nothing about who's actually buying this — designer, contractor, kitchen, courtyard. This is the one slot per SKU where size + color + place specificity is supposed to live, and it's running on autopilot.

### 3. Opens paragraph 2 with the Italian etymology Jamie said to soft-pedal
**Jamie's ask:** "I don't mind it like referencing that cotto is Italian for cooked. I think it just gets a little confusing to lean into Italian terracotta. So… I would take out the Italian in that second paragraph."
**Source:** 2026-05-08 morning Zia content review
**Where this piece falls short:** L10 opens with: "Cotto (derived from the Italian 'terra cotta', or 'baked earth') is a handmade tile from San Miguel de Allende." The Italian etymology is the first thing the second paragraph says — the structural position Jamie specifically said to demote. The piece is also confusing Italian terra cotta (the language) with the Mexican-made tile in the same sentence.

### 4. "Our Red Clay 8x8" framing only used once — doesn't fully claim the color the way Jamie set up for Casablanca
**Jamie's ask:** "Casablanca is a warm white, so I would just prefer to own Casablanca as a Zia color. So the framework would be something more like our Casablanca. Our Casablanca white tile. Our Casablanca Zalish tile."
**Source:** 2026-03-27 Zia Tile content discussion
**Where this piece falls short:** L8 says "Our Red Clay 8x8" once and then drops the possessive for the rest of the piece. By the FAQs the tile is just "Red Clay 8x8" or "Red Clay Cotto" — the ownership framing Jamie set up for Casablanca (and by extension every Zia colorway) is not carried through. The color name reads as a generic descriptor rather than as Zia's traditional red.

### 5. Doesn't position Red Clay as the anchor of Cotto's three traditional colorways
**Jamie's ask:** "Adobe fired Earth and Red Clay are the core, most traditional colors of terracotta. And then the whitewashed blanco, the Coco Madeira, and the matte black Oscar. … like, we've literally said that these are the traditional colors of terracotta. This is what Zia calls it."
**Source:** 2026-05-08 morning Zia content review
**Where this piece falls short:** L8 calls Red Clay "traditional, deep warm red" — but the piece never says Red Clay is one of Cotto's three traditional colorways (Adobe, Fired Earth, Red Clay) alongside the three contemporary tones (Blanco, Madera, Oscura). The collection-level narrative Jamie wants every PDP to plug into is absent — this PDP could be selling tile from any brand.

### 6. "Mix the Red Clay 8x8 with other Cotto colorways" FAQ misses the dark-brown / matte-black leadership angle
**Jamie's ask:** "Z has pushed terracotta into this dark brown category, leading the way with terracotta production across boutique tile. … dark brown, matte black, chocolate brown, we're missing that opportunity to lean into some of these trendier keywords."
**Source:** 2026-05-08 morning Zia content review
**Where this piece falls short:** L43 names "the unexpected tones of Blanco, Madera, and Oscura" alongside Red Clay — but treats them as equivalents to Adobe and Fired Earth. There's no claim that pairing Red Clay with Madera or Oscura is leaning into Zia's leadership in dark brown / matte black terra cotta (which Jamie called out as their bestseller and a category Zia is leading on). Madera being Zia's top SKU of 2026 (per 4/8 link-building review) is not surfaced here at all.

### 7. "you" / "your project" register throughout the FAQs
**Jamie's ask:** "We also don't like to use you too much and speak directly to the client in that way. So something like consider your installation or consider the installation a starting point, not a forever state."
**Source:** 2026-05-08 morning Zia content review
**Where this piece falls short:** L35–45 use "you" / "your" heavily: "What kind of variation should I expect" / "tiles that you might… handmade tile" / "Before installation, open and mix tiles from all boxes, and dry-lay the entire installation" (imperative), "Can I mix the Red Clay 8×8 with other Cotto colorways" / "What overage should I order." The 5/8 morning correction asked for the softer "consider the installation" construction — that isn't carried through. PDP FAQ register can be more direct than blog, but Jamie's specific note still applies.

### 8. Technical detail asserted as fact in FAQ while the rest of the technical content is "[Template Pending]"
**Jamie's ask:** "Clay is pressed into mold, sun dried in courtyards and high fired in wooden gas kilns. Not quite sure they're wood and gas kilns. … I just would want our team to have eyes on that because I just don't know for sure." Plus 4/30: "There was a lot in here about only sealing in wet spaces. If anyone had read our installation guide all installations with the leash need to be sealed. … We can't afford things like that to fall through the cracks."
**Source:** 2026-05-08 morning Zia content review; 2026-04-30 content connect
**Where this piece falls short:** L39 asserts "DCOF of 0.98" and "1/8″ grout joints" + "anti-slip treatment should be applied" with specificity. L41 details the two-step seal protocol and named products (511 Porous Plus, Fila Matte Wax). But the actual "Installation Guide" and "How It's Made" accordions are still "[Template Pending]." Either every technical assertion needs Zia-team verification before publish, or the accordion templates need to land first and the FAQs need to be a consistent subset of them.

### 9. Doesn't differentiate 8x8 within the Cotto square format set (4x4, 8x8, 13x13)
**Jamie's ask:** "Then it's also a collaborative process where you're like, oh, wow, like, let's run with matte black cotto, let's run with Belgian blue limestone, and let's all reference this famous building that we know it. … like, we know what has the most legs from a design and industry perspective."
**Source:** 2026-05-08 morning Zia content review
**Where this piece falls short:** "8x8 square" is a specific size in Cotto's standard square set (4x4, 8x8, 13x13 — per the collection page). This PDP never says what 8x8 is good for that 4x4 isn't (more grout joints for slip resistance / smaller scale) or that 13x13 isn't (anchoring larger rooms). Per 4/30 + 5/8 PM, the romance section is the place to lean into format specifics — and that argument isn't here.

### 10. "Cotto and Cotto Allende are both made from the same Mexican clay body" FAQ — answers a Cotto Allende question on a Cotto Red Clay PDP
**Jamie's ask:** "Even like Saltillo, like we talked about that a few times where I was like, we don't sell Saltillo. … please tell us and we'll get rid of it. But we don't understand this at the level you all do. So that's where it's like, we like this copy. But if you have a business reason why that shouldn't remain okay…"
**Source:** 2026-05-04 Zia Tile blog posts connect; 2026-04-08 link-building review (re: keyword vs. SKU mismatches)
**Where this piece falls short:** L36–37 takes one of only five FAQ slots on a Red Clay 8x8 PDP to explain Cotto vs. Cotto Allende differences. That comparison belongs on the Cotto collection page or in a dedicated comparison piece. On this PDP, the FAQ slot is better spent on something Red Clay 8x8 specific — sealing for high-traffic kitchen floors, pairing with other Cotto colorways for pattern work, or freeze/thaw exterior fit, all of which are Jamie-flagged angles.
