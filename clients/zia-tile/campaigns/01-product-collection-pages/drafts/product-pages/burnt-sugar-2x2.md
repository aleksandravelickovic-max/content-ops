## About

No two zellige tiles are the same. Pieces vary in size, surface, and glaze across each box, with hairline crazing and chamfered edges that come from how the material is hand-formed rather than flaws to correct.

Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with zellige, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with [California Prop 65](https://www.ziatile.com/proposition-65-warnings). Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered cuts or Schluter strips. Traditional trim and edge pieces do not exist for zellige.

Seal zellige according to our installation instructions, whether the application is dry or exposed to moisture (pools, spas, showers). Reach out to our team at [info@ziatile.com](mailto:info@ziatile.com) with any material or installation questions.

## How It's Made

Zellige is hand-formed in Morocco, where the technique has been refined for more than 800 years and passed across generations of artisans.

The process begins with raw clay, which is mined from local hillsides, soaked, filtered for impurities, and hand-pressed into molds. The tiles are laid to dry in the Moroccan sun, flattened and shaped by hand, then dried further before glazing.

Each tile is hand-dipped in glaze and arranged inside an earthen kiln, where uneven heat creates the tonal variation that defines zellige. No two pieces leave the kiln the same, and the handmade nature and variation are hallmarks of this material. To prepare for installation, open and mix all boxes before setting them to achieve an ideal layout. The tiles will carry the marks of the day they were fired.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What color is Burnt Sugar zellige?

Burnt Sugar zellige is a dark brown with a glaze that moves through deep chocolates, warm mocha tones, and amber undertones depending on the light and viewing angle. The darkest tiles carry a rich, saturated brown; lighter tiles shift toward warm amber. The variation is most apparent once tiles are laid together, where the difference in tone creates movement across the surface. Under warm artificial light, the amber undertones in the glaze become more pronounced.

### Can I use Burnt Sugar 2x2 zellige in a kitchen?

Yes. Burnt Sugar 2x2 is commonly installed as a kitchen backsplash, where the dark brown glaze provides contrast against lighter cabinetry and countertops. The small 2x2 format and close-set installation create the dense, mosaic-like field associated with traditional Moroccan zellige. As with all zellige, proper sealing is required, and routine cleaning with a pH-neutral cleaner is recommended in areas exposed to cooking oils, steam, and heat.

### Is Burnt Sugar 2x2 zellige good for bathroom floors?

Yes. Burnt Sugar 2x2 can be used on bathroom floors in both low- and high-traffic applications. The compact format contributes to slip resistance through higher grout joint density, making it appropriate for shower floors and wet-area floor installations. For wet floor applications, increase grout joints to a minimum of ⅛" and apply an anti-slip treatment after installation to improve traction.

### Does Burnt Sugar zellige require sealing?

Yes. Sealing is required for all zellige applications, including dry interior walls. Apply sealer before grouting and follow the instructions in the [Zellige Installation Guide](https://ziatile.com/pages/zellige-installation-guide) for your specific application. For pool and spa installations, apply sealer to all tiles and grout lines after grouting is complete and allow full cure before filling. Reach out to [info@ziatile.com](mailto:info@ziatile.com) with any sealing or installation questions.

### What tiles mix well with Burnt Sugar 2x2?

Burnt Sugar 2x2 pairs well with neutral and warm colorways in Zia's zellige collection, particularly Glazed Earth and Casablanca White. The dark brown field of Burnt Sugar pairs naturally with lighter neutrals used as borders or field alternates, and it works well alongside natural stone in travertine and limestone tones. Warm wood finishes and unlacquered brass fixtures complement the amber undertones in the glaze.

### What grout color works best with Burnt Sugar zellige?

A grout color matched closely to the mid-range brown of the glaze produces the most cohesive surface, allowing the tonal variation across tiles to read as a continuous field. Contrasting lighter grout will define each tile individually and draw more attention to the mosaic format. Use Mapei Ultracolor Plus FA, which works well with the glazed zellige surface and cleans off without staining the glaze if wiped promptly after application.

### Does Burnt Sugar 2x2 zellige work in a pool or spa?

Yes. Burnt Sugar 2x2 is suitable for pool and spa applications in both saltwater and chlorine environments. Sealing is required; apply sealer to all tiles and grout lines exposed to continuous moisture after grouting is complete. Consult the [Zellige Installation Guide](https://ziatile.com/pages/zellige-installation-guide) for specific sealing instructions and reach out to our team at [info@ziatile.com](mailto:info@ziatile.com) with any project-specific questions.
