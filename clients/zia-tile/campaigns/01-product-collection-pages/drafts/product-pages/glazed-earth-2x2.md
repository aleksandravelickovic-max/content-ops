## About

No two zellige tiles are the same. Pieces vary in size, surface, and glaze across each box, with hairline crazing and chamfered edges that come from how the material is hand-formed rather than flaws to correct.

Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with zellige, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with [California Prop 65](https://www.ziatile.com/proposition-65-warnings). Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered cuts or Schluter strips. Traditional trim and edge pieces do not exist for zellige.

Seal zellige according to our installation instructions, whether the application is dry or exposed to moisture (pools, spas, showers). Reach out to our team at [info@ziatile.com](mailto:info@ziatile.com) with any material or installation questions.

## How It's Made

Zellige is hand-formed in Morocco, where the technique has been refined for more than 800 years and passed across generations of artisans.

The process begins with raw clay, which is mined from local hillsides, soaked, filtered for impurities, and hand-pressed into molds. The tiles are laid to dry in the Moroccan sun, flattened and shaped by hand, then dried further before glazing.

Each tile is hand-dipped in glaze and arranged inside an earthen kiln, where uneven heat creates the tonal variation that defines zellige. No two pieces leave the kiln the same, and the handmade nature and variation are hallmarks of this material. To prepare for installation, open and mix all boxes before setting them to achieve an ideal layout. The tiles will carry the marks of the day they were fired.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What color is Glazed Earth zellige?

Glazed Earth zellige is a warm beige-neutral with a glaze that moves across warm cream, tan, and earth tones depending on light and viewing angle. Individual tiles carry different positions in that range — some sit closer to buff and light cream, others lean toward tan or a deeper clay tone — and the differences between tiles become most visible once they are laid together. The glaze reads warm under both natural and artificial light, with warmer light sources drawing out the clay and amber end of the range more clearly.

### Is Glazed Earth 2x2 zellige good for floors?

Yes. Glazed Earth 2x2 can be used on interior floors in both low- and high-traffic areas. The compact format contributes to slip resistance through higher grout joint density, which makes it appropriate for shower floors and wet-area floor installations. For wet floor applications, increase grout joints to a minimum of ⅛" and apply an anti-slip treatment after installation to improve traction.

### What materials pair well with Glazed Earth 2x2 zellige?

Natural stone in travertine, limestone, and sandstone tones pairs well with Glazed Earth's warm beige-neutral range. White and warm-white cabinetry provides a clean counterpoint without competing with the glaze color. Natural wood in medium and warm tones sits naturally alongside the tile. In Zia's zellige collection, Glazed Earth works alongside Casablanca White where a tonal, neutral composition is the direction, and pairs with deeper colorways like Burnt Sugar or Racing Green where contrast is needed.

### Can Glazed Earth 2x2 be used as a kitchen backsplash?

Yes. Glazed Earth 2x2 is commonly used as a kitchen backsplash, where the warm neutral glaze works well against a wide range of cabinet and countertop combinations. The reflective glaze responds to both natural and artificial kitchen light, and the small 2x2 format produces the dense, mosaic-like surface associated with traditional Moroccan zellige. Seal the tile per the installation guide and clean with a pH-neutral cleaner in areas exposed to cooking oils, steam, and heat.

### How do I clean Glazed Earth zellige?

Clean Glazed Earth zellige with a pH-neutral cleaner and a soft cloth or non-abrasive sponge. Avoid acidic cleaners, bleach, and abrasive scrubbers, which can damage the glaze over time. Wipe spills promptly, particularly oils and acidic substances. In kitchen backsplash applications, routine cleaning after cooking prevents buildup in the grout joints. Reach out to [info@ziatile.com](mailto:info@ziatile.com) with any care or maintenance questions.

### Can Glazed Earth 2x2 be mixed with other zellige colors?

Yes. Glazed Earth 2x2 can be mixed with other colorways in Zia's zellige collection. Each colorway is produced using the same traditional methods, so tiles share consistent surface character across the range. Glazed Earth works particularly well alongside Casablanca White for tonal, neutral compositions, and alongside deeper colorways like Burnt Sugar where a warm neutral field frames a richer accent color. It can also be mixed with the 4x4 format for combining field tile with detail work.

### Does Glazed Earth 2x2 zellige work in a pool or spa?

Yes. Glazed Earth 2x2 is suitable for pool and spa applications in both saltwater and chlorine environments. Sealing is required; apply sealer to all tiles and grout lines exposed to continuous moisture after grouting is complete.
