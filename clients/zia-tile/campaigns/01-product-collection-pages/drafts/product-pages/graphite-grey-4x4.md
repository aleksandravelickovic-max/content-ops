## About

No two zellige tiles are the same. Pieces vary in size, surface, and glaze across each box, with hairline crazing and chamfered edges that come from how the material is hand-formed rather than flaws to correct.

Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with zellige, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with [California Prop 65](https://www.ziatile.com/proposition-65-warnings). Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered cuts or Schluter strips. Traditional trim and edge pieces do not exist for zellige.

Seal zellige according to our installation instructions, whether the application is dry or exposed to moisture (pools, spas, showers). Reach out to our team at [info@ziatile.com](mailto:info@ziatile.com) with any material or installation questions.

## How It's Made

Zellige is hand-formed in Morocco, where the technique has been refined for more than 800 years and passed across generations of artisans.

The process begins with raw clay, which is mined from local hillsides, soaked, filtered for impurities, and hand-pressed into molds. The tiles are laid to dry in the Moroccan sun, flattened and shaped by hand, then dried further before glazing.

Each tile is hand-dipped in glaze and arranged inside an earthen kiln, where uneven heat creates the tonal variation that defines zellige. No two pieces leave the kiln the same, and the handmade nature and variation are hallmarks of this material. To prepare for installation, open and mix all boxes before setting them to achieve an ideal layout. The tiles will carry the marks of the day they were fired.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What color is Graphite Grey zellige?

Graphite Grey zellige is a deep, cool-toned grey with glaze movement that shifts between darker and lighter tones across each tile. The tone reads lighter or darker depending on light exposure, with variation in depth created by the hand-dipped glazing process. Natural surface features — chips, pits, and crazing — also contribute to how the color reads across a completed installation.

### What design styles suit Graphite Grey zellige?

Graphite Grey zellige suits a wide range of design styles due to its neutral, cool-toned palette. The tile works in modern minimalist interiors where surface subtlety is part of the design direction, as well as in industrial and contemporary settings where the handcrafted character adds warmth to harder materials. It also performs in classical and transitional interiors where glazed variation reads as handmade depth against more restrained surrounding finishes.

### Can Graphite Grey zellige be used on a shower floor?

Yes. Graphite Grey 4x4 can be used on shower walls, shower floors, and steam showers. Sealing is required for all shower applications. For shower floor installations, slip resistance can be increased by sizing grout joints to a minimum of ⅛" and applying an anti-slip treatment after installation is complete. Refer to the [Zellige Installation Guide](https://ziatile.com/pages/zellige-installation-guide) for full instructions.

### How do I clean Graphite Grey zellige?

Clean Graphite Grey zellige with a pH-neutral cleaner and warm water using a soft cloth or mop. Avoid acidic or abrasive products — including vinegar, bleach, and citrus-based cleaners — which can damage the glaze and grout surface over time. Sweep or vacuum regularly to prevent abrasive debris from scratching the sealed surface, and re-seal grout lines periodically as directed in the [Zellige Installation Guide](https://ziatile.com/pages/zellige-installation-guide).

### Can Graphite Grey zellige be mixed with other colors in the collection?

Yes. Graphite Grey 4x4 can be combined with other colorways in Zia's zellige collection. Each colorway is produced using the same traditional methods, so tiles share consistent thickness, format, and surface character across the range. Graphite Grey works particularly well alongside warm whites, creams, and earthy tones in the collection, where the cool grey reads as a grounded counterpoint to warmer fields.

### Does Graphite Grey zellige work in a pool or spa?

Yes. Graphite Grey 4x4 is suitable for pool and spa applications in both saltwater and chlorine environments. Sealing is required; apply sealer to all tiles and grout lines exposed to continuous moisture after grouting is complete. Consult the [Zellige Installation Guide](https://ziatile.com/pages/zellige-installation-guide) for specific sealing instructions and reach out to our team at [info@ziatile.com](mailto:info@ziatile.com) with any project-specific questions.
