## About

No two zellige tiles are the same. Pieces vary in size, surface, and glaze across each box, with hairline crazing and chamfered edges that come from how the material is hand-formed rather than flaws to correct.

Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with zellige, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with [California Prop 65](https://www.ziatile.com/proposition-65-warnings). Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered cuts or Schluter strips. Traditional trim and edge pieces do not exist for zellige.

Seal zellige according to our installation instructions, whether the application is dry or exposed to moisture (pools, spas, showers). Reach out to our team at [info@ziatile.com](mailto:info@ziatile.com) with any material or installation questions.

## How It's Made

Zellige is hand-formed in Morocco, where the technique has been refined for more than 800 years and passed across generations of artisans.

The process begins with raw clay, which is mined from local hillsides, soaked, filtered for impurities, and hand-pressed into molds. The tiles are laid to dry in the Moroccan sun, flattened and shaped by hand, then dried further before glazing.

Each tile is hand-dipped in glaze and arranged inside an earthen kiln, where uneven heat creates the tonal variation that defines zellige. No two pieces leave the kiln the same, and the handmade nature and variation are hallmarks of this material. To prepare for installation, open and mix all boxes before setting them to achieve an ideal layout. The tiles will carry the marks of the day they were fired.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What color is Amber zellige?

Amber zellige is a warm honey-toned neutral with a glaze that shifts in tone depending on light and viewing angle. The warmth of the Amber glaze is most apparent under natural light; under cooler artificial light, the tone softens slightly but remains within the warm range. The hand-dipping process and uneven kiln heat mean individual tiles carry different amounts of depth, so no two tiles carry exactly the same tone.

### Is Amber zellige warm or cool-toned?

Amber zellige is warm-toned. The glaze sits in the warm part of the neutral spectrum, pulling toward honey and amber. It reads as a warm neutral across a wide range of surrounding materials and lighting conditions, making it a versatile choice for installations where a grounded, warm surface is the intent without committing to a more saturated color.

### What grout color works best with Amber zellige?

A grout color matched closely to the warm honey tone of the tile produces the most cohesive surface. A lighter neutral grout will introduce contrast and draw more attention to individual tile variation. Avoid highly contrasting grout colors, which can stain the glazed surface and are difficult to remove fully if excess grout is not wiped away promptly after installation.

### Can Amber zellige be used in a pool or spa?

Yes. Amber 4x4 is suitable for pool and spa applications in both saltwater and chlorine environments. Sealing is required; apply sealer to all tiles and grout lines exposed to continuous moisture after grouting is complete. Consult the [Zellige Installation Guide](https://ziatile.com/pages/zellige-installation-guide) for specific sealing instructions.

### How does Amber zellige compare to Glazed Earth zellige?

Both Amber and Glazed Earth are warm neutral zellige colorways, but they sit in different parts of the neutral spectrum. Amber carries honey tones that read as a warm amber-neutral. Glazed Earth moves into beige territory and reads with more earth undertones — less golden, more clay. The choice between the two depends on whether a honey-warm tone or a more earthy, beige-neutral surface is the design intent.

### Can Amber zellige be mixed with other colors in the collection?

Yes. Amber 4x4 can be combined with other colorways in Zia's zellige collection. Each colorway is produced using the same traditional methods, so tiles share consistent thickness, format, and surface character across the range. Amber works well alongside warm whites, creams, and earthy tones in the collection, where the warm neutral reads as a grounded field against more saturated colorways.
