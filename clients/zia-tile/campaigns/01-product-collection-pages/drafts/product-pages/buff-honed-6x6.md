## How It's Made

Limestone is quarried from solid blocks near Fez, Morocco, and around Bordeaux, France, in workshops where the material has been cut and honed for centuries. Each block is sized down to the format dimensions and finished by stone masons at the source.

The honed finish is achieved by gently smoothing the surface to a clean, soft matte. The bush-hammered finish is achieved by striking the face with a textured hammer head, which uniformly pits the surface for a more rustic, aged character. Both finishes preserve the natural mineral signature of the stone.

Limestone is a living material. Subtle tonal variation across pieces is inherent to natural stone, and no two tiles are identical. The soft patina that develops over years of use is a defining feature of the material and the reason limestone has carried so many of the floors that survive from antiquity into the present.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is honed limestone?

Honed limestone is produced by grinding the stone's surface with fine abrasives until it reaches a smooth, flat, matte result. The process removes surface irregularities without introducing a reflective sheen, leaving the stone's natural color and markings visible. The matte surface also provides better traction than polished stone, making it practical for both floor and wall applications.

### Is honed limestone good for a bathroom floor?

Yes. Honed limestone is suitable for a bathroom floor. Its matte, non-glossy surface provides better traction in wet areas compared to polished stone, and the honed finish hides scratches, wear, and water spots more effectively. Proper sealing is required before grouting and should be maintained at regular intervals. In wet floor applications, slip resistance can be further increased by sizing grout joints to a minimum of ⅛" and applying an anti-slip treatment after installation.

### Can honed limestone be used outdoors?

Yes. Honed limestone may be used on exterior floors and walls in non-freeze/thaw climates. It is not suitable for outdoor installations where snow or ice are present. For outdoor applications, seal with 511 Porous Plus only — do not use Fila Matte Finish Protective Wax outdoors, as UV exposure causes discoloration over time.

### How do I maintain honed limestone over time?

To maintain honed limestone over time, re-seal as often as needed based on traffic and moisture exposure. Clean with pH-neutral products only and address spills promptly. Over time, the stone will develop a natural patina, which is an expected characteristic of limestone. Avoid acidic cleaners, including vinegar, lemon juice, and bleach, as these will etch the stone permanently.

### What grout should I use with honed limestone?

Mapei Flexcolor CQ works well with honed limestone. A lighter neutral or a color matched closely to the stone tends to produce the most cohesive result. Contrasting grout colors can stain the surface and may be difficult to remove from natural stone if excess grout is not wiped away promptly. Grout color is an important design decision — the same stone can read differently depending on whether the grout reads with the stone or against it.

### What causes the variation in honed limestone tiles?

The variation in honed limestone tiles comes from the stone's natural formation process. Limestone is a sedimentary rock built up over millennia from compressed mineral deposits. The honing process reveals these characteristics rather than concealing them, which is why variation across an order is expected and considered part of the material's defining quality. No two tiles share identical markings.
