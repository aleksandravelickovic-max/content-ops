## About

No two zellige tiles are the same. Pieces vary in size, surface, and glaze across each box, with hairline crazing and chamfered edges that come from how the material is hand-formed rather than flaws to correct.

Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with zellige, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with [California Prop 65](https://www.ziatile.com/proposition-65-warnings). Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered cuts or Schluter strips. Traditional trim and edge pieces do not exist for zellige.

Seal zellige according to our installation instructions, whether the application is dry or exposed to moisture (pools, spas, showers). Reach out to our team at [info@ziatile.com](mailto:info@ziatile.com) with any material or installation questions.

## How It's Made

Zellige is hand-formed in Morocco, where the technique has been refined for more than 800 years and passed across generations of artisans.

The process begins with raw clay, which is mined from local hillsides, soaked, filtered for impurities, and hand-pressed into molds. The tiles are laid to dry in the Moroccan sun, flattened and shaped by hand, then dried further before glazing.

Each tile is hand-dipped in glaze and arranged inside an earthen kiln, where uneven heat creates the tonal variation that defines zellige. No two pieces leave the kiln the same, and the handmade nature and variation are hallmarks of this material. To prepare for installation, open and mix all boxes before setting them to achieve an ideal layout. The tiles will carry the marks of the day they were fired.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What color is Absinthe zellige?

Absinthe zellige is an aqua-hued tile with a glaze that moves through green tones depending on light and viewing angle. The colorway sits in the blue-green range — closer to green than blue — and shifts between more saturated aqua tones and softer, more muted greens from tile to tile. This tonal variation is a product of the hand-dipping process and the uneven heat of the earthen kiln.

### How does Absinthe 4x4 compare to Absinthe in other formats?

The Absinthe colorway is available in multiple formats, including the 4x4 square, 2x6 bejmat rectangle, and trapezoid. Each format shares the same aqua glaze and production process, so the surface character is consistent across shapes. The 4x4 square suits installations where a standard grid or stacked layout is the intent, while the 2x6 and trapezoid formats introduce directional movement and pattern geometry.

### Can Absinthe zellige be used in a pool or spa?

Yes. Absinthe 4x4 is suitable for pool and spa applications in both saltwater and chlorine environments, where the aqua-green glaze is particularly well-suited. Sealing is required; apply sealer to all tiles and grout lines exposed to continuous moisture after grouting is complete. Consult the [Zellige Installation Guide](https://ziatile.com/pages/zellige-installation-guide) for specific instructions.

### What grout color works best with Absinthe zellige?

A grout color matched closely to the aqua-green of the tile keeps the surface reading as a continuous field, letting the variation within the glaze lead. A lighter neutral grout will introduce contrast and define each tile more clearly. Avoid highly saturated or dark contrasting grout, which can stain the glazed surface if not wiped away promptly after installation.

### Can Absinthe zellige be mixed with other colors in the collection?

Yes. Absinthe 4x4 can be combined with other colorways in Zia's zellige collection. Each colorway is produced using the same traditional methods, so tiles share consistent thickness, format, and surface character across the range. Absinthe pairs well alongside warm neutrals, creams, and deeper greens, where the aqua reads as a color-forward element against a quieter surrounding field.

### What floor materials complement Absinthe zellige?

Floors that pair well with Absinthe zellige typically fall within natural or matte material categories. Wood flooring in oak or walnut tones complements the aqua-green glaze by introducing warmth and texture. Stone surfaces such as limestone or travertine provide a neutral counterpoint that lets the wall installation remain the focal point. Concrete floors offer a more restrained backdrop, balancing the reflective quality of the zellige surface without competing with the colorway.
