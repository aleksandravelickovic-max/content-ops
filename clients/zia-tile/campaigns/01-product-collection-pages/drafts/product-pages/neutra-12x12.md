## About

Each terrazzo tile is a cement-based composite with crushed marble and stone aggregates ground and polished to a matte surface. Aggregate distribution varies tile to tile. Blend tiles from all boxes before installation and arrange a layout to disperse aggregate evenly.

Perhaps the most versatile tile Zia produces, with endless colorways, patterns, and shapes, Zia strives to offer the world's best curated collection of encaustic terrazzo tiles. While generally consistent in appearance, each handmade tile is inherently unique. Durable and versatile, matte terrazzo tile is an easy choice for many installations.

Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with terrazzo, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with [California Prop 65](https://www.ziatile.com/proposition-65-warnings). Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered edges or Schluter strips.

Proper installation and protection of terrazzo tile is key, so please ensure you work with an installer who has experience specifically installing terrazzo tile, and who has carefully reviewed [Zia's installation guide](https://ziatile.com/pages/terrazzo-installation-guide).

## How It's Made

Terrazzo is produced using techniques refined by Venetian stone masons since the 15th century, when leftover marble offcuts were combined with cement to floor courtyards and palazzo halls. The same craft is preserved in the workshops where Zia's terrazzo is produced today.

Crushed marble and stone aggregates are arranged in steel molds and covered with liquid cement, then hydraulically pressed into a powdered concrete base. Once cured, the surface is ground and polished to the matte finish that defines the line.

Each tile is finished by hand, which produces the subtle tonal variation, occasional crazing, and slight edge irregularity that separate hand-pressed terrazzo from machine-set composite tile. The cement matrix and the visible aggregate are what define terrazzo as a material; the press and the finish are what carry that definition into the floor.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is terrazzo tile?

Terrazzo tile is a composite material made by embedding crushed stone aggregates — such as marble and quartz — into a cement binder, then pressing and finishing the surface to expose the internal composition. Neutra 12x12 follows this process, producing a matte, full-body material where the visible pattern comes from the aggregate itself rather than a surface coating. Because the aggregate runs through the full thickness of the tile, the pattern wears with the floor rather than off it.

### How do I clean Neutra terrazzo tile?

To clean Neutra terrazzo tile, use a pH-neutral cleaner with warm water and a soft cloth or mop for routine maintenance. Avoid acidic or abrasive products — including vinegar, bleach, and ammonia — which can damage the cement matrix over time. Sweep or vacuum regularly to remove abrasive debris that can wear down the surface. Reseal every 1–2 years, or more often in moisture-exposed areas.

### How long does terrazzo tile last?

Terrazzo tile can last for decades when properly installed and maintained. The cement-based structure resists wear under regular use, and the surface can be refinished over time if needed. With correct sealing and routine cleaning, terrazzo tile retains both its structural integrity and its surface appearance across long-term residential and commercial installations.

### Can Neutra 12x12 be used in a kitchen?

Yes. Neutra 12x12 can be used in a kitchen on both floors and walls, including backsplash applications. The material performs well in high-use environments, though it must be sealed before and after grouting to protect against staining from oil, water, and food preparation. Ongoing maintenance with pH-neutral cleaners preserves the surface over time.

### Does Neutra 12x12 require sealing?

Yes. Cement terrazzo requires sealing before and after grouting. Apply 511 Porous Plus in thin layers until the tiles are fully saturated before grouting, wiping off any excess before it dries. For interior installations, finish with Fila Matte Finish Protective Wax as a top coat. For outdoor installations, apply a second coat of 511 Porous Plus in place of wax — UV exposure causes wax to discolor over time.

### How does the 12x12 format work in a terrazzo installation?

The 12x12 format creates a consistent grid across the installation. Blending tiles from multiple boxes before setting is essential to balance tone and aggregate density, as variation becomes more visible at this scale. A 1/8" grout joint is recommended, and installers should plan for minor alignment adjustments using wedge spacers or shims to maintain straight joint lines across the layout.
