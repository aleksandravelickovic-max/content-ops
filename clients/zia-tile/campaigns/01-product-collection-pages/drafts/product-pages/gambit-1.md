## About

No two zellige tiles are the same. The Gambit 1 mosaic uses Casablanca and Pure White — two light neutral colorways with glazes that shift subtly with changing light. Pieces vary in size, surface, and glaze across each box, with hairline crazing and chamfered edges that come from how the material is hand-formed rather than flaws to correct.

Before setting any sheet, separate and mix sheets from multiple boxes to distribute tonal variation evenly across the full layout. Lay out the composition on a flat surface before setting to confirm how the mosaic pattern reads and how the two glazes distribute. Individual tiles can be removed from the mesh backing if adjustments are needed at edges or perimeters.

Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with zellige, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with [California Prop 65](https://www.ziatile.com/proposition-65-warnings). Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered cuts or Schluter strips. Traditional trim and edge pieces do not exist for zellige. Seal Gambit 1 according to our installation instructions, whether in dry areas or areas exposed to moisture. ## How It's Made

Gambit 1 begins in the same place as all traditional zellige. The clay is pulled from the hillsides surrounding Fez, Morocco, soaked in water, and filtered for impurities before being worked by hand until it reaches the right consistency for pressing into molds. For Gambit 1, the molds shape the small geometric elements that make up the mosaic composition.

After drying in the Moroccan sun, each element is dipped individually in Casablanca or Pure White glaze. Because the glaze is applied by hand, coverage varies from piece to piece, and that variation is what produces the tonal range visible across the finished mosaic. Each element is then hand-assembled onto a mesh backing, forming the sheets that arrive on site ready for installation.

The final stage is the kiln. Earthen kilns do not distribute heat evenly, and the results of that unevenness are not incidental. The chips, pits, crazing, and shifting tones that emerge from the firing process are what make each Gambit 1 mosaic its own.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What floors pair well with Gambit 1 zellige?

Gambit 1 zellige pairs well with flooring that balances the white glazed surface and geometric mosaic pattern. Warm wood flooring creates an organic contrast that softens the rhythm of the mosaic, while natural stone flooring reinforces the handcrafted character of the installation. The light neutral palette of Casablanca and Pure White works with a wide range of flooring materials without competing.

### Can I use Gambit 1 zellige in a shower?

Yes. Gambit 1 can be used on shower walls, shower floors, and steam showers. Sealing is required for all shower applications. For shower floors, slip resistance can be increased by sizing grout joints to a minimum of ⅛" and applying an anti-slip treatment after installation. The mosaic format contributes to surface traction through higher grout coverage per square foot, which makes it particularly well suited to shower floor applications.

### What grout color works best with Gambit 1 zellige?

Grout color is a design decision that affects how the mosaic reads. A white grout matched closely to the Casablanca glaze produces a seamless surface that emphasizes the continuous field. Light grey or neutral grout softens the variation and highlights the hand-formed edges of each element. Darker grout introduces contrast and makes the geometric pattern more pronounced across the full installation. Use Mapei Ultracolor Plus FA, which works well with glazed zellige and cleans off without staining the glaze if wiped promptly after application.

### Is Gambit 1 zellige good for floors?

Yes. Gambit 1 can be used on interior floors, particularly in low-traffic residential areas such as guest bathrooms and bedrooms. The mosaic format contributes to slip resistance in everyday conditions, and performance improves further when grout joints are sized to a minimum of ⅛" and an anti-slip treatment is applied after installation. High-traffic floor installations require careful planning to account for the inherent variation in thickness and edge profile.

### Can Gambit 1 zellige be used on exterior walls?

Yes. Gambit 1 can be used on exterior walls in non-freeze/thaw climates. The mosaic performs well on vertical surfaces where the glazed finish and variation are protected from direct structural impact. It is not suitable for exterior floor installations in any climate that experiences freeze/thaw conditions. Sealing is required for all exterior applications.

### Can I mix Gambit 1 with other zellige colors?

Yes. Gambit 1 can be mixed with other colorways in Zia's zellige collection. Each colorway is produced using the same traditional methods, so tiles and mosaic sheets share consistent surface character across the range. The white Casablanca and Pure White glaze of Gambit 1 works particularly well alongside deeper solid colorways, where the geometric mosaic reads as a lighter patterned counterpoint to a larger field.
