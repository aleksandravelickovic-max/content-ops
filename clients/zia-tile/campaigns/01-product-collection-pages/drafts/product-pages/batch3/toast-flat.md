Extruded in central Japan and fired once, Zia's Toast Flat is a golden-brown ceramic tile in a 9.45" × 2.36" rectangle format with a smooth, flat surface. The color references the warm caramel-brown of toast: a golden, amber-inflected tan that sits between honey and dark wheat, warmer than a standard beige and more grounded than a yellow. Where Toast Furrowed uses raised ridges to introduce surface texture, Toast Flat presents the same colorway as a continuous, even plane — the warm golden-brown reads across the field as an undivided ground without the light-and-shadow play of a textured surface. In a room, the effect is warmer and more settled than a cool white or grey ceramic, with the elongated 9.45" × 2.36" rectangle format drawing a strong horizontal register across the wall when set in a standard stack. On a feature wall or kitchen backsplash, the flat surface allows the color and the tile line to carry the composition. On an exterior wall, the smooth surface sheds water cleanly without channels or ridges. The tile is 1/2" thick — slightly thicker than standard ceramic at 3/8" — which should be accounted for in transitions between materials and at finished edges. High variations in color, shade, and surface texture are natural characteristics of this extruded ceramic, and part of the material's identity, even in the flat version where the variation reads as tonal rather than textural. ## About

Field Trip: Japan is Zia's line of extruded Japanese ceramics — a distinct process and a distinct product category from the molded ceramics in the broader Zia ceramic collection. Where molded ceramics are pressed into a form, extruded tiles are pushed through a die-cut mold and cut to length, a process that produces a different surface character and a slightly thicker body.

The Toast series is produced in central Japan and fired once. The collection includes three finish variations — flat, furrowed, and etched — each sharing the same golden-brown colorway but differing in surface texture. High variation in color, shade, and surface texture is inherent to the extruded process and is a defining quality of the Field Trip: Japan line, including the flat version.

Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with ceramic, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with [California Prop 65](https://ziatile.com/pages/proposition-65-warnings). Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered edges or Schluter strips. The 1/2" body thickness of Field Trip: Japan tiles requires careful transition planning where this tile meets other materials.

## How It's Made

Field Trip: Japan tiles are produced using an extrusion process — the clay body is pushed through a die-cut mold and cut into individual tiles. This differs from the molded process used in the broader Zia ceramic collection, where clay is pressed into a form. The extrusion process produces the characteristic elongated format of Field Trip: Japan tiles and gives the body a slightly different texture and density than a molded tile.

Each tile is fired once in central Japan, where the high-firing tradition for ceramics extends back centuries. The single firing produces a dense, durable body suited to both wall and floor applications. The flat version of Toast receives a smooth surface during the extrusion process, allowing the golden-brown color to read without surface interruption.

High variation in color, shade, and surface texture is inherent to extruded ceramics and is more pronounced in Field Trip: Japan tiles than in Zia's standard molded ceramic line. Even in the flat version, individual tiles may vary in tone from one to the next. Mixing tiles from multiple boxes and arranging a dry layout before setting is essential for distributing variation evenly across the installation.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does the Toast Flat colorway read in a room?

Toast Flat is a golden-brown — a warm, amber-inflected tan that sits between honey and dark wheat. The flat surface presents the color as an even, continuous field across the installation, without the light-and-shadow play of the furrowed version. Against warm materials like natural oak or aged plaster, the golden-brown reads as a complementary tone in the same warm register. Against cooler materials — white stone, concrete, grey linen — the warmth of Toast Flat comes forward clearly. The 9.45" × 2.36" rectangle format introduces a strong horizontal direction when set in a standard stack layout.

### How does Toast Flat differ from Toast Furrowed?

Both tiles share the same golden-brown color, the same 9.45" × 2.36" rectangle format, the same extruded production method, and the same high variation characteristics of the Field Trip: Japan series. The difference is purely on the surface: Toast Flat is smooth, and the color reads as an even plane across the wall. Toast Furrowed has raised linear ridges that catch light at an angle and introduce shadow and depth across the field. Toast Flat is the quieter of the two — a surface where color and tile line lead, rather than texture.

### Does Toast Flat require sealing?

Toast Flat does not require a penetrating sealer before grouting. For specific installation questions about this material, contact [info@ziatile.com](mailto:info@ziatile.com) or 310-844-1170.

### Can Toast Flat be used on a shower floor or wall?

Yes. Zia's Toast Flat is suited for shower walls and shower floors. Plan for a minimum 1/8" grout joint on the shower floor surface. An anti-slip treatment applied after installation increases traction in high-exposure wet floor areas. For shower applications, the elongated tile format suits a vertical wall stack or horizontal field with strong directional grout lines.

### What does high variation mean for Toast Flat, and how should it be managed during installation?

High variation in color, shade, and surface texture is inherent to extruded ceramics and applies to Toast Flat even though the surface is smooth rather than textured. In the flat version, variation expresses as tonal shifts from tile to tile — some tiles reading slightly warmer or lighter within the same golden-brown range. Mixing tiles from all boxes thoroughly before installation and laying out a full dry arrangement before setting is the correct approach to distribute these tonal differences evenly across the field.

### How should the 1/2" body thickness of Toast Flat be handled at tile transitions?

At transitions to adjacent flooring or wall materials, the 1/2" body thickness — compared to the 3/8" standard for most ceramics — requires planning. Options include a Schluter strip designed for the height differential, a mitered edge where tiles meet at an exposed corner, or aligning adjacent materials to match the finished height of the Toast Flat surface. Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile where the tile terminates at a wall edge. For any transition questions, contact [info@ziatile.com](mailto:info@ziatile.com) or 310-844-1170.
