## About

Zia's ceramics are the design counterpoint to handmade zellige. They were developed with a Japanese potters' village outside Nagoya, where high-firing extends back more than 1,000 years, to produce a tile precise enough for tight grout joints and clean layouts.

The collection spans matte, satin, and glossy finishes plus the textured Field Trip: Japan series, in three core formats: 2×8 and 2×4 rectangles and 4×4 squares. Color and tone vary slightly across high-fired ceramics — less than handmade zellige, but enough that mixing boxes before the set still matters.

Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with ceramic, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with [California Prop 65](https://ziatile.com/pages/proposition-65-warnings). Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered edges or Schluter strips.

Sealing requirements vary by finish: matte ceramics must be sealed before grouting as a grout release; satin and glossy finishes do not require pre-grout sealing. For any material or installation questions, reach out to our team at [info@ziatile.com](mailto:info@ziatile.com).

## How It's Made

Zia's ceramics are produced in a potters' village outside Nagoya, where high-temperature ceramic firing has been practiced for more than 1,000 years. The collection was developed through a direct collaboration with these workshops to bring architectural-grade precision to Zia's tile range.

Each tile is molded and glazed, then fired at high temperature in a single pass. The high-firing process produces a dense, durable body with very minor variation in tone and dimension — consistent enough for tight grout joint layouts while still retaining the character of individually fired pieces.

Matte finishes are applied as a flat glaze that diffuses light evenly and requires a penetrating sealer before grouting. Satin and glossy finishes carry more reflectivity and do not require pre-grout sealing.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does the Alpha White 4x4 read differently from the 2x4 and 2x8 formats?

The 4×4 square is the largest format in the Alpha White rectangle and square range. Its balanced proportions mean horizontal and vertical grout lines carry equal visual weight, producing a symmetrical grid without a dominant direction. The 2×4 creates a tighter grid with more visual activity at the grout lines. The 2×8 introduces a strong horizontal or vertical directional register depending on layout orientation. All three share the same bright satin white; the format determines the surface rhythm.

### Does Alpha White 4x4 require sealing before installation?

No. The Alpha White 4x4 is a satin finish and does not require a penetrating sealer before grouting. Only matte-finish ceramics require pre-grout sealing as a grout release. For any installation questions, contact [info@ziatile.com](mailto:info@ziatile.com) or 310-844-1170.

### Can Alpha White 4x4 be used on a shower floor?

Yes. Zia's Alpha White 4x4 is suited for shower walls and shower floors. Plan for a minimum 1/8" grout joint on the shower floor to support traction underfoot. An anti-slip treatment applied after installation increases slip resistance in high-exposure wet floor areas.

### Can Alpha White 4x4 be installed in a pool or spa?

Yes. Zia's satin-finish ceramics are approved for pool and spa applications. The Alpha White 4x4 is compatible with saltwater and chlorine environments and suits pool waterline tile and spa interior wall applications, where the bright white and balanced square format produce a clean, even result.

### What materials pair well with Alpha White 4x4?

Alpha White pairs well across a wide range of material palettes — warm wood, cool stone, plaster, and metal — because the bright, undertone-neutral white holds without pulling the room in a warm or cool direction. A grout in a tone-on-tone white keeps the surface quiet and lets the grid recede. A dark or warm-toned grout articulates the grid pattern clearly, making the 4×4 tile format a stronger graphic element in the composition.

### Is the Alpha White 4x4 suited for a kitchen floor?

Yes. The 4×4 format produces a measured, even grid on a kitchen floor — consistent enough to read as orderly, small enough to suit the scale of a residential kitchen without the floor dominating the room. The satin finish cleans easily and does not require pre-grout sealing, which simplifies the kitchen installation sequence. For kitchens with in-floor radiant heating, the Alpha White 4x4 is compatible with all heated floor systems.
