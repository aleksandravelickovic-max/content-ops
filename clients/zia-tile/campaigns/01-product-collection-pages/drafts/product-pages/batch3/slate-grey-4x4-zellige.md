## About

No two zellige tiles are the same. Pieces vary in size, surface, and glaze across each box, with hairline crazing and chamfered edges that come from how the material is hand-formed rather than flaws to correct.

Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with zellige, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with [California Prop 65](https://www.ziatile.com/proposition-65-warnings). Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered cuts or Schluter strips. Traditional trim and edge pieces do not exist for zellige.

Seal zellige according to our installation instructions, whether the application is dry or exposed to moisture (pools, spas, showers). Reach out to our team at [info@ziatile.com](mailto:info@ziatile.com) with any material or installation questions.

## How It's Made

Zellige is hand-formed in Morocco, where the technique has been refined for more than 800 years and passed across generations of artisans.

The process begins with raw clay, which is mined from local hillsides, soaked, filtered for impurities, and hand-pressed into molds. The tiles are laid to dry in the Moroccan sun, flattened and shaped by hand, then dried further before glazing.

Each tile is hand-dipped in glaze and arranged inside an earthen kiln, where uneven heat creates the tonal variation that defines zellige. No two pieces leave the kiln the same, and the handmade nature and variation are hallmarks of this material. To prepare for installation, open and mix all boxes before setting them to achieve an ideal layout. The tiles will carry the marks of the day they were fired.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What does Slate Grey zellige look like in a room?

Zia's Slate Grey reads as a clean, resolved neutral — present enough to register as a design choice, restrained enough to support rather than direct the rest of the room. Against white cabinetry or plaster walls, the glaze takes on depth without going dark. Against stone, concrete, or charcoal fixtures, it holds its middle ground. The dynamic glazes across the field mean no two tiles read exactly the same, so the installed surface carries movement and tonal range rather than a flat, uniform grey.

### Can Slate Grey 4x4 zellige be used in a shower?

Yes. Zia's Slate Grey 4x4 is suited for shower walls, shower floors, and steam showers. Sealing is required for all shower applications. On shower floors, grout joints should measure a minimum of ⅛" and an anti-slip treatment should be applied after installation is complete. Refer to the Zellige Installation Guide for sealing specifications, or contact the team at [info@ziatile.com](mailto:info@ziatile.com) or 310-844-1170 with installation questions.

### Is Slate Grey zellige suitable for pool and spa applications?

Yes. Slate Grey zellige is approved for pool waterlines, spa interiors, and fountains in both saltwater and chlorine environments. Sealing is required per the installation instructions. The grey glaze suits pool surrounds that call for a mineral, stone-adjacent palette rather than a traditional blue or white waterline tile.

### What grout color reads best with Slate Grey?

A grey grout matched to the midpoint of the Slate Grey field — neither the lightest nor darkest tile in the box — allows the surface to read as a continuous field of tonal variation. A lighter grout brightens the joints and introduces more contrast between tiles. A darker grout recedes the grid and emphasizes the glaze variation across each tile. Avoid specifying grout by swatch name until a dry layout is complete and the actual tile range is visible on the surface.

### How does Slate Grey 4x4 differ from the Slate Grey 2x6?

Both formats carry the same Slate Grey colorway and are produced using the same traditional Moroccan methods. The 4x4 square reads as a field tile suited to broad, open surfaces. The 2x6 bejmat rectangle introduces a linear, directional quality and is ¾" thick — notably thicker than the standard 4x4 — which affects substrate preparation and edge finishing at transitions. The two formats can be combined in one installation where the design calls for a format change at a border or niche.

### Can Slate Grey 4x4 be mixed with other zellige colorways?

Yes. Slate Grey 4x4 can be combined with other zellige colorways in the same installation. It pairs well with deeper greys, blue-toned colorways, and warm neutrals, where it functions as the anchoring middle tone in a palette. Each colorway in Zia's zellige collection is produced using the same traditional methods, so formats and surface character remain consistent across the range.
