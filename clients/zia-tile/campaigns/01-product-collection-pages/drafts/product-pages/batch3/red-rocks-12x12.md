Each terrazzo tile is a cement-based composite with crushed marble and stone aggregates ground and polished to a matte surface. Aggregate distribution varies tile to tile. Blend tiles from all boxes before installation and arrange a layout to disperse aggregate evenly.

Perhaps the most versatile tile Zia produces, with endless colorways, patterns, and shapes, Zia strives to offer the world's best curated collection of encaustic terrazzo tiles. ## How It's Made

Terrazzo is produced using techniques refined by Venetian stone masons since the 15th century, when leftover marble offcuts were combined with cement to floor courtyards and palazzo halls. The same craft is preserved in the workshops where Zia's terrazzo is produced today.

Crushed marble and stone aggregates are arranged in steel molds and covered with liquid cement, then hydraulically pressed into a powdered concrete base. Once cured, the surface is ground and polished to the matte finish that defines the line.

Each tile is finished by hand, which produces the subtle tonal variation, occasional crazing, and slight edge irregularity that separate hand-pressed terrazzo from machine-set composite tile. The cement matrix and the visible aggregate are what define terrazzo as a material; the press and the finish are what carry that definition into the floor.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does Red Rocks 12x12 read in a room?

Red Rocks reads as a warm, rosy field with cream and earth-tone aggregate distributed through the pink base. The tone sits in the range of a sun-bleached rose rather than a vivid pink, and the earth-tone flecks prevent the surface from reading as purely warm or purely cool. Against white plaster, the pink registers as architectural and material rather than decorative. Against natural wood and terracotta, the palette coheres with an earthy, elemental register.

### Does Red Rocks 12x12 terrazzo require sealing?

Yes. Cement terrazzo requires sealing before and after grouting. Apply 511 Porous Plus in thin layers until the tiles are fully saturated before grouting, wiping off any excess before it dries. For interior installations, finish with Fila Matte Wax as a top coat. For outdoor installations in non-freeze/thaw climates, apply a second coat of 511 Porous Plus in place of wax, as UV exposure causes wax to discolor over time.

### Can Red Rocks 12x12 be used on a shower floor?

Yes. Red Rocks 12x12 can be used on shower floors and shower walls. Terrazzo carries a DCOF of 0.51, which meets the wet area slip resistance threshold for floor applications. Seal the tile before and after grouting as required, and size grout joints to a minimum of ⅛". An anti-slip product can be applied after installation to further increase traction in high-exposure wet floor areas.

### How do I clean Red Rocks terrazzo tile?

To clean Red Rocks terrazzo tile, vacuum or sweep regularly to remove abrasive debris that can scratch the matte surface. For routine cleaning, use a pH-neutral cleaner with warm water and a soft cloth or mop. Avoid acidic and abrasive products — including vinegar, lemon juice, and bleach — as these will damage the cement matrix over time. For heavier staining, a dedicated stone and tile cleaner formulated for cement surfaces is recommended.

### What design styles suit Red Rocks?

Red Rocks is suited to interiors where warmth and material texture are part of the design direction. The pink-earth palette connects naturally to Mediterranean, Southwestern, and desert-influenced interiors, and integrates well with Japandi and contemporary spaces that draw on warm neutral palettes. Red Rocks also works as a fireplace surround where the warmth of the tile complements the heat of the hearth.

### How does the 12x12 format affect how the aggregate reads?

At 12x12, the warm pink field and scattered aggregate in Red Rocks resolve into a continuous surface with enough scale for the speckled composition to register as a pattern across the installation. Blending tiles from all boxes and dry-laying the arrangement before setting is essential, as aggregate distribution varies tile to tile and becomes more visible at this format. A 1/8" grout joint is recommended, with minor shimming anticipated to maintain straight lines across the field.
