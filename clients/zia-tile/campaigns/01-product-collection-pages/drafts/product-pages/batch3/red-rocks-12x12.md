## How It's Made

Terrazzo is produced using techniques refined by Venetian stone masons since the 15th century, when leftover marble offcuts were combined with cement to floor courtyards and palazzo halls. The same craft is preserved in the workshops where Zia's terrazzo is produced today.

Crushed marble and stone aggregates are arranged in steel molds and covered with liquid cement, then hydraulically pressed into a powdered concrete base. Once cured, the surface is ground and polished to the matte finish that defines the line.

Each tile is finished by hand, which produces the subtle tonal variation, occasional crazing, and slight edge irregularity that separate hand-pressed terrazzo from machine-set composite tile. The cement matrix and the visible aggregate are what define terrazzo as a material; the press and the finish are what carry that definition into the floor.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does Red Rocks 12x12 read in a room?

Red Rocks reads as a warm, rosy field with cream and earth-tone aggregate distributed through the pink base. The tone sits in the range of a sun-bleached rose rather than a vivid pink, and the earth-tone flecks prevent the surface from reading as purely sweet or purely warm. Against white plaster, the pink registers as architectural and material rather than decorative. Against natural wood and terracotta, the palette coheres with an earthy, elemental register.

### Does Red Rocks 12x12 terrazzo require sealing?

Yes. Cement terrazzo requires sealing before and after grouting. Apply 511 Porous Plus in thin layers until the tiles are fully saturated before grouting, wiping off any excess before it dries. For interior installations, finish with Fila Matte Wax as a top coat. For outdoor installations in non-freeze/thaw climates, apply a second coat of 511 Porous Plus in place of wax, as UV exposure causes wax to discolor over time.

### Can Red Rocks 12x12 be used on a shower floor?

Yes. Red Rocks 12x12 can be used on shower floors and shower walls. Terrazzo carries a DCOF of 0.51, which meets the wet area slip resistance threshold for floor applications. Seal the tile before and after grouting as required, and size grout joints to a minimum of ⅛". An anti-slip product can be applied after installation to further increase traction in high-exposure wet floor areas.

### How do I clean Red Rocks terrazzo tile day to day?

Vacuum or sweep regularly to remove abrasive grit that can scratch the matte surface. For routine cleaning, a pH-neutral stone cleaner with warm water and a soft cloth or mop is sufficient. Avoid abrasive scrubbing pads, which can dull the ground surface over time. For heavier staining, a dedicated stone and tile cleaner formulated for cement surfaces is recommended.

### Why must acidic cleaners be avoided on terrazzo?

Terrazzo's cement matrix is sensitive to acid. Vinegar, lemon juice, bleach, and citrus-based cleaners break down the cement binder over repeated use, dulling the surface and compromising the stain resistance that sealing provides. The damage accumulates gradually — which is why a pH-neutral cleaning routine protects the surface across the life of the installation rather than requiring reactive repairs.

### What design styles suit Red Rocks?

Red Rocks is suited to interiors where warmth and material texture carry the design direction. The pink-earth palette connects naturally to Mediterranean, Southwestern, and desert-influenced interiors, and integrates well with Japandi and contemporary spaces drawing on warm neutral palettes. The rosy field reads against fireplace stone and natural limestone with an affinity that makes it a considered choice for hearth surrounds as well as floors.

### How does the 12x12 format affect how the aggregate reads?

At 12x12, the warm pink field and scattered aggregate in Red Rocks resolve into a continuous surface with enough scale for the speckled composition to register as a material across the installation. Blending tiles from all boxes and dry-laying the arrangement before setting is essential — aggregate distribution varies tile to tile, and that variation becomes more apparent at this format. A 1/8" grout joint is recommended, with minor shimming anticipated to keep lines straight across the field.

### What does blending tiles from multiple boxes involve on site?

Each box in the order should be opened and tiles intermixed before installation begins. Aggregate distribution in terrazzo varies between individual tiles, and setting pieces drawn sequentially from a single box can concentrate similar aggregate density in one area of the floor. Dry-laying the full arrangement — or the largest portion the site allows — gives the installer visibility into how the warm pink field and earth-tone speckles balance across the finished surface before any tile is set.

### How does terrazzo compare to polished concrete in appearance and maintenance?

The materials share a flat, continuous field quality, but terrazzo's aggregate is a deliberate composition set into the cement matrix, whereas polished concrete's surface reflects whatever aggregate was already in the slab. Red Rocks has a designed palette — the specific stone types, densities, and colors are fixed at production — which gives the installation a consistency that polished concrete cannot replicate. Maintenance protocols overlap: pH-neutral cleaners and periodic resealing apply to both. Terrazzo tiles can also be individually replaced if damaged, which is an advantage over in-place poured systems.

### What causes crazing on terrazzo, and should it be a concern?

Crazing is a fine network of surface-level cracks in the cement matrix that results from the pressing and curing process in hand-made terrazzo. It is visible at close range on some tiles and less apparent on others. Crazing does not affect the structural integrity of the tile or its bond to the substrate when properly installed, and it does not compromise water resistance in sealed applications. It is a characteristic of hand-pressed cement tile, not an indicator of a manufacturing defect.

### Can a cracked or chipped Red Rocks tile be repaired?

A cracked or chipped tile can be replaced individually without disturbing the surrounding field, provided overage was retained from the original order. Zia recommends keeping extra tiles from the original batch, as aggregate composition and color calibration can shift between production runs. A replacement tile from the same order provides the closest match for aggregate density and the rosy pink base tone. ### Is Red Rocks terrazzo suitable for freeze/thaw climates?

No. Cement terrazzo is not rated for exterior installation in freeze/thaw climates. Water penetrating the cement matrix during freeze cycles can cause surface damage over time. In non-freeze/thaw climates, Red Rocks 12x12 performs well in exterior floors, patios, and courtyard applications with proper sealing.
