## About

No two zellige tiles are the same. Pieces vary in size, surface, and glaze across each box, with hairline crazing and chamfered edges that come from how the material is hand-formed rather than flaws to correct.

Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with zellige, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with [California Prop 65](https://www.ziatile.com/proposition-65-warnings). Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered cuts or Schluter strips. Traditional trim and edge pieces do not exist for zellige.

Seal zellige according to our installation instructions, whether the application is dry or exposed to moisture (pools, spas, showers). Reach out to our team at [info@ziatile.com](mailto:info@ziatile.com) with any material or installation questions.

## How It's Made

Zellige is hand-formed in Morocco, where the technique has been refined for more than 800 years and passed across generations of artisans.

The process begins with raw clay, which is mined from local hillsides, soaked, filtered for impurities, and hand-pressed into molds. The tiles are laid to dry in the Moroccan sun, flattened and shaped by hand, then dried further before glazing.

Each tile is hand-dipped in glaze and arranged inside an earthen kiln, where uneven heat creates the tonal variation that defines zellige. No two pieces leave the kiln the same, and the handmade nature and variation are hallmarks of this material. To prepare for installation, open and mix all boxes before setting them to achieve an ideal layout. The tiles will carry the marks of the day they were fired.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What color is Racing Green zellige?

Racing Green zellige is a deep, saturated green with a glaze that shifts between richer and slightly lighter tones across tiles. The depth of the green varies — some tiles carry a more saturated tone, others sit slightly lighter — because the hand-dipping process and uneven kiln heat produce different results each time. The result is a surface with tonal depth rather than a flat, uniform color, and the variation becomes more apparent once the tiles are laid together.

### What layout patterns work with the Racing Green 2x6 Bejmat format?

Running bond with offset horizontal joints is the most common approach. Herringbone amplifies the directional quality of the format and creates more visual movement across the deep green surface. Vertical stack produces a quieter installation where each tile reads as a narrow vertical element. With Racing Green's depth of color, herringbone in particular draws attention to both the format and the glaze variation simultaneously.

### What materials pair well with Racing Green zellige?

Natural stone, plaster, and matte ceramic in neutral tones sit naturally alongside Racing Green. Unlacquered brass and raw metal fixtures complement the deep green without competing with it. Warm white and cream cabinetry provides a clean counterpoint that allows the Racing Green surface to anchor the space. Natural wood also pairs well, where the warmth of the grain contrasts with the cool depth of the green glaze.

### Can Racing Green 2x6 zellige be used in a shower?

Yes. Racing Green 2x6 can be used on shower walls, shower floors, and steam showers. Sealing is required for all shower applications. For shower floor installations, slip resistance can be increased by sizing grout joints to a minimum of ⅛" and applying an anti-slip treatment after installation is complete. Refer to the [Zellige Installation Guide](https://ziatile.com/pages/zellige-installation-guide) for full instructions.

### Does the Racing Green colorway vary between production runs?

Yes. Each batch of zellige is produced by hand, and the clay, glaze, and kiln conditions shift from one run to the next. Tiles from separate orders may differ in tone and surface character. If the project requires tiles from multiple orders, ordering the full quantity at once gives the most consistent result across the installation.

### Does Racing Green 2x6 zellige work in a pool or spa?

Yes. Racing Green 2x6 is suitable for pool and spa applications in both saltwater and chlorine environments. Sealing is required; apply sealer to all tiles and grout lines exposed to continuous moisture after grouting is complete. Consult the [Zellige Installation Guide](https://ziatile.com/pages/zellige-installation-guide) for specific sealing instructions and reach out to our team at [info@ziatile.com](mailto:info@ziatile.com) with any project-specific questions.
