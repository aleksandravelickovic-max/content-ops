We recommend ordering 15–25% overage so the installation has room for hand-selection, color blending, and the tile breakage inherent to the material. Before laying any piece, unpack every box and mix the tiles on a flat surface to plan the layout and confirm the color balance.

## About

No two zellige tiles are the same. Unglazed Natural pieces vary in size, surface, and clay tone across each box, with edge irregularity and surface texture inherent to the hand-formed process.

Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with zellige, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with [California Prop 65](https://www.ziatile.com/proposition-65-warnings). Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered cuts or Schluter strips. Traditional trim and edge pieces do not exist for zellige.

Seal Unglazed Natural zellige according to our installation instructions; the raw clay surface requires sealing for all applications including dry interior walls. Reach out to our team at [info@ziatile.com](mailto:info@ziatile.com) with any material or installation questions.

## How It's Made

Zellige is hand-formed in Morocco, where the technique has been refined for more than 800 years and passed across generations of artisans.

The process begins with raw clay, which is mined from local hillsides, soaked, filtered for impurities, and hand-pressed into molds. The tiles are laid to dry in the Moroccan sun, flattened and shaped by hand, then dried further before firing.

Unglazed Natural zellige skips the glaze-dipping stage that produces the reflective, color-saturated surface of other zellige colorways. The tiles go directly into earthen kilns, where the uneven heat and natural mineral variation in the clay body determine the final surface — a warm, matte earth tone with subtle variation from tile to tile. The chips, edge irregularity, and surface texture that emerge from the kiln are the full expression of the material, unmediated by a glaze layer.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What color is Unglazed Natural zellige?

Unglazed Natural zellige is a warm raw clay tone that shifts between pale buff, warm cream, and softened earth tones depending on the mineral variation within each tile and the light it receives. Without a glaze layer to control color saturation, the tone is determined entirely by the clay body and the conditions of the kiln, which produces more visible variation tile to tile than glazed zellige. The overall read across an installation is a quiet, earthy neutral with natural tonal movement built into the surface.

### How is Unglazed Natural different from glazed zellige?

Unglazed Natural zellige is fired without any glaze, which means the final surface is the raw clay body rather than a reflective, color-saturated layer. Glazed zellige derives its color from the glaze applied before firing; Unglazed Natural derives its color from the mineral composition of the clay itself. The result is a matte, earthen surface rather than a reflective one. Unglazed Natural also requires a different sealing approach — do not pre-soak before installation, as the raw clay surface absorbs water differently than a glazed tile.

### Does Unglazed Natural zellige require sealing?

Yes. Sealing is required for all Unglazed Natural zellige applications, including dry interior walls. Apply 511 Porous Plus before grouting to saturate the raw clay surface and protect it during installation. For interior applications, apply Fila Matte Wax after grouting as a protective top coat. For exterior applications in non-freeze/thaw climates, apply a second coat of 511 Porous Plus in place of wax — UV exposure causes wax to discolor outdoors. Do not use wax on exterior installations.

### Can Unglazed Natural zellige be used in a shower?

Yes. Unglazed Natural 4x4 can be used on shower walls and shower floors. Sealing is required before and after grouting for all shower applications. For shower floor installations, slip resistance can be increased by sizing grout joints to a minimum of ⅛" and applying an anti-slip treatment after installation. Refer to the [Zellige Installation Guide](https://ziatile.com/pages/zellige-installation-guide) for complete sealing instructions specific to unglazed zellige.

### Can Unglazed Natural zellige be used outdoors?

Yes, in non-freeze/thaw climates only. Unglazed Natural 4x4 can be used on exterior floors and walls in climates that do not experience freeze/thaw conditions. It is not suitable for exterior floor or wall installations in any climate that gets snow or ice, as freeze/thaw cycling will damage the unglazed clay body. For exterior installations, seal with 511 Porous Plus before and after grouting, and apply a second coat of 511 Porous Plus as a top coat — do not use wax outdoors.

### Can Unglazed Natural be mixed with glazed zellige colorways?

Yes. Unglazed Natural can be mixed with glazed zellige colorways in the same installation. The matte, raw clay surface reads as a quiet neutral counterpoint to the reflective glaze of adjacent colorways, and the contrast between the two surfaces — one matte, one glazed — can be a deliberate compositional element. Each tile is produced using the same hand-formed method, so tiles share consistent size range and edge character across the two types. Plan any mixed installation during the dry layout stage before setting begins.
