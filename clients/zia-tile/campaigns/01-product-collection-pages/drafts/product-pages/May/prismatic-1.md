## About

No two zellige tiles are the same. The Prismatic 1 mosaic uses Casablanca White — a light neutral with a glaze that shifts subtly with changing light. Pieces vary in size, surface, and glaze across each box, with hairline crazing and chamfered edges that come from how the material is hand-formed rather than flaws to correct.

Before setting any sheet, separate and mix sheets from multiple boxes to distribute tonal variation evenly across the full layout. Lay out the composition on a flat surface before setting to confirm how the mosaic pattern reads and how the glaze variation distributes across the geometric structure. Individual tiles can be removed from the mesh backing if adjustments are needed at edges or perimeters.

Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with zellige, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with [California Prop 65](https://www.ziatile.com/proposition-65-warnings). Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered cuts or Schluter strips. Traditional trim and edge pieces do not exist for zellige. Seal Prismatic 1 according to our installation instructions, whether in dry areas or areas exposed to moisture. Reach out to [info@ziatile.com](mailto:info@ziatile.com) with any material or installation questions.

## How It's Made

Prismatic 1 begins in the same place as all traditional zellige. The clay is pulled from the hillsides surrounding Fez, Morocco, soaked in water, and filtered for impurities before being worked by hand until it reaches the right consistency for pressing into molds. For Prismatic 1, the molds shape the small geometric elements that make up the mosaic composition.

After drying in the Moroccan sun, each element is dipped individually in Casablanca White glaze. Because the glaze is applied by hand, coverage varies from piece to piece, and that variation is what produces the tonal range visible across the finished mosaic. Each element is then hand-assembled onto a mesh backing, forming the sheets that arrive on site ready for installation.

The final stage is the kiln. Earthen kilns do not distribute heat evenly, and the results of that unevenness are not incidental. The chips, pits, crazing, and shifting tones that emerge from the firing process are what give Prismatic 1 its surface depth and ensure that no two installed panels read exactly alike.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What color is Prismatic 1 zellige mosaic?

Prismatic 1 is a monochromatic mosaic in Casablanca White — a light neutral with a glaze that shifts between near-white, pale grey, and warmer off-white tones tile to tile. Because the pattern uses a single colorway, the tonal variation within the Casablanca glaze becomes the surface interest: the geometric structure of the mosaic reads through the subtle differences in tone rather than through color contrast between two glazes. The mosaic reads as bright white at a distance and reveals more tonal complexity up close.

### What makes Prismatic 1 different from other zellige mosaics?

Prismatic 1 uses a single colorway — Casablanca White — across all elements of the geometric mosaic. Other Zia zellige mosaics use two or more colorways to define the pattern through color contrast. In Prismatic 1, the contrast comes from the geometric structure itself and the subtle glaze variation within the white range. The result is a mosaic that reads as quieter and more monolithic at standard viewing distances, with surface complexity that becomes more apparent in raking light or at close range.

### Can Prismatic 1 be used in a shower?

Yes. Prismatic 1 can be used on shower walls, shower floors, and steam showers. Sealing is required for all shower applications. For shower floors, the higher grout coverage per square foot in the mosaic format contributes to traction alongside a minimum ⅛" grout joint and an anti-slip treatment applied after installation is complete. Refer to the [Zellige Installation Guide](https://ziatile.com/pages/zellige-installation-guide) for full instructions.

### Can Prismatic 1 be used in a pool?

Yes. Prismatic 1 is suitable for pool and spa applications in both saltwater and chlorine environments. Sealing is required; apply sealer to all tiles and grout lines exposed to continuous moisture after grouting is complete. The light neutral Casablanca glaze reads naturally alongside pool water and pairs well with a wide range of pool interior and surround materials. Consult the [Zellige Installation Guide](https://ziatile.com/pages/zellige-installation-guide) for specific sealing instructions.

### What grout color works best with Prismatic 1 zellige mosaic?

With a single-colorway white mosaic, grout color shapes how prominently the geometric pattern and individual tile edges read. White grout matched closely to the Casablanca glaze produces the most seamless surface, where the mosaic pattern recedes and the field reads as near-continuous white. Light grey or neutral grout creates soft contrast that makes individual tile edges visible without introducing another color. Darker grout, such as warm grey or charcoal, frames each tile more distinctly and makes the geometric pattern more prominent — this approach reveals more of the mosaic's structure than a white grout does.

### Can Prismatic 1 be paired with solid zellige tiles?

Yes. Prismatic 1 mosaic can be used alongside solid Casablanca White zellige tiles — in the 4x4 square or 2x6 Bejmat format — to create a layered installation where the mosaic and the solid field share the same colorway but introduce a format and pattern contrast. Using Prismatic 1 as a feature area or border against a solid Casablanca White field tile is a common approach. Each component is produced using the same traditional methods and shares consistent surface character across the range.
