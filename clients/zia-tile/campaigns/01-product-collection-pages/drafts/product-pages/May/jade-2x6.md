## About

No two zellige tiles are the same. Pieces vary in size, surface, and glaze across each box, with hairline crazing and chamfered edges that come from how the material is hand-formed rather than flaws to correct.

Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with zellige, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with [California Prop 65](https://www.ziatile.com/proposition-65-warnings). Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered cuts or Schluter strips. Traditional trim and edge pieces do not exist for zellige.

Seal zellige according to our installation instructions, whether the application is dry or exposed to moisture (pools, spas, showers). Reach out to our team at [info@ziatile.com](mailto:info@ziatile.com) with any material or installation questions.

## How It's Made

Zellige is hand-formed in Morocco, where the technique has been refined for more than 800 years and passed across generations of artisans.

The process begins with raw clay, which is mined from local hillsides, soaked, filtered for impurities, and hand-pressed into molds. The tiles are laid to dry in the Moroccan sun, flattened and shaped by hand, then dried further before glazing.

Each tile is hand-dipped in glaze and arranged inside an earthen kiln, where uneven heat creates the tonal variation that defines zellige. No two pieces leave the kiln the same, and the handmade nature and variation are hallmarks of this material. To prepare for installation, open and mix all boxes before setting them to achieve an ideal layout. The tiles will carry the marks of the day they were fired.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What color is Jade 2x6 zellige?

Jade 2x6 zellige is a deep iridescent green with a glaze that shifts between rich emerald, shadowed forest green, and brighter jade tones depending on the light and viewing angle. Individual tiles carry different positions in that range — some read more deeply saturated, others more brightly green — and the variation distributes across the full installation to produce a surface with tonal depth and movement. The color reads most fully in natural light, where the iridescent quality of the glaze becomes more pronounced across the Bejmat field.

### What is the Bejmat format?

Bejmat is the traditional Moroccan name for this rectangular zellige format, sized here at 2x6 inches. The elongated rectangle offers more directional flexibility than a square format — the same tile can produce very different installations depending on whether it is laid horizontally, vertically, or in a pattern like herringbone. The ratio of the rectangle also reads differently at different scales: more prominent in a compact backsplash, quieter across a larger wall surface.

### What layout patterns work best with Jade 2x6 zellige?

Running bond with horizontal offset joints is the most common approach, which allows the iridescent green glaze to carry across the surface in a structured field. Herringbone introduces visual movement and draws attention to the format's directional quality. Vertical stack creates a column-like surface where the elongated tile reads with more formality. Each layout affects how the tonal variation in the green glaze distributes across the installation, so planning a dry layout before setting is essential for confirming the intended effect.

### Can Jade 2x6 zellige be used in a pool?

Yes. Jade 2x6 is suitable for pool and spa applications in both saltwater and chlorine environments. Sealing is required; apply sealer to all tiles and grout lines exposed to continuous moisture after grouting is complete. The deep green glaze of Jade works particularly well at pool waterlines, where the color interacts with reflected water light. Consult the [Zellige Installation Guide](https://ziatile.com/pages/zellige-installation-guide) for specific sealing instructions.

### What paint color goes with Jade 2x6 zellige?

Jade 2x6 pairs with paint colors that either complement or contrast the deep iridescent green. Warm whites and soft creams create a bright surround that allows the green to lead. Warm terracotta and clay tones create an earthy contrast that reinforces the handcrafted character of the zellige. Deep charcoal, navy, and black increase saturation and create a dramatic, high-contrast composition. For painted cabinetry or wainscoting, sage and soft olive greens tie into the green family while staying quieter than the tile itself.

### Can Jade 2x6 be mixed with other zellige formats in the same installation?

Yes. Jade 2x6 can be mixed with other formats or colorways in Zia's zellige collection. Each format is produced using the same traditional methods, so tiles share consistent surface character across the range. Jade 2x6 Bejmat used alongside Jade 4x4 square in the same installation is a common approach — the two formats share the same colorway but introduce a format contrast that reads as deliberate without introducing a second color. When mixing formats, plan the full layout during the dry stage before setting any tile.
