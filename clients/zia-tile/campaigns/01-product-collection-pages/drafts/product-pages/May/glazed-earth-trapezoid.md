## About

No two zellige tiles are the same. Pieces vary in size, surface, and glaze across each box, with hairline crazing and chamfered edges that come from how the material is hand-formed rather than flaws to correct.

Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with zellige, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with [California Prop 65](https://www.ziatile.com/proposition-65-warnings). Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered cuts or Schluter strips. Traditional trim and edge pieces do not exist for zellige.

Seal zellige according to our installation instructions, whether the application is dry or exposed to moisture (pools, spas, showers). Reach out to our team at [info@ziatile.com](mailto:info@ziatile.com) with any material or installation questions.

## How It's Made

Zellige is hand-formed in Morocco, where the technique has been refined for more than 800 years and passed across generations of artisans.

The process begins with raw clay, which is mined from local hillsides, soaked, filtered for impurities, and hand-pressed into molds. The tiles are laid to dry in the Moroccan sun, flattened and shaped by hand, then dried further before glazing.

Each tile is hand-dipped in glaze and arranged inside an earthen kiln, where uneven heat creates the tonal variation that defines zellige. No two pieces leave the kiln the same, and the handmade nature and variation are hallmarks of this material. To prepare for installation, open and mix all boxes before setting them to achieve an ideal layout. The tiles will carry the marks of the day they were fired.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What color is Glazed Earth Trapezoid zellige?

Glazed Earth Trapezoid zellige is a warm sandy beige with a glaze that shifts through tan, warm stone, and softened earth tones depending on the light and viewing angle. The palette sits in the quiet warm neutral range — grounded and earthy rather than cool or pale — and individual tiles carry different positions in that range, producing a surface with subtle tonal movement rather than uniform color. The glaze reads warmest in natural light and settles toward a softer stone tone under cooler or artificial light.

### How does the trapezoid format affect the Glazed Earth pattern?

The trapezoid format interlocks with adjacent pieces to form a repeating geometric structure across the installation, where the angled sides of each tile create a scale-like, layered composition. In horizontal orientation, the tiles stack to create the full pattern across the surface; in vertical orientation, the pointed ends read upward and the composition takes on more directional energy. With the Glazed Earth colorway, the warm neutral glaze allows the geometric structure of the pattern to lead — the color serves as a backdrop that makes the trapezoid format itself the visual focus.

### Can Glazed Earth Trapezoid be used in a pool?

Yes. Glazed Earth Trapezoid is suitable for pool and spa applications in both saltwater and chlorine environments. Sealing is required; apply sealer to all tiles and grout lines exposed to continuous moisture after grouting is complete. Confirm tile orientation across the full pool layout during the dry stage before setting, as the interlocking trapezoid format requires consistent positioning to resolve correctly. Consult the [Zellige Installation Guide](https://ziatile.com/pages/zellige-installation-guide) for specific sealing instructions.

### What sealing is required for Glazed Earth Trapezoid zellige?

Sealing is required for all Glazed Earth Trapezoid installations, including dry interior walls. Apply sealer before grouting and again after grouting is complete for all wet applications, including showers, pools, and spas. Use the sealer specified in the [Zellige Installation Guide](https://ziatile.com/pages/zellige-installation-guide). For pool and spa applications, ensure full cure before filling the vessel. Reach out to [info@ziatile.com](mailto:info@ziatile.com) with any project-specific questions.

### What materials pair well with Glazed Earth Trapezoid zellige?

The quiet warmth of the Glazed Earth palette pairs with a wide range of surrounding materials. Natural plaster, raked or polished, creates a unified earthy surface where the tile's pattern leads. Warm white and cream paint creates a cleaner backdrop that allows the trapezoid geometry to read more distinctly. Wood, woven materials, and linen-textured surfaces sit naturally alongside the sandy beige glaze. In Zia's zellige collection, Glazed Earth Trapezoid pairs particularly well with deeper colorways used in adjacent areas — the neutral trapezoid field reads as a refined counterpoint to a more saturated accent.

### Can Glazed Earth Trapezoid be mixed with other Glazed Earth formats?

Yes. Glazed Earth Trapezoid can be mixed with other Glazed Earth formats — including the 2x6 Bejmat and 4x4 square — or with other colorways in Zia's zellige collection. Each format is produced using the same traditional methods and shares consistent surface character. Using Glazed Earth Trapezoid as a feature zone against a square or Bejmat field tile in the same colorway is a common approach for adding geometric detail without introducing a second color. Plan any format-mixed layout during the dry stage before setting begins.
