## About

No two zellige tiles are the same. Pieces vary in size, surface, and glaze across each box, with hairline crazing and chamfered edges that come from how the material is hand-formed rather than flaws to correct.

Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with zellige, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with [California Prop 65](https://www.ziatile.com/proposition-65-warnings). Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered cuts or Schluter strips. Traditional trim and edge pieces do not exist for zellige.

Seal zellige according to our installation instructions, whether the application is dry or exposed to moisture (pools, spas, showers). Reach out to our team at [info@ziatile.com](mailto:info@ziatile.com) with any material or installation questions.

## How It's Made

Zellige is hand-formed in Morocco, where the technique has been refined for more than 800 years and passed across generations of artisans.

The process begins with raw clay, which is mined from local hillsides, soaked, filtered for impurities, and hand-pressed into molds. The tiles are laid to dry in the Moroccan sun, flattened and shaped by hand, then dried further before glazing.

Each tile is hand-dipped in glaze and arranged inside an earthen kiln, where uneven heat creates the tonal variation that defines zellige. No two pieces leave the kiln the same, and the handmade nature and variation are hallmarks of this material. To prepare for installation, open and mix all boxes before setting them to achieve an ideal layout. The tiles will carry the marks of the day they were fired.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What color is Brownstone zellige?

Brownstone zellige is a warm deep brown with a glaze that shifts between rich chocolate, burnt umber, and amber-brown tones depending on the light and viewing angle. Individual tiles carry different positions in that range, and those differences distribute across the full installation to produce a surface with depth rather than uniformity. The glaze reads darker in low light and picks up more amber warmth in direct natural light, which means the full character of the colorway changes throughout the day.

### What does Brownstone zellige pair with?

Brownstone pairs with materials that sit in the same warm, earthy range or that contrast it cleanly. Warm cream and off-white walls create a surround that allows the depth of the brown glaze to lead. Natural wood cabinets, shelving, and floors complement the warm tones without competing. Unlacquered brass and aged bronze fixtures read naturally alongside the dark, earthy brown. For a stronger composition, white cabinetry or pale plaster walls provide a clear contrast that makes the dark glaze more prominent.

### Can Brownstone 4x4 zellige be used in a shower?

Yes. Brownstone 4x4 can be used on shower walls, shower floors, and steam showers. Sealing is required for all shower applications. For shower floor installations, slip resistance can be increased by sizing grout joints to a minimum of ⅛" and applying an anti-slip treatment after installation is complete. Refer to the [Zellige Installation Guide](https://ziatile.com/pages/zellige-installation-guide) for full instructions.

### What grout color works best with Brownstone zellige?

A grout matched to the mid-brown range of the tile creates a continuous field where the tonal variation in the glaze reads as the main surface feature. A lighter buff or warm greige grout creates soft contrast that highlights the individual tile edges and the natural chips and crazing in the glaze. Dark espresso or near-black grout introduces strong contrast that makes the geometric regularity of the tile grid more apparent. Avoid cool grey grouts, which pull the warm brown tones toward a less intentional read.

### Can Brownstone 4x4 be used in a pool?

Yes. Brownstone 4x4 is suitable for pool and spa applications in both saltwater and chlorine environments. Sealing is required; apply sealer to all tiles and grout lines exposed to continuous moisture after grouting is complete. Consult the [Zellige Installation Guide](https://ziatile.com/pages/zellige-installation-guide) for specific sealing instructions and reach out to our team at [info@ziatile.com](mailto:info@ziatile.com) with any project-specific questions.

### Can Brownstone be mixed with other zellige colorways?

Yes. Brownstone can be mixed with other colorways in Zia's zellige collection. Casablanca White or Pure White zellige used as a field tile alongside Brownstone as a feature or insert area is a common approach that allows the deep brown to define a zone without dominating the full installation. Each colorway is produced using the same traditional methods, so tiles share consistent surface character and thickness across the range. Confirm any mixed-colorway layout during the dry stage before setting begins.
