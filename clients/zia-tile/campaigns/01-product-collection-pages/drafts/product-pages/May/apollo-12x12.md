## About

Each terrazzo tile is a cement-based composite with crushed marble and stone aggregates ground and polished to a matte surface. Aggregate distribution varies tile to tile. Blend tiles from all boxes before installation and arrange a layout to disperse aggregate evenly.

Perhaps the most versatile tile Zia produces, with endless colorways, patterns, and shapes, Zia strives to offer the world's best curated collection of encaustic terrazzo tiles. While generally consistent in appearance, each handmade tile is inherently unique. Durable and versatile, matte terrazzo tile is an easy choice for many installations.

Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with terrazzo, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with [California Prop 65](https://www.ziatile.com/proposition-65-warnings). Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered edges or Schluter strips.

Proper installation and protection of terrazzo tile is key, so please ensure you work with an installer who has experience specifically installing terrazzo tile, and who has carefully reviewed [Zia's installation guide](https://ziatile.com/pages/terrazzo-installation-guide).

## How It's Made

Terrazzo is produced using techniques refined by Venetian stone masons since the 15th century, when leftover marble offcuts were combined with cement to floor courtyards and palazzo halls. The same craft is preserved in the workshops where Zia's terrazzo is produced today.

Crushed marble and stone aggregates are arranged in steel molds and covered with liquid cement, then hydraulically pressed into a powdered concrete base. Once cured, the surface is ground and polished to the matte finish that defines the line.

Each tile is finished by hand, which produces the subtle tonal variation, occasional crazing, and slight edge irregularity that separate hand-pressed terrazzo from machine-set composite tile. The cement matrix and the visible aggregate are what define terrazzo as a material; the press and the finish are what carry that definition into the floor.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the Apollo colorway in terrazzo?

Apollo is a grey-based terrazzo with a multi-colored aggregate blend distributed across a mid-grey cement matrix. The aggregate introduces a range of stone tones — cream, warm grey, and occasional brighter flecks — that vary in size and distribution from tile to tile. This creates the characteristic terrazzo read of a unified base field animated by irregular aggregate, which gives the surface complexity without introducing strong color contrast. Apollo reads as a sophisticated, cool-toned grey floor tile with natural stone character.

### Does Apollo 12x12 terrazzo require sealing?

Yes. Cement terrazzo tile requires sealing before and after grouting. Apply 511 Porous Plus in thin layers until the tiles are fully saturated before grouting, wiping off any excess before it dries. For interior installations, finish with Fila Matte Finish Protective Wax as a top coat. For outdoor installations in non-freeze/thaw climates, apply a second coat of 511 Porous Plus in place of wax — UV exposure causes wax to discolor over time outdoors.

### Can Apollo 12x12 terrazzo be used on a shower floor?

Yes. Apollo 12x12 can be used on shower floors and shower walls. Terrazzo has a DCOF of 0.51, which meets the wet area slip resistance threshold for floor applications. Seal the tile before and after grouting as required, and size grout joints to a minimum of ⅛". An anti-slip product can be applied after installation to further increase traction in high-exposure wet floor areas.

### How do I clean Apollo terrazzo tile?

To clean Apollo terrazzo tile, vacuum or sweep regularly to remove abrasive debris that can scratch the surface. For routine cleaning, use a pH-neutral cleaner such as Miracle Sealants pH Neutral Tile and Stone Cleaner. For heavier staining, Miracle Sealants Heavy Duty Cleaner (Acid Substitute) is the recommended product. Avoid acidic or abrasive cleaners — including vinegar, lemon juice, and bleach — as these will damage the cement matrix over time.

### What grout color works with Apollo 12x12 terrazzo?

A light grey or warm greige grout matched closely to the grey cement matrix of Apollo creates a surface that reads as continuous, allowing the aggregate distribution to be the primary visual feature. White grout introduces more contrast and makes individual grout lines more visible. Darker grout shifts the composition and makes the tile grid pattern more prominent. For terrazzo, matching grout to the base color generally produces the most refined result because it allows the aggregate to define the surface rather than the grout line.

### Can Apollo 12x12 be used alongside other terrazzo formats?

Yes. Apollo 12x12 can be used alongside the Apollo 24x24 or other terrazzo colorways in the Zia collection. When mixing formats, ensure aggregate blend and base tone are compatible — tiles from different colorways should be assessed during a dry layout to confirm the visual balance before setting begins. Blend tiles from all boxes regardless of format to distribute aggregate variation evenly across the full installation.
