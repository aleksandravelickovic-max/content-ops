## About

Zia's ceramics are the design counterpoint to our handmade zellige. They were developed with a Japanese potters' village outside Nagoya, where high-firing extends back more than 1,000 years, to produce a tile precise enough for tight grout joints and clean layouts.

The collection spans matte, satin, and glossy finishes plus the textured Field Trip: Japan series, in three core formats: 2x8 and 2x4 rectangles and 4x4 squares. Color and tone vary slightly across high-fired ceramics — less than handmade zellige, but enough that mixing boxes before the set still matters.

Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with ceramic, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with [California Prop 65](https://ziatile.com/pages/proposition-65-warnings). Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered edges or Schluter strips.

Sealing requirements vary by finish: matte ceramics must be sealed before grouting as a grout release; satin and glossy finishes do not require pre-grout sealing. For any material or installation questions, reach out to our team at [info@ziatile.com](mailto:info@ziatile.com).

## How It's Made

Ceramic tile is designed in Los Angeles and made in Japan, where the line is produced using high-temperature firing and precision glaze application. The result is a clean, precisely sized tile with consistent color across boxes, a different product category from handmade clay tile.

Clay is molded into shape, dried, glazed, and fired at high temperature. The matte, satin, and gloss finishes are achieved through different glaze formulations, each producing distinct surface character and slip-resistance performance.

Each format is produced to consistent dimensions across the line, which allows for predictable installation and clean grout lines. Subtle variation in color and surface is normal and reflects the hand-finishing step in production.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What color is Frogtown Flat?

Frogtown is a muted green extruded clay tile with a flat, unglazed surface. The green sits in the earthy, restrained end of the green family — quiet and organic rather than saturated or bright. The color comes entirely from the clay body and high-fire kiln process, with no glaze modifying the final tone. Tile-to-tile variation in the green distributes evenly across the full installation, producing a surface with subtle tonal movement rather than uniform solid color.

### Does Frogtown Flat require sealing before grouting?

Yes. Frogtown Flat is an unglazed ceramic and requires a pre-grout penetrating sealer applied as a grout release before grouting. Apply 511 Porous Plus in a thin, even layer after laying the tile and allow it to cure fully before grouting. Refer to the Zia Ceramic Installation Guide for the full installation and sealing sequence.

### Can Frogtown Flat be used on a shower floor?

Yes. Frogtown Flat can be used on shower floors and shower walls. The textured, unglazed surface provides good slip resistance in wet conditions. Size grout joints to a minimum of 1/8" to support alignment and traction. An anti-slip product can be applied after installation to further increase slip resistance in high-exposure wet floor areas.

### Can Frogtown Flat be installed on an exterior floor?

No. Frogtown Flat is not suitable for exterior floor installations in either freeze/thaw or non-freeze/thaw climates. It can be used on exterior walls in non-freeze/thaw climates. For outdoor floor surfaces, a material rated for exterior floor use is required.

### What pairs well with Frogtown Flat?

Frogtown's muted green pairs naturally with warm neutrals and natural materials that sit within the same earthy register. Warm white, cream, and off-white walls keep the surrounding field light without competing with the green. Natural wood tones — oak, walnut, and olive — complement the earthy green and reinforce the organic character of the surface. Unlacquered brass and warm bronze fixtures add warmth alongside the green. For contrast, deep terracotta or warm clay tones placed in adjacent areas create a natural complementary pairing within the earthy color family.

### Can Frogtown Flat be mixed with other Field Trip Japan colorways?

Yes. Frogtown Flat can be mixed with other Field Trip Japan colorways within the ceramics collection. Because all Field Trip Japan tiles share the same extrusion process, format, and surface character, they can be combined across a layout — for example, mixing Frogtown with Feather or Buckeye for a varied earthy palette. Plan the full color arrangement during the dry stage before setting begins and blend tiles from all boxes to distribute tonal variation evenly.
