## About

No two zellige tiles are the same. Pieces vary in size, surface, and glaze across each box, with hairline crazing and chamfered edges that come from how the material is hand-formed rather than flaws to correct.

Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with zellige, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with [California Prop 65](https://www.ziatile.com/proposition-65-warnings). Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered cuts or Schluter strips. Traditional trim and edge pieces do not exist for zellige.

Seal zellige according to our installation instructions, whether the application is dry or exposed to moisture (pools, spas, showers). Reach out to our team at [info@ziatile.com](mailto:info@ziatile.com) with any material or installation questions.

## How It's Made

Zellige is hand-formed in Morocco, where the technique has been refined for more than 800 years and passed across generations of artisans.

The process begins with raw clay, which is mined from local hillsides, soaked, filtered for impurities, and hand-pressed into molds. The tiles are laid to dry in the Moroccan sun, flattened and shaped by hand, then dried further before glazing.

Each tile is hand-dipped in glaze and arranged inside an earthen kiln, where uneven heat creates the tonal variation that defines zellige. No two pieces leave the kiln the same, and the handmade nature and variation are hallmarks of this material. To prepare for installation, open and mix all boxes before setting them to achieve an ideal layout. The tiles will carry the marks of the day they were fired.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What color is Graphite Grey 2x6 zellige?

Graphite Grey 2x6 zellige is a deep cool grey with a glaze that shifts between charcoal, slate, and darker graphite tones depending on the light and viewing angle. Individual tiles carry different positions in that range, and those differences distribute across the installation to produce a surface with tonal depth rather than a flat, uniform grey. The color reads more darkly saturated in lower light and lightens slightly in direct natural light as the glaze picks up more surface reflection.

### What is the Bejmat format?

Bejmat is the traditional Moroccan name for this rectangular zellige format, sized here at 2x6 inches. The elongated rectangle offers more directional flexibility than a square format — the same tile can produce very different installations depending on whether it is laid horizontally, vertically, or in a pattern like herringbone. The ratio of the rectangle also reads differently at different scales: more prominent in a compact backsplash, quieter across a larger wall surface.

### What layout patterns work best with Graphite Grey 2x6 zellige?

Running bond with horizontal offset joints creates an architectural, linear field where the depth of the grey glaze carries across the surface in a structured pattern. Herringbone introduces more visual activity and draws attention to how the tonal variation in individual tiles shifts across different angles. Vertical stack creates a more formal, column-like surface. In all layouts, the directionality of the Bejmat format affects how the grey reads — planning the layout with tiles spread out during the dry stage helps confirm the intended effect before setting begins.

### Can Graphite Grey 2x6 zellige be used in a shower?

Yes. Graphite Grey 2x6 can be used on shower walls, shower floors, and steam showers. Sealing is required for all shower applications. For shower floor installations, slip resistance can be increased by sizing grout joints to a minimum of ⅛" and applying an anti-slip treatment after installation is complete. Refer to the [Zellige Installation Guide](https://ziatile.com/pages/zellige-installation-guide) for full instructions.

### What pairs well with Graphite Grey 2x6 zellige?

Graphite Grey reads as a versatile anchor in a room's material palette. White marble countertops and fixtures provide clean contrast that lightens the space and allows the grey to define the surface. Warm wood cabinetry and shelving pull warmth into the composition and soften the cool tone of the glaze. Matte black and unlacquered brass fixtures both work well alongside the deep grey — black reinforces the dark tone, while brass introduces warmth. Lighter grey zellige colorways mixed in the same installation can create a tonal, tone-on-tone effect without introducing a second hue.

### Can Graphite Grey 2x6 be mixed with other zellige formats in the same installation?

Yes. Graphite Grey 2x6 Bejmat can be mixed with other formats in Zia's zellige collection, including the 4x4 square format in a complementary colorway or the same Graphite Grey in a different size. Each format is produced using the same traditional methods, so tiles share consistent surface character across the range. When mixing formats, plan the full layout during the dry stage before setting any tile and blend tiles from all boxes to confirm even tonal distribution within each format.
