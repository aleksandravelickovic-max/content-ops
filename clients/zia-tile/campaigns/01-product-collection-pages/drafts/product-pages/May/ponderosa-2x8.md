## About

Zia's ceramics are the design counterpoint to our handmade zellige. They were developed with a Japanese potters' village outside Nagoya, where high-firing extends back more than 1,000 years, to produce a tile precise enough for tight grout joints and clean layouts.

The collection spans matte, satin, and glossy finishes plus the textured Field Trip: Japan series, in three core formats: 2x8 and 2x4 rectangles and 4x4 squares. Color and tone vary slightly across high-fired ceramics — less than handmade zellige, but enough that mixing boxes before the set still matters.

Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with ceramic, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with [California Prop 65](https://ziatile.com/pages/proposition-65-warnings). Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered edges or Schluter strips.

Sealing requirements vary by finish: matte ceramics must be sealed before grouting as a grout release; satin and glossy finishes do not require pre-grout sealing. For any material or installation questions, reach out to our team at [info@ziatile.com](mailto:info@ziatile.com).

## How It's Made

Ceramic tile is designed in Los Angeles and made in Japan, where the line is produced using high-temperature firing and precision glaze application. The result is a clean, precisely sized tile with consistent color across boxes, a different product category from handmade clay tile.

Clay is molded into shape, dried, glazed, and fired at high temperature. The matte, satin, and gloss finishes are achieved through different glaze formulations, each producing distinct surface character and slip-resistance performance.

Each format is produced to consistent dimensions across the line, which allows for predictable installation and clean grout lines. Subtle variation in color and surface is normal and reflects the hand-finishing step in production.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What color is Ponderosa 2x8?

Ponderosa is a muted warm green in glossy finish — an earthy, complex tone that draws from the forest-green family without reading as saturated or bright. The warmth in the green gives it more depth than a cool sage or olive and keeps the color grounded in an earthy register. The glossy glaze amplifies the color's tonal range across the field, shifting between lighter and deeper readings as light moves across the surface. Individual tiles carry slight variation in the green, but the overall reading across the installation is consistent.

### Does Ponderosa 2x8 require sealing before grouting?

No. Ponderosa 2x8 is a glossy finish ceramic and does not require pre-grout sealing. The glossy glaze provides a fully sealed surface — apply grout directly without a sealer step. Matte ceramics require pre-grout sealing as a grout release; satin and glossy ceramics do not. Refer to the Zia Ceramic Installation Guide for the complete installation sequence.

### Can Ponderosa 2x8 be used on a shower floor?

Yes, though slip resistance should be considered for floor applications. Glossy ceramic has a DCOF of 0.30, which is below the threshold typically recommended for wet floor applications without additional treatment. Ponderosa 2x8 is suitable for shower walls without restriction. For shower floor use, an anti-slip treatment applied after installation is strongly recommended to increase traction. Maintaining the minimum 3/16" grout joint also contributes to underfoot grip.

### Can Ponderosa 2x8 be used in a pool?

Yes. Ponderosa 2x8 is suitable for pool waterlines, spa interiors, and fountain installations in both saltwater and chlorine environments. No pre-grout sealing is required for glossy finish ceramics.

### Can Ponderosa 2x8 be installed on an exterior floor?

No. Ponderosa 2x8 is not suitable for exterior floor installations in either freeze/thaw or non-freeze/thaw climates. It can be used on exterior walls in non-freeze/thaw climates. For outdoor floor surfaces, a material rated for exterior floor use is required.

### What pairs well with Ponderosa 2x8?

Ponderosa's muted warm green pairs well with warm natural materials that reinforce its earthy, organic register. Warm white, cream, and off-white walls keep the surroundings open and allow the green to lead. Natural rattan, aged wood, and linen textiles complement the warmth embedded in the green. Unlacquered brass and warm bronze fixtures sit naturally alongside the earthy palette. For contrast, deep terracotta or rust tones placed in adjacent areas create a warm complementary pairing that emphasizes the green's warmth.
