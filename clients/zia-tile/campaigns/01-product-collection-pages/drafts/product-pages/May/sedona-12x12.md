## About

Cantera is the volcanic stone that built colonial Mexico, used continuously for centuries in courtyards, fountains, and the carved facades of cathedrals and haciendas. Zia's Cantera line carries that material into a residential and commercial collection sized for both intimate rooms and broad public installations.

The line is offered in eight earthy colorways, each carrying its own mineral signature: Mesa, Caldera, Acacia, Sierra, Yuma, Sedona, Badlands, and Volcan. Formats range from 6x6 and 4x12 mosaics to 12x12, 12x24, and 24x24 floor tiles, so a single Cantera scheme can run from a textured shower wall in a primary bathroom to a hotel courtyard or restaurant patio in the same colorway.

Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with cantera, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with [California Prop 65](https://www.ziatile.com/proposition-65-warnings). Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered edges or Schluter strips.

## How It's Made

Cantera begins as a porous volcanic rock, formed over millions of years from cooled lava and ash. Solid blocks are pulled from quarries in Mexico, then sized down to the format dimensions of the line: 6x6 and 4x12 mosaics, 12x12 and 12x24 planks, and 24x24 large-format floor tiles.

Each piece is honed and finished by hand at the source. The finishing approach intentionally preserves the visible aggregate, mineral inclusions, and oxidation markings of the volcanic chemistry, which is what gives each colorway its individual mineral signature rather than a surface treatment that would mask it.

Once finished, the tiles are graded and packed for shipping. After installation, the stone continues to develop. Cantera takes on a soft patina with use, similar to marble or limestone, and the sealing protocol on the install guide protects the surface while still allowing the material to age into the room around it.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What color is Sedona cantera?

Sedona cantera is a warm terracotta-toned volcanic stone with a mineral color that shifts between rose-clay, soft terracotta, and deeper ochre-pink across individual tiles. The iron-rich volcanic rock formation that produces the Sedona colorway gives it a warmth distinct from the brown or grey tones of other Cantera colorways. The color reads most richly in warm natural light, where the terracotta and pink-clay tones in the stone become more pronounced.

### What pairs well with Sedona 12x12 cantera?

The warm terracotta and rose-clay tones of Sedona pair naturally with materials in the warm earth register. Warm plaster walls in cream, sand, and ochre tones complement the stone without competing. Natural wood beams, ceiling, and cabinetry reinforce the earthy warmth. Unlacquered brass and oil-rubbed bronze fixtures sit well alongside the pink-clay mineral tones. In outdoor applications, the terracotta warmth of Sedona pairs with adobe, stucco, and desert garden planting in a way that reads as coherent and regionally grounded.

### Does Sedona 12x12 require sealing?

Yes. Cantera tile requires sealing before and after grouting. Apply 511 Porous Plus in thin layers until the stone is fully saturated before grouting, wiping off any excess before it dries. For interior installations, finish with Fila Matte Finish Protective Wax as a top coat. For outdoor installations, apply a second coat of 511 Porous Plus in place of wax — UV exposure causes wax to discolor over time.

### Can Sedona 12x12 be used outdoors?

Yes, in non-freeze/thaw climates only. Sedona 12x12 is suitable for exterior floor and wall applications in climates that do not experience freeze/thaw conditions. It is not suitable for outdoor installation in climates that get snow or ice. For exterior applications, seal before and after grouting and apply a second coat of 511 Porous Plus as a top coat rather than wax.

### How do I clean Sedona cantera?

To clean Sedona cantera, vacuum or sweep regularly to remove abrasive debris. For routine cleaning, use a pH-neutral cleaner such as Miracle Sealants pH Neutral Tile and Stone Cleaner. Avoid acidic cleaners — including vinegar, lemon juice, and bleach — which damage the porous volcanic stone surface over time. Blot spills promptly rather than wiping to prevent spreading and absorption into the stone.

### Can Sedona 12x12 be mixed with other cantera colorways or formats?

Yes. Sedona 12x12 can be mixed with other colorways or formats in Zia's Cantera line. The warm terracotta tone of Sedona can be paired with cooler or more neutral Cantera colorways — such as Volcan or Mesa — to introduce tonal contrast within the same material. When mixing colorways, assess the visual balance during a dry layout before setting any tile. Blend tiles from all boxes to distribute tonal and aggregate variation evenly across the installation.
