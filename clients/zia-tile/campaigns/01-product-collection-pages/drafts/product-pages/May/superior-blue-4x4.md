## About

No two zellige tiles are the same. Pieces vary in size, surface, and glaze across each box, with hairline crazing and chamfered edges that come from how the material is hand-formed rather than flaws to correct.

Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with zellige, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with [California Prop 65](https://www.ziatile.com/proposition-65-warnings). Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered cuts or Schluter strips. Traditional trim and edge pieces do not exist for zellige.

Seal zellige according to our installation instructions, whether the application is dry or exposed to moisture (pools, spas, showers). Reach out to our team at [info@ziatile.com](mailto:info@ziatile.com) with any material or installation questions.

## How It's Made

Zellige is hand-formed in Morocco, where the technique has been refined for more than 800 years and passed across generations of artisans.

The process begins with raw clay, which is mined from local hillsides, soaked, filtered for impurities, and hand-pressed into molds. The tiles are laid to dry in the Moroccan sun, flattened and shaped by hand, then dried further before glazing.

Each tile is hand-dipped in glaze and arranged inside an earthen kiln, where uneven heat creates the tonal variation that defines zellige. No two pieces leave the kiln the same, and the handmade nature and variation are hallmarks of this material. To prepare for installation, open and mix all boxes before setting them to achieve an ideal layout. The tiles will carry the marks of the day they were fired.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What color is Superior Blue zellige?

Superior Blue zellige is a deep, richly saturated blue with a glaze that shifts between navy, cobalt, and midnight tones depending on the light and viewing angle. Individual tiles carry different positions in that range — some reading closer to cobalt, others settling into deeper navy — and the distribution across the installation produces a surface with tonal depth rather than flat uniformity. The color reads most saturated in direct natural light and deepens toward the darker end of the range in lower or artificial light.

### What paint colors work with Superior Blue zellige?

Superior Blue pairs with paint colors that either provide a clean backdrop or reinforce the depth of the blue. Crisp white and bright off-white create the clearest contrast and allow the saturated glaze to lead the room. Soft greige and warm grey provide a more recessive surround that doesn't compete with the blue. Deep charcoal and dark navy used on adjacent walls create a saturated, enveloping composition that reads as moody and intentional. Warm terracotta and clay tones provide an unexpected earthy contrast that draws out the warmth in the glaze's deeper tones.

### Can Superior Blue 4x4 zellige be used in a pool?

Yes. Superior Blue 4x4 is suitable for pool and spa applications in both saltwater and chlorine environments. Sealing is required; apply sealer to all tiles and grout lines exposed to continuous moisture after grouting is complete. The deep blue glaze of Superior Blue works particularly well at pool waterlines, where the color reads alongside and through the water itself. Consult the [Zellige Installation Guide](https://ziatile.com/pages/zellige-installation-guide) for specific sealing instructions.

### Can Superior Blue 4x4 be used in a shower?

Yes. Superior Blue 4x4 can be used on shower walls, shower floors, and steam showers. Sealing is required for all shower applications. For shower floor installations, slip resistance can be increased by sizing grout joints to a minimum of ⅛" and applying an anti-slip treatment after installation is complete. Refer to the [Zellige Installation Guide](https://ziatile.com/pages/zellige-installation-guide) for full instructions.

### What grout color works best with Superior Blue zellige?

A grout matched to the mid-blue range of the tile or a shade slightly lighter creates a continuous surface where the tonal variation in the glaze reads as the defining surface feature. White grout introduces clear contrast and makes individual tile edges more visible, emphasizing the handmade quality of each piece. Dark navy or charcoal grout deepens the composition and minimizes the visibility of grout lines, making the blue field read as more continuous. Avoid warm-toned grouts, which sit in opposition to the cool blue range of the glaze.

### Can Superior Blue be mixed with other zellige colorways?

Yes. Superior Blue can be mixed with other colorways in Zia's zellige collection. Casablanca White or Pure White zellige used alongside Superior Blue creates a classic high-contrast composition where the deep blue and the light neutral balance each other across the installation. Each colorway is produced using the same traditional methods, so tiles share consistent surface character and thickness. Plan any mixed-colorway layout in full during the dry stage before setting begins, and blend tiles from all boxes to ensure even tonal distribution within each colorway.
