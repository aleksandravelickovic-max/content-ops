## About

Zia's ceramics are the design counterpoint to our handmade zellige. They were developed with a Japanese potters' village outside Nagoya, where high-firing extends back more than 1,000 years, to produce a tile precise enough for tight grout joints and clean layouts.

The collection spans matte, satin, and glossy finishes plus the textured Field Trip: Japan series, in three core formats: 2x8 and 2x4 rectangles and 4x4 squares. Color and tone vary slightly across high-fired ceramics — less than handmade zellige, but enough that mixing boxes before the set still matters.

Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with ceramic, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with [California Prop 65](https://ziatile.com/pages/proposition-65-warnings). Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered edges or Schluter strips.

Sealing requirements vary by finish: matte ceramics must be sealed before grouting as a grout release; satin and glossy finishes do not require pre-grout sealing. For any material or installation questions, reach out to our team at [info@ziatile.com](mailto:info@ziatile.com).

## How It's Made

Ceramic tile is designed in Los Angeles and made in Japan, where the line is produced using high-temperature firing and precision glaze application. The result is a clean, precisely sized tile with consistent color across boxes, a different product category from handmade clay tile.

Clay is molded into shape, dried, glazed, and fired at high temperature. The matte, satin, and gloss finishes are achieved through different glaze formulations, each producing distinct surface character and slip-resistance performance.

Each format is produced to consistent dimensions across the line, which allows for predictable installation and clean grout lines. Subtle variation in color and surface is normal and reflects the hand-finishing step in production.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What does Morning Light Furrowed look like?

Morning Light is a light blue-grey extruded clay tile with a furrowed texture — linear channels cut into the surface that run across each piece and catch light differently along each groove. The pale blue-grey color sits at the cool, restrained end of the blue-grey family and shifts between warmer and cooler readings as light moves through the space. The furrowed surface amplifies this shifting quality: the shadows in the channels and the highlights along the ridges create a surface that reads differently from different angles and at different times of day.

### What is the Furrowed finish in Field Trip Japan?

The Furrowed finish refers to the surface texture of the tile, produced through the Field Trip Japan extrusion and die-cut process: the clay is extruded and then cut to create linear channels, or furrows, across each piece. The furrowed surface introduces shadow and highlight across the field, making the tile's color appear more dynamic as light moves across it. The Furrowed finish contrasts with the Flat finish in the Field Trip Japan line, which produces a smooth, untextured unglazed surface.

### Does Morning Light Furrowed require sealing before grouting?

Yes. Morning Light Furrowed is an unglazed ceramic and requires a pre-grout penetrating sealer applied as a grout release before grouting. Apply 511 Porous Plus in a thin, even layer after laying the tile and allow it to cure fully before grouting. Refer to the Zia Ceramic Installation Guide for the full installation and sealing sequence.

### Can Morning Light Furrowed be used on a shower floor?

Yes. Morning Light Furrowed can be used on shower floors and shower walls. The furrowed surface provides improved slip resistance compared to smooth ceramic in wet conditions. Size grout joints to a minimum of 1/8" to further support traction. An anti-slip product can be applied after installation to further increase slip resistance in high-exposure wet floor areas if desired.

### Can Morning Light Furrowed be installed on an exterior floor?

No. Morning Light Furrowed is not suitable for exterior floor installations in either freeze/thaw or non-freeze/thaw climates. It can be used on exterior walls in non-freeze/thaw climates. For outdoor floor surfaces, a material rated for exterior floor use is required.

### What pairs well with Morning Light Furrowed?

Morning Light's pale blue-grey pairs well with warm and cool materials that allow the color's shifting quality to lead. White and warm white walls keep the surrounding field open and neutral, letting the blue-grey and its texture read clearly. Natural linen and light-textured fabrics reinforce the quiet, light-filled character of the tile. Aged brass and brushed nickel fixtures both sit naturally alongside the pale blue-grey. For a more minimal scheme, white or light grey grout keeps the field cohesive and allows the furrowed texture to define the surface rather than the grout lines.
