## About

Zia's ceramics are the design counterpoint to our handmade zellige. They were developed with a Japanese potters' village outside Nagoya, where high-firing extends back more than 1,000 years, to produce a tile precise enough for tight grout joints and clean layouts.

The collection spans matte, satin, and glossy finishes plus the textured Field Trip: Japan series, in three core formats: 2x8 and 2x4 rectangles and 4x4 squares. Color and tone vary slightly across high-fired ceramics — less than handmade zellige, but enough that mixing boxes before the set still matters.

Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with ceramic, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with [California Prop 65](https://ziatile.com/pages/proposition-65-warnings). Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered edges or Schluter strips.

Sealing requirements vary by finish: matte ceramics must be sealed before grouting as a grout release; satin and glossy finishes do not require pre-grout sealing. For any material or installation questions, reach out to our team at [info@ziatile.com](mailto:info@ziatile.com).

## How It's Made

Ceramic tile is designed in Los Angeles and made in Japan, where the line is produced using high-temperature firing and precision glaze application. The result is a clean, precisely sized tile with consistent color across boxes, a different product category from handmade clay tile.

Clay is molded into shape, dried, glazed, and fired at high temperature. The matte, satin, and gloss finishes are achieved through different glaze formulations, each producing distinct surface character and slip-resistance performance.

Each format is produced to consistent dimensions across the line, which allows for predictable installation and clean grout lines. Subtle variation in color and surface is normal and reflects the hand-finishing step in production.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is Field Trip Japan?

Field Trip Japan is a sub-line within Zia's ceramics collection, produced in Japan using extruded clay and die-cut molds rather than the pressed and glazed process used for the standard matte, satin, and glossy ceramic formats. The result is a tile with three-dimensional surface texture — either Flat or Furrowed — that reads differently from a conventional glazed ceramic. The line includes a range of colorways, each named with its own character, and suited to the same installation applications as the broader ceramics range.

### What does Feather Flat look like?

Feather is an off-white textured ceramic with a flat, unglazed surface that reads as a soft, consistent off-white across the installation. The flat finish is smooth but not reflective, and the color carries the subtle warmth of natural clay without the stark brightness of a pure white. Slight tonal movement is present tile to tile, which distributes evenly across the full installation.

### Does Feather Flat require sealing before grouting?

Yes. Feather Flat is a textured, unglazed ceramic and requires a pre-grout penetrating sealer applied as a grout release before grouting. Apply 511 Porous Plus in a thin, even layer after laying the tile and allow it to cure fully before grouting. Refer to the Zia Ceramic Installation Guide for the full installation and sealing sequence.

### Can Feather Flat be used on a shower floor?

Yes. Feather Flat can be used on shower floors and shower walls. The textured flat surface provides good slip resistance in wet conditions. Size grout joints to a minimum of 1/8" to further support alignment and traction underfoot. An anti-slip product can be applied after installation to increase slip resistance in high-exposure wet floor areas.

### Can Feather Flat be installed on an exterior floor?

No. Feather Flat is not suitable for exterior floor installations in either freeze/thaw or non-freeze/thaw climates. It can be used on exterior walls in non-freeze/thaw climates. For outdoor floor surfaces, a material rated for exterior floor use is required.

### What pairs well with Feather Flat?

The soft off-white of Feather pairs well with natural materials that reinforce its quiet, warm character. Warm wood tones — oak, walnut, and light teak — complement the off-white without competing with it. Aged brass fixtures and warm bronze hardware sit naturally alongside the pale clay surface. Linen-textured fabrics and woven rattan add tactile warmth that aligns with the tile's textured surface. For a more minimal scheme, white or pale grey grout keeps the field clean and lets the tile's texture read as the primary surface interest.
