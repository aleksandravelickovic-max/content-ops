## About

No two zellige tiles are the same. Pieces vary in size, surface, and glaze across each box, with hairline crazing and chamfered edges that come from how the material is hand-formed rather than flaws to correct.

Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with zellige, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with [California Prop 65](https://www.ziatile.com/proposition-65-warnings). Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered cuts or Schluter strips. Traditional trim and edge pieces do not exist for zellige.

Seal zellige according to our installation instructions, whether the application is dry or exposed to moisture (pools, spas, showers). Reach out to our team at [info@ziatile.com](mailto:info@ziatile.com) with any material or installation questions.

## How It's Made

Zellige is hand-formed in Morocco, where the technique has been refined for more than 800 years and passed across generations of artisans.

The process begins with raw clay, which is mined from local hillsides, soaked, filtered for impurities, and hand-pressed into molds. The tiles are laid to dry in the Moroccan sun, flattened and shaped by hand, then dried further before glazing.

Each tile is hand-dipped in glaze and arranged inside an earthen kiln, where uneven heat creates the tonal variation that defines zellige. No two pieces leave the kiln the same, and the handmade nature and variation are hallmarks of this material. To prepare for installation, open and mix all boxes before setting them to achieve an ideal layout. The tiles will carry the marks of the day they were fired.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What color is Rouge zellige?

Rouge zellige is a deep red with a glaze that shifts in tone depending on light and viewing angle. The depth of the red varies across tiles — some carry a richer, more saturated tone, others sit slightly lighter — because the hand-dipping process and the uneven heat of the earthen kiln produce different results each time. This variation is what gives a Rouge installation its character.

### What grout color works best with Rouge zellige?

A grout color matched closely to the deep red of the tile produces the most cohesive surface, allowing the tonal variation across tiles to read without interruption from the joints. A lighter grout will introduce contrast and define each tile more sharply. Avoid highly contrasting grout colors, which can stain the glazed surface and are difficult to remove fully if excess grout is not wiped away promptly.

### What materials pair well with Rouge zellige?

Rouge zellige pairs well with materials that allow the depth of the red glaze to lead. Natural stone, matte ceramic, and terrazzo in neutral tones sit naturally alongside it. Unlacquered brass and dark wood tend to complement the deep red tones without competing with the surface variation. White or cream cabinetry provides a clean counterpoint that lets the Rouge surface read clearly.

### Can Rouge zellige be used in a shower?

Yes. Rouge 4x4 is suitable for shower walls, shower floors, and steam showers. Sealing is required for all shower applications. For shower floor installations, slip resistance can be increased by sizing grout joints to a minimum of ⅛" and applying an anti-slip treatment after installation is complete. Refer to the [Zellige Installation Guide](https://ziatile.com/pages/zellige-installation-guide) for full instructions.

### Does the Rouge colorway vary between production runs?

Yes. Each batch of zellige is produced by hand, and the clay, glaze, and kiln conditions shift from one run to the next. Tiles from separate orders may differ in tone and surface character. If the project requires tiles from multiple orders, ordering the full quantity at once gives the most consistent result across the installation.

### Can Rouge zellige be used in a pool or spa?

Yes. Rouge 4x4 is suitable for pool and spa applications in both saltwater and chlorine environments. Sealing is required; apply sealer to all tiles and grout lines exposed to continuous moisture after grouting is complete. Consult the [Zellige Installation Guide](https://ziatile.com/pages/zellige-installation-guide) for specific sealing instructions and reach out to our team at [info@ziatile.com](mailto:info@ziatile.com) with any project-specific questions.
