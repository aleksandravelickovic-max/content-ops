## About

Zia's ceramics are the design counterpoint to our handmade zellige. They were developed with a Japanese potters' village outside Nagoya, where high-firing extends back more than 1,000 years, to produce a tile precise enough for tight grout joints and clean layouts.

The collection spans matte, satin, and glossy finishes plus the textured Field Trip: Japan series, in three core formats: 2x8 and 2x4 rectangles and 4x4 squares. Color and tone vary slightly across high-fired ceramics — less than handmade zellige, but enough that mixing boxes before the set still matters.

Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with ceramic, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with [California Prop 65](https://ziatile.com/pages/proposition-65-warnings). Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered edges or Schluter strips.

Sealing requirements vary by finish: matte ceramics must be sealed before grouting as a grout release; satin and glossy finishes do not require pre-grout sealing. For any material or installation questions, reach out to our team at [info@ziatile.com](mailto:info@ziatile.com).

## How It's Made

Ceramic tile is designed in Los Angeles and made in Japan, where the line is produced using high-temperature firing and precision glaze application. The result is a clean, precisely sized tile with consistent color across boxes, a different product category from handmade clay tile.

Clay is molded into shape, dried, glazed, and fired at high temperature. The matte, satin, and gloss finishes are achieved through different glaze formulations, each producing distinct surface character and slip-resistance performance.

Each format is produced to consistent dimensions across the line, which allows for predictable installation and clean grout lines. Subtle variation in color and surface is normal and reflects the hand-finishing step in production.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the Linen colorway?

Linen is a true ivory — a warm, softly neutral white without strong yellow or grey undertones. In matte finish, the surface reads as a consistent, non-gloss plane across the installation. The 2x8 format produces a classic subway layout with even horizontal grout lines, which can be stacked, offset at 50%, or arranged in herringbone depending on the installation direction.

### Does matte ceramic tile require sealing?

Yes. Matte ceramic tile must be sealed with a penetrating sealer — specifically 511 Porous Plus — before grouting. The sealer acts as a grout release to prevent grout haze from bonding permanently to the matte surface. Apply the sealer in a thin, even layer after laying the tile and allow it to cure fully before grouting. Satin and glossy finishes do not require this pre-grout sealing step.

### Can Linen 2x8 be used on a shower floor?

Yes. Linen 2x8 can be used on shower floors and shower walls. The matte finish has a DCOF of 0.74, which meets the wet area slip resistance threshold. Seal the tile before grouting as required for all matte ceramics, and size grout joints to a minimum of ⅛" to further support traction underfoot. An anti-slip product can be applied after installation to increase slip resistance in high-exposure wet floor areas.

### Can Linen 2x8 be installed on an exterior floor?

No. Linen 2x8 is not suitable for exterior floor installations, in either freeze/thaw or non-freeze/thaw climates. It can be used on exterior walls in non-freeze/thaw climates. For outdoor floor surfaces, a material rated for exterior floor use is required.

### How do I clean matte ceramic tile?

To clean matte ceramic tile, mop with warm water and a pH-neutral cleaner for routine maintenance. Sweep or vacuum regularly to remove grit that can scratch the surface. Blot spills promptly rather than wiping them across the tile. Avoid acidic cleaners — including vinegar, lemon juice, and bleach — as these will damage the matte surface. For heavier stains, Miracle Sealants Heavy Duty Cleaner (Acid Substitute) is the recommended product.

### Can I mix Linen 2x8 with other Zia ceramic finishes?

Yes. Linen 2x8 can be mixed with other Zia ceramics in a layout — for example, pairing matte Linen with a satin or glossy colorway on a feature section or contrasting field. Because finishes carry different light-reflective properties, the visual effect of mixing them is worth testing in a dry layout before setting begins. Sealing requirements differ by finish: matte tiles require pre-grout sealing with 511 Porous Plus; satin and glossy tiles do not.
