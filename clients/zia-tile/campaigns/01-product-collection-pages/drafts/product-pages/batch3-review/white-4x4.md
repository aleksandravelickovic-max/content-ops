## About

Each cement tile carries the hand of the maker in the pigment lay, the press, and the cure: slight irregularities at the edge and small color drifts that read as a single soft matte field once the floor is set. Color shifts subtly tile to tile within every colorway, with the deeper pigments carrying broader internal contrast than the lighter cream and bone tones.

Expect tonal variation across the box, surface character that softens with patina, and small pits or air pockets in the surface, all natural to a hand-poured pigmented cement tile.

Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with cement tile, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with [California Prop 65](https://www.ziatile.com/proposition-65-warnings). Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered edges or Schluter strips.

## How It's Made

Cement tile is hand-poured in Vietnam using techniques refined by French and Catalan craftspeople in the 1850s, when pigmented cement was developed as a more affordable alternative to stone mosaic. The Vietnamese workshops preserve the original method exactly: one tile at a time, by hand.

Each tile begins with mineral pigments laid by hand into a steel pattern frame, one color at a time, on top of a powdered concrete base inside a steel mold. The mold is closed, and the tile is hydraulically pressed at 3,000 lbs psi, soaked in water, and cured on drying racks for seven days. The tile is never fired, which is what distinguishes cement tile from ceramic tile.

The 3 mm pigmented surface layer is what gives cement tile its longevity and its characteristic patina. There is real color depth in the surface, not just a topcoat, which means the tile can wear and re-seal over decades without losing its color.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does the White 4x4 compare to the White 2x8 for the same colorway?

Both are the same pure matte white, but the format produces a different surface. The 4x4 square is non-directional — the grid reads evenly in all directions with no dominant axis. The 2x8 subway introduces a horizontal register; the eye follows the long axis of each tile, giving the surface a lateral quality that the square does not have. The 4x4 is the more neutral format for floors and backsplashes where no directional emphasis is the intent. The 2x8 suits applications where the subway character is part of the design.

### What grout colors create different visual effects with a white field?

A grout close in tone to the white tile produces a near-continuous white field — the grid lines are present but quiet, and the matte white reads as the primary surface. A medium grey grout produces a visible, articulated grid against the white field. A dark or black grout produces a strong high-contrast pattern that makes the 4x4 grid geometry prominent. Because white cement carries subtle tonal variation tile to tile, the grout choice also affects how that variation reads: a matching grout allows it to diffuse into the field; a contrasting grout puts more emphasis on individual tile edges.

### Can White 4x4 be used in a shower?

Yes. Zia's White 4x4 is suited for shower walls and shower floors. Seal the tile before grouting and again after, following the cement installation guide. White cement tile is particularly important to seal carefully before grouting — the light surface can show grout haze if the grout is not released before it sets. For shower floor applications, apply an anti-slip treatment after installation to support traction in wet conditions.

### Can White 4x4 be installed outdoors?

White 4x4 cement tile is suited for exterior floors and walls in non-freeze/thaw climates — patios, courtyards, and covered outdoor spaces in mild climates. It is not suitable for installation in climates where the surface may be subject to freeze/thaw cycles.

### What thinset is recommended for white cement tile?

A white latex-modified or polymer-modified thinset is recommended. Standard grey thinset can bleed through the porous pigmented surface and alter the white tone. Zia's cement installation guide provides specific thinset guidance; contact [info@ziatile.com](mailto:info@ziatile.com) or 310-844-1170 with installer-specific questions.

Zia recommends 1/16" grout joints for the White 4x4 in standard interior applications. For wet commercial floor applications, grout joints should measure at least 1/8" to meet slip-resistance standards.
