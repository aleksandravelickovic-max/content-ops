Zia's Pure White Trapezoid is a clean white zellige in a geometric 1x4 format, with a glaze that carries subtle tonal variation — ranging from bright white to faint warm or cool undertones — depending on the light source and viewing angle. ## About

No two zellige tiles are the same. Pieces vary in size, surface, and glaze across each box, with hairline crazing and chamfered edges that come from how the material is hand-formed rather than flaws to correct.

Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with zellige, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with [California Prop 65](https://www.ziatile.com/proposition-65-warnings). Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered cuts or Schluter strips. Traditional trim and edge pieces do not exist for zellige.

Seal zellige according to our installation instructions, whether the application is dry or exposed to moisture (pools, spas, showers). Reach out to our team at [info@ziatile.com](mailto:info@ziatile.com) with any material or installation questions.

## How It's Made

Zellige is hand-formed in Morocco, where the technique has been refined for more than 800 years and passed across generations of artisans.

The process begins with raw clay, which is mined from local hillsides, soaked, filtered for impurities, and hand-pressed into molds. The tiles are laid to dry in the Moroccan sun, flattened and shaped by hand, then dried further before glazing.

Each tile is hand-dipped in glaze and arranged inside an earthen kiln, where uneven heat creates the tonal variation that defines zellige. No two pieces leave the kiln the same, and the handmade nature and variation are hallmarks of this material. To prepare for installation, open and mix all boxes before setting them to achieve an ideal layout. The tiles will carry the marks of the day they were fired.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What color is Pure White trapezoid zellige?

Pure White trapezoid zellige is a clean white with a glaze that carries subtle tonal variation depending on light and viewing angle. Individual tiles range from bright white to faint warm or cool undertones, with the differences most visible once tiles are laid together. The reflective glaze responds differently to natural versus artificial light, and the tonal variation across tiles produces a surface with movement rather than a flat, uniform white.

### Why choose the trapezoid format over a square?

The trapezoid format produces a geometric interlocking pattern that a square tile cannot. The angled sides of adjacent tiles create a repeating visual structure across the surface, and the pattern changes based on orientation — horizontal settings produce a layered, overlapping effect while vertical orientations produce pointed, fan-like forms. The white glaze amplifies rather than competes with this geometry, since the light variation across each reflective tile reinforces the pattern produced by the interlocking shapes.

### Can Pure White Trapezoid be mixed with other formats?

Yes. Pure White Trapezoid can be mixed with other Pure White formats — including the 2x2 and 4x4 — or with other colorways in Zia's zellige collection. Each format is produced using the same traditional methods and shares consistent surface character. A common approach uses the trapezoid as a feature area against a square or Bejmat field tile in a coordinating or contrasting color.

### Where should I start the layout for trapezoid zellige?

Begin the dry layout from the center of the wall or floor and work outward, so that the pattern distributes symmetrically and edge cuts fall at the perimeter rather than in a visually prominent location. Confirm the orientation of each tile during the dry layout stage, since the interlocking geometry requires consistent direction throughout. Map corner and edge cuts before setting any tile — the angled sides mean perimeter cuts are more complex than a standard square or rectangle.

### How does orientation affect the Pure White Trapezoid pattern?

Horizontal orientation with alternating rows produces a layered, scale-like pattern where the wider face of each tile runs along the wall surface. Vertical orientation with the narrow top facing up reads as a pointed, fan-like arrangement. The glaze variation across tiles interacts differently with each pattern — plan the orientation during the dry layout stage and view the arrangement from the intended viewing distance before setting to confirm the result.

### Does Pure White Trapezoid zellige work in a pool or spa?

Yes. Pure White Trapezoid is suitable for pool and spa applications in both saltwater and chlorine environments. Sealing is required; apply sealer to all tiles and grout lines exposed to continuous moisture after grouting is complete. Consult the [Zellige Installation Guide](https://ziatile.com/pages/zellige-installation-guide) for specific sealing instructions and reach out to our team at [info@ziatile.com](mailto:info@ziatile.com) with any project-specific questions.
