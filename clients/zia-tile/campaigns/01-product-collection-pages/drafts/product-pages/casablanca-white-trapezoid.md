Zia's Casablanca White Trapezoid is a light neutral zellige in a geometric 1x4 format, with a glaze that shifts between near-white, pale grey, and warmer off-white tones depending on light and viewing angle. The trapezoid shape introduces directionality into the layout — tiles can be oriented horizontally or vertically, and the angled sides produce a repeating pattern across the installation that a square or rectangle does not. Herringbone and fan arrangements are both possible; the orientation chosen at installation determines how the geometric quality of the format reads across the full surface.

Casablanca White Trapezoid zellige is hand-formed, dipped in glaze, and fired in earthen kilns in Morocco, where the method has been refined over more than 800 years. Each tile carries the chips, pits, hairline crazing, and tonal shifts that come from working at this scale by hand. ## About

No two zellige tiles are the same. Pieces vary in size, surface, and glaze across each box, with hairline crazing and chamfered edges that come from how the material is hand-formed rather than flaws to correct.

Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with zellige, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with [California Prop 65](https://www.ziatile.com/proposition-65-warnings). Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered cuts or Schluter strips. Traditional trim and edge pieces do not exist for zellige.

Seal zellige according to our installation instructions, whether the application is dry or exposed to moisture (pools, spas, showers). Reach out to our team at [info@ziatile.com](mailto:info@ziatile.com) with any material or installation questions.

## How It's Made

Zellige is hand-formed in Morocco, where the technique has been refined for more than 800 years and passed across generations of artisans.

The process begins with raw clay, which is mined from local hillsides, soaked, filtered for impurities, and hand-pressed into molds. The tiles are laid to dry in the Moroccan Sun, flattened and shaped by hand, then dried further before glazing.

Each tile is hand-dipped in glaze and arranged inside an earthen kiln, where uneven heat creates the tonal variation that defines zellige. No two pieces leave the kiln the same, and the handmade nature and variation are hallmarks of this material. To prepare for installation, open and mix all boxes before setting them to achieve an ideal layout. The tiles will carry the marks of the day they were fired.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What color is Casablanca White zellige?

Casablanca White zellige is a light neutral with a glaze that shifts between near-white, pale grey, and warmer off-white tones depending on light and viewing angle. The colorway reads brightest under direct natural light and settles toward the pale grey and off-white end of the range under cooler or lower light. Individual tiles carry different positions in that tonal range, producing a surface that reads as unified without being uniform.

### What layout patterns work with the trapezoid format?

The trapezoid format produces a repeating geometric pattern that changes based on orientation. Oriented horizontally with alternating rows offset, each tile's angled sides interlock with adjacent tiles to create a distinctive overlapping pattern. Oriented vertically, the narrow end of the trapezoid leads and the composition reads differently across the surface. Plan the layout during the dry layout stage before setting any tile — the geometric quality of the format requires consistent orientation planning throughout.

### Can the Casablanca White Trapezoid be mixed with other Casablanca formats?

Yes. Casablanca White Trapezoid can be mixed with other Casablanca White formats — including the 2x2 and 2x6 Bejmat — or with other colorways in Zia's zellige collection. Each format is produced using the same traditional methods and shares consistent surface character across the range. Using the trapezoid as an accent field against a 2x2 or Bejmat field tile is a common approach in kitchen and bathroom installations.

### How does vertical versus horizontal orientation change the layout?

Oriented horizontally, the trapezoid's wider face runs along the wall and the angled sides create a layered, scale-like pattern across the surface. Oriented vertically, the narrow top faces up and the installation reads as a series of pointed forms. The choice affects how grout lines distribute across the surface and how the glaze variation reads across the full installation. Confirm the orientation during the dry layout stage and plan corner and edge cuts accordingly.

### Can Casablanca White Trapezoid zellige be used in a shower?

Yes. Casablanca White Trapezoid can be used on shower walls, shower floors, and steam showers. Sealing is required for all shower applications. For shower floor installations, slip resistance can be increased by sizing grout joints to a minimum of ⅛" and applying an anti-slip treatment after installation is complete. Refer to the [Zellige Installation Guide](https://ziatile.com/pages/zellige-installation-guide) for full instructions.

### Does cutting trapezoid zellige require special tools?

Cutting zellige requires a wet saw with a diamond blade. The trapezoid shape means corner and edge cuts involve angled geometry — plan edge cuts during the dry layout stage before setting any tile. Because each tile is hand-formed and varies in size, field cuts must be measured tile-by-tile rather than assuming uniformity. Installers should wear protective face and eye coverings throughout.

### Does Casablanca White Trapezoid zellige work in a pool or spa?

Yes. Casablanca White Trapezoid is suitable for pool and spa applications in both saltwater and chlorine environments. Sealing is required; apply sealer to all tiles and grout lines exposed to continuous moisture after grouting is complete. Consult the [Zellige Installation Guide](https://ziatile.com/pages/zellige-installation-guide) for specific sealing instructions and reach out to our team at [info@ziatile.com](mailto:info@ziatile.com) with any project-specific questions.
