## About

No two zellige tiles are the same. Pieces vary in size, surface, and glaze across each box, with hairline crazing and chamfered edges that come from how the material is hand-formed rather than flaws to correct.

Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with zellige, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with [California Prop 65](https://www.ziatile.com/proposition-65-warnings). Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered cuts or Schluter strips. Traditional trim and edge pieces do not exist for zellige.

Seal zellige according to our installation instructions, whether the application is dry or exposed to moisture (pools, spas, showers). Reach out to our team at [info@ziatile.com](mailto:info@ziatile.com) with any material or installation questions.

## How It's Made

Zellige is hand-formed in Morocco, where the technique has been refined for more than 800 years and passed across generations of artisans.

The process begins with raw clay, which is mined from local hillsides, soaked, filtered for impurities, and hand-pressed into molds. The tiles are laid to dry in the Moroccan sun, flattened and shaped by hand, then dried further before glazing.

Each tile is hand-dipped in glaze and arranged inside an earthen kiln, where uneven heat creates the tonal variation that defines zellige. No two pieces leave the kiln the same, and the handmade nature and variation are hallmarks of this material. To prepare for installation, open and mix all boxes before setting them to achieve an ideal layout. The tiles will carry the marks of the day they were fired.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the characteristics of Absinthe Trapezoid zellige?

The Absinthe Trapezoid presents an aqua-hued range that shifts between water-toned greens depending on light and placement. Each tile is hand-shaped into its angular form and hand-dipped in glaze, resulting in high inherent variation in color, shape, and thickness, along with chips, pits, and crazing as natural surface characteristics. The angled edges of the trapezoid define the layout's directional quality across the field.

### Can the Absinthe Trapezoid be used in a kitchen?

Yes. The Absinthe Trapezoid is well-suited to kitchen installations, particularly as a backsplash, where the glazed surface responds well to moisture and routine cleaning. The trapezoid format introduces directional movement across the wall, allowing for layouts that extend beyond a standard grid. As with all zellige, installation requires careful placement to manage variation and maintain a consistent composition across the full surface.

### What grout color works best with Absinthe zellige?

Grout color selection depends on how the installation should read. A tonal grout that aligns closely with the aqua-green glaze creates a continuous surface, allowing the variation within each tile to lead. A contrasting grout defines the trapezoid shape more clearly and draws attention to the pattern geometry. Grout color also affects how chips, pits, and crazing integrate into the finished layout; matching grout tends to absorb these characteristics, while contrasting grout makes them more visible.

### What floor materials complement Absinthe zellige?

Floors that pair well with Absinthe zellige typically fall within natural or matte material categories. Wood flooring in oak or walnut tones complements the aqua-green glaze by introducing warmth and texture. Stone surfaces such as limestone or travertine provide a neutral counterpoint that lets the wall installation remain the focal point. Concrete floors offer a more restrained backdrop, balancing the reflective quality of the zellige surface without competing with the colorway.

### Can the Absinthe Trapezoid be mixed with other zellige colors?

Yes. The Absinthe Trapezoid can be mixed with other colors in Zia's zellige collection. Each colorway is produced using the same traditional methods, so tiles share consistent surface character across the range. The aqua-hued palette works well alongside warm neutrals, creams, and deeper greens, where the glaze reads as a color-forward accent against a quieter surrounding field.

### How does the trapezoid format install?

The trapezoid format installs as a geometric composition where the angled edges of each tile interlock to form directional patterns across the field. The shape supports a range of layouts, from staggered chevron-like arrangements to more complex tessellations. Because the geometry depends on consistent orientation, a full dry run before setting is essential to confirm how the pattern resolves across the complete floor or wall. Wedge spacers help regulate alignment across the irregular edges inherent to the handmade format.
