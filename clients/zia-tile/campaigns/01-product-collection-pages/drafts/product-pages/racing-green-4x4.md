## About

No two zellige tiles are the same. Pieces vary in size, surface, and glaze across each box, with hairline crazing and chamfered edges that come from how the material is hand-formed rather than flaws to correct.

Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with zellige, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with [California Prop 65](https://www.ziatile.com/proposition-65-warnings). Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered cuts or Schluter strips. Traditional trim and edge pieces do not exist for zellige.

Seal zellige according to our installation instructions, whether the application is dry or exposed to moisture (pools, spas, showers). Reach out to our team at [info@ziatile.com](mailto:info@ziatile.com) with any material or installation questions.

## How It's Made

Zellige is hand-formed in Morocco, where the technique has been refined for more than 800 years and passed across generations of artisans.

The process begins with raw clay, which is mined from local hillsides, soaked, filtered for impurities, and hand-pressed into molds. The tiles are laid to dry in the Moroccan sun, flattened and shaped by hand, then dried further before glazing.

Each tile is hand-dipped in glaze and arranged inside an earthen kiln, where uneven heat creates the tonal variation that defines zellige. No two pieces leave the kiln the same, and the handmade nature and variation are hallmarks of this material. To prepare for installation, open and mix all boxes before setting them to achieve an ideal layout. The tiles will carry the marks of the day they were fired.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What color is Racing Green zellige?

Racing Green zellige is a deep green with a glaze that shifts between darker and lighter green tones depending on light and viewing angle. The depth of the color varies across tiles due to the hand-dipping process and the uneven heat of the earthen kiln. No two tiles carry exactly the same tone, and that variation is what defines the surface character of a completed Racing Green installation.

### What grout color works best with Racing Green zellige?

A grout color matched closely to the deep green of the tile produces the most cohesive surface, allowing the tonal variation between tiles to read without interruption from the joints. Contrasting grout will define each tile more sharply and draw more attention to the grid. Avoid highly contrasting colors that can stain the glazed surface if excess grout is not removed promptly after installation.

### What materials pair well with Racing Green zellige?

Racing Green zellige pairs well with materials that allow the depth of the green glaze to remain the focal point. Natural wood, raw stone, and warm-toned plaster sit naturally alongside it. In the surrounding space, unlacquered brass and dark metal finishes complement the deep green without competing with the surface variation. Cream and warm white cabinetry also work well as a counterpoint.

### Can Racing Green zellige be used in a pool or spa?

Yes. Racing Green zellige is suitable for pool and spa applications in both saltwater and chlorine environments. Sealing is required for all wet applications; apply sealer to all tiles and grout lines exposed to continuous moisture after grouting is complete. Consult the [Zellige Installation Guide](https://ziatile.com/pages/zellige-installation-guide) for specific sealing instructions.

### How does Racing Green zellige compare to Absinthe zellige?

Racing Green and Absinthe are both green-toned zellige colorways, but they sit in different parts of the green spectrum. Racing Green is a deep, saturated green that stays within the green range. Absinthe moves into aqua territory, carrying more blue-green in its tonal range. The choice between the two depends on whether a focused, deep green or a more dynamic aqua-green surface is the design intent.

### Is Racing Green zellige suitable for a high-traffic commercial kitchen?

Yes. Racing Green 4x4 is suitable for high-traffic commercial interior floors and walls, including restaurant kitchens. For wet-area commercial floor applications, increase grout joints to a minimum of ⅛" and consider an anti-slip treatment after installation. Sealing is required and should be maintained at regular intervals as directed in the [Zellige Installation Guide](https://ziatile.com/pages/zellige-installation-guide).
