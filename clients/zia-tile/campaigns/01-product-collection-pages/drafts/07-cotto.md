#### Order Details + Installation

We recommend a 15 to 20% overage on all cotto orders to account for cuts and natural variation. Before installation, blend tiles from all boxes and arrange a layout to disperse variation evenly. Wash tile immediately after cutting to prevent the cutting paste from drying on the surface.

Use 1/8" tile spacers for the recommended grout joint. Subfloors must be fully cured (minimum 30 days) to prevent efflorescence from depositing on the tile surface.

Each cotto tile is handmade in San Miguel de Allende, Mexico, with inherent variation in color, surface texture, and edge profile. Mix tiles from all boxes before installation and arrange a dry layout to confirm color balance. Disperse variation evenly across the surface.

## How It's Made *[Locked]*

Cotto is handmade in San Miguel de Allende, Mexico, one of the historic centers of Mexican tile-making. Straight from the earth to your home, the technique has been practiced in the region for generations and produces a tile that wears like natural stone.

Natural clay is mixed with water and blended to the ideal consistency, then pressed in a mold to dry for one to two weeks until the proper moisture level is achieved for firing. Each tile is shaped by hand and reflects the artisan's work that day.

The unglazed surface develops natural patina with use, a defining feature of cotto. With proper sealing, cotto handles indoor floors, exterior floors in any climate, and freeze/thaw exposure.
