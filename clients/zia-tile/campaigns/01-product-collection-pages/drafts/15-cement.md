## About *[Locked]*

Each cement tile is hand-poured in Vietnam using a 3 mm pigmented surface layer over a powdered concrete base. Pour. Press. Patina. Blend tiles from all boxes before installation and arrange a layout to disperse variation evenly. Minor crazing and irregular edges are expected.

Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with cement tile, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with [California Prop 65](https://www.ziatile.com/proposition-65-warnings). Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered edges or Schluter strips.

Cement tile must be sealed on-site, with both pre-grout sealing and a top coat per the [Cement Installation Guide](https://ziatile.com/pages/cement-installation-guide). Re-seal at least every 1 to 2 years. Reach out to our team at info@ziatile.com with any material or installation questions.

## How It's Made *[Locked]*

Cement tile is hand-poured in Vietnam using techniques dating back to French and Catalan craftspeople of the 1850s. The original technique was created as an alternative to traditional mosaic work, and the Vietnamese production retains the original method.

Steel molds are filled with natural pigments, one color at a time, inside a handmade metal frame, then hydraulically pressed at 3,000 psi. The pressed tiles are soaked in water and cured on drying racks for seven days. The tile is never fired, which is what distinguishes cement tile from ceramic tile.

The 3 mm pigmented surface layer is what gives cement tile its longevity and characteristic patina. Real color depth lives in the surface, not just a topcoat, which means the tile can wear and re-seal over decades without losing its color.
