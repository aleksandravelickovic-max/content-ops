#### Order Details + Installation

We recommend a 15 to 20% overage on all glass mosaic orders to account for cuts needed. Before installation, blend sheets from all boxes to distribute pattern variation evenly. All saw cuts must be made with a water-cooled tile saw or radial tile cutter, since glass requires careful handling during cutting.

Use epoxy thinset and epoxy grout for glass mosaic installations. Cement board or backer board are the standard substrates; waterproof underlayments are necessary for wet areas.

## About *[Locked]*

Each glass mosaic sheet is hand-placed on mesh backing in northern Spain, using 98% recycled glass produced in electric kilns powered by renewable energy. The line is precision-produced with minimal color variation. Blend sheets from all boxes before installation to distribute pattern variation evenly across the surface.

Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with glass mosaic, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with [California Prop 65](https://www.ziatile.com/proposition-65-warnings). ## How It's Made *[Locked]*

Glass mosaic is handcrafted in northern Spain using 98% recycled glass and electric kilns powered by renewable energy. The result is a tile with strong sustainability credentials and consistent color performance.

Recycled glass is melted and individually formed in molds, then fired in wind- and solar-powered electric kilns. Once cooled, individual tiles are hand-placed on mesh sheets in either solid layouts or intentional patterns. The line ships ready to install as designed sheets.

Color is highly consistent across the line. The hand placement on mesh gives installations a subtle artisan quality despite the consistency of each individual tile.
