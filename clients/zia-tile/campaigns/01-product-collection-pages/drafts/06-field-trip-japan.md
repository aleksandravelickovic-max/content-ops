## About *[Locked]*

Field Trip: Japan is a brick-format ceramic line in three textural finishes (flat, etched, and furrowed). The line is designed with high color and texture variation tile to tile. Blend thoroughly across boxes and verify your layout before installation.

Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with ceramic tile, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with [California Prop 65](https://www.ziatile.com/proposition-65-warnings). Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered edges or Schluter strips.

All matte Field Trip: Japan tiles must be sealed prior to grouting as a grout release to avoid grout haze and staining. See the [Field Trip: Japan Installation Guide](https://ziatile.com/pages/japanese-brick-ceramics-installation-guide) for the recommended sealer and step-by-step protocol. Reach out to our team at info@ziatile.com with any material or installation questions.

## How It's Made *[Locked]*

Field Trip: Japan is designed in Los Angeles and made in Japan, produced as a brick-format ceramic with three distinct surface textures. Crafted in central Japan, where the art of ceramics has been embraced and improved over thousands of years, the line was developed for wall applications where the tile's variation can carry the room.

Japanese clay is passed through a die-cut mold, then cut to create individual tiles. The tiles are formed, hand-glazed, and fired only once. Each finish (flat, etched, or furrowed) produces a distinct character and slip-resistance performance.

The brick proportion is unique to this line and gives installations a distinct visual rhythm. Color and texture variation across the line is intentional, so blend tiles thoroughly across boxes for an installation that reads as designed.
