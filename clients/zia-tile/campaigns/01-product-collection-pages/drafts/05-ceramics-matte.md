## About *[Locked]*

Each ceramic tile is high-fired clay with a glazed surface in matte finish. Architectural-grade and 1,000 years of tradition in the making, our ceramics are produced in a potters' village outside Nagoya, Japan. Blend tiles from all boxes and verify your layout before installation.

Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with ceramic tile, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with [California Prop 65](https://www.ziatile.com/proposition-65-warnings). Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered edges or Schluter strips.

All matte finish ceramic tiles must use a sealer as a grout release to avoid grout haze and staining. See the [Ceramics Installation Guide](https://ziatile.com/pages/ceramics-installation-guide) for the recommended sealer and step-by-step protocol. Reach out to our team at info@ziatile.com with any material or installation questions.

## How It's Made *[Locked]*

Ceramic tile is designed in Los Angeles and made in Japan, where the line is produced using high-temperature firing and precision glaze application. The result is a clean, precisely sized tile with consistent color across boxes, a different category of product from handmade clay tile.

Clay is molded into shape, dried, glazed in matte formulation, and fired at high temperature. The matte glaze produces both the surface character and the line's slip-resistance performance.

Each format is produced to consistent dimensions across the line, allowing for predictable installation and clean grout joints. Subtle variation in color and surface is normal and reflects the hand-finishing step in production.
