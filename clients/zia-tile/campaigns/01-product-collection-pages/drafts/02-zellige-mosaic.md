#### Order Details + Installation

We recommend a 15 to 20% overage on all zellige mosaic orders to properly curate your installation. For a more uniform appearance, we recommend 20 to 25%, which gives your installer the option to set aside any sheets with stronger variation.

Zellige installation is labor-intensive and is often closer to masonry than to standard tile-setting. Plan for installation to take 2 to 3 times longer than standard ceramic tile, accounting for sorting, arranging, and adjusting each sheet for size variation. Mesh-mounted sheets can be installed as set or removed from the mesh for a larger, more even grout joint.

## About *[Locked]*

Each handcrafted zellige mosaic is wholly unique, and this innate variation defines the line's charm. Mix all boxes in your shipment before beginning your installation, and arrange an ideal layout on a flat surface before setting any sheet to confirm the color balance and variation match your preference.

Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with zellige, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with [California Prop 65](https://www.ziatile.com/proposition-65-warnings). Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered edges or Schluter strips. Your installer should be familiar with cutting tile edges at a 45-degree angle.

For areas exposed to constant moisture, such as showers, pools, and spas, glazed tiles and grout lines should be sealed after grout is complete per the [Zellige Mosaics Installation Guide](https://ziatile.com/pages/zellige-mosaics-installation-guide). Reach out to our team at info@ziatile.com with any material or installation questions.

## How It's Made *[Locked]*

Zellige mosaic is handmade in Morocco using the same traditional techniques as field tile, refined by artisans whose families have practiced the craft for generations. Once fired, individual tiles are hand-placed on mesh sheets in solid layouts and intentional patterns.

The process begins with raw clay mined from local Moroccan hillsides. Artisans soak the clay in water and filter it for impurities, then hand-press the resulting paste into molds before laying the tiles to dry in the Moroccan sun. Each tile is flattened and shaped by hand before glazing.

Artisans hand-dip each tile in glaze and arrange them inside an earthen kiln, where uneven heat distribution creates the dynamic glazes and tonal variation that define traditional zellige. Each sheet is an expression of the artisan's work that day, beautifully imperfect and unique.
