#### Order Details + Installation

We recommend a 15 to 20% overage on all Cotto Allende orders to account for cuts and natural variation. Before installation, blend tiles from all boxes and verify your layout. Match grout color as closely as possible to tile color, since contrasting grout will accentuate crazing on the glazed surface.

## About *[Locked]*

Each Cotto Allende tile is handmade in San Miguel de Allende, Mexico, with the same clay base as standard cotto and a glazed surface in matte or glossy finish. Variation in color, shape, and thickness, alongside crazing on the glaze, are normal characteristics of handmade glazed clay tile.

Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with Cotto Allende, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with [California Prop 65](https://www.ziatile.com/proposition-65-warnings). Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered edges or Schluter strips.

Cotto Allende must be sealed on-site, with pre-grout sealing per the [Cotto Allende Installation Guide](https://ziatile.com/pages/cotto-allende-installation-guide). The sealer also acts as a grout release. Reach out to our team at info@ziatile.com with any material or installation questions.

## How It's Made *[Locked]*

Cotto Allende is handmade in San Miguel de Allende, Mexico, using the same clay base as standard cotto with a glaze applied before firing. Allende also means "beyond," a fitting description for a line that extends terra cotta into applications such as pools, spas, and steam showers, which aren't appropriate for unglazed terra cotta.

Natural clay is pressed into molds and sun-dried, then glazed in either matte or glossy finish before high-firing. The glaze develops fine hairline cracks during cooling (known as crazing), which is a normal characteristic of glazed clay tile and not a defect.

Color saturation is more controlled than unglazed cotto, while the underlying clay body still produces handmade variation across the line. Each colorway is specially designed with varying glossiness and speckling, showcasing the artistry in each piece.
