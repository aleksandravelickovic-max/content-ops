#### Order Details + Installation

We recommend a 15 to 20% overage on all marble orders to allow for cuts and natural veining variation. For a more uniform appearance, we recommend 20 to 25%, which gives your installer the option to set aside any pieces with stronger variation. Before installation, blend pieces from all boxes and lay out individual stones to confirm the variation matches your preference. Marble has a face and an underside, so install face side up.

Press tiles into place with the palm of your hand only; rubber mallets can create small cracks that grow over time. Use 1/8" tile spacers for the recommended grout joint. Subfloors must be fully cured (minimum 30 days).

## About *[Locked]*

Each marble tile is cut from solid stone blocks quarried across Italy and Spain, then honed by stone masons. Significant variation in color, tone, and veining is inherent to natural stone. Blend pieces from all boxes before installation and lay them out to confirm the composition matches your preference.

Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with marble, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with [California Prop 65](https://www.ziatile.com/proposition-65-warnings). Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered edges or Schluter strips.

Marble must be sealed on-site, with both pre-grout sealing and a top coat per the [Marble Installation Guide](https://ziatile.com/pages/marble-installation-guide). Different products are specified for interior and outdoor installations. Reach out to our team at info@ziatile.com with any material or installation questions.

## How It's Made *[Locked]*

Anchored in Carrara, our marble line is quarried across Italy and Spain, then cut into shape near Verona. Great design is forever, and marble is a prime example of the lasting power of the world's most iconic natural material.

Our LA-based design team curated the line across six varieties (Carrara, Nero Marquina, Grigio Carnico, Rosso Alicante, Giallo Reale, and Verde Alpi), each chosen for its veining patterns, mineral character, and place in European architectural history. Each block is cut into the format dimensions and honed to produce the matte surface that defines the line.

Veining and mineral variation are inherent to the stone, and no two pieces are identical. Marble develops natural patina with use, which is part of how the material ages.
