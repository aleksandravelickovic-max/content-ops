## About *[Locked]*

Glass Mosaics is Zia's recycled-glass mosaic line, designed to bring an old-world tile-setting tradition into a renewable-energy production method. The line was developed around seven intentional patterns drawn from artworks, fashion textiles, and locations like Los Angeles's own Union Station: Vaso, d'Orsay, Union, Ells, Cheque, Tesserae, and Murano.

Alongside the patterns, the line carries fifteen solid colorways, from quiet neutrals such as Paperwhite and Milk to saturated tones including Roma, Kumquat, Jade, and Jet Black. Together they read equally well in residential bathrooms, commercial pools, fountains, and feature walls, and patterns can be ordered in any of the solids so a single project can mix designed graphics with a coordinated solid field.

## Inherent Variation

- Color holds remarkably consistent across each colorway and pattern, with only minor shifts in shade and surface texture between individual tiles.
- The hand placement on mesh gives installations a craft-touched quality that machine-set glass tile cannot replicate, especially in the slight rhythm of grout lines and the subtle drift of a pattern across a wall.
- Expect minor variation in tile size, edge profile, and individual grout-joint width within each sheet, along with occasional recycled-content inclusions visible in the body of the glass.

## Preparation and Curation

Open every box on arrival and blend sheets across the order before laying anything on thinset. The colorways are uniform tile to tile, but patterns gain dimension when sheets from different boxes are interleaved across a single wall or floor.

We recommend a 15 to 20% overage on all glass mosaic orders to account for cuts, breakage in transit, and pattern alignment. For installations where a more uniform pattern read is the priority, plan for a 25% overage so the installer can set aside the small share of sheets that drift furthest from the average.

## Installation and Finishing

Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with glass mosaic, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with [California Prop 65](https://www.ziatile.com/proposition-65-warnings).

Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered edges or Schluter strips. Do not specify trim pieces; mitered edges hold the line of the pattern more cleanly.

Glass tile is naturally stain-resistant and does not require sealing. Optional grout sealing follows the grout manufacturer's instructions. For full step-by-step instructions, see the [Glass Mosaics Installation Guide](https://ziatile.com/pages/glass-mosaics-installation-guide). Reach out to our team at info@ziatile.com with any material or installation questions.

## Tile Usage *[Locked]*

Glass mosaic is suitable for interior and exterior installations, including freeze/thaw climates and continuous-water applications such as pools, spas, and fountains. The DCOF rating is 0.42; for wet floors and commercial wet rooms, increase grout joint size to a minimum of ⅛ inch and apply an anti-slip product after installation to raise traction further.

## How It's Made *[Locked]*

Production begins in northern Spain with 98% recycled glass. The glass is melted, individually formed in molds at the size of the finished tile, then fired in electric kilns powered by wind and solar energy. The pigment is integral to the glass at the melt stage, not applied as a surface coat, which is why color holds across the life of the installation.

Once the tiles cool, artisans hand-place each one on 12x12 inch mesh sheets, either as a uniform solid or in one of the line's designed patterns. The mesh is paper-backed for the solids and engineered for the patterns to hold the geometry through shipping and through the thinset cure.

After packing, sheets ship ready to install as a continuous surface. The combination of recycled feedstock, renewable-energy firing, and hand placement is what gives the line its register: machine-precise color and dimension at the individual tile, with the craft of an artisan visible at the level of the sheet.
