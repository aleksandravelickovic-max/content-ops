## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are green tiles suitable for kitchens and bathrooms?

Green tiles are suitable for both kitchens and bathrooms when the material is appropriate for the application. Zellige and ceramic are commonly used for backsplashes and shower walls. For floors, specify a matte or textured finish and hold a minimum ⅛" grout joint to support slip resistance. Seal zellige and cement tile every one to two years, and more often in shower, pool, and spa environments.

### How do you choose the right shade of green tile?

Choosing the right shade of green tile starts with the undertone. Some greens lean warm and earthy, while others read cooler or more blue. You can order samples and evaluate them in the actual installation space, since green shifts depending on natural and artificial light.

### Do green tiles vary in tone or shade?

Yes, green tiles vary in tone and shade, and the degree of variation depends on the material. Zellige shows the most movement, with visible shifts in tone along with chips, pits, and crazing. Stone and clay-based tiles also vary based on their composition and firing. Ceramic tends to read more consistently across the surface.

### Where does green tile perform best: backsplash, shower, or floor?

Green tile works across all three areas, but performance depends on the material. Backsplashes are the most flexible. Shower walls require materials suited for moisture, with sealing where required. On floors, hold a minimum ⅛" grout joint for slip resistance and apply an anti-slip treatment after installation where needed.

### How do you clean and maintain green tile?

You clean and maintain green tile with routine sweeping and pH-neutral products. Sweep or vacuum high-traffic areas before grit scratches the surface. Clean with a pH-neutral cleaner and avoid bleach, vinegar, and acidic products, which etch glazes and damage stone. Seal cement and zellige every one to two years, and more often in shower, pool, and spa environments. Matte green ceramic requires sealing with 511 Porous Plus as a grout release before grouting.
