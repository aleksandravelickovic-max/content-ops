### How do you clean cement tile?

Cleaning cement tile correctly starts with regular dry cleaning using a soft broom, dust mop, or vacuum to prevent buildup without affecting the surface. For wet cleaning, use a pH-neutral tile cleaner only. Avoid acidic or abrasive products, as these can stain or dull the surface. For deeper cleaning, a heavy-duty pH-neutral solution intended for natural stone – such as Miracle Heavy Duty Cleaner (Acid Substitute) – can be used safely on sealed surfaces.

Yes, cement tiles perform well on interior floors, or outdoor areas in non-freeze/thaw climates, when properly installed, and sealed, and maintained. ### How does cement tile compare to ceramic?

Cement tile and ceramic tile suit different design intentions and should be chosen based on the priorities of the project. Cement tile is thicker and mattestructurally denser, formed from cement, marble powder, and natural mineral pigments, then air-cured rather than being fired in a kiln. Ceramic tile is kiln-fired and engineered for uniformity and consistent color, suiting applications where a precise, repeatable surface is the goal.
