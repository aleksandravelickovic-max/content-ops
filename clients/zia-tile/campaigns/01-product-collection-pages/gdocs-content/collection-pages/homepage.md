### Browse Other Tile Collections: Sourced Across Continents & Shipped Worldwide

Tile collections are designed in Los Angeles and sourced from Morocco, Vietnam, Japan, Mexico, Italy, Spain, France, and more. Shop in-stock tile for quick delivery for residential and commercial projects.

### [Zellige](https://ziatile.com/collections/zellige)

Handmade in Morocco using a technique refined over eight centuries. Each tile is hand-pressed, hand-dipped in glaze, and fired in an earthen kiln, producing the dynamic tones and inherent variation that define the material.

### [Cotto](https://ziatile.com/collections/cotto)

Handmade terra cotta from San Miguel de Allende, Mexico. Each piece is pressed from natural clay, sun-dried, and high-fired, with an earthy, unglazed surface made to develop character with use.

### [Terrazzo](https://ziatile.com/collections/terrazzo)

Hand-poured and hydraulically pressed, built on a 500-year-old tradition rooted in Venetian craftsmanship. Each tile is ground and polished to reveal the crushed marble and stone aggregates set within.

### [Marble](https://ziatile.com/collections/marble-tile)

Quarried in Italy and Spain, finished by skilled stone masons. Each piece carries natural veining and mineral depth shaped over millions of years, available in honed and polished finishes.

### [Ceramics](https://ziatile.com/collections/ceramics)

Architectural-grade ceramic designed in Los Angeles and crafted in Nagoya, Japan. Available in matte, satin, and glossy finishes in flat and textured formats for floors, walls, and wet areas.

### [Cantera](https://ziatile.com/collections/cantera-tile)

Cut directly from volcanic rock quarried in Mexico, where the stone has been a staple in Mexican and Central American architecture for thousands of years.

### [Limestone](https://ziatile.com/collections/limestone-tile)

Quarried near Fez, Morocco, and Bordeaux, France, and finished by local stone masons. Each piece is cut from solid blocks and available in honed or bush-hammered finish, with consistent neutral tones and natural stone variation.

### [Cement](https://ziatile.com/collections/cement-tile)

Hand-poured in Vietnam using a technique rooted in French and Catalan craftsmanship from the 1850s. Each tile is pressed, soaked, and cured for seven days on drying racks, never fired.

## Every Collection Begins with a Maker

  - **Made by Hand:** Zellige is hand-pressed into molds and fired in earthen kilns. Cotto is pressed from natural clay and sun-dried before firing. Cement is hand-poured and cured on drying racks for seven days. Each method has been refined over generations and produces the inherent variation that defines the surface.
  - **Produced Where the Craft Lives**: Collections come directly from artisan producers around the globe, selected for production integrity and long-term performance rather than trend cycles.
  - **Supported Through the Install:** Zia works with designers, architects, and contractors at every stage of the project. Sample orders ship within two business days, and installation guidance covers wet areas, radiant heat, and large-format applications.
