# Tan Tile

Tan tile derives its color from the material itself, clay, stone, and volcanic rock, rather than a surface-applied print layer. Zia's tan tiles include handmade terra cotta, zellige, cantera, and limestone, among others. Each material produces its own range of tones, shaped by the raw material and the method of making. The result is a palette that suits kitchen floors, shower walls, backsplashes, and outdoor applications.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What's the best grout color for tan tiles?

The right grout color for tan tiles depends on how much you want it to show. Tan, cream, or warm beige grout blends into the surface and lets the tile read as one continuous field. Brown, charcoal, or deep grey makes the grid visible and graphic. Mid-tone grey lands between the two and holds up well in kitchens and entryways.

### How do you clean and maintain tan tile?

You clean and maintain tan tile with routine sweeping and pH-neutral products. Sweep or vacuum in high-traffic areas before grit scratches the surface. Clean with a pH-neutral cleaner, and avoid bleach, vinegar, and acidic products, which etch glazes and damage stone. Seal terra cotta tile, cantera tile, and limestone on the schedule specified for each material, and re-seal more often in wet areas. Cotto and limestone develop a natural patina with use, which is a characteristic of the surface and not a sign of wear.

### Is tan tile good for a kitchen floor?

Several tan tile materials work well on a kitchen floor. Zia's Cotto carries a DCOF of 0.98, one of the highest in the collection. Cantera ranges from 0.53 to 0.63, depending on the type, and limestone works for most residential kitchens. On any of these, hold a minimum ⅛" grout joint and seal every one to two years.

### Which tan tiles work in a shower or wet area?

Zellige, Cotto, Cotto Allende, cantera, and limestone are suited for shower walls and floors. On a shower floor, specify a matte or textured finish and hold a minimum ⅛" grout joint to improve slip resistance. An anti-slip treatment after installation adds further traction. Sealing is required in wet areas for all of these materials and should be repeated every one to two years.
