### Does ceramic tile need special care?

Yes, Zia's ceramic tiles require care to maintain their surface and finish over time. All matte ceramic tiles must be sealed before grouting using 511 Porous Plus Sealer as a grout release to avoid grout haze and staining. Using a pH-neutral cleaner and avoiding acidic or abrasive products will help preserve the material and prevent staining. Matte ceramics should be resealed at least every one to two years, or as often as needed, depending on traffic and wear. For areas exposed to moisture, more frequent resealing may be required. Always seal in accordance with the sealant manufacturer's instructions.
