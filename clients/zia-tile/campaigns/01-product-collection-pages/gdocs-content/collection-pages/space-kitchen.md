## Frequently Asked Questions

### What tiles are best for kitchen floors?

The kitchen floor is an opportunity to set the tone for the room with the right material. Suitable options include:

  - Cement: Available in solid colorways and a range of patterns, suited to mix-and-match installations.
  - Terrazzo: The speckled finish brings colorful notes to the space across a range of design directions, from mid-century modern to contemporary.
  - Cotto: Unglazed, clay-based terra cotta with natural warmth and character that develops with use.
  - Limestone: Understated and natural, bringing a soft, timeless look to the room.
  - Marble: Solid or patterned, marble is a considered choice for a kitchen that calls for elevated natural stone.

### What tiles make the best kitchen backsplashes?

Glazed surfaces like zellige, Cotto Allende, and satin or glossy ceramics are easiest to wipe clean. Any Zia tile can work for a backsplash with the right care, so let the design direction drive the choice. Options include:

  - Zellige: Handcrafted Moroccan tile that brings inherent variation in tone along with chips, pits, and crazing — each tile reads as its own surface.
  - Ceramics: A more uniform option, easy to work into solid or patterned layouts in matte, satin, or gloss finishes.
  - Marble: From veiny solids to bold patterns, smaller-format marble tiles bring natural stone character to a kitchen backsplash.
  - Patterned cement: Hand-poured geometric designs that let the room's personality come through.

### Should backsplash tiles be installed before or after kitchen cabinets?

Backsplash tile is best installed after the cabinets and countertops are in place. Working in this order lets the installer make precise cuts against finished edges, reduces waste, and ensures the tile sits flush against the surfaces it will meet. Installing tile before cabinets are set can lead to uneven reveals and unnecessary cuts that add material cost.

### Is marble a good choice for a kitchen?

Marble tile can be used in kitchens with an understanding of how it performs. Carrara, Grigio Carnico, Nero Marquina, and Giallo Reale are commonly used on backsplashes and islands. Marble will etch when exposed to acidic substances such as lemon, vinegar, or wine, and the change is permanent. Seal with a penetrating sealer before and after grouting. On floors, honed finishes show etching less visibly than polished surfaces.

### Is zellige a good choice for a kitchen?

Zellige is well-suited to kitchen backsplashes. Once sealed, the surface handles water and grease from daily use. Each tile shows variation in tone along with chips, pits, and crazing, which are inherent characteristics of the material. Seal according to Zia's installation guide after grouting for kitchen installations.

### How do kitchen tiles hold up over time?

Routine cleaning and sealing determine long-term performance. Wipe down backsplashes regularly to prevent grease from settling into grout joints. Use a pH-neutral cleaner and avoid acidic products such as bleach or vinegar, which can damage glaze and natural stone. Materials including cotto, cement, marble, and zellige require sealing, with resealing typically needed every one to two years depending on use.

### Can different tile materials be mixed in a kitchen?

Yes, different materials are often used across kitchen surfaces. A zellige backsplash paired with a marble or cement floor is a common approach, allowing each surface to perform according to its use. Establish one primary material, then select supporting materials that align in tone and scale. Reviewing samples together in the space helps confirm how they read under the room's lighting.

### How do you calculate how much tile is needed for a kitchen backsplash?

Measure the total square footage of the area to be tiled, including the space between the countertop and upper cabinets. Subtract openings such as windows. Add 15 to 20% overage to account for cuts and waste. For handmade materials like zellige and cement tile, 20% is recommended, with up to 25% for a more uniform layout.
