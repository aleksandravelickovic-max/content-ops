## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where do limestone tiles come from?

Zia's limestone tiles are quarried near Fez, Morocco, and Bordeaux, France, where distinct geological conditions produce stone with consistently stunningstable neutral tones and natural mineral variation. These regions are known for their rich heritage of stonecraft and architectural stone use, with limestone forming a foundational material in historic construction across Europe and North Africa.

Limestone has been used in architecture for thousands of years, from Egyptian pyramids to Mediterranean villas. This collection encourages a continuationcontinues that of this material lineage in contemporary applications where a natural stone tile surface is preferred for its durability and beautifulvisual restraint.

### How are Zia limestone tiles made?

Each stone tile is cut from solid blocks of natural limestone and finished by local stone masons. Our honed tiles are gently smoothed for a clean, matte surface, while our bush hammered finish uses a bush hammer to create a textured, rustic feel.

No two pieces are alike--each tile is shaped by time and technique. Variation in tone and texture, fossil marking, and surface movement is a defining characteristic of the natural material rather than a defect in production.

### Does limestone tile need special care?

Yes, limestone is a porous material and must be sealed before and after grouting to protect its surface against moisture and staining. Apply 511 Porous Plus as a penetrating sealer before grouting and apply Fila Matte Wax as a final protective coat for interior applications. For exterior applications, apply an additional coat of 511 Porous Plus instead of wax, as UV exposure causes wax to discolor over time. Reseal periodically, with extra attention to in high-traffic or moisture-prone areas. Use only pH-neutral cleaners to prevent surface etching or dulling. Over time, limestone develops a natural patina,. This is an expected characteristic of the material.

### What is honed limestone tile?

Honed limestone tile is a natural stone tile finished to a smooth, matte surface through a controlled grinding process and cut into pieces for use as tiles. The result is a flat, matte, non-reflective finish that reveals the stone's natural tonal variation and subtle fossil markings without a glazeossy surface. Honed limestone has a DCOF rating of 0.50, making it idealsuitable for floor applications. It is commonly specified for limestone flooring and bathroom walls where an understated, naturally consistent, matte surface is preferred.

### What is bush-hammered limestone?

Bush-hammered limestone is finished using a surface percussion process that creates a textured, tactile surface with increased traction compared to honed limestone. It has a DCOF rating of 0.56, making it well-suited for high-contact areas and exterior applications in non-freeze/thaw climates. The textured finish also makes it a practical choice for limestone bathroom floors where slip resistance is a priority.
