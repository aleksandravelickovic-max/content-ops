### How are Zia's terrazzo tiles made?

OurZia's terrazzo tiles are produced through a controlled molding and pressing process. Crushed natural marble and stone aggregates are set into a steel mold, liquid cement is poured over the aggregate layer, and the tile is hydraulically pressed into a powdered concrete base. Once setcured, the surface is ground to expose the aggregate distributionstructure and , then polished to a smooth matte finish with visible stone composition. ### How do you clean terrazzo tile?

Cleaning terrazzo tile correctly starts with regular sweeping or vacuuming to prevent surface abrasion from debris. Clean spills immediately to prevent absorption or staininginto the cement matrix. For routine cleaning, use warm water with a pH-neutral cleaner such as Miracle Sealants pH Neutral Tile and Stone Cleaner. For heavier buildup, use Miracle Sealants Heavy Duty Cleaner (Acid Substitute). Do not use bleach, vinegar, or acidic cleaners, as these dull the surface and weaken the sealer over time.

### How do I choose between 12x12 and 24x24 terrazzo?

Choosing between 12x12 and 24x24 terrazzo tile depends on the scale of the surface and the desired visual effect needed. The 12x12 format suits compact spaces, feature walls, and surfaces where the pattern compositions, such as Highball, Collins, and Bossa Nova, are the focal point of the design. The 24x24 format suits open floors and expansive walls where fewer grout lines produce a cleaner, less interrupted surface.

### What is the difference between terrazzo tile and marble tile?

Terrazzo tile and marble tile are both include stone materials-based surfaces, but differ greatly in composition, variation, and applicationsprocess (sounds a little strange) and appearance. Terrazzo is produced by embedding crushed marble and stone aggregates into a cement basematrix, which is then hydraulically pressed and ground to reveal the speckled aggregate-dotted surface. Marble is a naturally occurring stone, quarried then cut directly from solid blocks, with variation determined entirely by the stone's geological composition and natural veining.
