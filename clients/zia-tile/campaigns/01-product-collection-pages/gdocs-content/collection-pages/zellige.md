### How are Zia Moroccan zellige tiles made?

Zia's zellige tiles are handmade by Moroccan artisans with generations of experience, using raw materials sourced locally. The process begins with natural clay, which is mined, soaked, and filtered to remove impurities, at which point the clay becomes a soft, malleable substance that can be formed into our tiles. The refined clay is pressedformed into molds and left to dry in the sun. Once dried, each tile is shaped and finished by hand, creating variation in size and edges. Tiles are then hand-dipped in glaze and fired in an earthen kiln.

Heat distribution within the kiln varies, producing shifts in tone and surface across each tile. As a result, zellige tile develops inherent variation in color, shape, and thickness, along with surface characteristics such as chips, pits, and crazing. These are expected features of the material and contribute to the final installation. Each zellige tile is an expression of the artisan's work that day--beautifully imperfect and unique.
