# Zellige

## Hands. Heat. History.

Handcrafted in Fez, Morocco, Zia's zellige is made by artisans using traditional techniques refined over 800 years and passed down through generations. Each tile is formed from natural clay, hand-shaped, glazed, and kiln-fired, resulting in inherent variation in color, shape, and thickness. Chips, pits, and crazing across the surface reflect the material's deep history and hand-formed nature — these are expected characteristics of the material, not flaws.

Traditional zellige is installed in mosaics featuring decorative geometric and floral shapes, while single-tile installations work across diverse architectural styles. The surface is defined by dynamic glazes that respond to light, creating movement across the installation. Zia's collection holds over 200 tiles across colors, shapes, and mesh-backed mosaics, suited for kitchen backsplashes, bathroom walls, shower walls and floors, pools, spas, and fireplace surrounds.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where do zellige tiles come from?

Zia's zellige tile is a handcrafted Moroccan art form with more than 800 years of continuous production behind it. The collection is handmade in Morocco, where the material originated and has been refined through generations of skilled artisans. Historically installed in mosaics featuring decorative geometric and floral shapes within elaborately designed architectural spaces, this material remains central to Moroccan design. Zellige is not suitable for outdoor installation in freeze/thaw climates.

### How are Zia zellige tiles made?

Zia's zellige tiles are handmade by Moroccan artisans using raw materials sourced locally. The process begins with natural clay, which is mined, soaked, and filtered to remove impurities. The refined clay is formed into molds and left to dry in the sun. Once dried, each tile is shaped and finished by hand, creating variation in size and edges. Tiles are then hand-dipped in glaze and fired in an earthen kiln.

Heat distribution within the kiln varies, producing shifts in tone and surface across each tile. As a result, zellige develops inherent variation in color, shape, and thickness, along with surface characteristics including chips, pits, and crazing. These are expected features of the material and contribute to the final installation.

### Does zellige tile need special care?

All but one colorway in the zellige line is glazed. Both glazed and unglazed zellige require skilled installers and careful attention to sealing and grout selection, especially in moisture-exposed environments.

For unglazed natural zellige:

  - Seal before grouting with a penetrating sealer
  - Grout with Mapei Flexcolor CQ
  - After the grout cures, apply a matte protective wax in thin layers until the tiles are fully coated, then wipe any excess before it dries
  - Avoid over-sealing, as excessive application can dry unevenly and leave a film on the surface

For glazed zellige:

  - Use Mapei Ultracolor Plus FA grout
  - Apply a penetrating sealer after grouting when installing in showers, pools, or other wet areas

### How to clean zellige tile

Regular sweeping or vacuuming prevents surface abrasion; spills should be addressed promptly. Floors and walls can be wiped or mopped with warm water and a pH-neutral cleaner such as Miracle Sealants pH Neutral Tile and Stone Cleaner.

For deeper cleaning, use a non-acidic cleaner such as Miracle Sealants Heavy Duty Cleaner (Acid Substitute). Avoid bleach, vinegar, or acid-based products, as these dull the glazed surface over time.

### How do I install zellige tile?

Zellige tile requires a professional installer experienced with the material. Proper installation ensures variation is distributed correctly and performance requirements are met.

**Standard glazed zellige:**

  - Blend tiles from every box to evenly distribute natural variation
  - Soak all glazed zellige tiles in room-temperature water for 1–2 minutes
  - Use a high-quality medium-bed thinset
  - Press tiles into place by hand — do not use mallets
  - Let the thinset fully cure before grouting
  - Grout in small sections using Mapei Ultracolor Plus FA and wipe clean as work progresses
  - Apply a penetrating sealer as a topcoat when installing in wet areas

**Unglazed natural zellige:**

  - Blend tiles from every box to evenly distribute natural variation
  - Use a high-quality medium-bed thinset and wet the back of each tile before setting
  - Press tiles into place by hand — do not use a mallet
  - Let the thinset cure, then seal tiles before grouting with a penetrating sealer
  - Grout in small sections using Mapei Flexcolor CQ and wipe clean as work progresses
  - Finish with a protective topcoat sealer or wax, depending on the application

**Zellige mosaics:**

  - Blend tile sheets from multiple boxes before installation
  - Soak all glazed zellige tiles for 1–2 minutes — do not soak sheets that include Unglazed Natural tiles
  - Use a high-quality medium-bed thinset
  - Press tiles into place by hand
  - Let the thinset fully cure
  - If mosaics include Unglazed Natural zellige, seal the tiles before grouting with a penetrating sealer
  - Grout in small sections using Mapei Ultracolor Plus FA for glazed tiles and Mapei Flexcolor CQ for mosaics that include Unglazed Natural
  - For mosaics including Unglazed Natural zellige, finish with a matte protective wax as a topcoat
  - For glazed mosaic tiles, apply a penetrating sealer in wet areas or where additional protection is needed

### How does zellige tile hold up over time?

Zellige is a dense, kiln-fired clay tile that holds up well over time when properly installed and maintained. The chips, pits, and crazing that define the surface are structural characteristics of the handmade material, not vulnerabilities — they remain consistent over time. Sealing in wet areas and regular cleaning with pH-neutral products preserve the glazed surface. Zellige is not suitable for exterior installation in freeze/thaw climates.

### How much zellige tile is needed for a project?

Start with the total square footage of the surface. Measure the length and width of each surface area in feet and multiply for the area; for walls or multi-surface floors, add all sections together. Plan for a minimum of 15 to 20% overage to account for cuts and variation. For a more uniform look, plan for 25% across the full order. ### What is a zellige tile backsplash?

A zellige tile backsplash is a surface made from handmade Moroccan clay tiles, traditionally formed, glazed, and fired in small batches. Each tile is cut by hand, resulting in uneven edges, slight shifts in thickness, and surface variation that reflects light differently across the wall. This creates a backsplash with visible movement rather than a flat, uniform finish — which is central to the zellige aesthetic in residential and commercial interiors.

### Can zellige be used on floors?

Zia's zellige works on floors in select interior and exterior applications in non-freeze/thaw climates. It is commonly specified for bathrooms and feature floors where variation and texture are part of the design. For floor installations, a minimum 1/8" grout joint is recommended to support performance and slip resistance. Zellige is not suitable for exterior floors in freeze/thaw climates. The handmade surface and inherent variation are part of the finished floor's character and should be distributed evenly during installation.

### Can zellige tiles be mixed across colorways?

Zellige tiles can be mixed across colorways. Each tile is handmade using the same traditional process, so zellige from different colorways shares the same format dimensions, thickness range, and surface characteristics. Tile thickness does vary across sizes, so varying shapes should be accounted for when mixing. Blending colorways works particularly well in mosaic compositions where tonal variation across the field is part of the design intent.
