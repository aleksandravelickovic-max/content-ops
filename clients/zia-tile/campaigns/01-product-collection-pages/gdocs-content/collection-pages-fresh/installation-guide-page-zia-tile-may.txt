﻿Tile Installation Guides by Material
Zellige, natural stone, cement tile, and ceramic each have distinct installation requirements. Adhesive type, substrate preparation, sealing timing, and grout selection all differ by material. Matching the correct specification to the correct material is what allows an installation to perform as intended.
