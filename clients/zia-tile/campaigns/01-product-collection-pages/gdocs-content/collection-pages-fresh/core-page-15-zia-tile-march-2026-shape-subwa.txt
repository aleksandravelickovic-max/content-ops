Subway Tile
The subway tile takes its name from the glazed ceramic rectangles first installed in the New York City transit system. At Zia, the format is available in handcrafted and precision-manufacturepd options: zellige, Cotto, marble, ceramics, and more. The right choice depends on how much surface variation, texture, and character the installation calls for.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is a subway tile?
Subway tile is a rectangular tile format typically proportioned at a 1:2 or 1:3 ratio, named after the glazed white ceramic tiles installed in the New York City subway system. The format became a standard in residential kitchens and bathrooms through the twentieth century and now appears across ceramic, zellige, and natural stone in a wide range of colors, finishes, and sizes.
What is the difference between metro tile and subway tile?
Metro tile and subway tile refer to the same rectangular format under different names. Subway tile is the term used in North America, rooted in the New York City transit system. Metro tile is the term more common in the United Kingdom and Europe, referencing the same glazed rectangular format used in European metro stations of the same era.
How do you choose the right subway tile?
Material is the first decision. The format is consistent across ceramic, zellige, and stone, but each material has distinct maintenance requirements, approved applications, and visual character. Finish and grout color come next. Matte and handmade surfaces read warmer and less uniform. Glossy precision ceramics read cleaner and more graphic.
What are the best layout patterns for subway tile?
Layout choice depends on the spatial effect needed. A horizontal running bond is the most traditional setting and visually widens a surface. A vertical stack bond reads taller and more contemporary. A herringbone pattern adds geometry and works particularly well with smaller formats like 2x6 Bejmat zellige. For handmade materials like zellige, laying the full installation out dry before setting is essential to distribute variation evenly across the surface.
What is the best subway tile for a kitchen backsplash?
The right backsplash tile is one whose finish and maintenance requirements suit a surface exposed to heat, grease, and daily cleaning. Glazed ceramic and glazed zellige are both well-suited because the glaze provides a surface that cleans easily with a pH-neutral product. Field Trip: Japan's extruded ceramic formats offer a more textural surface in flat, furrowed, and etched finishes that add dimension without introducing the full variation of handmade zellige. The right choice depends on how much surface variation the design requires.
Is zellige subway tile suitable for outdoor use?
Zellige subway tile may be used on exterior walls and exterior floors in non-freeze/thaw climates. It is not suitable for exterior installations of any kind in freeze/thaw climates. For outdoor applications where a floor tile is needed, contact the Zia team at info@ziatile.com or 310-844-1170 to identify the right material.
How do you clean subway tile?
Cleaning depends on the material. For glazed surfaces, including ceramic and zellige subway tile, a soft cloth or sponge with warm water and a pH-neutral cleaner is sufficient for routine maintenance. Avoid acidic or abrasive products, including vinegar, bleach, and harsh scrubbing tools, as these damage the glaze over time. For grout lines, prompt attention to spills prevents staining, and resealing on a schedule appropriate to traffic and moisture exposure keeps the installation performing correctly.
How do you cut subway tile?
All cuts must be made with a water-cooled tile saw fitted with a diamond edge blade. This applies across all subway tile materials in the collection, including zellige, ceramic, and natural stone. If extensive cutting is required, work outdoors or in a well-ventilated area in accordance with California Prop 65 guidelines. For installation-specific guidance, contact the Zia team at info@ziatile.com or 310-844-1170 with any questions. 
Can subway tile be installed on a shower floor?
Yes. Zellige, ceramic, and limestone are all approved for shower floor installations in their subway formats. Sealing is required for zellige and natural stone in this application. To address slip resistance, increase grout joint size to a minimum of 1/8" and apply an anti-slip treatment after installation. Bring drywall flush with the finished tile surface where applicable, and finish exposed edges with mitered edges or Schluter strips. For material-specific guidance, contact the Zia team at info@ziatile.com or 310-844-1170 with any questions before work begins.
What grout joint size is recommended for subway tile?
Grout joint size for a subway tile backsplash or wall installation depends on the material. For ceramic, a consistent open joint of 3/16" is standard. For zellige, the traditional method uses no standard joint — tiles are laid edge to edge or with a 1/16" spacing if a specific grout line is preferred. For floor installations in wet or commercial areas across any subway tile material, the grout joint size should be increased to a minimum of 1/8" to address slip resistance requirements.
