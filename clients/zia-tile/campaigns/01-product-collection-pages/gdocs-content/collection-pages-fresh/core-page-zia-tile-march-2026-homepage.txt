Browse Other Tile Collections[a][b]
Our tile collections are designed in Los Angeles and produced in Morocco, Vietnam, Japan, Mexico, Italy, Spain, and France, and more. Shop our in-stock tile for quick delivery for residential and commercial projects.
Zellige
Handmade in Morocco using a technique refined over eight centuries. Each tile is hand-pressed, hand-dipped in glaze, and fired in an earthen kiln, producing the dynamic tones and inherent variation that define the material.
Cotto
Handmade terra cotta from San Miguel de Allende, Mexico. Each piece is pressed from natural clay, sun-dried, and high-fired, with an earthy, unglazed surface made to develop character with use.
Terrazzo
Hand-poured and hydraulically pressed, built on a 500-year-old tradition rooted in Venetian craftsmanship. Each tile is ground and polished to reveal the crushed aggregates set within.
Marble
Quarried in Italy and Spain, finished by skilled stone masons. Each honed piece carries natural veining and mineral depth shaped over millions of years.
Ceramics
Architectural-grade ceramic designed in Los Angeles and crafted in Nagoya, Japan. Matte, satin, and glossy tiles in flat and textured finishes for floors, walls, and wet areas.
Cantera
Cut directly from volcanic rock, this natural stone has been a staple in Mexican and South American architecture for thousands of years.
Limestone
Quarried near Fez, Morocco, and Bordeaux, France, and finished by local stone masons. Each honed stone piece is cut from solid blocks, with consistent neutral tones and natural variation.
Cement
Hand-poured in Vietnam using a technique rooted in French and Catalan craftsmanship from the 1850s. Each tile is pressed, soaked, and cured for seven days on drying racks, never fired.
