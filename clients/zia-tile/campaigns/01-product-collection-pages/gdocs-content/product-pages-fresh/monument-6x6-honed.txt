We recommend 15 to 20% overage to account for cuts and to give your installer the selection needed to curate the layout. Before installation, lay out all pieces and use a heavy spray to remove production dust; allow them to dry thoroughly before setting. Blend pieces from all boxes to disperse variation evenly. Limestone has a face and an underside (they are not reversible), so install the face side up.
Limestone requires a two-stage sealing sequence: a pre-grout penetrating sealer, and a post-grout top coat (a matte wax for interior installations, or a second pass of the pre-grout sealer for exterior, since UV exposure rules out wax outside). Use 1/8" tile spacers for the recommended grout joint. For floor installations, subfloors must be fully cured (minimum 30 days) to prevent efflorescence from depositing on the stone surface.

About 
Zia's limestone is sourced from two regions: Honed Limestone quarried near Fez in three colorways (Buff, Monument, and Basilica) across 6x6, 4x12, 12x12, and 12x24 formats; and French Limestone quarried in Bordeaux in 12-inch and 20-inch varying-length tiles plus French Cobblestone. Basilica is also offered in a single bush-hammered 4x12 variant. Variation in color, tone, and natural inclusions across pieces is inherent to the material; lay out the stone on a flat surface and confirm the color balance reads the way you want before setting any piece.
Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with limestone, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with California Prop 65. Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered edges or Schluter strips. The full installation procedure is in the Limestone Installation Guide. For any material or installation questions, reach out to our team at info@ziatile.com.

How It's Made
Limestone is quarried from solid blocks near Fez, Morocco, and around Bordeaux, France, in workshops where the material has been cut and honed for centuries. Each block is sized down to the format dimensions and finished by stone masons at the source.
The honed finish is achieved by gently smoothing the surface to a clean, soft matte. The bush-hammered finish is achieved by striking the face with a textured hammer head, which uniformly pits the surface for a more rustic, aged character. Both finishes preserve the natural mineral signature of the stone.
Limestone is a living material. Subtle tonal variation across pieces is inherent to natural stone, and no two tiles are identical. The soft patina that develops over years of use is a defining feature of the material and the reason limestone has carried so many of the floors that survive from antiquity into the present.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is honed limestone?
Honed limestone is a natural stone finished through controlled mechanical grinding to produce a flat, matte surface. In Monument 6x6, the honing process preserves the stone's natural mineral variation and subtle fossilization patterns while creating a consistent low-sheen plane suited to floors and walls. 
How do I care for honed limestone?
Seal the stone before and after grouting to reduce absorption and protect against staining. Ongoing maintenance requires pH-neutral stone cleaners and regular dry removal of dust and grit to prevent surface abrasion. Reseal periodically based on traffic and exposure, particularly for floors in high-use areas.
What kind of variation should I expect across Monument 6x6 tiles?
Monument 6x6 carries natural stone variation in tone, veining, and surface character. The honing process reveals these characteristics rather than concealing them. Subtle fossil impressions and mineral inclusions are part of the stone's geological history. Color reads from lighter grey to slightly deeper grey depending on the stone's mineral composition. 
Can Monument 6x6 be used outdoors?
Yes. Monument 6x6 can be used on exterior walls in non-freeze/thaw climates. It is not suitable for exterior floors or freeze/thaw climates. For outdoor applications, seal with 511 Porous Plus only — do not use Fila Matte Finish Protective Wax outdoors, as UV exposure causes discoloration. Contact info@ziatile.com or 310-844-1170 to discuss your specific location and climate before ordering.
What overage should I order for Monument 6x6?
For a standard layout, order 15-20% overage to account for curation and cuts. The consistent sizing of limestone means less overage is typically needed compared to handmade tile.
How should I prepare Monument 6x6 tiles before installation?
Before you lay out the installation, open all boxes and mix the tiles together. This distributes the tonal variation across the full field rather than concentrating lighter or darker greys in one area. Arrange them by thickness so you have a level plane to work with. Set aside any pieces you'll need for cuts or detail work. This curation step determines whether the grey reads as subtle movement or as tonal clustering.
