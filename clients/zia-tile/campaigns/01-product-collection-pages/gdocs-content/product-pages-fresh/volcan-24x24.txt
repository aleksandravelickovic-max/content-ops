Mesoamerican civilizations were among its earliest builders, drawn to an architectural stone soft enough to carve yet strong enough to stand for centuries. From ancient Aztec structures to the cathedrals of San Miguel de Allende and landmarks throughout Mexico City, this rock can be found in the buildings that matter most. About
Cantera is the volcanic stone that built colonial Mexico, used continuously for centuries in courtyards, fountains, and the carved facades of cathedrals and haciendas. Zia's Cantera line carries that material into a residential and commercial collection sized for both intimate rooms and broad public installations.


The line is offered in eight earthy colorways, each carrying its own mineral signature: Mesa, Caldera, Acacia, Sierra, Yuma, Sedona, Badlands, and Volcan. Formats range from 6x6 and 4x12 mosaics to 12x12, 12x24, and 24x24 floor tiles, so a single Cantera scheme can run from a textured shower wall in a primary bathroom to a hotel courtyard or restaurant patio in the same colorway.


Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with Cantera, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with California Prop 65. Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered edges or Schluter strips.

How It's Made
Cantera begins as a porous volcanic rock, formed over millions of years from cooled lava and ash. Solid blocks are pulled from quarries in Mexico, then sized down to the format dimensions of the line: 6x6 and 4x12 mosaics, 12x12 and 12x24 planks, and 24x24 large-format floor tiles.


Each piece is honed and finished by hand at the source. The finishing approach intentionally preserves the visible aggregate, mineral inclusions, and oxidation markings of the volcanic chemistry, which is what gives each colorway its individual mineral signature rather than a surface treatment that would mask it.


Once finished, the tiles are graded and packed for shipping. After installation, the stone continues to develop. Cantera takes on a soft patina with use, similar to marble or limestone, and the sealing protocol on the install guide protects the surface while still allowing the material to age into the room around it.

Frequently Asked Questions
What color is Volcan 24x24 Cantera?
Volcan Cantera is a warm brown volcanic stone with tonal variation inherent to the material. At 24x24 inches, each tile presents a wider cross-section of the volcanic rock's mineral composition than smaller formats, so the aggregate distribution, mineral inclusions, and oxidation markings within each piece are more visible across the surface. Blending from all boxes before the dry layout distributes that variation evenly and prevents tonal clustering across the floor.
Does Volcan 24x24 require sealing?
Yes. Cantera tile requires sealing before and after grouting. Apply 511 Porous Plus in thin layers until the stone is fully saturated before grouting, wiping off any excess before it dries. The sealer must fully cure before grouting begins. For interior installations, apply Fila Matte Finish Protective Wax as the top coat after the grout has cured. For outdoor installations, apply a second coat of 511 Porous Plus in place of wax, as UV exposure causes wax to discolor over time. Reseal periodically based on traffic and wear. See the Cantera Installation Guide for the full sequence.
Can Volcan 24x24 be used in a courtyard?
Yes, in non-freeze/thaw climates only. The porous volcanic stone body absorbs moisture, and freeze/thaw cycling causes cracking and surface damage. Volcan 24x24 is not suitable for any outdoor installation where the surface may get snow or ice. For exterior applications, seal with 511 Porous Plus before grouting and apply a second coat of 511 Porous Plus as the top coat after the grout has cured. Do not use Fila Matte Wax outdoors. See the Cantera Installation Guide for the outdoor sealing sequence.
How do I install 24x24 Cantera tiles correctly?
Large-format Cantera requires a fully prepared, flat substrate. Any variation in the substrate surface becomes more visible at this scale than it would with smaller formats. Blend tiles from all boxes during the dry layout stage before any adhesive is applied, and mark perimeter cut positions during the dry stage, as large-format cuts require planning. See the Cantera Installation Guide for the full installation sequence.
Can Volcan 24x24 be used alongside smaller Volcan formats?
Yes. Volcan 24x24 can be mixed with smaller Volcan formats, including the 12x12 and 12x24, in the same installation. All Cantera formats share the same volcanic rock source and consistent material character, so tonal range and surface variation are continuous across the line. Using the 24x24 in a main floor field with 12x12 or 12x24 in a border or transition zone is a common approach for large residential and commercial projects. Blend tiles from all boxes across all formats during the dry layout, and establish format transitions before setting begins.
