We recommend a 15-20% overage to best curate an Unglazed Natural zellige installation, allowing for sufficient tiles within the desired range. About 
Unglazed Natural zellige is the raw form of traditional Moroccan zellige. The glaze that defines our other zellige colorways is omitted, leaving the matte clay face exposed and letting the natural tone of the local clay carry the tile. Mix all boxes in your shipment before beginning your installation, and lay out an ideal arrangement on a flat surface before setting any tile to confirm the color balance reads the way you want.
Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with zellige, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with California Prop 65. Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered edges or Schluter strips. Traditional trim and edge pieces do not exist for this material.
This colorway requires a sealing sequence distinct from glazed zellige: seal before grouting and apply a top coat after, both per the Zellige Installation Guide. Always seal unglazed zellige according to our installation instructions, whether in dry areas or areas exposed to moisture such as pools, spas, and showers. Stains on the raw clay surface can become permanent if grouting or use begins before sealing is complete. For any material or installation questions, reach our team at info@ziatile.com.

How It's Made
Unglazed Natural zellige is hand-formed in Morocco by the same artisans who shape our glazed zellige, using techniques refined and passed down through generations. The glaze step is omitted, which preserves the raw clay face that gives this tile its matte color and natural surface tone.
The craft begins with clay pulled from local Moroccan hillsides. Artisans soak the raw clay in water and filter it for impurities, then hand-press the paste into wooden molds before laying the tiles to dry under the Moroccan sun. Each tile is hand-flattened and shaped, then dried again before firing.
Tiles fire in earthen kilns without glaze, and the uneven heat of the kiln produces the matte clay surface and tile-to-tile color shifts that read as the signature of unglazed zellige. As the surface meets daily use, it deepens into a patina the way unsealed limestone or terra cotta does, recording wear instead of resisting it.

Frequently Asked Questions 
What color is Unglazed Natural zellige?
Unglazed Natural zellige is an earthy neutral tone determined by the mineral composition of the clay body and kiln conditions, with natural variation from tile to tile. The overall read across an installation is a matte, grounded surface with tonal movement built into the clay itself.
How is Unglazed Natural different from glazed zellige?
Unglazed Natural zellige is fired without any glaze, which means the final surface is the raw clay body rather than a reflective, color-saturated layer. Glazed zellige derives its color from the glaze applied before firing; Unglazed Natural derives its color from the mineral composition of the clay itself. The result is a matte, earthen surface rather than a reflective one. Unglazed Natural also requires a different sealing approach and must not be pre-soaked before installation. Soaking raw clay causes efflorescence that is difficult to remove. Clean dust from the surface with a damp cloth instead.
Does Unglazed Natural zellige require sealing?
Yes. Sealing is required for all Unglazed Natural zellige applications, including dry interior walls. Apply 511 Porous Plus before grouting to saturate the raw clay surface and protect it during installation. For interior applications, apply Fila Matte Wax as a protective top coat after grouting. 
For exterior applications in non-freeze/thaw climates, apply a second coat of 511 Porous Plus in place of wax. UV exposure causes wax to discolor outdoors. Do not use wax on exterior installations.
Can Unglazed Natural zellige be used in a shower?
Yes. Unglazed Natural zellige can be used on shower walls and shower floors. The raw clay surface tests at a DCOF of 0.84, well above the 0.42 threshold for level interior wet floors. Sealing is required before and after grouting for all shower applications. For shower floor installations, slip resistance can be increased by sizing grout joints to a minimum of 1/8 inch and applying an anti-slip treatment after installation. Refer to the Zellige Installation Guide for complete sealing instructions specific to unglazed zellige.
Can Unglazed Natural zellige be used outdoors?
Yes, in non-freeze/thaw climates only. Unglazed Natural zellige can be used on exterior floors and walls in climates that do not experience freeze/thaw conditions. It is not suitable for any exterior installation in climates that get snow or ice, as freeze/thaw cycling will damage the unglazed clay body. For exterior installations, apply 511 Porous Plus Penetrating Sealer before grouting, then apply a second coat of 511 Porous Plus as the top coat after grouting is complete. Do not use wax on exterior installations, as UV exposure causes wax to discolor.
Can Unglazed Natural be mixed with glazed zellige colorways?
Yes. Unglazed Natural can be mixed with glazed zellige colorways in the same installation. The matte, raw clay surface reads as a quiet neutral counterpoint to the reflective glaze of adjacent colorways, and the contrast between the two surfaces (one matte, one glazed) can be a deliberate compositional element. Each tile is produced using the same hand-formed method, so tiles share a consistent size range and edge character across the two types. Plan any mixed installation during the dry layout stage before setting begins.
