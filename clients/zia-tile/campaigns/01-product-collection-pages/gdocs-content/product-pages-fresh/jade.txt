Zellige tile installation is labor-intensive, often likened more to masonry than traditional tiling due to the irregular nature of the handmade material. About
No two zellige tiles are the same. Pieces vary in size, surface, and glaze across each box, with hairline crazing and chamfered edges that come from how the material is hand-formed rather than flaws to correct.


Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with zellige, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with California Prop 65. Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered cuts or Schluter strips. Traditional trim and edge pieces do not exist for zellige.


Seal zellige according to our installation instructions, whether the application is dry or exposed to moisture (pools, spas, showers). Reach out to our team at info@ziatile.com with any material or installation questions.

How It's Made
Zellige is hand-formed in Morocco, where the technique has been refined for more than 800 years and passed across generations of artisans.


The process begins with raw clay, which is mined from local hillsides, soaked, filtered for impurities, and hand-pressed into molds. The tiles are laid to dry in the Moroccan sun, flattened and shaped by hand, then dried further before glazing.


Each tile is hand-dipped in glaze and arranged inside an earthen kiln, where uneven heat creates the tonal variation that defines zellige. No two pieces leave the kiln the same and the handmade nature and variation are hallmarks of this most special material. 


To prepare for installation, open and mix all boxes before setting them to achieve an ideal layout. The tiles will carry the marks of the day they were fired.

Frequently Asked Questions
What color is Jade zellige?
Jade is a zellige colorway with a glaze that shifts in tone depending on light and angle. The tonal range comes from how the glaze is applied by hand and fired in an earthen kiln, where uneven heat produces variation across the batch. No two tiles carry exactly the same tone, and the relationship between lighter and darker pieces shifts across the full installation.
Is Jade zellige appropriate for floor installations?
Yes, with appropriate precautions. Jade is suited to interior floor applications, though glazed zellige has a DCOF rating of 0.16, below the 0.42 wet-floor slip-resistance threshold for level interior floors.


For floor installations, widen grout joints to a minimum of 1/8" and apply an anti-slip treatment after installation is complete. Sealing is required for all floor applications; refer to the Zellige Installation Guide for full instructions.
Can Jade zellige be used in a pool or spa?
Yes. Zellige is approved for pool waterline, spa interior, and fountain applications in both residential and commercial settings. Sealing is required for all wet-area installations; apply 511 Porous Plus penetrating sealer to all tiles and grout lines exposed to continuous moisture after grouting is complete. Contact info@ziatile.com with project-specific questions.
Is Jade zellige rated for outdoor installations?
Jade is suited to exterior wall installations and exterior floor installations in non-freeze/thaw climates only. Zellige is not rated for exterior floors in freeze/thaw regions. For outdoor use in mild climates, apply a penetrating sealer in accordance with the installation guide and re-seal as needed depending on exposure. Full installation guidance is in the Zellige Installation Guide.
What sealing protocol does Jade zellige require?
Zellige requires sealing for all applications. For pools, spas, showers, and any surface exposed to continuous moisture, apply 511 Porous Plus penetrating sealer to all tiles and grout lines after grouting is complete. Full sealing instructions are in the Zellige Installation Guide.
How does the 4x4 square format suit different installation patterns?
The 4x4 square produces an all-directional field with no dominant linear axis, suited to backsplash fields, bathroom walls, and pool interiors where a consistent field without directional emphasis is the intended result. The square format reads differently from the rectangular bejmat format: the field carries the tonal variation of the glaze without the linear movement that a longer format introduces.
Does the Jade colorway vary between orders?
Yes. The Jade colorway can vary between production runs. Each batch of zellige is produced by hand, and the clay, glaze, and kiln conditions shift from one run to the next. Tiles from separate orders may differ in tone and surface character. Where the project requires tiles from multiple orders, ordering the full quantity at once gives the most consistent result across the installation.
