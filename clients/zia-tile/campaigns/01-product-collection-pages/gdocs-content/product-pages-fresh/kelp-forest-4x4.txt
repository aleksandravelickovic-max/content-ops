About
Zia's ceramics are the design counterpoint to our handmade zellige. They were developed with a Japanese potters' village outside Nagoya, where high-firing extends back more than 1,000 years, to produce a tile precise enough for tight grout joints and clean layouts.


The collection spans matte, satin, and glossy finishes plus the textured Field Trip: Japan series, in three core formats: 2x8 and 2x4 rectangles and 4x4 squares. Color and tone vary slightly across high-fired ceramics, less than handmade zellige, but enough that mixing boxes before the set still matters.


Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with ceramic, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with California Prop 65. Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered edges or Schluter strips.


Sealing requirements vary by finish: matte ceramics must be sealed before grouting as a grout release; satin and glossy finishes do not require pre-grout sealing. For any material or installation questions, reach out to our team at info@ziatile.com.

How It's Made
Ceramic tile is designed in Los Angeles and made in Japan, where the line is produced using high-temperature firing and precision glaze application. The result is a clean, precisely sized tile with consistent color across boxes, a different product category from handmade clay tile.


Clay is molded into shape, dried, glazed, and fired at high temperature. The matte, satin, and gloss finishes are achieved through different glaze formulations, each producing distinct surface character and slip-resistance performance.


Each format is produced to consistent dimensions across the line, which allows for predictable installation and clean grout lines. Subtle variation in color and surface is normal and reflects the hand-finishing step in production.

Frequently Asked Questions
What does the Kelp Forest colorway look like?
Kelp Forest is an olive green glossy ceramic with a reflective surface that returns light and shifts slightly in tone depending on the angle and the intensity of ambient light. The glossy finish introduces a degree of surface depth that reads clearly alongside natural stone, metal hardware, and both warm and cool cabinetry. Across a full field installation, the colorway reads as a grounded, mid-toned green.
Does Kelp Forest 4x4 glossy ceramic require sealing?
No. Glossy and satin ceramics do not require pre-grout sealing. The glaze on Kelp Forest is non-porous, so a grout-release sealer is not needed before grouting. Matte ceramics in the collection do require a penetrating sealer applied before grouting as a grout release; glossy finishes do not. Full installation guidance is in the Ceramic Installation Guide.
Can Kelp Forest 4x4 ceramic be used in a shower?
Yes. Kelp Forest 4x4 is suited to shower wall and floor installations. Glossy ceramics typically test around 0.30 DCOF, below the 0.42 wet-floor slip-resistance threshold for level interior floors. For shower floor use, widen grout joints to a minimum of 3/16" and apply an anti-slip treatment after installation is complete. 


For commercial or high-traffic wet-floor applications, consult with your licensed installer before proceeding. Contact info@ziatile.com or 310-844-1170 with project-specific questions.
Can the Kelp Forest 4x4 be used outdoors?
No. Zia's ceramic collection is not rated for exterior floor applications, in freeze/thaw or non-freeze/thaw climates. The glaze formulation and tile body are designed for interior floor and wall use, plus shower and pool applications. Kelp Forest is suited to exterior wall installations in non-freeze/thaw climates; for any outdoor wall use, confirm exposure conditions with the installer before setting.
What grout joint is recommended for the 4x4 format?
Plan for 3/16" grout joints as the standard for Kelp Forest 4x4, with a minimum of 1/8" where a tighter layout is intended. The uniform dimensions of high-fired ceramic allow for predictable spacing across the installation, and the 4x4 format reads cleanly with either a tight minimal joint or the wider 3/16" standard, depending on the design intent. Mixing box lots before setting is recommended, as subtle tone variation across high-fired ceramics is normal.
How does the glossy finish compare to matte and satin ceramics in the Zia collection?
Matte ceramics absorb light and produce a flat, non-reflective surface; they also require pre-grout sealing as a grout release. Satin ceramics carry a low-sheen surface between matte and gloss, returning more light than matte without the full reflectivity of a gloss finish. 


Glossy ceramics produce the highest surface sheen and are the most reflective of the three. Kelp Forest's glossy finish returns the most light of any finish in the ceramic line, and glossy ceramics typically test around 0.30 DCOF, below the 0.42 wet-floor threshold, making anti-slip treatment a requirement for shower floor and wet-floor applications.
Can the Kelp Forest 4x4 be mixed with other ceramic formats or colorways?
Yes. Kelp Forest 4x4 can be combined with other ceramic formats (including the 2x8 and 2x4 rectangles) and other colorways across the ceramic collection. Each format is produced to consistent dimensions and shares the same material character across the line. 


The olive green of Kelp Forest reads most clearly in contrast with lighter neutrals and natural stone surfaces, where the depth of the glaze carries the design intent without requiring additional accent materials.
