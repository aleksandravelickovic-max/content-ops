About
Zia's ceramics are the design counterpoint to our handmade zellige. They were developed with a Japanese potters' village outside Nagoya, where high-firing extends back more than 1,000 years, to produce a tile precise enough for tight grout joints and clean layouts. 
The collection spans matte, satin, and glossy finishes plus the textured Field Trip: Japan series, in three core formats: 2x8 and 2x4 rectangles and 4x4 squares. Color and tone vary slightly across high-fired ceramics — less than handmade zellige, but enough that mixing boxes before the set still matters. 
Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with ceramic, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with California Prop 65. Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered edges or Schluter strips.
Sealing requirements vary by finish: matte ceramics must be sealed before grouting as a grout release; satin and glossy finishes do not require pre-grout sealing. For any material or installation questions, reach out to our team at info@ziatile.com.

How It's Made 
Ceramic tile is designed in Los Angeles and made in Japan, where the line is produced using high-temperature firing and precision glaze application. The result is a clean, precisely sized tile with consistent color across boxes, a different product category from handmade clay tile.
Clay is molded into shape, dried, glazed, and fired at high temperature. The matte, satin, and gloss finishes are achieved through different glaze formulations, each producing distinct surface character and slip-resistance performance.
Each format is produced to consistent dimensions across the line, which allows for predictable installation and clean grout lines. Subtle variation in color and surface is normal and reflects the hand-finishing step in production.

Frequently Asked Questions 
What color is Frogtown Flat?
Frogtown is a muted green ceramic tile with a flat surface. The color comes from the clay body and high-fire kiln process, and each tile holds a slightly different position within the green's tonal range as a result of its mineral composition and position in the kiln. Those differences distribute evenly across the field once tiles from all boxes are blended before the dry layout, producing a surface with natural tonal movement built into the material.
Does Frogtown Flat require sealing before grouting?
Yes. Frogtown Flat requires a penetrating sealer applied as a grout release before grouting. Apply 511 Porous Plus in a thin, even layer after laying the tile and allow it to fully cure before grouting begins. Proceeding before the sealer has cured will result in grout absorbing into the surface and staining it. See the Japanese Brick Ceramics Installation Guide for the full sealing and installation sequence.
Can Frogtown Flat be used on a shower floor?
Yes, with slip resistance measures in place. Frogtown Flat has a DCOF of 0.40, just below the 0.42 wet-floor threshold for level interior floors. For shower floor installations, size grout joints to a minimum of 1/8 inch and apply an anti-slip treatment after installation is complete. Seal before and after grouting for all wet-area applications. 
Can Frogtown Flat be installed on an exterior floor?
No. Frogtown Flat is not suitable for exterior floor installations in either freeze/thaw or non-freeze/thaw climates. It can be used on exterior walls in non-freeze/thaw climates. For material and application questions, contact us at info@ziatile.com or 310-844-1170.
What is the difference between the Flat, Etched, and Furrowed finishes in Field Trip: Japan?
The three Field Trip: Japan finishes produce different surface characters and slip-resistance performance. Flat produces a smooth face. Etched adds linear scoring across the tile surface. Furrowed produces deep ridges that run the length of the tile. Slip resistance differs across the three: Flat has a DCOF of 0.40, Etched 0.45, and Furrowed 0.37. For Etched and Furrowed installations, use a grout bag to prevent grout from settling into the textured surface. The Flat finish does not require this step. For all three finishes in wet-area applications, size grout joints to a minimum of 1/8 inch and apply an anti-slip treatment after installation.
Can Frogtown Flat be mixed with other Field Trip: Japan colorways or finishes?
Yes. Frogtown Flat can be mixed with other colorways and finishes within the Field Trip: Japan series. All Field Trip: Japan tiles share the same high-fired ceramic body and consistent format dimensions, so installation requirements are compatible across the line. When mixing finishes within a single installation, establish where each finish transitions and confirm grout joint alignment during the dry layout stage before adhesive is applied. Note that Etched and Furrowed finishes require a grout bag during installation to prevent grout from settling into the textured surface. For complete installation requirements, see the Japanese Brick Ceramics Installation Guide.
