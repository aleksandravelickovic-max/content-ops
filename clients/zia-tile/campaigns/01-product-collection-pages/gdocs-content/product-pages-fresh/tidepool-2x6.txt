About
No two zellige tiles are the same. Pieces vary in size, surface, and glaze across each box, with hairline crazing and chamfered edges that come from how the material is hand-formed rather than flaws to correct.


Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with zellige, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with California Prop 65. Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered cuts or Schluter strips. Traditional trim and edge pieces do not exist for zellige.


Seal zellige according to our installation instructions, whether the application is dry or exposed to moisture (pools, spas, showers). Reach out to our team at info@ziatile.com with any material or installation questions.

How It's Made
Zellige is hand-formed in Morocco, where the technique has been refined for more than 800 years and passed across generations of artisans.


The process begins with raw clay, which is mined from local hillsides, soaked, filtered for impurities, and hand-pressed into molds. The tiles are laid to dry in the Moroccan sun, flattened and shaped by hand, then dried further before glazing.


Each tile is hand-dipped in glaze and arranged inside an earthen kiln, where uneven heat creates the tonal variation that defines zellige. No two pieces leave the kiln the same, and the handmade nature and variation are hallmarks of this material. To prepare for installation, open and mix all boxes before setting them to achieve an ideal layout. The tiles will carry the marks of the day they were fired.

Frequently Asked Questions
What layout patterns work with the Tidepool 2x6 Bejmat format?
Running bond with offset horizontal joints distributes the tonal variation across the surface evenly. Herringbone draws more attention to the elongated format and creates visual energy that works well with Tidepool's shifting blue-green glaze. Vertical stack creates a more ordered surface where each tile reads as a narrow vertical element, which can emphasize the variation between individual tiles. Plan the layout during the dry layout stage to understand how the pattern and the glaze interact before setting.
Can Tidepool 2x6 zellige be used in a shower?
Yes. Tidepool 2x6 can be used on shower walls, shower floors, and steam showers. Sealing is required for all shower applications. For shower floor installations, slip resistance can be increased by sizing grout joints to a minimum of ⅛" and applying an anti-slip treatment after installation is complete. Refer to the Zellige Installation Guide for full instructions.
Does Tidepool zellige work in a small bathroom?
Yes. Tidepool zellige works well in small bathrooms. The glazed surface reflects light, which helps a compact space feel more open. The blue-green tones read well under both natural and warmer artificial light, and the surface variation adds depth without overwhelming the room. In a small bathroom, vertical stack with the 2x6 Bejmat format can emphasize ceiling height; horizontal running bond broadens the perceived width.
Does Tidepool 2x6 zellige work in a pool or spa?
Yes. Tidepool 2x6 is suitable for pool and spa applications in both saltwater and chlorine environments. Sealing is required; apply sealer to all tiles and grout lines exposed to continuous moisture after grouting is complete. Consult the Zellige Installation Guide for specific sealing instructions and reach out to our team at info@ziatile.com with any project-specific questions.
Does the Tidepool colorway vary between orders?
Yes, the Tidepool colorway can vary between production runs. Each batch of zellige is produced by hand, and the clay, glaze, and kiln conditions shift from one run to the next. Tiles from separate orders may differ in tone and surface character. If your project requires tiles from multiple orders, ordering the full quantity at once gives the most consistent result.
How does Tidepool zellige compare to Aegean zellige?
Tidepool and Aegean share a blue tonal range, but behave differently in an installation. Where Aegean stays within a focused, deep blue, Tidepool moves more freely across the spectrum, picking up greens and seafoam tones alongside the blue, depending on the tile and the light.
