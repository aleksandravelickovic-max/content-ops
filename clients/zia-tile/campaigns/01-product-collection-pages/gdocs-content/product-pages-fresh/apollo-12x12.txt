Order Details + Installation
We recommend a 15–20% overage on all terrazzo orders to account for any cuts needed. Before installation, blend tiles from all boxes and arrange a layout to evenly disperse aggregate distribution. Use 1/8" tile spacers for the recommended grout joint. 


Installers will need to slightly adjust grout joint spacing (shim) or use wedge spacers to maintain straight grout lines. Where necessary, tile edges may be trimmed with a wet saw to assist with alignment.

About
Each terrazzo tile is a cement-based composite with crushed marble and stone aggregates ground and polished to a matte surface. Aggregate distribution varies tile to tile. Blend tiles from all boxes before installation and arrange a layout to disperse aggregate evenly.


Perhaps the most versatile tile Zia produces, with endless colorways, patterns, and shapes, Zia strives to offer the world's best curated collection of encaustic terrazzo tiles. While generally consistent in appearance, each handmade tile varies in aggregate distribution and surface character. Durable and versatile, matte terrazzo tile is an easy choice for many installations.


Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with terrazzo, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with California Prop 65. Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered edges or Schluter strips.


Proper installation and protection of terrazzo tile is key, so please ensure you work with an installer who has experience specifically installing terrazzo tile, and who has carefully reviewed Zia's installation guide.

How It's Made
Terrazzo is produced using techniques refined by Venetian stone masons since the 15th century, when leftover marble offcuts were combined with cement to floor courtyards and palazzo halls. The same craft is preserved in the workshops where Zia's terrazzo is produced today.


Crushed marble and stone aggregates are arranged in steel molds and covered with liquid cement, then hydraulically pressed into a powdered concrete base. Once cured, the surface is ground and polished to the matte finish that defines the line.


Each tile is finished by hand, which produces the subtle tonal variation, occasional crazing, and slight edge irregularity that separate hand-pressed terrazzo from machine-set composite tile. The cement matrix and the visible aggregate are what define terrazzo as a material; the press and the finish are what carry that definition into the floor.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the Apollo colorway in terrazzo?
Apollo is a grey-based terrazzo with a multi-colored aggregate blend distributed across a mid-grey cement matrix. The aggregate introduces a range of stone tones like cream, warm grey, and occasional brighter flecks that vary in size and distribution from tile to tile. 
Does Apollo 12x12 terrazzo require sealing?
Yes. Apollo 12x12 requires a two-stage sealing sequence. Before grouting, apply 511 Porous Plus in thin layers until the tiles are fully saturated, wiping off excess before it dries on the surface. After grouting, apply Fila Matte Finish Protective Wax for interior installations. For exterior installations in non-freeze/thaw climates, substitute a second coat of 511 Porous Plus for the wax, as UV exposure causes wax to discolor outdoors. Acidic cleaners must be avoided at every stage.
Can Apollo 12x12 terrazzo be used on a shower floor?
Yes. Apollo 12x12 is suited to shower wall and floor installations. Terrazzo has a DCOF of 0.51, above the 0.42 wet-floor slip-resistance threshold for level interior floors. Size grout joints to a minimum of 1/8 inch for shower floor use, seal before and after grouting, and apply an anti-slip treatment after installation for commercial or high-traffic wet-floor applications.
How do I clean Apollo terrazzo tile?
Apollo terrazzo tile requires regular sweeping or vacuuming to remove abrasive debris that can scratch the surface. For routine cleaning, use a pH-neutral cleaner such as Miracle Sealants pH Neutral Tile and Stone Cleaner. For heavier staining, Miracle Sealants Heavy Duty Cleaner (Acid Substitute) is the recommended product. Avoid acidic or abrasive cleaners, including vinegar, lemon juice, and bleach, as these damage the cement matrix over time.
What grout color works with Apollo 12x12 terrazzo?
For terrazzo, matching grout color closely to the tile's base tone produces the most consistent result, allowing the aggregate distribution to define the surface rather than the grout line. Darker or contrasting grout makes the tile grid pattern more prominent. Confirm grout color selection during the dry layout stage. For questions, contact us at info@ziatile.com or 310-844-1170.
Can Apollo 12x12 be used alongside other terrazzo formats?
Yes. Apollo 12x12 pairs naturally with Apollo 24x24 in the same installation, as the colorway stays consistent while the format shift changes how the aggregate reads at scale. When mixing with other terrazzo colorways, assess base tone and aggregate compatibility during a dry layout before setting begins. Blend tiles from all boxes regardless of format to distribute aggregate variation evenly across the field.
