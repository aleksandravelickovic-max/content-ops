About
No two zellige tiles are the same. Pieces vary in size, surface, and glaze across each box, with hairline crazing and chamfered edges that come from how the material is hand-formed rather than flaws to correct.


Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with zellige, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with California Prop 65. Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered cuts or Schluter strips. Traditional trim and edge pieces do not exist for zellige.


Seal zellige according to our installation instructions, whether the application is dry or exposed to moisture (pools, spas, showers). Reach out to our team at info@ziatile.com with any material or installation questions.

How It's Made
Zellige is hand-formed in Morocco, where the technique has been refined for more than 800 years and passed across generations of artisans.


The process begins with raw clay, which is mined from local hillsides, soaked, filtered for impurities, and hand-pressed into molds. The tiles are laid to dry in the Moroccan sun, flattened and shaped by hand, then dried further before glazing.


Each tile is hand-dipped in glaze and arranged inside an earthen kiln, where uneven heat creates the tonal variation that defines zellige. No two pieces leave the kiln the same, and the handmade nature and variation are hallmarks of this material. To prepare for installation, open and mix all boxes before setting them to achieve an ideal layout. The tiles will carry the marks of the day they were fired.

Frequently Asked Questions
What color is Glazed Earth zellige?
Glazed Earth zellige is a beige that moves across a wide tonal range. The glaze shifts from lighter to deeper, warmer tones within the same box, producing a surface that reads differently from tile to tile. The variation is a product of the hand-dipping process and the uneven heat of the earthen kiln.
Can Glazed Earth zellige be used on a kitchen floor?
Yes. Glazed Earth 4x4 is suitable for high-traffic interior floors, including kitchen floors. For floor applications in wet areas, slip resistance can be increased by sizing grout joints to a minimum of ⅛" and applying an anti-slip treatment after installation is complete. Sealing is required and should be maintained over time according to the Zellige Installation Guide.
Does the Glazed Earth colorway vary between production runs?
Yes. Each batch of zellige is produced by hand, and clay, glaze, and kiln conditions shift from one run to the next. Tiles from separate orders may differ in tone and surface character. Ordering the full project quantity at once gives the most consistent result across the installation.
Can Glazed Earth zellige be mixed with other colors in the collection?
Yes. Glazed Earth 4x4 can be combined with other zellige colorways in the same installation. Each colorway is produced using the same traditional methods, so tiles share consistent thickness, format, and surface character across the range. Glazed Earth pairs well alongside warm neutrals, creams, and earthy greens in the collection, where the beige reads as a grounded neutral field.
What kind of variation should I expect across Glazed Earth 4x4 tiles?
Glazed Earth 4x4 carries the natural variation that defines all handmade zellige. Before installation, open and mix tiles from all boxes, and dry-lay the entire installation to ensure the tonal range distributes evenly throughout the design.
What overage should I order for Glazed Earth 4x4?
For a standard layout, order 15 to 20% overage to account for curation and cuts. If your project requires a more uniform appearance, plan for 25% overage to give you more flexibility during the dry layout phase.
