About
Zia's ceramics are the design counterpoint to our handmade zellige. 
The collection spans matte, satin, and glossy finishes plus the textured Field Trip: Japan series, in three core formats: 2x8 and 2x4 rectangles and 4x4 squares. Color and tone vary across high-fired ceramics - less than handmade zellige, but enough that mixing boxes before the set still matters. 
Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with ceramic, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with California Prop 65. Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered edges or Schluter strips.
Sealing requirements vary by finish: matte ceramics must be sealed before grouting as a grout release; satin and glossy finishes do not require pre-grout sealing. For any material or installation questions, reach out to our team at info@ziatile.com.

How It's Made 
Ceramic tile is designed in Los Angeles and made in Japan, where the line is produced using high-temperature firing and precision glaze application. The result is a clean, precisely sized tile with consistent color across boxes, a different product category from handmade clay tile.
Clay is molded into shape, dried, glazed, and fired at high temperature. The matte, satin, and gloss finishes are achieved through different glaze formulations, each producing distinct surface character and slip-resistance performance.
Each format is produced to consistent dimensions across the line, which allows for predictable installation and clean grout lines. Subtle variation in color and surface is normal and reflects the hand-finishing step in production.

Frequently Asked Questions 
What color is Ponderosa 2x8?
Ponderosa is a muted warm green ceramic in a glossy finish. The glossy glaze produces a high-reflectivity surface that amplifies the color and shifts its reading as light angles change across the installation. Individual tiles carry slight tonal variation from the high-fire process, and those differences distribute evenly when tiles from all boxes are blended before the dry layout.
Does Ponderosa 2x8 require sealing before grouting?
No. Ponderosa 2x8 is a glossy finish ceramic and does not require pre-grout sealing. Apply grout directly without a sealer step. Matte ceramics require pre-grout sealing as a grout release. Satin and glossy ceramics do not. See the Ceramic Installation Guide for the complete installation sequence.
Can Ponderosa 2x8 be used on a shower floor?
Yes, with slip resistance measures in place. Glossy ceramic has a DCOF of 0.3, below the 0.42 wet-floor threshold. For shower floor installations, size grout joints to a minimum of 1/8 inch and apply an anti-slip treatment after installation. Ponderosa 2x8 is suitable for shower walls without additional measures.
Can Ponderosa 2x8 be used in a pool?
Yes. Ponderosa 2x8 is suitable for pool waterlines, spa interiors, and fountain installations in both saltwater and chlorine environments. Glossy finish ceramics do not require pre-grout sealing. Size grout joints to a minimum of 1/8 inch for all pool and spa installations.
Can Ponderosa 2x8 be installed on an exterior floor?
No. Ponderosa 2x8 is not suitable for exterior floor installations in either freeze/thaw or non-freeze/thaw climates. It can be used on exterior walls in non-freeze/thaw climates. For material and application questions, contact us at info@ziatile.com or 310-844-1170.
Can Ponderosa 2x8 be mixed with other Zia ceramics?
Yes. Ponderosa 2x8 combines with other colorways and finishes within Zia's ceramics collection. All standard ceramics in the same format size group share consistent dimensions. When mixing finishes, apply the pre-grout sealer to matte tiles only and allow it to cure fully before grouting the full field. Establish the full layout during the dry stage to confirm color balance and finish transitions before adhesive is applied.
