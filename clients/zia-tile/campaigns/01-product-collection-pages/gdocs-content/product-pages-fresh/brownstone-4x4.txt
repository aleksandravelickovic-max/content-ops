About
No two zellige tiles are the same. Pieces vary in size, surface, and glaze across each box, with hairline crazing and chamfered edges that come from how the material is hand-formed rather than flaws to correct.


Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with zellige, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with California Prop 65. Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered cuts or Schluter strips. Traditional trim and edge pieces do not exist for zellige.


Seal zellige according to our installation instructions, whether the application is dry or exposed to moisture (pools, spas, showers). Contact Zia's team at info@ziatile.com or 310-844-1170 with any material or installation questions.

How It's Made
Zellige is hand-formed in Morocco, where the technique has been refined for more than 800 years and passed across generations of artisans.


The process begins with raw clay, which is mined from local hillsides, soaked, filtered for impurities, and hand-pressed into molds. The tiles are laid to dry in the Moroccan sun, flattened and shaped by hand, then dried further before glazing.


Each tile is hand-dipped in glaze and arranged inside an earthen kiln, where uneven heat creates the tonal variation that defines zellige. No two pieces leave the kiln the same, and the handmade nature and variation are hallmarks of this material. To prepare for installation, open and mix all boxes before setting them to achieve an ideal layout. The tiles will carry the marks of the day they were fired.

Frequently Asked Questions
What color is Brownstone zellige?
Brownstone zellige has tonal variation built into the glaze, with individual tiles carrying different positions within the colorway's range. Those differences distribute across the full installation to produce a surface with depth rather than uniformity. The glaze reads differently in low light versus direct natural light, which means the full character of the colorway shifts throughout the day.
Can Brownstone 4x4 zellige be used in a shower?
Yes. Brownstone 4x4 can be used on shower walls, shower floors, and steam showers. Sealing is required for all shower applications. Glazed zellige has a DCOF of 0.16, below the 0.42 wet-floor threshold for level interior floors. Size grout joints to a minimum of 1/8 inch and apply an anti-slip treatment after installation to increase slip resistance on shower floors. Refer to the Zellige Installation Guide for full instructions.
What are the characteristic features of Brownstone zellige?
Brownstone 4x4 is a glazed zellige tile, and each piece carries chips, pits, and crazing as inherent characteristics of the handmade process. Color, shape, and thickness vary from tile to tile. These are not defects; they are the features that give each installation its surface depth and distinguish a zellige floor or wall from a manufactured tile. Mixing tiles from all boxes before the dry layout distributes that variation evenly across the field.
What grout color works best with Brownstone zellige?
Grout color is a compositional decision. A grout matched to the mid-brown range of the tile creates a continuous field where the tonal variation in the glaze reads as the main surface feature. A lighter buff or warm greige grout creates a soft contrast that highlights individual tile edges and the chips, pits, and crazing in the glaze. Dark espresso or near-black grout introduces a strong contrast that makes the tile grid more prominent. Confirm the selection during the dry layout stage before setting begins.
Can Brownstone 4x4 be used in a pool?
Yes. Brownstone 4x4 is suitable for pool and spa applications in both saltwater and chlorine environments. Sealing is required for all zellige installations; for pools and spas, apply 511 Porous Plus Penetrating Sealer after grouting is complete and allow it to cure fully before the installation is exposed to water. Consult the Zellige Installation Guide for application steps and re-sealing intervals
Can Brownstone be mixed with other zellige colorways?
Yes. Brownstone can be mixed with other colorways in Zia's zellige collection. Each colorway is produced using the same traditional methods, so tiles share consistent surface character and thickness across the range. Confirm any mixed-colorway layout during the dry stage before setting begins. For questions about colorway combinations, contact us at info@ziatile.com or 310-844-1170.
