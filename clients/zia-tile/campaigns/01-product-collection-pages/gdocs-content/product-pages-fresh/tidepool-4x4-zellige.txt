We recommend ordering 15–25% overage so the installation has room for hand-selection, color blending, and the tile breakage inherent to the material. Before laying any piece, unpack every box and mix the tiles on a flat surface to plan the layout and confirm the color balance.


Zellige tile installation is labor-intensive, often likened more to masonry than traditional tiling due to the irregular nature of the handmade material. Plan for installations 2–3 times longer than standard ceramic. Every piece needs to be sorted, soaked, arranged, and hand-adjusted for size.

About
No two zellige tiles are the same. Pieces vary in size, surface, and glaze across each box, with hairline crazing and chamfered edges that come from how the material is hand-formed rather than flaws to correct.


Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with zellige, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with California Prop 65. Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered cuts or Schluter strips. Traditional trim and edge pieces do not exist for zellige.


Seal zellige according to our installation instructions, whether the application is dry or exposed to moisture (pools, spas, showers). Reach out to our team at info@ziatile.com with any material or installation questions.

How It's Made
Zellige is hand-formed in Morocco, where the technique has been refined for more than 800 years and passed across generations of artisans.


The process begins with raw clay, which is mined from local hillsides, soaked, filtered for impurities, and hand-pressed into molds. The tiles are laid to dry in the Moroccan sun, flattened and shaped by hand, then dried further before glazing.


Each tile is hand-dipped in glaze and arranged inside an earthen kiln, where uneven heat creates the tonal variation that defines zellige. No two pieces leave the kiln the same, and the handmade nature and variation are hallmarks of this material. To prepare for installation, open and mix all boxes before setting them to achieve an ideal layout. The tiles will carry the marks of the day they were fired.

Frequently Asked Questions
What color is Tidepool zellige?
Tidepool zellige moves across a blue-green tonal range, shifting between kelpy greens and seafoam blues. The colorway does not sit in a single fixed tone because the hand-dipping process and uneven kiln heat mean some tiles read more green, others more blue, and the full range only becomes apparent across a completed installation.
How does Tidepool zellige compare to Aegean zellige?
Tidepool and Aegean are both blue-toned zellige colorways, but they occupy different positions within that range. Aegean reads as a deep, focused blue, and its tonal shifts stay within the blue range. Tidepool moves more broadly, straddling blue and green tones in a way that reads as more dynamic across a full installation. The choice between the two depends on whether a singular deep blue or a color-shifting blue-green surface is the design intent.
Can Tidepool zellige be used in a pool or spa?
Yes. Tidepool 4x4 is suitable for pool and spa applications in both saltwater and chlorine environments, where the blue-green glaze is particularly well-suited to aquatic settings. Sealing is required; apply sealer to all tiles and grout lines exposed to continuous moisture after grouting is complete. Consult the Zellige Installation Guide for instructions.
Does the Tidepool colorway vary between production runs?
Yes. Each batch of zellige is produced by hand, and the clay, glaze, and kiln conditions shift from one run to the next. Tiles from separate orders may differ in tone and surface character. If the project requires tiles from multiple orders, ordering the full quantity at once gives the most consistent result across the installation.
Can Tidepool zellige be mixed with other colors in the collection?
Yes. Tidepool 4x4 can be combined with other colorways in Zia's zellige collection. Each colorway is produced using the same traditional methods, so tiles share consistent thickness, format, and surface character across the range. 
Can Tidepool zellige be used on exterior walls?
Yes, Tidepool zellige can be used on exterior walls in non-freeze/thaw climates. For exterior applications, consult the Zellige Installation Guide for appropriate setting materials and sealing requirements.
