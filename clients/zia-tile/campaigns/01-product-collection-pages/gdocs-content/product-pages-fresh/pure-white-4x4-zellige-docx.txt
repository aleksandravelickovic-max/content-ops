Zia's Pure White zellige is a white zellige with a glaze that reads differently across a completed installation, depending on light and angle. At 4x4 inches, the square format is well-suited to kitchen backsplashes, bathroom walls, and shower enclosures where the white tones and surface variation are experienced at close range.

We recommend ordering 15–25% overage so the installation has room for hand-selection, color blending, and the tile breakage inherent to the material. Before laying any piece, unpack every box and mix the tiles on a flat surface to plan the layout and confirm the color balance.


Zellige tile installation is labor-intensive, often likened more to masonry than traditional tiling due to the irregular nature of the handmade material. The traditional method sets pieces edge-to-edge with minimal grout, but irregular shapes mean wedge spacers help keep layouts consistent and protect chamfered edges as the tiles are set. Plan for installations 2–3 times longer than standard ceramic. Every piece needs to be sorted, soaked, arranged, and hand-adjusted for size.

About
No two zellige tiles are the same. Pieces vary in size, surface, and glaze across each box, with hairline crazing and chamfered edges that come from how the material is hand-formed rather than flaws to correct.


Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with zellige, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with California Prop 65. Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered cuts or Schluter strips. Traditional trim and edge pieces do not exist for zellige.


Seal zellige according to our installation instructions, whether the application is dry or exposed to moisture (pools, spas, showers). Reach out to our team at info@ziatile.com with any material or installation questions.

How It's Made
Zellige is hand-formed in Morocco, where the technique has been refined for more than 800 years and passed across generations of artisans.


The process begins with raw clay, which is mined from local hillsides, soaked, filtered for impurities, and hand-pressed into molds. The tiles are laid to dry in the Moroccan sun, flattened and shaped by hand, then dried further before glazing.


Each tile is hand-dipped in glaze and arranged inside an earthen kiln, where uneven heat creates the tonal variation that defines zellige. No two pieces leave the kiln the same, and the handmade nature and variation are hallmarks of this material. To prepare for installation, open and mix all boxes before setting them to achieve an ideal layout. The tiles will carry the marks of the day they were fired.

Frequently Asked Questions
Will Pure White zellige look uniform once installed?
No. Each Pure White 4x4 is shaped by hand, which produces variation in thickness, shape, and glaze tone across the collection. The white glaze reads differently from tile to tile, and the chips, pits, and crazing inherent to handmade zellige add further surface variation. Mixing all boxes before installation helps distribute this variation evenly across the field.
Can Pure White zellige be used in a pool or spa?
Yes. Pure White zellige is suitable for pool and spa applications. Sealing is required for all wet applications; apply sealer to all tiles and grout lines exposed to continuous moisture after grouting is complete. Consult the Zellige Installation Guide and reach out to our team at info@ziatile.com or 310-844-1170 with project-specific questions.
Can Pure White 4x4 be mixed with other formats in the collection?
Yes. Pure White zellige is available in multiple formats and can be mixed in the same installation. Each format is produced using the same hand-pressing and kiln-firing methods. When mixing formats, plan the layout in advance to account for differences in edge definition and thickness across shapes.
Does the Pure White colorway vary between production runs?
Yes. Each batch of zellige is produced by hand, and clay, glaze, and kiln conditions shift from one run to the next. Tiles from separate orders may differ in tone and surface character. Ordering the full quantity at once ensures the most consistent result across a project requiring multiple pallets.
What's the best way to lay out Pure White zellige before installation?
Dry-lay the entire installation before setting any tile. Arrange tiles from all boxes together to distribute color variation, thickness, and surface character evenly across the field. Group tiles by thickness and create a balanced visual mix before the installer begins. This curation phase prevents clustering variation in one area and ensures the glaze shifts read naturally across the full surface.
What tools do I need to cut Pure White zellige?
Cutting zellige requires a wet saw with a diamond blade. Because each tile is hand-formed and varies slightly in size and thickness, cuts must be measured tile-by-tile rather than assumed uniform. Plan edge and corner cuts during the dry layout phase before setting tile. Installers should wear protective face and eye coverings throughout the cutting process.
