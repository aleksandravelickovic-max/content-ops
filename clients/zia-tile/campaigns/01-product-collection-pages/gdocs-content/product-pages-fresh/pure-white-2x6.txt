We recommend ordering 15–25% overage so the installation has room for hand-selection, color blending, and the tile breakage inherent to the material. Before laying any piece, unpack every box and mix the tiles on a flat surface to plan the layout and confirm the color balance.


Zellige tile installation is labor-intensive, often likened more to masonry than traditional tiling due to the irregular nature of the handmade material. Plan for installations 2–3 times longer than standard ceramic. Every piece needs to be sorted, soaked, arranged, and hand-adjusted for size.

About
No two zellige tiles are the same. Pieces vary in size, surface, and glaze across each box, with hairline crazing and chamfered edges that come from how the material is hand-formed rather than flaws to correct.


Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with zellige, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with California Prop 65. Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered cuts or Schluter strips. Traditional trim and edge pieces do not exist for zellige.


Seal zellige according to our installation instructions, whether the application is dry or exposed to moisture (pools, spas, showers). Reach out to our team at info@ziatile.com with any material or installation questions.

How It's Made
Zellige is hand-formed in Morocco, where the technique has been refined for more than 800 years and passed across generations of artisans.


The process begins with raw clay, which is mined from local hillsides, soaked, filtered for impurities, and hand-pressed into molds. The tiles are laid to dry in the Moroccan sun, flattened and shaped by hand, then dried further before glazing.


Each tile is hand-dipped in glaze and arranged inside an earthen kiln, where uneven heat creates the tonal variation that defines zellige. No two pieces leave the kiln the same, and the handmade nature and variation are hallmarks of this material. To prepare for installation, open and mix all boxes before setting them to achieve an ideal layout. The tiles will carry the marks of the day they were fired.

Frequently Asked Questions
How does Pure White 2x6 compare to Casablanca 2x6?
Both Pure White and Casablanca are white zellige colorways in the 2x6 Bejmat format, but they sit in slightly different positions. Pure White reads cleaner and closer to a true white, while Casablanca carries more movement between off-white tones, giving it a slightly softer, more varied surface. The choice depends on whether a cleaner white or a more tonal neutral is the design intent.
What layout patterns work with the 2x6 Bejmat format?
Running bond with offset horizontal joints is the most common approach. Herringbone adds visual movement and draws attention to the elongated format. Vertical stack creates a quieter, more ordered surface where each tile reads as a narrow vertical element. The layout choice affects how the glaze variation reads across the full installation, so plan the arrangement during the dry layout stage before setting any tile.
Can Pure White 2x6 zellige be used in a shower?
Yes. Pure White 2x6 can be used on shower walls, shower floors, and steam showers. Sealing is required for all shower applications. For shower floor installations, slip resistance can be increased by sizing grout joints to a minimum of ⅛" and applying an anti-slip treatment after installation is complete. Refer to the Zellige Installation Guide for full instructions.
Does Pure White 2x6 zellige work in a pool or spa?
Yes. Pure White 2x6 is suitable for pool and spa applications in both saltwater and chlorine environments. Sealing is required; apply sealer to all tiles and grout lines exposed to continuous moisture after grouting is complete. Consult the Zellige Installation Guide for specific sealing instructions and reach out to our team at info@ziatile.com or 310-844-1170 with any project-specific questions.
Why do some Pure White 2x6 tiles have fine lines on the surface?
Fine lines on the glazed surface of Pure White 2x6 zellige, known as crazing, occur naturally during the earthen kiln firing process. These characteristic marks, along with occasional chips and pits, are inherent to handmade Moroccan tile and do not affect the structural integrity of the tile.
Can I mix Pure White 2x6 zellige with other colors in the collection?
Yes, you can mix Pure White 2x6 zellige with other colors in Zia's handcrafted zellige collection. Artisans use the same traditional Moroccan methods for each shade, allowing you to combine Pure White 2x6 tiles with other tones to create a custom installation that showcases the full range of available dynamic tones.
