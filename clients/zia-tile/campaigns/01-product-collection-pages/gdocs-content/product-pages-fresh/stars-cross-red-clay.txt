About
Each Cotto tile is made from locally-sourced clays. Our handcrafted Cotto tiles are made just outside of historic San Miguel de Allende, Mexico.


The tiles' clay formulation is mixed with water and blended to the ideal consistency, then pressed in a mold to dry for 1–2 weeks until the proper moisture level is achieved for firing. At this point, wood ash is added to the surface of our 'Fired Earth' tiles to give them their distinctive surface coloration.


Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with Cotto, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with California Prop 65. Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered edges or Schluter strips.

How It's Made
Cotto, derived from the Italian terra cotta or "baked earth," is part of a long lineage of fired clays that date back to the dawn of civilization itself. From Egyptian water jugs to Chinese roof tiles to Roman bricks, terra cotta has been part of the human story for as long as we've had an alphabet. The materials in Cotto tile haven't changed much over the millennia, while the firing process has evolved: from kiln furniture to mold designs to firing method, our handmade and high-fired Cotto is our modern take on an ancient tradition.


Our Cotto is produced in San Miguel de Allende, Mexico, where natural clay is pressed into molds, sun-dried, and fired in kilns by local artisans. The unglazed surface is what gives Cotto its character at installation and over time. With proper sealing, Cotto handles indoor floors, exterior floors in any climate, and freeze/thaw exposure, and the surface wears like a natural stone product as use deepens its tone.

Frequently Asked Questions
What color is Stars & Cross + Red Clay terra cotta tile?
Stars & Cross + Red Clay is a warm red terra cotta with natural firing variation that creates tonal movement. Each handmade tile carries inherent differences in tone and surface density. These variations define the material's character rather than indicating defects. The overall effect across the installation is a continuous field of warm, sun-baked clay with subtle tonal movement built into the patterned surface.
Can Stars & Cross + Red Clay be used in a kitchen?
Yes. Stars & Cross + Red Clay can be used in a kitchen for both floors and backsplashes. The tile performs well when properly sealed to protect against moisture and staining. Before grouting, apply 511 Porous Plus in thin coats until fully saturated, then allow one to two days for curing. After grouting, finish with Fila Matte Wax for interior kitchens. A minimum 1/8" grout joint is recommended for kitchen floor installations.
What are the natural characteristics of terra cotta tile?
Zia's terra cotta is formed from natural clay, shaped, dried, and kiln-fired. This produces visible variation in tone, shape, edge profile, and thickness. Stars & Cross + Red Clay carries a DCOF of 0.98, well above the 0.42 wet-floor safety threshold, making it suitable for kitchen floors and shower applications when properly sealed.
How do I seal Stars & Cross + Red Clay terra cotta tile?
Stars & Cross + Red Clay requires a two-step sealing process. Before grouting, apply 511 Porous Plus in thin coats until fully saturated, then allow one to two days for curing. This initial application also acts as a grout release. After grouting, interior installations should be finished with Fila Matte Wax, while exterior applications require a second coat of 511 Porous Plus, since UV exposure discolors wax over time. Periodic resealing is required to maintain resistance to moisture and staining.
How does the Stars & Cross specialty shape install?
The Stars & Cross specialty shape interlocks across tile edges, so the pattern resolves only when each piece is oriented consistently. A full dry layout before setting is essential to confirm how the geometry reads across the space and to identify where cuts are needed at the perimeter. Increase overage to 20 to 25% for Stars & Cross orders to account for the additional cuts required for the interlocking edges. Contact info@ziatile.com or 310-844-1170 if you have questions about layout planning.
Can Stars & Cross + Red Clay be mixed with other Cotto colorways?
Yes. Stars & Cross + Red Clay can be combined with other colorways in the Cotto collection. Each Cotto tile is formed from the same San Miguel de Allende clay body and fired through the same kiln process, creating consistency across the line. Red Clay pairs naturally with traditional hues such as Adobe and Fired Earth, while contrasting with the unexpected tones of Blanco, Madera, and Oscura. When mixing colorways, blend tiles from all boxes to distribute variation evenly throughout the installation.
