About
No two zellige tiles are the same. Pieces vary in size, surface, and glaze across each box, with hairline crazing and chamfered edges that come from how the material is hand-formed rather than flaws to correct.


Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with zellige, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with California Prop 65. Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered cuts or Schluter strips. Traditional trim and edge pieces do not exist for zellige.


Seal zellige according to our installation instructions, whether the application is dry or exposed to moisture (pools, spas, showers). Reach out to our team at info@ziatile.com with any material or installation questions.

How It's Made
Zellige is hand-formed in Morocco, where the technique has been refined for more than 800 years and passed across generations of artisans.
The process begins with raw clay, which is mined from local hillsides, soaked, filtered for impurities, and hand-pressed into molds. The tiles are laid to dry in the Moroccan sun, flattened and shaped by hand, then dried further before glazing.
Each tile is hand-dipped in glaze and arranged inside an earthen kiln, where uneven heat creates the tonal variation that defines zellige. No two pieces leave the kiln the same and the handmade nature and variation are hallmarks of this most special material. 
To prepare for installation, open and mix all boxes before setting them to achieve an ideal layout. The tiles will carry the marks of the day they were fired.

Frequently Asked Questions
What color is Prismatic 1 zellige mosaic?
Prismatic 1 is a monochromatic mosaic in Casablanca White zellige glaze. In a single-colorway mosaic, the tonal variation inherent to zellige becomes the primary surface feature: each piece holds a slightly different position in the colorway's range, and those differences distribute across the mosaic field as the installation grows. The geometric structure of the pattern reads through that tonal variation rather than through color contrast between two glazes.
What makes Prismatic 1 different from other zellige mosaics?
The difference between Prismatic 1 and other zellige mosaics in the Zia collection is how the pattern is defined. Most zellige mosaics use two or more colorways, with color contrast between elements carrying the geometric design.  Prismatic 1 uses a single colorway throughout, so the geometric structure and the tonal variation within the Casablanca White glaze carry the pattern on their own. The design reads at two scales: the geometry at a normal viewing distance and the individual glaze variation up close.
Can Prismatic 1 be used in a shower?
Yes. Prismatic 1 can be installed on shower walls, shower floors, and in steam showers. Sealing is required for all shower applications. Glazed zellige has a DCOF of 0.16, below the 0.42 threshold for level wet floors. For shower floor installations, size grout joints to a minimum of 1/8 inch and apply an anti-slip treatment after installation is complete. See the Zellige Installation Guide for the full wet area protocol.
Can Prismatic 1 be used in a pool?
Yes. Prismatic 1 is suitable for pool and spa applications in both saltwater and chlorine environments. Sealing is required for all zellige installations; for pools and spas, apply 511 Porous Plus Penetrating Sealer after grouting is complete and allow it to cure fully before the installation is exposed to water. Consult the Zellige Installation Guide for application steps and re-sealing intervals. For project-specific questions, contact us at info@ziatile.com or 310-844-1170.
What grout color works best with Prismatic 1 zellige mosaic?
Grout color determines how prominently the individual tile edges and the geometric pattern register across the finished surface. A white grout matched closely to the Casablanca glaze produces the most continuous field, with the mosaic pattern receding into the surface. 


A light grey or warm neutral creates enough contrast to define individual tile edges without introducing a distinct secondary color. Darker grout, such as charcoal or warm grey, makes the geometric pattern more prominent and reveals the mosaic's structure more clearly at normal viewing distances.
Can Prismatic 1 be paired with solid zellige tiles?
Yes. Prismatic 1 shares the Casablanca White colorway with Zia's solid zellige formats, including the 4x4 square and 2x6 Bejmat. Installing Prismatic 1 as a feature zone or border alongside a solid Casablanca White field tile is a standard approach: the two formats share the same colorway while the mosaic introduces a contrast in surface complexity and pattern density. All Casablanca White zellige is produced using the same traditional methods, so surface character and glaze variation are consistent across formats within the colorway.
