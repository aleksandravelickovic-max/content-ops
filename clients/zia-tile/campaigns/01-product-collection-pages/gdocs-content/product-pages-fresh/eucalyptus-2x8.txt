About
Zia's ceramics are the design counterpoint to our handmade zellige. They were developed with a Japanese potters' village outside Nagoya, where high-firing extends back more than 1,000 years, to produce a tile precise enough for tight grout joints and clean layouts.


The collection spans matte, satin, and glossy finishes plus the textured Field Trip: Japan series, in three core formats: 2x8 and 2x4 rectangles and 4x4 squares. Color and tone vary across high-fired ceramics, less than handmade zellige, but enough that mixing boxes before the set still matters.


Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with ceramic, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with California Prop 65. Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered edges or Schluter strips.


Sealing requirements vary by finish: matte ceramics must be sealed before grouting as a grout release; satin and glossy finishes do not require pre-grout sealing. For any material or installation questions, reach out to our team at info@ziatile.com.

How It's Made
Ceramic tile is designed in Los Angeles and made in Japan, where the line is produced using high-temperature firing and precision glaze application. The result is a clean, precisely sized tile with consistent color across boxes, a different product category from handmade clay tile.


Clay is molded into shape, dried, glazed, and fired at high temperature. The matte, satin, and gloss finishes are achieved through different glaze formulations, each producing distinct surface character and slip-resistance performance.


Each format is produced to consistent dimensions across the line, which allows for predictable installation and clean grout lines. Subtle variation in color and surface is normal and reflects the hand-finishing step in production.

Frequently Asked Questions
What does the Eucalyptus colorway look like?
Eucalyptus is a light green satin ceramic with a finish that holds the colorway consistently under different lighting conditions. The light green reads as a soft, even field across the wall or floor, with the satin surface returning a low sheen that diffuses light rather than reflecting it directly.
Slight tonal variation tile to tile is normal for high-fired ceramics and reads as a continuous field once the installation is complete.
Does Eucalyptus 2x8 satin ceramic require sealing?
No. Satin and glossy ceramics do not require pre-grout sealing. The glaze on Eucalyptus is non-porous, so a grout-release sealer is not needed before grouting. Matte ceramics in the collection do require a penetrating sealer applied before grouting as a grout release; satin finishes do not. Full installation guidance is in the Ceramic Installation Guide.
Can Eucalyptus 2x8 ceramic be used in a shower?
Yes. Eucalyptus 2x8 is suited to shower walls and floor installations. For commercial or high-traffic wet-floor applications, consult with your licensed installer before proceeding. Contact info@ziatile.com or 310-844-1170 with project-specific questions.
Can Eucalyptus 2x8 be used outdoors?
No. Zia's ceramic collection is not rated for exterior floor applications, in freeze/thaw or non-freeze/thaw climates. The glaze formulation and tile body are designed for interior floor and wall use, plus shower and pool applications. Eucalyptus is suited to exterior wall installations in non-freeze/thaw climates; for any outdoor wall use, confirm exposure conditions with the installer before setting.
What grout joint is recommended for the 2x8 format?
Plan for 3/16" grout joints as the standard for Eucalyptus 2x8, with a minimum of 1/8" where a tighter layout is intended. The uniform dimensions of high-fired ceramic allow for predictable spacing across the installation, and the 2x8 format reads cleanly with either a tight minimal joint or the wider 3/16" standard, depending on the design intent. Mixing box lots before setting is recommended, as subtle tone variation across high-fired ceramics is normal.
How does the satin finish compare to matte and glossy ceramics in the Zia collection?
Satin ceramics carry a low-sheen surface between matte and gloss, returning more light than matte without the full reflectivity of a gloss finish. Matte ceramics absorb light and produce a flat, non-reflective surface; they also require pre-grout sealing as a grout release. Glossy ceramics produce the highest surface sheen and are the most reflective of the three. 
Can Eucalyptus 2x8 be mixed with other ceramic formats or colorways?
Yes. Eucalyptus 2x8 can be combined with other ceramic formats (including the 2x4 rectangle and 4x4 square) and other colorways across the ceramic collection. Each format is produced to consistent dimensions and shares the same material character across the line. 


The light green of Eucalyptus pairs naturally with neutral and white colorways in the collection, where the color can define zones within a larger installation without requiring different materials.
