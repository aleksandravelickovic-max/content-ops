About
No two zellige tiles are the same. Pieces vary in size, surface, and glaze across each box, with hairline crazing and chamfered edges that come from how the material is hand-formed rather than flaws to correct.


Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with zellige, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with California Prop 65. Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered cuts or Schluter strips. Traditional trim and edge pieces do not exist for zellige.


Seal zellige according to our installation instructions, whether the application is dry or exposed to moisture (pools, spas, showers). Reach out to our team at info@ziatile.com with any material or installation questions.

How It's Made
Zellige is hand-formed in Morocco, where the technique has been refined for more than 800 years and passed across generations of artisans.


The process begins with raw clay, which is mined from local hillsides, soaked, filtered for impurities, and hand-pressed into molds. The tiles are laid to dry in the Moroccan sun, flattened and shaped by hand, then dried further before glazing.


Each tile is hand-dipped in glaze and arranged inside an earthen kiln, where uneven heat creates the tonal variation that defines zellige. No two pieces leave the kiln the same, and the handmade nature and variation are hallmarks of this material. To prepare for installation, open and mix all boxes before setting them to achieve an ideal layout. The tiles will carry the marks of the day they were fired.

Frequently Asked Questions
What grout joint size works best for the 2x2 format?
For wall installations, zellige is typically laid edge-to-edge or with a 1/16" spacing if definition is desired. For floor installations, a minimum of ⅛" grout joint is recommended. For wet commercial floors, increase grout joints to a minimum of ⅛" and consider applying an anti-slip product after installation to ensure safety.
Can the Casablanca White 2x2 be mixed with other Casablanca formats?
Yes. Casablanca White 2x2 can be mixed with other Casablanca White formats — including the 4x4 and 2x6 — or with other colorways in Zia's zellige collection. Mixing the 2x2 with larger formats is a common approach for combining field tile with detail work in kitchens and bathrooms.
Can Casablanca White 2x2 zellige be used in a pool or spa?
Yes. Our Casablanca White 2x2 is suitable for pool and spa applications in both saltwater and chlorine environments. Sealing is required after grouting; always seal according to our Zellige Installation Guide. For project-specific questions, reach out to info@ziatile.com or 310-844-1170.
What are the chips, pits, and crazing I see on Casablanca White 2x2?
Chips, pits, and crazing are inherent characteristics of hand-pressed zellige — they are not defects. Chips and pits occur naturally during the sun-drying and hand-shaping stages. Crazing appears as fine hairline patterns across the glaze surface and is a signature feature of how the earthen kiln fires each tile. These variations create the visual depth and handmade character that define zellige. 
How should I prepare the tiles before installation?
Open all boxes and mix tiles together before layout begins. This disperses color variation, chips, pits, and crazing evenly across the installation. Arrange tiles by thickness to create an even plane. Sort out any pieces you want to reserve for cuts or detail work. This curation step is essential for a balanced final appearance.
Will my tiles look the same as the sample I ordered?
Zellige tiles have inherent variation in tone, glaze character, and surface detail. Your full order will contain the same range of variation as your sample. Mixing boxes and curating tiles during layout allows you to distribute this variation evenly. This is the handmade nature of zellige.
