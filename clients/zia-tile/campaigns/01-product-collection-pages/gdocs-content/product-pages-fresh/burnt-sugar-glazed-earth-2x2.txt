About
No two zellige mosaic sheets read alike. Tiles vary in size, surface, and glaze across each box, with hairline crazing and chamfered edges that come from how the material is hand-formed rather than flaws to correct. Open every box in your shipment and arrange a trial layout on a flat surface before setting tile, so the color balance and variation settle into the layout you want.


Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with zellige, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with California Prop 65. Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered cuts or Schluter strips. Traditional trim and edge pieces do not exist for zellige.


Seal the zellige mosaic according to our installation instructions, whether the application is dry or exposed to moisture (pools, spas, showers). Reach out to our team at info@ziatile.com with any material or installation questions.

How It's Made
Zellige mosaic is handmade in Morocco using the same traditional techniques as field tile, refined by artisans whose families have practiced the craft for generations. The mosaic format is hand-placed on mesh backing for installation as designed sheets.


The process begins with raw clay mined from local Moroccan hillsides. Artisans soak the clay in water and filter it for impurities, then hand-press the resulting paste into molds before laying the tiles to dry in the Moroccan sun. Each tile is flattened and shaped by hand before glazing.


Artisans hand-dip each tile in glaze and arrange them inside an earthen kiln, where uneven heat distribution creates the dynamic glazes and tonal variation that define traditional zellige. Once fired, tiles are hand-placed on mesh sheets. Every sheet is an expression of the artisan's work that day, beautifully imperfect and unique.

Frequently Asked Questions
What colors make up the Burnt Sugar + Glazed Earth combination?
Burnt Sugar is a deep, dark brown zellige glaze. Glazed Earth is a warm tan-beige. Set in alternating 2x2 squares, the contrast between the two reads as a classic checkerboard across the field. 


Because both glazes are hand-dipped and kiln-fired, each tile carries its own tonal position within that range: the dark tiles vary in depth, the light tiles vary in warmth. That variation shifts the checkerboard pattern from mechanical to handmade across the full installation.
Can Burnt Sugar + Glazed Earth 2x2 be used in a kitchen?
Yes. Burnt Sugar + Glazed Earth 2x2 is well-suited to kitchen backsplash installations, where the glazed surface holds up to moisture and routine cleaning. The two-colorway palette complements natural stone countertops, wood cabinetry, and matte metal fixtures. 


As with all zellige, a full dry layout across the backsplash surface before setting any tile is recommended, as hand-selection at the layout stage improves the tonal balance across the finished wall.
Is the 2x2 mosaic format appropriate for floor installations?
Yes, with appropriate precautions. Burnt Sugar + Glazed Earth 2x2 is suited to interior floor applications, though glazed zellige has a DCOF rating of 0.16, below the 0.42 wet-floor slip-resistance threshold.


Mesh-mounted sheets have less grout spacing due to hand-placement; for floor applications, individual tiles can be lifted from the mesh and placed with a minimum of 1/8" grout joints, and an anti-slip treatment should be applied after installation is complete. Refer to the Zellige Installation Guide for full instructions.
Can Burnt Sugar + Glazed Earth 2x2 be used in a pool or spa?
Yes. Zellige is approved for pool waterline, spa interior, and fountain applications in both residential and commercial settings. Sealing is required for all wet-area installations; apply 511 Porous Plus penetrating sealer to all tiles and grout lines exposed to continuous moisture after grouting is complete. Contact info@ziatile.com with project-specific questions.
Does the Burnt Sugar + Glazed Earth colorway vary between orders?
Yes. The Burnt Sugar and Glazed Earth colorways can vary between production runs. Each batch of zellige is produced by hand, and the clay, glaze, and kiln conditions shift from one run to the next. 


Tiles from separate orders may differ in tone and surface character. Where the project requires tiles from multiple orders, ordering the full quantity at once gives the most consistent result across the installation.
Can Burnt Sugar + Glazed Earth 2x2 be mixed with other zellige colorways or formats?
Yes. The Burnt Sugar + Glazed Earth 2x2 can be combined with other zellige colorways and formats within the same installation. Each format is produced using the same traditional methods and shares consistent material character across the zellige collection. The two-colorway combination can define zones within a larger installation without introducing competing colorways.
What grout color works best with Burnt Sugar + Glazed Earth zellige?
A tonal grout that aligns closely with the Burnt Sugar and Glazed Earth range creates a continuous surface, allowing the glaze variation within each tile to lead. A lighter neutral grout defines the 2x2 format more clearly and introduces a grid reading across the field. 


Grout color selection is one of the most impactful design decisions in a zellige installation; visit the Zia website or Instagram to view installation examples and decide what grout color works best for your project.
