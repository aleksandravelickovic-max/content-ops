About
No two zellige tiles are the same. Pieces vary in size, surface, and glaze across each box, with hairline crazing and chamfered edges that come from how the material is hand-formed rather than flaws to correct.


Installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with zellige, thinset, sealants, and grout, in accordance with California Prop 65. Bring drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, or finish exposed edges with mitered cuts or Schluter strips. Traditional trim and edge pieces do not exist for zellige.


Seal zellige according to our installation instructions, whether the application is dry or exposed to moisture (pools, spas, showers). Reach out to our team at info@ziatile.com with any material or installation questions.

How It's Made
Zellige is hand-formed in Morocco, where the technique has been refined for more than 800 years and passed across generations of artisans.


The process begins with raw clay, which is mined from local hillsides, soaked, filtered for impurities, and hand-pressed into molds. The tiles are laid to dry in the Moroccan sun, flattened and shaped by hand, then dried further before glazing.


Each tile is hand-dipped in glaze and arranged inside an earthen kiln, where uneven heat creates the tonal variation that defines zellige. No two pieces leave the kiln the same, and the handmade nature and variation are hallmarks of this material. To prepare for installation, open and mix all boxes before setting them to achieve an ideal layout. The tiles will carry the marks of the day they were fired.

Frequently Asked Questions
What layout patterns work with the Casablanca White Trapezoid?
The trapezoid generates herringbone, fan, and linear patterns depending on orientation. Oriented horizontally with alternating rows offset, the angled sides interlock to create a layered, scale-like pattern. Oriented vertically, the narrow end leads and the composition reads as pointed forms. Plan the layout during the dry phase before setting tile, because orientation consistency determines how the pattern reads across the surface.
Can the Casablanca White Trapezoid be mixed with other Casablanca formats?
Yes. Casablanca White Trapezoid can be mixed with other Casablanca formats, including the 2x2 and 2x6 Bejmat, or with other colorways in Zia's zellige collection. Each format is produced using the same hand-pressing and kiln-firing methods. Using the trapezoid as an accent field against 2x2 or Bejmat field tile is a common approach in kitchen and bathroom installations.
Can Casablanca White Trapezoid be used on shower walls and floors?
Yes. Casablanca White Trapezoid is suitable for shower walls, shower floors, and steam showers. Sealing is required for all shower applications. For shower floor installations, grout joints should measure at least 1/8" and an anti-slip treatment should be applied after installation. Refer to the Zellige Installation Guide for full sealing and slip-resistance instructions.
Does cutting the Casablanca White Trapezoid require special tools?
Cutting zellige requires a wet saw with a diamond blade. The trapezoid's angled geometry means corner and edge cuts must be planned during the dry layout stage before setting tile. Because each tile varies in size, field cuts must be measured tile-by-tile rather than assumed. Installers should wear protective face and eye coverings throughout.
Can Casablanca White Trapezoid be used in a pool or spa?
Yes. Casablanca White Trapezoid is suitable for pool and spa applications in both saltwater and chlorine environments. Sealing is required after grouting for all tiles and grout lines exposed to continuous moisture. Refer to the Zellige Installation Guide for specific sealing instructions, or reach out to info@ziatile.com or 310-844-1170 with project-specific questions.
Is the Casablanca White Trapezoid suitable for outdoor use?
Casablanca White Trapezoid is suitable for exterior walls in non-freeze/thaw climates only. It is not suitable for exterior floors or freeze/thaw climates. Contact info@ziatile.com or 310-844-1170 to discuss your specific location and climate before ordering.
