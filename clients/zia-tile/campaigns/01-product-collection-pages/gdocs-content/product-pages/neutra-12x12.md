## About 

### Tile Character

  - Our Neutra 12x12 is a beige terrazzo tile with small grey stone aggregates embedded in a neutral cement field, producing a continuous but non-repetitive composition across the installation.
  - Each handmade cement terrazzo tile remains wholly unique, and this inherent variation in aggregate placement defines the line's character.
  - Expect natural variation in color, tone, and aggregate distribution across the layout, along with the chips, pits, and crazing that may develop during the curing process.

### Preparation and Curation

  - Mix tiles from multiple boxes before installation to distribute aggregate variation evenly and prevent clustering of similar compositions across the layout.
  - Complete a full dry layout before setting. Terrazzo does not repeat patterns, and aggregate variation becomes more visible at installation scale, so the dry layout is where tonal balance is resolved.
  - Disperse more pronounced aggregate clusters evenly rather than concentrating them in one area of the layout.
  - We suggest a 15-20% overage on all terrazzo orders. For a more uniform look, we recommend 25% overage.

### Installation and Finishing

  - Installers must always wear protective face and eye coverings when working with terrazzo, thinset, sealants, and grout in accordance with California Prop 65.
  - For finishing, bring the drywall flush with the finished tile surface, miter exposed edges at 45 degrees, or complete the edge with Schluter strips.
  - Install over a fully cured substrate using high-quality medium-bed thinset mortar. Maintain 1/8" grout joints and use wedge spacers or shims to preserve alignment across the field.
  - Seal Neutra 12x12 with 511 Porous Plus before and after grouting. For interior applications, finish with Fila Matte Wax as a final coat. For exterior applications, apply a second coat of 511 Porous Plus in place of wax.
  - Press tiles into place using palm pressure only. Do not use a rubber mallet, which can create small cracks in the terrazzo body.

## How It's Made

Our Neutra 12x12 is made by hand, one tile at a time, using crushed marble and stone aggregates, cement, steel molds, and hydraulic pressure. The process begins with the preparation of the aggregates, which are selected and combined to establish the visible composition of each cement terrazzo floor tile before casting begins.

  

The aggregates are placed into steel molds and bound with a cement mixture that forms the structural body of the tile. The full assembly is then hydraulically pressed, compressing the aggregate and cement into a dense full-body composition where stone and binder are unified under pressure. Unlike ceramic or stone tile, terrazzo is not fired and carries no surface glaze. The durability of each tile comes from the hardened cement matrix that locks the aggregate in place during curing.

  

Once pressed, each tile cures until it reaches full strength. The surface is then ground and polished to expose the internal stone structure. The final appearance of Neutra 12x12 is the direct result of embedded aggregate revealed through mechanical finishing rather than a printed or coated design layer. Each tile reflects the specific aggregate distribution and pressing conditions of its production cycle, which is why variation in tone, density, and stone placement is inherent across the line.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is terrazzo tile?

Terrazzo tile is a composite material made by embedding crushed stone aggregates such as marble and quartz into a cement binder, then pressing and finishing the surface to expose the internal composition. Neutra 12x12 follows this process, producing a matte, full-body material where the visible pattern comes from the aggregate itself rather than a surface coating. Because the aggregate runs through the full thickness of the tile, the pattern wears with the floor rather than off it.

### How do I clean Neutra terrazzo tile?

To clean Neutra terrazzo tile, use a pH-neutral cleaner with warm water and a soft cloth or mop for routine maintenance. Avoid acidic or abrasive products such as vinegar, bleach, or ammonia, which can damage the cement matrix over time. Sweep or vacuum regularly to remove abrasive debris that can wear down the surface. Reseal every 1-2 years, or more often in moisture-exposed areas.

### How long does terrazzo tile last?

Terrazzo tile can last for decades when properly installed and maintained. The cement-based structure resists wear under regular use, and the surface can be refinished over time if needed. With correct sealing and routine cleaning, terrazzo tile retains both its structural integrity and its surface appearance across long-term residential and commercial installations.

### Can Neutra 12x12 be used in a kitchen?

Yes, Neutra 12x12 can be used in a kitchen on both floors and walls, including backsplash applications. The material performs well in high-use environments, though it must be sealed before and after grouting to protect against staining from oil, water, and food preparation. Ongoing maintenance with pH-neutral cleaners preserves the surface over time.

### How does the 12x12 format work in a terrazzo installation?

The 12x12 format creates a consistent grid with more visible grout joints than larger terrazzo formats, which helps distribute aggregate variation evenly across the installation. Blending tiles from multiple boxes before setting is essential to balance tone and aggregate density. A 1/8" grout joint is recommended, and installers should plan for minor alignment adjustments to maintain straight joint lines across the layout.
