## How It's Made

The clay that becomes each Graphite Grey 4x4 is pulled from the hillsides surrounding Fez, Morocco. Artisans soak it in water, filter it for impurities, and work it by hand until it reaches the right consistency for pressing into molds. The shaping is done entirely by hand, which is why no two tiles sit at the same depth or carry the same edge.

  

After drying in the Moroccan sun, each tile is dipped individually in glaze. The Graphite Grey glaze is where the deep grey tones take form, and because each dip is done by hand, the coverage varies from tile to tile. That variation is what produces the cool tonal range visible across a full installation of this dark grey Moroccan tile.

  

The final stage is the kiln. Earthen kilns do not distribute heat evenly, and the results of that unevenness are not incidental. The chips, pits, crazing, and shifting tones that emerge from the firing process are what make each Graphite Grey 4x4 its own.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I clean Graphite Grey zellige?

To clean Graphite Grey 4x4 zellige, use a pH-neutral cleaner with warm water and a soft cloth. Avoid acidic or abrasive products such as vinegar, bleach, and citrus-based cleaners, which can damage the glaze and grout surface over time. Wipe surfaces dry after cleaning to prevent mineral buildup, and seal the grout lines periodically to maintain the integrity of the installation.

### What color is Graphite Grey zellige?

Graphite Grey zellige is a deep, cool-toned grey with shifting glaze movement across each tile. The tone reads lighter or darker depending on light exposure, with variation in depth created by the hand-dipped glazing process. Natural surface irregularities and the chips, pits, and crazing inherent to zellige also contribute to how the color reads across the installation.

### Can Graphite Grey zellige be used on a shower floor?

Yes, Graphite Grey 4x4 can be used on shower walls, shower floors, and steam showers. Sealing is required for all shower applications. For shower floor installations, slip resistance can be increased by sizing grout joints to a minimum of ⅛" and/or applying an anti-slip treatment after installation is complete. The hand-textured surface also contributes natural traction underfoot.

### Can I mix Graphite Grey with other zellige colors in the collection?

Yes, you can mix Graphite Grey 4x4 with other colors in Zia's zellige collection. Each colorway is produced using the same traditional methods, so tiles share consistent thickness, format, and surface character across the range. Graphite Grey works particularly well alongside warm whites, cream, and earthy tones in the collection, where the cool grey reads as a grounded counterpoint to warmer fields.

### What design styles suit Graphite Grey zellige?

Graphite Grey zellige suits a wide range of design styles due to its neutral, cool-toned palette. The tile works in modern minimalist interiors where surface subtlety is part of the design direction, as well as in industrial and contemporary settings where the handcrafted character adds warmth to harder materials. It also performs in classical and transitional interiors where the glazed variation reads as handmade depth against more restrained surrounding finishes.
