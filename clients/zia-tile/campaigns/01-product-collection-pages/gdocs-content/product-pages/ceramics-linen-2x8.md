## How It's Made — \[ACCORDION\]

Zia's Linen 2x8 is produced outside Nagoya, Japan, in a region where ceramic production has been practiced for over 1,000 years. The process begins with carefully selected raw materials that are refined and formed through an extrusion process to achieve the consistent 2x8 format.

Once formed, tiles are dried under controlled conditions before being fired at high temperatures. The high-firing process is what gives the tile its density, low porosity, and long-term durability. The matte finish is achieved through a controlled glazing process that produces a non-reflective ivory surface.

Minor shifts in tone and surface texture emerge during firing, giving each tile a measured degree of variation while maintaining a consistent overall appearance. The handcrafted quality of the production process is most visible up close, where the subtle variation in surface texture comes through.

## Frequently Asked Questions — \[ACCORDION\]

### What is matte ceramic tile?

Matte ceramic tile is a ceramic tile with a low-sheen, non-reflective surface produced through a controlled glazing process. The matte finish absorbs light rather than reflecting it, producing a soft, understated surface that is easier to maintain in high-use areas. Matte ceramic also offers better traction than a glossy finish, making it well-suited for floor and wet-area applications.

### Is matte ceramic tile good for a bathroom?

Yes, matte ceramic tile works well in a bathroom. The non-reflective surface provides better traction than a glossy finish and is better at concealing water spots and everyday wear. For shower floors, slip resistance can be increased by sizing grout joints to a minimum of ⅛" and/or applying an anti-slip treatment after installation.

### Does the Linen 2x8 require sealing?

Yes, the Linen 2x8 is a matte finish ceramic and must be sealed before grouting using 511 Porous Plus as a grout release. This step prevents grout haze and staining on the matte surface. Apply 511 Porous Plus in a thin layer over the tile surface, wipe off any excess before it dries, and allow it to cure fully before grouting.

### What is the difference between ceramic and porcelain tile?

The difference between ceramic and porcelain tile comes down to density, water absorption, and the conditions each material is suited for. Ceramic tile is high-fired from natural clay and is well-suited to interior walls and floors, including wet areas and pools. Porcelain tile is fired at higher temperatures using denser clay, resulting in lower water absorption, which allows it to perform in exterior floor applications and more demanding environments.

### How do I clean matte ceramic tile?

To clean matte ceramic tile, use a pH-neutral cleaner and warm water for routine maintenance. Sweep or vacuum regularly to remove abrasive debris that can scratch the surface. Avoid acidic or abrasive cleaning products, as these can affect the matte glaze over time. For heavier staining, Miracle Sealants Heavy Duty Cleaner (Acid Substitute) is the recommended product.

### How does the 2x8 format work in an installation?

The 2x8 format is an elongated subway tile that introduces a strong directional quality to the surface. Installed horizontally, it emphasizes the width of the surface and works well on backsplashes and lower wall applications. Installed vertically, it draws the eye upward and is commonly used in shower enclosures and floor-to-ceiling wall applications. The format also lends itself to herringbone and stacked arrangements, each of which changes how the tile reads across the surface. Agreeing on orientation and pattern with your installer before laying out the tile is an important step in the preparation process.
