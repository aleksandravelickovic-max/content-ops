## How It's Made — \[ACCORDION\]

Zia's Racing Green zellige tiles are handmade by Moroccan artisans using traditional methods refined over centuries.

Artisans begin with raw clay mined from local hillsides, which they soak in water and filter to remove impurities. The clay becomes soft and malleable, hand-pressed into molds and left to dry in the Moroccan sun. Each tile is then flattened and shaped by hand before being left to dry further.

Each piece is hand-dipped in glaze and placed in an earthen kiln. Heat distributes unevenly throughout the kiln, producing the dynamic glazes, surface variation, and characteristic marks that define each Racing Green 4x4. The chips, pits, and crazing are the result of this process, not incidental to it.

## Frequently Asked Questions — \[ACCORDION\]

### Does each Racing Green 4x4 tile arrive with a consistent thickness?

No, each Racing Green 4x4 tile does not arrive with a consistent thickness. Artisans shape each Racing Green zellige tile by hand, which results in innate variation in thickness, shape, and color. Expect this variation across the order. It is what allows your installer to create the organic, layered surface that defines a traditional zellige aesthetic.

### What grout color works best with Racing Green zellige?

Mapei Ultracolor Plus FA works well with glazed zellige. For Racing Green, a grout color matched closely to the tile tends to produce the most cohesive result, allowing the deep green tones to read across the surface without interruption. Contrasting grout colors can stain the glazed surface and may be difficult to remove if excess grout is not wiped away promptly.

### What materials pair well with Racing Green zellige?

Racing Green zellige pairs well with materials that allow the tile's color and variation to remain the focal point. Wood cabinetry, natural stone surfaces, and warm metal finishes complement the tile without competing with it.

### Do Racing Green zellige tiles need grout?

Yes, Racing Green zellige tiles require grout joints. Grout joints protect the tile edges, accommodate the innate variation in thickness and shape across each handmade tile, and support the long-term durability of the installation. Your installer can advise on joint width based on your chosen layout.

### Should Racing Green zellige tiles be uneven?

Yes. Variation in size, edge definition, and surface texture is innate to handmade zellige. Each tile is shaped by hand and fired in an earthen kiln where heat distributes unevenly, which is why no two tiles read identically. This variation is the defining characteristic of an authentic zellige installation, not a quality concern.

### Is Racing Green zellige high maintenance?

Racing Green zellige is not high maintenance when properly installed and sealed. The glazed surface can be maintained with a pH-neutral cleaner such as Miracle Sealants pH Neutral Tile and Stone Cleaner. In wet areas, re-seal at least every one to two years to maintain protection.
