## How It's Made — \[ACCORDION\]

Each Rouge 4x4 follows the same production process as all traditional zellige. Artisans source raw clay from Moroccan hillsides, soak it in water, filter it for impurities, and work it until it reaches a consistency suitable for hand-pressing into molds.

Once formed, the tiles dry in the Moroccan sun. Each piece is shaped and flattened by hand before being dipped individually in glaze. The Rouge glaze develops its depth at this stage, with the deep red tones settling differently on each tile. No two dips produce identical results.

The glazed tiles are placed in earthen kilns, where heat moves unevenly through the chamber. The chips, pits, crazing, and tonal shifts that result from that unevenness are what make each Rouge 4x4 perfectly imperfect and entirely its own.

## Frequently Asked Questions — \[ACCORDION\]

### What color is Rouge zellige?

Rouge zellige is a deep red zellige with a glaze that shifts in tone depending on the light. The depth of the red varies across tiles due to the hand-dipping process and the uneven heat of the kiln, which means no two tiles carry exactly the same tone. This variation is what gives a Rouge installation its character.

### What design styles suit Rouge zellige?

Rouge zellige works well in interiors where a single bold surface is the design anchor. The deep red glaze sits naturally in Moroccan, Mediterranean, and maximalist spaces where layered color and handcrafted materials are part of the design language. It also works in more restrained contemporary interiors where the Rouge surface provides contrast against neutral tones and clean lines.

### What grout color works best with Rouge zellige?

Mapei Ultracolor Plus FA works well with glazed zellige. For Rouge, a grout color matched closely to the tile tends to produce the most cohesive result, allowing the deep red tones to read across the surface without interruption. A lighter or contrasting grout will draw more attention to the individual tile and the variation between pieces. Contrasting grout colors can stain the glazed surface and may be difficult to remove if excess grout is not wiped away promptly.

### Can Rouge zellige be used in a shower?

Yes, our Rouge zellige 4x4 is suitable for shower walls, shower floors, and steam showers. Sealing is required for all shower applications. Soak glazed zellige tiles in room-temperature water for one to two minutes before setting to ensure proper adhesion. For shower floors, slip resistance can be increased by sizing grout joints to a minimum of ⅛" and/or applying an anti-slip treatment after installation is complete.

### What materials pair well with Rouge zellige?

Rouge zellige pairs well with materials that let the depth of the red glaze lead. Natural stone, matte ceramic, and terrazzo in neutral tones sit naturally alongside it. In the surrounding space, unlacquered brass and dark wood tend to complement the deep red tones without competing with the surface variation.

### Does the Rouge colorway vary between orders?

Yes, the Rouge colorway can vary between production runs. Each batch of zellige is produced by hand, and the clay, glaze, and kiln conditions shift from one run to the next. Tiles from separate orders may differ in tone and surface character. If your project requires tiles from multiple orders, ordering the full quantity at once gives the most consistent result across the installation.
