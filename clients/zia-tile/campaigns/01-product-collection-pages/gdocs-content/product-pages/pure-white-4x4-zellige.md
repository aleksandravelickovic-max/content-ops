Zellige installation has more in common with masonry than with conventional tile work, given the irregular nature of handmade material. ## How It's Made -- \[ACCORDION\]

Our Pure White 4x4 zellige tiles are made by Moroccan artisans whose technique has been passed down across generations, using methods that produce tiles with both lasting quality and innate variation.

The process begins with raw clay drawn from local Moroccan hillsides, which artisans soak in water and filter for impurities. Once the clay reaches a workable consistency, it is hand-pressed into molds and set out under the Moroccan sun to dry. Each Pure White 4x4 is then flattened and shaped by hand before drying further.

Each tile is hand-dipped in glaze and placed into an earthen kiln, where heat moves unevenly through the chamber. That uneven distribution is what produces the dynamic glazes and surface variation that define the collection. Each Pure White 4x4 emerges as a quiet record of the artisan's day at the kiln--imperfect, characterful, and entirely its own.

## Frequently Asked Questions -- \[ACCORDION\]

### Will Pure White zellige look uniform once installed?

No, each Pure White 4x4 is shaped by hand, which produces variation in thickness, shape, and color across the collection. These differences are part of what allows your installer to build the organic surface that defines a traditional zellige finish.

### How does lighting affect the appearance of Pure White zellige?

Lighting changes how Pure White zellige reads once installed. Light shifts across the day will draw out variation in tone, texture, and glaze reflection on the surface of each tile. Reviewing samples in your actual space before installation gives you a clear sense of how the tile will respond to your lighting conditions.

### Do I need to seal Pure White zellige before installation?

Yes, Pure White zellige must be sealed for any application. Always refer to the [Zellige Installation Guide](https://ziatile.com/pages/zellige-installation-guide) for specific sealer recommendations and application steps.

### Can I combine different layouts using Pure White 4x4 tiles?

Yes, our Pure White 4x4 supports a range of layouts, including straight stack and offset. Each layout draws out the tile's variation differently across the field. Planning your layout in advance allows you to control how chips, pits, and crazing read across the finished installation.

### Is Pure White zellige suitable for high-traffic floors?

Yes, Pure White zellige performs well on high-traffic floors when properly installed. In wet-area commercial floor applications, slip resistance can be increased by sizing grout joints to a minimum of 1/8 inch and/or applying an anti-slip treatment after installation is complete.

### Why do some Pure White 4x4 tiles have fine lines on the surface?

Fine lines on the glazed surface of Pure White zellige, known as crazing, occur naturally during the kiln firing process. These characteristic marks, along with occasional chips and pits, are innate to handmade Moroccan tile and do not affect the structural integrity of the tile.
