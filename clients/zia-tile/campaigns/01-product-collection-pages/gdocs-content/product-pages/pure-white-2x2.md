## How It's Made 

Our Pure White 2x2 carries the legacy of Moroccan craftsmanship in a small 2x2 format, produced using the same traditional methods artisans have refined over centuries. The clay is pulled from the hillsides surrounding Fez, Morocco, soaked in water, and filtered for impurities before being worked by hand into the compact 2x2 molds that shape each element of the mosaic.

  

After partial curing in the Moroccan sun, each tile is shaped again by hand to refine its edges. The edges remain intentionally irregular, which is what allows zellige to be set edge-to-edge or with minimal grout joints, depending on the layout. Artisans then hand-dip each piece in a pure white glaze. The glaze pools and settles differently across each element, producing the shifts from opaque white to softer, translucent areas where the clay body subtly influences tone.

  

The final stage is the kiln. Earthen kilns do not distribute heat evenly, and the results of that unevenness are not incidental. Fine surface crazing develops as the glaze and clay body expand and contract at different rates during firing, and the chips, pits, and tonal shifts that emerge are what make each Pure White 2x2 mosaic its own.

### What is white Zellige tile?

White zellige tile is a handcrafted Moroccan clay tile finished with a hand-dipped glaze that preserves natural irregularities in color, edge, and surface. Each tile is formed, glazed, and fired individually, producing inherent variation in tone and texture across the installation. Zia's Pure White 2x2 is a white Moroccan tile that reflects light through its hand-dipped glaze, shifting between soft white and subtle clay undertones depending on viewing angle and layout.

### Is the Pure White 2x2 Zellige tile good for floors?

Yes, Pure White 2x2 zellige can be used on floors in both low- and high-traffic areas. The mosaic format contributes to slip resistance through higher grout joint density, which makes it particularly appropriate for shower floors and wet-area installations. For wet floor applications, increase grout joints to a minimum of ⅛" and/or apply an anti-slip treatment after installation to improve traction.

### What tile to pair with Pure White Zellige?

Pure White 2x2 pairs well with materials that introduce contrast or tonal depth. Natural stone such as travertine, limestone, and marble provides visual grounding, while warm woods balance the brightness of the glaze. Darker colorways from Zia's zellige collection can be used to define borders or patterned layouts, particularly in kitchens and bathrooms, where a white field frames a deeper accent color.

### Can I install the Pure White Zellige tile in the kitchen?

Yes, Pure White 2x2 is commonly installed as a kitchen backsplash, where the reflective glaze responds well to both natural and artificial light. The small 2x2 format and close-set installation create the dense, mosaic-like field associated with traditional Moroccan zellige. Because the material is porous beneath the glaze, proper sealing and routine cleaning are required in areas exposed to cooking oils, steam, and heat.

### Are white zellige tiles difficult to clean?

No, zellige tiles are not difficult to clean. The glazed surface of Pure White zellige resists most surface staining, though the irregular edges and slight surface undulations can collect residue if left uncleaned. A pH-neutral cleaner and a soft cloth are recommended for routine maintenance. Avoid harsh acids, bleach, and abrasive pads, as these can affect the glaze over time.

### Can I mix Pure White with other zellige colors in the collection?

Yes, you can mix Pure White 2x2 with other colors in Zia's zellige collection. Each colorway is produced using the same traditional methods, so tiles and mosaic sheets share consistent surface character across the range. Pure White works particularly well alongside deeper solid colorways, where the white mosaic frames or counterpoints a saturated field, and pairs naturally with warm neutrals where a more cohesive, tonal composition is the design direction.
