## How It's Made — \[ACCORDION\]

Production starts where all traditional zellige begins. Artisans source raw clay from local Moroccan hillsides, soak it in water, filter it for impurities, and work it by hand until it reaches a consistency suitable for pressing into molds.

  

Once formed, the tiles dry in the Moroccan sun. Each piece is shaped and flattened by hand before being dipped individually in glaze. The Amber glaze develops its honey tones at this stage, settling differently on each tile as the glaze meets the clay surface. No two dips produce identical results.

  

Firing takes place in earthen kilns, where heat moves unevenly through the chamber. The chips, pits, crazing, and tonal shifts that emerge from the firing process are what make each Amber 4x4 perfectly imperfect and entirely its own.

## Frequently Asked Questions — \[ACCORDION\]  

### What color is Amber zellige?

Amber zellige is a warm honey-toned neutral with a glaze that shifts in tone depending on the light. The warmth of the Amber glaze varies across tiles due to the hand-dipping process and the uneven heat of the kiln, which means no two tiles carry exactly the same tone. This variation is what gives an Amber installation its character.

### What grout color works best with Amber zellige?

Mapei Ultracolor Plus FA works well with glazed zellige. For Amber, a grout color matched closely to the tile tends to produce the most cohesive result. A lighter neutral grout will draw more attention to the individual tile and the variation between pieces. Contrasting grout colors can stain the glazed surface and may be difficult to remove if excess grout is not wiped away promptly.

### Can Amber zellige be used in a shower?

Yes, our Amber zellige 4x4 is suitable for shower walls, shower floors, and steam showers. Sealing is required for all shower applications. Soak glazed zellige tiles in room-temperature water for one to two minutes before setting to ensure proper adhesion. For shower floors, slip resistance can be increased by sizing grout joints to a minimum of ⅛" and/or applying an anti-slip treatment after installation is complete.

### Does the Amber colorway vary between orders?

Yes, the Amber colorway can vary between production runs. Each batch of zellige is produced by hand, and the clay, glaze, and kiln conditions shift from one run to the next. Tiles from separate orders may differ in tone and surface character. 

### How does Amber zellige compare to Desert Bloom zellige?

Amber and Desert Bloom are both warm-toned zellige colorways. Amber carries honey tones that read as a warm neutral, grounded enough to work alongside a wide range of palettes. Desert Bloom moves into pink territory and reads more as a color statement than a neutral. The choice between the two depends on whether a warm neutral foundation or a warm color-forward surface is the design intent.

### Is Amber zellige warm or cool-toned?

Amber zellige is warm-toned. The glaze sits in the warm part of the neutral spectrum, pulling toward honey and amber. Under natural light, the warmth is most apparent. Under cooler artificial light, the tone softens slightly, but the warm character of the colorway remains consistent across lighting conditions.

### How do I protect Amber zellige in a high-use kitchen?

To protect Amber zellige in a high-use kitchen, ensure the tile is properly sealed before grouting and that grout lines are sealed after installation. Clean the surface regularly with a pH-neutral cleaner such as Miracle Sealants pH Neutral Tile and Stone Cleaner, and address spills promptly by blotting rather than wiping. Avoid acidic or abrasive cleaning products, including vinegar and bleach, as these will damage the glaze over time.
