## How It's Made

The clay that becomes each Tidepool Trapezoid is pulled from the hillsides surrounding Fez, Morocco. Artisans soak it in water, filter it for impurities, and work it by hand until it reaches the right consistency for pressing into trapezoid molds. Each tile is then shaped again by hand to refine its edges and surface, carrying the directional geometry of the trapezoid format into a handcrafted Moroccan tile.

  

After drying in the Moroccan sun, each tile is dipped individually in glaze. The Tidepool glaze is where the kelpy greens and seafoam blues take form. Because each dip is done by hand, the coverage varies from tile to tile, and that variation is what produces the multi-tonal surface movement visible across a full installation.

  

The final stage is the kiln. Earthen kilns do not distribute heat evenly, and the results of that unevenness are not incidental. The chips, pits, crazing, and shifting tones that emerge from the firing process are what make each Tidepool Trapezoid its own.

## Frequently Asked Questions 

### What is Tidepool zellige?

Zia's Tidepool trapezoid is a handcrafted Moroccan zellige produced in a directional trapezoid format, where the glaze reads as a marriage of sea and shore. The surface moves between kelpy greens and seafoam blues. While artisans historically installed zellige in mosaics featuring decorative geometric and floral shapes within elaborately designed palaces, our Tidepool Trapezoid brings that heritage into contemporary kitchens, bathrooms, and commercial interiors where the angled geometry carries directional movement across the surface.

### Can Tidepool trapezoid zellige be used in a pool?

Yes, Tidepool trapezoid zellige can be used in pools and spas in both saltwater and chlorine environments. The format performs particularly well on waterlines, vertical pool walls, and spa interiors, where the hand-chiseled texture and directional geometry read as deliberate design elements. Sealing is required for all pool and spa installations. Apply 511 Porous Plus to all tiles and grout lines exposed to continuous moisture after grouting is complete.

### Does Tidepool zellige require sealing?

Yes, Tidepool zellige requires sealing in all wet applications, including showers, pools, and spas. A penetrating sealer should be applied to both the tile and grout lines after grouting is complete to protect against continuous moisture and chemical exposure. In dry interior applications, sealing is not required for the tile surface itself, though grout protection remains recommended based on use conditions.

### What design materials pair well with Tidepool zellige?

Tidepool zellige pairs well with natural materials that balance its multi-tonal glaze. Light oak, unlacquered brass, and matte black fixtures create contrast against the reflective surface, while muted stone and cement finishes let the glaze variation remain the visual center of the composition. The tile also works alongside deeper neutrals where the teal undertones sit in deliberate counterpoint to warmer materials.

### How does the trapezoid format affect the final layout?

The trapezoid format affects the final layout by introducing directional movement that a square or rectangular tile does not carry. Depending on how the angled edges are oriented, the format can produce chevron patterns, herringbone variations, or linear diagonal runs. Because the geometry depends on consistent orientation, a full dry layout before installation is essential to confirm how the pattern resolves across the field.

### Can I mix Tidepool with other zellige colors in the collection?

Yes, you can mix Tidepool with other colors in Zia's zellige collection. Each colorway is produced using the same traditional methods, so tiles share consistent surface character across the range. The multi-tonal teal of Tidepool works particularly well alongside neutral whites and earthy tones in the collection, where the cooler glaze reads as a focal point against a quieter surrounding palette.
