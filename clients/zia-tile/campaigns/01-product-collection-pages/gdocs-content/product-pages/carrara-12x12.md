## About

### Inherent Variation

  - Our Carrara 12x12 is a white Italian marble with soft grey veining that shifts in direction and density from tile to tile.
  - Each Carrara tile carries its own geological history, and this inherent variation defines the line's character.
  - Expect significant variation in veining pattern, tonal shift, and surface character across the layout, along with the minor imperfections that are natural to quarried stone.

### Preparation and Curation

  - Mix tiles from multiple boxes before installation to distribute veining and tonal variation evenly across the layout.
  - Lay out the full composition before setting to assess how the veining reads across the field. Marble has a face and an underside that are not reversible; confirm each tile is oriented face up.
  - Disperse more pronounced veining evenly rather than clustering it in one area of the layout.
  - We suggest a 15-20% overage on all marble orders. For a more uniform look, we recommend 25% overage.

### Installation and Finishing

  - Installers must always wear protective face and eye coverings when working with marble, thinset, sealants, and grout in accordance with California Prop 65.
  - For finishing, bring the drywall flush with the finished surface of the stone, miter exposed edges at 45 degrees, or complete the edge with Schluter strips.
  - Seal Carrara 12x12 with 511 Porous Plus before and after grouting. For interior applications, finish with Fila Matte Wax as a final coat. For exterior applications, apply a second coat of 511 Porous Plus in place of wax.
  - Grout in small sections no larger than 2'x2' and wipe away excess immediately. Marble stains readily if grout is left to sit on the surface.
  - For installation questions, contact our team at info@ziatile.com or 310-844-1170.

## How It's Made

Carrara marble is quarried in Italy. The white stone with soft grey veining that characterizes this colorway forms through millions of years of geological pressure and mineral movement, and the patterns that emerge in each tile are a direct record of that process.

  

Our Carrara 12x12 is cut from solid blocks of stone and finished by skilled Italian masons. The cutting process reveals the veining that was previously hidden within the block, and the finishing stage establishes the surface character of the tile. Because the veining is inherent to the stone rather than applied, no two tiles carry identical patterns, and the full visual range of a Carrara installation only emerges once the tiles are set together on the surface.

  

The minor variations in size, tone, and surface texture present in each shipment are characteristics of natural stone, not evidence of inconsistent production. They are part of what distinguishes quarried marble from manufactured alternatives.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can Carrara 12x12 be used on a shower floor?

Yes, Carrara 12x12 can be used on a shower floor, shower walls, and steam showers. Sealing is required for all shower applications to protect the natural stone from continuous moisture exposure. Slip resistance can be increased by sizing grout joints to a minimum of 1/8" and/or applying an anti-slip product after installation is complete.

### How should I seal Carrara marble?

To seal Carrara marble, apply 511 Porous Plus before grouting until the stone is fully saturated, wiping off any excess before it dries. Allow the sealer to cure fully, then grout in sections no larger than 2'x2' and wipe away excess immediately. After the grout has cured, apply a second coat of 511 Porous Plus. For interior applications, finish with Fila Matte Wax as a final protective coat. For exterior walls, apply a second coat of 511 Porous Plus in place of wax.

### What grout color works best with Carrara?

Grout color is a design decision that affects how Carrara reads across the installation. A grout matched closely to the stone produces a quiet composition where the veining reads as the primary visual element. A slightly darker grey grout emphasizes the grid of the 12x12 format and can echo the grey tones already present in the veining. Avoid strong contrasting grout in wet areas, as it can stain the marble surface if applied across the full field.

### How do I clean Carrara marble?

To clean Carrara marble, use a soft cloth or mop with a pH-neutral stone cleaner for routine maintenance. Wipe spills immediately, particularly anything acidic such as wine, citrus, or vinegar, which can etch the stone's surface. Sweep or vacuum regularly to remove abrasive debris that can scratch the finish. Never use acidic cleaners or abrasive scrubbers on marble, as these will damage the surface permanently.

### Can I mix Carrara 12x12 with other marble colorways?

Yes, you can mix Carrara 12x12 with other marble colorways in Zia's collection. Each colorway is quarried and finished using consistent methods, so tiles share comparable thickness and format across the range. Carrara pairs particularly well with Nero Marquina for graphic contrast, or with Grigio Carnico for a more tonal composition that stays within a grey range. A dry layout helps confirm how the materials interact across the field.

### What should I expect from natural veining variation?

Natural veining variation in Carrara can range from barely visible soft grey threads to more pronounced directional patterns across a single tile. Because the veining is formed by geological movement rather than applied to the surface, each tile will differ from the next. The full visual range of the material only becomes apparent when multiple tiles are set together, which is why the dry layout stage is important for Carrara installations.
