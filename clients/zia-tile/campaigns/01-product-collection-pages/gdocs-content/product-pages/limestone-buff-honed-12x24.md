## How It's Made — \[ACCORDION\]

Zia's Buff Honed Limestone 12x24 is quarried outside of Fez, Morocco, where local stone masons have worked this material for generations. Each block is formed over millennia from compressed sedimentary deposits, carrying color, tone, and surface variation that no cutting or finishing process removes.

  

Once quarried, blocks are cut into individual tiles at the 12x24 format. The surface is then mechanically ground with progressively finer abrasives until it reaches a smooth, flat, matte result. The honing process does not seal the stone. It reveals it, leaving natural markings and tonal shifts intact.

  

Over time, foot traffic and exposure will develop a soft patina on the surface. This is expected behavior for limestone and is considered part of its character in a long-term installation.

## Frequently Asked Questions — \[ACCORDION\]  

### What does honed limestone mean?

Honed limestone means the stone's surface has been mechanically ground with fine abrasives to produce a smooth, flat, matte finish. The process removes surface irregularities without introducing a reflective sheen, leaving the stone's natural color and markings visible. The result is a practical surface suited to a wide range of floor and wall applications.

### What is the difference between honed and bush-hammered limestone?

The difference between honed and bush-hammered limestone comes down to surface treatment. Honed limestone is ground smooth, producing a consistent, flat, matte surface. Bush-hammered limestone is finished with a high-impact tool that uniformly pits the surface, creating a rougher, more textured result. Honed finishes work well across a wide range of interior applications. Bush-hammered finishes are typically chosen where a more tactile, rustic surface is preferred.

### Is honed limestone suitable for a bathroom floor?

Yes, honed limestone is suitable for a bathroom floor. The matte surface provides better traction than polished stone and is better at concealing water spots and minor surface wear. Sealing is required before grouting and should be maintained as often as needed, depending on traffic and moisture exposure.

### How do I seal honed limestone?

To seal honed limestone, clean and dry the surface thoroughly before applying 511 Porous Plus in thin layers until the stone is fully saturated. Wipe off any excess before it dries. Over-application can result in an uneven film on the surface. Allow the sealer to cure fully before proceeding to grouting.

### How do I clean honed limestone?

To clean honed limestone, mop with warm water and a pH-neutral cleaner for routine maintenance. Sweep or vacuum regularly to remove grit and debris that can scratch the surface. Blot spills as soon as they occur rather than wiping them across the stone. For stubborn staining, Miracle Sealants Heavy Duty Cleaner (Acid Substitute) is the recommended product. Acidic cleaners, including vinegar, lemon juice, and bleach, should never be used on limestone as they will etch and permanently damage the surface.

### Does the 12x24 format work differently from smaller limestone formats?

The 12x24 format produces a more continuous, expansive surface than smaller formats. Fewer grout joints means the stone's tonal variation reads across a wider field, which works particularly well on large floors and floor-to-ceiling wall cladding. The format also reduces installation time on large surfaces.
