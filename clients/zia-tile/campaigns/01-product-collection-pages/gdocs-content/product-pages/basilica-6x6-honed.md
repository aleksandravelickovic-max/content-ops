## How It's Made

Limestone is a sedimentary stone formed through the compression of marine minerals and organic material over geological time. Our Basilica 6x6 is quarried near Fez, Morocco, where large natural stone blocks are extracted and selected for structural consistency before fabrication. Each block is then cut into 6x6 tile formats by local stone masons, preserving the internal grain and tonal movement of the stone.

  

The surface is finished through controlled mechanical abrasion into a honed plane. The honing process produces a smooth, matte face that softens natural variation while keeping the stone's mineral detail visible. Unlike polished limestone, which reads as reflective and glossy, the honed finish absorbs light rather than bouncing it.

  

Moderate variation in tone, fossil markings, and subtle veining remains across each Basilica 6x6 because limestone is extracted rather than manufactured. These characteristics cannot be replicated through artificial surface treatments, and they define the material's behavior across every installation.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What color is Basilica limestone?

Basilica limestone is a cool grey natural stone with subtle tonal shifts across each piece. As a honed limestone tile, the surface is smooth and matte, which softens reflectivity. Natural variation is present across each tile, including slight movement in tone and the mineral markings inherent to sedimentary stone, and these characteristics become part of the installed surface.

### Is Basilica 6x6 suitable for an entryway?

Yes, Basilica 6x6 is well-suited to an entryway. The dense natural stone structure supports high-traffic use, and the matte honed finish helps mask everyday wear more effectively than a polished surface. Installed in an entry zone, the stone develops a natural patina over time that adds depth to the surface without compromising structural integrity.

### What design styles suit honed limestone?

Honed limestone works across modern, transitional, and classical interiors because it stays visually quiet while still contributing material depth. In contemporary spaces, the smooth matte surface supports minimal layouts with clean architectural lines. In traditional and rustic environments, the stone introduces softness and earthy texture without visual heaviness. The consistency of the honed finish is what makes it versatile across these different design directions.

### What is the difference between honed and polished limestone?

The difference between honed and polished limestone is primarily in surface finish and light reflection. Honed limestone has a matte, smooth surface that minimizes reflectivity and helps conceal minor wear, which makes it well-suited to flooring in active areas. Polished limestone is ground further to produce a glossy, reflective surface that intensifies color but reveals scratches and etching more readily. The choice depends on how reflective the surface should be and how visible everyday wear is allowed to become.

### Can Basilica 6x6 be used outdoors?

Yes, Basilica 6x6 can be used outdoors in climates without freeze/thaw conditions. The stone is suitable for exterior floor and wall applications in stable climates when properly sealed, making it appropriate for patios, covered outdoor kitchens, and vertical exterior cladding. For exterior installations, apply a second coat of 511 Porous Plus in place of Fila Matte Wax, as UV exposure causes wax to discolor over time. Basilica is not suitable for outdoor installations in freeze/thaw climates.

### How do I care for Basilica limestone?

To care for Basilica limestone, seal the stone before and after grouting to reduce absorption and protect against staining. Ongoing maintenance requires a pH-neutral stone cleaner and regular dry removal of dust and grit to prevent surface abrasion. Avoid acidic and abrasive products, including vinegar, bleach, and citrus-based cleaners, which can etch the stone permanently. Periodic resealing is required based on traffic and exposure, particularly for floors in high-use areas.
