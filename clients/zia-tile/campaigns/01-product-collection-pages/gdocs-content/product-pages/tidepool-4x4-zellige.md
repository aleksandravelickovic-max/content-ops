## How It's Made — \[ACCORDION\]

The process behind each Tidepool 4x4 has not changed in centuries. Raw clay is pulled from Moroccan hillsides, soaked, filtered, and worked by hand until it holds the right consistency for pressing into molds. The shaping is done entirely by hand, which is why no two tiles sit at exactly the same depth or carry exactly the same edge.



Glazing is where the Tidepool colorway takes form. Each tile is dipped individually, and the blue-green tones develop in the moment the glaze meets the clay surface. The coverage is never perfectly uniform, and that irregularity is what produces the tonal range visible across a full installation.



The final stage is the kiln. Earthen kilns do not distribute heat evenly, and the variation that results is not incidental. The chips, pits, crazing, and shifting tones that characterize each Tidepool 4x4 are a direct product of that heat, that day, in that kiln.

## Frequently Asked Questions — \[ACCORDION\]

### Why is Zellige tile so popular?

Zellige tile is popular for its handcrafted Moroccan origin and its perfectly imperfect aesthetic. Each Tidepool zellige tile features inherent variation in color, shape, and glaze, producing a surface that shifts with changing light and reads differently across a full installation.

### Can Tidepool zellige be used in a pool or spa?

Yes, our Tidepool zellige 4x4 functions reliably in both saltwater and chlorine environments. We recommend sealing the tile for pool and spa installations. Apply 511 Porous Plus to all tiles and grout lines exposed to continuous moisture after grouting is complete. Consult the Zellige Installation Guide for full sealing instructions.

### What tiles pair well with Tidepool zellige?

Tidepool zellige pairs well with materials that keep the surrounding palette restrained, so the blue-green tonal range has room to read. In the surrounding space, unlacquered brass and natural wood complement the glaze without competing with the surface variation.

### Does zellige tile work with radiant heating?

Yes, zellige tile is compatible with radiant heating systems, including heated floor installations. No additional setting materials are required beyond standard zellige installation practice. Consult the Zellige Installation Guide for full installation instructions.

### Can Tidepool zellige be used on exterior walls?

Yes, Tidepool zellige can be used on exterior walls in non-freeze/thaw climates. It is not suitable for exterior wall installations in climates that experience freezing temperatures. For exterior applications, consult the Zellige Installation Guide for appropriate setting materials and sealing requirements.

### Does the Tidepool colorway vary between orders?

Yes, the Tidepool colorway can vary between orders. Each batch of zellige is produced by hand, and the clay, glaze, and kiln conditions shift from one production run to the next. This means tiles from separate orders may differ in tone, depth, and surface character. If your project requires tiles from multiple orders, we recommend ordering the full quantity at once to ensure the most consistent result possible.
