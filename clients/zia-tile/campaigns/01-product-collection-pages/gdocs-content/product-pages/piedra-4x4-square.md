### Installation and Finishing

  - All installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with Cotto Allende, thinset, sealants, and grout in accordance with California Prop 65.
  - For finishing, bring the drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile, miter exposed edges at 45 degrees, or complete the edge with Schluter strips.
  - Seal Piedra 4x4 with 511 Porous Plus before and after grouting. Fila Matte Wax is not recommended for Cotto Allende; use 511 Porous Plus as the sole sealer.
  - Match grout color closely to the tile to integrate the crazing into the composition. Contrasting grout can stain the glazed surface and emphasizes crazing, and should be avoided across the full field.
  - Always seal Piedra 4x4 according to the Zia Tile Installation Guide, whether in dry spaces or in areas exposed to moisture, such as pools, spas, and showers.

## How It's Made

Zia's Piedra 4x4 Square Cotto Allende is produced in San Miguel de Allende, Mexico, using locally sourced natural clay selected for mineral consistency and structural stability. The clay is soaked in water, filtered, and refined to remove impurities before being brought to a workable state for forming.



Each batch is hand-pressed into 4x4 molds, where the tile takes shape through direct compression rather than mechanical extrusion. After forming, each tile is removed and placed in open-air drying conditions for 1 to 2 weeks. This drying phase allows moisture to release gradually, stabilizing density and reducing internal stress before firing.



A glaze is applied to the surface prior to kiln firing, interacting directly with the terra cotta body during high-temperature exposure. The kiln environment does not distribute heat evenly, which produces controlled variation in glaze response, surface tone, and edge definition. This firing process is responsible for the blue-grey tonal range and surface movement that add fluidity to terracotta tile flooring installations.



Chips, pits, and crazing form during drying and firing and remain visible in the finished tile. These characteristics are expected outcomes of the production process and reflect the behavior of handmade terracotta tile formed under traditional firing conditions.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is Piedra 4x4 Cotto Allende?

Piedra 4x4 Cotto Allende is a glazed terra cotta tile made from natural clay and produced in San Miguel de Allende, Mexico. Each tile is hand-pressed, sun-dried, and high-fired with a glaze applied before firing. This process creates a moody blue-grey surface with inherent variation in tone, thickness, and glaze response, along with the chips, pits, and crazing that develop as natural characteristics of handmade terra cotta.

### What color is Piedra terra cotta?

Piedra terra cotta is a moody blue-grey with tonal shifts that range from soft slate to deeper charcoal depending on glaze interaction and kiln variation. Each tile carries subtle movement across its surface, which is why no two pieces read identically within a single installation. This variation is part of what gives the surface its fluid character across a full floor or wall field.

### Is Piedra 4x4 good for a bathroom?

Yes, Piedra 4x4 is suitable for a bathroom, including shower walls, shower floors, and bathroom floors. The glazed surface supports moisture exposure when properly sealed according to installation requirements. For floor applications in wet areas, a 1/8" grout joint is required, and slip resistance can be further increased by maintaining that joint size and/or applying an anti-slip treatment after installation.

### Is terra cotta tile easy to clean?

Yes, terra cotta tile is easy to clean when installed and sealed correctly, though it requires specific care compared to porcelain or standard ceramic surfaces. Routine cleaning should use a pH-neutral cleaner with a soft mop or cloth. Avoid abrasive tools and acidic cleaners, which can affect the glazed surface and make crazing more visible over time. Regular maintenance preserves the material's character and long-term performance.

### How long does terra cotta floor tile last?

Terra cotta floor tile typically lasts for decades when properly installed, sealed, and maintained. Piedra 4x4 develops a natural patina over time while maintaining its structural integrity under normal residential and commercial use. Long-term performance depends on consistent cleaning, correct sealing with 511 Porous Plus at each stage of installation, and adherence to the care guidelines for glazed terra cotta tile.

### Can Piedra 4x4 be mixed with other Cotto Allende colorways?

Piedra 4x4 can be combined with other colorways in the Cotto Allende collection. Each colorway shares the same clay body and firing process, so tiles carry consistent thickness, format, and surface character across the range. The moody blue-grey of Piedra works well alongside warmer colorways such as Cacao and Condesa, where the contrast between cool and warm tones creates a layered composition.
