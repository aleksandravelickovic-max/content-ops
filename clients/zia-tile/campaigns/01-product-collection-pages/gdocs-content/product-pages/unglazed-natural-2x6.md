### Installation and Finishing

  - Installers must always wear protective face and eye coverings when working with zellige, thinset, sealants, and grout in accordance with California Prop 65.
  - While traditional trim and edge pieces do not exist for zellige, we recommend bringing the drywall flush with the finished surface of the tile or finishing your project with mitered edges or Schluter strips.
  - Seal Unglazed Natural 2x6 with 511 Porous Plus before grouting to protect the clay body from grout staining. This is a required step for this colorway.
  - For interior applications, apply Fila Matte Wax as a final coat after grouting. For exterior applications, apply a second coat of 511 Porous Plus in place of wax, as UV exposure causes wax to discolor over time.
  - Use Mapei Flexcolor CQ grout for Unglazed Natural, rather than the Ultracolor Plus FA used for glazed zellige.
  - You may reach out to our team directly at info@ziatile.com with any specific installation questions.

## How It's Made

Our Unglazed Natural 2x6 Zellige follows the process of an authentic Bejmat tile, where material and method define the finished surface. Artisans source raw clay from Moroccan hillsides, then soak and filter it to remove impurities. The clay is hand-pressed into molds and left to dry under the Moroccan sun. Each tile is then hand-shaped, creating irregular edges and varied thickness.



These tiles remain unglazed, leaving the clay body exposed. Each piece is fired in an earthen kiln where uneven heat distribution produces variation in tone and density across the surface. This process creates the full range of inherent variation that defines unglazed Moroccan Zellige tiles. Each tile reflects the conditions of its making, resulting in a surface that accentuates the material's perfectly imperfect character.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does Unglazed Natural differ from glazed zellige?

Unglazed Natural differs from glazed zellige in surface, sealing, and approved applications. The absence of a glaze layer produces a matte, porous surface rather than the reflective hand-dipped finish of glazed colorways, which changes both the visual character and the technical requirements of the tile. Unglazed Natural must be sealed before grouting to protect the exposed clay body, uses Mapei Flexcolor CQ grout rather than Ultracolor Plus FA, and is not approved for pool or spa applications.

### What color is the Unglazed Natural Zellige?

The Unglazed Natural Zellige presents a range of warm, earthy tones drawn directly from the clay body. Each zellige tile remains unglazed, so the surface reads as matte and light-absorbing. Variation in color, tone, shape, thickness, chips, pits, and crazing is inherent, and these characteristics define the final zellige tile pattern across the installation.

### Can Unglazed Natural Zellige be used in a shower?

Yes, Unglazed Natural can be used in Zellige tile shower flooring or wall applications. The tile must be sealed before grouting and again after installation to protect the clay body from moisture exposure. For shower floor installations, increase grout joints to a minimum of 1/8" and/or apply an anti-slip treatment after installation to improve slip resistance.

### Can Unglazed Natural Zellige be used in a pool or spa?

No, Unglazed Natural Zellige is not recommended for pools or spas. The unglazed surface and exposed clay body require sealing but are not intended for continuous submersion. This limitation protects the long-term structural integrity of the tile and ensures the material performs as intended in non-submerged applications.

### What grout color works best with Unglazed Natural zellige?

A grout color that closely matches the tile is recommended for the Unglazed Natural Zellige. This approach allows the surface variation, including chips, pits, and crazing, to read as a continuous field rather than emphasizing grout lines. A contrasting grout will highlight irregular edges and surface characteristics, which may shift the visual balance of the installation.

### Does the Unglazed Natural colorway vary between orders?

Yes, the Unglazed Natural colorway varies between orders. Each batch reflects differences in clay composition, drying conditions, and kiln firing, which influence the final tone. Variation in color, shape, thickness, chips, pits, and crazing should be expected across shipments, and ordering sufficient material at one time helps maintain consistency within a single installation.
