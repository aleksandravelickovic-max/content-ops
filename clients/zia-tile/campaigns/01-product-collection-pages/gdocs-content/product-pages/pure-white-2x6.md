## How It's Made — \[ACCORDION\]

Each Pure White 2x6 is made by Moroccan artisans whose technique has been passed down across generations, producing tiles with both lasting quality and innate variation.

  

The process begins with raw clay drawn from local Moroccan hillsides. Artisans soak the clay in water and filter it for impurities until the material becomes soft and workable, then press it into rectangular molds and set it out under the Moroccan sun to dry. Once the clay has firmed up, each tile is flattened and refined by hand.

  

The tiles are dipped individually in glaze and placed into an earthen kiln, where heat moves unevenly through the chamber. That uneven distribution is what produces the dynamic glazes and tonal range specific to the collection. Each Pure White 2x6 carries the marks of the artisan's day at the kiln.

## Frequently Asked Questions — \[ACCORDION\]  

### What is a Bejmat zellige tile?

A Bejmat zellige tile is a traditional Moroccan zellige format defined by its elongated rectangular shape. The 2x6 proportion supports a wider range of layout patterns than a square format. Running bond, vertical stack, and herringbone each distribute the tile's inherent variation differently across the surface. Zia's Pure White Bejmat zellige is handmade by Moroccan artisans using the same traditional methods as each tile in the zellige collection.

### Can I use Pure White Bejmat zellige in a shower?

Yes, you can install Pure White Bejmat zellige on shower walls and shower floors. Sealing is required for this application. To address slip resistance on shower floors, increase grout joint size to a minimum of ⅛" and/or apply an anti-slip treatment after installation is complete. Refer to the Zellige Installation Guide for sealer recommendations.

### What materials pair well with Pure White Bejmat zellige?

Zia's Pure White Bejmat zellige tile pairs well with natural and refined materials that balance its texture and reflectivity. Pure white zellige complements wood cabinetry, marble or quartz surfaces, and warm metal finishes such as brass, allowing the tile's variation and glaze to remain the focal point of the space.

### Why do some Pure White 2x6 tiles have fine lines on the surface?

Fine lines on the glazed surface of Pure White 2x6 zellige, known as crazing, occur naturally during the earthen kiln firing process. These characteristic marks, along with occasional chips and pits, are innate to handmade Moroccan tile and do not affect the structural integrity of the tile.

### Can I mix Pure White 2x6 zellige with other colors in the collection?

Yes, you can mix Pure White 2x6 zellige with other colors in Zia's handcrafted zellige collection. Artisans use the same traditional Moroccan methods for each shade, allowing you to combine Pure White 2x6 tiles with other tones to create a custom installation that showcases the full range of available dynamic tones.

### Is Pure White Bejmat zellige high maintenance?

Pure White Bejmat zellige is not high maintenance when properly installed and sealed. The glazed surface can be maintained with a pH-neutral cleaner such as Miracle Sealants pH Neutral Tile and Stone Cleaner. In wet areas, re-seal at least every one to two years to maintain protection.
