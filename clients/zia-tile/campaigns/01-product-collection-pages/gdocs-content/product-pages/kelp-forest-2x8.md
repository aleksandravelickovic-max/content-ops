## How It's Made — \[ACCORDION\]

Each Kelp Forest 2x8 is produced in Nagoya, Japan, in a region where ceramic production has been practiced for over 1,000 years. Raw materials are extruded into the 2x8 format and dried under controlled conditions before firing.

  

High-temperature firing gives the tile its density and low porosity. The glossy finish is achieved through a controlled glazing process applied before firing, and the heat of the kiln is where the Kelp Forest color develops its final depth.

  

Minor tonal shifts emerge during firing, giving each tile a measured degree of variation. At installation scale, Kelp Forest reads as a consistent, color-forward surface.

## Frequently Asked Questions — \[ACCORDION\]  

### What color is Kelp Forest ceramic tile?

Kelp Forest ceramic tile is a vibrant green with a glossy finish that reflects light across the surface. The depth of the green shifts slightly depending on the angle and light source. Minor tonal variation between tiles is a natural characteristic of the high-fired production process.

### Is glossy ceramic tile suitable for a bathroom floor?

Glossy ceramic tile can be used on a bathroom floor, but the DCOF rating of 0.30 means slip resistance is lower than a matte finish. For wet floor applications, slip resistance can be increased by sizing grout joints to a minimum of ⅛" and/or applying an anti-slip treatment after installation is complete. For shower floors specifically, a matte finish ceramic with a higher DCOF rating may be a more practical choice.

### What design styles suit Kelp Forest ceramic tile?

Kelp Forest ceramic tile works across a range of design contexts. The vibrant green gloss sits naturally in interiors where color and natural references are part of the design language. It also works well in more restrained settings where a single color-forward surface is the design anchor. 

### What grout color works best with Kelp Forest ceramic tile?

For Kelp Forest, a grout color matched closely to the tile tends to produce the most cohesive result, allowing the green tones to read across the surface without interruption. A lighter or contrasting grout will draw more attention to the individual tile and the grout joint pattern. Consistent open joints of 3/16" are recommended, with a minimum of 1/8".

### How does the 2x8 format work in an installation?

The 2x8 format is an elongated subway tile that introduces a strong directional quality to the surface. Installed horizontally, it emphasizes the width of the surface and works well on backsplashes and lower wall applications. Installed vertically, it draws the eye upward and is commonly used in shower enclosures and floor-to-ceiling wall applications. The format also lends itself to herringbone and stacked arrangements, each of which changes how the tile reads across the surface.

### Can Kelp Forest ceramic tile be used in a pool or spa?

Yes, Kelp Forest ceramic tile is suitable for pools and spas, including saltwater and chlorine environments. Sealing is required for pool and spa installations. Refer to the Zia Tile Installation Guide for recommended sealers and application instructions.
