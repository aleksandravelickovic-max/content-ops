## How It's Made

Our Glazed Earth 2x2 begins in the same place as all traditional zellige. The clay is pulled from the hillsides surrounding Fez, Morocco, soaked in water, and filtered for impurities before being worked by hand until it reaches the right consistency for pressing into molds. 

  

After drying in the Moroccan sun, each element is dipped individually in the Glazed Earth glaze. Because the glaze is applied by hand, coverage varies from piece to piece, and that variation is what produces the tonal range of cream, tan, and earth visible across a full installation. 

  

The final stage is the kiln. Earthen kilns do not distribute heat evenly, and the results of that unevenness are not incidental. The chips, pits, crazing, and shifting tones that emerge from the firing process are what make each Glazed Earth tile its own.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the characteristics of Glazed Earth zellige?

Glazed Earth zellige is a 2x2 handcrafted Moroccan tile defined by its neutral palette and highly varied surface. The surface includes chips, pits, and crazing, which are inherent characteristics of handmade zellige. Irregular edges and tonal movement across each tile produce a grounded, layered surface consistent with traditional Moroccan tilemaking.

### Is Glazed Earth zellige good for floors?

Yes, Glazed Earth zellige can be used on interior floors, particularly in bathrooms and other controlled-traffic areas. The mosaic format performs well underfoot and contributes to slip resistance through higher grout joint density. For wet floor applications, increase grout joint size to a minimum of ⅛" and/or apply an anti-slip treatment after installation. Zellige is not suitable for exterior floor installations in freeze/thaw climates.

### What tiles and materials pair well with Glazed Earth zellige?

Glazed Earth zellige pairs well with soft neutrals such as warm white, cream, and beige, where the tile reads as a continuous tonal field. It also contrasts effectively against white subway tile or deeper solid colorways, where Glazed Earth provides the textured focal point against a cleaner field. Natural materials such as matte wood, honed stone, and unlacquered brass reinforce the tonal depth of the glaze without competing with the surface variation.

### What is a zellige tile backsplash?

A zellige tile backsplash is a surface finished with handcrafted Moroccan tiles installed behind kitchen or bathroom work areas. Each tile is hand-pressed, hand-dipped in glaze, and kiln-fired, producing slight variations in color, surface, and edge definition. The installation is typically close-set or edge-to-edge, which allows the natural irregularity of each tile to resolve into a continuous, light-reflective surface with visible variation across the layout.

### How do I clean zellige tile?

To clean zellige tile, use a soft cloth or sponge with warm water and a pH-neutral cleaner. Avoid acidic or abrasive products such as vinegar, bleach, or rough scrubbing pads, as these can damage the glaze over time. For routine maintenance, wipe surfaces dry after cleaning to prevent mineral buildup. Grout lines should be sealed and maintained separately to preserve the overall installation.

### Can I mix Glazed Earth with other zellige colors in the collection?

Yes, you can mix Glazed Earth 2x2 with other colors in Zia's zellige collection. Each colorway is produced using the same traditional methods, so tiles and mosaic sheets share consistent surface character across the range. The neutral palette of Glazed Earth works particularly well alongside deeper solid colorways, where the mosaic reads as a textural counterpoint to a larger field of color.
