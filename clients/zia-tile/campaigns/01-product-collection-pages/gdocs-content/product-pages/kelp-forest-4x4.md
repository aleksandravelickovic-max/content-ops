## How It's Made

Nagoya, Japan, has been a center of ceramic production for over 1,000 years, and each Kelp Forest 4x4 is inspired by these artisanal ceramic traditions. The process begins with refined clay that is prepared, filtered, and shaped into precise 4x4 molds to ensure consistency in format while preserving the behavior of handmade ceramic material.

  

After forming, each tile is dried under controlled conditions to stabilize the body before firing. The ceramic tiles are then high-fired at temperatures that create density, strength, and low porosity, which are essential characteristics of architectural-grade ceramic tile.

  

The glazed green ceramic tile surface is achieved through a hand-applied glaze that is formulated to respond to kiln heat. Each application is slightly responsive to temperature and placement in the kiln, which produces controlled variation in tone across the surface. This variation is most visible when light interacts with the green ceramic tile at different angles.

  

The firing process defines the final surface quality of the Kelp Forest ceramic tile. Subtle shifts in glaze movement and color depth occur naturally during kiln exposure, reflecting the material behavior that is unique to traditional ceramic production methods.

### What color is the Kelp Forest ceramic tile?

### Can I use the Kelp Forest 4x4 ceramic tile in the bathroom?

Yes, Kelp Forest 4x4 can be mixed with other colorways in Zia's ceramic collection. Yes, the Kelp Forest 4x4 ceramic tile can be used in entryway settings as part of flooring applications. ### How to clean ceramic tile floors?

To clean ceramic tile floors, use a pH-neutral cleaner with warm water and a soft mop or cloth. This preserves the integrity of the glossy glaze and prevents surface residue buildup. Avoid acidic or abrasive cleaners, which can affect the finish over time. Regular maintenance helps maintain the reflective quality of glazed surfaces, especially in high-traffic ceramic tile flooring installations.
