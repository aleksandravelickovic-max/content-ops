## How It's Made

Moonlight 2x8 is produced in a ceramic workshop outside Nagoya, Japan, a region where ceramic tile production has been refined through more than 1,000 years of controlled firing and material discipline. 

  

Each tile begins with refined clay that is formed into a precise 2x8 format using calibrated molds to maintain dimensional consistency across production batches. The formed ceramic tiles are dried under controlled conditions to stabilize moisture content before entering high-temperature kilns. This firing process develops the dense ceramic body required for durable ceramic tile walls, backsplash installations, and flooring applications. 

  

The satin glaze is applied before firing and reacts to kiln heat to produce a smooth, low-sheen surface with stable pale blue and blue-grey tonal behavior. Variation in tone is introduced through kiln exposure rather than surface application. 

  

As tiles move through temperature zones during firing, subtle shifts emerge in glaze response, which become visible when installed across larger ceramic tile floors or continuous wall surfaces.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the color of Moonlight ceramic tile?

Moonlight ceramic is a pale blue with cool grey undertones with a smooth satin finish that brings a quiet presence to the installation. Each tile is part of a controlled production process that maintains tonal consistency while allowing subtle, handcrafted variation to come through across walls and floors. The overall effect at installation scale is quiet, atmospheric, and elevated rather than a saturated color statement.

### Is the Moonlight 2x8 ceramic tile good for the kitchen?

Yes, the Moonlight 2x8 ceramic tile is well-suited for kitchen applications. It is commonly installed as a ceramic tile backsplash due to its smooth satin surface, which supports easy cleaning and consistent performance behind cooking areas. The elongated 2x8 format is well-suited for using ceramic tiles for kitchen flooring layouts where visual rhythm is required.

### What grout color works best with the Moonlight 2x8?

Neutral grout colors such as soft white, light gray, or muted silver work best with the Moonlight 2x8 ceramic tile. These tones maintain visual continuity with the pale blue surface while allowing the tile layout to stand out. A warmer grout tone can also be used to introduce contrast, but it will visually emphasize the joint pattern across ceramic tile flooring or wall applications.

### Can the Moonlight 2x8 be used in a pool or spa?

Yes, the Moonlight 2x8 ceramic tile is suitable for pools and spas, including saltwater and chlorine environments. It is frequently specified for submerged and waterline installations, including ceramic shower tile and spa wall systems. Sealing is required before installation and maintained according to standard ceramic care practices.

### How long will ceramic tiles last?

Properly installed ceramic tile can last decades under normal residential and commercial conditions. Longevity depends on substrate preparation, sealing compliance, and maintenance practices. When used in flooring, walls, or wet environments such as ceramic tile bathroom installations, performance is sustained through correct installation and periodic resealing of grout systems.

### How to clean a ceramic floor tile? 

Ceramic tile floors should be cleaned using a pH-neutral cleaner and warm water. Abrasive products, bleach, and acidic solutions such as vinegar should be avoided as they can affect the surface finish over time. Regular cleaning maintains the performance of ceramic tile flooring in high-use areas such as kitchens, bathrooms, and entryways without altering the satin surface of the tile.
