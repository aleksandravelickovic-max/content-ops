## How It's Made — \[ACCORDION\]

Zia's Glazed Earth 4x4 zellige tiles are handmade by Moroccan artisans using traditional production methods refined over centuries.

  

Artisans begin with raw clay sourced from local hillsides, which is soaked and filtered to remove impurities. The clay is hand-pressed into molds and dried under the Moroccan sun. Each tile is shaped by hand before being left to dry further.

  

The tiles are then hand-dipped in glaze and placed in an earthen kiln. Heat distributes unevenly throughout the kiln, producing the inherent variation in tone, surface, and thickness that defines each tile. The beige glaze settles differently on each tile at the dipping stage, which is what produces the wide tonal range that makes Glazed Earth read differently from tile to tile across a completed installation.

## Frequently Asked Questions — \[ACCORDION\]  

### What does zellige mean?

Zellige is a traditional form of Moroccan tilemaking in which natural clay is hand-shaped, glazed, and kiln-fired by skilled artisans. This centuries-old technique originated in Morocco and remains closely associated with the city of Fez.

### Will grout color affect how the Glazed Earth zellige reads once installed?

Yes, grout color will affect how Glazed Earth zellige reads once installed. A grout color close to the tile will reduce contrast between pieces, while a darker grout will emphasize joints and highlight variation in shape and surface, including chips, pits, and crazing.

### Will the edges of the Glazed Earth zellige align evenly during installation?

No, the edges of Glazed Earth zellige will not align evenly due to the hand-shaped production process. Variation in size and edge definition is expected and contributes to the character of the final installation.

### Can I use Glazed Earth zellige to create a continuous surface across corners?

Yes, you can use Glazed Earth zellige across corners with proper planning. Edges are typically finished using mitered edges or Schluter strips, as traditional trim pieces are not used with zellige.

### Can I adjust the layout during installation if the variation feels uneven?

Yes, you can adjust the layout of Glazed Earth zellige during installation. Because each tile varies, installers often make adjustments while setting to maintain a consistent distribution of color, thickness, and surface.

### Does the glaze finish affect how the surface interacts with light?

Yes, the glaze finish of Glazed Earth zellige affects how the surface reflects light. Each tile responds differently due to variation in surface and firing, creating shifts in appearance across the installation.
