## How It's Made — \[ACCORDION\]

Zia's Glazed Earth 2x6 zellige tiles are handmade by Moroccan artisans using traditional production methods refined over centuries.



Artisans begin with raw clay sourced from local hillsides, which is soaked and filtered to remove impurities. The clay is hand-pressed into rectangular molds and shaped by hand into the 2x6 Bejmat format before being set to dry in the Moroccan sun.



Once dry, each tile is hand-dipped individually in glaze. The beige glaze settles differently on each tile at the dipping stage, producing the wide tonal range that makes Glazed Earth move across shades of beige from tile to tile. The tiles are then placed in earthen kilns where heat distributes unevenly, producing the chips, pits, crazing, and tonal shifts that make each Glazed Earth 2x6 perfectly imperfect and entirely its own.
