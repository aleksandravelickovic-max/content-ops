## About 

### Inherent Variation

  - Our Basilica 12x24 is a grey limestone tile with a muted palette and gradual tonal shifts that become more or less visible depending on light direction and surface absorption.
  - Each grey limestone tile remains wholly unique, and this innate variation defines the line's charm.
  - Expect natural variation in color, tone, fossil trace, mineral density, thickness, and edge behavior across the layout. Chips, pits, and crazing appear as natural characteristics of sedimentary stone.

### Preparation and Curation

  - Mix tiles from all cartons before setting to distribute tonal variation, fossil markings, and mineral movement evenly across the full field.
  - Establish layout direction prior to setting to manage tonal transitions across the 12x24 format. Avoid grouping similar tones in isolated areas, as the large-format proportion makes clustering more visible than it would be at smaller scales.
  - Disperse any darker or heavily fossilized pieces evenly across the layout rather than concentrating them in isolated areas.
  - We suggest a 15–20% overage on all Basilica orders. For tighter tonal control or a more uniform composition, increase to 25% overage.

### Installation and Finishing

  - All installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with limestone, thinset, sealants, and grout in accordance with California Prop 65.
  - Install Basilica 12x24 using a medium-bed thinset on a fully prepared substrate. In dry interior conditions, wall applications may be installed directly over stable, properly prepared drywall.
  - Seal the tile with 511 Porous Plus before and after grouting. Interior installations require a Fila Matte Wax finish as a final coat; exterior installations should receive a second application of 511 Porous Plus in place of wax.
  - For finishing, bring the drywall flush with the finished tile surface, miter exposed edges at 45 degrees, or complete the edge with Schluter strips.
  - You may reach out to our team directly at info@ziatile.com with any specific installation questions.

## How It's Made

Our Basilica 12x24 is cut from dense limestone blocks quarried outside Fez, Morocco, in a region where stone extraction and hand-finishing techniques have been refined over generations. Each block is selected for structural consistency before being cut into large-format tiles that preserve the internal mineral composition of the stone, including the neutral grey field, fossil traces, and subtle tonal movement that define the material.

  

Once cut, each tile is finished through a controlled honing process that mechanically smooths the surface to a matte, non-reflective plane. This process reduces surface glare while retaining the geological movement within the stone, which allows the tile to read as a continuous architectural surface rather than a decorative layer. 

  

Because the color and character come from the stone itself, the palette remains stable across the full body of the tile as it wears.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Basilica 12x24 good for a kitchen?

Yes, Basilica 12x24 is well-suited to kitchen installations when properly sealed and maintained. As a flooring material, it performs well in kitchen environments where natural stone continuity is preferred over synthetic surfaces, and the large-format proportion reduces grout density across the floor. The stone must be sealed before and after grouting to reduce absorption from oils, acids, and food-related spills. On vertical surfaces, Basilica also works as a backsplash, where the honed finish reads as a grounded matte field behind cooking areas.

### What is honed limestone?

Honed limestone is a natural stone that has been mechanically ground to create a smooth, matte surface with low reflectivity. On Basilica 12x24, the honed finish produces a controlled, non-gloss appearance that preserves the stone's natural mineral movement and fossil trace. Unlike polished limestone, honed limestone reduces glare and softens visual contrast, which makes it particularly suitable for residential and commercial applications where tonal restraint is required.

### How do I clean limestone?

To clean limestone, use a pH-neutral stone cleaner with water and a soft cloth or mop. Avoid acidic or abrasive cleaning products, including vinegar and bleach, which can etch the calcium-based structure of the stone and damage the sealed surface. Regular sweeping removes abrasive debris, and prompt cleaning of spills helps prevent staining. Proper sealing at each stage of installation supports easier maintenance over time.

### Is limestone durable?

Yes, limestone is highly durable when used in appropriate applications and maintained correctly. It performs reliably under regular foot traffic, though it remains a porous natural stone and requires sealing to protect against moisture and staining. With proper care, the material maintains its structural integrity and develops a subtle patina over time that reflects normal architectural use rather than wear.

### How does the 12x24 format install?

The 12x24 format installs as a large-format field where the extended proportion reduces grout interruptions and carries the stone at scale. The tile works well in horizontal running bond, which emphasizes the width of a floor or wall, or in vertical orientation on walls to draw the eye upward. For any layout, a full dry run before setting is essential to balance tonal shifts, fossil markings, and mineral variation across adjacent tiles.
