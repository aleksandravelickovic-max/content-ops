## How It's Made

The Burnt Sugar + Glazed Earth Checkerboard is formed through traditional Moroccan zellige production, where each tile begins as raw clay sourced from local hillsides. The clay is soaked, filtered, and hand-pressed into molds before being shaped into its final 2x2 format.

Tiles are sun-dried, then refined by hand to correct edges and surface alignment before glazing. Each piece is hand-dipped, allowing glaze thickness to vary across the surface. This creates the tonal depth that defines both Burnt Sugar and Glazed Earth fields within the checkerboard composition.

Firing takes place in earthen kilns where heat distribution is intentionally uneven. This produces shifts in tone, surface movement, and glaze pooling across the batch. The result is a surface where no two tiles behave identically, reinforcing the material logic of zellige tile patterns built on variation rather than uniformity.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the Burnt Sugar + Glazed Earth Checkerboard?

The Burnt Sugar + Glazed Earth Checkerboard is a handcrafted Moroccan zellige mosaic built from two tonal fields arranged in a checkerboard pattern. The composition pairs a deep Burnt Sugar brown with a lighter Glazed Earth to produce a structured, two-tone surface with natural variation across each tile. Each element is a 2x2 zellige hand-formed from clay and hand-dipped in glaze, and the checkerboard sheets are mesh-mounted for installation efficiency.

### Can the Burnt Sugar + Glazed Earth Checkerboard be used in a bathroom?

Yes, the Checkerboard can be used in a bathroom, including shower walls and feature surfaces. It performs particularly well as a backsplash or full wall installation where the two-tone pattern is part of the design direction. For shower floors or other wet-zone applications, installation must follow proper sealing requirements, and slip resistance can be increased by sizing grout joints to a minimum of 1/8" and/or applying an anti-slip treatment after installation.

### How does the checkerboard pattern resolve across a full installation?

The checkerboard pattern resolves at installation scale. Each sheet carries a section of the alternating composition, and when sheets are set together, the Burnt Sugar and Glazed Earth tiles interlock into a continuous two-tone field. Because each colorway carries its own natural variation, the final pattern reads with both graphic structure and handcrafted depth, rather than as a flat repeating grid.

### What grout color works best with the Checkerboard?

The best grout color depends on how the installation should read. A mid-tone neutral grout lets both colorways lead equally and keeps the checkerboard pattern reading clearly. A grout matched closely to the darker Burnt Sugar tone produces a more structured composition where the lighter Glazed Earth tiles pop against the field. A lighter grout matched to the Glazed Earth tone softens the contrast and lets the pattern read more atmospherically across the surface.

### Can I mix the Checkerboard with solid zellige colorways?

Yes, the Burnt Sugar + Glazed Earth Checkerboard can be mixed with other colorways in Zia's zellige collection. Each tile is produced using the same traditional methods, so the Checkerboard shares consistent surface character with the full range. The mosaic works particularly well as a feature surface framed by larger solid-color zellige fields, where the two-tone pattern serves as a focal accent against a quieter surrounding composition.
