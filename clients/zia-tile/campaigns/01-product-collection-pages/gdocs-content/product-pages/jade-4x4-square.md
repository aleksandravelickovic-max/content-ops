## How It's Made

Our Jade 4x4 handmade green tile is produced in Fez, Morocco, where zellige production follows the artisanal practices refined over centuries. Artisans begin with raw clay extracted from local hillsides, washing it repeatedly to remove natural impurities. The refined clay is then pressed into square molds and left to dry under controlled sun exposure, allowing moisture to release gradually and reduce internal stress before firing.

Each tile is then hand-shaped at the edges, producing subtle variation in line and dimension that defines authentic zellige in its raw form. Once shaped, each piece is hand-dipped into a deep jade glaze formulated to create tonal depth across the surface. The glaze interacts differently with each tile depending on absorption and thickness, which is what produces the iridescent green spectrum visible across a full installation.

The tiles are fired in traditional earthen kilns where heat distribution is intentionally uneven. This firing method produces variation in tone, surface movement, and glaze response across the batch, and each tile exits the kiln with visible differences in depth of color, surface reflection, and edge definition.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the characteristics of Jade 4x4 zellige tiles?

The characteristics of Jade zellige include inherent variation in color, tone, thickness, and surface, with a deep iridescent green glaze that shimmers and shifts under changing light. Each tile features chips, pits, and crazing as natural outcomes of this handcrafted Moroccan art form. The result is a surface that moves tonally across the installation and defines a fluid field rather than a uniform finish.

### Can Jade 4x4 Zellige tiles be used in the kitchen?

Yes, Jade 4x4 is well suited to kitchen applications, particularly as a backsplash where the iridescent green introduces vibrant color to the surrounding design. The glazed surface is suitable for vertical applications behind cooking areas when properly installed and sealed in accordance with wet-area guidelines. Grout joints and installation method should account for the surface variation and edge irregularity inherent to zellige.

### Do zellige tiles require grout?

Yes, zellige tiles require grout for installation. While traditional installations minimize grout joints for a tight aesthetic, grout is still necessary to stabilize edges and accommodate natural variation. A 1/16" to 1/8" grout joint is typical for most applications. For wet floor installations, grout joint size should be increased to a minimum of ⅛", and slip resistance can be further improved with an anti-slip treatment after installation is complete.

### Can I mix Jade with other zellige colors?

Yes, you can mix Jade 4x4 with other colors in Zia's zellige collection. Each colorway is produced using the same traditional methods, so tiles share consistent surface character across the range. The iridescent green palette of Jade pairs particularly well alongside warm neutrals, creams, and deeper greens, where the glaze reads as a color-forward accent against a quieter surrounding field.

### How do I clean Jade zellige?

To clean Jade zellige, use a pH-neutral cleaner and warm water with a soft cloth or sponge. Avoid acidic or abrasive products, as they can affect the glaze and dull the iridescent quality over time. Regular light cleaning is more effective than infrequent deep cleaning, particularly in kitchens and bathrooms. In wet areas, sealed grout joints should also be wiped down to prevent staining and mildew.
