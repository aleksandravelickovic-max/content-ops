## How It's Made — \[ACCORDION\]

Each Racing Green 2x6 follows the same production process as all traditional zellige. Artisans source raw clay from local Moroccan hillsides, soak it in water, filter it for impurities, and work it until it reaches a consistency suitable for hand-pressing into molds.

Once formed, the tiles dry in the Moroccan sun. Each piece is shaped and flattened by hand before being dipped individually in glaze. The Racing Green glaze settles differently on each tile, which is where the range of deep green tones develops. No two dips produce identical results.

Firing takes place in earthen kilns, where heat moves unevenly through the chamber. That unevenness is the condition that produces the chips, pits, crazing, and tonal shifts that make each Racing Green 2x6 entirely its own.

## Frequently Asked Questions — \[ACCORDION\]

### What grout color works best with Racing Green zellige?

Mapei Ultracolor Plus FA works well with glazed zellige. For Racing Green, a grout color matched closely to the tile tends to produce the most cohesive result, allowing the deep green tones to read across the surface without interruption. A lighter neutral grout will draw more attention to the individual tile and the variation between pieces.

### What is the difference between a stacked and herringbone layout with the 2x6 format?

A stacked layout places each tile directly above or beside the next in a uniform grid, which emphasizes the linear quality of the 2x6 format and produces a clean, directional surface. A herringbone layout arranges tiles at 90-degree angles to each other in a repeating V-shape, which introduces more pattern across the surface.

### Which direction should I run the 2x6 format — horizontal or vertical?

The direction of a 2x6 format installation depends on the surface and the intended layout.  A horizontal layout emphasizes the width of the surface and works well on backsplashes and lower wall applications. A vertical layout draws the eye upward and can make a surface feel taller, which works well in shower enclosures and floor-to-ceiling installations.

### Can the Racing Green 2x6 be mixed with other zellige formats?

Yes, the Racing Green 2x6 can be mixed with other formats in Zia's zellige collection. Each format is produced using the same traditional methods and shares consistent material character across the range. Mixing the 2x6 with square or trapezoid formats in the same colorway is a common approach for installations that combine field tile with detail work. Racing Green can also be mixed with other colors in the collection, where a tonal contrast is part of the design intent.

### How do I seal zellige tile in a pool or spa?

To seal zellige tile in a pool or spa, apply 511 Porous Plus to all tiles and grout lines after grouting is complete. Apply in thin layers until the surface is fully saturated, wiping off any excess before it dries. Over-application can result in an uneven film on the surface. Allow the sealer to cure fully before the pool or spa is filled or put into use.

### How do I remove stains from zellige tile?

To remove stains from zellige tile, act as quickly as possible. Blot the stain rather than wiping to avoid spreading it across the surface. For routine staining, clean with a pH-neutral cleaner such as Miracle Sealants pH Neutral Tile and Stone Cleaner. For more stubborn staining, Miracle Sealants Heavy Duty Cleaner (Acid Substitute) is the recommended product. Avoid vinegar, lemon juice, bleach, and any acid-based cleaners, as these will damage the glaze permanently. If the tile is properly sealed, most stains will not penetrate the surface.
