### Inherent Variation

  - Our Monument 6x6 is a honed limestone tile in a soft neutral grey, with a flat matte surface and gently smoothed finish that preserves the stone's natural mineral structure.
  - Each handcrafted limestone tile remains wholly unique, and this inherent variation defines the line's charm.
  - Expect moderate variation in color, tone, and surface texture across the layout, along with subtle fossilization patterns and the edge irregularity typical of sedimentary stone.

### Preparation and Curation

  - Blend tiles from multiple boxes before installation to distribute tonal variation evenly across the layout.
  - Set out a full dry layout before committing to placement. Limestone has a face and an underside that are not reversible; confirm each tile is oriented face up.
  - Disperse more pronounced tonal shifts and fossilization patterns evenly rather than clustering them in one area of the layout.
  - We suggest a 15–20% overage on all limestone orders. For a more uniform look, we recommend 25% overage.

### Installation and Finishing

  - Installers must always wear protective face and eye coverings when working with limestone, thinset, sealants, and grout in accordance with California Prop 65.
  - For finishing, bring the drywall flush with the finished stone surface, miter exposed edges at 45 degrees, or complete the edge with Schluter strips.
  - Seal Monument 6x6 with 511 Porous Plus before and after grouting. For interior applications, finish with Fila Matte Wax as a final coat. For exterior applications, apply a second coat of 511 Porous Plus in place of wax.
  - Grout in small sections no larger than 2'x2' and wipe away excess immediately. Limestone stains readily if grout is left to sit on the surface.
  - You may reach out to our team directly at info@ziatile.com with any specific installation questions.

## How It's Made

Zia's Monument 6x6 + Honed Limestone is cut from solid blocks of natural limestone quarried near Fez, Morocco, where stone has been shaped for architectural use across centuries. Each block is selected for structural consistency before being cut into precise 6x6 formats by local stone masons.

  

The surface is mechanically finished into a honed limestone slab through controlled abrasion, producing a smooth matte plane that preserves the stone's natural mineral structure. In some production runs, a bush-hammered finish is applied to introduce a denser surface texture, increasing tactility without altering the underlying composition.

  

Each tile retains moderate variation in tone and texture because the stone is not manufactured but extracted. These variations define the material and ensure that every installation develops a continuous, natural field rather than a repeating surface pattern.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is honed limestone?

Honed limestone is a natural stone finished through controlled mechanical grinding to produce a flat, matte, gently smoothed surface. In Monument 6x6, the honing process preserves the stone's natural mineral variation while creating a consistent low-sheen plane suited to horizontal and vertical applications. Honed limestone is commonly used where a controlled, non-reflective stone surface is part of the design intent, across both flooring and wall installations.

### How do I care for honed limestone?

To care for honed limestone, seal the stone before and after grouting to reduce absorption and protect against staining. Ongoing maintenance requires pH-neutral stone cleaners and regular dry removal of dust and grit to prevent surface abrasion. Avoid acidic and abrasive products, including vinegar, bleach, and citrus-based cleaners, which can etch the stone permanently. Periodic resealing is required based on traffic and exposure, particularly for floors in high-use areas.

### What design styles suit Monument 6x6?

Monument 6x6 suits restrained architectural palettes where material tone defines the space rather than surface pattern. The honed limestone tile works particularly well in contemporary, modern rustic, and Mediterranean-influenced interiors where continuity across floors or wall planes is part of the design direction. The neutral grey palette also supports checkerboard layouts when paired with a contrasting light stone, as well as monochrome installations where the stone carries the full visual field.

### Can Monument 6x6 be used on a shower floor?

Yes, Monument 6x6 can be used on shower floors, shower walls, and steam showers. Sealing is required for all shower applications to protect the natural stone from continuous moisture exposure. Slip resistance can be increased by sizing grout joints to a minimum of ⅛" and/or applying an anti-slip product after installation. The 6x6 format and honed finish together produce a shower surface with consistent traction underfoot.

### Can Monument 6x6 be installed in a checkerboard layout?

Yes, Monument 6x6 installs well in a checkerboard layout. The 6x6 square format is particularly suited to this pattern, and the soft grey of Monument pairs naturally with a lighter contrasting stone, such as a warmer limestone, a white marble, or a cream-toned terrazzo, to produce the classic checkerboard rhythm across a floor or wall. A full dry layout is recommended before setting to confirm alignment and the visual balance of the two stones.
