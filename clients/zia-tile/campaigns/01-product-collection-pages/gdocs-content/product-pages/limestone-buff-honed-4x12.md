## How It's Made -- \[ACCORDION\]

Zia's Buff Honed Limestone 4x12 is quarried and cut outside of Fez, Morocco, where artisans have been cutting and honing this building material for centuries. Formed over millennia from natural sedimentary rock, each block carries the inherent variation in color, tone, and surface texture that defines the material.

  

Each tile is cut from solid limestone blocks by local stone masons and shaped into precise formats before moving to the finishing stage.

  

The tiles then undergo the honing process. The surface is mechanically ground with fine abrasives to produce a smooth, flat, matte finish. Over time, the stone will develop a soft patina, a natural characteristic of limestone and an expected part of its life in an installation.

## Frequently Asked Questions -- \[ACCORDION\]  

### What is honed limestone?

Honed limestone is produced by grinding the stone's surface with fine abrasives to create a smooth, flat, matte finish. The process removes surface irregularities without introducing a reflective sheen, leaving the stone's natural color and markings visible. The matte surface also offers better traction than polished stone, making it well-suited for flooring and wall applications.

### What is the difference between honed and bush-hammered limestone?

The difference between honed and bush-hammered limestone comes down to surface treatment. Honed limestone is ground smooth, producing a consistent, flat, matte surface. Bush-hammered limestone is finished with a high-impact machine that uniformly pits the surface, creating a rough, textured feel. Honed finishes work well across a wide range of interior applications; bush-hammered finishes are often chosen where a more tactile, rustic surface is preferred.

### Is honed limestone a good choice for a bathroom floor?

Yes, honed limestone is a good choice for a bathroom floor. The matte surface provides better traction than polished stone and helps conceal water spots and minor surface wear. Proper sealing is required before grouting and at regular intervals to protect against staining.

### How do I seal limestone tile?

To seal limestone tile, ensure the surface is clean and dry before applying 511 Porous Plus in thin layers until the stone is fully saturated. Wipe off any excess before it dries -- over-application can result in an uneven film. Allow the sealer to cure fully before grouting.

### How do I re-seal limestone tile?

Limestone should be re-sealed as often as needed, depending on traffic, wear, and moisture exposure. Apply a fresh coat of 511 Porous Plus in thin layers, wiping off any excess before it dries. For interior installations, finish with Fila Matte Finish Protective Wax. For outdoor installations, use 511 Porous Plus only -- do not use Fila Matte Finish outdoors, as UV rays cause discoloration.

### How do I clean limestone tile?

To clean limestone tile, use a microfiber mop with warm water and a pH-neutral cleaner for routine maintenance. Vacuum or sweep regularly to remove grit and debris. For spills, blot immediately rather than wiping. For heavier staining, use Miracle Sealants Heavy Duty Cleaner (Acid Substitute). Avoid vinegar, lemon juice, and bleach -- these will etch and damage the surface over time.
