Zellige tile installation is labor-intensive, often likened more to masonry than traditional tiling due to the irregular nature of the handmade material. Traditionally, zellige is installed edge-to-edge with minimal to no grout joints; however, we recommend wedge spacers to adjust the spacing between irregular tiles to maintain a consistent layout and protect the tiles' edges. ## How It's Made — \[ACCORDION\]

Our Casablanca 4x4 zellige tiles are handmade by Moroccan artisans with generations of experience, using authentic, traditional methods to produce tiles with lasting quality and innate variation.

  

Our artisans in Morocco start with raw clay, mined from local hillsides, which they soak in water and filter for impurities. The clay becomes a soft, malleable substance that is hand-pressed into various molds before being placed to dry in the Moroccan sun. Artisans flatten and shape each Casablanca 4x4 by hand, then allow the material to dry further.

  

Each tile is then hand-dipped in glaze and methodically placed in an earthen kiln. Heat distributes unevenly throughout the kiln, which creates the variation and dynamic glazes of the collection. Every zellige tile is an expression of the artisan's work that day—perfectly imperfect and unique.

## Frequently Asked Questions — \[ACCORDION\]  

### Does each Casablanca 4x4 tile arrive with a consistent thickness?

Artisans shape each zellige tile by hand, which results in innate variation in thickness, shape, and color across the collection. Expect and embrace these unique characteristics, as they allow the installer to create the organic surface that defines the traditional zellige aesthetic.

### Can the Casablanca 4x4 be installed on a shower floor?

Yes, our 4x4-inch zellige tiles function well on shower floors, shower walls, and within steam showers. Slip resistance can be improved by increasing grout joint size to a minimum of 1/8 inch and/or applying an anti-slip treatment after installation is complete. Sealing is required for all shower installations.

### Why do some Casablanca 4x4 tiles feature small surface cracks?

Fine lines on zellige's glazed surface, known as *crazing*, occur naturally during the earthen kiln firing process. These characteristic marks, along with occasional pits and edge chips, represent the artisan's craftwork and do not compromise the structural integrity of the tile.

### How should zellige tile be cleaned once the installation is complete?

Clean zellige tiles with pH-neutral products to maintain the tiles' glaze. Regularly vacuum or sweep the surface to remove abrasive debris that might scratch the tile over time.

### What are the characteristic features of each Casablanca 4x4 tile?

Our Casablanca zellige 4x4 tile features variation in color, shape, and thickness. Each tile exhibits unique characteristics such as chips, pits, and crazing. These elements are not defects; rather, they are traditional characteristics of handmade Moroccan tile that create a perfectly imperfect aesthetic.

### Can Casablanca 4x4 be mixed with other colors in the collection?

Yes, our Casablanca zellige 4x4s complement any of the 35+ colors in our handcrafted zellige collection. Artisans use the same traditional Moroccan methods for every shade, allowing these 4x4 squares to be mixed with other tones to create a custom installation that showcases the full spectrum of available glazes.
