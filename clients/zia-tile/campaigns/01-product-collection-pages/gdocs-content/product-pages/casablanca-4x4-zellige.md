## How It's Made — \[ACCORDION\]

Simultaneously traditional and on-trend, our Casablanca 4x4 zellige tiles are handmade by Moroccan artisans with generations of experience, using authentic, traditional methods to produce tiles with lasting quality and innate variation.

  

Our artisans in Morocco start with raw clay, mined from local hillsides, which they soak in water and filter for impurities. The clay becomes a soft, malleable substance that is hand-pressed into various molds before being placed to dry in the Moroccan sun. Artisans flatten and shape each Casablanca 4x4 by hand, then allow the material to dry further.

  

Each tile is then hand-dipped in glaze and methodically placed in an earthen kiln. Heat distributes unevenly throughout the kiln, which creates the stunning variation and dynamic glazes of the collection. Every zellige tile is an expression of the artisan's work that day—beautifully imperfect and unique.
