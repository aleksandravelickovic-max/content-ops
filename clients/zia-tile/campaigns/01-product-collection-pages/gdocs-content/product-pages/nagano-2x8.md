## How It's Made 

The Nagano Ceramic Tile is produced in Nagoya, Japan, a region where ceramic craftsmanship has been practiced for over 1,000 years. The process begins with refined clay that is prepared, extruded, and shaped into precise 2x8 ceramic tile formats using die-cut molds designed for architectural consistency. Each tile is then dried under controlled conditions to stabilize form before entering a high-temperature firing cycle.

  

A hand-applied glazing process is completed before firing, where the glossy surface is fixed during kiln exposure rather than applied afterward. This firing stage is where the Nagano color develops its final dark grey-blue depth, with the kiln heat influencing subtle shifts in tone across each batch. 

  

These shifts are controlled but present, reflecting the nature of traditional Japanese ceramic production, where material response is the outcome, not an imperfection. After firing, each tile is inspected for dimensional accuracy and surface consistency before being prepared for ceramic design installations.  After installation, Zia's Nagano ceramic tile presents a continuous, reflective surface that holds its tone across light changes while retaining the material character formed during firing.

## Frequently Asked Questions 

### What color is Nagano ceramic tile?

Nagano ceramic is an aqua tone with a glossy, reflective finish that shifts subtly under changing light conditions. The glossy surface amplifies the color across both vertical and horizontal installations, which is why the tile reads differently on a backsplash versus a shower wall versus a pool surround. Each tile carries a subtle variation in tone that becomes more visible once installed as a full field.

### What grout color works best with Nagano ceramic tile?

The best grout color depends on the intended visual contrast. Light grey or soft white grout is commonly used for a cohesive surface that softens the grid of a 2x8 subway layout while maintaining definition. Stark white grout creates a sharper contrast that highlights each tile edge, while darker grey or charcoal grout produces a more graphic effect across backsplash installations. A closely matched aqua or blue-grey grout produces the most continuous field.

### Can I use Nagano ceramic tile in the kitchen?

Yes, Nagano 2x8 is well-suited to kitchen applications, particularly as a backsplash. The glossy surface is resistant to moisture and easy to clean, which makes it appropriate for cooking areas where splashes and heat exposure occur. The elongated subway format also works on adjacent wall surfaces to maintain continuity in modern kitchen layouts, where the aqua palette can read as a color-forward feature against neutral cabinetry.

### Can Nagano 2x8 be used in a pool or spa?

Yes, Nagano 2x8 is suitable for pools and spas, including saltwater and chlorine environments. The aqua palette reads particularly well underwater, where the reflective surface interacts with the movement of the water. Sealing is required before grouting, and periodic resealing is recommended to maintain long-term performance across submerged and waterline installations.

### How long does ceramic tile last?

High-quality ceramic tile can last for decades with proper installation and regular care. Longevity depends on substrate preparation, traffic levels, and maintenance of the grout joints. When correctly installed, ceramic tile on walls and floors maintains its performance for decades without structural degradation. What typically requires refreshing over time is the grout rather than the tile itself.
