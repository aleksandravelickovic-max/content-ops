## How It's Made

Our Desert Bloom 2x6 carries the legacy of Moroccan craftsmanship in a rectangular format. The clay is pulled from the hillsides surrounding Fez, soaked in water, and filtered for impurities before being worked by hand into slabs and set to dry in the Moroccan sun. Each 2x6 is shaped into its final rectangular profile by hand, which is why no two tiles sit at exactly the same depth or carry exactly the same edge.

After drying, each tile is hand-dipped in glaze. The Desert Bloom glaze is where the warm pink tones take form, and because each dip is done by hand, the coverage varies from tile to tile. That variation is what produces the tonal range visible across a full installation.

The final stage is the kiln. Earthen kilns do not distribute heat evenly, and the results are not incidental. The chips, pits, crazing, and shifting tones that emerge from the firing process are the conditions that make each Desert Bloom 2x6 its own.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which orientation is best for the Desert Bloom 2x6 rectangle?

The Desert Bloom 2x6 rectangle can be installed in either a horizontal running bond or a vertical stack. A horizontal layout creates a classic subway appearance that extends the visual width of a surface, while a vertical stack emphasizes height and draws the eye upward. The warm pink tones and inherent variation remain visible in either orientation.

### Can I create a herringbone pattern with Desert Bloom 2x6 zellige tiles?

Yes, you can create a herringbone pattern with Desert Bloom 2x6 zellige. The rectangular Bejmat format supports geometric layouts such as herringbone and basketweave, both of which add directional movement to the warm pink glaze. A dry layout before installation helps confirm alignment and distribute tonal variation evenly across the pattern.

### What colors complement Desert Bloom 2x6 zellige?

Desert Bloom 2x6 zellige pairs well with materials that let the warm pink tones lead. Neutral stone, matte ceramic, and terrazzo in off-white and buff tones sit naturally alongside it. In the surrounding space, unlacquered brass and natural wood tend to complement the warm glaze without competing with the surface variation.

### Do the Desert Bloom 2x6 rectangles require a specific grout width?

No, the hand-shaped edges vary from tile to tile, which allows for a traditional installation with a close grout joint. For wet floor applications, increase grout joint size to a minimum of ⅛" and consider applying an anti-slip treatment after installation is complete.

### Can I mix Desert Bloom 2x6 with other colors in your collection?

Yes, you can mix Zia's Desert Bloom 2x6 Bejmat with other colors in the zellige collection. Each tile shares the same production method, allowing for cohesive multi-tone installations. Desert Bloom pairs particularly well with lighter neutrals and earthy tones in the collection, where the contrast in color creates a layered, considered composition.

### Does Desert Bloom zellige work in a small bathroom?

Yes, Desert Bloom zellige works well in a small bathroom. The glazed surface reflects light, which helps a smaller space feel less enclosed, and the 2x6 rectangle can be oriented vertically to emphasize height. The warm pink tones also read well under the warmer artificial light common in bathrooms.
