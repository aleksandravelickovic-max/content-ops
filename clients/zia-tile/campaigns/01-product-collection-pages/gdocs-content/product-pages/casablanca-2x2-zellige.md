## How It's Made — \[ACCORDION\]

Each Casablanca 2x2 begins where all traditional zellige begins. Artisans source raw clay from local Moroccan hillsides, soak it in water, filter it for impurities, and work it until it reaches a consistency suitable for hand-pressing into molds.

  

Each tile is shaped individually by hand into the 2x2 format, which is why variation in thickness, edge, and surface texture is inherent to this scale. Once formed, the tiles dry in the Moroccan sun before each piece is dipped individually in glaze.

  

The light neutral Casablanca glaze settles differently on each tile. Firing takes place in earthen kilns, where heat moves unevenly through the chamber. That unevenness produces the chips, pits, crazing, and tonal shifts that make each Casablanca 2x2 entirely its own.

## Frequently Asked Questions — \[ACCORDION\]  

### What grout joint size works best for the 2x2 format?

For wall installations using the 2x2 format, the traditional method uses no standard grout joint — tiles are laid edge to edge or with a 1/16" spacing if a specific grout line is preferred. For floor installations, a minimum of ⅛" grout joint is recommended. In wet or commercial floor applications, grout joints can be widened beyond ⅛" to increase slip resistance.

### Can the Casablanca 2x2 be mixed with other Casablanca formats in one installation?

Yes, the Casablanca 2x2 can be mixed with other Casablanca formats within the same installation. Each format is produced using the same traditional methods and shares consistent material character across the range. Mixing the 2x2 with larger formats such as the 4x4 is a common approach for creating installations that combine field tile with detail work.

### How does the 2x2 format work on a floor versus a wall?

On a wall, the 2x2 format produces a surface where the variation in each tile is experienced up close. On a floor, the smaller format means more grout joints across the surface, which increases traction but also requires more careful curation to distribute tonal variation evenly. For floor installations, a minimum of ⅛" grout joint is recommended, and slip resistance can be further increased by widening joints and/or applying an anti-slip treatment after installation.

### Does the Casablanca glaze look different under artificial light versus natural light?

Yes, the Casablanca glaze can appear different under artificial light versus natural light. The dynamic glazes in the glaze shift depending on the light source and conditions in the space. This is a characteristic of handmade zellige and is part of what gives a Casablanca installation its variation across the surface.

### Does zellige tile require sealing?

We recommend sealing zellige tile in moisture-exposed applications, including showers, pools, and spas. Apply 511 Porous Plus to all tiles and grout lines exposed to continuous moisture after grouting is complete. Glazed zellige in dry applications does not require sealing, but wet areas should be re-sealed at least every one to two years, depending on traffic and exposure.

### What grout should I use with zellige?

Mapei Ultracolor Plus FA works well with glazed zellige. A color matched closely to the tile tends to produce the most cohesive result, integrating naturally with the chips, pits, and crazing. Contrasting grout colors can stain the surface and may be difficult to remove from the glazed tile if excess grout is not wiped away promptly.
