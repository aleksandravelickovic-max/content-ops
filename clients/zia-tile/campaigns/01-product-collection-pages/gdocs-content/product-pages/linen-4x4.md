## How It's Made — \[ACCORDION\]

Nagoya, Japan, has been a center of ceramic production for over 1,000 years, and each Linen 4x4 is made within that tradition. Raw materials are formed into the square 4x4 format and dried under controlled conditions before entering the kiln.



High-temperature firing gives the tile its density and low porosity. The matte finish is achieved through a controlled glazing process applied before firing, producing a true ivory surface that does not reflect light.



Minor tonal shifts emerge during firing. Those shifts are most apparent up close, where the handcrafted character of the production process is visible in the surface.

## Frequently Asked Questions — \[ACCORDION\]

### What is the difference between the Linen 4x4 and the Linen 2x8?

The Linen 4x4 and the Linen 2x8 share the same true ivory matte finish and are produced using the same process. The difference is in how the format reads across a surface. The choice between the two depends on whether an all-over neutral field or a directional surface is the design intent.

### Does the Linen 4x4 require sealing?

Yes, the Linen 4x4 is a matte finish ceramic and must be sealed before grouting using 511 Porous Plus as a grout release. This step prevents grout haze and staining on the matte surface. Apply 511 Porous Plus in a thin layer over the tile surface, wipe off any excess before it dries, and allow it to cure fully before grouting. Reseal every 1-2 years, depending on traffic and moisture exposure.

### Is the Linen 4x4 suitable for a bathroom floor?

Yes, the Linen 4x4 is suitable for a bathroom floor. The matte finish has a DCOF rating of 0.74, which provides good traction across floor applications, including wet areas. For shower floors, slip resistance can be further increased by sizing grout joints to a minimum of 1/8" and/or applying an anti-slip treatment after installation is complete.

### What grout color works best with the Linen 4x4?

For the Linen 4x4, a lighter neutral grout matched closely to the true ivory tile tends to produce the most cohesive result. A contrasting grout will draw attention to the grid pattern of the square format, which can work as a deliberate design choice but changes how the surface reads at a distance.

### Can the Linen 4x4 be used in a pool or spa?

Yes, the Linen 4x4 Matte Ceramic is suitable for pools and spas, including saltwater and chlorine environments. Sealing is required for pool and spa installations. Matte finish ceramics must be sealed before grouting using 511 Porous Plus. Refer to the Zia Tile Installation Guide for full sealing instructions.

### Can the Linen 4x4 be mixed with the Linen 2x8 in the same installation?

Yes, the Linen 4x4 and the Linen 2x8 can be mixed within the same installation. Mixing the two formats is a common approach for installations that combine a uniform field tile with a more directional surface, such as a 4x4 floor paired with a 2x8 wall, or a 4x4 backsplash field with a 2x8 border or accent band.

### How do I calculate square footage for a square tile installation?

To calculate square footage for a square tile installation, measure the length and width of the surface in feet and multiply the two numbers together. For irregular surfaces, break the area into smaller rectangles, calculate each section separately, and add the totals together. Once you have the total square footage, add 15-20% overage to account for cuts, layout adjustments, and any tiles outside your preferred range.
