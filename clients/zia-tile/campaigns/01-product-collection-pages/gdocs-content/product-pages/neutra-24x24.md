### Preparation and Curation

  - Mix tiles from all boxes before setting to distribute aggregate composition and tonal variation evenly across the full layout.
  - Arrange a full dry layout before committing to placement to evaluate tonal flow and adjust the composition across the large-format 24x24 field.
  - Maintain consistent 1/8" grout joints across the installation. Because the hand-pressed cement body carries subtle dimensional variation, shimming or wedge spacers may be required to preserve straight grout lines.

### Installation and Finishing

  - All installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with terrazzo, thinset, sealants, and grout in accordance with California Prop 65.
  - Press tiles into place using palm pressure only. Do not use a rubber mallet, as the impact can create hairline cracks in the cement matrix over time.
  - Seal Neutra 24x24 with 511 Porous Plus before and after grouting. Interior installations require a Fila Matte Wax finish as a final coat; exterior installations should receive a second application of 511 Porous Plus in place of wax.
  - For finishing, bring the drywall flush with the finished tile surface, miter exposed edges at 45 degrees, or complete the edge with Schluter strips.

## How It's Made

Zia's Neutra 24x24 is produced through a Venetian-inspired terrazzo tilemaking process that dates back over 500 years, when crushed stone was first embedded into cementitious surfaces to create durable architectural flooring. Our contemporary methods preserve this structural logic while refining precision through controlled hydraulic pressing.

  

Each tile begins with a curated blend of marble, granite, and quartz aggregates placed into a steel mold. Mineral pigments and cement are introduced as a binding matrix, forming a dense composite that is compressed under hydraulic pressure. This process locks the aggregates into a unified body rather than the surface layer.

  

Once pressed, each tile is cured before undergoing surface grinding and polishing. This step exposes the internal aggregate distribution, revealing the full-body composition of the material rather than an applied finish. The matte surface of Neutra 24x24 terrazzo is achieved through controlled refinement that maintains texture while stabilizing the cement matrix for long-term performance.

  

Since each production cycle is influenced by aggregate placement, pressure distribution, and curing conditions, no two tiles are identical. This controlled variability is an essential characteristic of an authentic terrazzo tile, where material structure defines the final visual outcome.

## Frequently Asked Questions  

### What is a terrazzo tile?

Terrazzo tile is a cement-based composite made from marble, granite, or quartz aggregates embedded into a cement binder and hydraulically pressed into a solid tile. The aggregate is exposed through grinding and polishing, which is what produces the continuous speckled surface defined by inherent aggregate variation. Zia's Neutra 24x24 is a matte-finish terrazzo with a neutral base, and because the aggregate runs through the full body of the tile, the composition remains consistent as the surface wears.

### Is the Neutra 24x24 terrazzo good for bathroom flooring?

Yes, Neutra 24x24 is well-suited to bathroom flooring applications when properly sealed before and after grouting. The cement-based terrazzo structure performs well in moisture-prone environments such as bathrooms, including shower floors and walls. Slip resistance can be increased by sizing grout joints to a minimum of ⅛" and/or applying an anti-slip treatment after installation.

### Is the Neutra 24x24 terrazzo good for a kitchen floor?

Yes, the Neutra 24x24 terrazzo floor tile performs well in kitchen environments due to its durability and resistance to daily wear. The large-format proportion reduces grout density across the installation, which supports easier maintenance in a kitchen environment. When properly sealed and maintained with pH-neutral cleaning products, the surface develops a subtle patina over time that reflects normal material aging rather than degradation.

### How does the 24x24 format work in an installation?

The 24x24 format reduces grout density and creates a more continuous surface field compared to smaller tile sizes. This large-format proportion supports installations where aggregate composition reads across a wider visual plane, which is particularly effective in open kitchens, hospitality lobbies, and expansive bathrooms. Installers must carefully manage leveling, maintain a 1/8" grout joint throughout, and use shimming or wedge spacers to preserve alignment across the larger tile body.

### How to clean terrazzo tile?

To clean terrazzo tile, sweep or vacuum daily to remove grit that can abrade the surface. For wet cleaning, use a pH-neutral cleaner with a damp microfiber mop. Avoid acidic products such as vinegar, bleach, and citrus-based solutions, which can etch the cement matrix and affect the polished surface. Spills should be removed promptly to prevent absorption, and deeper cleaning can be performed with pH-neutral stone cleaners designed for cement-based surfaces.
