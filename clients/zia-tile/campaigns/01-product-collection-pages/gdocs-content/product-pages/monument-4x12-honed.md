### Installation and Finishing

  - All installers must wear protective face and eye coverings when working with limestone, thinset, sealants, and grout in accordance with California Prop 65.
  - Install Monument 4x12 using a medium-bed thinset on a fully prepared substrate. In dry interior conditions, wall applications may be installed directly over stable, properly prepared drywall.
  - Seal the tile with 511 Porous Plus before and after grouting. Interior installations require a Fila Matte Wax finish as a final coat; exterior installations should receive a second application of 511 Porous Plus in place of wax.
  - For finishing, bring the drywall flush with the finished tile surface, miter exposed edges at 45 degrees, or complete the edge with Schluter strips.

## How It's Made 

Our Monument 4x12 is quarried outside Fez, Morocco, in a region where sedimentary limestone has been used for architectural construction across generations. Each block is extracted from dense geological formations and evaluated for structural consistency before being selected for processing. The stone is then cut into large slabs at the quarry level, preserving the integrity of the natural bedding and mineral composition that give the material its character.

  

Each slab is dimensioned into the 4x12 format by local stone masons who specialize in centuries-old regional stone-cutting practices. The elongated proportions are achieved through precision cutting that follows the natural behavior of the stone, allowing each tile to retain subtle variation in tone and edge definition. This stage determines the alignment and structural rhythm used in linear floor and wall installations.

  

The surface is then mechanically honed, a process that reduces surface reflectivity while maintaining the stone's internal mineral movement. Honing produces a matte, consistent face without eliminating natural variation. Each Monument 4x12 reflects the geology of the material it came from rather than a manufactured surface, which is what distinguishes quarried limestone from engineered alternatives.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Monument 4x12 limestone tile suitable for flooring?

Yes, Monument 4x12 limestone tile is suitable for interior flooring applications, including installations in kitchens, hallways, and bathrooms when properly sealed and installed with correct substrate preparation.

### Is Monument 4x12 honed limestone suitable for shower walls and floors?

Yes, Monument 4x12 can be used as a shower tile for walls and floors when sealed before and after grouting to protect against moisture absorption. For shower floor applications, slip resistance can be increased by sizing grout joints to a minimum of ⅛" and/or applying an anti-slip treatment after installation. Periodic resealing is recommended to maintain long-term performance under continuous water exposure.

### What color is the Monument honed limestone tile?

Monument honed limestone is a muted grey with balanced warm and cool undertones that shift subtly under different lighting conditions. The surface reads as a grounded neutral rather than a flat tone, with natural mineral movement visible throughout each tile. Because the color comes from the stone itself rather than an applied surface treatment, the palette remains stable across the full body of the tile as it wears.

### What colors look good with the Monument 4x12 limestone?

Monument 4x12 pairs well with warm whites, soft creams, and muted beige tones that preserve its neutral grey character without flattening its natural variation. The tile also works well with light to medium woods like oak or walnut, which add warmth and contrast against the cool surface. Muted greens and dusty blues introduce subtle color while staying aligned with the stone's natural mineral undertones, and unlacquered brass fixtures draw out the warmer notes in the palette.

### How do I clean honed limestone?

To clean honed limestone, use a pH-neutral stone cleaner with water and a soft cloth or mop. Avoid acidic or abrasive cleaning products, including vinegar and bleach, which can etch the stone and damage the sealed surface. Regular sweeping removes abrasive debris, and prompt cleaning of spills helps prevent staining. Proper sealing at each stage of installation supports easier maintenance over time.

### Is limestone durable?

Yes, limestone is highly durable when used in appropriate applications and maintained correctly. It performs reliably under regular foot traffic, though it remains a porous natural stone and requires sealing to protect against moisture and staining. With proper care, the material maintains its structural integrity and develops a subtle patina over time that reflects normal architectural use rather than wear.
