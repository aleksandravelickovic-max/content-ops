## How It's Made

Our Eucalyptus 2x8 satin green ceramic tile comes from a region of Japan where ceramic production has been practiced for more than a millennium. The process begins with carefully selected raw clay, refined and formed through extrusion to achieve the consistent 2x8 rectangular profile that defines the line.

Once formed, tiles dry under controlled conditions before high-temperature firing. The high-firing process produces the density, low porosity, and long-term durability that distinguish Japanese ceramic production from other ceramic traditions. The satin finish is achieved through a controlled glazing process that produces the serene green surface characteristic of Eucalyptus.

Subtle shifts in tone and surface texture develop during firing, giving each tile a measured degree of variation while maintaining a composed overall appearance across the installation. Up close, the handcrafted quality of the production process becomes visible in the gentle play of the satin glaze against the clay body beneath.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is satin finish ceramic tile?

Satin finish ceramic tile is a ceramic tile with a soft, low-luster surface that sits between matte and gloss. The finish reflects light gently without mirroring it, producing a composed surface that reads as quieter than a high-gloss glaze and more refined than a flat matte. Satin ceramics are particularly well suited to walls, where the subtle sheen adds depth without visual noise.

### Does Eucalyptus 2x8 require pre-grout sealing?

No, Eucalyptus 2x8 does not require pre-grout sealing as a grout release. This is one of the practical differences between satin and matte ceramic tile. Matte finishes can absorb grout pigment during application and must be sealed beforehand to prevent haze. The sealed surface of a satin glaze allows grout to be applied and cleaned off directly. For wet applications such as showers and pools, seal the tile and grout lines after grouting to protect against continuous moisture exposure.

### Where does Eucalyptus 2x8 work best in a home?

Eucalyptus 2x8 works particularly well on kitchen backsplashes, bathroom walls, and shower enclosures, where the serene green glaze introduces color at close range. The 2x8 subway format lends itself to these contexts naturally, whether installed in a horizontal running bond behind a stove or stacked vertically in a shower to emphasize height.

### How does the 2x8 format work in an installation?

The 2x8 format is an elongated subway tile that introduces a clear directional quality to the surface. Installed horizontally, the format emphasizes the width of a wall and works well on backsplashes and lower wall applications. Installed vertically, it draws the eye upward and performs particularly well in shower enclosures and floor-to-ceiling wall installations. Herringbone and stacked arrangements each change how the format reads, and confirming the orientation with the installer before the dry layout is an important preparation step.

### How is Eucalyptus 2x8 ceramic tile cleaned?

For routine maintenance, use a pH-neutral cleaner with warm water and a soft cloth or mop. Sweep or vacuum regularly to remove abrasive debris that can scratch the satin glaze over time. Avoid acidic or abrasive cleaning products, which can affect the glaze and dull the surface. For heavier staining, Miracle Sealants Heavy Duty Cleaner (Acid Substitute) is the recommended product.

### Can Eucalyptus 2x8 be mixed with other ceramic colors in the collection?

Eucalyptus 2x8 mixes well with other colors and finishes in Zia's ceramic collection. Each tile is produced using the same Japanese production methods and shares consistent thickness, format, and surface character across the range. Eucalyptus pairs particularly well with warm neutrals like Linen and softer whites in the collection, where the serene green reads as a considered point of color against a quieter surrounding palette.
