3. Voice playbook (condensed)

Sentence formula (the design-forward signature)

[Specific actor] + [verb of making/placement] + [named material with origin] + [physical/spatial detail].

"Hand-chiseled in Fez, each zellige tile carries a chamfered edge that catches light unevenly across a wall."

Lead pattern (for the opening "Variable per SKU" paragraph)

  • Three-word tagline above (Zia convention: HANDS. HEAT. HISTORY. / POUR. PRESS. PATINA. / FROM FIRE COMES FINERY).
  • First sentence: place + duration anchor (Morocco + 800 years; Nagoya + 1,000 years; France + Morocco; Italy + 500 years; Mexico + millennia).
  • Second sentence: physical move on the tile itself (chiseled, hand-poured, fluted, knapped).
  • Third sentence: cite a specific landmark, era, or use case — never generic.

Verbs to use (vocabulary palette)

  • Making: chiseled, hand-poured, hand-molded, hand-glazed, salvaged, fired, extruded, sculpted, cast, layered, pressed.
  • Placement: clad, wrapped, carried, threaded, enveloping, stacked, cascading.
  • Texture: drippy, chalky, matte, glossy, undulating, shimmering, fluted, honed, knapped, ridged.
  • Color hedges: tone-on-tone, muted, dusty, sun-baked, ochre, blackened.
  • Imperfection: defiance of uniformity, no two… are exactly the same, subtle variations, beautifully nuanced.
  • Time: patina over time, centuries-old, handed down through generations, antique, salvaged.

Words/patterns to avoid

  • Banned (Jamie's list): matrix, systems, full sequencing, technical requirements, vertical surfaces, spaces (use "rooms"), production process (use "craft" or "method"), straightforward maintenance, stock vs. curated.
  • Generic intensifiers: stunning, luxurious, powerful, robust, premium, elevate, transform, unlock, seamless.
  • Rule-of-three openers: "Bold, beautiful, and timeless…" — zero instances in AD/Wallpaper/Remodelista.
  • Em dashes for rhetorical pivot ("it's not just tile — it's a statement"). Avoid. Em dashes appear only as parenthetical asides in editorial pubs.
  • CTA closers ("Explore our collection," "Transform your space"). Editorial pubs never invite the reader to shop.
  • Generic provenance ("made overseas," "sourced globally"). Always Fez, Catalonia, Sausalito, San Miguel de Allende, Bordeaux.

Three rewrites (do-this / not-this)

Generic SaaS-SEO (current) Design-forward rewrite
Our beautiful zellige tiles add timeless luxury to any space. Hand-chiseled in Fez, each zellige tile carries a chamfered edge that catches light unevenly across a wall.
Cantera is offered in eight earthy colorways, each with a distinct mineral composition. Cantera arrives in eight quarried colorways — Mesa, Caldera, Acacia, Sierra, Yuma, Sedona, Badlands, and Volcan — each shaped by the volcanic rock it was cut from.
Perhaps the most versatile tile Zia produces, with endless colorways, patterns, and shapes, our terrazzo line offers a curated mix… Terrazzo has shaped architecture for over 500 years, from historic Italian interiors to the sunlit modernism of Palm Springs. Zia's line covers solids (Neutra, Mirage, Savoye) and pattern series (Highball, Collins, Constellation), each ground and polished to surface its decorative aggregates.
