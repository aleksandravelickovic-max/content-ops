# Zellige Tile for the Bathroom

Zia's zellige collection is a handcrafted Moroccan art form, made by skilled artisans using a technique refined over 800 years. Each tile is hand-pressed into molds, sun-dried, hand-shaped, hand-dipped in glaze, and fired in earthen kilns; chips, pits, and crazing present as signature features of the material. In a bathroom, dynamic glazes that shift with light angle and source are visible at close range throughout the day, making zellige well-suited to an enclosed space where the surface is rarely more than a few feet from the eye.

## In the Shower

For shower walls, glazed zellige is approved for both residential and commercial installations. On shower walls, 1/16" grout joints or edge-to-edge installation keeps the tile continuous; variation in glaze depth, chips, pits, and crazing reads as close detail. Each tile shifts in tone throughout the day as light conditions change.

In a small shower, specifying zellige on a single feature wall (the back wall, visible from the bathroom) concentrates the material on a single plane without covering every surface in pattern. Seal glazed zellige according to Zia's [Installation Guides](https://ziatile.com/pages/zellige-installation-guide), whether in dry spaces or in areas exposed to moisture, such as pools, spas, and showers. For edge finishing, specify mitered edges or Schluter strips and bring drywall flush with the finished tile surface before tiling.

## The Floor Question

Glazed zellige is approved for interior [bathroom](https://ziatile.com/pages/bathroom-collection) floors in both high- and low-traffic conditions. On dry floors outside the wet zone (powder rooms, dressing areas adjacent to the bath), it installs without modification. 

On wet floors within or adjacent to a shower zone, it requires specific installation measures: grout joints increased to a minimum of 1/8" and an anti-slip product applied after installation is complete. Ensure floor installations have proper drainage. These measures address the material's DCOF of 0.16, which sits below the ANSI A137.1 minimum of 0.42 for wet-area floors. For any outdoor installation in freeze/thaw climates, zellige is not suitable.

For a warm-toned floor, Zia's [Glazed Earth 4x4 zellige](https://ziatile.com/products/glazed-earth-zellige-tile) and [Amber 4x4 zellige](https://ziatile.com/products/amber-zellige-tile) carry deep, earthy glaze registers.

## Beyond the Shower

Glazed zellige is approved for pool and spa environments, including submerged applications and splash zones. The dynamic glaze responds to water and light together: submerged, the color deepens, and the glaze surface reads as continuous with the water rather than as a boundary above it. Seal glazed zellige according to Zia's Installation Guides for any pool or spa installation.

For pool interiors, [Zia's Prairie Green 4x4 zellige](https://ziatile.com/products/prairie-green-zellige-tile) deepens to an aquatic tone when submerged. For spa surrounds in a warmer register, [Brownstone 4x4 zellige](https://ziatile.com/products/brownstone-zellige-tile) brings a deep, warm tone to splash zones and wet walls.

## The Glaze and the Light

Dynamic glazes mean the zellige color is never flat or fixed. A tile's apparent tone shifts with the light source, glaze application depth, and firing; two tiles from the same colorway can read differently depending on position and time of day. This variance is intentional and is the material's central design quality.

In a bathroom with natural light, cooler colorways read differently under morning light than under evening fixtures. In a bathroom with primarily artificial light, warmer glazes shift less visibly between natural and artificial light. The standard format across the collection is [4x4](https://ziatile.com/collections/zellige?filter=4x4-squares). The [2x6 bejmat](https://ziatile.com/collections/zellige?filter=2x6-bejmat) suits both walls and floors; the 2x2 is the standard mosaic and checkerboard format.

For earthy greens and warm neutrals, [Za'atar 4x4 zellige](https://ziatile.com/products/zaatar-4x4) and [Savanna 4x4 zellige](https://ziatile.com/products/savanna-4x4) sit in the mid-tone register. For installations where pattern amplifies glaze variation, Zia's three-color checkerboard sets bring compound color movement to walls and floors. [The Burnt Sugar \+ Terra Rosa 2x2](https://ziatile.com/products/burnt-sugar-terra-rosa-2x2), [Burnt Sugar \+ Glazed Earth \+ Amber 2x2](https://ziatile.com/products/burnt-sugar-glazed-earth-amber-2x2), and [Tidepool \+ Casablanca \+ Jade 2x2](https://ziatile.com/products/tidepool-casablanca-jade-2x2) each pair distinct glaze registers in a single installation.

Where zellige covers multiple surfaces, order all surfaces from the same production run. Each run varies in tone and glaze depth; ordering the full quantity at once gives the most consistent result across the installation.

## From Zia's Collection to the Wall

The right zellige specification for a [bathroom](https://ziatile.com/pages/bathroom-collection) is a surface-by-surface decision. Shower walls call for 1/16" grout joints, proper sealing, and an installer with specific experience in handmade Moroccan tile. Wet bathroom floors call for a minimum 1/8" grout joint and an anti-slip product. Pool and spa applications call for sealing per Zia's Installation Guides before use.

All zellige installations require protective face and eye coverings when working with thinset, sealants, and grout. See Zia's [Proposition 65 warnings](https://ziatile.com/pages/proposition-65-warnings) for full safety information.

Plan for a minimum of 15% overage on any zellige order. For a more uniform installation, or one spanning multiple surfaces, order up to 25% above the measured square footage.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Zellige in the Bathroom

### Does zellige require sealing in the bathroom?

Glazed zellige requires sealing according to [Zia's Installation Guides](https://ziatile.com/pages/zellige-installation-guide), whether in dry spaces or in areas exposed to moisture, such as pools, spas, and showers. Unglazed Natural Zellige follows a separate sealing protocol; see Zia's Installation Guides for the full steps.

### Is zellige suitable for exterior walls in cold climates?

For exterior walls, zellige is suitable in non-freeze/thaw climates. It is not suitable for any outdoor installation in freeze/thaw climates. For outdoor bathrooms or exterior wall applications in cold regions, contact Zia's team at [info@ziatile.com](mailto:info@ziatile.com) or 310-844-1170 to discuss the project.

### How much variation should be expected in a zellige bathroom installation?

Inherent variation in color, shape, thickness, and glaze depth is characteristic of zellige, with chips, pits, and crazing present as signature features of each tile. This quality is the defining characteristic of a handmade zellige installation. In a bathroom, this variation is most visible on flat wall surfaces at close range.

### How much tile overage is recommended for a zellige bathroom?

Plan for a minimum of 15% overage. For a more uniform installation, or where zellige covers multiple surfaces (walls and floors, or walls and a pool surround), order up to 25% above the measured square footage.
