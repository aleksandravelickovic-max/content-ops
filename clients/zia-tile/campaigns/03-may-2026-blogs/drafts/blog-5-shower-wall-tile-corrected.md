# Shower Wall Tile: How to Use Zellige in Wet Environments

Glazed zellige, a handcrafted Moroccan art form, is approved for shower walls and shower floors. A successful wet-area installation requires three distinct layers: a waterproofing membrane at the substrate, grout joints between tiles, and a topical sealer on the finished surface. This guide covers substrate waterproofing, mortar application, grout selection, and sealing, along with format recommendations for shower walls and floors.

## What a Shower Asks of a Tile

Shower walls are one of the few surfaces in a home viewed from within inches, under changing light, against a wet surface. The right tile for this environment handles moisture at every layer of the system and rewards close viewing.

[Glazed zellige](https://ziatile.com/collections/zellige) meets both conditions. The irregular glaze, chips, pits, and crazing that define each tile become more visible up close, and the fired glaze responds differently to water than to dry light. No two tiles are identical; a surface of several hundred tiles in an enclosed shower produces a result that cannot be industrially replicated.

The installation is more demanding than precision-cut tile. Full back-butter mortar is required, an installer with specific experience is necessary, and overage runs higher at a 25% minimum.

## Substrate Preparation and Waterproofing

The waterproofing membrane is the primary barrier against moisture reaching the wall framing and subfloor. Grout fills the joints between tiles; a topical sealer protects the finished surface. Remove any one layer, and moisture finds a path through.

Installation sequence:

1. Install a waterproofing membrane across the full [shower floor](https://ziatile.com/pages/shower-collection) and up the walls to at least showerhead height. Treat all seams and corners per the membrane manufacturer's instructions.

2. Apply medium-bed thin-set mortar to both the substrate and the back of each tile. Standard comb-trowel application is not sufficient for zellige's irregular back profile.

3. Before setting begins, open all boxes, mix tiles from each, and lay them out dry across the full surface to distribute variation intentionally.

4. Set tiles. Allow thinset to cure at least 48 hours before grouting.

5. Fill joints according to Zia's [Zellige Installation Guide](https://ziatile.com/pages/zellige-installation-guide), allow 48 hours to cure, then seal.

Before setting begins, bring the drywall flush with the finished tile surface. At transitions and exposed edges, use mitered edges or Schluter strips.

Hire an installer with specific experience in handmade Moroccan tile. Variation in thickness and edge profile demands a different approach than precision-cut tile.

## How Format Shapes the Shower

Any zellige format from Zia's zellige collection is approved for shower walls. Format affects how the shower reads as much as color does.

### 4x4 Square

The 4x4 square distributes glaze and tonal variation evenly across the wall. In an enclosed shower, the consistent field lets the dynamic glazes of each tile carry the surface. Cool colorways like [Zia's Portuguese Blue](https://ziatile.com/products/portuguese-blue-zellige-tile) can shift in tone under low, indirect light. Warm colorways like [Amber](https://ziatile.com/products/amber-zellige-tile) and [Burnt Sugar](https://ziatile.com/products/burnt-sugar-zellige-tile) contrast with white fixtures and grout.

### 2x2 Square

The [2x2 square collection](https://ziatile.com/collections/zellige-2x2-squares) increases the number of joints across the same surface area, making chips, pits, and crazing more visible and the handmade character more pronounced. The format suits small shower walls and niche features where a denser surface reads well at close range.

### 2x6 Bejmat

The 2x6 [bejmat](https://ziatile.com/collections/bejmat) set horizontally emphasizes the horizontal axis of a narrow shower. Set vertically, it emphasizes the vertical axis, a natural orientation in showers with frameless glass enclosures where there is no hard horizontal terminus. Zia's Jade in a vertical stack suits a tall shower with a single fixed head; Casablanca in herringbone reads as a continuous field without drawing the eye to any single plane.

### 3.5 Hex

[Hexagon formats](https://ziatile.com/collections/zellige-tile-3-5-inch-hex) work on shower walls and are particularly well-suited to shower floors. The geometry increases the ratio of grout joint to tile face, raising the effective grip surface across the installed floor.

## Completing the Surface

For grout selection, refer to Zia's Zellige Installation Guide. Joint sizing differs by surface. Walls: edge-to-edge or 1/16" spacing. Floors: minimum 1/8", required for slip resistance.

Color is worth determining before work begins. The right tone changes how the surface reads, and the only correction once the joints have cured is removal and replacement.

* Matched tone (close to the tile color) reduces contrast between joints and tile faces, keeping the focus on zellige's glaze variation.

* Mid-tone softens the pattern, landing between both effects.

* Contrasting amplifies the grid structure of the installation, making each tile read as a discrete unit.

Once the joints have cured, seal glazed zellige according to Zia's Zellige Installation Guide; this applies in dry spaces and wet areas alike. 

For ongoing care:

* Clean with a pH-neutral cleaner.

* Avoid bleach, vinegar, and acid-based products, which break down the sealant and can affect the glaze surface over time.

* Reseal periodically based on wear and moisture exposure.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Zellige Shower Wall Tile

### What type of tile is recommended for a shower?

Glazed tile is the standard choice for shower walls; the fired glaze provides a moisture-resistant surface that is easy to clean and maintain. For shower floors, slip resistance is the primary technical requirement: the DCOF threshold for wet interior floors is 0.42. Glazed zellige, with a DCOF of 0.16, is approved for both shower walls and floors when installed over a waterproofed substrate with grout joints at a minimum of 1/8" and an anti-slip product applied after installation.

### Does zellige tile waterproof a shower wall?

No, [zellige tile](https://ziatile.com/collections/zellige) does not waterproof a shower wall. A waterproof shower requires a membrane system installed at the substrate level before any tile is set. The membrane prevents moisture from reaching the wall framing; the grout seals the joints between tiles; and a topical sealer protects the grout and tile surface. All three layers are required, and none substitutes for the others.

### What substrate preparation does zellige require in a shower?

The substrate must be flat, stable, and fully cured, with a waterproofing membrane installed before any tile is set. Because each zellige tile varies in thickness and back profile, medium-bed thin-set mortar must be applied to both the substrate and the back of each tile to ensure complete contact and prevent air pockets. Hire an installer with specific experience in handmade Moroccan tile; the irregular thickness and edge profile demand a different approach than precision-cut tile. Allow thinset to cure at least 48 hours before grouting.

### How much overage should be ordered for a zellige shower installation?

Order a minimum of 25% overage above the calculated square footage to account for curation and cuts and to give the installer flexibility to blend tone and surface variation before setting begins. Each production run varies; ordering the full quantity at once gives the most consistent result.
