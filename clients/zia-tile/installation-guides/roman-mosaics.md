# Roman Mosaics Installation Guide

Read this guide in full with your installer before beginning. Roman Mosaics installation requires a skilled installer experienced with marble. Zia Tile is not responsible for installation, ongoing care, or issues arising after receipt and installation of the product.

---

## Safety

Wear a dust mask and eye protection when unpacking, cutting, and installing, and when working with mortar, sealants, and grout.

**⚠️ WARNING: Cancer and Reproductive Harm — www.P65Warnings.ca.gov** (California Prop 65)

For extensive cutting, work outdoors or in a well-ventilated area. Wash hands with soap and water after handling tiles and before eating.

---

## About the Transparent Film

Roman Mosaics ship with a transparent film covering the tile face. This film has a specific removal sequence that must be followed exactly:

- **Do not remove the film before laying.** Keep it attached through cuts and throughout the setting process.
- **Remove the film after the thinset has fully cured** (minimum 48 hours after laying).
- **Remove the film before sealing or grouting.**

---

## Inspecting Your Order

- Inspect all sheets upon receipt. Zia Tile cannot address issues with stone that has already been installed.
- Marble is a naturally occurring stone. Significant variation in size, color, tone, veining, and surface texture is inherent to this material. Minor surface imperfections are a natural part of the look.
- Order at least 15–20% more than your measured area to account for cuts, pattern, variation. For a more uniform result, order 20–25% extra. Matching grout color helps blend the overall aesthetic.

---

## Before You Begin

1. **Blend the sheets.** Mix sheets from all boxes and lay them out across the installation area. Distribute color and tone variation evenly before starting.
2. **Choose your grout color.** Grout color is nearly as important as the stone color. Explore Mapei Flexcolor CQ grouts — a lighter neutral or a color that closely matches the stone. Visit the Zia Tile website or Instagram to see installation examples.

## What You'll Need

**For installation:**
- Sponge and bucket
- High-quality medium-bed thin set mortar
- Trowel
- Water-cooled tile saw with diamond blade
- Grout float
- Mapei Flexcolor CQ grout
- Grout bag (if using a contrasting grout color)

**For sealing:**
- pH neutral cleaner (e.g., Miracle Sealants Heavy Duty Cleaner — Acid Substitute)
- Paint roller, paint tray, sponge, and water bucket
- Lint-free cotton cloth
- 511 Porous Plus Sealer (Miracle Sealants) — all installations
- Fila Matte Finish Protective Wax — interior installations only

---

## Surface Requirements

**Floors:** The subfloor must be even and fully cured — typically a minimum of 30 days. An uncured sub-surface can cause efflorescence deposits or cracks. An uncoupling membrane (such as DITRA) may be advised.

**Walls:** Install only on stable, even surfaces. Marble may be installed directly to drywall in dry areas (e.g., a kitchen backsplash). Never apply directly to a painted surface.

**Commercial and wet areas:** Slip resistance can be increased by widening grout joints and/or applying an anti-slip product after installation. Marble has a DCOF rating of 0.28–0.62 depending on type. Consult a licensed installer on slip-specific requirements before starting.

---

## Installation Steps

1. Make all required cuts with a water-cooled tile saw. **Keep the transparent film attached during cutting.**
2. Apply thin set evenly to the install surface using a trowel, working in one direction to prevent air pockets.
3. Wet the back of each sheet with a sponge, then apply thin set to the back as well.
4. Lay each sheet with the transparent film still attached.
5. Work in sections no larger than 2'×2'.
6. **Press each sheet into place using only the palm of your hand.** Do not use a rubber mallet or hammer — this can create small cracks that grow over time.
7. Ensure even, consistent contact with the adhesive. Keep all sheets perfectly flat.
8. Allow the thinset to cure for at least 48 hours.
9. **Remove the transparent film** after the thinset has fully cured and before sealing or grouting.

---

## Pre-Grout Sealing

**Marble must be sealed before grouting.**

1. After removing the transparent film, clean the stone with a pH neutral cleaner. Avoid acidic cleaners — they damage marble.
2. Apply 511 Porous Plus sealer in thin layers until the stone is fully saturated. Wipe off any excess before it dries. Do not over-apply — thick coats dry unevenly and leave an unwanted waxy film.
3. Allow the sealer to cure, typically 1–2 days. Always follow the sealant manufacturer's instructions.
4. **Do not proceed to grouting until the sealer has fully cured.**

---

## Grouting

1. Using Mapei Flexcolor CQ, fill the gaps between stones with a rubber grout float. Work in sections no larger than 2'×2'.
2. **Do not grout the entire surface at once.** Excess grout left on marble is very difficult to remove and can stain permanently.
3. Wipe away excess grout immediately as you work.
4. If using a contrasting grout color, apply with a grout bag rather than a float to reduce the risk of staining or grout haze.
5. Allow the grout to fully cure, typically 48 hours.

---

## Top-Coat Sealing

Clean and dry the stone completely with pH neutral detergent. Remove all residual mortar and debris. Do not seal over spills, drips, footprints, or other marks.

**Interior installations:**
Apply Fila Matte Finish Protective Wax in thin layers until the stone is fully coated. Wipe off any excess before it dries. Allow 1–2 days to cure before using the space. Follow the manufacturer's instructions.

**Outdoor installations:**
Apply 511 Porous Plus sealer in thin layers until fully coated. Wipe off excess before it dries. Allow 24–48 hours to cure. **Do not use Fila Matte Finish Protective Wax outdoors** — UV exposure causes discoloration over time.

Cover the marble with durable paperboard during and after installation to protect it until construction is complete.

---

## Trim & Edges

Install Roman Mosaics from floor to ceiling where possible, or bring drywall flush with the finished stone surface to eliminate the need for trim.

For exposed edges, miter the marble: cut two pieces at a 45-degree angle so they form a finished corner. This is done on-site by an experienced installer.

If trim is required, Schluter strips are recommended — available at www.schluter.com.

---

## Ongoing Care

Marble is a natural stone product. Expect natural wear and patina to develop over time — this is part of the material's character.

### Cleaning

- Vacuum or sweep regularly to remove abrasive particles that can scratch the surface.
- Clean up spills promptly, especially oil and grease.
- Mop or wipe floors and walls with warm water and a pH neutral cleaner.
- **Use only pH neutral cleaners.** Acidic cleaners damage marble.
  - Regular cleaning: Miracle Sealants pH Neutral Tile and Stone Cleaner
  - Heavy stains: Miracle Sealants Heavy Duty Cleaner (Acid Substitute)

### Re-Sealing

Re-seal as often as needed based on traffic and wear. Always follow the sealant manufacturer's instructions.
