# Cotto Allende Installation Guide

Read this guide in full with your installer before beginning. Cotto Allende installation requires a skilled installer experienced with glazed terra cotta tile. Zia Tile is not responsible for installation, ongoing care, or issues arising after receipt and installation of the product.

---

## Safety

Wear a dust mask and eye protection when unpacking, cutting, and installing, and when working with mortar, sealants, and grout.

**⚠️ WARNING: Cancer and Reproductive Harm — www.P65Warnings.ca.gov** (California Prop 65)

For extensive cutting, work outdoors or in a well-ventilated area. Wash hands with soap and water after handling tiles and before eating.

---

## About Crazing

Hairline cracks on the tile surface — called crazing — are a natural characteristic of glazed terra cotta and should be expected on Cotto Allende. Match your grout color as closely as possible to the tile color to minimize the visual appearance of crazing. Contrasting grout will accentuate crazing and should be avoided.

---

## Inspecting Your Order

- Inspect all tiles upon receipt. Zia Tile cannot address issues with tiles that have already been installed.
- Each piece of Cotto Allende is handmade. Significant variation in color, shape, and thickness is expected. Irregular edges, crazing, and small hairline cracks may be present.
- Order at least 15% more than your measured area to account for cuts, variation.

---

## Before You Begin

1. **Clean the tiles.** Dust and residue from shipping are typical. Wipe tiles with a pH neutral cleaner and a soft cloth before installing.
2. **Blend the boxes and plan your layout.** Due to the significant size and color variation in Cotto Allende, mix tiles from all boxes and lay them out before beginning. **Be an active participant in the layout** — select color tones and distribute variation to your liking before any tile is set.
3. **Choose your grout color.** Match grout color as closely as possible to the tile color to minimize the appearance of crazing.

---

## What You'll Need

**For installation:**
- Sponge and bucket
- 1/8" tile spacers
- High-quality medium-bed thin set mortar
- Trowel
- Rubber mallet
- Water-cooled tile saw with diamond blade
- Grout float
- Mapei Flexcolor CQ grout

**For sealing:**
- pH neutral cleaner (e.g., Miracle Sealants Heavy Duty Cleaner — Acid Substitute)
- Paint roller, paint tray, sponge, and water bucket
- Lint-free cotton cloth
- 511 Porous Plus Sealer (Miracle Sealants)

---

## Surface Requirements

**Floors:** The subfloor must be even and fully cured — typically a minimum of 30 days. An uncured sub-surface can cause efflorescence deposits or cracks. An uncoupling membrane (such as DITRA) may be advised.

**Walls:** Install only on stable, even surfaces. Cotto Allende may be installed directly to drywall in dry areas (e.g., a kitchen backsplash). Never apply directly to a painted surface.

**Commercial and wet areas:** Slip resistance can be increased by widening grout joints and/or applying an anti-slip product after installation. Cotto Allende has a DCOF rating of 0.48. Consult a licensed installer on slip-specific requirements before starting.

---

## Installation Steps

1. Make all required cuts with a water-cooled tile saw.
2. Apply thin set evenly to the install surface using a trowel, working in one direction to prevent air pockets.
3. Wet the back of each tile with a sponge, then apply thin set to the back as well, continuing in the same direction.
4. Work in sections no larger than 2'×2', using 1/8" spacers. An open joint of 1/8" is recommended for Cotto Allende.
5. Press each tile into place, then compact with a rubber mallet to ensure even, consistent contact with the adhesive. Keep all tiles perfectly flat.
6. **Wipe off any residual thin set from the tile surface immediately.** Dried thin set is very difficult to remove without damaging the tile.
7. After all tiles are laid, allow the thin set to cure for at least 48 hours before sealing or grouting.

---

## Pre-Grout Sealing

**Cotto Allende must be sealed before grouting. This sealer also acts as a grout release.**

1. Clean the tile with a pH neutral cleaner. Avoid acidic cleaners — they damage Cotto Allende.
2. Apply 511 Porous Plus sealer in thin layers. Wipe off any excess before it dries. Do not over-apply — thick coats dry unevenly and leave an unwanted waxy film.
3. Allow the sealer to cure, typically 1–2 days. Always follow the sealant manufacturer's instructions.
4. **Do not proceed to grouting until the sealer has fully cured.**

---

## Grouting

1. Using Mapei Flexcolor CQ, fill the gaps between tiles with a rubber grout float. Work in sections no larger than 2'×2'.
2. **Do not grout the entire surface at once.** Excess grout is very difficult to remove and can stain permanently.
3. Wipe away excess grout immediately as you work.
4. Clean the tiles with pH neutral detergent to remove any residual mortar or grout haze. Use a wet-vac or squeegee for cleanup.
5. Allow the grout to fully cure, typically 48 hours.

---

## Top-Coat Sealing — Wet Areas

For wet applications (showers, pools, spas), apply a final coat of 511 Porous Plus Penetrating Sealer after grouting. This step protects the grout from continuous moisture exposure.

If you wish to seal the grout as well, do so following the grout and sealant manufacturer's instructions.

Cover the tiles with durable paperboard during and after installation to protect them until construction is complete.

---

## Trim & Edges

Install Cotto Allende from floor to ceiling where possible, or bring drywall flush with the finished tile surface to eliminate the need for trim.

For exposed edges, have your installer miter the tile or finish the edge with a thin layer of grout.

If trim is required, Schluter strips are recommended — available at www.schluter.com.

---

## Ongoing Care

### Cleaning

- Vacuum or sweep regularly to remove abrasive particles that can scratch the surface.
- Mop or wipe floors and walls with warm water and a pH neutral cleaner.
- Cotto Allende can be cleaned like most glazed ceramic tiles. Use only pH neutral products.
  - Regular cleaning: Miracle Sealants pH Neutral Tile and Stone Cleaner

### Re-Sealing

Re-seal wet-area installations at least once a year, or more frequently depending on traffic and moisture exposure. Always follow the sealant manufacturer's instructions.
