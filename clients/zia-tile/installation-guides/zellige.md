# zellige Installation Guide

Read this guide in full with your installer before beginning. zellige installation requires a skilled installer experienced with Moroccan zellige tile. Zia Tile is not responsible for installation, ongoing care, or issues arising after receipt and installation of the product.

---

## Safety

Wear a dust mask and eye protection when unpacking, cutting, and installing, and when working with mortar, sealants, and grout.

**⚠️ WARNING: Cancer and Reproductive Harm — www.P65Warnings.ca.gov** (California Prop 65)

For extensive cutting, work outdoors or in a well-ventilated area. Wash hands with soap and water after handling tiles and before eating.

---

## Glazed vs. Unglazed Natural zellige

This guide covers both glazed and unglazed natural zellige. Several steps differ between the two. Instructions that apply only to unglazed natural are marked with **(Unglazed only)**.

---

## About zellige Characteristics

Authentic zellige contains intentional variation. Approximately 15–20% of tiles may have chips, pits, crazing, edge imperfections, or hairline cracks — these are characteristics of traditional hand-production, not defects. Grout fills and blends these features. Work with these qualities; they are part of the aesthetic.

Samples may not exactly match later orders due to the handmade nature of the tile and variation between production batches.

---

## Inspecting Your Order

- Inspect all tiles upon receipt. Zia Tile cannot address issues with tiles that have already been installed.
- Handmade zellige has significant variation in color, shape, and thickness. Irregular edges, crazing, pits, and hairline cracks are all standard. Ink marks from cutting can be removed with a damp cloth.
- Order at least 15% more than your measured area to account for cuts, variation, and breakage. Order 20–25% more if you prefer a more curated appearance — the extra allows your installer to select and arrange tiles during the dry layout.

---

## Before You Begin

1. **Blend the boxes.** Mix tiles from all boxes and lay them out across the full installation area. Distribute variation evenly before starting.
2. **Soak glazed tiles.** Soak glazed zellige in room-temperature water for 1–2 minutes before installing. **(Unglazed only: do not soak unglazed natural zellige** — soaking prevents proper adhesion and can cause efflorescence.)
3. **Discuss the desired look with your installer.** zellige can be installed organically (embracing natural variation) or with a more level, curated appearance. Communicate your preference before starting — the approach affects how the installer applies thin set and manages variation.
4. **Grout color affects the overall aesthetic significantly.** Match grout color closely to the tile color to complement the variation. Visit the Zia Tile website or Instagram to see installation examples.

## What You'll Need

**For installation:**
- Sponge and bucket
- Tile leveling spacers (wedge-style)
- High-quality medium-bed thin set mortar
- Trowel

**Grout:**
- Glazed zellige: Mapei Ultracolor Plus FA
- Unglazed natural zellige: Mapei Flexcolor CQ

- Grout float (or rubber spatula for mosaics)
- Water-cooled tile saw with diamond blade

**For sealing (varies by finish — see Sealing sections below):**
- pH neutral cleaner (e.g., Miracle Sealants Heavy Duty Cleaner — Acid Substitute)
- Paint roller, paint tray, sponge, and water bucket
- Lint-free cotton cloth
- 511 Porous Plus Sealer (Miracle Sealants) — wet areas (glazed) and all applications (unglazed)
- Fila Matte Wax Sealer — unglazed natural only, interior applications

## Surface Requirements

All substrates must be well-prepared and leveled by a licensed professional before zellige is laid.

**Wall installations:** Tiles may be placed edge-to-edge or with a 1/16" spacing. Use wedge spacers to align the naturally uneven edges.

**Floor installations:** A minimum 1/8" grout spacing is recommended. Stagger the layout to increase slip resistance.

**Commercial and wet areas:** Slip resistance can be increased by widening grout joints and/or applying an anti-slip product after installation. Glazed zellige has a DCOF rating of 0.16. Consult a licensed installer on slip-specific requirements before starting. **Do not dry-stack zellige** — grout protects tile edges over time and is required for all installations.

---

## Installation Steps

1. Make all required cuts with a water-cooled tile saw.
2. Soak glazed tiles for 1–2 minutes. **(Unglazed only: wipe with a damp cloth instead — do not soak.)**
3. Apply thin set to the install surface and also butter the back of each tile individually. Varying the thin set thickness allows for a level installation even with naturally uneven tiles.
4. Place tiles individually using wedge spacers to align edges. Work in sections no larger than 2'×2'. **(Unglazed only: work in sections no larger than 2'×2' and do not exceed this.)**
5. Press each tile firmly into place. Level as placed.
6. **(Unglazed only: wipe off any residual thin set from the tile surface immediately.** Dried thin set is very difficult to remove without damaging unglazed tile.)
7. Allow the thin set to cure for at least 48 hours before sealing or grouting.

---

## Pre-Grout Sealing — Unglazed Natural Only

**Glazed zellige does not require pre-grout sealing. Unglazed natural zellige must be sealed before grouting.**

1. Clean the tile with a pH neutral cleaner.
2. Apply 511 Porous Plus sealer in thin layers until the tile is fully saturated. Wipe off any excess before it dries. Do not over-apply — thick coats dry unevenly and leave an unwanted waxy film.
3. Allow the sealer to cure, typically 48 hours. Always follow the sealant manufacturer's instructions.
4. **Do not proceed to grouting until the sealer has fully cured.**

---

## Grouting

1. Use a grout float (or rubber spatula) to fill all spaces, gaps, pits, chips, and cracks between tiles. Work in sections no larger than 2'×2'.
   - Glazed zellige: use Mapei Ultracolor Plus FA
   - Unglazed natural: use Mapei Flexcolor CQ
2. **Do not dry-stack zellige** — grout is required in all installations to protect tile edges.
3. Remove excess grout immediately with a damp sponge. Dried grout is very difficult to remove.
4. Allow the grout to fully cure, typically 48 hours.

---

## Top-Coat Sealing

### Glazed zellige — Wet Areas Only

After grouting, apply a coat of 511 Porous Plus Penetrating Sealer to all surfaces in wet areas (showers, pools, spas). This protects the grout from continuous moisture.

Verify the surface is stable and properly waterproofed before installation in wet areas. Use a wet-area appropriate adhesive and grout.

If you wish to seal the grout, follow the grout and sealant manufacturer's instructions.

### Unglazed Natural zellige

Clean and dry the tiles completely before sealing. Do not seal over spills, drips, footprints, or other marks.

**Interior installations:**
Apply Fila Matte Wax Sealer in thin layers. Wipe off any excess before it dries. Allow 1–2 days to cure before using the space. Follow the manufacturer's instructions.

**Outdoor installations:**
Apply 511 Porous Plus sealer in thin layers until fully coated. Wipe off excess before it dries. Allow 24–48 hours to cure. **Do not use Fila Matte Wax Sealer outdoors** — UV exposure causes discoloration over time.

## Trim & Edges

Install zellige from floor to ceiling where possible, or bring drywall flush with the finished tile surface to eliminate the need for trim.

For exposed edges, miter the tile: cut two pieces at a 45-degree angle so they form a finished corner. This is done on-site by an experienced installer.

If trim is required, Schluter strips are recommended — available at www.schluter.com.

---

## Ongoing Care

zellige wears like a natural stone product. Expect fine surface lines (crazing), patina, and natural aging over time — this is part of the material's appeal.

### Cleaning

- Vacuum or sweep regularly to remove abrasive particles that can scratch the surface.
- Clean up spills promptly. **(Unglazed only: spills on unsealed unglazed zellige may be permanent — clean immediately.)**
- Use only pH neutral cleaners.
  - Regular cleaning: Miracle Sealants pH Neutral Tile and Stone Cleaner
  - Heavy stains: Miracle Sealants Heavy Duty Cleaner (Acid Substitute)

### Re-Sealing

- Unglazed natural zellige: re-seal every 1–2 years minimum.
- Glazed zellige in wet areas: re-seal every 1–2 years minimum.
- Areas exposed to high moisture may require more frequent re-sealing.
- Always follow the sealant manufacturer's instructions.
