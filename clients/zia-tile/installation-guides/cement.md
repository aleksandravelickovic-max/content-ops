# Cement Tile Installation Guide

Read this guide in full with your installer before beginning. Cement tile installation requires a skilled installer experienced with handmade cement tiles. Zia Tile is not responsible for installation, ongoing care, or issues arising after receipt and installation of the product.

---

## Safety

Wear a dust mask and eye protection when unpacking, cutting, and installing, and when working with mortar, sealants, and grout.

**⚠️ WARNING: Cancer and Reproductive Harm — www.P65Warnings.ca.gov** (California Prop 65)

For extensive cutting, work outdoors or in a well-ventilated area. Wash hands with soap and water after handling tiles and before eating.

---

## Inspecting Your Order

- Inspect all tiles upon receipt. Zia Tile cannot address issues with tiles that have already been installed.
- Cement tiles are handmade. Subtle variations in color, shape, and thickness are expected. Irregular edges and minor crazing are a normal part of this tile.
- Order at least 15% more than your measured area to account for cuts, pattern, variation.

---

## Before You Begin

1. **Blend the boxes.** Mix tiles from all boxes and lay them out across the full installation area. Distribute variation evenly before starting.
2. **Choose your grout color.** Grout color significantly affects the final look. A lighter neutral Mapei Flexcolor CQ grout works for a traditional aesthetic; matching the grout closely to the tile color gives a more modern look. Visit the Zia Tile website or Instagram to see installation examples.

## What You'll Need

**For installation:**
- Sponge and bucket
- 1/16" tile spacers
- High-quality medium-bed thin set mortar
- Trowel
- Water-cooled tile saw with diamond blade
- Grout float
- Mapei Flexcolor CQ grout (neutral color)
- Grout bag (if using a contrasting grout color)

**For sealing:**
- pH neutral cleaner (e.g., Miracle Sealants Heavy Duty Cleaner — Acid Substitute)
- Paint roller, paint tray, sponge, and water bucket
- Lint-free cotton cloth
- 511 Porous Plus Sealer (Miracle Sealants) — all installations
- Fila Matte Finish Protective Wax — interior installations only

---

## Surface Requirements

**Floors:** The subfloor must be even and fully cured — typically a minimum of 30 days. An uncured sub-surface can cause efflorescence deposits or cracks. An uncoupling membrane (such as DITRA) may be advised.

**Walls:** Install only on stable, even surfaces such as cement backer board. Cement tile may be installed directly to drywall in dry areas (e.g., a kitchen backsplash). Never apply directly to a painted surface.

**Commercial and wet areas:** Slip resistance can be increased by widening grout joints and/or applying an anti-slip product after installation. Cement tile has a DCOF rating greater than 1. Consult a licensed installer on slip-specific requirements before starting.

---

## Installation Steps

1. Make all required cuts with a water-cooled tile saw.
2. Apply thin set evenly to the install surface using a trowel, working in one direction to prevent air pockets.
3. Wet the back of each tile with a sponge, then apply thin set to the back as well, continuing in the same direction.
4. Where tiles are uneven, vary the thin set thickness slightly to achieve a level installation.
5. Work in sections no larger than 2'×2', using 1/16" spacers to separate tiles.
6. **Press each tile firmly by hand only.** Do not use a rubber mallet or hammer — this can damage cement tiles.
7. Keep all tiles perfectly flat during installation.
8. **Wipe off any residual thin set from the tile surface immediately.** Dried thin set is very difficult to remove without damaging the tile.
9. After all tiles are laid, allow the thin set to cure for at least 48 hours before sealing or grouting.

---

## Pre-Grout Sealing

**Cement tile must be sealed before grouting.**

1. Clean the tile surface with a pH neutral cleaner. Avoid acidic cleaners — they damage cement tiles.
2. Apply 511 Porous Plus sealer in thin layers until the tile is fully saturated. Wipe off any excess before it dries. Do not over-apply — thick coats dry unevenly and leave an unwanted waxy film.
3. Allow the sealer to cure, typically 24–48 hours. Always follow the sealant manufacturer's instructions.
4. **Do not proceed to grouting until the sealer has fully cured.**

---

## Grouting

1. Using Mapei Flexcolor CQ, fill the gaps between tiles with a rubber grout float. Work in sections no larger than 2'×2'.
2. **Do not grout the entire floor at once.** Excess grout left on cement tile is very difficult to remove and can stain permanently.
3. Wipe away excess grout immediately as you work.
4. If using a contrasting grout color, apply with a grout bag rather than a float to reduce the risk of staining or grout haze.
5. Allow the grout to fully cure, typically 48 hours.

---

## Top-Coat Sealing

Clean and dry the tile completely with pH neutral detergent. Remove all residual mortar and debris. Do not seal over spills, drips, footprints, or other marks.

**Interior installations:**
Apply Fila Matte Finish Protective Wax in thin layers until the tile is fully coated. Wipe off any excess before it dries. Allow 1–2 days to cure before using the space. Follow the manufacturer's instructions.

**Outdoor installations:**
Apply 511 Porous Plus sealer in thin layers until fully coated. Wipe off excess before it dries. Allow 24–48 hours to cure. **Do not use Fila Matte Finish Protective Wax outdoors** — UV exposure causes discoloration over time.

Cover the tiles with durable paperboard during and after installation to protect them until construction is complete.

---

## Trim & Edges

Install cement tile from floor to ceiling where possible, or bring drywall flush with the finished tile surface to eliminate the need for trim.

For exposed edges, miter the tile: cut two pieces at a 45-degree angle so they form a finished corner. This is done on-site by an experienced installer.

If trim is required, Schluter strips are recommended — available at www.schluter.com.

## Ongoing Care

Cement tile wears like a natural stone product. Expect natural wear, fine crazing lines, and patina to develop over time — this is part of the appeal.

### Cleaning

- Vacuum or sweep regularly to remove abrasive particles that can scratch the surface.
- Clean up spills promptly, especially oil and grease.
- Mop or wipe floors and walls with warm water and a pH neutral cleaner.
- **Use only pH neutral cleaners.** Acidic cleaners damage cement tile.
  - Regular cleaning: Miracle Sealants pH Neutral Tile and Stone Cleaner
  - Heavy stains: Miracle Sealants Heavy Duty Cleaner (Acid Substitute)

### Re-Sealing

Re-seal at least every 1–2 years, or more frequently in areas exposed to moisture or heavy traffic. Always follow the sealant manufacturer's instructions.
