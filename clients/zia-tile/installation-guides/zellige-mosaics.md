# zellige Mosaics Installation Guide

Read this guide in full with your installer before beginning. zellige mosaic installation requires a skilled installer experienced with Moroccan zellige tile. Zia Tile is not responsible for installation, ongoing care, or issues arising after receipt and installation of the product.

---

## Safety

Wear a dust mask and eye protection when unpacking, cutting, and installing, and when working with mortar, sealants, and grout.

**⚠️ WARNING: Cancer and Reproductive Harm — www.P65Warnings.ca.gov** (California Prop 65)

For extensive cutting, work outdoors or in a well-ventilated area. Wash hands with soap and water after handling tiles and before eating.

---

## Glazed vs. Unglazed Natural zellige

This guide covers both glazed and unglazed natural zellige mosaics. Several steps differ between the two. Instructions that apply only to unglazed natural are marked with **(Unglazed only)**.

---

## About zellige Characteristics

Authentic zellige contains intentional variation. Approximately 15–20% of tiles may have chips, pits, crazing, edge imperfections, or hairline cracks — these are characteristics of traditional hand-production, not defects. Grout fills and blends these features.

Samples may not exactly match later orders due to the handmade nature of the tile and variation between production batches.

---

## Inspecting Your Order

- Inspect all sheets upon receipt. Zia Tile cannot address issues with tiles that have already been installed.
- Order at least 15–20% more than your measured area to account for cuts, variation. Order 20–25% more if you prefer a more curated, uniform appearance.

---

## Before You Begin

1. **Blend the sheets.** Mix sheets from all boxes and lay them out across the full installation area. Distribute variation evenly before starting.
2. **Soak glazed tiles.** Soak glazed zellige mosaic sheets in room-temperature water for 1–2 minutes before installing. **(Unglazed only: do not soak** — use a damp cloth instead.)
3. **Plan your mosaic layout.** zellige mosaics arrive on mesh backing. Sheets can be installed whole or individual tiles can be separated from the mesh for more precise placement and spacing.
4. **Choose your grout color.** Grout color significantly affects the final look. Visit the Zia Tile website or Instagram to see installation examples.

## What You'll Need

**For installation:**
- Sponge and bucket
- Tile leveling spacers (wedge-style)
- High-quality medium-bed thin set mortar
- Trowel

**Grout:**
- Glazed zellige mosaics: Mapei Ultracolor Plus FA
- Unglazed natural zellige mosaics: Mapei Flexcolor CQ

- Grout float or rubber spatula
- Water-cooled tile saw with diamond blade

**For sealing (varies by finish — see Sealing sections below):**
- pH neutral cleaner (e.g., Miracle Sealants Heavy Duty Cleaner — Acid Substitute)
- Paint roller, paint tray, sponge, and water bucket
- Lint-free cotton cloth
- 511 Porous Plus Sealer (Miracle Sealants) — wet areas (glazed) and all applications (unglazed)
- Fila Matte Finish Protective Wax — unglazed natural only, interior applications

## Surface Requirements

All substrates must be well-prepared and leveled by a licensed professional before zellige is laid.

**Wall installations:** Sheets may be kept on mesh backing or individual tiles may be removed and placed separately. Use wedge spacers to align the naturally uneven edges. Traditional wall installation uses edge-to-edge placement with minimal grout joints.

**Floor installations:** For larger grout joints, remove individual tiles from the mesh backing and space them by hand. Mesh-mounted sheets result in smaller grout joint spacing. Allow extra time for leveling — uneven floors result from inadequate leveling during installation.

**Commercial and wet areas:** Slip resistance can be increased by widening grout joints and/or applying an anti-slip product after installation. Glazed zellige has a DCOF rating of 0.16. Consult a licensed installer on slip-specific requirements. **Do not dry-stack zellige** — grout is required in all installations to protect tile edges.

**Pools and fountains:** Only glazed zellige is suitable for pools or fully submerged applications. Unglazed natural zellige cannot be used in pools.

---

## Installation Steps

1. Make all required cuts with a water-cooled tile saw.
2. Soak glazed mosaic sheets for 1–2 minutes. **(Unglazed only: wipe with a damp cloth instead — do not soak.)**
3. Apply thin set to the install surface and butter the back of each sheet. Varying the thin set thickness allows for a level installation with naturally uneven tiles.
4. Lay each sheet — with mesh backing intact if keeping it — and press firmly into place. Level as placed. Use wedge spacers to align edges.
5. **(Unglazed only: work in sections no larger than 2'×2' and wipe off any residual thin set immediately.**  Dried thin set is very difficult to remove without damaging unglazed tile.)
6. Allow the thin set to cure for at least 48 hours before sealing or grouting.

---

## Pre-Grout Sealing — Unglazed Natural Only

**Glazed zellige mosaics do not require pre-grout sealing. Unglazed natural zellige mosaics must be sealed before grouting.**

1. Clean the tile with a pH neutral cleaner.
2. Apply 511 Porous Plus sealer in thin layers until the tile is fully saturated. Wipe off any excess before it dries. Do not over-apply — thick coats dry unevenly and leave an unwanted waxy film.
3. Allow the sealer to cure fully before proceeding. Always follow the sealant manufacturer's instructions.
4. **Do not proceed to grouting until the sealer has fully cured.**

---

## Grouting

1. Use a rubber spatula to fill all spaces, gaps, pits, chips, and cracks between tiles.
   - Glazed zellige mosaics: use Mapei Ultracolor Plus FA
   - Unglazed natural: use Mapei Flexcolor CQ
2. **Do not dry-stack zellige** — grout is required in all installations to protect tile edges.
3. Remove excess grout immediately with a damp sponge. Dried grout is very difficult to remove.
4. Allow the grout to fully cure, typically 48 hours.

---

## Top-Coat Sealing

### Glazed zellige Mosaics — Wet Areas Only

After grouting, apply a coat of 511 Porous Plus Penetrating Sealer to all surfaces in wet areas (showers, pools, spas). This protects the grout from continuous moisture.

Verify the surface is stable and properly waterproofed before installation in wet areas.

### Unglazed Natural zellige Mosaics

Clean and dry the tiles completely before sealing. Do not seal over spills, drips, footprints, or other marks.

**Interior installations:**
Apply Fila Matte Finish Protective Wax in thin layers. Wipe off any excess before it dries. Allow 1–2 days to cure before using the space. Follow the manufacturer's instructions.

**Outdoor installations:**
Apply 511 Porous Plus sealer in thin layers until fully coated. Wipe off excess before it dries. Allow 24–48 hours to cure. **Do not use Fila Matte Finish Protective Wax outdoors** — UV exposure causes discoloration over time.

---

## Trim & Edges

Install zellige mosaics from floor to ceiling where possible, or bring drywall flush with the finished tile surface to eliminate the need for trim.

For exposed edges, miter the tile: cut two pieces at a 45-degree angle so they form a finished corner. This is done on-site by an experienced installer.

If trim is required, Schluter strips are recommended — available at www.schluter.com.

---

## Ongoing Care

zellige wears like a natural stone product. Expect fine surface lines (crazing), patina, and natural aging over time.

### Cleaning

- Vacuum or sweep regularly to remove abrasive particles that can scratch the surface.
- Clean up spills promptly. **(Unglazed only: spills on unsealed unglazed zellige may be permanent — clean immediately.)**
- Use only pH neutral cleaners.
  - Regular cleaning: Miracle Sealants pH Neutral Tile and Stone Cleaner
  - Heavy stains: Miracle Sealants Heavy Duty Cleaner (Acid Substitute)

### Re-Sealing

- Unglazed natural zellige mosaics: re-seal every 1–2 years minimum.
- Glazed zellige mosaics in wet areas: re-seal every 1–2 years minimum.
- Areas exposed to high moisture may require more frequent re-sealing.
- Always follow the sealant manufacturer's instructions.
