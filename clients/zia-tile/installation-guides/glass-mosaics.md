# Glass Mosaics Installation Guide

Read this guide in full with your installer before beginning. Glass mosaic installation requires a skilled installer experienced with glass mosaic tile. Zia Tile is not responsible for installation, ongoing care, or issues arising after receipt and installation of the product.

---

## Safety

Wear a dust mask and eye protection when unpacking, cutting, and installing, and when working with mortar and grout.

**⚠️ WARNING: Cancer and Reproductive Harm — www.P65Warnings.ca.gov** (California Prop 65)

For extensive cutting, work outdoors or in a well-ventilated area. Wash hands with soap and water after handling tiles and before eating.

---

## Inspecting Your Order

- Inspect all sheets upon receipt. Zia Tile cannot address issues with tiles that have already been installed.
- Glass mosaics have inherent variation. Order 10–15% more than your measured area to account for cuts, pattern, and breakage.

---

## Before You Begin

1. **Blend the sheets.** Mix sheets from all boxes and lay them out across the installation area. Distribute any variation evenly before starting.
2. **Clean the tiles.** Wipe away any dust from shipping before installing.
3. All mosaic tiles are shipped on paper backing. The paper backing is removed as part of the installation process — see step 2 of Installation Steps below.
4. **Cutting:** Use a water-cooled tile saw with a diamond blade or a radial tile cutter for all cuts.

---

## What You'll Need

**For installation:**
- Sponge and bucket
- Epoxy thin set mortar
- Trowel
- Epoxy grout
- Grout float
- Water-cooled tile saw with diamond blade or radial tile cutter

**For cleaning:**
- pH neutral cleaner (e.g., Miracle Sealants Heavy Duty Cleaner — Acid Substitute)

---

## Surface Requirements

**All installations:** The underlayment must be uniform and in good condition. Cement board or backer board are the standard substrates. **Waterproof underlayment is required in wet areas.**

**Commercial and wet areas:** Slip resistance can be increased by widening grout joints and/or applying an anti-slip product after installation. Glass mosaic tile has a DCOF rating of 0.42. Consult a licensed installer on slip-specific requirements before starting.

---

## Installation Steps

1. Lay out all sheets in the installation area, blended from all boxes, before beginning.
2. **Remove the paper backing** from each sheet before laying.
3. Apply epoxy thin set evenly to the underlayment using a trowel, working in one direction to prevent air pockets.
4. Press each sheet into place, keeping all tiles perfectly flat.
5. **Wipe off any residual thin set from the tile surface immediately.** Dried thin set is very difficult to remove without damaging the tiles.
6. After all tiles are laid, allow the thin set to cure for at least 48 hours before grouting.

---

## Grouting

1. Using an epoxy grout, fill the gaps between tiles with a rubber grout float. Work in sections no larger than 2'×2'.
2. **Do not grout the entire installation at once.** Excess grout is very difficult to remove and can stain tiles permanently.
3. Wipe away excess grout immediately as you work.
4. If using a contrasting grout color, apply with a grout bag rather than a float to reduce the risk of staining or grout haze.
5. Allow the grout to fully cure, typically 48 hours.
6. Clean the tiles to remove any residual mortar or debris. Do not leave spills, drips, footprints, or other marks on the surface.
7. Cover the tiles with durable paperboard during and after installation to protect them until construction is complete.

---

## Ongoing Care

Glass mosaics do not require sealing.

### Cleaning

- Clean up spills promptly, especially oil and grease.
- Wipe surfaces with a pH neutral cleaner.
  - Regular cleaning: Miracle Sealants pH Neutral Tile and Stone Cleaner
  - Heavy stains: Miracle Sealants Heavy Duty Cleaner (Acid Substitute)
