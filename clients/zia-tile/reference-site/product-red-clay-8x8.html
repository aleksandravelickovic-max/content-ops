Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of variation should I expect across the Red Clay 8×8 tiles?

Red Clay 8×8 Cotto carries the natural variation that defines all handmade Cotto tile. Subtle differences in tone, shape, edge profile, and thickness from tile to tile are all part of its character. Before installation, open and mix tiles from all boxes, and dry-lay the entire installation to ensure variation is distributed evenly throughout the design.

What's the difference between Cotto and Cotto Allende, and where does each one belong?

Cotto and Cotto Allende are both made from the same Mexican clay body, but the finish defines their appearance and application. While Cotto remains unglazed, Cotto Allende is finished with distinctive glazes. Because of the varying finishes, Cotto tiles are suited for exterior floors in any climate, but not for applications where they are fully submerged in water, like pools or spas. Glazed Cotto Allende tile is approved for pools and spas, but should not be installed outdoors in freeze/thaw climates.

Are the Red Clay 8×8 tiles suitable for shower floors and wet areas?

Yes. The Red Clay Cotto 8×8 tiles are suitable for shower floors and wet areas, with a Dynamic Coefficient of Friction (DCOF) of 0.98, above the 0.42 wet-floor safety threshold. For commercial wet-floor applications, grout joints should measure at least 1/8", and an anti-slip treatment should be applied to the terra cotta after installation. Sealing is required for all wet-area installations. Additional guidance can be found in the sealing FAQ below.

What's the sealing protocol for Red Clay 8×8 tiles?

Red Clay 8×8 tiles require a two-step sealing process. Before grouting, apply 511 Porous Plus Penetrating Sealer in thin coats until the tile reaches full saturation, then allow one to two days for curing. This initial application also acts as a grout release. After grouting, interior installations should be finished with Fila Matte Finish Protective Wax, while exterior applications require a second coat of 511 Porous Plus, since Fila Matte Wax discolors under UV exposure. Full instructions are available in Zia's Installation Guides, and the Zia team is available at info@ziatile.com or 310-844-1170.

Can I mix the Red Clay 8×8 with other Cotto colorways and formats?

Yes. Red Clay Cotto can be combined with other colorways and formats throughout the Cotto collection. Each Cotto tile is formed from the same San Miguel de Allende clay body and fired through the same kiln process, creating consistency across the line. Red Clay pairs naturally with traditional hues such as Adobe and Fired Earth, while contrasting with the unexpected tones of Blanco, Madera, and Oscura. Please note there will be some variation in size and shape across any of our handmade tiles, though the colorway will not affect the sizing. When incorporating specialty geometric formats, increase overage to 20 to 25%, as perimeter cuts typically require additional material.

What overage should I order for Red Clay 8×8 tiles?

For a standard layout, order 15 to 20% overage to account for curation and cuts. For projects that incorporate specialty formats, plan for 20 to 25% across the full order, as edge cuts can affect the number of pieces needed.
