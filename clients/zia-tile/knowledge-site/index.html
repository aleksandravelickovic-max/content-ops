How this site is organized

The sidebar groups pages into five sections:

  • Editorial. The style guides (v3 active, v2 historical), the editorial voice research, and Jamie's tracked-edit highlights. The voice and rules that govern every piece of copy.
  • Reference. Source-of-truth research files: what the live site says, what the installation guides say, what the editorial voice research found. These are inputs to writing, not outputs.
  • Reference Content. The three actual pieces of customer-facing content that Jamie reviewed on May 12 — the templates all March, April, and May content will be rewritten against.
  • Account State. The full ClickUp channel recap and the workspace README. Context for anyone reviewing the engagement as a whole.

Voice precedence

  • Blogs and editorial collection pages follow Style Guide v3.0 + the Editorial Voice Research — closer to Architectural Digest than to generic SaaS SEO.
  • Product pages follow Style Guide v3.0 — required terminology, freeze/thaw rules, structured sections, the nine-section template.
  • When sources conflict, Jamie's tracked edits take precedence over all prior documentation. v3.0 captures every tracked edit from the May 11–13 review.
