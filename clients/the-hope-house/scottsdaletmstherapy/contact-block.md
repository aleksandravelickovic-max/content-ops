## Crisis note (required)

Every piece of content that discusses mental health treatment must include the following line, or a version of it, near the closing CTA:

> If you are in crisis, call or text 988.

Do not imply that TMS, Spravato, or any clinic service is an emergency treatment. For crisis situations, 988 or emergency services are the appropriate resource.

## Rules

- Never substitute a generic "contact us" without the actual phone number.
- Always include the crisis note (988) in closing CTAs on any mental health content.
- Tone: helpful, informative, and direct — not a hard sell.
- Do not position TMS or Spravato as emergency or crisis interventions.
