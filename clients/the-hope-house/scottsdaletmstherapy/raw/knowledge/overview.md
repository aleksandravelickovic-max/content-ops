## Services

### TMS Therapy (Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation)

FDA-cleared treatment for depression, OCD, and anxiety. Non-invasive; no anesthesia required.

Mechanism: Magnetic pulses stimulate brain cells in the prefrontal cortex, which is underactive in depression. Brain mapping is used to locate precise coil placement (MRI or motor cortex measurement).

Protocol:
- Session length: 20–40 minutes
- Frequency: 5 days per week
- Duration: 4–6 weeks; 30–36 total sessions

Cost: $2,000–$12,000 for a full course. Insurance coverage varies; major insurers increasingly recognize benefits.

Side effects: scalp discomfort, headaches, lightheadedness, muscle twitching. Rare: seizures (higher risk for patients with seizure/epilepsy history).

### Spravato (Esketamine)

FDA-approved nasal spray for treatment-resistant depression. Administered in-clinic under medical supervision. REMS-certified program. 1–2 sessions per week. Rapid-acting results.

### Psychiatric Medication Management

Comprehensive psychiatric evaluation and personalized treatment planning. Addresses biological, psychological, and environmental factors. Includes ongoing monitoring, medication adjustments, and follow-up appointments. Integrates non-medication elements: therapy, nutritional changes, supplements, exercise, and mindfulness.

Collaborative model: works across licensed therapists and TMS team.

Indicated for: depression, anxiety, OCD, ADHD, mood disorders; first-time medication users; patients needing medication adjustment.
