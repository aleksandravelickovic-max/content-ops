## Primary CTAs

Use these calls to action when directing readers to take the next step. Choose the CTA that matches the page context — do not stack all four on every page.

- "Call Now" — use in crisis or high-urgency contexts; pairs with the phone number
- "Get Help" — general entry point; use when the reader's intent is unclear or broad
- "Verify Insurance" — use on insurance-focused sections and landing pages
- "Schedule Free Confidential Assessment" — use when the page goal is initial consultation

## Where it must appear

1. The closing section of every page and every content piece.
2. Any FAQ answers where the reader's next step is to contact the facility (e.g., "How do I start?" or "What insurance do you accept?").

Both phone and email must appear together in the closing section. Do not split them across sections.

## Rules

- Never substitute a generic "contact us" without the actual phone number and email.
- The word "confidential" is appropriate and confirmed — use it when referencing the assessment or intake call.

- Do not use em dashes.
- Match grammatical person to the surrounding copy (second person is appropriate for this client's voice).
