# Scottsdale Detox Service Taxonomy

Source: STYLE-SYSTEM.md, refreshed website intelligence, and local client research.

## Detox Programs

- Medical Detox: Medically supervised detox with 24/7 staff support and individualized planning.
- Alcohol Detox: Supervised detox program for alcohol withdrawal needs.
- Opioid / Heroin Detox: Supervised detox program for opioid and heroin withdrawal needs.
- Fentanyl Detox: Supervised detox program for fentanyl withdrawal needs.
- Benzodiazepine Detox: Supervised detox program for benzodiazepine withdrawal needs.
- Methadone Detox: Supervised detox program for methadone withdrawal needs.
- Prescription Drug Misuse Treatment: Treatment support for prescription drug misuse, limited to confirmed program language.
- Polysubstance Addiction Treatment: Treatment support for clients dealing with more than one substance.

## Facility Differentiators

- Private Rooms: Private-room detox setting for comfort and privacy.
- 24/7 Medical Staff: Round-the-clock medical staff support during detox.
- Medication-Assisted Treatment: Medication support where clinically appropriate. Spell out before MAT.
- Insurance Verification: Admissions support for checking insurance options before treatment.
