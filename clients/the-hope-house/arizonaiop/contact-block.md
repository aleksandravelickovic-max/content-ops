# Contact block — Arizona IOP

The contact block must appear in the closing section of every piece. The `contact-line-check` agent validates that phone, address (where relevant), and at least one CTA appear together in the closing section.

## Canonical values

- **Phone:** (480) 418-1570
- **Address:** 7287 E Earll Drive, Building D, Scottsdale, AZ 85251
- **Email:** contact@arizonaiop.com
- **Website:** arizonaiop.com

## Primary CTAs

Use one or more of these per piece. Match to the content topic:

| CTA | When to use |
|---|---|
| Call Now | General; always acceptable in the closing section |
| Verify Insurance | Pages or sections covering insurance coverage |
| Schedule Free Assessment | Pages or sections addressing first steps or uncertainty |
| Get Help | Sections following fear-based or emotional content |

## Where the contact block must appear

1. The closing section of every piece (blog, service page, program page).
2. Naturally after any section that addresses fear, uncertainty, or first-step hesitation.

Both phone and CTA must appear together. Do not split them across sections.

## Approved phrasings

Closing (general):
> Call (480) 418-1570 to speak with the Arizona IOP team. Same-day placement is available.

Insurance-focused closing:
> To verify your insurance coverage, call (480) 418-1570 or email contact@arizonaiop.com. Arizona IOP accepts Cigna, GEHA, Blue Cross Blue Shield, Aetna, UnitedHealthcare, and UMR.

First-step / uncertainty context:
> If you are ready to take the first step, or just have questions, call (480) 418-1570. A free assessment can be scheduled same day.

After fear-addressing section:
> You do not have to decide alone. Call (480) 418-1570 or schedule a free assessment at arizonaiop.com.

## Rules

- Never substitute a generic "contact us" without the phone number.
- Do not invent a direct staff name or extension — no confirmed individual names exist.
- No em dashes.
- Do not use second person if the surrounding copy is in third person — match the piece's grammatical person.
- Do not imply 24/7 phone availability unless confirmed; "same-day placement" and "24/7 accessibility" are confirmed for the program, not necessarily for phone response.
