## Company Overview

Arizona IOP is an Intensive Outpatient Program facility located in Scottsdale, Arizona. It provides structured addiction treatment and mental health care at the outpatient level, allowing clients to maintain their daily lives — including employment, family responsibilities, and their own housing — while receiving clinical treatment.

The facility is part of a group that began operations starting in 2013/2017. Arizona IOP opened alongside or after the group's residential facility.

Key aspects:
- Primary service: Intensive Outpatient Program (IOP)
- Additional levels of care: Partial Hospitalization Program (PHP), Standard Outpatient Program, Executive Treatment Program, Dual Diagnosis Program, Luxury Rehab Program, Golf Rehab Program
- Located at 7287 E Earll Drive, Building D, Scottsdale, AZ 85251
- Small group model: 12 to 15 clients per group (Arizona industry average is 29)
- Clients maintain daily responsibilities during treatment — cell phones are permitted
- Same-day placement available; 24/7 accessibility
- One of the few centers nationwide providing simultaneous treatment for both addiction and mental health (confirmed site claim)
