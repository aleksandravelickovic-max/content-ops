# Arizona IOP Service Taxonomy

Source: STYLE-SYSTEM.md, refreshed website intelligence, and local client research.

## Programs and Care Levels

- Intensive Outpatient Program: Structured outpatient addiction and mental health treatment for clients who do not need 24-hour medical supervision.
- Partial Hospitalization Program: Higher-structure outpatient level of care, spelled out on first use before using PHP.
- Standard Outpatient Treatment: Lower-intensity outpatient care for clients maintaining daily responsibilities.
- Addiction Treatment: Clinical treatment for substance use concerns delivered in an outpatient setting.
- Mental Health Treatment: Treatment support for co-occurring mental health needs within the confirmed outpatient model.
- Dual Diagnosis Treatment: Simultaneous treatment for addiction and co-occurring mental health conditions.
- Medication-Assisted Treatment: Medication-supported treatment where clinically appropriate. Spell out before MAT.
- Same-Day Placement: Confirmed differentiator for clients who need faster admission support.

## Differentiators

- Small Group Therapy: Groups of 12 to 15 clients, compared with the stated Arizona average of 29.
- Masters Level Licensed Therapists: Confirmed credential class for clinical staff references.
- Neurofeedback Room: Industry-leading neurofeedback room, with no extra technology claims beyond confirmed source language.
