## Where CTAs must appear

1. The closing section of every blog post.
2. Any program or service page where a next step toward treatment is offered.
3. Insurance and admissions pages.

Both the phone number and at least one CTA must appear together — not split across sections.

## Approved CTA phrasing

**Standard close (blog posts):**
> If you or someone you care about is ready to take the next step, The Hope House is here. Call **480-447-4252** or contact us at thehopehouse.com to speak with someone today.

**Insurance-focused close:**
> The Hope House accepts UnitedHealthcare, Blue Cross Blue Shield, GEHA, Aetna, and Cigna. Call **480-447-4252** to verify your coverage — the process takes minutes.

**Executive program close:**
> Treatment at The Hope House is built around your schedule and your privacy. Call **480-447-4252** or visit thehopehouse.com to learn how the Executive Treatment Program works.

**Soft, compassionate close (crisis-adjacent content):**
> Recovery is possible. The Hope House limits each facility to 10 clients at a time so that every person receives individual attention from masters-level clinicians. Call **480-447-4252** — someone is available 24 hours a day.

## Rules

- Never substitute a generic "contact us" without the actual phone number.
- Tone: compassionate and action-directing — not a hard sell, not minimizing.
- Do not promise outcomes in the CTA ("call now and get better" is prohibited).
- Do not reference affiliated companies (Arizona IOP, Scottsdale Detox) in the CTA block.
- "Someone is available 24 hours a day" is an approved phrasing for the 24/7 access claim.
