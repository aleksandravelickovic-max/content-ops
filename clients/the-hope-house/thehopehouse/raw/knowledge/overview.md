## Company Overview

The Hope House is a luxury residential addiction treatment facility in Scottsdale, Arizona. Founded in 2017 by a co-founder motivated by his son's addiction and recovery experience, the facility provides individualized, clinical addiction treatment in a private, upscale setting.

The facility operates on a maximum of 10 clients per location at any time — compared to an Arizona average of 29. This low census model is the central operational and marketing differentiator: it enables individualized attention from masters-level clinicians and 24/7 addiction specialists.

The Hope House holds LegitScript Certification as a treatment center. It has been named "Top Treatment Destination" by Luxury Rehabs (5-star rating) and "Best Suboxone Treatment Center in Scottsdale" by the Addiction Group.

## Primary Audience

The Hope House serves adults seeking private, individualized residential addiction treatment. Key audience segments:

- Adults in active addiction who require residential-level care
- Executives and professionals who need to maintain work obligations during treatment
- Individuals with co-occurring addiction and mental health conditions (dual diagnosis)
- High-income or self-pay individuals who choose a private, upscale setting
- Individuals covered by UnitedHealthcare, Blue Cross Blue Shield, GEHA, Aetna, or Cigna

Families of people in addiction are a secondary audience for trust-building and admissions-guidance content.

## Programs

### Executive Treatment Program
Designed for professionals who cannot fully step away from work obligations. Clients receive private rooms with electronics access and a modified treatment schedule that accommodates professional responsibilities.

### Dual Diagnosis Program
Treats addiction and co-occurring mental health conditions at the same time. A mental health assessment is conducted upon arrival. Conditions addressed include anxiety, depression, and trauma. Equine therapy is part of this program. Equine therapy is confirmed for this program only.

### Luxury Rehab Program
Evidence-based care delivered in an upscale setting. Individual plans are built around each client's situation. The site uses the phrase "addiction is unique to each person." Privacy is explicitly guaranteed under this program.

### Executive Golf Rehab
Combines addiction treatment with golf as part of the recovery experience.

### Luxury Spa Rehab
Combines addiction treatment with spa-focused wellness experiences.

## Content Rules Summary

- Never guarantee recovery outcomes
- Never invent or name individual staff members
- Never claim conditions, programs, or modalities not confirmed on the site
- Never name affiliated companies (Arizona IOP, Scottsdale Detox) — use "partner programs" or "outpatient care is available" only in step-down context
- "Battle" and "fight" are off-brand for addiction — use "confront" and "heal"
- MAT is spelled out "Medication-Assisted Treatment" on first reference; MAT on second reference
- Equine therapy applies only to the Dual Diagnosis Program
- 10 clients per facility is the confirmed number — never inflate it
- Person-first language throughout (no "addict," "junkie," "alcoholic" as identity labels)
