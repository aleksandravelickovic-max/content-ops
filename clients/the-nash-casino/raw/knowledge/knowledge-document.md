\#\#\# \*\*HHR vs. Slot Machines — What\*\*\*\*'\*\*\*\*s the Difference?\*\*

Historical Horse Racing (HHR) machines draw outcomes from a database of past horse races, using an algorithm to determine results. Unlike traditional slots (which use a random number generator), HHR machines blend horse racing data with modern gaming mechanics. This distinction is what allows charitable gaming in New Hampshire.
