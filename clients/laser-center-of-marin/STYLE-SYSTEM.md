## 1. Brand & Audience

Laser Center of Marin is a physician-supervised aesthetic clinic in Marin County, California. In operation since 2003. Locally owned and operated. One of the largest laser centers in the Bay Area.

Provider designations:
- Allergan Black Diamond provider (Botox, Juvederm)
- Galderma Platinum provider (Restylane)

Services: laser treatments, injectables, non-invasive cosmetic procedures. All performed under physician supervision.

**Audience:** Patients seeking medically supervised aesthetic treatments in Marin County and the Bay Area. Typically professionals, individuals interested in laser and injectable treatments, patients who prioritize physician oversight and clinical experience.

## 2. Voice & Tone

**Register:** Professional, welcoming, reassuring.

Content must feel trustworthy and medically grounded. It should not feel promotional, spa-adjacent, or sales-driven. This is a medical practice — write to the standard of a clinical setting, not a lifestyle brand.

| Goal | Not this | Use this |
|---|---|---|
| Communicate results | "Transform your appearance" | "Improve the appearance of [specific concern]" |
| Reference expertise | "The best in Marin" | "Allergan Black Diamond and Galderma Platinum provider" |
| Describe treatments | "Revolutionary laser technology" | "[Specific device/treatment] for [specific concern]" |
| Frame outcomes | "Guaranteed results" | "Results vary based on individual skin type and treatment plan" |

## 3. Terminology

### 3.1 Required terms

- **Physician-supervised** (not "doctor-run" or "medically overseen")
- **Allergan Black Diamond provider** — use when referencing injectable expertise
- **Galderma Platinum provider** — use when referencing Restylane expertise
- Reference injectable brands by name when relevant: Botox, Juvederm, Restylane, Dysport
- **Personalized treatment plan** or **tailored treatment plan** (not "custom plan")

### 3.2 Strict avoidance list

| Term | Reason |
|---|---|
| "guaranteed results" | Cannot guarantee cosmetic outcomes |
| "guarantee" | Same — any form is off-limits |
| "transform your appearance" | Exaggerated claim |
| "look years younger" | Outcome promise — not medically responsible |
| "eliminate" (for a condition) | Treatments improve or reduce, not eliminate |
| "revolutionary" | Vague promotional language |
| "best clinic in Marin" | Unsupported superlative |
| "luxury spa" | Misframes the practice as a spa |
| "affordable" / "affordable prices" | Premium positioning — no price framing |
| "dramatic results" | Exaggerated — implies more than medically responsible |

## 4. Accuracy & Technical Standards

- Do not promise specific outcomes for any treatment.
- Do not present any procedure as universally effective or risk-free.
- Do not make comparative claims against competitor clinics.
- Allergan Black Diamond and Galderma Platinum designations are earned — use them accurately. Black Diamond is an Allergan designation; Platinum is Galderma. Do not conflate.
- When referencing specific treatments (Botox, laser types), reference them by their correct names. Do not invent technical specifications.
- "Physician-supervised" means a physician oversees the practice and protocols — not necessarily that a physician performs every treatment. Do not overclaim the level of direct physician involvement without confirmation.

---

## 5. Specificity Rules

- Prefer specific claims over general ones. "Serves Marin County since 2003" is better than "a long-standing local clinic."
- Use provider designation names (Black Diamond, Platinum) when establishing authority.
- Reference specific treatments rather than vague categories. "IPL photofacial" or "Fraxel resurfacing" is more credible than "advanced laser treatments."
- Attribute patient outcomes to individual factors: skin type, treatment history, compliance with post-care instructions.
