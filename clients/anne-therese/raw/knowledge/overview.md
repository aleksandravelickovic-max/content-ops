## Company Overview

Anne Therese Aesthetic Medicine is a medical aesthetics practice offering injectables, laser treatments, facials, and body contouring procedures. Services focus on non-surgical cosmetic treatments designed to improve skin quality and overall appearance.

Locations: Lewis Center, Gahanna, Cape Coral, and Bonita Springs.

Treatments include Botox injections, Hydrafacial treatments, CoolSculpting, laser-based skin treatments, and other advanced skincare services. Treatments are performed by licensed medical professionals and licensed estheticians using modern aesthetic technologies and clinically guided protocols.

## Primary Audience

Patients typically come from communities surrounding the clinic locations.

Common patient profiles:
- Individuals interested in non-surgical cosmetic treatments
- Patients exploring injectables and facial rejuvenation
- Clients seeking professional skincare treatments
- Individuals looking for medically guided aesthetic care

Many patients are focused on improving confidence while maintaining natural-looking results.

## Core Problems the Clinic Solves

Common concerns:
- Choosing experienced providers for injectables and skin treatments
- Ensuring procedures are performed in a medically supervised setting
- Addressing skin concerns with updated aesthetic techniques
- Receiving treatment recommendations based on individual goals
- Achieving subtle cosmetic improvements rather than dramatic changes

## Brand Positioning

- A medical aesthetics practice focused on non-surgical treatments
- A clinic supported by experienced medical professionals
- A provider that invests in modern aesthetic technology
- A practice emphasizing patient relationships and individualized care

## Brand Voice

A balance of medical professionalism and aesthetic sophistication. Credible and approachable while maintaining a premium aesthetic focus.

## Messaging Themes

**Medical expertise:** Experienced medical professionals and licensed estheticians using clinically guided techniques.
**Technology-driven treatments:** The practice invests in advanced technology and stays informed about emerging aesthetic procedures.
**Individualized care:** Treatment plans developed around each patient's goals, skin concerns, and aesthetic preferences.
**Confidence-focused outcomes:** Many patients pursue aesthetic procedures to improve confidence and maintain a refreshed appearance.

## Writing Considerations

Content should highlight:
- Injectables and facial rejuvenation treatments
- Non-surgical aesthetic procedures
- Modern skincare technologies
- Personalized treatment planning
- Medical oversight and patient safety

Avoid:
- Guaranteed cosmetic results
- Exaggerated transformation claims
- Aggressive marketing language
