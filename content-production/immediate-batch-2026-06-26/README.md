## Why each article was selected, with evidence

### 01 — AI Marketing Coworker (launch pillar)
- **Why:** The Search Atlas Coworker launches this week (Slack live June 2026; Teams + ClickUp July 2026) and has zero blog presence. Selected on the strategic-product-gap criterion, not search demand.
- **Evidence:** No coworker post exists in the 870-URL inventory. Adjacent category demand is real and rising: `agentic marketing summit` 1,450 impr (pos 2.9), `search atlas ai agent` pos 1, and the agentic cluster already ranks at positions 6–14. `atlas-coworker.md` supplies verified product facts.
- **Strategic role:** Category-defining hub for agentic marketing / AI CMO. Anchors internal links from the ~30 existing agentic posts. Establishes proprietary language (Multiplayer Marketing, the self-healing loop).
- **Honest caveat:** "ai marketing coworker" is a contested SERP (Coworker.ai owns the brand term; Adobe, Claude Cowork, Motion, Sintra present). Ranking upside is medium; the value is category ownership and launch support.

### 02 — White-Label SEO Platform for Agencies (highest demand)
- **Why:** Largest unaddressed commercial cluster in GSC, aimed at Search Atlas's primary audience (agencies).
- **Evidence (GSC, 90 days):** `white label seo tool` 14,982 impr / pos 23.1; `white label seo platform` 13,544 / pos 22.1; `white label seo audit tool` 13,544 / pos 24.1; `white label seo reports` 13,179 / pos 29.9; `white label rank tracker` 11,788 / pos 33.6; `white label seo reporting tool` 11,767 / pos 21.2; plus ~10 more. ~150k combined impressions at positions 21–49. Only the product page ranks (~27); no blog buyer-guide pillar exists.
- **Strategic role:** Captures the agency dual-anchor, feeds the `/white-label-seo-software/` product page, and supports the Coworker launch (agencies = more clients without headcount).
- **Differentiation:** Objective decision framework (margin math, rebranding-depth tiers, what breaks at scale, full reporting sub-cluster), not a self-ranking "best tools" listicle.

### 03 — SEO Automation Software (demand + autonomous-product bridge)
- **Why:** Winnable commercial cluster sitting just off page one, directly showcasing the autonomous execution story (OTTO) that the Coworker extends.
- **Evidence (GSC, 28 days quick-wins):** `automated seo software` pos 9.1 (3,058 impr); `seo automation tools` pos 8.1 (2,764); `seo automation` pos 11.3 (3,134); `ai seo software` pos 12.3 (4,314); `automated seo` pos 9.7 (1,950). Positions 8–13 = realistic page-one reach with strong content.
- **Strategic role:** Connects demand to the product wedge (execution-automation vs data-automation). Bridges the Coworker pillar and the white-label pillar.
- **Differentiation:** Data-automation vs execution-automation split, an automate/keep-human framework, and the guardrails (review, rollback, change logs) that make autonomy safe.
