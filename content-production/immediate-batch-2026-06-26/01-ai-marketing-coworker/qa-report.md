4. **Modal verb "can align"** — same Multiplayer sentence. Rewrote to active "updates the ads."
5. **Possessive apostrophes (x3)** — "team's work," "person's head," "tool's settings." Rewrote to "the work the team would otherwise do," "the head of one person," "the settings of one tool."
