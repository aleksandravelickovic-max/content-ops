## Unique thesis
Most "SEO automation software" surfaces data or drafts content for a human to act on. A smaller class executes live changes itself. The buying decision is which class you need, what you keep human regardless, and whether the execution layer carries the guardrails (review-before-deploy, selective deploy, rollback, change logs, audit trails) that make autonomy safe.
