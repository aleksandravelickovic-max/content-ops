# 08 — 2026 Search & AI-Search Trends Research

## Trend 1 — AI Overviews cut top-position CTR ~58%
- **What changed:** The AIO click penalty roughly doubled in 8 months — Ahrefs measured −34.5% on position-one CTR (Apr 2025) then −58% (Dec 2025).
- **Evidence:** Ahrefs, 300,000 keywords (150k with AIOs / 150k without); position-one CTR for AIO keywords fell 0.073 (Dec 2023) → 0.016 (Dec 2025). Corroborated by Seer (49–65%), Kevin Indig (>50%), Authoritas (47.5%).
- **Source:** Ahrefs — https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overviews-reduce-clicks-update/ (Dec 2025).
- **Implication:** Ranking #1 no longer guarantees traffic; pre-2024 CTR/forecast curves are wrong by half.
- **Audience relevance:** Agencies must reset client traffic baselines; "we got you to #1" is no longer a defensible deliverable on informational queries.
- **Content opportunity:** "What ranking #1 is actually worth in 2026 — real CTR after AI Overviews," with the multi-study comparison table and a recalculated forecast model.
- **Format:** Data-backed analysis post. **Lifespan:** durable. **Confidence:** high.

## Trend 2 — AI Mode crossed 1B monthly users, but is ~0.34% of searches
- **What changed:** Google's conversational AI Mode scaled to 1B+ monthly users in ~12 months; queries "more than doubling every quarter." Gemini 3.5 Flash is the new default; agentic booking + "information agents" roll out summer 2026.
- **Evidence:** Google I/O 2026 (1B MAU). **Counter-data:** SparkToro clickstream found only 0.34% of searches flowed into AI Mode Jan–Apr 2026. Both true: MAU ≠ query share.
- **Sources:** Google — https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/search-io-2026/ (2026-05-19); SparkToro (see Trend 5).
- **Implication:** AI Mode is the trajectory, not yet the volume driver; inline AIOs are where impact lands today.
- **Audience relevance:** Lets credible strategists separate signal from hype for clients.
- **Content opportunity:** "AI Mode hit 1B users — but only 0.34% of searches use it. What the gap means for your 2026 plan."
- **Format:** Myth-vs-data explainer. **Lifespan:** developing. **Confidence:** high (primary for both figures).

## Trend 3 — Being *cited inside* the AIO is the new goal
- **What changed:** AIO coverage expanded (Pew ~18% Mar 2025; aggregators claim ~48% by early 2026 — see flag). Being one of the 3+ cited sources appears to recover clicks.
- **Evidence:** Pew (68,879 searches, 900 US adults): clicks 15% (no AIO) → 8% (AIO present); only 1% click a source inside the summary; AIOs cite 3+ sources 88% of the time; median 67 words.
- **Source:** Pew — https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/07/22/google-users-are-less-likely-to-click-on-links-when-an-ai-summary-appears-in-the-results/ (2025-07-22).
- **Implication:** Goal shifts from "rank above the AIO" to "be one of the cited sources."
- **Audience relevance:** Reframes the agency deliverable from ranking → citation — sellable AEO work.
- **Content opportunity:** "How to become one of the 3 sources Google cites in an AI Overview," with a source-citation checklist (structured data, concise answer blocks, entity clarity).
- **Format:** Tactical how-to. **Lifespan:** durable. **Confidence:** high (Pew); the "cited brands get +35% organic / +91% paid" stat is **medium** (single aggregator — flag).

## Trend 4 — AEO and GEO are converging into one discipline; AEO is the label
- **What changed:** By 2026 most practitioners treat AEO and GEO as one practice (optimize for *mentions/citations* in AI answers); AEO leads in B2B content, GEO persists in academic/technical use. E-E-A-T is the unifying signal.
- **Evidence:** Jasper, Surfer, HubSpot, Frase all publish "AEO/GEO 2026" guides treating them as overlapping.
- **Sources:** Jasper — https://www.jasper.ai/blog/geo-aeo ; Surfer — https://surferseo.com/blog/answer-engine-optimization/ (both 2026).
- **Implication:** Don't pick an acronym dogmatically; clients are confused by the alphabet soup — clarity differentiates.
- **Audience relevance:** "Do I need AEO or GEO?" is a live sales question.
- **Content opportunity:** "AEO vs GEO vs SEO: stop arguing about acronyms — one workflow covers all three." Position SA as the platform that does the unified work.
- **Format:** Definitional explainer + one combined-workflow diagram (don't add a 4th acronym). **Lifespan:** developing. **Confidence:** high.

## Trend 5 — Zero-click hit 68% of US Google searches in early 2026
- **What changed:** Zero-click crossed two-thirds. Only ~276 of every 1,000 searches reach the open web.
- **Evidence:** SparkToro/Fishkin (Similarweb clickstream): 68.01% of US Google searches ended without a click (Jan–Apr 2026), up from 60.45% (2024). Desktop ~50% zero-click, mobile ~77%; AIOs on 20%+ of searches, cutting CTR ~60% when present.
- **Source:** SparkToro — https://sparktoro.com/blog/in-2026-less-than-one-third-of-google-searches-still-send-a-click/ (2026-06-08).
- **Implication:** Click-volume goals are structurally capped; value must be captured in-SERP (brand/citation) or off-Google.
- **Audience relevance:** Forces the reporting conversation — impression share and citation share become the defensible metrics.
- **Content opportunity:** "Google Zero is here: 68% of searches end without a click — the metrics agencies should report instead of sessions."
- **Format:** Authoritative data post + new-KPI framework (link-bait). **Lifespan:** durable. **Confidence:** high.

## Trend 6 — LLM-visibility tracking is now a funded software category
- **What changed:** Brand-citation tracking across ChatGPT/Perplexity/Gemini/Claude/AIOs went from spreadsheets to venture-backed category in ~18 months.
- **Evidence:** Profound raised $96M Series C at a $1B valuation (>$155M total), 700+ enterprises, 10%+ of the Fortune 500. Competitors: Peec AI, Scrunch, Otterly, Allmond, Nightwatch.
- **Source:** Profound — https://www.tryprofound.com/blog/profound-raises-96m-series-c (2026-02-24).
- **Implication:** "AI visibility" is a measurable KPI with a vendor ecosystem; buyers expect a dashboard.
- **Audience relevance:** This is SA's competitive set. Buyers weigh a point tool (Profound/Peec) vs an all-in-one platform — SA's consolidation pitch.
- **Content opportunity:** "AI visibility tools compared: standalone tracker vs all-in-one platform?" (honest matrix, no invented features). Pairs with a "how AI citation tracking works" explainer. **NOTE:** the AI-visibility-native competitors are NOT yet in `raw/knowledge/competitors/` — requires new competitor research before drafting (per `briefable-set.md` blocker A3).
- **Format:** Buyer's-guide comparison. **Lifespan:** durable. **Confidence:** high.

## Trend 7 — LLM citations favor Reddit, Wikipedia, YouTube over brand sites
- **What changed:** LLMs disproportionately cite community/reference platforms; optimization now includes off-site presence, and citation mix differs sharply by platform.
- **Evidence:** 680M+ citation index (Aug 2024–Apr 2026): Reddit ~40% of citations across major models; ChatGPT leans Wikipedia (~13%) + Reddit (~12%); Perplexity ~24% Reddit; Gemini barely cites Reddit (~0.1%). Cite as **ranges** — figures vary by study/quarter.
- **Sources:** Search Engine Land — https://searchengineland.com/ai-search-engines-cite-reddit-youtube-and-linkedin-most-study-473138 ; 5W/PR Newswire — https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/wikipedia-and-reddit-now-drive-over-25-of-chatgpt-citations-in-the-us-new-5w-research-finds-302768339.html (both 2026).
- **Implication:** AEO is partly digital-PR and community work; per-platform strategy matters (Gemini ≠ Perplexity).
- **Audience relevance:** Agencies need a Reddit/Wikipedia/community service line alongside on-page work.
- **Content opportunity:** "Where ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini actually get their answers — and how to be a source," with the per-platform citation-mix table.
- **Format:** Data study/explainer + off-site checklist. **Lifespan:** developing (mixes are volatile). **Confidence:** medium-high.

## Trend 8 — LLMs are now a primary B2B vendor-research channel, peaking mid-funnel
- **What changed:** B2B buyers research and shortlist vendors in LLMs before contacting sales; usage peaks at comparison/shortlisting. AI returns only 3–4 brands per response; top-20 domains ≈ 66% of citations (winner-take-most).
- **Evidence:** Forrester 2026 Buyers' Journey Survey (18,000 buyers): generative AI/conversational search is now the most meaningful vendor-research source, outranking vendor sites and reps. Aggregated stats (94% use AI in purchase; 55% to compare vendors; 68% as primary initial research) are **flagged** — directionally consistent, primary attribution inconsistent.
- **Sources:** Forrester — https://www.forrester.com/research/buyer-insights/ ; Development Corporate — https://developmentcorporate.com/startups/94-of-b2b-buyers-now-use-llms-to-research-software/ (2026).
- **Implication:** If AI doesn't name your brand for "best X," you're invisible at shortlisting.
- **Audience relevance:** The core sales argument for AEO to in-house B2B teams.
- **Content opportunity:** "How B2B buyers shortlist software in 2026 — and why you're invisible if AI doesn't name you." Lead with the verified Forrester finding; soften the precise percentages.
- **Format:** Buyer-journey explainer. **Lifespan:** durable. **Confidence:** high (directional); medium (exact %).

## Trend 9 — Reddit dominates the long-tail commercial queries B2B buyers run
- **What changed:** Reddit out-ranks major B2B SaaS brands on conversational multi-word queries and feeds the LLMs (Trend 7) — a compounding loop.
- **Evidence:** Foundation (Ross Simmonds), 8,566 keywords vs 13 B2B SaaS domains: Reddit beats all competitors on 66.5% of Sales-Tech keywords; wins 73–87% of 6+ word queries; 67.3% win rate on $50+ CPC keywords; 77% of winning volume is non-review queries.
- **Source:** Foundation — https://foundationinc.co/lab/reddit-b2b-saas (2026-04-30).
- **Implication:** Owned content alone can't win the long tail; community presence is table stakes for both ranking and citation.
- **Audience relevance:** A Reddit/community SEO service line most teams don't yet offer.
- **Content opportunity:** "Reddit beats your SaaS site on 2 of 3 commercial keywords — the community-SEO playbook," with the vertical win-rate table.
- **Format:** Data-driven playbook. **Lifespan:** durable. **Confidence:** high.

## Trend 10 — Agentic commerce protocols shipped; agents transact, not just recommend
- **What changed:** OpenAI launched Instant Checkout + Agentic Commerce Protocol (with Stripe); Google shipped Universal Commerce Protocol and donated Agent Payments Protocol (AP2) to the FIDO Alliance (2026-04-28).
- **Evidence:** ACP lets US ChatGPT users buy in-chat (1M+ Shopify merchants onboarding, 4% fee). McKinsey projects $3–5T agent-orchestrated retail spend by 2030 (**forecast — flag**).
- **Sources:** OpenAI — https://openai.com/index/buy-it-in-chatgpt/ ; Checkout.com — https://www.checkout.com/blog/openai-acp-google-ucp-difference (2026).
- **Implication:** Brands must be discoverable/selectable by agents, not just humans.
- **Audience relevance:** B2B is mostly affected via agentic *research/shortlisting* (Trend 8), not checkout; SA's "agentic platform" positioning maps to the macro shift.
- **Content opportunity:** "Agentic commerce vs agentic research: which one actually affects B2B buyers today." Draw the B2C/B2B line; don't overclaim checkout for B2B.
- **Format:** Trend explainer. **Lifespan:** developing. **Confidence:** high (protocols); medium (B2B impact).

## Trend 11 — "Agentic marketing" splits into two meanings buyers conflate
- **What changed:** It means (a) marketers using agents to run their own ops, and (b) optimizing so agents acting for customers recommend you. SA sits on (a) — the operational side.
- **Evidence:** Profound markets both (analytics + "Profound Agents" that execute); industry guides split along the two definitions.
- **Source:** Profound (as Trend 6); EICTA — https://www.eicta.iitk.ac.in/knowledge-hub/artificial-intelligence/ai-agents-digital-marketing (2026).
- **Implication:** Buyer confusion = authority opportunity.
- **Audience relevance:** Clarifying the two meanings separates SA's "agentic AI marketing platform" from AEO-only point tools — reusable as sales enablement.
- **Content opportunity:** "Agentic marketing means two things — make sure your vendor solves the one you have." (SA already ranks pos ~8.7 for /agentic-marketing/ — expand into a pillar.)
- **Format:** Positioning explainer. **Lifespan:** developing. **Confidence:** medium.

## Trend 12 — Three core updates in 7 months reward information gain, punish AI farms
- **What changed:** Dec 2025, Mar 2026, May 2026 core updates in quick succession. Continued emphasis on original insight + genuine expertise. AI content farms without expertise dropped 50–90%; disclosed AI-assisted publishers with editorial standards stayed stable.
- **Evidence:** May 2026 update ran May 21–Jun 2 ("felt larger" than March); Google advises a full week post-completion before analyzing Search Console (clean window ~Jun 9). **This directly explains the searchatlas.com impression deflation in file 04.**
- **Sources:** Search Engine Land — https://searchengineland.com/google-may-2026-core-update-rollout-is-now-complete-479119 ; Search Engine Roundtable — https://www.seroundtable.com/google-may-2026-core-update-done-41435.html (2026-06).
- **Implication:** Volume AI content without expertise is a liability; "information gain" is the durable winning strategy.
- **Audience relevance:** SA's scaled AI-content use case must pair with editorial standards + expertise signals — the responsible-AI-content narrative SA should own (and apply to itself).
- **Content opportunity:** "What three core updates in 7 months tell us: how to scale AI content without getting hit," using stable-vs-penalized contrasts; ties to the Scholar grading capability (file 07 §5).
- **Format:** Update-recap + information-gain/E-E-A-T checklist. **Lifespan:** durable (principle); the specific update dates will age. **Confidence:** high.

## Trend 13 — "AI visibility" is the durable buyer-facing term
- **What changed:** Practitioners standardized on **AEO** (discipline), **GEO** (technical synonym), and **"AI visibility" / "AI search" / "get cited in AI"** (the plain-language outcome buyers ask for). "LLMO"/"LLM SEO" trail.
- **Evidence:** HubSpot, Surfer, Frase, Jasper anchor on AEO; the tool category markets itself as "AI visibility tools"; Profound brands "the marketing platform for the AI era," sidestepping acronyms.
- **Sources:** HubSpot — https://blog.hubspot.com/marketing/answer-engine-optimization-trends ; Nick Lafferty — https://nicklafferty.com/blog/llm-tracking-tools/ (2026).
- **Implication:** Target "AEO" + "AI visibility" as primary; "GEO"/"get cited in ChatGPT" as secondary. Buyers search the outcome more than the acronym.
- **Audience relevance:** SA content should lead with outcome language and use AEO/GEO as the technical anchor — matching both acronym-fluent agencies and outcome-focused in-house marketers.
- **Content opportunity:** A terminology/glossary hub ("AEO, GEO, LLMO, AI visibility — what each means and which to use") — a strong internal-linking hub and the natural home of the AI-Search cluster.
- **Format:** Glossary/pillar page. **Lifespan:** developing. **Confidence:** high.
