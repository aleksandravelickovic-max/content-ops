The recommendation

The site is past its peak, not growing. The Q3 win is to fix snippets, consolidate duplicates, and build one real AI-Search cluster on content Search Atlas already ranks for — not to publish more.

Why — the four findings

01

Flat clicks, deflating impressions, brand carries the site

Clicks flat (+1.6%) while impressions fell −38% and position improved. Branded queries are ~half of all clicks. The lever is non-branded demand SA already ranks 4–20 for — not more impressions.

02

Thousands of clicks sit in broken snippets

/features — 190k impr, pos 7.3, 0.05% CTR — models ~7,500 recoverable clicks alone. A ~30-page title/meta sprint is the highest-ROI, lowest-effort work in Q3.

03

The AI-Search cluster is best-fit and orphaned

SA already ranks for aeo, geo, aio, agentic — but 20+ posts have no hub, mis-link to AI-CMO, and never route to the LLM Visibility product (itself orphaned at pos 26.8). The durable 2026 demand shift, with a product to match.

04

Duplicates & a staging subdomain split equity

Perplexity-alternatives ×2, Claude-review ×2, a 4-way Domain-Authority split, and labs.searchatlas.com staging leaking into the index. Cheap to fix, immediate recovery.

The three moves

Move 01 · recover

Fix the snippets

Title / meta / answer-snippet sprint on ~30 high-impression, low-CTR pages. Target ≥8,000 recovered clicks at near-zero effort.

Move 02 · clean

Consolidate & de-index

Merge + 301 the duplicates, canonicalize FAQ-anchor URLs, and block the leaking staging subdomain. Six cannibalization clusters resolved.

Move 03 · build

Stand up AI-Search discoverability

One hub + internal-linking sprint + tool routing across the existing AEO/GEO cluster, into the LLM Visibility product. Target +30% cluster clicks.
