Summary

LinkGraph is visible enough to be found by AI systems, but not yet defined strongly enough to be recommended as a leading enterprise SEO, AEO, or GEO provider.

The strongest current association is link building. That is valuable because authority acquisition is central to SEO and AI citation visibility, but it is too narrow for the enterprise buyer. The opportunity is to connect LinkGraph's authority-building strength to a broader category claim: enterprise SEO for brands that need to win in Google, AI answers, and citation-led search.

Main risk LinkGraph can be included as one option in AI answers without becoming the named authority. That creates weak recall: the brand appears occasionally, but competitors shape the category narrative.
Main advantage LinkGraph has direct access to Search Atlas, which means the team can run the exact workflow that most competitors can only describe: SERP analysis, keyword gaps, LLM visibility, prompt testing, topic monitoring, and iteration from one operating system.

Positioning Diagnosis

The market is not missing another generic SEO agency page. The market is missing a credible bridge between enterprise SEO execution and the new answer-engine layer.

Current machine association

Partial

LinkGraph appears most naturally in link building and authority conversations. In the prompt test, the cleanest mention was for enterprise link building services.

Needed machine association

Target

LinkGraph should be understood as an enterprise SEO and AI visibility partner that combines technical SEO, content systems, link acquisition, digital PR, and Search Atlas intelligence.

The category claim to build around

LinkGraph helps enterprise brands grow search visibility across Google, AI answers, and citation-driven discovery by combining technical SEO, content authority, digital PR, and Search Atlas intelligence.

This claim is broader than link building, but it does not abandon the existing strength. It uses authority acquisition as the wedge into GEO and AEO.

Required page mechanics

  • Answer the core query in the first paragraph.
  • Use definition blocks that LLMs can reuse.
  • Include process sections with named deliverables.
  • Add comparison tables where buyer evaluation is likely.
  • Use FAQ schema on pages with obvious buyer questions.
  • Add Organization, Service, Breadcrumb, and sameAs schema where appropriate.
  • Include proof blocks: screenshots, anonymized audit examples, case study summaries, and metrics only where verified.

Entity and Citation Plan

GEO and AEO require more than publishing pages. LinkGraph needs entity reinforcement across sources AI systems already trust.

Workstream Action Why it matters
Third-party lists Earn inclusion in best enterprise SEO agencies, best technical SEO agencies, best AI SEO agencies, best AEO agencies, and best link building services lists. LLMs repeatedly use list pages and directories as recommendation sources.
Industry publications Pitch original data from Search Atlas on AI citation patterns, enterprise SEO volatility, and prompt visibility. Editorial citations help AI systems connect LinkGraph with the topic, not only its own website.
Comparison pages Create LinkGraph vs WebFX, LinkGraph vs Coalition, LinkGraph vs Page One Power, LinkGraph vs SEOReseller, and LinkGraph vs Onely pages where fair and factual. Comparison pages capture evaluation intent and clarify the category difference.
Entity consistency Standardize descriptions across LinkGraph profiles, agency directories, social profiles, author bios, and partner pages. AI systems need repeated, consistent associations to resolve the brand cleanly.
Proof library Build reusable case studies, service deliverable examples, audit screenshots, and AI visibility baselines. Proof turns claims into citeable assets.

Roadmap

The roadmap should move in three waves: establish the owned foundation, reinforce the entity off-site, then monitor and iterate with Search Atlas.

0-30 days

  • Publish or rebuild Enterprise SEO Services.
  • Publish AI SEO / AEO / GEO service hub.
  • Publish Technical SEO Services.
  • Add service and organization schema.
  • Finalize weekly prompt tracking set in Search Atlas.

30-60 days

  • Publish 8-12 support pages across enterprise, AI search, and technical SEO clusters.
  • Start third-party list inclusion outreach.
  • Publish one original Search Atlas-backed AI visibility study.
  • Create first comparison pages.
  • Build reusable proof blocks for service pages.

60-90 days

  • Expand into B2B SaaS, ecommerce, marketplace, and multi-location pages if services are confirmed.
  • Refresh pages based on Search Atlas rank and LLM mention movement.
  • Publish case studies and audit examples.
  • Run quarterly entity consistency audit.
  • Report GEO/AEO share of voice alongside organic rankings.
