C2 — "Our" ownership language in pricing sections and FAQs
CRITICAL
FindSelfStorage.com is a marketplace aggregator — not an operator. Possessive "our" language implies ownership and is a style system violation on every page.
Car: "facilities listed through our search tool"
Boat: "facilities you'll find through our search tool"
RV: "facilities in our search network"
Wine FAQ: "Use our search tool to find..."
Motorcycle FAQ: "Use our search tool to find..."

→ "facilities listed on FindSelfStorage.com"
→ "the FindSelfStorage.com search"
→ "facilities in the network"
Car Boat RV Wine Motorcycle
C3 — RV page is missing the climate control section
CRITICAL
Every other vehicle page has an explicit H2 covering when climate control matters (car: "Should You Look for Climate Controlled Car Storage?"; boat, motorcycle: same pattern). The RV page has no equivalent. This is both a scope gap and a cross-set inconsistency — climate control for RVs is a real search query.

Fix: Add a "Do You Need Climate Controlled RV Storage?" section, matching the structure on the other vehicle pages. Note that availability varies by facility and direct users to check listings.

Major Findings — Fix Before Publish
M1 — Car page H1 uses "Vehicle" instead of "Car"
MAJOR
The primary keyword is "car storage" not "vehicle storage." Every other page keeps its noun consistent through the H1. This dilutes exact-match signal.
H1: "Car Storage Units: Find Indoor, Covered & Outdoor Vehicle Storage Near You"
→ "Car Storage Units: Find Indoor, Covered & Outdoor Car Storage Near You"
Car
M2 — Car page H2 implies agency ("We Connect You With")
MAJOR
The H2 "Types of Car Storage Units We Connect You With" implies the platform operates as a connector/agent, not a neutral marketplace. It's the only heading across all 5 pages with this framing.
"Types of Car Storage Units We Connect You With"
→ "Types of Car Storage Units" or "Car Storage Unit Types Explained"
Car
M3 — Car and Boat intros follow identical syntactic template
MAJOR
Both intros use the same sentence scaffold: "Whether you [X, Y, or Z], [noun] units offer a safer and more [adjective] [solution/alternative] than leaving your [item] exposed in a [location]." Anyone reading both pages notices this immediately. It signals bulk production, not original writing.
Car: "Whether you're reclaiming your garage from a seasonal sports car, protecting a classic during winter, or need long-term secure parking while you travel, car storage units offer a safer and more accessible alternative than leaving your vehicle exposed on a driveway..."

Boat: "Whether you need winter storage for a fishing boat, a secure spot to park your pontoon between outings, or a protected space for a cabin cruiser you only use seasonally, boat storage units offer a safer and more practical solution than leaving your watercraft exposed in a driveway..."
Fix: Rewrite the Boat intro with a distinct structure. Boats have specific triggers — dock access, HOA parking bans, haul-out timing — that can open the page without mirroring the Car intro.
Boat Car
M4 — "Specialty Wine Vaults" may not be in the network
MAJOR
The wine page introduces Specialty Wine Vaults as a storage type "available through" the platform. This is not a confirmed storage category in the FindSelfStorage.com style system or website research. If the platform does not list specialty wine vaults, this describes a service it can't deliver — a factual accuracy issue.
Action required: CSM to confirm whether specialty wine vault facilities are in the network. If not confirmed, reframe as general market context: "Some markets have dedicated wine vault facilities, though availability varies. Filter for climate-controlled options near you to see what's listed."

What's Working — Preserve These
Wine page intro
"Wine is one of the few things you store that actively changes based on its environment." — Best opener in the set. Specific, human, immediately useful. This is the voice model for the other pages.
RV class table
Unique to this page. Class A/B/C classification directly affects unit sizing decisions — this is information users genuinely need and competitors don't always provide clearly.
Motorcycle "Ready to Ride After Storage" section
Differentiating section not present on any other page. Creates a natural second visit for seasonal riders. Well-executed, practical, earns the page a longer dwell time.
Boat dry stack section
Well-structured, honest about limitations (advance notice, staffing hours), not oversold. Exactly the kind of decision-support content the scope called for.
All unit size tables
Complete, specific, and matched to real vehicle types. These tables answer the actual search query and are well-formatted for scanning.
Prep checklists (detail level)
11, 12, 10, and 9 steps respectively — thorough and specific. The depth is appropriate for high-intent informational searchers and competitive with the best-performing pages in this SERP category.
FAQ count and structure
All 5 pages hit 6 FAQs, each structured as a discrete question with a focused answer. The PAA-style phrasing is consistent throughout.
Marketplace framing in body copy
Body sections consistently avoid "our facilities" and "our units." Violations are limited to FAQs and pricing summaries — the main content is clean on this dimension.
Cross-linking between vehicle pages
Car, boat, RV, and motorcycle cross-link to each other appropriately in the "Also looking for..." sections. Internal equity is flowing correctly across the vehicle cluster.
