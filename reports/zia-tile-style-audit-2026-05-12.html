Remaining Issues

CRITICAL — red-clay-8x8-square.md: Incomplete (Score 47)

5 of 9 required sections are "Template Pending" with no content: About, Tile Usage, How It's Made, Order & Shipping, Installation Guide. Only a romance paragraph and FAQ exist. Cannot be published. Also contains sealer product names (511 Porous Plus, Fila Matte Finish Protective Wax) in FAQ — per §4.3 these should not appear in customer-facing pages.

CRITICAL — shape-large-format.md: Marble Outdoor Rule Incorrect

States "marble is not suitable for any exterior floor." §4.1 explicitly says: "Can be used outdoors in appropriate climates. Do not state that it cannot." Must be corrected before publishing.

CRITICAL — cotto-allende.md: Pre-grout Sealing Error

Install FAQ states sealing is required before AND after grouting. §4.3 specifies Cotto Allende only requires post-grout sealing in moisture-exposed areas — not pre-grout sealing. Risk of customer confusion and incorrect installation.
