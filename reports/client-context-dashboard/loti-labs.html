Voice

How this client should sound.

  • Authoritative without academic: Confident in the science, plain in the language. Explain technical terms at first use.
  • Precise, not promotional: Specific numbers (1 PM EST, 1–2 hour response, 30ml, 99.91%) beat vague superlatives.
  • Boutique and personal: Small team. The service commitment is a genuine differentiator — name it specifically.
  • Compliant by default: Research-only positioning is not a disclaimer. It is built into how every compound is described.
  • Researcher-to-researcher: Treat the reader as a qualified professional. Do not condescend or pad with marketing fluff.
  • "Every batch is tested by an independent third-party lab in Orlando, FL. COAs are available on the product page." — specific, verifiable, trust-building
  • "Liquid tadalafil is studied for vascular and PDE-5 research applications." — research-only framing, accurate
  • "Orders placed before 1 PM EST (M–F) ship the same day." — concrete, believable

2. Voice & Tone

Voice: professional, scientific, warm. Think researcher-to-researcher — not salesperson-to-prospect. The brand is explicitly not a "massive operation." Copy can sound human and boutique, especially in About, FAQ, and customer service contexts.

| Attribute | Rule |
|---|---|
| Authoritative without academic | Confident in the science, plain in the language. Explain technical terms at first use. |
| Precise, not promotional | Specific numbers (1 PM EST, 1–2 hour response, 30ml, 99.91%) beat vague superlatives. |
| Boutique and personal | Small team. The service commitment is a genuine differentiator — name it specifically. |
| Compliant by default | Research-only positioning is not a disclaimer. It is built into how every compound is described. |
| Researcher-to-researcher | Treat the reader as a qualified professional. Do not condescend or pad with marketing fluff. |

2.1 Voice in practice

Do this:

  • "Every batch is tested by an independent third-party lab in Orlando, FL. COAs are available on the product page." — specific, verifiable, trust-building

  • "Liquid tadalafil is studied for vascular and PDE-5 research applications." — research-only framing, accurate

  • "Orders placed before 1 PM EST (M–F) ship the same day." — concrete, believable

  • "The founders bring 9+ years of prior experience in research supply." — defensible tenure language

    • Not this:

  • "Trust the experts at Loti Labs for unmatched quality you can count on!" — generic superlative

  • "Liquid tadalafil helps with ED and improves circulation." — therapeutic claim, non-compliant

  • "With 14+ years of proven expertise, Loti Labs has been the trusted leader…" — tenure inconsistency, propagates unresolved issue

  • "Lightning-fast shipping you can rely on." — filler; the 1 PM EST cutoff is the claim

    • 2.2 Sentence and paragraph rules

  • Every paragraph answers one specific question (explicit or implied by the heading).

  • The first sentence of any paragraph must be a direct answer or direct statement — no throat-clearing.

  • Paragraphs: 2–5 lines. One main idea.

  • Do not use dashes — never, under any circumstances, in any form (em dash, en dash, hyphen-as-separator). Use a period or restructure the sentence.

  • Use active voice. Passive only where the subject is unknown or irrelevant.

  • Each H2 section begins with a short introductory paragraph that includes the primary keyword (or close variation) and previews what the section covers. Do not repeat the H3 content verbatim in the intro.

    4.3 What writers must never do

  • Make therapeutic, medical, or human-benefit claims about any product ("helps with weight loss," "improves recovery," "reduces wrinkles").

  • Provide reconstitution, dosing, administration, or research methodology guidance. Loti Labs' position is that buyers are qualified researchers with their own training.

  • Compare products to FDA-approved drugs in a way that implies equivalent therapeutic effect.

  • Frame products as supplements, nutraceuticals, or cosmetics for personal use.

  • Use before/after imagery or testimonials describing physical effects on the body.

  • State a specific company founding year. (See §11 — unresolved.)

  • Assert GMP compliance without confirmation. (See §11 — unresolved.)

  • Assert 99%+ purity as a universal blanket claim across all products. State per-batch purity with a COA link, or use "typically high-purity — see COA for batch-specific results."
