  • **Company:** Altify — the Account Planning AI Cloud for enterprise revenue teams.

  • **Brand promise:** Plan smarter. Execute consistently. Grow predictably.

  • **Positioning:** Not positioned as "just software." Altify is a combination of Salesforce-native software, AI-enhanced workflows, proven sales methodology, relationship intelligence, account planning, opportunity management, and revenue enablement services.

  • **Care level:** High Touch / High Value client. Maintain strict terminology adherence and a professional enterprise tone at all times.

  • Already using Salesforce

    2. Voice & Tone

    Altify content should sound: enterprise-ready, clear, direct, confident, strategic, practical, outcome-driven.

    **Litmus test:** Does this read like a senior revenue strategist, or a vendor selling?

    2.1 Core tone attributes

    | Attribute | Writing rule |
    |---|---|
    | Confident | Speak with authority about enterprise sales strategy |
    | Educational | Explain frameworks, methodology, and revenue strategy — don't just assert |
    | Results-focused | Emphasize measurable outcomes: win rate, deal size, revenue growth, forecast accuracy |
    | Strategic | Focus on planning, execution, and long-term customer relationships |
    | Practical | Ground claims in specific business impact, not abstract capability |

    2.2 Voice rules

  • Use first-person plural when Altify is the speaker ("we help," "our platform," "Altify gives teams").

  • Avoid casual or generic language — this is enterprise content for senior buyers.

  • Do not copy guideline sentences verbatim into output.

  • Do not repeat "AI-powered" more than once per page.

  • Limit em dashes to one per page.

    • 2.3 Avoid

  • Generic AI buzzwords

  • Long abstract claims without business impact

  • Excessive hype or promotional padding

  • The word "tool" — replace with: solution, platform, engine, system

  • Fluffy language without a grounded outcome

  • Content that sounds like basic sales tips rather than enterprise strategy

    Altify maps buying groups, influence networks, and stakeholder relationships.

    For executive personas: position around consistency, risk visibility, and forecast confidence.

    Use: predictable revenue growth, forecasts leaders can defend, earlier risk visibility, cleaner pipeline, stronger commit accuracy, more reliable deal health, consistent execution across teams.

    5.5 Reducing manual work

    For sellers and operators: reduced admin, less manual research, fewer spreadsheets, more selling time.

    Use: reduce admin drag, eliminate manual account planning, stop rebuilding org charts, replace static plans, automate research, spend more time selling, keep insights current inside Salesforce.

    7. Landing Page Structure

    Use this structure for all persona-driven landing pages.

    Section 1 — Hero

    Elements: H1 with primary keyword, short subheadline, primary CTA.

    Purpose: state the main value proposition immediately.

    Section 2 — Core Pain

    Elements: H2, intro paragraph, H3 pain points with short explanations.

    Purpose: show the reader Altify understands their specific problems.

    Section 3 — Altify Solution

    Elements: H2, H3 solution blocks, short explanation under each, optional micro-CTA.

    Purpose: connect Altify's capabilities directly to the persona's problems.

    Section 4 — Trust / Social Proof

    Elements: H2, bullet-point outcomes, stats, or proof points.

    Purpose: make the claims credible.

    Section 5 — FAQ

    Elements: persona-specific questions, slightly expanded answers, no repetition of the page body.

    Purpose: handle objections and clarify fit.
