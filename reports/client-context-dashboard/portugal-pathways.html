    • 2.1 Golden Visa Framing

    **Frame the Golden Visa as a mobility tool, not an emigration decision.**

    Most clients are not trying to move country. They want freedom of movement without visa friction, tax disruption, or residency constraints. Almost none are motivated by financial upside from the investment itself. Lead with structural and logistical advantages.

    3. Terminology

    3.1 Required terms and spellings (British English)

    **Residency programmes:**

  • **Golden Visa** — residency-by-investment programme. Current qualifying routes include: (1) subscription of at least €500,000 in a CMVM-regulated investment fund; (2) certain business creation and capital investment routes; (3) cultural or artistic production donations from €250,000 (reduced thresholds apply in low-density areas). Direct residential property purchase is not a qualifying route since end of 2023. Do not imply property qualifies. When listing routes, present the fund route first as it is the most common. Never describe the investment as the benefit — lead with the residency rights and optionality it unlocks.

  • **D7 Visa** — passive income / retirement visa; requires genuine physical presence (6 consecutive months or 8 months across a year)

  • **D2 Visa** (Entrepreneur Visa) — for non-EU nationals starting or investing in a business in Portugal; requires genuine residence

  • **D8 Visa** (Digital Nomad Visa) — for remote workers; requires genuine physical presence (6 consecutive months or 8 months across a year)

  • **IFICI** (NHR 2.0; full name: Incentivo Fiscal à Investigação Científica e Inovação) — the current incentivised tax regime; 20% flat income tax rate; 10-year period. Narrower than NHR: eligible professions are specifically defined and broadly limited to R&D, highly qualified technical roles, and senior-level business roles in qualifying sectors. Self-employed and passive income earners who qualified under NHR may not qualify under IFICI. Pension income is not covered at favourable rates. Always characterise IFICI as narrower and more targeted than its predecessor. Application deadline is 15 January of the year following the year in which Portuguese tax residency begins — this is strict and widely missed.

  • **NHR** (Non-Habitual Residency) — closed to new entrants 31 December 2023. Existing holders continue their individual 10-year period. Never imply NHR can be renewed or that it is available to new applicants. Do not describe NHR as expiring globally — it expires for individuals when their 10-year period ends, not as a system on a single date.

    • **Portuguese institutions and regulators:**

  • **AIMA** — the Portuguese immigration authority (do not say "SEF" — AIMA replaced SEF)

  • **CMVM** — Portuguese financial regulator (fund governance)

  • **SNS** (Serviço Nacional de Saúde) — the public health system

    • **Property terms:**

  • **CPCV** (Contrato-Promessa de Compra e Venda) — promissory contract; define at first use

  • **Escritura** — completion deed

  • **NIF** — Portuguese tax identification number

  • **IMT** — Property Transfer Tax

  • **AIMI** — additional annual property tax on high-value assets

  • **off-plan** — hyphenated

  • **off-market** — hyphenated

  • **Golden Triangle** — the Algarve's prime resort area (Quinta do Lago, Vale do Lobo, Vilamoura)

  • "luxury new homes" — the primary term for Portugal Pathways' own property service; encompasses new-build, off-plan, and off-market. Do not use "property" or "real estate" when describing PP's own services. "Luxury new developments" is acceptable for location page H1s and meta titles.

  • "new-build" — for completed homes sold new

    4. Accuracy & Technical Standards

    4.1 Regulatory accuracy

  • **Golden Visa:** Current qualifying routes — see §3.1. Direct property purchase is not a qualifying route since end of 2023. Do not imply property qualifies. Do not bundle property and Golden Visa in the same logical chain — they are entirely separate matters.

  • **Golden Visa physical presence:** "On average approximately seven days per year" — broadly around fourteen days across the initial two-year residence period. This low-presence structure is specific to the Golden Visa. It is not a general feature of Portuguese residency. Never attribute the 7-day figure to Portugal broadly or to any D visa. Present it as approximate and subject to legislative change, not as a fixed contractual entitlement.

  • **Tax residency vs. immigration residency:** Holding a Portuguese Golden Visa does not automatically make an individual a Portuguese tax resident. Tax residency is determined separately, according to Portuguese tax law and individual circumstances. This distinction must be stated clearly wherever Golden Visa and tax planning are discussed in proximity. It is one of the highest-risk conflation points.

  • **D7 / D8 / D2 physical presence:** These visas require genuine residence in Portugal — typically six consecutive months, or eight months across a year. They are suited to substantive relocation, not low-presence optionality. The approximately 7-day annual presence figure does not apply to these routes.

  • **Property and residency:** Property ownership in Portugal does not confer residency or any low-presence benefit. The real estate route was withdrawn at end of 2023. Never imply otherwise.

  • **Schengen access:** A Portuguese residency permit provides visa-free travel within the Schengen Area for short stays. Always qualify: "generally up to 90 days in any 180-day period under current rules." Never state Schengen access as unlimited or unconditional.

  • **Residency approval:** Always discretionary, subject to AIMA review, policy changes, and applicant suitability. Never imply automatic approval.

  • **NHR:** Closed to new entrants 31 December 2023. Existing holders continue their individual 10-year term. Do not present NHR as available to new applicants. Reference IFICI for the current incentivised tax regime. Do not imply NHR is universally expiring on a fixed future date — it is expiring for specific individuals when their own 10-year period ends.

  • **Portugal Nationality Law (2026):** The naturalisation timeline was extended and now differs by nationality and individual circumstances. Do not write "citizenship in 5 years" or any fixed period — this is outdated and inaccurate.

  • **AIMA vs SEF:** AIMA is the current immigration authority. SEF was dissolved. Use AIMA.

  • **D2 timeline:** Company formation 1–2 days (simple cases); D2 visa processing typically 2–4 months.

    • 4.2 Portugal Pathways scope

    Portugal Pathways does not provide:

  • Regulated investment advice

  • Regulated tax advice

  • Regulated legal advice

    • Content must make this clear when topics approach these areas. Use phrasings like:

  • "Portugal Pathways provides introductions to licensed professionals rather than regulated advice."

  • "Suitability, risk assessment, and specific product recommendations require authorised professionals."

  • "Cross-border tax and investment considerations typically require specialist guidance."

    • 4.3 Property and financial claims

  • Capital appreciation: always note returns are market-driven and not guaranteed.

  • Cost of living comparisons: only cite verified figures, attributed to Portugal Pathways research or a named source (e.g., PMI Report).

  • Off-market access claims: **Do not use the "35%" figure** ("35% of luxury off-market and off-plan opportunities are not listed") — this is explicitly in the Luxury New Homes compliance guide AVOID list (unverifiable). Instead, use: "many off-plan and off-market luxury new homes are not widely advertised on mainstream portals" or "not openly marketed." The "more than 250 developers, architects, builders and designers" figure is verified — use it accurately; do not inflate.

  • Portugal's listing landscape: Portugal does not operate a nationwide multiple listing service. This is factual and approved framing for explaining off-market access.

  • Payment structures: "typically" or "common frameworks include" — not presented as universal.

    5. Specificity Rules

    **Location content must include:**

  • Named districts and neighbourhoods (not just "central Lisbon")

  • Specific distances and travel times (format: "approximately X minutes' drive")

  • Named schools, hospitals, and cultural venues where relevant

  • Factual descriptions of what defines the area (planning framework, heritage protections, geography)

    • **Do not describe locations with generic lifestyle superlatives.** Instead, describe what is factually true about the place and let the reader draw the conclusion.

    • Bad: "Lisbon is one of Europe's most vibrant cities with an unrivalled lifestyle offer."

    • Good: "Lisbon's food scene ranges from neighbourhood tascas to Time Out Market Lisboa, where some of Portugal's leading chefs present regional produce in a single riverside location."

    **Supply constraint framing (from approved examples):**
    When explaining why prime property is scarce:

  • "New supply in [area] is structurally limited, with construction tightly restricted by planning rules."

  • "Heritage protections, geography, and planning controls limit where new homes can be delivered."

  • "New homes enter the market through redevelopment or replacement within established plots — not through new construction phases."

    9. Audience-Specific Writing Rules

    Asian and Middle Eastern Audiences

    Asian and Middle Eastern prospects have distinct constraints that require a different content approach. Do not treat them as a geo-targeted variant of western campaigns.

    Key differences:

  • Many cannot or do not want second passports — **renewable residency is the priority**, not citizenship

  • Education-led pathways are highly valued

  • Messaging should lead with **compliance, compatibility, and renewable structure**

  • These audiences prioritise residency flexibility over complete relocation

    • Content for this audience must be developed with a distinct copy logic. Do not apply western campaign language or lifestyle framing here.

    LGBTQ+ Audience Signal

    Portugal's social stability, safety record, and inclusivity are named conversion triggers for LGBTQ+ prospects. Secondary messaging should include subtle signals around inclusivity, gender safety, racial safety, and European social climate. Resonates strongly across HNWI segments broadly and the "pink pound" specifically.

    Family as a Cross-Segment Driver

    Family protection and children's future mobility are dominant emotional drivers across **all** HNW segments — even asset-focused or operationally-minded audiences. Content touching on education access, children's safety, and family continuity has broad relevance beyond the Family Legacy Planner segment.
