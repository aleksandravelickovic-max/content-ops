Client Snapshot

Deer Solution is a deer control repellent service company. The core offer is professional deer control for homeowners who want to protect flowers, shrubs, and expensive landscaping from deer damage.

The company positions itself around customized deer control plans, Certified Deer Damage Experts, free at-home consultations and estimates, and a proprietary deer repellent that works by taste and smell. Locations are independently owned and operated.

Core Offerings

  • Deer Control Repellent Service: Professional recurring service that helps deter deer from eating plants and shrubs.

  • Free At-Home Consultation and Estimate: Initial visit where a Certified Deer Damage Expert evaluates the property and creates a customized deer control protection plan.

  • Customized Deer Control Protection Plan: Property-specific plan built around the customer's landscape and deer damage problem.

  • Professional Application Service: Deer repellent service performed by trained and licensed deer control professionals.

  • Franchise Opportunity: Separate franchise ownership offer linked from the main site. Treat this as a business opportunity, not a homeowner service.

    • Voice

    Practical, reassuring, and homeowner-focused. The writing should sound like a specialist who understands how frustrating deer damage is and can explain the plan plainly.

    Use concrete homeowner language:

    • plants and shrubs

    • flower buds

    • expensive landscaping

    • deer damage

    • customized protection plan

    • deer control repellent service

    Avoid broad pest-control language. Deer Solution is not a general pest control company in our writing.

    Positioning Rules

  • Say `Deer Solution` as the brand name unless source material requires a specific location/franchise name.

  • The service is deer control and deer repellent service.

  • Do not expand the client into lawn care, landscaping, mosquito control, tick control, or general pest control.

  • Do not guarantee that deer will disappear, never return, or cause zero damage in all cases.

  • Claims about the repellent should stay close to the mechanism: it works by taste and smell to deter deer from eating treated plants.

  • Any customer-result language should be framed as testimonial or example, not a universal promise.

  • Mention that branches are independently owned and operated when writing about local branches, franchise coverage, or service availability.

    • Avoid

  • guaranteed deer removal

  • permanent deer elimination

  • chemical-heavy pest control framing

  • lawn care or landscaping provider framing

  • claims that the service works for every property or every plant without conditions

  • implying Deer Solution offers services beyond deer control and deer repellent service

    • Open Items

  • Confirm CSM / point of contact.

  • Confirm active monthly volume.

  • Confirm priority and production cadence.

  • Confirm whether content is for national pages, local franchise pages, blogs, or optimizations.

  • Confirm whether any location-specific terminology or service-area disclaimers are required.
