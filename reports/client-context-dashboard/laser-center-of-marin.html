    4. Accuracy & Technical Standards

  • Do not promise specific outcomes for any treatment.

  • Do not present any procedure as universally effective or risk-free.

  • Do not make comparative claims against competitor clinics.

  • Allergan Black Diamond and Galderma Platinum designations are earned — use them accurately. Black Diamond is an Allergan designation; Platinum is Galderma. Do not conflate.

  • When referencing specific treatments (Botox, laser types), reference them by their correct names. Do not invent technical specifications.

  • "Physician-supervised" means a physician oversees the practice and protocols — not necessarily that a physician performs every treatment. Do not overclaim the level of direct physician involvement without confirmation.

    5. Specificity Rules

  • Prefer specific claims over general ones. "Serves Marin County since 2003" is better than "a long-standing local clinic."

  • Use provider designation names (Black Diamond, Platinum) when establishing authority.

  • Reference specific treatments rather than vague categories. "IPL photofacial" or "Fraxel resurfacing" is more credible than "advanced laser treatments."

  • Attribute patient outcomes to individual factors: skin type, treatment history, compliance with post-care instructions.
