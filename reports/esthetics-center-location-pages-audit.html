Issues by Category
Category 1  ·  Style & Terminology
Credential Hyphenation Error — "double-board-certified"
WARNING

The STYLE-SYSTEM requires double board-certified — a hyphen only between "board" and "certified." Two pages incorrectly add an extra hyphen between "double" and "board," producing double-board-certified.

Affected Pages (2)
Rocklin – Lip Flip line 59 Fair Oaks – Oxygen Facial line 56
Exact Lines
Rocklin – Lip Flip, line 59
"double-board-certified facial plastic and reconstructive surgeon and a Fellow"
Fair Oaks – Oxygen Facial, line 56
"double-board-certified facial plastic and reconstructive surgeon who has treated more than 12,000 patients"
Fix

Remove the hyphen between "double" and "board" on both pages.
double-board-certified → double board-certified

Category 2  ·  Terminology — §3.1
"Non-invasive" Instead of "Non-surgical" on All Oxygen Facial Pages
WARNING

STYLE-SYSTEM §3.1 states: "Non-surgical — preferred over 'non-invasive' for injectable and energy-based treatments." All 4 oxygen facial pages open their "What Is an Oxygen Facial?" section with "non-invasive" in the first sentence.

The 8 non-oxygen pages (all lip flip and all lymphatic drainage pages) correctly avoid this term and pass this check.

Affected Pages (4)
Sacramento – Oxygen Facial Fair Oaks – Oxygen Facial El Dorado Hills – Oxygen Facial Rocklin – Oxygen Facial
Exact Language
Sacramento & El Dorado Hills:
"an oxygen facial is a non-invasive skin treatment"
Fair Oaks & Rocklin:
"An oxygen facial is a non-invasive treatment"
Fix

Replace non-invasive with non-surgical in the opening sentence of each oxygen facial page's "What Is an Oxygen Facial?" section.

Category 5  ·  Documentation Gap
Fair Oaks Not Listed in STYLE-SYSTEM §4 — Documentation Needs Update
INFO

STYLE-SYSTEM §4 states: "Three locations: Sacramento, Rocklin, El Dorado Hills. Do not imply other locations exist." However, the ClickUp task explicitly commissioned Fair Oaks as a 4th location, and all 3 Fair Oaks pages carry a real address: 5442 Hazel Ave, Suite 504, Fair Oaks, CA 95628 — (279) 202-1844.

This is a documentation gap, not a content error. The Fair Oaks page content is well-written. The style guide was likely not updated when the 4th location opened.

Affected Pages (3)
Fair Oaks – Lip Flip Fair Oaks – Oxygen Facial Fair Oaks – Lymphatic Drainage Massage
Action Required

CSM to confirm Fair Oaks location is live, then update STYLE-SYSTEM §4 to list all 4 locations. No changes needed to the Fair Oaks page content itself.

Note: This finding does not block publishing. It flags an internal documentation inconsistency only.
